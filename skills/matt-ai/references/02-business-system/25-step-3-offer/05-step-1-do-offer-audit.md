### Framework & Criteria

There are eight criteria worth five points each.

Maximum Score: 40/40

#### Criterion #1: Built for Cold Traffic

Does the Offer make sense to someone who does not know or trust you?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** They immediately understand what you sell and why it is relevant.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** They understand after a short, simple explanation.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** They need additional context before they understand.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** They need substantial education before the Offer makes sense.

⭐ **1 Star:** They are still confused after you explain it.

They should not need to consume 10 hours of your content before understanding what you sell and why it is relevant to them.

Cold Traffic Score: ___ / 5

#### Criterion #2: Dream Outcome / Something People MUST Buy

How badly does your specific ideal customer want or need the result?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** Solves an urgent problem the customer feels they must address now.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** Solves an important problem the customer clearly wants to address.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** Solves a real and valuable problem, but the customer is comfortable waiting.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** Solves a minor problem or provides a useful nice-to-have.

⭐ **1 Star:** Solves a problem the customer barely cares about.

The strongest Offers solve urgent, important and expensive problems connected to something the customer already wants and spends money trying to achieve.

Dream Outcome Score: ___ / 5

#### Criterion #3: Creates Obvious Value

Does the Offer help the customer make more money, save time, reduce costs, reduce risk, or increase speed?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** Creates a substantial, measurable improvement whose value clearly exceeds the price.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** Creates a substantial improvement, but its value relative to the price needs more explanation.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** Creates a clear benefit, but its size or financial value is difficult to establish.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** Creates a vague or minor improvement.

⭐ **1 Star:** Creates no obvious value for the customer.

How substantial is that improvement, and how clearly can the customer see its value relative to what they pay?

One extremely valuable result can earn five stars. The Offer does not need to do everything on the list.

Obvious Value Score: ___ / 5

#### Criterion #4: Perceived Likelihood of Achievement

How strongly does the customer believe your Offer will work for someone like them?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** Has overwhelming proof from customers in similar situations and makes success feel extremely likely.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** Has strong relevant proof, a clear mechanism and answers to the major objections.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** Has a believable explanation and some proof.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** The promise sounds possible but is mostly unsupported.

⭐ **1 Star:** The customer has no reason to believe it will work.

Increase their belief through relevant proof, case studies, clear deliverables, a believable explanation of how the Offer works and answers to their major objections.

Perceived Likelihood Score: ___ / 5

#### Criterion #5: Clear Result or Deliverable

Does the customer know exactly what they are buying and receiving?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** Clearly defines the result or deliverable, what is included and when the customer will receive it.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** Clearly defines the result or deliverable and what is included, but the timing is unclear.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** The customer understands the general deliverable, but important details about what they receive are unclear.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** Describes activities without clearly explaining what the customer receives.

⭐ **1 Star:** The Offer is completely vague.

Ideally, sell a result. If you do not control the final result, sell a clear deliverable directly connected to it.

Clear Result Score: ___ / 5

#### Criterion #6: Time to First Value

How quickly does the customer receive their first meaningful result or benefit?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** Within 24 hours.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** More than 24 hours, but within seven days.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** More than seven days, but within 30 days.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** More than 30 days, but within 90 days.

⭐ **1 Star:** More than 90 days.

This does not have to be the complete result. It should be something the customer can use or benefit from while you work toward it.

Measure from when the customer buys.

Time to First Value Score: ___ / 5

#### Criterion #7: Customer Effort and Sacrifice

How much work must the customer complete to receive the value you promise?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** Almost everything is done for them, with minimal onboarding and almost no ongoing work.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** The customer completes some onboarding and a small amount of ongoing work.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** The customer must participate regularly, but the workload is manageable.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** The customer must complete substantial work throughout the service.

⭐ **1 Star:** The customer must do almost everything.

Include their onboarding, meetings, decisions, approvals and required implementation.

Effort and Sacrifice Score: ___ / 5

#### Criterion #8: Strong Risk Reversal

How much downside does the customer face if the Offer does not work out?

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** There is almost no realistic downside for the right customer.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** Their financial, time and implementation risks are substantially limited.

⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** Some risks are limited, but meaningful downside remains.

⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** There is very little protection if the Offer does not work out.

⭐ **1 Star:** The customer assumes all the risk.

Their fee is not their only risk. They can also lose time, money, opportunity and reputation.

A guarantee can help, but the price, scope and commitment should also limit their downside.

Risk Reversal Score: ___ / 5

### Scoring Scale

- 8-15: Terrible
- 16-23: Weak
- 24-31: Acceptable, with clear weaknesses to improve
- 32-35: Strong
- 36-40: Exceptional

### Example

**Offer: Agency Program**

Built for Cold Traffic ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: Clear pitch, "the program that gets agency owners to $50K a month," no confusion about what it is.

Dream Outcome / Something People MUST Buy ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: Agencies live or die on consistent client acquisition, this isn't a nice-to-have, it's survival.

Creates Obvious Value ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5: Directly targets the thing that determines whether their business grows or stalls.

Perceived Likelihood of Achievement ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: Member results and case studies back it up, with answers to the objections that come up before someone joins.

Clear Result or Deliverable ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5: A defined curriculum, community access, and swipe-ready templates, nothing vague about what's included.

Time to First Value ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5: Access is instant, they can start the first module the day they pay.

Customer Effort and Sacrifice ⭐⭐ 2/5: They watch the content, build everything themselves, and do all the implementation, nobody is doing the work for them.

Strong Risk Reversal ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: A real guarantee backs it, not just a vague promise.

Total Score: 33/40, Strong

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of your [Offer Audit Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10trg6w97DUTYKPTB1YtRkgCMXHD8cHTDglJ4sZmKP5o/edit?tab=t.2smnutna74tv).** This is where you'll score your Offer on eight criteria, each worth up to five stars, and add them up for a score out of 40. That score tells you whether your Offer is actually good before you try to improve it. [in this skill: assets/templates/offer-audit-template/]
2. **Score Built for Cold Traffic using the template.** This is whether the Offer makes sense to someone who does not know or trust you. They should not need to consume 10 hours of your content before understanding what you sell and why it is relevant to them.
3. **Score Dream Outcome / Something People MUST Buy using the template.** This is how badly your specific ideal customer wants or needs the result. The strongest Offers solve urgent, important, and expensive problems connected to something the customer already wants and spends money trying to achieve.
4. **Score Creates Obvious Value using the template.** This is whether the Offer helps the customer make more money, save time, reduce costs, reduce risk, or increase speed, and how clearly they can see its value relative to what they pay. One extremely valuable result can earn five stars, the Offer does not need to do everything on the list.
5. **Score Perceived Likelihood of Achievement using the template.** This is how strongly the customer believes your Offer will work for someone like them. You increase their belief through relevant proof, case studies, clear deliverables, a believable explanation of how the Offer works, and answers to their major objections.
6. **Score Clear Result or Deliverable using the template.** This is whether the customer knows exactly what they are buying and receiving. Ideally you sell a result, and if you do not control the final result, you sell a clear deliverable directly connected to it.
7. **Score Time to First Value using the template.** This is how quickly the customer receives their first meaningful result or benefit. It does not have to be the complete result, and you measure from when the customer buys.
8. **Score Customer Effort and Sacrifice using the template.** This is how much work the customer must complete to receive the value you promise. Include their onboarding, meetings, decisions, approvals, and required implementation.
9. **Score Strong Risk Reversal using the template.** This is how much downside the customer faces if the Offer does not work out. Their fee is not their only risk, they can also lose time, money, opportunity, and reputation.
10. **Add up all 8 scores for a total out of 40 and compare it to the scoring scale to see how your Offer ranks.** Scores run from 8-15 Terrible up through 36-40 Exceptional.
11. **Fix every individual criterion scoring below 4 stars, even if your total score is high.** Start with your lowest score, improve that part of the Offer, and complete the audit again.

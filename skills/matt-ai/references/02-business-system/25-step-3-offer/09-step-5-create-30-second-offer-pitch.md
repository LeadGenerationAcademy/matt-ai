Create a short explanation of your Offer that you can deliver whenever someone asks "What do you do?" The Pitch should make your Offer sound valuable and make them want to ask the next question.

### Framework & Criteria

**What Your Pitch Should Communicate**

- Your ICP
- What you do
- The Dream Outcome
- How the Offer works
- Time to first value
- How much work you handle for them
- One relevant proof point, if you have one

These components apply the Value Formula without forcing you to explain the Formula during a conversation.

**Use This Template**

"I run a [type of business] for [ICP]. We [what you do] so they can [Dream Outcome]. We normally [time to first value], and we handle [the difficult work] for them."

If you have one strong proof point, add: "We have already done this for [relevant proof]."

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [30-Second Offer Pitch Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10trg6w97DUTYKPTB1YtRkgCMXHD8cHTDglJ4sZmKP5o/edit?tab=t.phhlekvz54ao).** If you've already made a copy of the Offer Templates document for an earlier step, just use that same copy instead of making a new one. [in this skill: assets/templates/offer-audit-template/]
2. **Fill in the basic format.** Write it in your exact voice, if it sounds unnatural when you say it out loud, rewrite it.
3. **Add "We have already done this for [relevant proof]" if you have one strong proof point.** Don't force this in if you don't have a real one, an empty proof point is worse than none at all.
4. **Copy your Pitch into the [Talk Time Calculator](https://wordstotime.com/).** Click into the Word Count field and paste your script straight in, it counts the words for you and Result shows how long it takes to say out loud. Leave it on Reading Aloud and Average, that's the speed you'll actually talk at.
5. **Time yourself saying it out loud.** This is a second, low-tech way to check your timing if you don't want to use the calculator.
6. **Shorten it if it takes longer than 30 seconds.** Cut complicated terminology and unnecessary differentiation first, those add length without adding value.
7. **Repeat until you can comfortably deliver it in 30 seconds or less.** Comfortably is the key word, rushing through it doesn't count.
8. **Practice saying it out loud until it comes out automatically.** The other person should immediately understand who you help, what you do, what result you produce, and why the Offer is valuable.
9. **Practice until there is no hesitation, no "umms," and no "uhhs."** You need to know your Pitch so well you can deliver it the instant someone asks what you do.

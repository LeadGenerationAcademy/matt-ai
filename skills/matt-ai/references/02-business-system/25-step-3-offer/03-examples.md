### Offer Audit: Agency Program

Built for Cold Traffic ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: Clear pitch, "the program that gets agency owners to $50K a month," no confusion about what it is.

Dream Outcome / Something People MUST Buy ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: Agencies live or die on consistent client acquisition, this isn't a nice-to-have, it's survival.

Creates Obvious Value ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5: Directly targets the thing that determines whether their business grows or stalls.

Perceived Likelihood of Achievement ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: Member results and case studies back it up, with answers to the objections that come up before someone joins.

Clear Result or Deliverable ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5: A defined curriculum, community access, and swipe-ready templates, nothing vague about what's included.

Time to First Value ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5: Access is instant, they can start the first module the day they pay.

Customer Effort and Sacrifice ⭐⭐ 2/5: They watch the content, build everything themselves, and do all the implementation, nobody is doing the work for them.

Strong Risk Reversal ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4/5: A real guarantee backs it, not just a vague promise.

Total Score: 33/40, Strong

### Save Time

An offer that saves four hours per day and costs $15,000.

4 hours × 5 days × 50 weeks = 1,000 hours saved per year.

At $15,000, those 1,000 hours only need to be worth $15 each to break even.

- $30 per hour: $120 per day, $30,000 per year, 2.0x
- $50 per hour: $200 per day, $50,000 per year, 3.3x
- $75 per hour: $300 per day, $75,000 per year, 5.0x
- $100 per hour: $400 per day, $100,000 per year, 6.7x
- $125 per hour: $500 per day, $125,000 per year, 8.3x

At $75 per hour, that is $75,000 of annual value from a $15,000 purchase.

That is a 5x value-to-cost multiple.

### Reduce Risk

A compliance offer sold to healthcare professionals who need to securely store patient data. There are laws around this, and if they get hacked, they can face pretty hefty fines.

The average fine is $250,000 and the compliance service costs $2,500 per year.

Spell it out for them:

"If you have a data breach once every hundred years, this will pay for itself."

### Desired Result

Selling to agency owners doing $500k-$5m per year.

Bad: "Get your first client." They already have clients.

Better:

- Build a second acquisition channel
- Get out of sales
- Scale paid ads without CPA exploding
- Build a marketing team that generates customers without them
- Create consistent lead flow so revenue stops being feast or famine

### Perceived Likelihood Of Achievement

Bad: "We help businesses generate more leads."

Better:  "We work exclusively with companies with three or more crews. Here are 27 examples of companies like yours where we generated qualified leads, what they paid per lead and how many turned into jobs."

### Time Delay

The weight loss industry.

- On your own: calorie-tracking and fitness apps at $9.99 per month.
- Personal trainer and meal plan: still takes time, but faster, so they charge more than the apps.
- Liposuction: works extremely fast, so surgeons charge thousands or tens of thousands of dollars.
- A magic method where the moment someone swiped their credit card, they instantly had a six-pack: $100,000 per person or more.

### Effort & Sacrifice

Two companies promise the exact same result. Assume the result, price, time delay and perceived likelihood of achievement are identical.

Offer A (Bad): a 30-minute onboarding call, you give them access to what they need, they do everything else.

Offer B (Better): three calls per week, homework between calls, you learn the process yourself, you build everything yourself, another five hours per week implementing it.

### Offer Sales Letter

**Example #1**

**1. ICP**

This is for residential HVAC companies doing $1M+ a year in the United States.

You need at least 5 technicians on staff, authority over the marketing budget, and you want a repeatable system instead of relying on referrals alone. If you're a two-truck operation, this isn't for you yet.

**2. Headline**

Get 20 qualified homeowner leads booked on your calendar in the next 30 days.

**3. Their main problem**

Your business is dependent on referrals.

Some weeks your guys have more jobs than they can handle. Other weeks the schedule is wide open and nobody knows why. You're living off referrals and repeat customers, so your calendar depends on stuff you don't control.

**4. Their current circumstances**

Right now you're either running ads yourself with no real system behind them, or you already tried an agency that took your budget, sent a handful of unqualified leads, and never delivered what was promised.

You've started to believe paid advertising just doesn't work for a business like yours. You're doing the math on whether it's worth trying again.

**5. Their desired outcome**

You want a calendar booked out weeks in advance.

You want ready-to-buy homeowners filling your technicians' schedules, not more phone calls to sort through. You want recurring revenue predictable enough to plan hiring around, and you want to spend your time running the business instead of chasing your own leads.

**6. Why previous solutions failed**

Referrals don't scale. You can't grow a business waiting for the phone to ring.

SEO takes months to produce anything, which is more patience than most HVAC owners can afford.

Generic agencies build the same funnel for every industry, so nothing is built around how homeowners actually search for and buy HVAC service.

Hiring an in-house marketer means paying a full salary before you know whether they can deliver.

**7. Your mechanism**

We only do residential HVAC.

Every ad, landing page, and offer is built around how homeowners search for and buy HVAC service. We run the same campaign system across every HVAC client, so it's already tested before it ever runs for you.

Your dedicated account manager builds, launches, and optimizes your campaigns on that system. All you have to do is pick up the phone and close the leads we send you.

**8. How it works**

We start by getting access to your ad platforms and CRM. We build your ad creative and landing page and send it to you for approval. We set up call tracking and test the CRM connection end to end. Then campaigns go live across Google Local Service Ads and Meta, and your account manager optimizes them daily from the first click.

**9. What's included**

Fully built and managed Google Local Service Ads and Meta campaigns

A custom landing page built specifically for residential HVAC offers

Call tracking and lead notifications connected to your CRM or inbox

A dedicated account manager for your account

Weekly reporting on ROAS, cost per lead, and campaign performance

**10. Timeline**

Days 1-4: Onboarding call, account setup, and access to your ad platforms and CRM.

Days 5-10: Ad creative and landing page built and sent to you for approval.

Days 11-14: Call tracking and CRM integration tested and confirmed working.

Day 15: Campaigns go live across Google Local Service Ads and Meta, and your account manager monitors performance from the first click.

Days 15-30: Leads come in while your account manager optimizes daily, then we review your numbers with you on day 30 to plan month two.

**11. Proof**

We've worked with hundreds of residential HVAC companies. Our average is 44 qualified leads per client in the first 30 days.

**12. Objections**

"I hired an agency before and got garbage leads." Most agencies take the same funnel they built for a dentist and point it at you. Everything we run was built for residential HVAC and tested on other HVAC companies first. You also see your cost per lead every week, so you'll know exactly what you're getting.

"Are you selling these same leads to my competitors?" No. Every lead we generate is yours exclusively.

"I don't have time to manage another vendor." You get one account manager and a weekly report. You don't touch the ad account.

"I don't want to be locked into anything." You aren't. Month to month, no setup fee, cancel whenever.

**13. Price**

$3,000 a month to build, manage, and optimize your campaigns, billed month to month.

Ad spend is separate. You pay Google and Meta directly and set the budget at whatever level you want.

No setup fee, no long-term contract, no cancellation fee.

**14. Next step**

Book a free strategy call. We'll go through your market, your technician capacity, and your current lead flow, then show you exactly what your campaigns would look like before you spend a dollar on ads.

**Example #2:**

**1. ICP**

This is for agency owners doing $500K to $5M a year in the US, Canada, the UK, or Australia.

You need a proven offer, paying clients, and at least one person who can take sales calls. You want a second acquisition channel instead of more referrals. If you're still figuring out what you sell, this isn't for you.

**2. Headline**

Add a second acquisition channel that books 15 qualified calls in 60 days.

**3. Their main problem**

Every new client comes from somewhere you don't control.

Referrals, your network, and old relationships built this agency. The problem is you can't turn any of them on. Some months three deals close on their own. Other months the pipeline is empty, and all you can do is wait and follow up with people who already told you no.

**4. Their current circumstances**

You've probably bought a list, sent cold emails from your main domain, and watched your replies dry up without ever finding out why.

Or you hired an appointment setter who filled your calendar with people who were never going to afford you.

You know outbound works, because your competitors are doing it. You just haven't made it work without it eating the time you owe your clients.

**5. Their desired outcome**

You want a channel you can turn on.

Qualified calls landing on your calendar every week from people who've never heard of you, so growth stops depending on who happens to refer you this month. Revenue predictable enough to hire ahead of the work instead of scrambling after it. And you want to stop being the only reason new business walks in the door.

**6. Why previous solutions failed**

Sending from your main domain burns your deliverability. Your emails quietly stop reaching inboxes and nobody tells you.

Bought lists are full of wrong titles and dead addresses, so you pay to message people who could never buy from you.

Appointment setters get paid per call booked, not per deal closed, so they book anyone who says yes.

Doing it yourself means the list building, the copy, the sending, and the follow-up all land on the person who should be running the agency.

**7. Your mechanism**

We build and run a complete outbound system outside your business.

We send from separate domains we buy and warm for you, so your main domain is never at risk and your deliverability holds no matter how much volume we push.

We build the list ourselves against your exact client profile instead of buying one, so every person we contact could realistically sign with you.

Our team writes, sends, tests, and follows up every day using the same system we already run across dozens of agency accounts. The only thing that reaches you is a booked call on your calendar.

**8. How it works**

We buy and authenticate your sending domains, then warm the inboxes before a single email goes out. While they warm, we build and verify your list and write the first campaigns for you to approve. Sending goes live on day 15. From there our team runs it daily, tests new angles, keeps what gets replies, and cuts what doesn't.

**9. What's included**

30 secondary domains and inboxes, fully authenticated and warmed before a single email goes out

A verified list built to your exact client profile and refreshed every month

All cold email copy written, tested, and rewritten based on what actually gets replies

Daily sending, reply handling, and follow-up managed entirely by our team

Calls booked straight onto your calendar with context on the prospect before you join

Weekly reporting on sends, reply rate, calls booked, and cost per call

**10. Timeline**

Days 1-7: Onboarding call, domain purchase, inbox setup, and full authentication.

Days 8-14: Inboxes warming, list built and verified, first campaigns written and approved by you.

Day 15: Sending goes live and your first replies start coming back.

Days 15-30: We test new angles daily, keep what gets replies, and cut what doesn't.

Days 30-60: Volume scales as deliverability holds, then we review your numbers with you on day 60 to plan the next quarter.

**11. Proof**

We've run this for more than 80 agencies doing $500K to $5M. Across the last 12 months the average is 17 qualified calls booked in the first 60 days.

A creative agency that had never done outbound booked 22 calls in their first 60 days.

A paid ads agency closed $180,000 in new retainers from their first three months of sending.

A web design agency replaced referrals as their largest source of new business inside two quarters.

**12. Objections**

"Cold email is going to wreck my domain." It won't, because we never send from it. Everything goes out on separate domains we buy and warm for this.

"Isn't cold email dead?" Untargeted cold email is. We build the list against your exact client profile instead of buying one, which is the entire difference.

"My clients are too senior to be reached this way." The people you want are reachable. What gets ignored is a generic email sent to 50,000 people at once.

"I don't have time to sit through unqualified calls." You won't. We qualify before booking, and you get context on the prospect before you join the call.

**13. Price**

$4,500 a month to build, run, and optimize your entire outbound channel, billed month to month.

Domains and inboxes run about $300 a month, billed to you directly, so you own the infrastructure even if you stop working with us.

No setup fee, no long-term contract, no cancellation fee.

**14. Next step**

Book a free outbound audit. We'll go through your current client list, your offer, and who you should actually be targeting, then show you the exact campaign we'd run and the numbers we'd expect from it.

You'll see all of it before you commit to anything.

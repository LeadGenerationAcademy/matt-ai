# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #3: Offer
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=46fff78e59354f97868480f35548ef5e

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Offer Audit

- Made a copy of the Offer Audit Template
- Scored all 8 criteria
- Added up your 8 scores for a total out of 40
- Compared your total to the scoring scale
- Fixed every criterion scoring below 4 stars

### Offer Amplification

- Made a copy of the Offer Amplification Template
- Scored all 4 parts of the Value Formula
- Added up your 4 scores for a total out of 20
- Compared your total to the scoring scale
- Improved your lowest score first, then re-scored until every category hit at least 4 stars

### Offer Presentation

- Made a copy of the Offer Presentation Template
- Filled in all 13 content slides
- Used one clear idea per slide, matched proof to your ICP's level, and addressed the major objections
- Ended by clearly asking them to buy and showing the next step

### Offer Sales Letter

- Made a copy of the Offer Sales Letter Template
- Copied the nine Keep The Offer Consistent fields straight from your Sales Presentation
- Wrote every section of the letter, from ICP through next step
- Kept it concise, in your own voice, and understandable from the headlines alone
- Ended with a clear call to action

### Pitch & One-Liner

- Made a copy of the 30-Second Offer Pitch Template
- Filled in the pitch format and added a proof point if you have one
- Timed it and trimmed it to 30 seconds or less, then practiced until it came out automatically
- Made a copy of the Offer One-Liner Template
- Wrote and timed three versions, trimming each to 7 seconds or less
- Tested it on someone unfamiliar with your business and simplified it until they could repeat it back
- Wrote your finished One-Liner into the document

### One-Pager

- Made a copy of the One-Pager Template
- Filled in every section of the One-Pager
- Confirmed it fits in one iPhone screenshot with nothing cut off
- Saved it as a PDF, an image, and a shareable link

### 3-Min Loom + Message

- Made a copy of the 3-Min Loom + Message Template
- Recorded a Loom under three minutes covering who it's for, the problem, the result, how it works, and proof
- Confirmed the Loom link is viewable by anyone
- Filled in the message template and saved both where you and your team can access them

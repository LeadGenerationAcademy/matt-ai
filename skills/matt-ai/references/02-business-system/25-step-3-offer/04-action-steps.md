# Action Steps

Course: Step #3: Offer
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=c662ac3f07c741979beb11560d27606c

---
The Theory explains that a great offer carries most of the weight for your funnel, while a weak one forces your ads, sales calls, and fulfillment to compensate for it.

This course is where you actually build and prove out your offer.

You'll audit your offer, sharpen it using Hormozi's Value Formula, then package it into a presentation, a sales letter, a 30-second pitch, a one-liner, a one-pager, and a Loom video so it's ready the second someone asks what you do.

### Table of Contents

1. **Do Offer Audit:** Score your Offer across 8 criteria to get an objective read on how good it actually is, then add the 8 scores together and compare the total against the scoring scale to see whether your Offer is terrible, weak, acceptable, strong, or exceptional.
2. **Amplify Your Offer:** Score the 4 parts of the Value Formula and improve your lowest score first, repeating the process until every part scores at least 4 stars.
3. **Create Offer Presentation:** Build a presentation that walks through your Offer using the same ICP, problem, dream outcome, deliverables, timeline, price, mechanism, proof, and risk reversal you've already built.
4. **Create Offer Sales Letter:** Write a version of your Offer that explains it clearly, answers the objections that come up, and asks the customer to buy.
5. **Create 30-Second Offer Pitch:** Create a pitch you can say out loud in 30 seconds or less whenever someone asks what you do, one that makes them want to ask you more.
6. **Create Offer One-Liner:** Write one sentence that makes it immediately clear who you help and what result or deliverable you provide, one you can say out loud in 7 seconds or less.
7. **Create One-Pager:** Build a one-page summary of your Offer that anyone unfamiliar with your business can understand within seconds, fitting inside one iPhone screenshot with nothing cut off.
8. **Create 3-Min Loom + Message:** Record a Loom video under three minutes and prepare a copy-paste message ready to send the instant someone asks for more information about your Offer.

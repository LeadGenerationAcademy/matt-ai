# Step #8: Create 3-Min Loom + Message

Course: Step #3: Offer
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=f7792b2c54204b4e9edaafc958d784c4

---
Create a short Loom video and copy-and-paste message to send whenever someone asks for more information about your Offer. This is especially useful for referrals, people you meet in real life, and works well in the normal cold-traffic sales funnel for those who apply.

This is not a complete sales presentation, it's you talking through your One-Pager and going over some proof. Its purpose is to quickly explain the Offer and move the prospect to the next step.

### Framework & Criteria

1. Who the Offer is for
2. The main problem it solves
3. The result or deliverable
4. How the Offer works
5. What they receive
6. How quickly they receive value
7. Relevant proof
8. The next step

**Use This Template**

"Hey [Name], here is a three-minute video explaining exactly what we do, who it is for, what you receive, and how the process works: [Loom Link]
If it looks relevant, the next step is [book a call, reply to this message, complete the application, or purchase here]: [Next-Step Link]"

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [3-Min Loom + Message Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10trg6w97DUTYKPTB1YtRkgCMXHD8cHTDglJ4sZmKP5o/edit?tab=t.ml9mpx55gdvt).** If you've already made a copy for an earlier step, just use that same copy instead of making a new one, this is the same tab-based document. [in this skill: assets/templates/offer-audit-template/]
2. **Share your screen and walk through your One-Pager or a shortened version of your Offer Presentation, keeping yourself visible in a small circle and the video under three minutes.** This is easier than building something new from scratch for this video. Being visible on screen is what makes it feel personal instead of like a generic recorded demo. This isn't a complete sales presentation, it's meant to be quick.
3. **Explain who the Offer is for.** Use the same ICP you created during the previous Steps. Name the specific characteristics of your ICP, so whoever is watching can qualify or unqualify themselves.
4. **Explain the main problem it solves.** Keep this consistent with the problem you've already established elsewhere in your Offer. Describe it as the main problem your ICP is facing, and use their language and experiences.
5. **Explain the result or deliverable.** Use the same Dream Outcome your Offer is already built around. Ideally, you're selling a result. If you do not control the final result, explain the clear deliverable that is directly connected to it.
6. **Explain how the Offer works.** Keep this consistent with the mechanism you've already established, don't create a different Offer inside the Loom. Your mechanism is simply the believable explanation for why your Offer should produce the result. It does not need to be fancy. Whoever is watching should hear it and think "That makes sense."
7. **Explain what they receive.** This should list the same deliverables your Offer already promises. Tell them what they actually receive. If you only describe the activities you do, they still won't know what they're getting.
8. **Explain how quickly they receive value.** Use the same timeline you've already committed to elsewhere in your Offer. Focus on when they get their first meaningful result or benefit, measured from when they buy. It doesn't have to be the complete result, just something they can use or benefit from while you work toward it.
9. **Show relevant proof.** Use the same proof you created during the previous Steps. A running list of your past deals, each with its actual financials and a short video breakdown, is a strong version of this if you have the material for it.
10. **Explain the next step.** Make it a single, specific action, not a vague suggestion.
11. **Upload the Loom and confirm that anyone with the link can watch it.** Don't spend hours editing the video, this is meant to be quick and simple. A broken or restricted link defeats the whole purpose of having this ready to send.
12. **Fill in the message template in the "Write Your 3-Min Loom + Message" section of the document, then save the Loom link and message somewhere you and your team can access immediately.** Don't send the Loom link without context, always pair it with this message. Save both in your own Apple Notes if you can, so you have them on the go. The next time someone asks for more information, you should be able to send both within seconds.
13. **Send the Loom and message on your call confirmation page and from whoever booked the call.** This gets it in front of the lead before the call itself, not just whenever someone happens to ask for more information.

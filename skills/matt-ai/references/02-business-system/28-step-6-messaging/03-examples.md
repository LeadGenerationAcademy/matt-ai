A Roofing Lead Generation Agency wanted to attract state-level and multi-state roofing companies with established sales teams.

However, its messaging was extremely generic:

**“We help roofing companies get more leads.”**

This attracted the correct Industry but the wrong level of ICP.

Most of its calls came from broke owner-operator roofers with no employees, no sales team, and almost no money available for marketing.

### Step #1) Audit Past 100+ Sales Calls

The agency reviewed its last 100 sales calls.

It found:

- 24% were financially qualified.
- 71% had decision-making power.
- 11% purchased within seven days.
- 14% matched the intended ICP.

The decision-making percentage was misleading. Most prospects had decision-making power because they were the only person in the company.

The agency attracted roofers, but not the level of roofing company it wanted.

**Messaging Score: 1**

### Step #2) Collect Messaging Research

The owner-operators described problems such as:

- “I just need a few more leads.”
- “I cannot afford that much per month.”
- “Can I pay you after I close a job?”

These were legitimate problems, but they came from the wrong level of ICP.

State-level and multi-state roofing companies described different problems:

- “Our salespeople do not have enough qualified appointments.”
- “Some branches have full calendars while others are empty.”
- “We need consistent appointments across every market.”
- “We cannot clearly connect our marketing spend to booked jobs.”

The agency separated the two groups and only used research from its intended ICP.

### Step #3) Create the Initial Top 25 Document

The agency grouped the problems mentioned by qualified state-level and multi-state roofing companies.

The most common problem was:

**The company could not consistently fill its sales team’s calendar with qualified appointments across every branch and market.**

The agency used its sales calls to create as much of the Top 25 Document as possible.

### Step #4) Complete the Top 25 Document

The sales calls did not produce all 25 problems, so the agency completed the remaining research through:

- Interviews with larger roofing companies
- Customer surveys
- Competitor reviews
- Roofing business groups
- Reddit, Quora, and YouTube comments

The agency only used research from established roofing companies with sales teams.

It did not fill the Document with problems experienced by owner-operators simply because they operated in the same Industry.

### Step #5) Create the Direct Offer Messaging

The most popular problem automatically became the Direct Offer Messaging.

The agency changed its messaging from:

**“We help roofing companies get more leads.”**

To:

**“We help state-level and multi-state roofing companies fill their sales teams’ calendars with exclusive, qualified roof-replacement appointments.”**

The shorter headline became:

**Fill Your Sales Team’s Calendar**

This immediately communicated:

- The offer was for established roofing companies.
- The company needed to have a sales team.
- The problem was an empty sales calendar.
- The outcome was qualified roofing appointments.

Broke owner-operators would recognize that the offer was not designed for them.

Established roofing companies with sales teams would recognize themselves immediately.

### What Happens Next

The agency would build its initial funnel around:

**Fill Your Sales Team’s Calendar**

This Direct Offer Messaging would control its initial paid ads, outreach, headline, sales page, VSL, application, and follow-up.

The Industry remained the same.

The level of ICP and the messaging changed completely.

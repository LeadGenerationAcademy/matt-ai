You have already identified the 2-5 characteristics shared by at least 80% of your customers.

Now determine whether your current Messaging resonates with those people.

Whether you realize it or not, you already have Messaging: something in your paid ads, content, outreach, or funnel caused people to book a sales call, and the quality of those people tells you how well that Messaging is working.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Record Your Calls For every call, record whether the prospect:**
  1. Matched all 2-5 ICP characteristics
  2. Was financially qualified
  3. Had decision-making power
  4. Purchased within seven days
2. **Score Your Messaging Give yourself one point for every statement that is true:**
  1. At least 80% matched the majority of your ICP characteristics (will depend on how many you have)
  2. At least 80% were financially qualified
  3. At least 80% had decision-making power
  4. At least 50% purchased within seven days of the calls
3. **Scoring Scale**
  1. Score of 1: You passed zero or one test. Your Messaging is consistently attracting or resonating with the wrong people.
  2. Score of 2: You passed two or three tests. Your Messaging is partially working, but at least one major part needs improvement.
  3. Score of 3: You passed all four tests. Your Messaging consistently attracts qualified decision-makers matching your ICP and causes enough of them to buy quickly.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Messaging Audit Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pvUnPwUhN16OCTgx1pr7Pa0b9WRpulS6tHW_jt_xX0s/edit?gid=874568521#gid=874568521).** This is where you'll log your recent sales calls and score them against four tests. Your score tells you whether your current Messaging is attracting people who match your ICP, can afford you, can make the decision, and buy quickly. [in this skill: assets/templates/messaging-audit-template/]
2. **Pull your last 100 sales calls that came from paid ads or outreach.** These calls show you who your current Messaging is actually attracting. If you have fewer than 100, use every call you have. Work with whatever real data exists instead of waiting for a full 100.
3. **If you have no sales calls, skip this Audit and continue to the Messaging Research steps.** There's nothing to audit yet if no calls have happened.
4. **Log one row per call and paste the full call transcript, or a link to it, into the Call Transcript column.** The transcripts are what the built-in prompt reads to fill in the next columns for you.
5. **Record whether each prospect matched all of your ICP characteristics.** Paste your 2-5 ICP characteristics into the built-in prompt once, then use that same prompt with ChatGPT or Claude to fill in this column for every transcript.
6. **Record whether each prospect was financially qualified and had decision-making power.** The same built-in prompt fills in both columns from your call transcripts. Financially qualified means they had the budget for your Offer. Decision-making power means they had the authority to buy it.
7. **Record whether each prospect purchased within seven days.** This column must be filled out yourself after the call, once you know the result.
8. **Check your auto-calculated score against the scoring scale.** This tells you how many of the four tests your Messaging actually passed. A score of 1 means your Messaging is attracting or resonating with the wrong people. A score of 2 means it's partially working, but at least one major part needs improvement. A score of 3 means it consistently attracts qualified decision-makers who match your ICP and buy quickly.
9. **Continue to the Messaging Research steps regardless of your score.** Even a strong score doesn't mean you skip the research. The research is where you collect the exact language your ICP uses, so your Messaging can mirror how they actually talk.

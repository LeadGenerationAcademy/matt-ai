### Framework & Criteria

1. **Choose the Primary Problem**
  1. Review your completed Top 25 Problems, Circumstances, and Outcomes Document
  2. Select the problem appearing most frequently
  3. Confirm it came from the specific level of ICP you intend to target
  4. Confirm it connects directly to the outcome your Offer produces
  5. Confirm your Offer can actually solve it
2. **Use Their Exact Language**
  1. Who they are
  2. Their current circumstances
  3. Their main problem
  4. Their desired outcome
  5. Why they want that outcome
  6. What they have already tried
  7. Why those previous solutions failed
  8. How your Offer solves the problem

**Use This Template**

"We help [specific level of ICP] solve [main problem] and achieve [desired outcome] through [Offer or mechanism]."

If you have a guarantee, add: "If [desired outcome] does not happen, [what they get instead]."

### Example

"We help agency owners stop relying on referrals and build predictable lead flow through our done for you ads and funnel system. If we don't generate 20 qualified leads in 30 days, you get your money back.”

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Direct Offer Messaging Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1s4COJJQxCssA8PMcHL5jKmwJAthZdHQFA2vXywMAVws/edit).** This is where you'll turn the problem that showed up most often in your research into the Messaging that runs through your whole funnel. It's a different tab in the same document you made a copy of in the previous step. [in this skill: assets/templates/top-25-pco-template/]
2. **Review your completed [Top 25 PCO Document](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=4e8e17f34e47499fa2e6477a5b8369ab) and select the problem appearing most frequently.** This is the same Document you built in the previous step, sorted from most to least frequently mentioned. Don't skip over the top problem because another one sounds more interesting or is easier to write about.
3. **Confirm the problem came from the specific level of ICP you intend to target, connects directly to the outcome your Offer produces, and is something your Offer can actually solve.** Mixing in the wrong level of ICP will point your Messaging at the wrong audience. If the connection to your Offer's outcome isn't direct, this Messaging won't actually lead anywhere useful. If your Offer does not solve the most common problem, you may have an Offer problem, not a Messaging problem.
4. **Write down who they are, using their exact language.** This should read like your ICP describing themselves, not you describing them.
5. **Write down their current circumstances, using their exact language.** This is the situation they currently find themselves in, usually a negative one, before finding a solution.
6. **Write down their main problem, using their exact language.** This is the problem you just selected from your Top 25 PCO Document.
7. **Write down their desired outcome, using their exact language.** This is what they actually want to happen.
8. **Write down why they want that outcome, using their exact language.** This is the deeper motivation behind the outcome itself.
9. **Write down what they have already tried and why those previous solutions failed, using their exact language.** This shows you what solutions already failed to win their trust. Why they failed is often the strongest Messaging material, since it tells you what to avoid repeating.
10. **Write down how your Offer solves the problem, using their exact language.** This connects your mechanism directly to the language they already use.
11. **Write your Direct Offer Messaging using the template, and add the guarantee line if you have one.** Use "We help [specific level of ICP] solve [main problem] and achieve [desired outcome] through [Offer or mechanism]" as your starting point. Only add the guarantee line if you have a real guarantee, and never guarantee a result you do not fully control.
12. **Rewrite it using the exact language collected during your research.** The template is only a starting structure, so don't stop there. Someone from your specific level of ICP should immediately think: this is for me, this describes my current situation, this solves a problem I actually have, and this offers an outcome I actually want.

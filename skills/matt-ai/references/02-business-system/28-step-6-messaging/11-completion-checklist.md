# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #6: Messaging
System: Business System

Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Messaging Audit

- Made a copy of the Messaging Audit Template
- If you had sales calls, scored each one against the four tests: right ICP level, financially qualified, spoke to the decision-maker, and purchased within 7 days
- Checked the auto-calculated score for each call against the scoring scale
- If you had no sales calls yet, skipped ahead to Messaging Research

### Messaging Research From Calls

- Made a copy of the Sales Call Messaging Research Template
- If you had sales calls, exported up to 100 recent calls and removed any from the wrong ICP level
- Logged each remaining call's main problem and current circumstances, in their exact language
- Logged each call's desired outcome and reason for buying now, in their exact language
- Logged what they'd already tried and why it failed, in their exact language
- Logged their objections, in their exact language
- If you had no sales calls, skipped ahead to Messaging Research Via Internet

### Messaging Research Via Internet

- Made a copy of the Messaging Research Via Internet Template
- Found 1,000+ reviews matching your ICP level across Trustpilot, Google Reviews, G2, Capterra, and other relevant sites, including both positive and negative
- Logged each review's company and source site, along with its star rating
- Logged each review's problem, circumstances, and desired outcome, in the reviewer's exact language
- Logged each review's likes and dislikes, in the reviewer's exact language
- Logged the reason it failed and their objections, in the reviewer's exact language
- Left fields blank rather than guessing when information wasn't in the review

### Top 25 PCO Document

- Made a copy of the Top 25 PCO Template
- Checked whether your research already contained at least 25 Problems, Circumstances, and Outcomes each
- If not, gathered additional research from Reddit, Quora, YouTube comments, existing customers, or industry communities, staying within your correct ICP level
- Recorded every entry in the exact language of the source, without letting ChatGPT invent or add content
- Sorted the Problems, Circumstances, and Outcomes by frequency

### Direct Offer Messaging

- Made a copy of the Direct Offer Messaging Template
- Reviewed your completed Top 25 PCO Document and selected the most frequent problem
- Confirmed the problem matched your ICP level and that your Offer actually solves it
- Wrote out who the customer is and their circumstances, in their exact language
- Wrote out their problem and desired outcome, in their exact language
- Wrote out why they want that outcome, in their exact language
- Wrote out what they'd already tried and why it failed, in their exact language
- Wrote out how your Offer solves the problem, in their exact language
- Drafted your final messaging using the "We help [ICP] solve [main problem] and achieve [desired outcome] through [Offer or mechanism]" template
- If applicable, added a guarantee line
- Rewrote the final messaging fully in your ICP's exact language

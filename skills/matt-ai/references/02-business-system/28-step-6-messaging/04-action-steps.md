The Theory explains that messaging is what determines who your marketing actually attracts, and that the exact language your prospects and customers already use, pulled from real sales calls and real reviews, is what makes an offer statement land instead of falling flat like a guess.

This section is where you actually build yours.

You will audit your current messaging against four tests, pull the exact language from at least 100 sales calls and at least 1,000 public reviews, compile the most frequent problems and outcomes into one ranked document, then turn the single most common problem into one direct offer statement.

None of this works if you write the offer statement from your own head instead of pulling it from what your prospects and customers are actually saying.

### Table of Contents

1. **Do A Messaging Audit:** Score your Messaging by reviewing your last 100 sales calls against four tests, to see whether it's attracting the right people and getting them to buy.
2. **Messaging Research From Calls:** Record the main problem, current circumstances, desired outcome, timing, previous attempts, and objections from at least 100 recent sales calls, using your prospects' own exact language.
3. **Messaging Research Via Internet:** Collect the exact language your ICP uses to describe what they like, dislike, want, and struggle with from at least 1,000 online reviews.
4. **Create Top 25 PCO Document:** Research and list 25 problems, circumstances, and desired outcomes experienced by your specific level of ICP, sorted from most to least frequently mentioned.
5. **Create Direct Offer Messaging:** Use the most frequently mentioned problem from your research to write the primary Direct Offer Messaging that runs through your whole funnel.

This step gathers the exact language your ICP uses on real sales calls, so your Messaging can mirror how they actually talk instead of your own paraphrasing.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Export Your Past 100 Calls**
  1. Export the recordings, transcripts, or notes from at least 100 recent sales calls
  2. Remove calls from people who clearly do not match the level of ICP you are trying to attract
  3. If you do not have any sales calls, skip Step #2A and continue to Step #2B
2. **Record the Research**
  1. Their main problem
  2. Their current circumstances
  3. Their desired outcome
  4. Why they wanted to solve the problem now
  5. What they had already tried
  6. Why those previous solutions failed
  7. Their main objections
  8. Use their exact language
  9. Do not summarize what they said using your own terminology

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Messaging Research From Calls Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pvUnPwUhN16OCTgx1pr7Pa0b9WRpulS6tHW_jt_xX0s/edit?gid=2135564468#gid=2135564468).** This is where you'll log your recent sales calls and record what each prospect said about their problem, circumstances, desired outcome, past attempts and objections, in their exact words. It's a different tab in the same spreadsheet you made a copy of for the Messaging Audit. [in this skill: assets/templates/messaging-audit-template/]
2. **Finish the [ICP Exercise](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/3dc179b5?md=0a7aea85b2ec48439ec4c34255e611d0&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) and the [Messaging Audit](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=171a5f3512d64b6fa5bf60bb4dab5fe6&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) first, if you haven't already.** You need to know whose language you're looking for before this research will be useful. Both are required before this research step, so don't skip either one.
3. **Export the recordings, transcripts, or notes from your last 100 sales calls, or all of them if you have fewer than 100.** Work with whatever real calls exist rather than waiting to hit 100.
4. **If you have no sales calls, skip to [Step #2B: Messaging Research Via Internet](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=913451d7c5ce45c9ae9cd0639cda8481).** There's nothing to research yet if no calls have happened.
5. **Remove calls from people who clearly do not match the level of ICP you are trying to attract.** Different levels of customers inside the same industry have completely different problems. Mixing in the wrong level of ICP pollutes your research with language that doesn't apply to who you're actually targeting.
6. **Log one row per remaining call and paste the full raw call transcript, or a link to it, into the Call Transcript column.** Paste the raw transcript, not a summary. Prospect Name, Email, and Phone Number are optional if you'd rather keep the transcripts anonymous.
7. **Record their main problem.** This is something going wrong that they want fixed. Use the built-in prompt with ChatGPT or Claude to auto-fill this column, and the research columns after it, from your call transcripts.
8. **Record their current circumstances.** This is the situation they currently find themselves in, usually a negative one. Leave this field blank if the call transcript doesn't cover it, rather than guessing.
9. **Record their desired outcome.** This is something they want to achieve. It's what they actually want, in their own words.
10. **Record why they wanted to solve the problem now.** This tells you what triggers someone to actually take action, not just think about it.
11. **Record what they had already tried and why those previous solutions failed.** This shows you what solutions already failed to win their trust. Why they failed is often the strongest messaging material, since it tells you what to avoid repeating.
12. **Record their main objections.** These are the same objections your Offer Presentation and Sales Letter need to address.
13. **Use their exact language, and do not summarize using your own terminology.** We want the exact way they describe it, because that's what makes Messaging resonate in ads and content. Look for what keeps repeating across the calls. One mention could be a fluke, but repeated mentions are what your Messaging should reflect.

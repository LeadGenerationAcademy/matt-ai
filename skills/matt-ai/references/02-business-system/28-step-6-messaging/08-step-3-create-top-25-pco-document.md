# Step #3: Create Top 25 PCO Document

Course: Step #6: Messaging
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=4e8e17f34e47499fa2e6477a5b8369ab

---
Research and list 25 problems, circumstances, and desired outcomes experienced by your specific level of ICP.

Problem: the real issue, the actual block that is stopping them.

Circumstance: how your prospect sees their world right now.

Outcome: what they actually want.

It's normal for many of these to sound very similar to one another.

### Examples

**Problems**

1. Client acquisition rests on a single uncontrollable channel
2. They cannot forecast revenue with any confidence
3. They are the constraint on their own growth

**Circumstances**

1. Most of my clients still come from referrals
2. My revenue swings up and down every month
3. I am wearing every hat in this business

**Outcomes**

1. Get off referral dependency for good
2. Escape the feast-or-famine cycle
3. Stop being the bottleneck in my business

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Top 25 PCO Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1s4COJJQxCssA8PMcHL5jKmwJAthZdHQFA2vXywMAVws/edit?tab=t.w3ckyts5xisl).** This is where you'll list 25 problems, 25 circumstances and 25 desired outcomes your ICP experiences, sorted by how often each one comes up. [in this skill: assets/templates/top-25-pco-template/]
2. **Complete [Step #2A: Messaging Research From Calls](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=72137c7f43c24d3bb594a6bde9ddb80b&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) and [Step #2B: Messaging Research Via Internet](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=913451d7c5ce45c9ae9cd0639cda8481&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) first, if you haven't already.** Your calls and your reviews are the two main sources this Document draws from.
3. **Check whether your research already produced 25 legitimate Problems, Circumstances and Outcomes.** If all three already have 25, skip additional research and finalize the Document. There's no need to keep digging once you've hit the target.
4. **If any of them has fewer than 25, continue researching.** Only the categories still short of 25 need more work.
5. **Search Reddit, Quora, the comments under relevant YouTube videos, and industry-specific communities.** Look for people matching your specific level of ICP openly discussing their problems in their own words. Comment sections often surface complaints and frustrations people wouldn't put in a formal review. Forums, Slack groups, and Facebook groups built around your specific industry are often full of this language too.
6. **Ask your existing customers.** They can tell you directly what almost stopped them from buying or what they were dealing with beforehand.
7. **Use ChatGPT to help locate sources and organize your research, but do not let it invent problems, circumstances, outcomes, or customer quotes.** It's a research assistant here, not a source of the actual content. Only use it to locate, group, count, and organize real research. It cannot replace the actual research itself.
8. **Only include research from people or companies closely matching the level of ICP you intend to target.** An owner-operator, small business, mid-sized company, and Fortune 5000 company can experience completely different problems despite operating within the same Industry.
9. **Look for repeated Problems.** A problem is the real issue, the actual block that is stopping them. The problem your ICP states out loud is often not their real problem, so dig a level or two deeper to find what's actually stopping them. A pattern that shows up once might be a fluke, but one that shows up repeatedly is real signal.
10. **Look for repeated Circumstances.** A circumstance is how your prospect sees their world right now. It's normal for many of these to sound very similar to one another.
11. **Look for repeated Desired Outcomes.** An outcome is what they actually want. These patterns are what eventually become your Messaging.
12. **Record the exact language your ICP uses.** Don't paraphrase it into your own words, that defeats the purpose of the research.
13. **Add each legitimate recurring pattern to the Document until it contains 25 legitimate entries in each category.** Do not cherry-pick a short list later. The goal of collecting all 25 is that you never know which one will be the entry that resonates most. Don't stop early just because you've found a few strong ones.
14. **Sort them from most frequently mentioned to least frequently mentioned.** This ordering is what tells you which problem becomes your Direct Offer Messaging in the next step.

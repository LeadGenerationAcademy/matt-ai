### Framework & Criteria

1. **Complete the ICP Exercise First:** Only collect reviews from companies matching your Industry and level of ICP.
2. **Find at Least 1,000 Reviews:** Search Trustpilot, Google Reviews, G2, Capterra, or other relevant sites, both positive and negative.
3. **Load Everything Into a Spreadsheet:** Record Company, Review website, Star rating, and the rest of the fields per review.
4. **Preserve Their Exact Language:** Copy their exact words and phrases, don't rewrite in your own terminology.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Messaging Research Via Internet Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pvUnPwUhN16OCTgx1pr7Pa0b9WRpulS6tHW_jt_xX0s/edit?gid=998399835#gid=998399835).** This is where you'll collect at least 1,000 reviews from companies like yours and record what each reviewer says about their problem, circumstances, desired outcome, likes, dislikes and objections, in their exact words. It's a different tab in the same spreadsheet you made a copy of for the Messaging Audit. [in this skill: assets/templates/messaging-audit-template/]
2. **Complete the [ICP Exercise](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/3dc179b5?md=4ca62a4e667f48a6bdd28a45824bafd6) first, if you haven't already.** Only collect reviews from companies serving the same Industry and level of ICP you're targeting.
3. **Find companies selling products or services similar to yours.** If you target large companies, don't base your Messaging on reviews written by beginners or owner-operators.
4. **Search Trustpilot, Google Reviews, G2, Capterra, and any other review site your ICP actually uses.** G2 and Capterra are especially useful if you're in a software or B2B space. Not every industry lives on the same four sites, so look wherever your specific ICP actually leaves reviews.
5. **Collect at least 1,000 reviews, both positive and negative.** Positive reviews reveal what customers value. Negative reviews reveal their problems, frustrations, disappointments, and objections. This volume is what lets real patterns emerge instead of one-off opinions.
6. **Paste each reviews page into AI alongside the prompt found in the spreadsheet, so every review lands in its own row.** You can paste an entire page with many reviews at once, and each review becomes its own row. That means one row per individual review, not one row per company.
7. **Record the Company, Review website, Star rating, whether it's positive or negative, and the Full review.** The AI pulls the Company, Review website and Star rating straight from the page. Positive or negative tells you which bucket the review's language belongs in. Keep the entire review text, not a trimmed version.
8. **Record the Main problem.** This is something going wrong that they want fixed, and what drove them to look for a solution in the first place.
9. **Record the Current circumstances.** This is the situation they were in before finding a solution, usually a negative one.
10. **Record the Desired outcome.** This is something they want to achieve. It's what they actually wanted to happen.
11. **Record what they liked and what they disliked.** What they liked tells you what to keep emphasizing in your own Messaging. What they disliked tells you what to avoid or address head-on in your own Offer.
12. **Record why previous solutions failed.** Not every review will contain every answer, so record whatever information is actually available.
13. **Record their Main objections.** These are objections your Offer Presentation and Sales Letter should already be answering.
14. **Leave a field blank if a review doesn't cover it, rather than guessing.** Don't invent an answer just to fill every cell.
15. **Copy the exact words and phrases they use, and do not rewrite using your own terminology.** The exact way they describe it is what makes your Messaging resonate with the people you're trying to attract.

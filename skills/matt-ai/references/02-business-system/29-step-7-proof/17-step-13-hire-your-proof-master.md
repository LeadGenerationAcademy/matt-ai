Course: Step #7: Proof
System: Business System

Hire a part-time $5 per hour VA whose entire job is to screenshot wins, collect proof, and post it in the appropriate place.

![image](https://assets.skool.com/f/1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce/73a7004d708b4fafb07e02b0fff983ca3e875f1072c4437a9ba0ab169174f31d.png)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write a simple job description for the Proof Master role.** This gives you a clear, hireable role instead of a vague task nobody owns.
2. **Hire a part-time VA at approximately $5 per hour.** This role does not require a large budget or a full-time hire to be effective.
3. **Show them where to find wins and proof.** This is what actually lets them do the job without needing you to hand them material every time.
4. **Show them where each type of proof should be posted.** This keeps the proof landing in the same places you've already built out for it across your personal and company profiles and website.
5. **Have them screenshot wins and collect proof.** This is the core, ongoing task the role exists to handle.
6. **Have them post the proof in the appropriate place.** This is what actually turns collected proof into visible content people can see.
7. **Review their work regularly.** This keeps the quality and placement of the proof consistent over time.

### Completion Audit

- Did you write a simple job description for the Proof Master role?
- Did you hire a part-time VA at approximately $5 per hour?
- Did you show them where to find wins and proof?
- Did you show them where each type of proof should be posted?
- Are they screenshotting wins and collecting proof?
- Are they posting the proof in the appropriate place?
- Are you reviewing their work regularly?

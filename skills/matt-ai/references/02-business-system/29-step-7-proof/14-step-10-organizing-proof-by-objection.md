Again, you should know that there are usually 6 to 12 main objections, some more important than others, that stop people from buying. You should know what those are, especially if you have any form of access to AI, access to the internet, or call recordings.

List out all of these things, especially on your proof page in Step #11, in the proof document in Step #12, and on your main website or funnel.

Put the objection headline, something like "Here's what people who had this experience first," and put all of that proof below it. It will smash the objection more than any sales training or sales tactic ever could.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Identify the 6 to 12 main objections that stop people from buying, using AI research, internet research, or your own call recordings.** Some objections matter far more than others, so know which ones are actually costing you sales. You don't have to guess at this. You already recorded their main objections in your Messaging research, from your [calls](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=72137c7f43c24d3bb594a6bde9ddb80b) and your [reviews](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=913451d7c5ce45c9ae9cd0639cda8481), so start there.
2. **Rank the objections from most important to least important.** The objection killing the most sales should get addressed first, not buried underneath smaller ones.
3. **Write an objection headline for each one.** Something like "Here's what people who had this experience first" tells the reader exactly what problem the proof below it is about to answer.
4. **Group your existing proof under the objection each piece answers, with the strongest piece first in each group.** Logos, testimonials, screenshots, case studies, and everything else you've built should already have a home once you know which objection they speak to. For example, a piece of proof might show the service works, justify the price, reduce perceived risk, or show how fast results come.
5. **Organize the proof on your main website or funnel this way.** Wherever proof shows up, it should be doing this same job instead of sitting there unorganized.
6. **Use this same structure when you build your [Proof Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=c6f0daaab4e04f5886a72dbefa4f23cc) and [Proof Document](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=bd6f60f34d8e4a2d88533642f3806ff7) in the next two steps.** This turns a page of scattered proof into something that actively argues against the specific reasons people hesitate. The document should carry the same structure so it works just as hard as the page does.

### Completion Audit

- Did you identify the 6 to 12 main objections that stop people from buying, using AI research, internet research, or your own call recordings?
- Did you rank the objections from most important to least important?
- Does each objection have its own headline?
- Is your existing proof grouped under the objection each piece answers, with the strongest piece first in each group?
- Is the proof on your main website or funnel organized this way?
- Did you use this same structure when you built your Proof Page and Proof Document?

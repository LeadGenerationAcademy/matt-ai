Third-party review sites are scary to a lot of people because they can't control them. If someone has a bad experience and leaves a review on Clutch.co or Trustpilot, they can put it up, and you can't do anything about it.

To me, third-party reviews are about five times more powerful than reviews on your own website because of that. I would sign up for Clutch.co, GoodFirms, Google Business, Trustpilot, and anything related to your niche, and try to get 5 to 10 reviews on each, not all concentrated on one.

This will look extremely powerful. These sites usually rank in search results, and they'll bring your company to the top of the search results if you don't have it there already. It looks really strong when someone's doing their due diligence.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create business accounts on [Trustpilot](https://signup.business.trustpilot.com/create-account), [Clutch.co](http://Clutch.co), [GoodFirms](https://myaccount.goodfirms.co/users/register) and [Google Business](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/).** Trustpilot is one of the most recognized third-party review sites, and it carries weight with almost any audience. [Clutch.co](http://Clutch.co) carries real weight, especially for agencies and B2B services. GoodFirms costs you nothing but time to set up. Google Business also directly affects your visibility in local and general search results.
2. **Create a business account on any other review site related to your niche.** Some industries have their own trusted review sites beyond the general ones, so cover those too if they exist for you.
3. **Reach out to real clients and ask them for a review.** This isn't something people do on their own most of the time, you have to actually ask. Third-party reviews are about five times more powerful than reviews on your own website, because you can't control them.
4. **Get 5 to 10 reviews on each site, spread across all of them instead of concentrated on one.** This is enough to look established without needing hundreds on any single platform. A handful of sites each showing real activity looks far stronger than one site loaded up while the rest sit empty. These sites usually rank in search results, so they also help push your company to the top when someone searches for you.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create business accounts on Trustpilot, [Clutch.co](http://Clutch.co), GoodFirms and Google Business?
- Did you create a business account on any other review site related to your niche?
- Did you reach out to real clients and ask them for a review?
- Do you have 5 to 10 reviews on each site, spread across all of them instead of concentrated on one?

# Action Steps

Course: Step #7: Proof
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=dbe6bb5bf1f946a589d19eac9b0ea078

---
Proof is what turns claims about your business into things a prospect can actually verify. Logos, testimonials, screenshots, case studies, your own work, and third parties talking about you all do the same job from different angles, proving the result is real and repeatable, not a fluke.

This course is where you actually build all of it.

You will collect and format ten different types of proof, from your fastest credibility (logos) through your slowest to build (PR and speaking).

Then you will organize all of it by the objection each piece answers, and publish it on a dedicated Proof Page and Proof Document.

Finally, you will hand the ongoing collection off to a dedicated hire and track how much new proof you add every day so it never stops growing.

Do not fake any of this. A fake logo, an anonymous testimonial, a retyped screenshot, or an invented case study destroys the exact credibility this module exists to build, and once a prospect catches one piece of fake proof, they stop trusting all of it.

### Table of Contents

1. **Logos:** Collect at least 10 real customer logos, rank them by importance, and place them in a clean section directly below the fold.
2. **Video Testimonials:** Collect at least five video testimonials, lead each with its strongest result, and make sure the thumbnail proves the point before anyone presses play.
3. **Written Testimonials:** Collect at least 10 written testimonials, attach real identifying details to each one, and group them by the objection they overcome.
4. **Screenshots:** Collect at least 25 real screenshots of client wins, keep them in their native format, and group them by the objection or proof type they answer.
5. **Case Studies:** Turn real client results into in-depth case studies covering the result, timeline, and process, and publish them everywhere on your site.
6. **Proof Of Work:** Build an organized portfolio of your actual work with a named client, business, and type attached to each piece.
7. **Speaking At Events:** Seek out speaking opportunities related to your service and capture photo and video proof that you spoke there.
8. **PR Articles:** Get featured in relevant PR outlets and add a working "As Featured In" section linking to the real articles.
9. **3rd-Party Review Sites:** Set up accounts on third-party review sites and get 5 to 10 real reviews spread across each one.
10. **Organizing Proof By Objection:** Identify your top 6 to 12 objections and reorganize every piece of proof you've built around the objection it answers.
11. **Create Proof Page:** Build one page on your website holding every form of proof, ready to send in one second.
12. **Create Proof Document:** Build a document or slides version of your Proof Page that can be sent directly and attached as a PDF.
13. **Hire Your "Proof Master":** Hire a part-time VA whose only job is to keep collecting and posting new proof.
14. **Add To Inputs Spreadsheet:** Log how much proof you add across all nine proof types every day in your Input Spreadsheet.

You want to have all of this on one page, with links to it, and you can send it to somebody by having [yourdomainname.com/proof](http://yourdomainname.com/proof). Have it ready to send to people in one second.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a page on your website at [yourdomainname.com/proof](http://yourdomainname.com/proof).** This gives you one dedicated place instead of proof scattered across different pages of your site.
2. **Put your proof logos at the top of the page.** This is the first thing someone sees, so it should establish credibility immediately. Put the most impressive and relevant ones first.
3. **Add your proof of work.** This lets people see the actual work you've done rather than only reading claims about it. Name the client for each piece, and show a before-versus-after wherever you can.
4. **Add your video testimonials.** These carry more weight than text alone since someone can see and hear a real person saying it. Most people won't press play, so put the strongest result in the thumbnail.
5. **Add your written testimonials.** These add volume and depth on top of your video testimonials. Lead each one with its strongest result, and add the customer's name, picture and company.
6. **Add your case studies.** This gives anyone who wants more detail a place to go deeper on the actual results. Link to the PDF version so they can download it.
7. **Add your screenshots and photos of you speaking at events.** This stacks on more proof and authority in a format nothing else on the page provides. Keep screenshots in their original format with the result highlighted.
8. **Add your PR articles from different websites.** This shows third parties have already covered you, not just your own clients. Link each one to the actual article.
9. **Add your reviews from third-party sites.** This is proof you don't control, which makes it carry even more weight. It looks really strong when someone's doing their due diligence.
10. **Link out to each piece of proof directly from the page.** Someone should be able to click through and verify anything on this page for themselves.
11. **Keep this page ready to send to people in one second.** If someone asks for proof, you should never be scrambling to pull it together.

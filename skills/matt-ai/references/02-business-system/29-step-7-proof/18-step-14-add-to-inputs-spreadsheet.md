### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Input Tracking Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eT95b4OnSVAOaqrRJ5zKZrcBX0ul6EewACqs38u_Z20/edit?usp=sharing).** This is the same spreadsheet you'll use for the rest of your input tracking later on, so keep it somewhere you'll find it again. We go much deeper into input tracking in the Metrics Tracking course. [in this skill: assets/templates/input-tracking-template/]
2. **Open the Proof tab.** This is where your day-by-day proof tracking lives.
3. **Fill in how many Logos you added that day.** This keeps a running count instead of relying on memory later.
4. **Fill in how many Video Testimonials you added that day.** Every one you collect should get logged the same day it happens.
5. **Fill in how many Written Testimonials you added that day.** This keeps this proof type just as visible as the others in your daily count.
6. **Fill in how many Screenshots you added that day.** These add up fast, so tracking them daily keeps the number accurate.
7. **Fill in how many Case Studies you added that day.** These take longer to produce, so even one in a day is worth logging.
8. **Fill in how many Proof Of Work pieces you added that day.** This keeps your portfolio growth visible alongside everything else.
9. **Fill in how many Speaking At Events pieces you added that day.** This is rarer than the others, so logging it keeps it from getting lost in the mix.
10. **Fill in how many PR Articles you added that day.** These are worth tracking closely since each one takes real time or money to land.
11. **Fill in how many 3rd-Party Reviews you added that day.** This shows whether you're actually keeping up with asking clients for reviews.
12. **Add a note if anything worth flagging happened.** This gives you context later on why a certain day or week looked the way it did.
13. **Fill in every column daily.** A column skipped on a given day breaks the accuracy of your weekly and monthly totals.

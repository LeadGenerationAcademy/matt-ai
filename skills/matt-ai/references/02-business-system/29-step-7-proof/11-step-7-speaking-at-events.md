# Step #7: Speaking At Events

Course: Step #7: Proof
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=45d8b3b3358643a7910d6151af1b1bc9

---
One of the most authoritative things you can do, both for your online presence and in real life, is speaking at events. If you're speaking at events, you must be important, right?

You should actively seek out and speak at events that have to do with your service. Take the pictures, the videos, the clips, all of it, proof that you spoke on whatever stage. It lends authority to you and builds you as an expert, so always include this.

### Prerequisites

- [Step #6: Proof Of Work](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=7dac82b45ce442b2ae04f9ed791de869) (Current Course)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Identify events related to your service and actively seek out speaking opportunities at them.** Speaking at an event that has nothing to do with your service does little to build authority with the people you actually want to reach. This isn't something that happens by accident, you have to go after it the same way you'd go after any other opportunity.
2. **Speak at the event, and take pictures, videos and clips of yourself on stage.** Follow through on the opportunity once you get it. The pictures are the visual proof that it actually happened, not just something you claim. Video adds another layer of proof beyond a still photo. Together, they lend you authority and build you as an expert, both online and in real life.
3. **Name each photo, video and clip after the event, and add them to your [Proof Of Work folder](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=7dac82b45ce442b2ae04f9ed791de869).** Naming the file after the event means anyone looking through it immediately knows what they're looking at and where it happened. If you're speaking at events, you must be important, so always include this alongside your other proof.

### Completion Audit

- Did you identify events related to your service and actively seek out speaking opportunities at them?
- Did you speak at the event, and take pictures, videos and clips of yourself on stage?
- Did you name each photo, video and clip after the event, and add them to your Proof Of Work folder?

Logos are the fastest credibility you can put on a page. Right below the fold, before someone scrolls any further, they need to see real companies that you have worked with or working with.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Collect at least 10 real customer logos.** Logos are a basic form of social proof because they show that real companies have trusted you. Do not use fake logos, prospects you never worked with, or companies you barely touched and now pretend are major clients.
2. **Rank the logos from most important to least important.** Do not throw them onto the page randomly. Decide which logos create the most credibility with your exact ICP.
3. **Put the most impressive and relevant logos first.** The logos that matter most should be seen first. A recognizable company is useful, but a company that closely matches your ICP may be even more relevant. Use your judgment.
4. **Write a headline introducing the logos.** Do not dump a row of unexplained logos onto the page. The headline should immediately tell the prospect what the logos represent.
5. **Write a subheadline adding context to the headline.** Use the subheadline to give the prospect enough additional information to understand why these logos matter. Keep it short and do not make claims you cannot prove.
6. **Make every logo consistent in size and visual treatment.** The logos should look like one deliberately designed section. Do not make one enormous, another microscopic, and the rest completely different. That looks amateur.
7. **Display the logos in a stacked section or a spinning carousel.** Either format can work. Stack them or make them spin, but keep the order intentional and make sure the strongest logos appear first.
8. **Place the entire logo section directly below the above-the-fold section.** Do not bury this halfway down the page. The purpose is to establish credibility immediately after the prospect sees the ICP, offer, and promised result.

### Completion Audit

- Did you collect at least 10 real customer logos?
- Did you rank the logos from most important to least important?
- Are the most impressive and relevant logos shown first?
- Does the section have a headline?
- Does the section have a subheadline?
- Are all logos consistent in size and visual treatment?
- Are the logos displayed in a clean stacked section or spinning carousel?
- Is the logo section placed directly below the above-the-fold section?

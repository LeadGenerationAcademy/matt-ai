A real screenshot from Slack, iMessage, email, or Telegram is more believable than the same claim retyped onto your website, so keep it in its native format and don't touch that.

The result inside it needs to be highlighted and readable on mobile without anyone zooming in, with a short snippet above it naming the customer and their company so it's clear who actually said it.

Group them by whatever objection or proof type they answer, strongest one first in each group.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Collect at least 25 real screenshots of client wins.** Screenshots become substantially more powerful when there are enough of them to show that the results are repeatable. One random Slack message is not overwhelming proof.
2. **Keep every screenshot in its original native format.** A real message inside Slack, iMessage, email, Telegram, or another recognizable platform is more believable than you retyping the same claim onto your website.
3. **Identify the strongest result inside each screenshot, prioritizing a dollar or numerical result whenever one exists.** Find the exact sentence, number, or statement that makes the screenshot worth showing. Do not force people to read an entire conversation to find the point. Specific numbers are faster and easier to understand than vague praise. If the screenshot contains a legitimate monetary result, lead with it.
4. **Highlight the result inside the screenshot.** Visually emphasize the exact sentence or number that matters. The prospect should immediately know where to look instead of scanning every line of the conversation.
5. **Add a short written snippet above each screenshot explaining the result, with the customer's name and company.** Summarize the soundbite so the prospect understands what happened before reading the screenshot. Keep it direct and do not exaggerate what the screenshot actually proves. Proof becomes weaker when nobody knows who the message came from. Keep the snippet small enough that the screenshot remains the proof, and do not put an enormous block of marketing copy above a tiny piece of proof.
6. **Make every screenshot readable on mobile without zooming in.** If someone has to pinch the screen to read it, you fucked it up. Make the screenshot large enough that the important message can be read immediately on a phone.
7. **Identify the objection or proof type each screenshot represents, and group the screenshots by it.** Decide what each screenshot proves. It may show financial results, speed, service quality, ease of implementation, reliability, or another important point. Do not randomly dump every screenshot onto the page. Organize them so each group systematically proves one thing or destroys one objection.
8. **Put the strongest and most relevant screenshots first inside each group.** Lead with the clearest result from the customer who most closely resembles your ICP. Do not bury the proof that matters most.

### Completion Audit

- Did you collect at least 25 real screenshots of client wins?
- Is every screenshot shown in its original native format?
- Did you identify the strongest result inside each screenshot, prioritizing a dollar or numerical result whenever one exists?
- Is the important result visibly highlighted?
- Does every screenshot have a short written snippet above it explaining the result, with the customer's name and company?
- Can every screenshot be read on mobile without zooming in?
- Did you identify the objection or proof type each screenshot represents, and group the screenshots by it?
- Are the strongest and most relevant screenshots shown first inside each group?

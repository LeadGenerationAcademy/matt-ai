# Step #5: Case Studies

Course: Step #7: Proof
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=d5fb9b3266da4a43b893248d69e80f7a

---
You should have as many case studies as possible. There's no such thing as too many, and every one you add is another reason for someone to trust you.

Make them genuinely in-depth. Cover the result, how long it took, and the details of the process, without revealing anything confidential, and film yourself walking through it instead of just handing someone a PDF.

Have these on your homepage, on your website, and downloadable as a PDF, at minimum a one-pager with a longer version available.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Identify a client result worth turning into a case study.** Pick a result that's specific enough to be believable and strong enough to matter to someone deciding whether to hire you.
2. **Get the client's permission to publish it.** Confirm what they're comfortable with you sharing before you write or film anything, so nothing gets published that upsets them later.
3. **Write out the result achieved.** Whether it's a dollar amount or any other outcome, this is what actually proves the work worked.
4. **Write out how long it took to get that result.** This sets realistic expectations for anyone reading it and considering hiring you.
5. **Write out the details of the process without revealing confidential information.** Go as deep as you can into how you actually got the result without exposing anything that would upset the client or give away confidential details.
6. **Turn that writeup into a one-page version and a longer version, and make both downloadable as a PDF.** The one-page version is the minimum format, quick enough for someone to scan and understand the result. The longer version gives anyone who wants more depth on the process somewhere to go. PDFs give people something they can save, send around, or reference later.
7. **Film yourself walking through the finished case study.** A video walkthrough of the actual writeup is stronger proof than a PDF or a page of text sitting there unexplained.
8. **Place case studies on your homepage and elsewhere on your website where they can be found.** The homepage is one of the first things people see when deciding whether to trust you. Don't limit this proof to one page, make it easy to find throughout the site.
9. **Turn every new client result you get into another case study.** There's no such thing as too many. Every case study you add is another reason for someone to trust you, so keep building the collection as new results come in.

# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #7: Proof
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=c21ee0fdd4d14d62985e746bc99e33f5

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Logos

- Collected at least 10 real customer logos and ranked them from most to least important
- Built a headline and subheadline introducing the logos, made them visually consistent, and placed the section directly below the above-the-fold

### Video Testimonials

- Collected at least five real video testimonials, each leading with its strongest result
- Built a thumbnail and name, business type, and company identification for every testimonial that proves the point before anyone presses play
- Ranked the testimonials from strongest and most relevant to weakest

### Written Testimonials

- Collected at least 10 real written testimonials, each headlined by its strongest soundbite
- Added a picture, name, company, and industry to every testimonial, linked to the original review wherever one exists
- Identified the objection each testimonial overcomes and grouped them accordingly, strongest first

### Screenshots

- Collected at least 25 real screenshots of client wins, kept in their native format
- Highlighted the result in each screenshot and added a short snippet naming the customer and company
- Grouped the screenshots by objection or proof type, strongest first, and confirmed every one is readable on mobile

### Case Studies

- Turned real client results, with permission, into in-depth case studies covering the result, timeline, and process
- Built both a one-page and longer version of each, made them downloadable as a PDF, and recorded a video walkthrough
- Published the case studies on your homepage and elsewhere on your site

### Proof Of Work

- Organized your real client work into a categorized "Proof Of Work" folder, each piece labeled with a named client, business, and type
- Built before-versus-after versions wherever possible
- Turned the portfolio into a standalone document and published it on your website

### Speaking At Events

- Sought out and spoke at events related to your service
- Captured and labeled photo and video proof from each event and added it to your Proof Of Work folder

### PR Articles

- Got featured in PR outlets relevant to your industry
- Built a working "As Featured In" section on your website linking directly to each live article

### 3rd-Party Review Sites

- Created business accounts on Trustpilot, Clutch, GoodFirms, Google Business, and any niche-specific review sites
- Collected 5 to 10 real reviews spread across each site

### Organizing Proof By Objection

- Identified your top 6 to 12 objections and ranked them by importance
- Wrote an objection headline for each one and regrouped all of your existing proof underneath the objection it answers

### Proof Page & Proof Document

- Built a Proof Page on your website containing every form of proof you have, each piece linked out directly
- Built a Proof Document or slide deck version of the same content, downloadable as a PDF
- Kept both ready to send at a moment's notice, including right after someone books a call

### Proof Master Hire

- Wrote a job description and hired a part-time Proof Master
- Showed them where to find wins and where each type of proof gets posted

### Proof Input Tracking

- Made a copy of the Input Tracking Template
- Logged your daily count for all nine proof types

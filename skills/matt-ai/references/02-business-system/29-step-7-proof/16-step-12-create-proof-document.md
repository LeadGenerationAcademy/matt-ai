### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose a format for your proof document: [Google Slides](https://slides.new) or [Document](https://docs.new).** Either works, what matters is that someone can move through it easily on their own. Build it from the same proof you put on your Proof Page in the previous step.
2. **Include your proof logos.** This is the first thing someone sees, so it should establish credibility immediately. Put the most impressive and relevant ones first.
3. **Include your proof of work.** This lets people see the actual work you've done rather than only reading claims about it. Name the client for each piece, and show a before-versus-after wherever you can.
4. **Include your video testimonials.** These carry more weight than text alone since someone can see and hear a real person saying it. Most people won't press play, so put the strongest result in the thumbnail.
5. **Include your written testimonials.** These add volume and depth on top of your video testimonials. Lead each one with its strongest result, and add the customer's name, picture and company.
6. **Include your case studies.** This gives anyone who wants more detail a place to go deeper on the actual results. Link to the PDF version so they can download it.
7. **Include your screenshots and photos of you speaking at events.** This stacks on more proof and authority in a format nothing else in the document provides. Keep screenshots in their original format with the result highlighted.
8. **Include your PR articles from different websites.** This shows third parties have already covered you, not just your own clients. Link each one to the actual article.
9. **Include your reviews from third-party sites.** This is proof you don't control, which makes it carry even more weight. It looks really strong when someone's doing their due diligence.
10. **Format it so it can be scrolled or flipped through easily, and make it attachable or downloadable as a PDF.** A document nobody can navigate defeats the purpose of building it in the first place. A PDF is what makes it forwardable inside a company instead of tied to one specific link.
11. **Send this document to people when they book a call with you.** This is one of the strongest moments to use it, since they're already leaning toward buying.

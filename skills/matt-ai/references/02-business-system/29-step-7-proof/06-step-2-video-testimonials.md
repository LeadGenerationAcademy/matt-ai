Most people never press play on a video testimonial. They just want proof it exists and that it's real, so it has to work before anyone hits the button.

That means leading with a real dollar result right in the thumbnail, and naming the actual customer, their business, and their company with a link to verify it, ranked strongest first.

### Example

- [KlientBoost](https://www.klientboost.com/results/) - They have good video testimonials on their Results page

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Collect at least five real video testimonials.** One testimonial is not overwhelming proof. You need enough video testimonials to show that the result was not a random accident.
2. **Pull the strongest soundbite or result from each testimonial.** Watch each video and identify the single statement that makes the testimonial worth showing. Do not summarize the entire conversation. Find the strongest line.
3. **Prioritize a clear dollar result whenever one is available.** Money is usually the fastest and easiest result to understand. If the customer states a legitimate dollar result, use it instead of burying it underneath vague praise.
4. **Put the result as the first line of the testimonial.** Lead with the outcome. Do not make the prospect read the customer’s entire life story before discovering what happened.
5. **Create a thumbnail showing the strongest soundbite or result.** Assume that most people will never press play. The thumbnail must communicate the main result without requiring them to watch one second of the video.
6. **Add the customer’s name, business type, company name, and website.** Anonymous testimonials look fake. Identify who the person is, what type of business they own, and which company they represent.
7. **Link the company name or website to the customer’s real website.** Give anyone who wants to verify the testimonial a direct path to do it. Do not make them search around trying to determine whether the company exists.
8. **Organize the testimonials from strongest and most relevant to weakest.** Put the testimonials with the clearest results and closest match to your ICP first. Your strongest proof should never be buried at the bottom.
9. **Make sure someone can understand the result without watching the video.** Look at the finished testimonial without pressing play. If the customer and result are not immediately obvious, the thumbnail and written information are not good enough.

### Completion Audit

- Did you collect at least five real video testimonials?
- Did you pull the strongest soundbite or result from each one?
- Did you prioritize dollar results whenever they were available?
- Is the result shown as the first line of every testimonial?
- Does every testimonial have a thumbnail showing the soundbite or result?
- Does every testimonial identify the customer's name, business type, and company name?
- Is the company name or website linked to the real website?
- Are the strongest and most relevant testimonials shown first?
- Can someone understand every result without watching the video?

# Step #1: List 20 Potential Big Ideas

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=e1cc8ea38c984102868c9da18adb42c0

---
You will rarely create the right Big Idea on your first try. This step is about generating enough legitimate possibilities that you have something worth evaluating.

Your Big Idea will eventually sit above the entire business. Spending one or two focused hours generating possibilities is worth it. Do not rush it.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Big Idea Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1hbE54FaLMwVyLM__GkgitRlPYUNiUIOalTcYtEOse24/edit?usp=sharing).** Every step in this module happens inside this one document, and each step has its own tab. Open the File menu at the top left and click Make a Copy before you write anything down. [in this skill: assets/templates/big-idea-template/]
2. **Schedule one to two uninterrupted hours for this exercise.** Your Big Idea is too important to create during 10 distracted minutes between emails. As the quote commonly attributed to Abraham Lincoln goes, if you have an hour to cut down a tree, spend most of it sharpening the axe. Take the same approach here.
3. **Go to Tab 1 of your copy.** The numbered list from 1 to 20 is already built for you. Seeing 20 empty lines makes the required output obvious. You are not finished because you found three ideas you vaguely like. You are finished when all 20 lines contain legitimate possibilities.
4. **Review your ICP, offer, and the problems, beliefs, circumstances, and desired outcomes of your market.** Your Big Idea must come from something the ICP already experiences, believes, fears, or wants. Do not create random slogans disconnected from the actual customer and offer.
5. **Use your own brain to generate as many potential Big Ideas as possible.** Think before outsourcing the entire exercise. Write down every reasonable possibility, even if it is not perfect yet. More volume gives you more opportunities to find something strong.
6. **Research customer comments, reviews, conversations, and Reddit threads for additional ideas.** Look for repeated language, frustrations, desires, circumstances, and beliefs. Your market will often tell you exactly what matters if you stop talking and pay attention.
7. **Give your ICP, offer, and research to AI and use it to generate more possibilities.** AI should expand the work, not replace the thinking and research. If you give it vague garbage with no context, it will give you vague garbage back.
8. **Write every potential Big Idea as one clear belief expressed in one sentence.** A Big Idea is not a tagline, company description, offer, or collection of medium-sized ideas. Each possibility should communicate one belief that could eventually sit above the entire business.
9. **Do not evaluate, combine, rewrite, or eliminate the ideas yet.** This is the generation stage. Judging every line while writing it will slow you down and reduce the number of possibilities. Evaluation happens after the full list exists.
10. **Fill in every line from 1 to 20 on Tab 1.** Use your brain, research, comments, reviews, conversations, Reddit, and AI. Do whatever it takes to reach 20. Do not stop early because you got bored.

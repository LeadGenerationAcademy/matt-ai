# Step #2: Remove Ideas ICP Doesn't Already Believe

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=08201a176e09479f93ebcc1aa7b18cd5

---
A strong Big Idea should begin with something a large percentage of your ICP already believes, wants, fears, or values.

You are expanding or reframing an existing belief, not trying to manufacture belief and demand from zero.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Tab 2 of your Big Idea Template.** This is the same copy you made in Step #1. Twenty blocks are already built, one for every idea sitting on Tab 1.
2. **Copy all 20 potential Big Ideas from Tab 1 into the Big Idea field of each block.** Do not evaluate the ideas from memory. Put all 20 in front of you so every idea goes through the same process.
3. **Fill in the Existing Belief or Desire field under every idea.** Write down the specific problem, belief, fear, desire, or outcome that must already matter to the ICP for the idea to work.
4. **Research sales calls, applications, reviews, testimonials, comments, support conversations, and Reddit threads from your exact ICP.** Do not guess what the market believes. Go look at what real people inside the market repeatedly say when nobody is feeding them the answer.
5. **Fill in the Evidence (Their Exact Words) field under every idea.** Copy the specific phrases they use, not your version of them. When you do enough research, the strongest beliefs will begin jumping off the page at you.
6. **Mark Keep in the Keep / Remove field when significant evidence shows that a large part of the ICP already believes, wants, fears, or values the idea.** One random comment is not significant evidence. You should see the same underlying belief or desire appearing repeatedly across the market.
7. **Mark Remove in the Keep / Remove field when you would need to convince the ICP that the underlying problem, belief, or desired outcome matters.** You want to sell something people already understand and value. You do not want to begin the sales process by arguing that they should care.
8. **Remove any idea that requires you to create demand from zero.** Creating demand from scratch is substantially harder than directing existing demand toward your offer. If the market does not care about the underlying subject, kill the idea.
9. **Rewrite every surviving idea using the exact language the ICP uses.** Do not replace clear market language with corporate garbage. Describe the belief the same way the ICP describes it.
10. **Fill in the Final List at the bottom of Tab 2 with only the ideas supported by significant evidence.** Every remaining idea should begin with something the market already believes or wants. If you cannot produce evidence for an idea, it does not survive this step.

### Completion Audit

- Did you go to Tab 2 of your Big Idea Template?
- Did you copy all 20 potential Big Ideas from Tab 1 into the blocks?
- Did you fill in the Existing Belief or Desire field under every idea?
- Did you research real sales calls, applications, reviews, testimonials, comments, support conversations, or Reddit threads?
- Did you fill in the Evidence field using the exact language your ICP uses?
- Does every idea marked Keep have significant evidence behind it?
- Did you mark Remove on the ideas that require convincing the ICP that the subject matters?
- Did you remove the ideas that require creating demand from zero?
- Did you rewrite the surviving ideas using the ICP's language?
- Does your Final List contain only evidence-backed ideas?

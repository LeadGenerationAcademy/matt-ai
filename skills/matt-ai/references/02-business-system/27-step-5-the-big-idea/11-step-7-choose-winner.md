# Step #7: Choose Winner

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=8421bde9605b493b9a6ae8923e6c2556

---
The previous steps removed Big Ideas with obvious weaknesses. This step chooses the single winner.

The scores matter, but the highest score alone does not automatically make something the right Big Idea.

The winner must perform strongest across the previous tests, make complete logical sense, and create an emotional reaction inside you. When you get it right, it should feel undeniable.

If the winner does not jump off the page and get you excited, you have not found it yet.

### Framework & Criteria

The winning Big Idea must pass all three criteria:

1. **Scores:** It performs strongest across the previous tests.
2. **Logic:** It makes complete sense for the ICP, Offer, and business.
3. **Emotion:** It jumps off the page and makes you genuinely excited to build around it.

If one of these is missing, you do not have the winner.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Tab 7 of your Big Idea Template.** Three comparison blocks are already built, along with the three questions that decide the winner.
2. **Copy every Big Idea that survived Step #6 into the Big Idea field of each block.** Put them side by side where you can see all of them at once. Do not rely on your memory of how each idea performed.
3. **Fill in the Made to Stick Total field for each Big Idea using the totals from Tab 5.** Bring the number across exactly as it was scored. Nothing gets re-scored on this tab.
4. **Fill in the Durability & Ownability Total field for each Big Idea using the totals from Tab 6.** Same as above. This tab compares work you have already done rather than repeating it.
5. **Fill in the Strengths field for each Big Idea.** Your Notes from Tabs 5 and 6 are where this comes from. Write down what the idea does better than the others.
6. **Fill in the Weaknesses field for each Big Idea.** Write down where the idea is soft, even when it scored a three or a four. This is what separates two ideas sitting on similar totals.
7. **Answer which Big Idea performed strongest across the previous tests.** The scores are not the entire decision, but they still matter. The winner should have survived every fatal-weakness test and performed strongly across Made to Stick, Durability, Ownability, and Willingness to Commit.
8. **Answer which Big Idea makes the most logical sense for your ICP, Offer, and business.** The belief should fit what the ICP already believes, change how they understand the problem, and make your exact Offer feel like the obvious solution. You should not need to force or explain the connection.
9. **Answer which Big Idea makes you most excited to build around it.** Be honest about your reaction. The winner should jump off the page and make you want to build the entire business around it. A technically correct idea that leaves you feeling nothing is not the winner.
10. **Choose the winner only if the same Big Idea answers all three questions.** Do not choose an idea only because it received the highest score. Do not choose another only because it feels exciting. The correct winner must satisfy both the logical and emotional sides of the decision.
11. **Write the winner into The Winning Big Idea field and remove the other candidates.** There is now one Big Idea. Not two. Not three. It is called the Big Idea, not your collection of medium-sized ideas.
12. **Return to Step #1 and create a new list if no Big Idea clearly wins all three tests.** Do not force a winner. If nothing feels undeniable, you have not done it properly yet. Starting again is better than building your entire marketing system around an idea you do not believe in.

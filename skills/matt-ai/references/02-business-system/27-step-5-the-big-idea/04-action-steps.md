# Action Steps

System: Business System

---
The Theory explains what a Big Idea is and what makes one work.

This section is where you actually create yours.

You will start with 20 potential Big Ideas, remove the weak ones, score what remains, choose the winner, write it in one sentence, and build the system required to support and repeat it.

Do not skip steps and do not fall in love with the first decent idea you have.

### Table of Contents

1. **List 20 Potential Big Ideas:** Generate enough possibilities that you are not forced to settle for your first decent idea.
2. **Remove Ideas the ICP Does Not Already Believe:** Eliminate anything that requires you to manufacture demand or convince the ICP to care from zero.
3. **Connect Each Idea to the Offer:** Make sure accepting the Big Idea makes your exact Offer feel like the obvious next step.
4. **Narrow the List to Three:** Keep the three strongest candidates so you can compare them properly before choosing a winner.
5. **Compare Against Made to Stick:** Score each idea based on whether it is Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, and supported through Stories.
6. **Test Durability and Ownability:** Determine whether the idea will remain relevant, whether you can own it, and whether you are willing to repeat it for years.
7. **Choose the Winner:** Choose the idea that makes the most logical sense and feels emotionally undeniable.
8. **Write It in One Sentence:** Test approximately 20 versions until the entire belief is clear, strong, and repeatable in one sentence.
9. **Build the Supporting System:** Collect the proof, stories, testimonials, case studies, and examples required to make the belief credible.
10. **Repeat It Across the Entire Business:** Plan how the same belief will appear throughout your marketing and sales without changing its meaning.

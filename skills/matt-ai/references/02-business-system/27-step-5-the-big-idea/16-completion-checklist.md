# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=1151a7f074854eecb1b6c3a1c3f7bdb5

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Generate & Filter Ideas

- Created a list of 20 potential Big Ideas, each written as one sentence
- Removed every idea the ICP doesn't already believe, want, or fear
- Removed every idea that doesn't connect obviously to your Offer using the "so" test
- Narrowed the surviving ideas down to your three strongest candidates

### Score & Select

- Scored all three candidates against Made to Stick and removed any with a fatal score
- Scored the survivors on Durability, Ownability, and Willingness to Commit, and removed any with a fatal score
- Chose the single Big Idea that scored strongest, made logical sense, and felt emotionally undeniable

### Finalize & Deploy

- Reduced the winning Big Idea to one final, repeatable sentence
- Built a Big Idea Supporting System document with your proof, stories, and testimonials, and listed what's still missing
- Made the Big Idea visible across your bios, cover images, website, landing page, first email, and VSL

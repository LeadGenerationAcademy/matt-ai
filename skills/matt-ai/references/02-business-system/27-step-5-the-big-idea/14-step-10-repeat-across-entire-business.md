# Step #10: Repeat Across Entire Business

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=378dd7764c164f868ce3896f47c4341f

---
Your Big Idea needs to be unified across every platform.

On every profile, it needs to be extremely visible above the fold. Someone should immediately understand some version of:

“I help X do Y because [Big Idea].”

You do not need to use that exact template literally, but anyone looking at the profile should be able to see it clearly.

The same Big Idea should then continue through the website, landing page, first email, and VSL.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Tab 10 of your Big Idea Template.** Every placement in your business has its own field on this tab. You write the wording here first, then go and put it live.
2. **Fill in the Your Version field using the template on the tab.** You do not need to use this exact template publicly. It is there to make sure someone can clearly see who you help, what you help them do, and the Big Idea behind it.
3. **Fill in the Instagram Bio field.** Bio space is tight here, so this is usually the shortest version of the belief you will write.
4. **Fill in the Facebook Bio field.** The wording can be adjusted to fit the platform, but it must communicate the same underlying belief.
5. **Fill in the LinkedIn Bio field.** This audience will sit through a longer and more direct version than Instagram will.
6. **Fill in the X/Twitter Bio field.** Another tight one. The short version you wrote for Instagram usually works here with minor changes.
7. **Fill in the YouTube Description field.** You have more room here than anywhere else, but the belief still needs to appear early instead of sitting at the bottom where nobody expands it.
8. **Fill in the TikTok Bio field.** The shortest of all of them. Cut it down to the belief and nothing else.
9. **Fill in the Facebook Cover Image field.** Write the wording that goes on the image itself. It does not need to use the exact same wording as the bio, but the Big Idea should still be clearly visible.
10. **Fill in the LinkedIn Cover Image field.** The cover image is the largest piece of real estate on the profile, and most people waste it on a stock photo.
11. **Fill in the X/Twitter Cover Image field.** Same again. Keep it short enough to read on a phone screen.
12. **Fill in the YouTube Cover Image field.** This is the banner people see before they have watched anything, so the belief should land before a single video does.
13. **Fill in the Website Above The Fold (Subheadline) field.** Someone should be able to see and understand the Big Idea without scrolling. The subheadline is usually the best place to explain it.
14. **Fill in the Landing Page Above The Fold (Subheadline) field.** You build the landing page later in Funnel Pages. Write the wording now so the page gets built around the Big Idea instead of having it bolted on afterward.
15. **Fill in the First Email In The Flow field.** Same again, the flows get built in Funnel Emails. Write the line now so the first email continues the belief instead of introducing a different message.
16. **Fill in the VSL (First 15 Seconds) field.** The VSL gets recorded in Funnel Videos. Someone watching it should understand the Big Idea almost immediately, so write those opening lines here and bring them with you.
17. **Update every profile and page you already have with the wording you just wrote.** Writing the wording on Tab 10 changes nothing by itself. Go through every platform and put all of it live now. The landing page, emails and VSL get updated as you build them in the Marketing System.
18. **Mark Yes or No for whether the Big Idea is unified across every platform.** Someone should not see one belief on Instagram, another on LinkedIn, and something completely different on YouTube. Every platform should reinforce the same Big Idea.
19. **Mark Yes or No for whether the Big Idea is immediately visible above the fold on every profile.** Open every profile yourself and look at it. Do not count something that is technically there but buried or difficult to see. It should be obvious to almost everyone looking at the profile.

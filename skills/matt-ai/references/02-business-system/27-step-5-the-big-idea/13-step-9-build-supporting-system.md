You now have the final Big Idea written in one sentence.

The next step is building the supporting system around it.

A Big Idea cannot survive on repetition alone. You need proof, stories, testimonials, demonstrations, and real examples that make people believe it.

We are not building the complete Proof System here because that will happen in the Proof Section later. However, you need to inventory what already exists, identify what is missing, and know that you could assemble the entire supporting system into one PDF immediately.

### Framework & Criteria

The supporting system should include:

1. Every available form of proof
2. Stories that demonstrate the Big Idea
3. Proof that you personally achieved or experienced it
4. Proof that your customers achieved it
5. Proof that your team can produce or support it
6. One central place where everything can be found immediately

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Tab 9 of your Big Idea Template.** This tab is the central inventory for everything that supports the Big Idea. Do not leave the evidence scattered across random folders, inboxes, platforms, and people.
2. **Copy the final Big Idea sentence from Tab 8 into the From Tab 8 field.** Every item you write underneath it should help the ICP understand, believe, remember, or emotionally connect with that exact belief.
3. **Fill in the Logos field.** Include relevant customers, clients, companies, partners, publications, or other recognizable names that make the belief more credible.
4. **Fill in the Screenshots field.** Include real messages, results, wins, conversations, and feedback in their native formats whenever possible.
5. **Fill in the Written Testimonials field.** Prioritize testimonials that clearly identify the person, company, problem, result, and relationship to the belief.
6. **Fill in the Video Testimonials field.** Include videos where real people explain the problem, what changed, what result they achieved, and why the Big Idea is true.
7. **Fill in the Proof Of Work field.** Show the actual work, deliverables, systems, assets, or results instead of relying entirely on people saying you are good.
8. **Fill in the Founder, Customer, And Team Stories field.** Include your own story, customer transformations, team experiences, and specific before-and-after examples that demonstrate the belief in the real world.
9. **Fill in the Case Studies field.** Use cases that clearly show the original problem, what was done, and the final result.
10. **Fill in the Total Number Of Relevant Customers, Clients, Or People field.** If the belief has been demonstrated across a meaningful number of people, make that visible. Do not make someone count everything manually.
11. **Fill in the How You Personally Achieved Or Experienced The Result field.** Show why you believe the Big Idea based on firsthand experience. Explain what happened, what you did, and what result followed.
12. **Fill in the How Your Customers Achieved The Result field.** The belief becomes stronger when it works beyond the founder. Show that customers reach the same result without you being the one producing it.
13. **Fill in the How Your Team Produces The Result field.** Show that the team can produce the result repeatedly instead of the whole thing depending on you personally.
14. **Fill in the Still Missing list.** Do not pretend the gaps do not exist. Write them down so they can be built properly during the full Proof Section.
15. **Answer the Final Check question at the bottom of Tab 9.** You do not need to design the final PDF during this step. You do need to know where everything is and be capable of assembling it without spending days searching for evidence.
16. **Continue collecting as much supporting proof as possible.** There is no minimum. Proof is a spectrum. More strong and relevant proof makes everything easier. The supporting system should continue growing for as long as the business exists.

# Step #8: Write In One Sentence

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=a9f4d77f13e9428f8428d04311225d46

---
You have chosen the winning Big Idea. Now you need to reduce it to one sentence.

One sentence means one sentence.

The sentence should communicate the complete belief clearly enough to understand and repeat after one exposure. It should also jump off the page and feel just as undeniable as the idea you selected.

You will probably not get the wording right on your first attempt. Write approximately 20 versions before choosing the final sentence.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Tab 8 of your Big Idea Template.** The winner carries over at the top, the 20 numbered lines sit in the middle, and the final sentence goes at the bottom.
2. **Copy the winning Big Idea from Tab 7 into the From Tab 7 field.** Start with the actual belief you selected. Do not start changing the underlying idea while trying to improve the wording.
3. **Fill in all 20 lines in the Write 20 Versions list.** Change the wording, order, emphasis, and level of specificity while preserving the exact same core belief. More volume gives you more chances to find the sentence that says it properly.
4. **Remove every version that requires a second sentence.** If the sentence needs another sentence underneath it to make sense, it has failed.
5. **Remove every version that contains multiple competing beliefs.** If it tries to communicate several ideas at once, it has failed.
6. **Remove every version that changes the meaning of the Big Idea.** If the improved wording quietly changes the actual belief, it has failed. This is the easiest one to miss, because wording that sounds better hides it.
7. **Identify the surviving version that is easiest to understand and repeat.** The ICP should be able to understand the belief immediately and repeat it after seeing it once. Do not choose complicated wording because it makes you sound intelligent.
8. **Choose the final sentence only if it jumps off the page and feels logically and emotionally undeniable.** The sentence should make complete sense and create the same internal reaction that caused you to choose the Big Idea in Step #7. If it feels flat, it is not finished.
9. **Write the winner into the Final Big Idea Sentence field.** Every rejected version stays on the tab above it for reference, but the sentence in this field is the only one that gets used throughout the business.
10. **Return to Step #1 if none of the sentences feels undeniable.** Better wording cannot save the wrong Big Idea. If approximately 20 serious attempts still produce nothing powerful, the underlying idea is probably the problem.

### Completion Audit

- Did you go to Tab 8 of your Big Idea Template?
- Did you copy the winning Big Idea from Tab 7 into the From Tab 7 field?
- Did you fill in all 20 lines in the Write 20 Versions list?
- Did you remove every version that requires a second sentence?
- Did you remove every version containing multiple competing beliefs?
- Did you remove every version that changes the meaning of the Big Idea?
- Is the version you identified immediately understandable and repeatable after one exposure?
- Does the final sentence jump off the page and feel logically and emotionally undeniable?
- Did you write one final sentence into the Final Big Idea Sentence field?
- If nothing felt undeniable, did you return to Step #1 instead of forcing it?

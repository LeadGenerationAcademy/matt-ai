# Step #3: Connect Each Idea To Offer

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=863fd21f9d7942a1ba5401e3ce8afc75

---
A Big Idea is useless if it does not connect directly to your Offer.

It can be true, memorable, and interesting, but if accepting the Big Idea does not make your Offer feel like the obvious next step, it will not do much selling.

In this step, we will test every Big Idea that survived Step #2, remove every idea with a weak or forced connection, and keep every idea that passes. We are not choosing the final winner yet.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Tab 3 of your Big Idea Template.** Ten blocks are already built. If more than ten ideas survived Tab 2, duplicate a block for each extra one.
2. **Copy every Big Idea that passed Step #2 into the Big Idea field of each block.** Only bring forward the ideas your ICP already has a reason to believe. Do not add new ideas here and do not bring rejected ideas back from the dead.
3. **Write your exact Offer in the Your Offer field at the top of Tab 3.** Write what you actually sell, who it is for, and what it helps them accomplish. Do not hide behind vague language like "we help businesses scale." The connection cannot be evaluated if the Offer itself is unclear.
4. **Fill in The Connection field for every Big Idea using this structure: "[BIG IDEA], so [OFFER]."** The Big Idea should change how the ICP understands the problem. The Offer should naturally follow from that new understanding. You should be able to state the entire connection clearly without giving someone a lecture.
5. **Mark Remove on any Big Idea where the connection to your Offer is not immediately obvious.** Do not talk yourself into keeping it. If you need to explain why the two things are related, the connection is already too weak. The prospect will not sit there and solve it for you.
6. **Fill in the Why The Old Way Does Not Work field for every remaining Big Idea.** A strong Big Idea reframes the problem. It explains why the ICP's current understanding or approach keeps producing the wrong result. One sentence is enough.
7. **Fill in the Why Your Approach Makes More Sense field for every remaining Big Idea.** Once the ICP understands why the old way fails, your approach should become more logical. The Offer must feel like the natural next step, not something randomly attached to the end. One sentence is enough.
8. **Mark Remove on any Big Idea that requires multiple paragraphs or additional logical jumps after the word "so."** The word "so" is a test. It cannot manufacture a connection that does not exist. If you need several paragraphs after it to force the Offer into place, remove the idea.
9. **Mark Remove on any Big Idea that could lead equally well to 20 unrelated Offers.** The connection must be specific to what you sell. If agencies, coaches, software companies, consultants, and dozens of unrelated businesses could all use the same Big Idea, it is probably too broad to do much selling.
10. **Mark Keep on every Big Idea that passes all of these tests.** Do not select the final winner yet. This step only removes ideas that fail to connect properly to the Offer. Every idea with an obvious, logical, inevitable, and specific connection survives.
11. **Fill in the Final List at the bottom of Tab 3.** Only the ideas you marked Keep belong on this list. These are the ideas that move into Step #4.

### Completion Audit

- Did you go to Tab 3 of your Big Idea Template?
- Did you copy every Big Idea that passed Step #2 into the blocks?
- Did you write your exact Offer in one sentence in the Your Offer field?
- Did you fill in The Connection field for every Big Idea using "[BIG IDEA], so [OFFER]"?
- Is every connection immediately obvious?
- Does every remaining Big Idea explain why the old way does not work?
- Does every remaining Big Idea make the approach inside your Offer feel more logical?
- Did you remove every idea that requires multiple paragraphs or additional logical jumps?
- Did you remove every idea broad enough to support numerous unrelated Offers?
- Did you keep every idea that passed instead of choosing the final winner too early?
- Does your Final List contain only the ideas you marked Keep?

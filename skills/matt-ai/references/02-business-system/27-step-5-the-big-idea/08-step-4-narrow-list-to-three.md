# Step #4: Narrow List To Three

Course: Step #5: The Big Idea
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/278a13f9?md=5876c5dee5164387986fe2c82e633c33

---
You should now have several Big Ideas that your ICP already believes and that connect directly to your Offer.

We do not need to drag every surviving idea through the rest of the process. Narrow the list to the three strongest candidates before testing them against Made to Stick.

If you cannot produce three legitimate candidates, you did not do the previous steps properly.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Tab 4 of your Big Idea Template.** Two lists are already built. The first holds everything that survived Tab 3. The second holds the three you carry forward.
2. **Copy every Big Idea that passed Step #3 into the Ideas That Passed Tab 3 list.** Only include ideas that your ICP already believes and that make your Offer feel like the logical solution.
3. **Fill in the Number Remaining field.** Writing the number down forces you to deal with how many ideas actually survived instead of guessing at it.
4. **Return to the previous steps and create more if fewer than three survived.** One idea is not enough. Two ideas are not enough. You need at least three legitimate options before moving forward. Do not lower the standard just to fill the list.
5. **Remove the weakest ideas until exactly three remain.** If you still have more than three, use your judgment and cut the weakest ones. Do not overcomplicate this. The next steps will test the final three in much greater detail.
6. **Write your three candidates into the Your Final Three list.** Do not choose the winner yet. These are candidates, not the final decision. All three will now be tested against Made to Stick before you select the winner.

### Completion Audit

- Did you go to Tab 4 of your Big Idea Template?
- Did you copy every Big Idea that passed Step #3 into the Ideas That Passed Tab 3 list?
- Did you fill in the Number Remaining field?
- If fewer than three survived, did you return to the previous steps and create more?
- Did you narrow the list to exactly three Big Ideas?
- Did you write all three into the Your Final Three list without selecting the winner early?

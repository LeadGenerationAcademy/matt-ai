A Big Idea is easier to understand when you can see how the entire business is built around one belief.

These examples are different, but each one shows the same thing: one belief repeated until the market associates it with the company or person.

##### “If You Have a Body, You Are an Athlete.” — Nike

**Big Idea:** Anyone with a body is an athlete.

Nike does not merely sell shoes and clothing. It sells the identity of being an athlete.

Its products, advertisements, sponsored athletes, customer stories, and overall brand all reinforce this belief.

If you accept that you are an athlete, buying Nike products becomes the logical next step.

##### “A Diamond Is Forever.” — De Beers

**Big Idea:** A diamond represents permanent love and commitment.

De Beers did not merely advertise diamonds. It connected diamonds with marriage and permanent commitment.

The company repeated this belief until buying a diamond became the expected thing to do when getting engaged.

The Big Idea changed how the market understood the product and made the purchase feel logical.

##### Financial, Time, and Location Freedom Through Remote High-Ticket Sales — Shelby Sapp

**Big Idea:** Young women can create financial, time, and location freedom by learning remote high-ticket sales.

Her content, lifestyle, proof, customer results, stories, and Offer all reinforce this belief.

She does not need to repeat the exact same sentence in every piece of content. She uses different problems, circumstances, outcomes, and examples to reinforce the same idea.

If you think of remote high-ticket sales for women, the goal is for you to think of Shelby Sapp.

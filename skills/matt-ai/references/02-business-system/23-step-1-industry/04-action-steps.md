The Theory explains why your industry has an enormous effect on the potential of your business, why the main reason to choose one is fulfillment rather than marketing, and what each of the six criteria is actually measuring.

This course is where you actually score your industry against those six criteria.

Do not score a non-industry. Ecommerce, SaaS, agencies, coaches and local businesses tell you something about the customer and very little about how you will solve their problem.

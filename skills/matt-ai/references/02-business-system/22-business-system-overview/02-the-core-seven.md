There are seven things that work together to determine the potential of your business: your industry, your ICP, your offer, your unit economics, your big idea, your messaging, and your proof.

Your actual offer is only one of these seven things. And that is the problem with a lot of advice about offers. People focus on making bigger promises, lowering their price, or having a stronger guarantee, when they should be fixing the fundamentals of the business around the offer.

All seven are inseparably connected. They are only good or bad as they relate to each other.

You could have a great industry, a great ICP, a great offer, great messaging, a great big idea, and great proof. But if the unit economics behind it are trash, it’s not going to work very well.

These things surround everything you do. Your paid ads, content, outreach, partnerships, landing pages, email flows, and sales process all depend on them. It’s kind of like your marketing system is the fish and the Core Seven are the water.

I think of the Core Seven as 80% and the funnel as 20%. We still have to do the funnel well, but obsessing over your booking rate while six of these seven things are broken is not going to fix your business.

They are also both one-time setups and never-ending loops. You choose your original industry, ICP, and offer, but you keep improving them as you get customers, collect proof, hear objections, and learn who is easiest and most profitable to serve.

People can obsess over their click-through rates, ad creatives, landing page, VSL, show rate, close rate and more (and these are of course essential), but most problems in your funnel are the result of the Core Seven. Because these things are theoretical instead of tangible, people will often not consider these when something goes wrong.

If I had to put a number on it, I would say that these Core Seven things make up 80% of your performance, and then your funnel makes up 20%. These are of course round numbers used for illustration, but I believe them to be directionally correct.

The major business stuff matters most. Obviously, we cannot completely botch our ads, funnel sales process and fulfillment, but if we really want to multiply and make order of magnitude improvements, we should focus more on the Core Seven than we do on our actual tactics.

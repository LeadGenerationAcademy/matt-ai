Your big idea is the core defining concept that everything in your marketing comes back to.

It makes your offer make sense and become desirable. It also connects your different messaging angles back to the offer.

When you have a strong big idea, your ads become easier to write, your content becomes more consistent, your offer becomes easier to explain, and your proof becomes easier to organize. You are saying the same core thing in a hundred different ways.

Ideally, we build the big idea around something our ICP already believes. Changing what someone believes is extremely difficult, especially quickly. It is much easier to connect an existing belief to why our offer makes sense as the solution.

A good big idea should be simple enough to understand and repeat in one sentence. It needs to be relevant to the exact ICP, concrete, credible, and important enough to make them care.

We should be able to demonstrate it through stories and reinforce it across the ads, content, funnel, sales process, and proof. We also want an idea we can repeat for years and become associated with.

One example from the course is Shelby Sapp: girls can be in sales and make their own income in sales. It speaks directly to her ICP, and her content shows women taking calls, closing deals, and doing the job.

Once someone believes they can make money in sales, training them to do it becomes a logical offer.

Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Customer Data Export

- Made a copy of the ICP Spreadsheet
- Exported up to your last 100 customers from the previous six months
- Pasted their names into Column A
- Deleted irrelevant columns and adjusted the ranges to fit your business

### Customer Categorization (B2B or B2C)

- If selling to businesses, categorized every customer on the ICP B2B Exercise tab
- If selling to consumers, categorized every customer on the ICP B2C Exercise tab
- Estimated any answer you didn't know for certain

### Finding Your ICP

- Reviewed the Most Common Answer and % of Total for each field
- Identified which characteristics were shared by at least 80% of your customers
- Chose 2-5 of those characteristics to form your initial ICP
- Collected more data or kept your ICP broader if fewer than two characteristics hit 80%

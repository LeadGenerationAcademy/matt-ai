The Theory explains that your ICP is the exact buyer inside the industry you already scored, and that finding them means exporting your last 100 customers, categorizing them, and pulling out the 2 to 5 characteristics that roughly 80% of them share.

This course is where you actually build your ICP.

You'll export your customer list, run it through whichever exercise matches how you sell, B2B or B2C, then narrow the results down to the handful of characteristics that describe most of your customer base.

### Table of Contents

1. **Download the Spreadsheet:** Export your last 100 customers into the ICP Spreadsheet and prepare it for whichever exercise, B2B or B2C, matches how you sell.
2. **Complete B2B ICP Exercise:** Categorize every business customer across six fields, age, gender, revenue, location, job title, and employee count, so patterns can be spotted across your customer base.
3. **Complete B2C ICP Exercise:** Categorize every consumer customer across nine fields, age, gender, location, marital status, children, homeownership, income, net worth, and employment, so patterns can be spotted across your customer base.
4. **Find Between 2-5 Shared Characteristics:** Review the Most Common Answer and % of Total for each field and select 2-5 characteristics shared by at least 80% of your customers to form your initial ICP.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Review:** Look at the Most Common Answer and % of Total for each field in your completed spreadsheet.
2. **Threshold:** A characteristic qualifies if it's shared by at least 80% of your customers.
3. **Selection:** Choose 2 to 5 qualifying characteristics to form your initial ICP.
4. **Fallback:** If fewer than two characteristics hit 80%, collect more customer data or keep your ICP broader until a clear pattern appears.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the ICP Spreadsheet you made a copy of in Step #1.** This is the same spreadsheet where you categorized every customer in Step #2A or Step #2B.
2. **If you completed the B2B exercise, go to the "Most Common Answer & % of Total" section on your ICP B2B Exercise tab.** This section is already built into the tab, right next to your customer data.
3. **If you completed the B2C exercise, go to the "Most Common Answer & % of Total" section on your ICP B2C Exercise tab.** Same section, just on the B2C tab instead.
4. **Look at the Most Common Answer for each field.** This tells you which single answer showed up most often for that field across your customers.
5. **Look at the % of Total for each field.** This tells you how dominant that most common answer actually is.
6. **Identify which characteristics are shared by at least 80% of your customers.** Your ICP should be based on hard customer data, not who you assume your customers are.
7. **Choose two to five of those characteristics.** These become your initial ICP.
8. **Do not include a characteristic unless the spreadsheet supports it.** Resist the urge to add a trait just because it feels true, the data has to back it up.
9. **If fewer than two characteristics meet the 80% requirement, collect more customer data.** A larger sample size can reveal patterns that a smaller one doesn't.
10. **Or keep your ICP broader until a clear pattern appears.** It's better to have a wider, accurate ICP than a narrow one the data doesn't actually support.

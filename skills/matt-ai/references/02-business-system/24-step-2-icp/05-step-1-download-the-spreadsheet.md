You can usually describe approximately 80% of your customers using two to four recurring combinations of shared characteristics. These patterns can be used for paid-ad targeting, outreach list building, and the Research Step of the Messaging Module.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [ICP Exercise Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1grqp01dMkZXEDt9jk_XLpTNY7-pNL3JGd-QSM8LpeVo/edit?gid=1919030740#gid=1919030740).** This becomes your working document for exporting and sorting your last 100 customers by shared characteristics. [in this skill: assets/templates/icp-exercise-template/]
2. **Export up to your last 100 customers from the previous six months.** Using a recent window keeps your data reflective of who you're actually selling to right now.
3. **Paste their names into Column A.** This gives you one row per customer to fill out characteristics across the rest of the spreadsheet.
4. **Delete any irrelevant columns.** Only keep the characteristics that make sense to track for your specific business.
5. **Adjust the available ranges to fit your business.** If your typical revenue or income numbers don't match the default ranges, edit them so they reflect your real customers.
6. **Make your best guess whenever you do not know something.** In most cases the answer will be obvious even if you can't confirm it exactly, so don't leave a cell blank.
7. **If you sell to businesses, continue to Step #2A: [Complete the B2B ICP Exercise](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/3dc179b5?md=72545caf6e424a49b737b69bba3b8fd2&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce).** That exercise has you categorize each business customer by age, gender, revenue, location, job title, and employee count.
8. **If you sell directly to consumers, continue to Step #2B: [Complete the B2C ICP Exercise](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/3dc179b5?md=ef0236b61fd14ae89d24cb0d4268271d&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce).** That exercise has you categorize each individual customer by age, gender, location, marital status, children, homeownership, income, net worth, and employment.

The Theory explains that your unit economics are the math behind your offer, that an offer can be profitable and still be cash-flow negative, and that the number we use is the cash actually collected on the first payment rather than the value of the whole contract. The target is to collect at least 2X your combined acquisition and fulfillment costs.

This course is where you run your own numbers.

You will work out your cash collected, your acquisition cost, and your fulfillment cost, then divide one by the other to find which of the three tiers you are in. After that you work on the three things that move you up a tier. You lower what it costs to acquire a customer, you lower what it costs to fulfill the order, and you raise your price as long as your close rate holds.

Do not use the total value of the contract when you run this. Use what the customer actually pays you at the beginning, because that is the only money you have to work with.

### Table of Contents

1. **Find Your Tier:** Divide your cash collected by your combined acquisition and fulfillment costs to see whether you are in Tier #1, Tier #2, or Tier #3.
2. **Lower Acquisition Costs:** Work through the clicks, calls, shows and closes phases to reduce what you spend getting each new customer.
3. **Lower Fulfillment Costs:** Use technology, labor, SOPs, templates and offer standardization to reduce what it costs to deliver the order.
4. **Increase Price:** Raise your price by 20% every four new customers while your close rate stays above 20%.

# Step #1: Lower Acquisition Costs

Course: Step #4: Unit Economics
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/a8dd5ad9?md=c428424f99044e4badf5632072ed159c

---
You can lower your acquisition cost by getting more of the right people to click, more of those people to book, more booked calls to show, and more sales calls to close.

Here is a list of things you could try, broken into those four phases.

##### Phase #1: Clicks

- Look at what your best customers have in common. Use that to make your targeting more specific.
- Test ads about different problems your offer solves.
- Make the opening of your ad immediately relevant to the person you want to reach.
- Take the exact words prospects use on sales calls and put them into your ads.
- Show a customer result in the ad and explain how you achieved it.
- Test different ways of presenting the same offer: talking to the camera, demonstrating your work, or walking through a result.
- Make content answering questions your ideal customers are already asking.
- Compare which ads bring paying customers before deciding where to spend more.

##### Phase #2: Calls

- Make sure your sales page follows through on the promise that made someone click.
- Rewrite your headline so someone can quickly understand what you help them achieve.
- Show an actual example of what the customer receives.
- Explain your process in the VSL so people understand how you get the result.
- Add proof from customers similar to the people you want booking calls.
- Answer the objections you keep hearing on sales calls directly on the page.
- Make your lead magnet useful to someone who would also need your paid offer.
- Use your welcome emails to explain the offer, share proof, and invite people to book.
- Remove application questions you do not use to decide whether someone is a fit.
- Follow up with suitable people who applied but never booked.

##### Phase #3: Shows

- Send a booking confirmation with the correct date, time, and meeting link.
- Send reminders before the appointment.
- Use your thank-you page to explain what will happen on the call and how to prepare.
- Send a short personal video introducing yourself after someone books.
- Send a relevant case study before the call so they can see what you have done for someone like them.
- Create short videos answering questions people usually ask at the beginning of the call.
- Personally contact prospects to confirm they are attending.
- Use pre-call retargeting ads to show booked prospects useful content and customer results.

##### Phase #4: Closes

- Review calls where someone did not buy. Find the questions or concerns you failed to address.
- Ask enough questions to understand their situation before explaining your offer.
- Connect what you sell to the specific problem they told you they want solved.
- Have relevant proof ready for common objections. If they doubt it works for a business their size, show a result from one.
- Practice answering the objections you regularly struggle with.
- Review successful calls with your sales team so they can learn from what worked.
- Book the next meeting before ending the call when another conversation is needed.
- Follow up with the information they actually needed to make a decision.
- Keep in touch with suitable prospects who were interested but not ready to buy.

Start with the phase that is currently holding you back. If people are booking but barely anyone attends, work on Shows. If plenty of qualified people attend but nobody buys, work on Closes.

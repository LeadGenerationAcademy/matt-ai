# Tier #2: Breaking Even And Working Toward 2X

Course: Step #4: Unit Economics
System: Business System

---
You are in Tier 2 when you collect at least 1X, but less than 2X, your combined acquisition and fulfillment costs.

At exactly 1X, the numbers look like this:

- Cash collected: $2,500.
- Acquisition and fulfillment costs: $2,500.
- $2,500 ÷ $2,500 = 1X.

The sale covers the cost of acquiring and serving the customer.

Great. That is a good first step.

The problem is that all $2,500 is needed for this customer. The sale has not given you any additional cash to fund growth or pay your other business expenses.

There is also a timing issue.

You spend money on ads. Someone books a call. The call might be several days away. They might need a follow-up before buying. Eventually, you collect the payment.

You have to float the money during that period.

As you increase your spending, the amount you need to float gets bigger. Being able to float $10,000 does not mean you can float $100,000 or a million.

That is why breaking even is a useful milestone, but we want to keep going.

If your combined costs stay at $2,500 and you collect $3,750, you are now at 1.5X.

You have $1,250 left after acquisition and fulfillment, before your other business expenses. That gives you more room, although it still does not cover the full $2,500 needed for another customer.

This is the work we do throughout Tier 2.

We improve the ads, funnel, and sales process. We make delivery more efficient. We accumulate proof and client results that support a higher price.

You do not necessarily need to throw away your offer and start again. You need to improve the economics of the offer you are already selling.

Our target is to collect enough to cover this customer and the next one.

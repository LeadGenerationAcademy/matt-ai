# Tier #3: Collecting 2X Or More

Course: Step #4: Unit Economics
System: Business System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/a8dd5ad9?md=ff88d3cc6991489abe1c6541e29922f6

---
You are in Tier 3 when the cash collected from a sale is at least twice the combined cost of acquiring and fulfilling for that customer.

For example:

- Cash collected: $5,000.
- Acquisition and fulfillment costs: $2,500.
- $5,000 ÷ $2,500 = 2X.

The first $2,500 covers this customer.

The other $2,500 is enough to acquire and fulfill for another customer at the same costs, before accounting for your other business expenses.

That next customer also pays you $5,000.

This is what I mean by self-funding growth. Each sale gives you cash you can put toward the next sale.

This is the position we are trying to reach with your front-end offer.

We are counting the money actually collected when someone buys. We are also including the full cost of delivering what you sold, even if some of that work happens later.

Otherwise, it is very easy to look at your bank account, think you have money available for growth, and spend money you still need to fulfill for existing customers.

Say we sold 100 qualified, exclusive leads for $100 each, paid upfront.

That would give us $10,000 cash collected.

It would cost $2,500 in ad spend to generate those leads, $500 in labor for the first order, and $2,000 to acquire the customer.

Our total acquisition and fulfillment costs would be $5,000.

$10,000 collected ÷ $5,000 in costs = 2X.

We would have $5,000 left before our other business expenses. At the same economics, that is enough to acquire and fulfill the first order for another customer.

Once you can do this consistently, fulfillment capacity becomes a much bigger concern.

Can you actually deliver for the customers you now have the cash to acquire?

This is why we want a standardized front-end offer. You can build your training, processes, templates, and technology around delivering the same thing repeatedly. Improvements carry over to the next customer.

You still need to watch the numbers as you grow. Acquisition costs can rise as you increase ad spend, and delivery costs can change as you hire.

Getting to 2X gives you the economics we want to scale. Keeping it there is part of running the business.

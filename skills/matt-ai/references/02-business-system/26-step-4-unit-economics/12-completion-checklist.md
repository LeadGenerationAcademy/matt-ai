### Finding Your Tier

- Wrote down the cash you actually collected on the first payment, not the full contract value
- Wrote down what it costs to acquire one customer
- Wrote down what it costs to fulfill the order, including the work you finish after the money arrives
- Divided your cash collected by the two costs combined
- Identified whether you are in Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3

### Lowering Acquisition Costs

- Identified which of the four phases is currently holding you back
- Worked on that phase before the others
- Tried the things listed under Clicks, Calls, Shows and Closes that apply to your business

### Lowering Fulfillment Costs

- Identified which fulfillment costs are worth attacking first
- Tried the things listed under Technology, Labor, SOPs, Templates and The Offer Itself that apply to your business

### Increasing Price

- Tracked your close rate using completed, qualified sales calls
- Sold to four new customers at your current price
- Raised your price by 20% while your close rate stayed above 20%
- Kept the offer the same while testing the price

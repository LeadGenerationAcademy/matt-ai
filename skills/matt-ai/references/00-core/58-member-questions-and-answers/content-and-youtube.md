## YouTube content strategy: recreate competitors or create new ideas?

Asked: October 15, 2026
Where: Telegram group "Lead Generation Academy General Chat"
Topic: Content and YouTube

**Question:** A member asks on YouTube whether to start by recreating competitors’ proven topics with better content and own data or by creating completely new ideas no one is talking about yet, and when to shift from one to the other.

**My answer (with the back-and-forth):**

Member 25: I think there's a nuance because if you're recreating a competitor's proven topic, you have to take into consideration the competitor itself (whether or not they are the same size or much bigger than you). You also have to search for that same topic and see what the inventory is around that topic to see potentially how you could stand out.

I find that sometimes if I create a version of somebody else's topic, it usually doesn't do well unless I'm adding something significantly different (because why else would they watch mine when they can just watch the original content piece). 

To me the order is:
1. You could recreate a competitor's proven topic.
2. Recreate the topic but put your own unique spin on it so that it is different. If that other person's YouTube video is getting traction, then yours might show up in suggestions.
3. Create something new and interesting where there's little inventory on that topic. Of course that's always going to be the hardest.

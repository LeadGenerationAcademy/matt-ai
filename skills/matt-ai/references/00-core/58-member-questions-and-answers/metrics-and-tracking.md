## What is ideal agency capacity and profit margin at $60-70k/month revenue?

Asked: October 7, 2026
Where: Telegram group "Lead Generation Academy General Chat"
Topic: Metrics and Tracking

**Question:** A member asks how close to capacity an agency should be and what a good profit margin looks like at around $60-70k/month revenue, wondering if 85-90% capacity is too high.

**My answer:** Your capacity is determined by how well you can get clients

If you just have ads or something that is working then need to hire at 75% ish

If not then it’s tough to even hire at all

## System for tracking employee time on client work

Where: Telegram group "Lead Generation Academy General Chat"
Topic: Metrics and Tracking

**Question:** Do you have a good system for time tracking employees doing client work to identify clients that take too much time/resources and justify firing them?

**My answer:** usually you would get them to track for one week and explain to them why you’re doing it

As opposed to just being a tyrant and making them track

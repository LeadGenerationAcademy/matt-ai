## Hardest part of keeping clients happy in agency customer success

Asked: October 14, 2026
Where: Skool community post "What’s the hardest part of keeping clients happy in your agency?" (https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/whats-the-hardest-part-of-keeping-clients-happy-in-your-agency)
Topic: Other

**Question:** What is the hardest part of keeping clients happy in an agency, specifically on the customer success side, and what specific struggles or solutions have you experienced?

**My answer (with the back-and-forth):**

Member 2: Hi Member 1

For us the hardest part early on was clients feeling out of the loop between updates. Even when the work was getting done, delayed communication from our end made them anxious, and anxious clients start questioning everything before they eventually churn

What fixed it for us was a defined weekly rhythm. We stay in touch with the client throughout the week for anything ongoing, and then every week we send a comprehensive report with a weekly meeting on top of it. The report follows a fixed structure and includes everything we got done in the prior week, the stats and performance numbers, and the priorities for the coming week. Since we are a paid marketing agency, the numbers do a lot of the talking, but pairing them with clear priorities shows the client there is always a plan in motion.

So if I had to give you one idea other agencies could use, it would be that consistent communication during the week plus a structured weekly report and meeting.

Me: Other than you actually getting the result they paid for, I would always try to make these conditions true.

- Always a clear roadmap for next 90 days that they can check whenever
- Real-time reporting on whatever the key metrics are
- Twice per day written updates via Slack or equivalent. Morning is what you will do today. Night is what you did today
- Getting them some quick wins and/or making the onboarding experience world-class will get them very happy

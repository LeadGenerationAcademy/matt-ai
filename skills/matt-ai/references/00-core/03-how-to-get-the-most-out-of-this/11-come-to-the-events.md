# Come To The Events

Course: How To Get The Most Out Of This
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/11ef4a4f?md=f1df384f2e39412da3c16958a5bec0ce

---
These won't be the usual piece-of-shit mastermind where you fly across the world to sit there and listen to someone talk about how great they are. They'll be closer to a three-day bootcamp, focused on networking, personal transformation and making a 90-day plan to execute until the next event.

There'll be four events per year, one per quarter. The first one is scheduled for March 2027, at a hotel right next door to where I live. Three of them will be in Q1, Q3, and Q4 in Dubai, as that is where I currently live. The other one in the summer months will likely be somewhere in North America or Europe, as it's way too hot in Dubai.

The current plan is Friday, Saturday and a half-day Sunday:

- **Friday:** Speed networking in groups of three, so by the end you know everybody, or someone who knows everybody. Then personal transformation work.
- **Saturday:** Business and marketing, then a padel tournament and a steakhouse dinner.
- **Sunday:** Build your 90-day plan for the period between this event and the next one, then a yacht day.

The speed networking comes first on purpose. Once you know everybody in the room, the other two days are a lot better.

You leave every event with a 90-day plan. The next event, three months later, is where you show up having executed it.

The plan is to include the events in your Skool membership with no additional event fee. You cover your travel and hotel.

This is still the tentative plan, so some details may change before March.

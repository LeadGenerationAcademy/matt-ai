One of the primary ways I can help you is by judging your ads or giving you feedback.

Your Meta Ad Library shows me which ads you have running right now. That lets me judge the creative itself.

Is it clear at the top who the ad is for?

Do they understand who it's for and what the offer is within one second of looking at it?

Does the ad work together with the top of your landing page and your sales video?

The Ad Library can't tell me whether an ad is actually working. A lot of people go into someone else's Ad Library, see an ad that's been running since May, and assume it must be working. You have no idea what that business's economics or conversion rates are.

That's what your Ads Manager screenshots are for. Include the spend, outbound clicks, booked calls and cost per booked call for each ad. Then I can see which ads are bringing in calls at a cost you can actually afford, and which ones are worth making more variations of.

Send both if you can. Show me what you built, what you tried and what happened.

Please send me screenshots if you can. You can do that in private chat, on Telegram, or in Skool. I know it's a little sensitive, so sometimes people prefer private chat. I will review them when I can.

If you send screenshots versus something else like a link or Loom, you're going to get a response much, much faster. A link or Loom takes me exponentially more time to look at, especially when I have tons of messages.

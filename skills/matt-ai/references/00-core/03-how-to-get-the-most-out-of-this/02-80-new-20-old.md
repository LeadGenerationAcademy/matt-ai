# 80% New, 20% Old

Course: How To Get The Most Out Of This
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/11ef4a4f?md=9dc41fe1a1904847a120f54c8cb50355

---
One of the best things to understand comes from a book called [10X Is Easier Than 2X](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=10X+Is+Easier+Than+2X) by Dan Sullivan and Dr. Benjamin Hardy: it's more like 80% new and 20% old.

To get the most out of this Skool, expect roughly 80% of how you work, live and run your business to change. The other 20% is what you keep. If you want a different result while keeping everything exactly as it is, this Skool will probably be very frustrating and not valuable at all for you.

I've worked with about 1,800 businesses, and I've never seen anyone succeed without changing their business and their life in a serious way.

When you're trying to get a 2X improvement, there are a million different ways to do it. You could do this, you could do that, you could squeeze out 10%, 20%, 50%. When you go for 10X, your path narrows. There are only a very few ways forward, and you have to wipe everything clean and completely reimagine yourself, your lifestyle, your business and your marketing.

Whatever you currently do brought you to your current position. If you could already achieve your goals, you would have done it by now. Inside this Skool, we work on becoming what I call the 2.0 version of you. The 1.0 version is the beliefs, character traits and skills you have today. The 2.0 version is the person capable of building, running and scaling a $10 million per year business.

Try to make everything you do 10 times better than the previous version. Your next YouTube video, your next ad, your client fulfillment. Will it actually be 10 times better every time? Probably not. Aiming for it will still get you a much better result than making the same thing over and over without trying to improve.

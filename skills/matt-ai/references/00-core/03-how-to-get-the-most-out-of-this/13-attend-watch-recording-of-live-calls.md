There are three live calls with me every week:

- **Tuesday, Personal:** Working on you, the entrepreneur.
- **Wednesday, Business:** Working on the business itself and the fundamentals underneath it, including your offer.
- **Thursday, Funnel:** Working on your funnel and marketing system.

These are teaching calls covering information I don't publish on YouTube or anywhere else. Each call is about an hour and focused on one specific topic, with roughly 45 minutes of me teaching and talking. A lot of the time, I'll explain the theory first and then build the entire thing from scratch live, as far as I can get in the time available.

If I had to rank them, the Personal call is the most valuable, then Business, then Funnel. The funnel is laid out so clearly in the courses, with so many templates, assets and AI projects, that you can copy and paste a lot of the steps. For almost everyone, the personal side is the roadblock.

You'll find the date and time of every call in the [Calendar](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/calendar) tab. [Click here](https://www.addevent.com/calendar/6vv6c2c84d2g) to add all weekly calls to your own calendar so you don't miss any. The schedule is subject to change as the Skool grows.

You don't have to attend live. Every call is recorded and posted in two places:

- **The [Matt's Call Recordings](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/54117aee) course:** Every past weekly call, in the Classroom.
- **The [Call Recordings](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/call-recordings) thread:** A post in the Skool community with every call recording we have ever done, labeled with the date, the type of call and the topic. This is constantly updated.

If Tuesday doesn't work for you, watch it on Wednesday.

If you need feedback or have a question, post it inside Skool or Telegram. You don't have to wait for the next call.

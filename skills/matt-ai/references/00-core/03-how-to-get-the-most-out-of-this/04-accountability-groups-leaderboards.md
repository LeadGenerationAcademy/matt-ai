# Accountability Groups/Leaderboards

Course: How To Get The Most Out Of This
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/11ef4a4f?md=efc13e2e48c3412a9d5fb2a7bc4e336c

---
After every hour of deep work, you fill out a Work Form. That's a short Google Form where you write what you planned to get done that hour, what you actually got done and how many times you got distracted. On its own, it keeps you honest with yourself. An accountability group makes it public.

For several years, I tracked my hours on my own. It still worked. Adding other people made it significantly more powerful.

Your accountability group is a group of friends, coworkers, business partners or other members of this Skool who also do these hourly deep work hours. Everyone completes their own hours and fills out their own Work Form. Nobody sits in the group as a spectator.

Every time someone fills out a Work Form, everyone else in the group gets a notification by email, text or both. That gives you a few layers of accountability:

- You can't quietly stop doing the work and pretend you're still following the system. Everyone can see it.
- If you're screwing around and see that someone just finished another hour, you'll probably get back to work.
- You're competing with other people, trying to keep up with them and eventually trying to beat them.

Then there's the Leaderboard, a shared spreadsheet that updates every time someone fills out a Work Form. It shows how many hours each person has completed today, this week, this month, this quarter, this year and all time, along with how quickly each person finishes their first hour after waking up. If someone falls behind, everyone sees it. If someone moves into first place, everyone else knows exactly what they need to do to catch them.

There's also a thread pinned to the top of Skool where members post their results, like their wake-up time, when they finished their first hour, how long it took them to finish all four hours, and how many hours they completed that day. I post mine there too, so don't be shy. Seeing other people complete their work motivates everyone in the group.

# Send Me Your Content

Course: How To Get The Most Out Of This
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/11ef4a4f?md=81e1583e95274390a155594ac94c2d4a

I don't know much about getting millions or hundreds of thousands of followers or views, but I do know a thing or two about getting high-ticket B2B sales from your content. It is probably one of the best in terms of efficiency, with views translating to actual dollars in your bank. It's just one of those things.

Content gives you leverage, which means getting more out than you put in. If I make a YouTube video, I don't need to fly around the world or get on a Zoom call and explain the same thing 10,000 times to everybody who watches it. I record it once, and that many people watch it.

It also makes every other channel work better. If someone has seen your content for a long time and then gets outreach from you, they're much more likely to respond. If someone sees your ad, clicks on your profile and there's a bunch of content on it, they're more likely to book a call than if it was blank.

Great content is proof too. It shows that you know what you're talking about. If someone takes an idea from your content, applies it and gets a result before paying you, they now have personal proof that your ideas work.

When I look at your content, I'm judging whether it would attract the people who can actually buy from you. If you talk about beginner problems, you're going to attract beginners.

Send me your content, prioritizing YouTube and your newsletter first, then Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Twitter. The best option is if you can send the file or a screenshot in Skool or Telegram. I'm not going to watch an entire YouTube video, but I'll definitely watch the first 30 seconds and all the packaging too, meaning your title and thumbnail.

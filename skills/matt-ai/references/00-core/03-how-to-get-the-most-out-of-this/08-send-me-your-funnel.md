When I look at your funnel, I'm checking whether your ad, your above the fold and your VSL actually work together. Your above the fold is the top section of your sales page, everything someone sees before they scroll. Your VSL is your video sales letter, the video on that page that explains and sells your offer.

Someone should understand who it's for and what the offer is within one second of looking at your ad and above the fold. Then your VSL should show a form of proof within five seconds and wrap up the whole offer within 15 seconds. A lot of people explain the whole offer in the ad, explain it again on the landing page, then re-explain it in the VSL.

The thank-you page matters because a booked call does not mean someone will show up. That page, along with the videos and emails they get before the call, is what gets them to remember the call, still want to have it, and show up already understanding what you offer.

Send me your funnel. Prioritize screenshots, especially the above the fold sections. I would want to see:

- The above the fold section on the sales page
- The first 15 seconds of the VSL
- The rest of the sales page
- The thank-you page, or wherever they get redirected after booking

Make sure you have all of your content connected, and send me that too. A lot of people see your ad, click on your profile, or go to Google and find your YouTube before they ever book. Whatever they find there is part of your funnel.

Send your screenshots in Skool or Telegram, or in a private chat if it's sensitive. Screenshots get a much faster response than a link or a Loom.

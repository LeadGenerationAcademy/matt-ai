# Use The Hourly Deep Work System

Focus, hard work and actually getting stuff done is the number one constraint of almost every business owner. The most effective way I know to fix it is what I call the Work System. It's exactly what I do every hour, and I never tell you to do anything I don't do myself.

You work in focused 60-minute hours, and you use a few simple things to keep yourself honest:

- **A kitchen timer:** A cheap physical timer that sits under your monitor, set to 60 minutes. Do not use your phone as your timer.
- **A distraction counter:** A small handheld tally counter. You press it once every time you get distracted.
- **A Work Form:** A short Google Form you fill out at the end of every hour.
- **Sticky notes:** One for every hour you finish, stuck on your wall.

You'll buy the timer, the distraction counter and the sticky notes in the [Buy Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/3b482dba?md=3db790738d9c418fb9ba7e9a3f2274b7), along with everything else you need for your workspace.

Every work hour runs the same way:

1. Plan exactly what you'll get done in the next 60 minutes.
2. Start the timer.
3. Work until the timer goes off, pressing the distraction counter every time you lose focus.
4. Fill out your Work Form with what you planned, what you actually got done and how many times you got distracted.
5. Write the date, the time and which hour of the day it was on a sticky note in red pen, then put it on your wall.
6. Take a 15-minute break and start the next hour.

You can't lie to yourself about how focused you were when the information is right in front of you. A lot of people say they worked all day. When they actually time it, they worked for 90 minutes.

My wall has hundreds, and eventually thousands, of sticky notes. Every one is physical proof that I did an hour of work. This really changes how you view yourself. Every hour you finish stacks up undeniable proof that you're someone who works and can focus, and it turns into almost a game.

Do four of these hours every day, inside the four-hour block on your calendar. You'll set up the whole thing step by step in the [Work System Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5c9acee6?md=3590a140ca74479baaaa9c007740bb4e).

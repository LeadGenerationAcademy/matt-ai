# Meet Other Members In The 1:1s

Course: How To Get The Most Out Of This
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/11ef4a4f?md=0c99a7cb113549af983b7e112ca40bd2

---
When you join a group like this, one of the best parts, if not the best, is usually the people you meet. The problem is that it's normally left to chance. You might click with someone in the chat. You might not. That's why we do it in a more systematic and structured way.

Every week, we make one curated 15-minute introduction between you and another member. These always take place on Tuesdays. We do our best to match you with someone at approximately the same level as you based on revenue. Someone at your level is usually dealing with the same problems you are, and two minds are usually better than one. The matching won't be perfect every time, but it gives you a regular way to get to know other members individually and build relationships over time.

You have to explicitly opt in and commit to attending. You'll usually do this through the [Onboarding Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScoV-Pz1rl4eEs7-GwiUx6vQUQs4nYWKoF97XY4J_e_DuK64A/viewform?usp=header), but you can also message me directly inside Skool. Give complete and thoughtful answers on that form. It's what we use to match you. See [Step #2: Opt In/Out To Weekly Intros](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e25e3498?md=d97b28a3bb3e402e91c834e0a7b91825) (Onboarding Course).

If you like the person and vibe with them, take it offline and stay in touch after.

After each meeting, we'll send you a short form asking for about two minutes of feedback on the person you met. This helps us see whether both people are taking the intros seriously and putting effort into them.

Respect other people's time. If you opt in and miss a total of three meetings for any reason, you'll be removed from the intros and permanently barred from rejoining. We're not going to baby you or make excuses.

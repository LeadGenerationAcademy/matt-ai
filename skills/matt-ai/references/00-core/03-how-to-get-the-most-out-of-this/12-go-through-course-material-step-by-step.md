Everything in this Skool is laid out for you step by step.

You could spend years finding the information, figuring out which parts apply to you and working out what order to do everything in. Or you can just follow the steps I laid out inside this Skool.

Go through the courses in the [Classroom](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom) in the order they're laid out:

1. **[Onboarding](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e25e3498?md=d97b28a3bb3e402e91c834e0a7b91825):** Go through this course if you haven't already.
2. **[Personal Courses](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/fb01b0e3?md=8a83046779ea46a79c67450e63132995):** The most important courses in this Skool. Out of the roughly 1,800 businesses I've worked with, the biggest constraint was the owner about 97% of the time. Start these as soon as possible.
3. **[Business Courses](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ddd78715?md=b5aa9ce091b940009a81f1d86895e1f9):** Where you build and fix the foundation of your business: your industry, your ideal customer, your offer, your unit economics, your big idea, your messaging and your proof.
4. **[Marketing Courses](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/23eccc38?md=058b82ff2a3d4f56aa8e0b2508003ebc):** Where you set up your full marketing system.

The courses are split into Setup Courses, where you build everything, and Systems Courses, the strategies you use to run what you've built. All of the Setup Courses are unlocked the moment you join.

The Systems Courses are unlocked separately. Once you've finished the setup course that goes with one, message me and I'll manually add you. For example, finish the Paid Ads Setup Course and I'll give you the Paid Ads System. More than 40 courses at once will overwhelm you, and you can't use the systems properly without the setup done first.

Click the complete checkmark in the top right corner of each module when you finish it. Skool doesn't make it very obvious, but it tracks your progress so you know where you are.

We spend over 60 hours per week updating this Skool. If something is unclear, broken or outdated, use the feedback form at the bottom of every module. It goes straight to our Slack.

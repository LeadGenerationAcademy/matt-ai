# Timeblock Four Hours Per Day

Course: How To Get The Most Out Of This
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/11ef4a4f?md=8919b984e44841e9bc97de8301ef4364

---
To me, you literally never achieve anything in business unless you have a set period of time to actually work on the high-leverage stuff. Unless you have at least a four-hour time block where people can't distract you or book meetings, you will never achieve this. I almost guarantee it.

Block the first four hours of your day for deep work, meaning focused work on the projects that actually push your business forward. Rearrange the rest of your schedule around them. It doesn't have to be at the start of the day if you genuinely work better at the end, but it has to be in your calendar, set to repeat every day and marked as busy. If someone can book a meeting during that time, you didn't protect it. If you leave your schedule open, other people will happily fill it with their meetings, calls, favors and messages.

Especially if you're under $1 million per year, most of those four hours are going to go to acquisition, whether that's content, ads, outreach, partnerships, or you name it. You're also going to have to build systems, processes, and all this kind of stuff.

Keep all four hours on one project. Splitting four hours across four different things turns four deep work hours into four shallow ones. I recommend putting at least one of those hours toward working through this Skool.

For example, I once put four hours into building SOPs for a new offer I was running. Without that time blocked off, it never would have gotten done.

Do this for 30, 60, 90 days and your business will be night and day. You'll set this up properly in [Step #2: Block The First Four Hours](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c15cebb8?md=0e4c1da5f9d44f46bb4428172bd18b60) (Time Block Course).

In any community or Skool group or mastermind, one of the best parts is usually going to be whatever chat and whatever community comes with it.

Skool is where the courses and community posts live. Telegram is our main group chat, and it's where almost every conversation happens. I think it's better for building community and having ongoing conversations between members, and it's a quicker way to ask questions, connect and build relationships without opening Skool.

If you haven't already, join the [Telegram General Chat](https://t.me/+r25JTNYihpozNjA5). The chats are split by revenue level, because nothing kills a community faster than mixing people at completely different levels in every conversation. Someone doing $1 million or $10 million a year has different problems from someone just getting started. To get into a higher-level chat, send me a screenshot showing your revenue over the previous 365 days. Do not use AI to make a fake screenshot. I will shake my head in shame.

Introduce yourself in both Telegram and Skool with your name, your location, your business name, what you do and your 6 month goal. Post your accountability updates in there too. I post mine, and so do other members.

Ask your questions publicly so other members can see the answer and add to it. Don't DM me normal questions. My inboxes are flooded with random people trying to sell me stuff, and there's a 95% chance I won't see your message.

The more you put into it, the more you're going to get out of it. One of my first mentors told me that the best way to gain status or maximize every group you're a part of is to be the most valuable person and help the most in that group. If that's the case, and if he was right, then maybe you should adopt that mindset too.

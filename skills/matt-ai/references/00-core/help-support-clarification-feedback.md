If something is broken, doesn't make sense, needs clarification or if you have any feedback (positive or negative), do not just move on and not say something.

You are paying for this and to be here, and it should work and be the best it can possibly be.

The best and fastest way to get this fixed is to submit this form below. Use as much detail as you can. Please put your name on it too. Don't be anonymous, in case we need to further clarify.

This form is connected to our team's Slack so we will see the notification when it is submitted.

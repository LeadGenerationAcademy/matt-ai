# What Gets Measured Gets Managed

Course: Core Business Thesis & Philosophy
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/889a0260?md=524be70331644077a45142cb0fa16984

---
Tracking is so important, and it's going to be a recurring theme in this Skool, in the personal modules, and in the business modules. If I asked you almost any core metric in your business, you should be able to either tell me off the top of your head or find it within 30 seconds. You should have 100% confidence that it's true, accurate, and up to date.

This describes less than 1% of the businesses I've ever talked to. The problem is that almost everybody thinks they're doing better than they are. I've never met someone who overestimated their churn, overestimated their net margin, or underestimated their customer acquisition cost. People are never doing as well as they think they are, and they don't even know that because they don't track anything.

The good news is that when you start to track stuff, you can no longer deny what is right there in front of you, and in many ways, things will get better almost automatically. Be motivated to act. You'll be motivated to improve them, and you'll have great clarity on what you need to do.

If I were to ask you, right this moment, the following questions, how confident would you be?

What is your revenue?

What is your net margin?

What is your monthly churn?

What is your median client payment?

What is your close rate of booked calls?

What is your show rate?

What is your close rate of live calls?

What is your call DQ or unqualified rate?

What is your cost per call from ads?

Is your cost per call from outreach?

What are your unit economics as far as cash collected?

If you couldn't answer all of these within 30 seconds and find the right answer, in my opinion, you're not a serious business owner right now, and you need to fix that. Business is just one big game of numbers and one big game of math. Some people might measure slightly different things, but at the end of the day, it's a collection of numbers. Some we're trying to make go up, some we're trying to make go down. That is all there is to it.

Everything is easier when you sell stuff people must buy. They either legally must buy it, like auto insurance; functionally must buy it, like food and shelter; or need it to compete, like email marketing for ecommerce stores.

The further you get away from this, the harder selling becomes. Someone buying from you must understand what it is, why they need it, and why you are the best person to provide it.

When they already understand the first two, you can spend your time on number three.

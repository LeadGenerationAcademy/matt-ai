You need a system that takes someone from discovering you to buying from you. It should capture their contact information, help them understand and trust you, and determine whether they need and can afford what you sell.

At the top, build toward four methods of acquiring customers: paid ads, content, outreach, and partnerships. These methods can make each other more effective.

Someone might discover you through an ad, watch your content, and arrive on a sales call already familiar with you. You also become less dependent on one source of customers.

At the bottom, have different offers for different needs.

**Concepts:**

- Multiple Ways To Drive Traffic
- System To Capture, Nurture & Qualify Leads
- Multiple Ways To Convert Leads Into Calls
- Different Offers For Different Levels Of The Market
- Activation Points (see Activation Points)

There are things people do that make them much more likely to buy from you. It could be watching a demonstration, trying your product, or completing something that helps them get a result.

Find the actions that actually help people understand the value of what you sell, then get more people to take those actions. Don't just assume something works because your best customers happened to do it. Test it.

The activation point refers to a critical moment or action in a user's experience with a product, service, or system that significantly increases their engagement or commitment. This point is crucial for businesses because it is often strongly tied to increased customer retention and lifetime value.

In the case of companies like Netflix, the activation point might be a particular episode that, once watched, greatly increases the likelihood of a viewer watching an entire series. Similarly, for ClickFunnels, an activation point could be when a customer sets up their own domain, significantly increasing their engagement and retention.

Businesses often focus on designing experiences that efficiently lead customers to activation points, as these are indicators leading to sustained use of their service or product.

These things are incredibly important to identify throughout your entire business because, in many ways, there are different activation points. There are activation points that happen before someone becomes a customer, and those are different from the activation points for someone who is a customer.

The activation points before they become a customer are, of course, things that are more likely to get them to book a call or close. After that, the points are more about getting them more bought in and less likely to churn.

As far as a goal goes, I think you should probably have 5 activation points before they become a customer. If they hit any one of them, they're more likely to hit all 5. After they become a customer, we want to have:

- An immediate activation point
- Another activation point within 24-48 hours
- Another activation point within 7 days
- Another activation point within 30 days
- Activation points every 30 days after that, if we can

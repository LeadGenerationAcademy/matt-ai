The purpose of these principles is to build a business so valuable that people will be begging to buy your entire business. Not everybody might have ambitions to sell their business, but the things that would make it valuable enough for someone to buy it are the exact same things that would make it valuable for you to keep.

We want something that consistently gets customers, makes substantial money from those customers, and keeps working without everything depending on you.

I'm going to be creating and refining this core business thesis and philosophy. This is going to be the North Star of this Skool, and I think everybody should be trying to make these things true.

This is going to be a work in progress for Matt, especially in the coming weeks. My goal is to have this completely dialed in, but these are the things and the overall goals that we should be working towards.

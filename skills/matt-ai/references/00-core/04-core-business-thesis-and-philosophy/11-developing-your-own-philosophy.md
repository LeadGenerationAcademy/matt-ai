# Developing Your Own Philosophy

Course: Core Business Thesis & Philosophy
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/889a0260?md=cf2408164eb548a1824cd1464cb5638b

---
Your philosophy is what you believe works and why. Your system is how someone puts those beliefs into practice.

If people get results from running their business based on your philosophy or system, they have a reason to keep buying from you. That can substantially increase their lifetime value.

You need your own opinions, intellectual property, frameworks, and way of doing things. You will struggle to stand out if you're a clone of everyone else.

The reason your content probably never hits, or you just get price-shoved, is that you don't really have any original system, opinions, process, or way of running a business. That shows in your content.

The phrase to remember is that if you can get someone to run their business based on your philosophy or your way of doing things, your LTV will skyrocket. It'll also make it so that you are always relevant, especially in the age of AI.

How should someone run their business?

What should they do?

What are the things they need to understand?

Do you have a framework for this?

How is this different than the usual way of doing things?

A lot of people do not have their own philosophy

What you're looking at now in this course, Core Business Thesis and Philosophy, is my way of doing it. If I can convince you that these are all objectives you need to get for your business, and you understand them and are set on achieving them, you're probably not going to leave this Skool. You are bought into my way of doing things, and I am the best person to help you. That is what you are trying to replicate in your own business, and I will admit this is not easy. It has to come from experience, trial and error, and success, but it is necessary.

It should be noted that all of the things I'm trying to convince you that you need to do are actually true and are actually the things you need to do. They're not just made-up or random things that don't make sense. That is a very important point, in my opinion. I have to convince you that all of these things will make an amazing business, which they will, but I need you to believe that. As such, I better make my argument pretty damn strong.

When you have this, becomes very difficult for others to simply copy what you're doing, because even if they do, they're just going to reinforce that you are the owner or the OG of this business philosophy. It also helps you build a unified content strategy where you have 3, 5, or 10 different parts of your business philosophy, and all your content will be tied back to one of those. This makes it compound and more effective over time, and it also makes it very cohesive.

Think of how easy it would be to:

- Make a Miro board or some slides, hit record, and post a very low-production-value but high-value YouTube video every day or multiple times per week.
- Write a newsletter every day.
- Put three posts a day on all social media platforms when you have a core philosophy that it ties back to.

You never really have to think of new content. It's all content that reinforces this philosophy.

The purpose of this philosophy is to build a business so valuable that people will be begging to acquire it. What you sell affects how easily you get customers, what it costs to deliver, and how much money you keep. We are building all of it together.

**Time Blocking:** Block off the first four hours of your day for uninterrupted work on the single thing holding your business back or the improvement that would make the biggest difference. That is your biggest constraint or highest-leverage opportunity. You need time to actually build these things. Think of the 80/20 rule on steroids.

**Funnel System:** Build a system that takes someone from discovering you to buying from you. Your funnel should capture contact information, build trust, and determine whether someone needs and can afford your offer. Feed it through paid ads, content, outreach, and partnerships. Each can make the others more effective, and you are less dependent on one source. At the bottom, have different offers for different needs.

**2x+ Cash Collected / (Acquisition + Fulfillment Cost):** My target is to collect at least twice what it costs to acquire and fulfill a customer. If those costs total $2,500, target $5,000 upfront. That covers the customer and leaves $2,500 before overhead and taxes. The goal is to help fund the next customer with the money from this one. Cash collected matters. A $5,000 contract paid over twelve months does not give you $5,000 today.

**12x+ Gross Profit LTV:CAC:** The first target helps fund growth now. This target is about how much money each customer makes you over time. Aim for $12 in lifetime gross profit for every $1 spent acquiring them. If they pay $15,000 and delivery costs $3,000, their gross profit lifetime value (LTV) is $12,000. If their customer acquisition cost (CAC) is $1,000, that is 12:1. Increase prices, keep customers longer, lower fulfillment costs, or lower acquisition costs. This is how you build a business that shoots off cash.

**Owning A Keyword:** When someone thinks of lead generation, I want them to think of Matthew Larsen immediately. That is owning a word in your target market's mind. Create useful, bingeable content that solves their problems. Generate fans, not just followers. Give them enough value that they come back, instead of only worrying about views.

**Copy & Paste Fulfillment:** Once you get good at acquiring customers, delivering what they bought can become the constraint. Use the same proven process, tools, and templates across customers instead of rebuilding everything. Get as close to copy-and-paste fulfillment as possible, and as close to selling air as you can while still delivering the result.

**Selling Stuff People MUST BUY:** Start with something people must buy: legally, like auto insurance; functionally, like food and shelter; or competitively, like email marketing for ecommerce stores. The further you get away from this, the harder selling becomes. People must understand what you sell, why they need it, and why they should buy from you. When they already understand the first two, focus on number three.

**Activation Points:** Within your funnel, find the actions that make someone more likely to buy. It could be watching a demonstration, trying your product, or getting a result from something you taught. Test what works, then get more people to do it. Hammer those things home.

**Developing Your Own Philosophy:** Have your own opinions, frameworks, systems, and way of getting results. Your philosophy is what you believe works and why. Your system puts it into practice. If people successfully run their business using your system, they have a reason to keep buying from you. You will struggle to stand out if you're a clone of everyone else.

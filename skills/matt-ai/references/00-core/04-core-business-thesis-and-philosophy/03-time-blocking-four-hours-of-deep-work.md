# Time Blocking + Four Hours Of Deep Work

Course: Core Business Thesis & Philosophy
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/889a0260?md=25a3204ac80d4c789ab78de298efa820

---
You will never get very far in your business if you don't have enough time to do the deep work that pushes it forward. Block off the first four hours of your day and work without distractions on the single thing that is either your biggest constraint or would have the most leverage.

Your constraint is what is currently holding the business back. Leverage means one improvement produces a much bigger result elsewhere.

Think of the 80/20 rule on steroids.

To me, I think this is one of the most important habits in the entire existence of the world. Alex Hormozi actually made a short-form video saying the exact same thing: it's the one thing that led to success in his career. The video link will be here.

We have to time block, but then we actually have to do deep work during that. That is the work form system. This is going to be in my core personal thesis and philosophy too, but it's the one thing that unlocks everything in the business one, so we're going to do it here.

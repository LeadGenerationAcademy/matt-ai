# "Copy & Paste" Fulfillment

Course: Core Business Thesis & Philosophy
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/889a0260?md=cf67411ffed64bb6b09ee812ffa4b93c

---
Fulfillment means delivering what the customer paid for. There will come a point where you are so good at lead generation and booking calls that your primary constraint is no longer acquisition. It is fulfillment.

You want the same proven process, tools, and templates to work across customers instead of rebuilding everything every time.

Get as close to copy-and-paste fulfillment as possible, and as close to "selling air" as you can get while still delivering the result.

The primary blockers for copy and face fulfillment are that you are in an industry that I refer to as a non-industry, something like e-commerce. The only thing clients in that industry have in common is that they use Shopify and sell products. Literally nothing else is the same, so if you get something that works for one client, you can't automatically use it for another client and expect the same results.

That is a lot different from something like lead generation for roofers, where you have an ad, a funnel, appointment setting, and a sales process that really work. You can very much clone it, change the name, logo, website, and location, and expect the same results for another client.

The second primary blocker is offer standardization. It's very hard, or even impossible, to copy and paste results if everything is custom, isn't it? It's very important that this offer is essentially the same offer you are running to get that 2x cash collected. You have a little bit more wiggle room with the 12+ LTV to CAC upsell a recurring service, but still not much.

My target is to collect at least two times the combined cost of acquiring a customer and delivering what they bought. If those costs total $2,500, we want to collect at least $5,000 upfront.

That covers the customer we just acquired and leaves another $2,500 before overhead and taxes. The goal is for customer payments to help fund the next customer, and then the next one.

This is about cash actually collected. A $5,000 contract paid over twelve months does not give you $5,000 to use today.

This pillar is about you and your ability to do what your goals require.

It includes:

- Skills.
- Focus.
- Leadership.
- Work ethic.
- Consistency.
- Beliefs.
- Character traits.
- Knowledge.
- Organised planning.

You can know exactly what you should be doing and still not do it. You can have a good idea, start working on it, get distracted, and leave it unfinished. If that keeps happening, knowing more about marketing will only get you so far.

We are going to work on how you spend your time, how you approach difficult work, and how consistently you follow through. We will also work on understanding what you actually want from your life.

As you develop these things, you become more capable of using everything else in the course. You can spend longer on something that matters, learn what you need to learn, and keep working when the first attempt does not go well.

That carries into both your business and your funnel.

This pillar is about the business you are asking people to buy from.

We will work through seven things, which I call the Core Seven:

- Industry: The market you operate in.
- ICP: Your ideal customer profile. The specific type of person or business you want to serve.
- Offer: What you sell and what the customer gets.
- Unit Economics: What you earn from a customer compared with what it costs to acquire and serve them.
- Big Idea: The central idea behind your offer and why your approach makes sense.
- Messaging: How you explain the problem, your offer, and the reasons someone should care.
- Proof: The evidence that you can do what you say you can do.

These affect how easy it is to get clients and whether getting those clients produces a worthwhile business.

You could build a funnel that brings in plenty of people, but if the offer does not make sense to them, you will struggle to sell it. If you do sell it but it costs too much to deliver, more sales can create more work without leaving you much money.

We need to improve the business that sits behind the funnel as well.

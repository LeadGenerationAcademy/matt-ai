In almost every case, the person running the business was the problem.

If I had to guess, I would break down the biggest constraint like this:

- 97% of the time, it was them.
- 2.9% of the time, it was their business.
- 0.1% of the time, it was their funnel.

Your ability to focus and consistently finish what you start affects everything else. You can understand what needs to change in your business and still spend months without actually changing it.

As you improve personally, it becomes easier to improve your business and build your funnel. You can learn something, apply it, and keep working on it long enough to get somewhere.

The opposite does not automatically happen. A better funnel does not make you more disciplined or organised. You can get more leads and still struggle to do the work.

The business itself is usually the next biggest constraint. That is where we work on the Core Seven.

The lead generation and funnel part is usually quite straightforward after a while.

That is why we are going to work on all three, starting with you. You probably watch my YouTube videos for the lead generation stuff, and we will cover that. But I also want to help you become the best version of yourself, because that makes everything we do afterwards much easier.

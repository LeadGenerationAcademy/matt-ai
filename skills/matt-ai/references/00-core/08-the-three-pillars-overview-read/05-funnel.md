Your funnel is how people find you, learn about what you do, and become customers.

This is where we work on the paid ads, content, pages, videos, follow-up, and sales process you have been seeing in the diagrams.

For a book a call funnel, we break it into four phases:

- Clicks: Get people interested enough to click through.
- Calls: Get qualified people to book.
- Shows: Get them to attend.
- Closes: Make the sale.

We will work on the individual parts and how they connect. Someone should be able to move from finding you to buying without having to figure out what to do next.

Your business gives you something worth selling. Your funnel gives people a way to discover it and buy it. Your personal ability determines how well you build, operate, and improve both.

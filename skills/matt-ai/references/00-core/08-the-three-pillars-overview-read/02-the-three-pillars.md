There are three things we are going to work on in this Skool Group.

1. You.
2. Your business.
3. Your lead generation funnel.

We are going to work on all three, starting with you.

You probably watch my YouTube videos for the lead generation stuff. We will cover that, but I also want to help you become the best version of yourself.

As you improve your ability to focus, learn, and follow through, it becomes easier to improve your business and build your funnel. You can understand what needs to happen and actually do it.

The opposite does not automatically happen. A better landing page does not make you more disciplined. Getting more leads does not teach you how to organize your day or finish what you start.

That is why we begin with the personal pillar. You are the person who has to make the other two work.

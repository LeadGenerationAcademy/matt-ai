# Quick Win #5: Five Life Pillars + Template

Course: Five Quick Wins
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/da2f150a?md=4d87e24ada204a9d8f96ba0a5795da87

---
Your five life pillars are the five things you would achieve if you could only achieve five things in the next 10 to 20 years. This quick win uses a goal template to write each one out in depth.

Most people's goals don't motivate them because they're surface level and have no meaning. Writing out what each one actually means to you is what makes it powerful enough to motivate you.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Goal Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/121OlUONCeMXAQlhiTLywKPTjB8G8KqwY4w9TvYTmXpI/edit?usp=sharing).** Most people don't work hard enough, and their goals don't motivate them because they're very surface level and have no meaning. This template is how you fix that. [in this skill: assets/templates/goal-template/]
2. **Make five copies of the template, one for each pillar.** You're going to have this sheet times five.
3. **Choose the five things you would achieve if you could only achieve five things in the next 10 to 20 years.** That's what a pillar is. Think 10 to 100 times bigger than what you think you could achieve. Make sure you actually want these. It's fine if they change over time, but don't change them too much, because that resets the process.
4. **Fill in Vision for each pillar.** This is your overall goal. For example, "I am a billionaire. I have over $1 billion liquid in the S&P 500." Then keep writing from there.
5. **Fill in What Does This Represent To Me for each pillar.** This is the magic question, and the one that really lit me on fire when I was doing this exercise. You want this goal because it represents something to you, and this is where you put that into words. Keep it very personal. If you can't fill it in easily, you probably don't actually want the goal. It's just what society tells you to want, because you've never thought deeply about it.
6. **Fill in What Do I Achieve for each pillar.** These are the tangible things, like the cars, the houses, absolute financial freedom, all the material possessions you ever wanted and recognition from your peers. It's probably the least deep part of the template, so don't spend all your time here.
7. **Fill in Who Do I Become for each pillar.** These are the beliefs, character traits and skills you develop, both in order to achieve the vision and as a result of achieving it. For example, you might become someone with extraordinary consistency and work ethic across decades.
8. **Fill in What Do I Think for each pillar.** These are the thoughts you're training yourself to think as you work toward it.
9. **Fill in What Do I Say for each pillar.** What you say usually follows what you think, so fill this in after your thoughts.
10. **Fill in How Does It Feel for each pillar.** This is a big one, because it's where you combine meaning and feeling together. For example, "It feels satisfying because I always knew I was capable of this, and it shows I overcame tremendous struggles and persevered for 20 years."
11. **Fill in What Does It Mean for each pillar.** This is probably the deepest question here. A lot of people don't really go hard on their goals because the goal doesn't actually mean anything to them. When you give real meaning to a goal, it becomes a thousand times more powerful.
12. **Fill in What Are The Causes for each pillar.** These are the things you could do today that would get you there if you did them over and over, like waking up at 5:30 a.m. or spending four hours a day on your most important project. New causes get added as you progress and learn more.
13. **Aim for five to ten things under each section.** That's a good rule of thumb. But don't add fake things or things that don't really matter just to fill the space.
14. **Check whether writing it is making you tear up.** That's not required, but it's a good sign.
15. **Check whether you would never want to show the finished document to anyone.** If you wouldn't, that's another excellent sign. It means you probably got to the root of what you actually want.
16. **Refine your pillars every day.** This is just the initial setup. It should be a living, breathing document that you keep working on and improving.

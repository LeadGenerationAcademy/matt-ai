# Quick Win #1: Block Off Four Hours

Course: Five Quick Wins
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/da2f150a?md=d6ac077ab5ec4df384dc1207d598eb73

---
This quick win is blocking off the first four hours of your day in your calendar for deep work. Deep work is focused time on the projects that actually push your business forward, like building your marketing, your systems and the assets your business needs. Nobody gets to book a meeting or pull you into something else during these four hours.

It's very unlikely you'll ever build great projects or assets without having this time in your calendar. Most people's days get filled with messages, calls and whatever feels urgent, and the important work never gets done.

If you have these four hours blocked off in your calendar and train yourself to sit there and do the work every single day, your business will be night and day after just 30, 60 or 90 days.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you actually use. If the four hours aren't sitting in your calendar, it's very unlikely you'll ever build the great projects and assets your business needs to move forward.
2. **Block the first four hours of your day for deep work.** This time is only for work that actually pushes the business forward. Not answering messages, not taking calls.
3. **Move the block to the end of the day if you genuinely work better then.** It doesn't have to be at the start. What matters is that the four hours are in there and protected.
4. **Set it to repeat every day.** You're training yourself to sit there and do this over and over. Do it for 30, 60 or 90 days and your business will be night and day.
5. **Plan to work the four hours in 60-minute sprints.** That's the next quick win. The four hours plus the hour sprints together are what make the difference.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Did you block the first four hours of your day for deep work?
- Did you move the block to the end of the day if you genuinely work better then?
- Did you set it to repeat every day?
- Are you planning to work the four hours in 60-minute sprints?

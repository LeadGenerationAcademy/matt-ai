# Quick Win #4: Ad + Above The Fold + VSL Congruency

Course: Five Quick Wins
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/da2f150a?md=a52c4ee63aaa4ed5888a47aa714a898d

---
When someone clicks your ad, they see three things in a row: your ad, the above the fold section of your landing page (everything they see before they scroll), then your VSL (the video on that page that sells your offer). Congruency means all three work together as one.

If you're running ads or plan to, this is one of the most important things to get right. In a lot of people's campaigns, nothing connects, and they explain the whole offer in the ad, again on the landing page, then again in the VSL.

When it's done properly, someone knows who it's for and what the offer is within one second. Your VSL can then start straight with proof instead of re-explaining what you do.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Instant Recognition:** Someone understands who it's for and what the offer is within one second of looking at your ad and above the fold.
2. **Proof Within Five Seconds:** Your VSL shows a form of proof within the first five seconds.
3. **Offer Within 15 Seconds:** The whole offer is wrapped up within the first 15 seconds of your VSL.
4. **Proof And Objections:** After that, your VSL alternates between more proof and answering objections.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Put your ad, above the fold and VSL side by side.** If you're running ads or plan to, these three have to work together as one unit. The customer should recognize the same offer, aimed at the same person, as they move through all three.
2. **Make sure someone knows who it's for and what the offer is within one second.** Financial advisors. Boom, they know who it's for. Qualified leads. Boom, no problem. This has to be true in your ad, then again on your above the fold. Your ad gives them a reason to click, and your above the fold immediately confirms the offer they clicked for and makes the next step obvious.
3. **Fix the design and continuity of your above the fold.** A lot of people have a badly designed above the fold with no connection to the ad that sent people there. Look at them together and ask whether the page continues what the ad promised. Don't make them search through the entire page to find the connection.
4. **Stop re-explaining the whole offer in your VSL.** A lot of people explain the whole offer in the ad, explain it again on the landing page, then re-explain it in the VSL. If your ad and above the fold already did that job, your VSL doesn't need to hook them again or explain what you do. It continues from the reason they clicked.
5. **Show proof within the first five seconds of your VSL.** Screenshots and testimonials on screen work well. "All of these financial advisors you see on screen are in different states, with different teams and different amounts of assets under management, but they all have one thing in common. They bought leads from us."
6. **Wrap up the whole offer within the first 15 seconds.** By then, they should know exactly what you're offering and have seen a second form of proof.
7. **Alternate between more proof and answering objections for the rest of your VSL.** Keep going back and forth between the two until the end.

### Completion Audit

- Did you put your ad, above the fold and VSL side by side?
- Does someone know who it's for and what the offer is within one second?
- Did you fix the design and continuity of your above the fold?
- Did you stop re-explaining the whole offer in your VSL?
- Does your VSL show proof within the first five seconds?
- Is the whole offer wrapped up within the first 15 seconds?
- Does the rest of your VSL alternate between more proof and answering objections?

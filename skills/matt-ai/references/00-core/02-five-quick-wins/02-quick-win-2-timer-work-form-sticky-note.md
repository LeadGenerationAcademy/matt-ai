# Quick Win #2: Timer + Work Form + Sticky Note

Course: Five Quick Wins
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/da2f150a?md=7b7527ffa5e44946b034a193aca3f033

---
This quick win is how you actually work through the four hours you blocked off. You work in focused 60-minute hours with a timer, fill out a short Google Form called a Work Form with what you did after every hour, and put a sticky note on your wall for every hour you finish.

Focus and actually getting stuff done is the number one constraint of almost every business owner, and this is the most effective way I know to fix it. Every sticky note is physical proof you did an hour of work, and that really changes how you view yourself.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Work Form Template](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1i7ZgFfPYRK6CgJ7BtblZNm2lQR2_QoEnl853HOL15co/copy).** The link takes you straight to the copy screen. Click "Make a copy" and rename it to [Your Name]'s Work Form. Mine would be Matt's Work Form. Change both the form title and the file name in the top left corner.
2. **Put a 60-minute kitchen timer directly underneath your monitor.** Leave it set to 60, so starting an hour is one button press. Do not use your phone as a timer. Picking up your phone during deep work is like inviting a crackhead into your office and trusting him to sit quietly. Your phone has beaten you before.
3. **Plan exactly what you'll get done in the next 60 minutes.** Write it down before the timer starts. This becomes the Planned Output on your Work Form, and it forces you to plan the hour instead of sitting down and deciding as you go.
4. **Start the timer.** I have mine sitting right on my desk while I'm working. I never tell you to do anything I don't do myself.
5. **Work all out until the timer ends.** One hour, fully focused on what you planned. Focus and actually getting stuff done is the number one constraint of almost every business owner. This is the most effective way I know to fix it.
6. **Fill out your Work Form with what you did that hour.** Write what you actually produced, not what you meant to, and compare it against what you planned. Once you have an accountability group, every submission sends them a notification, basically bragging that you finished another hour.
7. **Write the date, the time and which hour of the day it was on a sticky note.** I use a red pen. One note per completed hour.
8. **Put the sticky note on your wall.** Reserve a large section of wall for these, because it fills up faster than you expect. Every note is physical proof you did an hour of work. My wall has hundreds of them, and eventually it'll have thousands. That really changes how you view yourself, and it builds undeniable proof that you're a hard worker who can focus.
9. **Take a 15-minute break.** Stretch, drink some water, walk around. Whatever you do has to fit inside the 15 minutes, and you're back at your desk before it's up.
10. **Start the next hour.** Repeat the same cycle for all four hours you blocked off in Quick Win #1. Four completed hours means four more sticky notes on the wall every successful day.

If you want a good recording setup for videos and calls, this is what I recommend. It is not a budget setup, but I think it is good value for money.

Do not underestimate how much looking and sounding professional matters on sales calls and in your content.

#### Recording Equipment

- **Camera:** Sony ZV-E10, body only ([link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09BBGN298))
- **Lens:** Sigma 16mm F1.4 DC DN Contemporary, Sony E-mount ([link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B077BWD2BB))
- **Light:** Elgato Key Light ([link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07L755X9G))
- **Capture Card:** Elgato Cam Link 4K ([link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07K3FN5MR))
- **Microphone:** Shure MV7, USB/XLR ([link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08G7RG9ML))

#### Recording and Audio Software

- **Recording Software:** OBS Studio ([link](https://obsproject.com/))
- **Audio Enhancement:** Auphonic ([link](https://auphonic.com/))

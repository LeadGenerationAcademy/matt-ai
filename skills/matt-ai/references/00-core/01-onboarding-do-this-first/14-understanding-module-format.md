Course: Onboarding (DO THIS FIRST)

---
I went back and forth a lot when I was making this Skool and eventually settled on a consistent format for the lessons.

I want them to feel familiar without being overly rigid. As you go through the courses, you will get used to how everything is laid out.

Every lesson should have a video unless it is so small that it doesn't need one. It will be clear at the top of the page whether there is a video.

I have also tried to include as many picture examples as possible so you can see what each step should look like.

At the bottom of each lesson, there is a feedback form. If something is absolute shit, unclear, broken or not good enough for the price you paid, submit that form.

It goes directly to our company Slack so we get notified and can fix it.

Tell me if something is wrong or if you don't feel like it is my best work. Doing this makes the Skool better for you and everyone else.

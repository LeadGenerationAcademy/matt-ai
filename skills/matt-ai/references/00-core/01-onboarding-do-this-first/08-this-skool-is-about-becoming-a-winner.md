The tangible goal of this Skool is to generate leads. It is called Lead Generation Academy, after all.

But the goal of this entire thing is personal transformation. It is to become the type of person who could build, run and scale a $10m+ per year business. That is much different than having some technical knowledge of how to run a paid ad.

If you were capable of succeeding, you would have already done it.

We want you to learn how to build and run a full marketing system. But, more importantly, we want you to become the person who is capable of doing that.

##### Why $10 Million Per Year?

The goal of this entire Skool is $10 million per year. That is a large goal, and the math on being a winner is pretty brutal.

They claim that about 4% of businesses do $1 million a year. That is 1 in 25. About 0.4% of people do $10 million per year. That is 1 in 250.

That is very interesting, is it not? Out of 250 people, 249 of them are losers.

But $10 million is not the goal just because it is a nice round number. It is the goal because of the person you have to become to achieve it.

The money aside, $10 million per year in revenue is an excellent result. The even better and more important result is the person you would have to become to build, run and scale that business.

That means:

- Your beliefs
- Your character traits
- Your skill set
- Your self-image
- How well you relate to and take advantage of the laws of nature and the laws of the universe
- How good you are at your particular business

That is what the personal transformation is about.

##### Becoming A Winner

This Skool is about becoming a winner. We don't want any losers in here, and being a loser is a state of mind more than an actual revenue number.

You want to be the type of person who gets fired up by the idea of building a $10 million per year business. You know that you don't want to be average and you don't want to be a loser.

That is why I put so much emphasis on the personal modules. To be quite honest, the more important part of this Skool is the transformation you go through on the way from where you are now to an absolute winner who would be successful in whatever you do.

Whether you continue on your current path and with your current business model or not, becoming that person is the more important result.

The goal is $10 million per year because of who you have to become to achieve it.

# Step #1: Onboarding Form

Course: Onboarding (DO THIS FIRST)
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e25e3498?md=8418b93e5811456bb9e7114d5f5edc3e

---
You need to fill out the [Onboarding Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScoV-Pz1rI4eEs7-GwiUx6vQUQs4nYWKoF97XY4J_e_DuK64A/viewform). This should only take approximately 5–10 minutes and is important for matching, access, and understanding how we can help you.

There are a fair number of questions. Answer every one thoroughly and honestly. We are going to judge how seriously to take you, and how much effort we should put into helping you, based on how much effort you put into this form.

If you half-ass it, we will help you when we can. If you give complete and thoughtful answers, we will treat you like a serious person.

Everything you tell us will remain 100% confidential. We read every Onboarding Form in its entirety, and every submission is sent directly to our company Slack channel.

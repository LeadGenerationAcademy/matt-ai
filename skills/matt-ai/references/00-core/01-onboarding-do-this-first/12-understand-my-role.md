Obviously, I am Matthew Larsen writing this.

I want you to understand your role and my role.

My role is to teach you. Everything you need to know already is, and always has been, available on YouTube or inside my content for free. Inside this Skool, my job is to help you go faster.

If you are a winner, you would succeed with or without me. I will help you get there faster. If you have to ask whether you are a winner, you are not. It will be as obvious to you as it is to me.

If you are a loser, you are going to fail anyway. There is not much I can do. I expect you to churn shortly, and best of luck in your endeavors.

A good litmus test for whether you are a loser is whether reading this offends you. If you were not a loser, and did not already know it deep down, reading this would not provoke any reaction because you would not believe it was true.

(The good news is that a loser can become a winner real quick by taking the Personal Course seriously.)

If you are somewhere in the middle, I have provided the step-by-step lists, examples, templates, assets, and everything else you need to maximize your chances of success.

I highly recommend paying close attention to the Personal Course and possibly repeating it once per week.

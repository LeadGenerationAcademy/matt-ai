# Step #2: Opt In/Out To Weekly Intros

Course: Onboarding (DO THIS FIRST)
System: Core
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e25e3498?md=d97b28a3bb3e402e91c834e0a7b91825

---
We have a feature where we make one curated one-on-one introduction between members each week for networking and community purposes.

We do our best to match you with someone at approximately the same level as you based on revenue. These meetings always take place on Tuesdays.

You have to explicitly opt in and commit to attending. You will usually do this through the Onboarding Form, but you can also message Matt directly inside Skool.

After each meeting, we will send you a short form asking for approximately two minutes of feedback about the person you met. This helps us determine whether both people are taking the introductions seriously and putting effort into them.

If you opt in and miss a total of three meetings for any reason, you will be removed from this feature and permanently barred from rejoining.

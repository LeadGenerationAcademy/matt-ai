Do everything inside this Skool. It is all completely laid out for you.

The door is open. You just have to walk through it.

This is your business, not mine. If you expect me to run your entire business for you, I will send you a $400,000-per-year invoice, not the $500 per month you pay here.

Instead of turning this into some retarded motivational speech designed to remove all responsibility from the guru so he cannot be blamed, here is exactly what you are expected to do.

There should be no ambiguity.

1. **Block off the first four hours of your day for deep work.** Rearrange the rest of your schedule around those hours. Keep a timer on your desk and work toward completing the four hours more efficiently every day. I recommend dedicating at least one of those hours to working through this Skool.
2. **Schedule one hour of learning every day.** Do this later in the day. It does not matter whether you use videos, books, podcasts, articles, skill practice or something else. Just learn for one hour.
3. **Try to make everything you do 10 times better than the previous version.** Making a YouTube video? This one should be 10 times better. Will everything actually improve by 10 times? Probably not. Aiming for it will still produce a much better result than making the same thing repeatedly without trying to improve.
4. **Set your Top Five Goals and go deep during the exercise.** You will understand exactly what I mean when you work through the Personal Courses.
5. **Think, speak and act as if your goals are already done and already yours.** Once your goals are set, keep your behavior completely aligned with that belief.
6. **Run your business. Actually care about it.** Do not delegate shit. You are the owner, and it is your responsibility. Take pride in it, care about it and put tons of focused hours into it without making excuses about time, effort or anything else.

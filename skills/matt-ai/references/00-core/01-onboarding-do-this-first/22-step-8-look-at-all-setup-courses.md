By default, all of the Setup Courses are unlocked and immediately available.

There is a ton of work to do, and you have to do it.

Every course is clearly labeled based on the order in which it should be completed. I guess you could jump into whichever course you want, but I will always recommend completing them in the order they are laid out.

##### Course Updates

There is a Change Log pinned to the top of Skool.

I update these courses frequently, and I have two full-time employees working on improving this Skool. Every change is added to the Change Log each day with a direct link.

Go back through the courses regularly to see what has changed. I would especially revisit the Personal Courses once per week to check for updates and repeat the material.

##### Access To Systems Courses

The Systems Courses are unlocked separately. You will need to complete the required setup work before I manually give you access.

There are a few reasons for this.

More than 40 courses will overwhelm you. You need to follow one clear path instead of jumping randomly between everything.

The fundamentals and setup also come before the systems. You will not be able to use the systems properly without completing the required setup first.

I have also had problems with theft and piracy. People have joined, downloaded everything and then sold it or uploaded it to the internet for free. This is extremely annoying for me, and it ruins the value of what you paid to receive.

When you have completed the required setup for a specific Systems Course, message me to request access.

For example, if you want access to the Paid Ads System, complete the Paid Ads Setup Course first. Once everything is properly set up, message me to confirm that you have completed it, and I will manually add you.

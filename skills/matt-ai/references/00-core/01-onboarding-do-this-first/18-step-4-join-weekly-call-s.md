These are not Q&A calls. If you need feedback or have a question, post it inside Skool or Telegram.

The weekly calls are teaching calls covering information that I do not publish publicly on YouTube or anywhere else. Each call will be approximately one hour long and focused on one specific topic.

A lot of the time, I will explain the Theory first and then build the entire thing from scratch live to the best of my ability within the time available.

There are two weekly calls: a General Call and a $1M+ Call. Sometimes they will cover the same topic at different levels, and sometimes they will cover completely different topics.

The General Call will focus on broader concepts. The $1M+ Call will deal with more advanced and nuanced versions of those concepts.

For example, the General Call may cover how to get your advertisements working at all. The $1M+ Call may cover how to scale advertisements that are already working.

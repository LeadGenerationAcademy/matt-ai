We use both the Skool community and Telegram group chats. If you have a question, post it publicly inside Skool whenever possible so other members can see the answer and contribute.

Do not message Matt privately on Telegram or Skool with normal questions. His inboxes are flooded with random people trying to sell him stuff, which makes them almost unusable, and there is a 95% chance he will not see your message.

Before asking something privately, check Google, ChatGPT, or YouTube. Do not message him asking basic questions like, “How do I set up my pixel?”

Private messages should be reserved for high-level, nuanced business situations that you genuinely cannot discuss publicly. If something is extremely private, you can message Matt on Telegram, but be respectful of his time. Otherwise, post it publicly inside Skool.

We have a general Telegram group for everyone and a separate group for members doing more than $1 million per year. Members of the higher-level group can participate in both.

[Join the General Telegram Group](https://t.me/+u5FSTebIS_5mYmM5)

To join the $1M+ group, send Matt a screenshot showing at least $1 million in revenue over the previous 365 days.

We separate the groups because nothing kills a community faster than mixing people at completely different levels inside every conversation. Someone doing $1 million, $10 million, or more has different problems from someone getting started. Once you reach those levels, you will understand exactly why this matters.

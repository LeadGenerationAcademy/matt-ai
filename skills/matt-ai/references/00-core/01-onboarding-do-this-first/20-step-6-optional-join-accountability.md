We like accountability around here.

We use the Work Form System covered in the Personal Courses. There is also a thread pinned to the top of Skool where you can post your results.

Some things you can post include:

- Your wake-up time
- Your completion time for the first hour of deep work
- Your completion time for each additional hour
- Your total time and efficiency for completing four hours
- How many hours you completed that day

You can post your accountability updates inside Skool or Telegram.

Do not be shy. I do this myself, and you will see me posting mine too.

Seeing other people complete their work motivates everyone in the group. You are doing yourself a favor, but you are also helping everybody else.

Let's get it.

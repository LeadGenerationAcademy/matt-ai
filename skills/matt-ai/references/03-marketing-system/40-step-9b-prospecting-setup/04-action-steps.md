The Theory explains that Dream 100 prospecting is manual outreach to 100 specific people you want to work with, and that no matter how big you get, nothing keeps your pipeline full quite like it.

This course is where you set up your Dream 100 prospecting.

You will build your Dream 100 List, write the opening message you send to everyone on it, and hand the sending and the inbox to a VA or someone on your team.

### Table of Contents

1. **Build Dream 100 List:** Put 100 specific people you want as clients into one spreadsheet with their contact details and profiles, then engage with their posts so you are on their radar before you ever message them.
2. **Create Dream 100 Copy:** Write a short opening message that says who you help and the result, names the clients you have worked with, and offers to send your One-Pager, so you can pitch them honestly and open a conversation at the same time.
3. **Delegate Inbox Management:** Hand the sending and the replies to a VA or someone on your team, since this is a $10/hour task, and add inbox managers in other timezones as you grow.

Prospecting is a different thing from cold email.

Cold email is volume. You blast a list and see who replies. Prospecting is manual. You, or someone on your team, reach out to specific people one at a time, and when a conversation gets serious, you take over.

I still do this every day. No matter how big you get or how successful you are, nothing keeps your pipeline full quite like Dream 100 prospecting.

Is it low leverage? Maybe. It doesn't matter if something isn't scalable. This is your shield against good and bad months.

As you grow, you keep doing it. You just move your Dream 100 further and further upmarket, the same way you would with getting clients in general.

### Your Dream 100 Are Real People

Your Dream 100 is the 100 people you would love to work with. Actual people or companies, with actual names.

Your ICP describes a type of person. This list is 100 specific names you personally want as clients.

For each one, you find their name, email, phone, website, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Facebook, and all of it goes into one spreadsheet.

Understand who is on this list. These people have in no way expressed interest in you, and most of them probably don't need your service right now. That's fine. The goal is to be the person they remember when they do.

Even the ones who never hire you are worth the effort. People know people who are like themselves. Someone on your list who never buys can still refer you to someone who does.

### Engage First, Message Second

Get on their radar before you ever message them. Like their posts, comment on them, then message them and get to know them as a real person.

Make the comments thoughtful. Every person on Earth can tell the difference between a comment someone actually thought about and one they didn't.

Every time they see your name, you get a little more familiar. By the time your message lands, they already recognize who you are.

It helps a lot if your profiles are set up properly and you post content. The more likely they are to have seen you, your ads, or your content, the more likely they are to reply, and to reply in a positive way.

Spend 30 to 60 minutes a day working through the list. This is a long game. What you do in the next 30 days usually shows up in your results around 90 days later, so the start can feel like nothing is happening. Keep going.

### The Message Is Simple On Purpose

The first message says who you help and the result they get, names the clients you've worked with, and offers to send your One-Pager.

"Hey [First Name], we help [Niche] achieve [Result]. We have worked with [X, Y, Z]. I can send over a one pager if you're interested."

Yes, you are pitching them. You are just doing it honestly.

The worst version of this is the greasy one. Someone messages me, "Hey Matt, love your YouTube channel." I say thanks. Then they come back with, "By the way, I do thumbnails and edits, do you want a trial?"

That is so disingenuous. They are now the last person on Earth I would hire to work on my channel.

If you want to be friendlier, open with something like "Hey Matt, big fan," and close with "if not, keep it up." Just never hide the pitch behind a fake compliment.

For the clients you name, use ones at the same level as the person you're messaging. The only thing you ask for is permission to send the One-Pager.

Send it manually on the platform you're most dominant on, or the one your research says they prefer. Done this way, the same message pitches them and opens a conversation you can build a relationship from. It might not look like much written out. It is a very strong way to do it.

### Hand Off The Sending

Sending the initial messages is a $5 or $10 an hour task. Your time is worth $500 or $1,000 an hour. Give the sending and the inbox to a VA or someone on your team.

The messages still go out from your Instagram, your LinkedIn, and your Twitter, so you see every reply yourself. There is no complex system to build here. When a conversation gets serious, you step in.

Make sure whoever is sending uses a VPN that roughly matches your location, at least your state or city. Otherwise you might get your account restricted, and that is happening more and more.

As you grow, add inbox managers in other timezones. One in South America, one in South Africa, and one in the Philippines gets you coverage 24/7.

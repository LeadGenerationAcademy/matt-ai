Inbox management is a $10/hour task and not something you should be doing.

Give it to a VA or someone on your team.

As you grow, you can even have inbox managers in different timezones, one in South America, one in South Africa, one in the Philippines, so you have 24/7 coverage.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Assign a VA or someone on your team to manage the inbox.** Sending the initial messages is a $5 or $10 an hour task, and your time is worth $500 or $1,000 an hour. Either a VA or someone already on your team works, as long as you are not the one doing it.
2. **Have them take over sending the messages and managing responses.** They send your Dream 100 Copy to the people on your Dream 100 List from your own Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter. Since everything goes out from your accounts, you can see every reply yourself without building any complex system around it.
3. **Make sure they use a VPN that matches your location.** It should match at least your state or your city. If it doesn't, your account might get restricted, and that is happening more and more.
4. **Take over the conversation yourself when it gets serious.** They handle the initial messages and the back and forth that follows. Once a conversation turns serious, you step in and take it from there.
5. **Add inbox managers in other timezones as you grow.** Having one in South America, one in South Africa, and one in the Philippines is what gets you to 24/7 coverage.

### Completion Audit

- Is a VA or someone on your team assigned to manage the inbox?
- Are they sending the messages and managing responses?
- Are they using a VPN that matches your location?
- Are you taking over the conversation yourself when it gets serious?
- Are you adding inbox managers in other timezones as you grow?

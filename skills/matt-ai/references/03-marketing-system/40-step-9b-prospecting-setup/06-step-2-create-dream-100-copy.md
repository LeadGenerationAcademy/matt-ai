### Framework & Criteria

"Hey [First Name], we help [Niche] achieve [Result]

We have worked with [X, Y, Z].

I can send over a one pager if you're interested."

### Example

Hey John, we help agency owners generate more leads.

We have worked with Northpoint Digital, Southpoint Media, and Eastpoint Agency.

I can send over a one pager if you're interested.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Dream 100 Copy Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MoC-sAdanGvtM6xnY7JSHY2mee4niAP-DcPQYyac1jM/edit?usp=sharing).** The format sits at the top of the document and your finished message goes in the Write Your Dream 100 Copy section underneath it. [in this skill: assets/templates/dream-100-copy-template/]
2. **Write the line saying who you help and what result you get them.** This is your Offer One-Liner. Who you help and what result they get, nothing more.
3. **Add the clients you have worked with.** Three names. This is the proof line, so use clients at the same level as the person you are messaging.
4. **Add the offer to send your One-Pager.** This is the One-Pager from the Offers module, the one that fits inside a single iPhone screenshot. The ask is for permission to send it, not for a call.

### Completion Audit

- Did you make a copy of the Dream 100 Copy Template?
- Did you write the line saying who you help and what result you get them?
- Did you write the clients you have worked with?
- Did you write the offer to send your One-Pager?

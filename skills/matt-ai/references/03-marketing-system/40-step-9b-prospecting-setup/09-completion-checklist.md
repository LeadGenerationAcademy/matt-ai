Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Dream 100 List

- You have made a copy of the Dream 100 List Template.
- You have added 100 specific prospects who fit your service, with their Name, Email, Phone, Website, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Facebook.
- You have started engaging with their posts through likes and comments, and messaging them to build a relationship.
- You are spending 30 to 60 minutes a day working through your list.

### Dream 100 Copy

- You have made a copy of the Dream 100 Copy Template.
- You have written the line saying who you help and what result you get them.
- You have added three clients you have worked with, at the same level as the person you are messaging.
- You have added the offer to send your One-Pager.

### Inbox Management

- You have assigned a VA or someone on your team to manage the inbox.
- You have had them take over sending outreach and managing responses.
- You have had them pass every reply to whoever is doing the calling.
- You are adding inbox managers in other timezones as you grow.

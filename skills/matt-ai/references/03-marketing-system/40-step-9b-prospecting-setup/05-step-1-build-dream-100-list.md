Course: Step #9B: Prospecting Setup

---
Your dream 100 list is a list of people you're going to do manual prospecting to, not cold email spam. These are specific prospects you actually want to work with, people you research and build real relationships with over time through personalized outreach.

You're going to do this on LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, email, and wherever it works.

They may not need your service right away, the goal is to be the person they remember when they do.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Dream 100 List Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1LIQV55BkuhGvXr0FCrWDwzd--Hb_d8xBCLNCdjzCe7g/edit?gid=131227563#gid=131227563).** This is your working copy, everything you fill in stays on your own sheet. [in this skill: assets/templates/dream-100-list-template/]
2. **Identify 100 specific prospects who fit your service and add their Name, Email, Phone, Website, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Facebook to your template.** This is a manual selection process, you're picking real people you'd actually want as clients, not pulling a scraped list, and you're building out their full profile in one pass rather than working column by column.
3. **Start engaging with their posts, likes and comments, and message them to start building a relationship.** Engaging first is how you get on their radar before you ever message them directly, then get to know them as a real person, this isn't a pitch.
4. **Spend 30 to 60 minutes a day working through your list.** Consistency matters more than intensity here, this is a long game, not a one-time blast.

### Completion Audit

- Did you make a copy of the Dream 100 List template?
- Did you identify 100 specific prospects who fit your service and add their Name, Email, Phone, Website, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Facebook to your template?
- Did you start engaging with their posts, likes and comments, and message them to start building a relationship?
- Are you spending 30 to 60 minutes a day working through your list?

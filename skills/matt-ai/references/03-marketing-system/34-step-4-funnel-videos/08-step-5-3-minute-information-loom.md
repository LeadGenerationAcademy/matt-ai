# Step #5: 3-Minute Information Loom

Course: Step #4: Funnel Videos
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/7c941b44?md=d1021fdff9e648979fb762d4ee8ac133

---
The 3-Minute Information Loom goes with your one-pager.

This is not another VSL or some overproduced presentation. Open the one-pager, share your screen, turn your camera on, and naturally talk the prospect through it.

This will be sent after someone applies and again after they book a call. Keep it simple and keep it under three minutes.

### Framework & Criteria

1. Share the one-pager on your screen.
2. Keep your face visible.
3. Talk through the one-pager naturally.
4. Keep the entire Loom under three minutes.
5. Put the One-Pager and Calendly links inside the Loom.
6. Put the Loom and Calendly links inside the one-pager.
7. Put the Loom and One-Pager links inside Calendly.
8. Send the Loom after someone applies and after someone books.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Finish your one-pager before recording the Loom.** The Loom exists to talk someone through the one-pager. If the one-pager is incomplete, the Loom will also be incomplete. Finish the document first.
2. **Open your one-pager on your computer.** Have the final version open and ready before recording. Remove anything from your screen that the prospect should not see.
3. **Start a Loom screen recording of the one-pager.** This needs to be recorded in Loom format. The prospect should be able to watch you move through the actual one-pager while you explain it.
4. **Turn your camera on and keep your face visible.** Do not record a faceless screen share. Half the point is letting the prospect see you and hear you before the call.
5. **Talk through the one-pager naturally from beginning to end.** Do not read every word from the page. Explain what they are looking at as if you were personally walking one prospect through it.
6. **Keep the Loom under three minutes.** This is called the 3-Minute Information Loom for a reason. Get to the point and explain the important information quickly.
7. **Re-record the Loom if it is longer than three minutes.** Do not excuse a five-minute recording because every section feels important. Remove the rambling and record it again.
8. **Add the One-Pager link inside the Loom.** Use the Loom settings to place the direct One-Pager link somewhere the prospect can easily find and click.
9. **Add the Calendly link inside the Loom.** Use the Loom settings to include the direct Calendly link so someone can book a call immediately after watching.
10. **Add the Loom link inside the one-pager.** Someone who opens the one-pager should be able to watch the Loom without searching through another email or text message.
11. **Add the Calendly link inside the one-pager.** Someone who reads the one-pager should also be able to book a call directly from it.
12. **Add the Loom and One-Pager links inside Calendly.** Someone who reaches Calendly should still be able to access both assets. No matter which one they click first, every other link should be available.
13. **Include the Loom when someone submits an application.** Add it to the confirmation message sent after the application so the prospect can understand the offer before deciding whether to book.
14. **Include the Loom when someone books a call.** Add it to the call-booked confirmation message so the prospect can review the offer before the scheduled call.
15. **Test every link from the Loom, one-pager, and Calendly.** Open each asset and click every link. The Loom, one-pager, and Calendly should all connect to one another without any broken links or dead ends.
16. **Watch the final Loom on your phone before using it.** Confirm that the one-pager is readable, your face is visible, the audio is clear, the video stays under three minutes, and every link works.

### Completion Audit

- Is the one-pager completely finished?
- Does the Loom show the actual one-pager?
- Is your face visible throughout the Loom?
- Do you talk through the one-pager naturally?
- Is the Loom less than three minutes long?
- Does the Loom include the One-Pager link?
- Does the Loom include the Calendly link?
- Does the one-pager include the Loom link?
- Does the one-pager include the Calendly link?
- Does Calendly include the Loom and One-Pager links?
- Is the Loom included after someone applies?
- Is the Loom included after someone books a call?
- Do all links work in every direction?
- Did you watch and test the final Loom on your phone?

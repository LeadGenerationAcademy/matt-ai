You are going to create a library of short videos answering the 30 to 50 questions people ask before buying from you.

These are not content videos. You are not trying to hook people, entertain them, or cram five ideas into one video.

Read the question. Answer the question. Stop talking.

These videos will be posted everywhere, organized on your website, and run as retargeting ads to people who submitted an application or booked a call.

### Framework & Criteria

- Brainstorm up to 100 FAQs. Do as many as possible, but don't randomly make shit up. It is best to pull all of these from your call recordings, even if they only show up a few times.
- Put the question at the top of the video
- Film yourself answering the question
- This should be 60 seconds or less

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Brainstorm every question prospects ask before buying from you.** Write down every question you can remember prospects asking before they bought, booked, or decided not to move forward. Do not judge the questions yet. Get everything onto the page.
2. **Separate the questions into first-order questions, second-order questions, expectations, and objections.** First-order questions are the obvious questions someone asks when they first discovers your Offer. Second-order questions come after they understand the basics and want more detail. Expectations explain what will happen, how the process works, and what they should expect. Objections are the reasons they may hesitate, delay, or refuse to buy.
3. **Use ChatGPT or another AI tool to help expand the list to [30 to 50 questions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.ykft664sm5qp#heading=h.n9w00zb5gra8).** Give the AI your existing questions and the four categories. Ask it what important questions are missing. Do not let it replace your actual knowledge of your customers. Use it to expand the list, not invent a completely different sales process. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
4. **Remove duplicate questions from the final list.** Several questions may say basically the same thing using different words. Combine them into one clear question unless the difference is important enough to justify separate videos.
5. **Write one clear question for each video.** Each video should answer one question. Do not combine several questions into one giant video because you were too lazy to record them separately.
6. **Choose the setting where you will record the videos.** Pick a clean and professional location that you can use repeatedly. The setting should look intentional and remain consistent across the entire library.
7. **Face a window for natural light if you do not have a full studio.** The production value does not need to be insane, but it cannot look like complete shit. Facing a window will usually give you acceptable lighting without requiring a studio.
8. **Put your phone in position and make sure your face is clearly visible.** Record vertically from your phone. Frame yourself properly, look into the camera, and make sure the prospect can clearly see and hear you.
9. **Make A Copy of the [Retargeting Ads Canva template](https://canva.link/7c9t4rkkjcvkvnd) and put the exact question at the top of each video.** The prospect should understand what the video answers before pressing play. Keep the question visible and easy to read.
10. **Put the exact question on the cover photo for each video.** Someone looking through your profile, playlist, or website should be able to find the exact question they care about without watching every video.
11. **Read the question out loud at the beginning of each video.** Start by reading the question exactly as it appears. Do not waste the first 15 seconds with a hook, introduction, or explanation of who you are.
12. **Answer only that question.** Get to the point. Do not introduce three unrelated ideas, promote your entire company, or turn every answer into a motivational speech. Answer the fucking question.
13. **Keep every answer under 60 seconds.** If the answer takes longer than 60 seconds, simplify it or record it again. These videos should be extremely easy to consume.
14. **Include proof or a short customer story when possible.** If you can answer the question using a real result or a short story about how you did this for someone, use it. You do not need edits or graphics. Just explain the proof using your words.
15. **Record all 30 to 50 videos using the same setting.** Using the same setting makes the videos look like one complete library instead of a random collection of clips recorded over several years.
16. **Re-record the videos in additional settings if you want multiple versions.** You can create more versions later using different locations, clothing, or backgrounds. The question and answer can remain the same while the creative looks different.
17. **Post every video to your company Instagram.** Upload the complete FAQ library to the company Instagram using the question as the cover and topic.
18. **Post every video to your company LinkedIn.** Upload the same FAQ videos to the company LinkedIn so prospects researching the company can find the answers there.
19. **Post every video to your company YouTube.** Upload the videos to the company YouTube channel and keep every question clearly labelled.
20. **Post every video to your company Facebook.** Upload the videos to the company Facebook page so people who click through from your Meta ads can find useful information.
21. **Post every video to your company X/Twitter.** Upload the videos to the company X/Twitter profile using the question as the main topic of the post.
22. **Organize the videos into playlists.** Group the videos so prospects can quickly find first-order questions, second-order questions, expectations, and objections.
23. **Create a page on your website containing every video.** Build one page where someone can access the complete FAQ video library without searching through five different social platforms.
24. **Clearly label every video on the website using its question.** The website page should be easy to scan. Prospects should be able to find the exact question they care about and watch the answer immediately.
25. **Run the videos as retargeting ads to people who submitted an application.** Use the FAQ videos to continue educating people who applied but have not booked or purchased.
26. **Run the videos as retargeting ads to people who booked a call.** Show the videos to booked prospects so their questions and objections are answered before they speak to your sales team.
27. **Spend approximately $5 to $20 per day on the retargeting campaign.** This does not require a huge budget. The audience is small and specific. The goal is to repeatedly show useful answers to the people closest to buying.

The Theory explains that video builds trust faster than text because it lets a prospect see your face and hear your voice, and that it's high leverage since one recording can educate and pre-sell hundreds of people before they ever talk to you.

This module is where you actually build that video library.

You will script and record your Sales Page VSL, your Thank You Page Main VSL, a reusable Pre-Call Selfie Video, a set of Thank You Page FAQ videos, a Loom walking prospects through your One-Pager, and a full retargeting library answering every question people ask before buying.

Do not get stuck perfecting the editing or production on any of these. Worry about pacing, content, and clarity instead, that's what actually makes them work.

### Table of Contents

1. **VSL:** Script and record a VSL for your sales page that gets your ICP, Offer, and proof across within seconds and answers your major objections.
2. **Thank You Page Main VSL:** Record a two-to-five-minute video for your Thank You Page that answers your top FAQs and explains what happens next.
3. **Pre-Call Selfie Video:** Send every booked prospect a short, reusable selfie video that puts a face to your name before the call.
4. **Thank You Page FAQ Videos:** Record and publish 3-5 videos answering your biggest sales call objections before the call happens.
5. **3-Minute Information Loom:** Record a Loom under three minutes walking a prospect through your One-Pager, cross-linked with Calendly.
6. **Retargeting/FAQ Videos:** Build a library of 30-50 short videos answering every question prospects ask before buying, and run them as retargeting ads.

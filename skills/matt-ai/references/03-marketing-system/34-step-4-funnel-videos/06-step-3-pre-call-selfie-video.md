The Selfie Video puts a face to the name before the Sales Call.

It should feel like you recorded it specifically for that prospect, even though you use the same video for everyone.

### Framework & Criteria

- 60 seconds or less
- Film in a way that is could plausibly be custom-filmed for them
- This should be paired with the appointment setting reach out in the next step

**Use This Template**

“Hey, it is [Your Name] from [Company].

I saw that you booked. That is great.

I am sending you a follow-up text with a few options, but other than that, I just wanted to put a face to the name.

I will talk to you soon, and we will see if what we offer is right for you.”

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Record the Selfie Video on your phone.** This should look and feel like a normal video you recorded directly for one person. Do not record it inside a studio or turn it into a polished advertisement.
2. **Film it like you are speaking directly to one person.** Look into the camera and speak naturally. The prospect should feel like you recorded the video after seeing that they booked.
3. **Keep the video natural and unedited.** Do not add complicated editing, slides, music, captions, or production. A polished video works against the entire point of this step, which is to make the interaction feel personal.
4. **Introduce yourself and your company.** Start by clearly saying your name and the name of your company so the prospect immediately knows who is speaking.
5. **Acknowledge that they booked.** Tell them that you saw they booked a call. Keep it generic enough that the same sentence works for every prospect.
6. **Tell them to expect a follow-up text.** Let them know that you are also sending a text with additional information or options so they know to look for it.
7. **Set a simple expectation for the call.** Tell them that you will speak soon and determine whether what you offer is right for them. Do not make promises or turn the video into a sales pitch.
8. **Remove any mention of the prospect’s name.** Do not say their name anywhere in the video. The moment you include it, the video can no longer be reused.
9. **Remove any mention of the prospect’s company.** Do not mention a specific business, Industry, situation, or other detail that only applies to one prospect.
10. **Remove any mention of the call date.** Do not say today, tomorrow, Tuesday, or include a specific date or time. The video needs to work regardless of when the call is booked.
11. **Save the video somewhere easy to find on your phone.** Put it inside your favorites, a dedicated album, or another location you can access within seconds. Do not bury it inside thousands of other videos.
12. **Create a call-booked SMS notification in Zapier.** The automation should trigger whenever someone books a call and send an SMS directly to your phone. The message should tell you who booked and remind you to send the Selfie Video.
13. **Send the Selfie Video manually whenever someone books.** When the SMS arrives, open the prospect’s contact information, find the saved video, and send it directly from your phone. Do not automate the video message itself. It should appear to come directly from you.
14. **Test that the SMS notification arrives correctly.** Book a test call yourself and confirm that the notification arrives immediately, identifies who booked, and clearly reminds you to send the video. Do not launch this until the notification fires reliably.
15. **Test that you can find and send the video quickly.** Use the test booking to confirm that the video is easy to find and can be sent from your phone within seconds. If the process is annoying, you will stop doing it.

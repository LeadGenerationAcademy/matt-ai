A VSL is a prerecorded, one-sided sales call that works for you 24 hours per day.

It lets prospects see your face, hear your voice, understand your Offer, review your proof, and have their questions and objections answered before they ever speak to anyone.

Do not try to hook them again. They already saw your advertisement, clicked it, and read the headline. Get to the point.

Also, do NOT worry too much about editing, production value. Worry about pacing, content and clarity.

### Framework & Criteria

- Congruent with the advertisement and sales page headline
- ICP and Offer clear within one second
- First form of proof shown within five seconds
- Second form of proof shown within 15 seconds
- Entire Offer explained within 15 seconds
- Every important FAQ and objection answered
- Face visible immediately
- Clear, professional audio
- Accurate subtitles below the chin
- Length matched to the service and ICP
- Clear thumbnail
- Funnel still works without watching the VSL

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Review the advertisement, sales page headline, and Offer before writing the VSL.** The VSL needs to continue the same conversation. The ICP, problem, promised result, and Offer should remain congruent from the advertisement to the headline and then into the VSL.
2. **Write an opening that makes the ICP and Offer clear within one second.** The right prospect should know immediately that the video is for them and what you are offering. Do not make them wait through a story or vague introduction before understanding why they are watching.
3. **Get directly into the Offer instead of trying to hook the prospect again.** They already saw your advertisement, clicked it, and read the headline. You already have their attention. Trying to manufacture another hook wastes time and can create a disconnect. Get to the point.
4. **Plan your first form of proof within the first five seconds.** Show a result, screenshot, testimonial, case study, logo, or another relevant form of proof almost immediately. Do not spend the opening making unsupported claims about yourself.
5. **Plan a second form of proof within the first 15 seconds.** One piece of proof can be ignored as an exception. A second form begins establishing a pattern and makes the Offer more believable.
6. **Explain the entire Offer within the first 15 seconds.** The prospect should understand who the Offer is for, what result it produces, and what you actually do. You can explain the details later, but the complete core Offer needs to be clear immediately.
7. **Write the rest of the VSL to answer every important FAQ and objection while continuing to show proof.** Go through the questions and objections that prevent prospects from moving forward. Answer each one directly and support the answer with proof whenever possible. You could create a great VSL by doing nothing except showing proof and answering the right questions.
8. **Match the length of the VSL to the service and ICP.** More complicated Offers may require more explanation. Higher-level people generally have less time and are less likely to watch a long VSL. Do not make the video longer than the Offer and ICP require.
9. **Record the VSL with your face visible within the first second.** Half of the value of a VSL comes from letting the prospect see your face and hear your voice. You should look serious, professional, and appropriate for the ICP you are selling to.
10. **Record with clear, professional audio.** The video does not need to look like a television commercial, but the prospect needs to hear and understand you without struggling.
11. **Add accurate subtitles below your chin.** A lot of people will play the video without sound. Keep the subtitles below your chin so they can read them without covering your face.
12. **Create a clear thumbnail for the VSL.** The thumbnail should make the video look intentional and worth playing. It should also remain congruent with the advertisement, headline, branding, and Offer.
13. **Upload the VSL to your video hosting platform.** Make sure the video loads properly, plays on desktop and mobile, and does not require the prospect to leave the sales page.
14. **Place the VSL above the fold on your sales page.** The prospect should be able to see and play it immediately without scrolling.
15. **Confirm that the prospect can still continue through the funnel without watching the VSL.** The sales page needs to explain and sell the Offer on its own. Watching the VSL should help, but it should never be required to understand the Offer or take the next step.

### Completion Audit

- Is the VSL congruent with the advertisement and sales page headline?
- Are the ICP and Offer clear within the first second?
- Does the VSL get directly to the point without adding another unnecessary hook?
- Is the first form of proof shown within five seconds?
- Is a second form of proof shown within 15 seconds?
- Is the entire Offer explained within 15 seconds?
- Does the VSL answer every important FAQ and objection?
- Does it continue showing proof throughout the video?

- Does the VSL have a clear thumbnail?

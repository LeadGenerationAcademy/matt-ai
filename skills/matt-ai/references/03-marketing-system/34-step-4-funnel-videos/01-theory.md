Using video at multiple points in your funnel lets prospects see your face and hear your voice, and that builds trust far faster than being just another anonymous business on the internet.

Video also just works better for a lot of people. Some prefer to read, some prefer to watch, so you want both available on every page.

Video is also high leverage. You record it once and hundreds or thousands of people can watch it.

The more videos you have, the clearer they are, and the more of them a prospect watches, the more educated and pre-sold they will already be by the time they actually talk to you.

Do not get stuck perfecting the editing or production on any of these. Worry about pacing, content, and clarity instead, because that is what actually makes them work.

Set these up in an intelligent order where someone can actually consume them, and you will see a real difference in the quality of your sales calls.

### Sales Page VSL

A VSL is a prerecorded, one-sided sales call that works for you 24 hours a day. It lets a prospect see your face, hear your voice, understand your Offer, review your proof, and get their objections answered before they ever talk to anyone.

It has to stay congruent with the ad they clicked and the headline they just read. Do not try to hook them again here, because they already saw your ad, clicked it, and read the headline, so get straight to the point instead of re-selling the click.

The opening is tight. Your ICP and your Offer need to be clear within the first second, your proof shows up somewhere between 5 and 15 seconds, and the entire Offer is explained by the 15 second mark. Everything after that is FAQs and objections.

The production bar is simple. Your face needs to be visible, the audio needs to be clean, and the subtitles need to be accurate and sitting below your chin.

It goes above the fold on the sales page with a clear thumbnail, and the funnel still has to work for someone who never presses play.

### Thank You Page Main VSL

The Main VSL is the first video someone sees right after they book a call or complete your main conversion step. It runs two to five minutes and sits above the fold on the Thank You Page.

Its job is to answer the 6 to 12 biggest questions and objections as fast as possible, explain exactly how the call or the process works, and then point them toward whatever steps are left below the video.

### Pre-Call Selfie Video

The Selfie Video puts a face to your name before the call happens. It should feel like you recorded it specifically for that one prospect, even though the same video gets reused for everyone, which is exactly why it can never mention their name, their company, or the call date.

Keep it short and natural. You introduce yourself and your company, and that is the whole video.

After that the only thing that matters is speed. It should live somewhere on your phone you can find instantly, and a notification should tell you the moment a call gets booked, so it goes out within seconds instead of the next morning.

### Thank You Page FAQ Videos

These take the 3 to 5 biggest objections you actually hear on sales calls and answer each one in its own short video, placed on your sales Thank You page. Pull them from your real call recordings, or from what you already know intuitively are the top objections.

Record each one with your face on one side and your screen share on the other, and give every video a thumbnail showing your face alongside the question in bold text, so someone scanning the page can find the exact doubt they have.

Someone's biggest doubts should already be handled before they ever get on the call with you or your closer.

### 3-Minute Information Loom

This one goes with your One-Pager, so the One-Pager has to be finished before you record anything.

It is not another VSL or a polished presentation. It is you opening the One-Pager, sharing your screen, turning your camera on, and talking a prospect through it naturally, the way you would in person. Keep the whole thing under three minutes.

It goes out in the application confirmation and again in the call-booked confirmation. The Loom, the One-Pager, and your Calendly all link to each other in every direction, so wherever a prospect lands they can get to the other two.

### Retargeting/FAQ Video Library

This is a library of short videos answering the 30 to 50 questions people actually ask before they buy from you. Split those questions into four buckets: first-order questions, second-order questions, expectations, and objections. That is what keeps the library from turning into 40 versions of the same three questions.

These are not content videos. You are not hooking anyone and you are not entertaining anyone. You read the question out loud, answer it in under 60 seconds, and stop talking, working in proof or a short story wherever you can.

The question goes in the video and on the cover photo, because nobody is going to hunt for the answer they need.

They get posted across Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, and X, then organized into playlists and a labeled page on your site.

The same library becomes a retargeting audience. Run it at roughly $5 to $20 a day against anyone who applied or booked a call but hasn't bought yet.

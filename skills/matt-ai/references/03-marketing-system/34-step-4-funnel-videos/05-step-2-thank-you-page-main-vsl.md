The Main VSL is the first video a prospect sees after booking a call or completing the main conversion step.

The purpose is to prepare them for what happens next, answer their biggest questions as quickly as possible, and tell them to complete the remaining steps below the video.

### Framework & Criteria

- Two to five minutes long
- Face visible
- Six to 12 FAQs answered
- Answers delivered as quickly as possible
- Call or process clearly explained
- Remaining steps below the video clearly introduced
- Thumbnail that says "WATCH THIS" and "2 MINUTE VIDEO" with your face

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Identify the six to 12 biggest FAQs and objections prospects have after booking.** Use the questions that come up most often during the sales process. These are the things prospects still need to understand or believe before they feel ready for the call.
2. **Write the shortest complete answer to each FAQ and objection.** Get through the answers as quickly as possible without leaving out anything important. Do not turn every answer into a five-minute explanation. Answer the question and move on.
3. **Write an opening confirming what the prospect has just done and what the video will explain.** Immediately acknowledge that they booked the call or completed the previous step. Tell them that the video will explain what happens next and answer the most common questions.
4. **Explain exactly how the upcoming call or process will work.** Tell the prospect what will happen, what will be discussed, what they should expect, and anything they need to prepare. Remove as much uncertainty as possible before the call begins.
5. **Tell the prospect to complete the remaining steps below the video.** Make it clear that the video is not the final step. Direct them to scroll down and complete the remaining instructions on the Thank You Page.
6. **Combine everything into a script that is two to five minutes long.** The video needs to be complete, but it also needs to move quickly. If it takes longer than five minutes, your answers are probably too long or you are including information that belongs elsewhere.
7. **Record the VSL with your face visible.** The prospect should be able to see the person or company they are about to speak with. Do not hide behind slides for the entire video.
8. **Record with clear, professional audio.** The production does not need to be complicated, but the prospect needs to hear and understand every word without struggling.
9. **Upload the VSL to your video hosting platform.** Confirm that the video loads quickly and plays properly on both desktop and mobile.
10. **Place the Main VSL above the fold on your Thank You Page and test it.** The prospect should see the video immediately after reaching the page. Test the entire page yourself and confirm that the video works and the steps below it are easy to find.

### Completion Audit

- Is the Main VSL between two and five minutes long?
- Is your face visible?
- Does the opening confirm what the prospect has just done?
- Does the VSL answer the six to 12 biggest FAQs and objections?
- Are the answers delivered as quickly as possible?
- Does the VSL clearly explain how the call or process will work?
- Does it tell the prospect what to expect and prepare?
- Does it direct the prospect toward the remaining steps below the video?
- Is the audio clear and professional?
- Is the video uploaded and working properly?
- Is the Main VSL placed above the fold?
- Does the video work on desktop and mobile?

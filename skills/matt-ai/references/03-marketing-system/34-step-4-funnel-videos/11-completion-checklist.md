Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Sales Page VSL

- VSL scripted to be congruent with your advertisement and sales page headline
- ICP and Offer made clear within the first second, proof shown within 5 and 15 seconds, and the entire Offer explained within 15 seconds
- Got straight to the Offer without adding another hook
- Every important FAQ and objection answered, with proof shown throughout
- Length matched to the service and ICP
- Recorded with your face visible within the first second, clear audio, and accurate subtitles below your chin
- Clear thumbnail created
- VSL uploaded and placed above the fold on the sales page
- Confirmed the funnel still works without watching the VSL

### Thank You Page Main VSL

- Six to 12 biggest FAQs and objections identified and answered as quickly as possible
- Opening confirms what the prospect just did and what the video will explain
- Explained exactly how the call or process works, what to expect, and what to prepare
- Directed the prospect to the remaining steps below the video
- Recorded as a 2-5 minute video with your face visible and clear audio
- Uploaded, placed above the fold on the Thank You Page, and tested on desktop and mobile

### Pre-Call Selfie Video

- Recorded a short, natural, unedited Selfie Video on your phone introducing yourself and your company
- Acknowledged that they booked, told them to expect a follow-up text, and set a simple expectation for the call
- Confirmed the video never mentions the prospect's name, company, or call date
- Saved somewhere easy to find on your phone
- Call-booked SMS notification set up in Zapier and tested
- Sending the video manually every time someone books
- Confirmed you can find and send the video within seconds of the notification

### Thank You Page FAQ Videos

- 3-5 biggest FAQs/objections pulled from real sales calls or known intuitively
- Short answer written for each question before recording
- One 2-5 minute video recorded for each question, with your face on one side and your screen share on the other
- Thumbnail template built in Canva with your face and the question as bold text, one per video
- Videos uploaded to a hosting platform and embedded in their placeholders on the sales Thank You page

### 3-Minute Information Loom

- One-Pager finished before recording
- Loom recorded under three minutes, face visible, walking through the One-Pager naturally
- Loom, One-Pager, and Calendly links cross-linked in all three directions
- Loom included in the application confirmation and the call-booked confirmation
- All links tested and the final Loom watched on your phone

### Retargeting/FAQ Video Library

- 30-50 questions brainstormed and separated into first-order, second-order, expectations, and objections
- Missing questions found with AI and duplicates removed
- All videos recorded in the same clean setting, with your face well lit and clearly visible
- One short video recorded per question, question read aloud and answered in under 60 seconds, proof or a short story included when possible
- Question visible in the video and on the cover photo
- Videos posted across Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, and X/Twitter
- Videos organized into playlists and a labeled page on your website
- Videos running as retargeting ads to applicants and booked calls, at roughly $5-$20/day

We want to take the 3-5 main FAQs and objections that you hear on sales calls and record 2-5 minute videos addressing them. We're going to display those on our sales Thank You page. We have placeholders for those already built in our Webflow project.

To me, I would go by your call recordings, or if you already know intuitively what the top 3-5 FAQs or objections are, you can just use those.

We also want a thumbnail for each video that shows your face and puts the question in there as the headline, big and bold at the top of the thumbnail.

Your video itself can be as simple as sharing your screen while you record. Usually, you're going to go on the left side of your screen, and the right side of your screen is going to be your screen share. Like my YouTube videos, if you've ever watched them.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Count:** 3-5 videos, each covering a major question/objection.
2. **Length:** 2-5 minutes each. Long enough to fully answer the question, short enough that a busy prospect will actually watch it.
3. **Selection:** Pull the questions from your actual sales call recordings or you may already know them.
4. **Format:** You on one side of the screen, your screen share on the right.
5. **Thumbnail:** Your face, with the exact question written as a big, bold headline at the top.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Pull your 3-5 biggest FAQs and objections from your sales call recordings.** These need to be the questions that actually come up before someone buys, not just questions you think sound good.
2. **If you already know your top 3-5 objections intuitively, skip the recordings and list them directly.** You don't need to sit through hours of call recordings if you already know what they are.
3. **Narrow your list down to exactly 3-5 questions.** Pick the ones that come up the most or carry the most weight in someone's decision to buy.
4. **Write a short answer for each question.** This becomes the script for your video, so you're not figuring out what to say while the camera is rolling.
5. **Set up your screen recording software, such as OBS.** Have it ready to go before you start recording so you're not fumbling with settings between questions.
6. **Position your face on one side of the screen and your screen share on the other.** This is the same setup I use on my own YouTube videos.
7. **Record a 2-5 minute video answering each question.** Keep it as simple as sharing your screen while you talk through your written answer.
8. **Build your thumbnail template in Canva.** This is one template you'll reuse for every FAQ video, not something you rebuild from scratch each time.
  1. **Add your background.** Pick something simple and tied to your brand.
  2. **Add your face.** This is what makes the answer feel personal instead of like a generic support article.
  3. **Add the question as bold text.** This needs to be the biggest thing on the thumbnail so someone can tell exactly what the video answers before they click it.
  4. **Duplicate that design for each FAQ, swapping in the new question each time.** This keeps every thumbnail consistent while giving each one its own headline.
9. **Upload each finished video to a video hosting platform, such as Wistia.** Webflow doesn't host video directly, so this is where the actual file lives.
10. **Get the embed code for each video from your video hosting platform.** This is the code that actually displays the video once it's placed on the page.
11. **Paste each embed code into its correct placeholder on the sales Thank You page.** Once all 3-5 are live, every major objection is already handled before the prospect ever gets on the call with you.

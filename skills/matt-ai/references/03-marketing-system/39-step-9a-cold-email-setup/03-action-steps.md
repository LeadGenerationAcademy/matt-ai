# Action Steps

Course: Step #9A: Cold Email Setup
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/25ab2241?md=1244fc56b12d4c54a92d19a43b6a6374

---
The Theory explains that cold email works, that it is a skill you have to learn, practice and execute, and that deliverability is the whole game.

This course is where you build your cold email system.

You will buy and warm the cold email infrastructure, write and spin the sequence, and log what you send every day.

### Table of Contents

1. **Buy Cold Email Domains:** Buy 30 .com domains that redirect back to your main site, and authenticate every one of them with SPF, DMARC and DKIM.
2. **Buy Cold Email Inboxes:** Buy 30 inboxes and attach them to your domains so you can send around 1,000 emails a day, Monday to Friday.
3. **Buy Cold Email Tool:** Choose a sending tool and pick the plan around $97 per month.
4. **Warm Up Cold Emails:** Add every inbox to the warm up pool and let it run 14 to 30 days before you send anything real.
5. **Setup Cold Email Sending:** Set your send time, send days and stop conditions, and build the empty 3-part campaign your sequence goes into.
6. **Setup Cold Email Integrations:** Connect Slack, your CRM and your appointment-setting channel so every interested reply reaches the right person instead of sitting unnoticed.
7. **Export Cold Email List:** Export a list with first name, email, website and company, then sample it and confirm at least 80% is actually your ICP.
8. **Create Cold Email Copy:** Write the pitch, the follow-up with supporting details and an example, and the ask about whether this becomes a general follow-up.
9. **Add Spintax:** Spin every word across all three emails with at least 20 variations each, including versions of your own name.
10. **Add To Inputs Spreadsheet:** Log how many emails and DMs went out every day in the Outreach tab of your inputs spreadsheet.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Naming:** no dashes, no junk in the name. Add a word to the beginning or end, like "[tryleadgenerationacademy.com](http://tryleadgenerationacademy.com)" or "[getleadgenerationacademy.com](http://getleadgenerationacademy.com)."
2. **Top Level Domain:** .com only. Never .info, .help, or anything else, high-level .com domains give you much better deliverability, and without deliverability the whole thing is pointless.
3. **Redirect:** every domain you buy needs to redirect to your main domain, so if someone googles your domain or copies and pastes it, it takes them to your main website.
4. **Authentication:** SPF, DMARC, and DKIM all need to be set up before you connect anything to your email tool.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy 30 domains.** Buy them through Google Domains, .com top level domains only. Follow the naming rule: no dashes, no junk, add a word to the beginning or end like "try" or "get."
2. **Buy 30 email addresses.** These get attached to the domains you just bought.
3. **Set up SMTP.** This is what actually lets your inboxes send mail.
4. **Set up DMARC.** Part of the authentication your domains need before you can send.
5. **Set up DKIM.** Same purpose, another piece of the authentication.
6. **Set up SPF.** The last piece of authentication.
7. **Redirect every domain to your main domain.** If someone gets your email and googles your domain, or copies and pastes it into their browser, it needs to take them to your real website, not a blank page.
8. **Add your domains and inboxes to your email account.** Once everything is authenticated, connect it to your email account, usually [Instantly.com](http://Instantly.com).
9. **Ask ChatGPT or Claude if you get stuck on any step.** Platforms change their interfaces constantly, so if something on screen doesn't match what's described here, let AI walk you through the current version.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Send Time:** 9:00 a.m. ET by default.
2. **Send Days:** Monday through Friday only.
3. **Stop Conditions:** stop texting them if they reply, or stop emailing them if they're applying, to avoid getting this marked as spam.
4. **Other Settings:** any other relevant settings you find in the cold email sending tool.
5. **Campaign Structure:** set up the [3-part campaign](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/25ab2241?md=2cee5b639a274c519a5aaf803da189f4) you'll use for your sequence.
  1. The pitch
  2. Follow-up with supporting details and an example
  3. Ask if it'll be a general follow-up

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Set your default send time to 9:00 a.m. ET.** This is the default starting point for when your emails go out.
2. **Set your sending days to Monday through Friday only.** Don't send on weekends.
3. **Set it to stop texting them if they reply, or stop emailing them if they're applying.** This keeps you from getting marked as spam by continuing to message someone who's already responded or taken action.
4. **Review any other relevant settings in your cold email sending tool.** Every tool is a little different, so check for anything else relevant to your specific setup.
5. **Set up the 3-part campaign from [Step #5A: Create Cold Email Copy](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/25ab2241?md=2cee5b639a274c519a5aaf803da189f4&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce).** This creates the structure that will hold your sequence, the pitch, the follow-up, and the final ask, once you write it.
6. **Ask ChatGPT, Claude, or the AI built into these platforms if you get stuck on any step.** Interfaces change constantly, so if something on screen doesn't match what's described here, let AI walk you through the current version.

# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #9A: Cold Email Setup
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/25ab2241?md=108f539b7af645ddaafccc22e0886865

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Cold Email Setup

- You have bought 30 .com domains following the naming rule.
- You have set up SMTP, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC on every domain.
- You have redirected every domain back to your main domain.
- You have bought 30 inboxes and attached them to those domains.
- You have bought a cold email sending tool on the plan around $97 per month.
- You have connected your domains and inboxes to the tool.
- You have added every inbox to the warm up pool and let it run 14 to 30 days.

### Cold Email Sending & Integrations

- You have set your send time to 9:00 a.m. ET and your send days to Monday through Friday.
- You have set the sequence to stop the moment someone replies or applies.
- You have built the empty 3-part campaign your sequence goes into.
- You have set up the Slack integration that notifies you when a lead is marked Interested.
- You have built the Zap that syncs interested leads to your CRM and tags them as cold email.
- You have built the Zap that notifies your appointment setter with the lead's name, email, phone, and website.

### Cold Email List

- You have chosen your list-building method.
- You have exported your list with first name, email address, website, and company.
- You have sampled 25, 50, or 80 leads and confirmed at least 80% match your ICP.
- You have changed your method and re-exported if the sample came in under 80%.

### Cold Email Copy

- You have made a copy of the Cold Email Template.
- You have written Email 1, the pitch.
- You have written Email 2, the follow-up.
- You have written Email 3, the ask.
- You have loaded all three emails into your 3-part campaign.
- You have created at least 20 spintax variations per word, including versions of your own name.
- You have applied spintax to every word across all three emails.

### Input Tracking

- You are logging emails sent and DMs sent in the Outreach tab of your inputs spreadsheet every day.

These are the actual email accounts that you're going to want to buy. Normally, you can get 30 to 40 emails per day per account. Some people say up to 50, and some people will say 100. Who knows.

I will typically get 30 inboxes and 30 domains because then I can send about 1,000 cold emails per day, Monday to Friday. That's usually enough to book between 1 and 2 calls back in the day. You could book 10 calls with this amount, but spam filters are getting better.

More people are doing cold email, and it's more competitive. It's not a novel thing, so just buy these and attach them to the domains you bought.

You can buy these through Smart Lead, ListKit, or Instantly. I personally use Instantly just because I've been using it for so long.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose a platform.** [Smart Lead](https://www.smartlead.ai/), [ListKit](https://www.listkit.io/), and [Instantly](https://instantly.ai/) are all basically the same for this, pick whichever one you're already using or leaning toward.
2. **Buy 30 email inboxes.** Match this to the 30 domains you already bought, this combination lets you send about 1,000 cold emails per day, Monday to Friday.
3. **Attach each inbox to the domains you already bought.** Every inbox needs to be tied to one of the domains from Step #1A before you can start sending.
4. **Ask ChatGPT or Claude if you get stuck on any step.** Platforms change their interfaces constantly, so if something on screen doesn't match what's described here, let AI walk you through the current version.

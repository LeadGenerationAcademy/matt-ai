We're going to need a column in our input sheet to track how many emails and how many DMs we're sending.

This should be done in combination with an end-of-day report.

However you need to get it done, it's very difficult to automate this.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the Outreach tab of your [Input Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eT95b4OnSVAOaqrRJ5zKZrcBX0ul6EewACqs38u_Z20/edit?gid=620892265#gid=620892265).** This is where your cold email and DM volume gets tracked alongside the rest of your inputs. [in this skill: assets/templates/input-tracking-template/]
2. **Fill in the Date.** You need to know exactly when each day's outreach happened.
3. **Fill in Emails Sent.** This is your cold email volume for the day, log it however you can, an end-of-day report is the easiest way to get this one done consistently.
4. **Fill in LinkedIn DMs Sent, Twitter/X DMs Sent, and Instagram DMs Sent.** Each platform has its own column on the Outreach tab. Together they track how many DMs you're sending, the same way Emails Sent tracks how many emails you're sending.
5. **Fill in Other Outreach Sent #1 if used, or replace it with your actual platform name.** This catches any additional channel you end up using that isn't already listed, and if you're not using another channel, you can remove the column entirely.
6. **Fill in Notes.** This is where you capture anything worth remembering about that day's outreach. Total Outreach Sent calculates on its own once the rest of the row is filled in, there's nothing to enter there.
7. **Repeat this every day.** However you need to get it done, whether that's a manual log or an end-of-day report, get it done, this one doesn't automate easily.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Outreach tab of your inputs spreadsheet?
- Did you fill in the Date?
- Did you fill in Emails Sent?
- Did you fill in LinkedIn DMs Sent, Twitter/X DMs Sent, and Instagram DMs Sent?
- Did you fill in Other Outreach Sent #1 if used, or replace it with your actual platform name?
- Did you fill in Notes?
- Are you repeating this every day?

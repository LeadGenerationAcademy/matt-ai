If you buy your own domains, they'll have to warm up for 14 to 30 days.

This will be a setting in whatever tool you use, like Instantly or Smart Lead. It will say "warm up."

You add each inbox to the warm-up pool, and it will send emails to other people in the pool, open a percentage of them, and reply to a percentage of them to mimic human behavior.

Internet service providers such as Microsoft and Google now view that as a human email.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Find the warm up setting in your cold email tool.** Whatever tool you use, like Instantly or Smart Lead, will have a setting that says "warm up."
2. **Add every inbox to the warm up pool.** Once added, it will send emails to other people in the pool, open a percentage of them, and reply to a percentage of them to mimic human behavior.
3. **Let it run for 14 to 30 days.** This is how long it takes for internet service providers like Microsoft and Google to start viewing your emails as coming from a real human instead of a bot.
4. **Ask ChatGPT, Claude, or the AI built into these platforms if you get stuck on any step.** Interfaces change constantly, so if something on screen doesn't match what's described here, let AI walk you through the current version.

How you get a cold email list is going to vary depending on what you actually do. A lot of the time, you can just go to Apollo and then scrape it with Apify. If you are targeting local businesses, for example, it's usually best to use a Google Maps scraper.

There are so many different ways that it would be impossible for me to cover here. I will do an example in the video, but I would definitely do your own research on this stage because it would not be responsible for me to give you a step-by-step.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Required Fields:** First name, email address, website, company.
2. **Sample Size:** most tools give you 25 examples per page, use 25, 50, or 80 as your check.
3. **Quality Threshold:** at least 80% of the sample needs to match your ICP, it's never going to be 100% perfect, but it has to be 80%.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose your list-building method.** Going to [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/) and scraping it with [Apify](https://apify.com/) is common, a [Google Maps scraper](https://outscraper.com/google-maps-scraper/) tends to work better if you're targeting local businesses, but your exact approach depends on your niche, and there are plenty of other tools out there worth researching too.
2. **Export your list with first name, email address, website, and company.** These are the fields you need on every lead regardless of which tool or method you used to build the list.
3. **Sample 25, 50, or 80 leads from the list.** Most tools give you 25 examples per page, so use that as a natural sample size to check against.
4. **Check that at least 80% of the sample matches your ICP.** Go through the sample and count how many are actually a real fit.
5. **If less than 80% match, change your method and re-export.** It's never going to be 100% perfect, but if it's below 80%, something about your source or filters is off and needs to change before you send to it.
6. **Ask ChatGPT, Claude, or the AI built into these platforms if you get stuck on any step.** Interfaces change constantly, so if something on screen doesn't match what's described here, let AI walk you through the current version.

There are no action steps here. You're going to have to hire an appointment setter or cold caller. Nowadays, to really succeed and really bang it out with cold email, you have to actually call them. The sequence is:

1. You blast people with a mass cold email.
2. You get a response, and it notifies your Slack.
3. Someone calls them and sets them.

You will literally book 3x, 5x, or 10x more calls this way, and it almost isn't worth it to do it if you're not going to do this. In my opinion, it's just the reality of cold email in 2026, 2027, and probably beyond. They get so many different emails per day that you have to stand out. Essentially, the sequence is:

1. Double-text with your name, saying, "Hey, you responded to my cold email. It's Matt from Lead Generation Academy."
2. Text that, because it will show up when you call them as "Matthew Larsen."
3. Double-call them to get them through Do Not Disturb.
4. Try to set them.

I wouldn't recommend you doing this yourself. It's something that you should have a cold caller do, but it really is kind of non-negotiable if you want to do this these days. This is not the case when we talk about dream 100 prospecting later, but it definitely is the case for cold email.

If you're not gonna do this, I would just ignore steps 1 to 5 because you're not gonna succeed.

You could try and then hire a cold caller for this later, but if you know deep down that you're not going to do it, just don't even bother.

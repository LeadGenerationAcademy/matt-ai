We are going to create a 3-part cold email sequence, this is the same 3-part campaign container you set up back in [Step #2: Setup Cold Email Sending](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/25ab2241?md=2cee5b639a274c519a5aaf803da189f4&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce). We don't need to do more than that.

Almost all of our qualified positive replies will become a 1, 2, or 3. Anything else is just asking to be reported as spam.

We are going to make a very simple cold email:

1. The pitch
2. Follow-up with supporting details and an example you got
3. Ask if it'll be a general follow-up

### Framework & Criteria

**Email 1, The Pitch:** the initial outreach email.

Subject line: quick question

Body: Hi {first name},

{Personalized first line},

If I could {generate X result} {risk minimizer}, {social proof} would you be interested in speaking?

Regards,
{Your Name}

**Email 2, Follow-Up:** supporting details and an example.

Subject line: Re: quick question

Body: Hey {first name}, it's {Your Name} again.

We {your offer}, that is how {client result}.

Do you want to see what that would look like for you?

Regards,
{Your Name}

**Email 3, The Ask:** ask if it'll be a general follow-up.

Subject line: Re: quick question

Body: Hey {first name}, it's {Your Name} again.

I reached out about {generate X result} {risk minimizer}, same as we did for {social proof}.

Is this something you want to look at now, or should I follow up in a few months?

Regards,
{Your Name}

### Example

**Email 1, The Pitch**

**Subject line:** quick question

**Body:** Hi Nick,

Saw your LinkedIn post about the 123 marketing summit, how was it?

If I could generate you 15 meeting-ready leads in 60 days on a performance basis, like we did for XYZ Marketing, would you be interested in speaking?

Regards,
Matt

**Email 2, Follow-Up**

**Subject line:** Re: quick question

**Body:** Hey John, it's Matt again.

We run the ads and qualify every lead, so you only get the ones ready to book, that is how XYZ Marketing got 15 of them and 4 closed jobs in 60 days.

Do you want to see what that would look like for you?

Regards,
Matt

**Email 3, The Ask**

**Subject line:** Re: quick question

**Body:** Hey John, it's Matt again.

I reached out about generating you 15 meeting-ready leads in 60 days on a performance basis, same as we did for XYZ Marketing.

Is this something you want to look at now, or should I follow up in a few months?

Regards,
Matt

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Cold Email Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1W9vHOSwkNXOVNa8c6GGRcoqwP6_LEKp1mU1v948Q9cs/edit?usp=sharing).** This is the document you'll write all three emails in, each email has its own tab on the left side. [in this skill: assets/templates/cold-email-template/]
2. **Write Email 1, the pitch.** Go to the Email 1 tab on the left side of the document and fill in your five parts, this is the first message, your qualified positive replies will almost always come from a 1, 2, or 3 email sequence, adding more than that is just asking to get reported as spam.
3. **Write Email 2, a follow-up with supporting details and an example.** Go to the Email 2 tab on the left side of the document, give them more context and a concrete example to back up the pitch from Email 1.
4. **Write Email 3, asking if it'll be a general follow-up.** Go to the Email 3 tab on the left side of the document, this is the final email in the sequence.
5. **Load all three emails into the 3-part campaign from Step #2.** This is the same campaign container you already built, now you're filling it in with actual copy.

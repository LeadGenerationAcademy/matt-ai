# Step #9: Schedule Weekly Metric Review

Course: Step #1: Metrics Tracking
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=0713cfb340624270a7ca939bf378b720

---
Tracking Metrics does nothing if you never look at them.

You need a recurring time every week to review the numbers, identify problems, and decide what needs to change.

This should be an actual event inside your calendar so you do not conveniently forget.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a recurring one-hour calendar event called Weekly Metrics Review.** Set it to repeat every week, at a time you're unlikely to be interrupted. One hour is the minimum, not a target to shrink as the business gets busier.
2. **Add the link to your Metrics folder inside the event.** Every Sheet and Updating SOP you need is in that folder, one click away when the review starts.
3. **Check your Net Margin Sheet.** Confirm the most recent bank transactions were added. Then look at whether Net Margin is holding up as you scale, or whether costs are creeping in faster than revenue.
4. **Check your Monthly Churn Sheet.** Confirm new clients, churned clients, and Client Payments were all updated. Then check whether churn is coming from one lead source or client type, and whether Median Client Payment moved because of a pricing change or a different tier of client.
5. **Check your Close Rate Sheet.** Confirm Sales Calls were updated. Then check whether a change in Close Rate is coming from lead quality, sales execution, or the offer itself.
6. **Check your Cost Per Call Sheet.** Confirm Acquisition Spend was updated. Then check whether a rising Cost Per Qualified Call is coming from more expensive traffic, or from fewer bookings actually qualifying.
7. **Check your Input Tracking Sheet.** Confirm Inputs were updated, then go through every channel you use. If an outcome is poor and the inputs behind it are also low, you've probably found the problem.
8. **Check your Reverse Engineering Sheet.** Update the Actual numbers first if your Sheets aren't connected to it. Then compare what it says you need, like Qualified Calls Required and Required Acquisition Spend, against what your other Sheets show you're actually producing.
9. **Identify the single biggest constraint preventing growth.** It may be low Net Margin, high Churn, low Client Payment, low Close Rate, high Cost Per Qualified Call, not enough Qualified Calls, or not enough inputs. Fix that one first instead of every Metric at once.
10. **Record the next action, the responsible person, and the deadline for each.** Review these actions again during the following week's meeting.
11. **Complete the review every week, no exceptions.** If you can't attend the original time, move it to another time in the same week, don't skip it.

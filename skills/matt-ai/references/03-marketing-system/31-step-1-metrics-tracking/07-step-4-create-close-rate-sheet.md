Your Close Rate Sheet tracks what happens to every prospect after they enter your sales process.

You need to know how many people apply, book, show up, qualify, receive an Offer, and become clients.

If you set this up properly, you can combine it with the Cost Per Qualified Call Sheet in Step #5.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Sales Call Database tab:** one row per unique sales opportunity. Track Opportunity ID, Name, Email, Phone Number, Company, Website, Lead Source, Campaign, Ad, Application Date, Call Booked Date, Scheduled Call Date, Completed Call Date, Closed-Won Date, Revenue Closed, and Notes.
2. **Milestone checkboxes:** check off each as the opportunity moves through your process: Application Submitted, Call Booked, Call Shown, Qualified, Offer Sent, Closed Won, Closed Lost, No-Show, Unqualified.
3. **One opportunity, one row:** a reschedule, a second call, a follow-up, a different closer, or a reapply in the same process all stay on the original row. Don't create a new one.
4. **Dashboard tab:** enter a Period Start date to add a new week or month. Everything else fills in on its own from the Sales Call Database.
5. **Ongoing updates:** update every opportunity as it moves through your process, in real time going forward.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Close Rate Tracker Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1zycDT-jyDWh8f64735DvVNrNYqk9O3zfBGjJLm06BAk/edit?usp=sharing).** This gives you the Sales Call Database and Dashboard tabs. [in this skill: assets/templates/close-rate-tracker-template/]
2. **In the Sales Call Database tab, backfill your last 6-12 months of sales calls.** Pull from your CRM, calendar, application form, call recordings or notes, payment processor, contracts, or setter records. If you don't have that much, three months is the minimum.
3. **For each opportunity, record the Opportunity ID, Name, Email, Phone Number, Company, and Website.** The Opportunity ID is what keeps each opportunity uniquely identified across your CRM, notes, and this sheet. The rest lets you or your team find the account without digging back through your CRM.
4. **For each opportunity, record the Lead Source, Campaign, and Ad.** These go from broad to specific. Together they show you which channel, which campaign, and which exact ad is producing closed business, not just applications.
5. **For each opportunity, record the Application Date.** This is the date the Dashboard uses to bucket the opportunity into a specific week or month, so it has to be accurate for every Dashboard number in that period to be correct.
6. **For each opportunity, record the Call Booked Date, Scheduled Call Date, Completed Call Date, and Closed-Won Date.** If a call gets rescheduled, update the Scheduled Call Date rather than creating a new row. Closed-Won Date shows you how long a deal actually took to close.
7. **For each opportunity, record the Revenue Closed and any Notes.** Revenue Closed feeds the Dashboard's Revenue Closed total for each period. Notes give you context on why an opportunity stalled or didn't close.
8. **Check off Application Submitted, Call Booked, Call Shown, and Qualified as each opportunity reaches them.** The Dashboard counts these checkboxes to calculate Show Rate, Qualification Rate, and the Qualified Calls your Close Rate is based on.
9. **Check off Offer Sent, Closed Won, or Closed Lost as each opportunity reaches them.** Closed Won is what the Dashboard counts toward Close Rate and Offer Close Rate. Tracking Closed Lost separately shows you your full pipeline outcome, not just your wins.
10. **Check off No-Show or Unqualified when that's where an opportunity ends.** Both are left out of Close Rate entirely. Only completed calls with people who could realistically buy count.
11. **On the Dashboard tab, add a Period Start date in Column A for each week or month you backfilled.** This is the only manual entry on the Dashboard, every other column fills in on its own.
12. **Check your Close Rate on the Dashboard tab.** Your most recent period's Close Rate goes into Actual Close Rate on your Reverse Engineering Sheet. Then look at Show Rate, Qualification Rate, Offer Rate, and Offer Close Rate to find the exact stage where prospects are dropping off.
13. **Fix any number that looks wrong on the Sales Call Database tab.** The Dashboard is auto-calculated from the Sales Call Database, so the fix is never on the Dashboard. Look for a missing date or an unchecked milestone.
14. **Log new opportunities and check off milestones daily.** Add a new row the moment someone applies or books a call, and check off each milestone as it happens. Keeping this current is what makes Show Rate, Qualification Rate, Offer Rate, and Offer Close Rate useful.

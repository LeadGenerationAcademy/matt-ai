Your Metrics Sheets will become useless if nobody knows how to update them correctly.

Create a short SOP showing exactly where every number comes from, how to enter it, and how to confirm that the Sheet is still working.

This should primarily be a video of you doing the work.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Create An SOP For Each Sheet:**
  1. Reverse Engineering Sheet
  2. Net Margin Sheet
  3. Monthly Churn and Median Client Payment Sheet
  4. Close Rate Sheet
  5. Cost Per Qualified Call Sheet
  6. Input Tracking Sheet
2. **Record A Real Update:**
  1. Open the source of the data
  2. Select the correct date range
  3. Export or copy the data
  4. Clean the data and remove duplicates
  5. Enter it into the correct Sheet
  6. Check the formulas
  7. Review the Dashboard
  8. Confirm the final numbers make sense
3. **Show The Exact Source For Every Number:**
  1. Bank accounts
  2. Payment processors
  3. Accounting software
  4. CRM
  5. Calendar
  6. Application form
  7. Sales call records
  8. Advertising platforms
  9. Outreach platforms
  10. Social media profiles
  11. Content platforms
4. **State The Update Frequency:**
  1. Input Tracking: Daily
  2. Sales Calls: Daily or weekly
  3. Acquisition Spend: Weekly
  4. Client Status and Payments: Weekly
  5. Net Margin: Weekly, monthly at the absolute minimum
  6. Reverse Engineering: Pulls from the other Sheets, nothing manual
5. **Document The Accuracy Rules:**
  1. How duplicate Sales Calls are identified
  2. How second calls are handled
  3. What qualifies as a Qualified Call
  4. When a client becomes active or churned
  5. Which expenses count as Acquisition Spend
  6. How internal bank transfers are handled
  7. How currencies are converted
  8. Which profiles and channels belong in Input Tracking
6. **Show How To Verify:**
  1. The correct date range was used
  2. No rows are missing
  3. No duplicates were added
  4. The formulas still work
  5. The Dashboard updated
  6. The totals match the original source

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Record yourself completing a real update for one Sheet.** Use Loom or another screen recorder and share your actual screen. Don't make a theoretical video about what someone should probably do.
2. **Show exactly where the source data comes from.** Show where to click and which number to use. "Get the advertising numbers" is not an instruction.
3. **State the update frequency for that Sheet.** Every Sheet has its own cadence and the SOP has to name it.
4. **Document the rules that protect the data's accuracy.** Anything that could be counted two different ways needs a written definition. Your numbers stop meaning anything the moment two people define a Qualified Call differently.
5. **Show how to verify the update is correct.** The update isn't finished until it has been checked against the original source.
6. **Save the video in your Metrics folder.** Keep it with the written instructions so nobody has to search for it.
7. **Save the written instructions in your Metrics folder.** Someone should be able to follow along step by step without asking you what to do.
8. **Add a direct link to the SOP at the top of that Spreadsheet.** Whoever updates the Sheet should never go hunting for the instructions.
9. **Repeat steps 1-8 for every Sheet.** If Close Rate and Cost Per Qualified Call live in the same Spreadsheet, one shared SOP covers both.

### Completion Audit

- Did you record yourself completing a real update for this Sheet?
- Did you show exactly where the source data comes from?
- Did you state the update frequency for this Sheet?
- Did you document the rules that protect this data's accuracy?
- Did you show how to verify the update is correct?
- Did you save the video in your Metrics folder?
- Did you save the written instructions in your Metrics folder?
- Did you add a direct link to the SOP at the top of that Spreadsheet?
- Did you repeat this process for every Sheet?

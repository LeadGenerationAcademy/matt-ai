Reverse engineering takes your business goals and works backward to show you exactly how many clients, qualified calls, and dollars of acquisition spend you need to reach them. For many people, this is the first time they learn something real about their own business.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Major Goal**
  1. Net Profit Goal: The monthly net profit you're targeting.
  2. Net Margin: The percentage of revenue you keep after all costs.
2. **Churn**
  1. Monthly Churn Rate: The percentage of clients you lose each month.
  2. Avg. Client Relationship Months: How long a client stays with you on average.
3. **LTV**
  1. Median Monthly Retainer: The typical monthly payment per client.
4. **Clients**
  1. Close Rate: The percentage of qualified calls that become clients.
  2. Working Days Per Year: How many days per year your team actually works.
  3. Calls a Closer Takes/Week: How many calls one closer can handle weekly.
  4. Calls a Setter Books/Week: How many calls one setter can book weekly.
  5. Acquisition Source: Where your qualified calls come from (ads, referrals, etc).
  6. Cost Per Call: What it costs you to generate one qualified call.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Reverse Engineering Sheet Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1oe9aSPQvqiGhuWr-LV3osKEJ5NiVIRpwhAO8DCanwJI/edit?usp=sharing).** This sheet takes your Core Metrics and works backward from your profit goal, so you know exactly how many clients, qualified calls, and dollars of ad spend it takes to get there. You only fill in the yellow cells, and the blue cells calculate on their own. [in this skill: assets/templates/reverse-engineering-sheet-template/]
2. **Bookmark it in your Metrics folder.** Keep every metrics sheet in one place so your team always knows where to find current numbers.
3. **Enter your yearly Net Profit Goal in both the Actual and Goal columns.** This is the yearly net profit you're targeting. Every other calculation on the sheet works backward from this number.
4. **Enter your Actual and Goal Net Margin.** This is the percentage of revenue you keep after all costs. The sheet uses it to work out how much revenue you need to hit your profit goal. Your Actual comes from your Net Margin Sheet in [Step #2](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=8f2b232b63364fe8bd0b7ed2adb3e759).
5. **Enter your Actual and Goal Monthly Churn Rate.** This is the percentage of clients you lose each month. The sheet uses it to calculate how long the average client stays with you. Your Actual comes from your Monthly Churn Sheet in [Step #3](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=6280556845e94c1c962d20c63e7d400d).
6. **Enter your Actual and Goal Median Monthly Retainer.** This is the middle value of what your clients pay per month, not the average. The sheet combines it with your churn to get your average lifetime value per client, which decides how many clients you need. Your Actual also comes from your Monthly Churn Sheet in [Step #3](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=6280556845e94c1c962d20c63e7d400d).
7. **Enter your Actual and Goal Close Rate.** This is the percentage of calls that become paying clients. The sheet uses it to work out how many calls you need. You can measure it on booked or shown calls, as long as your Cost Per Call matches. Your Actual comes from your Close Rate Sheet in [Step #4](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=74769e2dd7ac4cf6b31bc71707aecbfc).
8. **Enter your Actual and Goal Working Days Per Year.** This is how many days per year your team actually works, not a flat 365. The sheet uses it to turn your yearly calls into calls per day.
9. **Enter your Actual and Goal Calls a Closer Takes / Week.** This is how many calls one closer can handle each week. The sheet uses it to work out how many closers you need.
10. **Enter your Actual and Goal Calls a Setter Books / Week.** This is how many calls one appointment setter can book each week. The sheet uses it to work out how many setters you need.
11. **Select your Acquisition Source from the dropdown in both columns.** This is where your qualified calls come from, either Ads, Email, Call or DMs.
12. **Enter your Actual and Goal Cost Per Call.** This is what it costs you to generate one qualified call. The sheet uses it to work out how much you need to spend on ads per day. Your Actual comes from your Cost Per Call Sheet in [Step #5](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=2cc69732478a430ab44f6ce53dda9918).
13. **Check the % Difference column.** This shows how far your Actual numbers are from your Goal numbers, so you can see where the biggest gaps are. Come back and update your Actual numbers every month.

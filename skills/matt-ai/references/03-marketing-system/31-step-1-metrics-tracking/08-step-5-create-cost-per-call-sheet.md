Your Cost Per Qualified Call Sheet connects your acquisition expenses to the calls those expenses produce.

You need to know how much it costs to generate:

- One Call Booked
- One Call Shown
- One completed Qualified Call

If you set this up properly, it can use the same Sales Call Database you created in Step #4.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Paid Ads tab:** log your daily numbers: Date, CPM, Outbound CTR, Outbound Clicks, Calls Booked, Calls Shown, Qualified Calls (matching what your Sales Call Database shows for that day, no duplicates, reschedules, or follow-ups), and Amount Spent.
2. **Expense tab:** log every non-ad cost by month: Salary / Labor, Contractor, Media Buyers, Software, Lead Data, and Other. Add more columns if you track another recurring category, just make sure Total includes them.
3. **Dashboard tab:** Ad Spend, Other Expenses, Total Acquisition Spend, Calls Booked, Calls Shown, Qualified Calls, and all three Cost Per Call numbers pull automatically, by month.
4. **Update cadence:** update the Paid Ads tab daily and the Expense tab monthly.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Cost Per Call Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1j72glCi3eEGZi25Cz_R8qALWbZjtMcGCbpFmfx_0kDU/edit?usp=sharing).** This gives you the Paid Ads, Expense, and Dashboard tabs. [in this skill: assets/templates/cost-per-call-template/]
2. **In the Paid Ads tab, backfill your last 6-12 months of daily numbers.** This tab is Paid Ads only, log other business costs like labor and software on the Expense tab instead.
3. **For each day, record the Date, CPM, Outbound CTR, Outbound Clicks, and Amount Spent.** Pull these from [Facebook Ads Manager](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/adsmanager). Watching CPM and Outbound CTR together shows whether a rising Cost Per Call is coming from more expensive traffic or a weaker ad. Don't touch the monthly summary rows, they total themselves.
4. **For each day, record the Calls Booked.** This is what the monthly summary row totals into the Dashboard's Calls Booked column, the denominator for Cost Per Call Booked. A separate booking link for ads, like [calendar.com/fb](http://calendar.com/fb), makes it easy to see which calls came from ads.
5. **For each day, record the Calls Shown and Qualified Calls.** Put these on the day the calls were booked, once they've happened, not on the day the call took place. Match your Sales Call Database, with no duplicates, reschedules, or follow-ups, or your Cost Per Call will look artificially low.
6. **In the Expense tab, backfill your last 6-12 months of monthly costs.** This feeds the Dashboard's Cost Per Call numbers, so keep it updated every month.
7. **For each month, record your Salary / Labor, Contractor, and Media Buyers costs.** Only count team members and contractors whose work goes toward generating calls, not your entire payroll. Media Buyers is what you pay whoever manages your ads, separate from the ad spend itself.
8. **For each month, record your Software, Lead Data, and any Other costs.** Software covers outreach, landing page, tracking, and email tools. Lead Data is any purchased lists or enrichment tools. Don't leave real costs out because they make the numbers look worse.
9. **Check your Cost Per Call on the Dashboard tab.** Total Acquisition Spend is Ad Spend plus Other Expenses, divided by each stage's call count. Look at whether your Cost Per Call is rising or falling month to month, and only compare spend and calls from the same month.
10. **Put the Cost Per Call that matches your Close Rate into your Reverse Engineering Sheet.** Use Cost Per Call Booked if you measure Close Rate on booked calls, or Cost Per Call Shown if you measure it on shown calls. It goes into Actual Cost Per Call.
11. **Log Paid Ads numbers daily and Expense costs monthly.** Review the raw tabs regularly, not just the Dashboard. Missing or wrong data underneath will throw off every Cost Per Call number.

### Completion Audit

- Did you make a copy of the Cost Per Call Template?

- Did you check your Cost Per Call on the Dashboard tab?

- Are you logging Paid Ads numbers daily and Expense costs monthly?

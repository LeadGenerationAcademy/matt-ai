There are five Core Metrics that every business needs to track.

They are the most important numbers in your business, but they also allow you to calculate and extrapolate almost every other important metric.

If these five numbers are accurate, you can use them to understand what is currently happening, diagnose problems, and reverse engineer what needs to happen next.

If one of them is fucked up, every calculation built on top of it will also be fucked up.

### Use Whatever System Works

These do not technically have to be Spreadsheets.

You could track them inside a custom application, CRM, accounting platform, or another system.

However, I recommend using Spreadsheets because they are free, versatile, easy to customize, and available to almost everyone.

You can always move the data into a more advanced system later.

The important thing is that you start tracking it now.

### The Five Core Metrics

The Five Core Metrics are:

1. Net Margin
2. Monthly Churn Rate
3. Monthly Median Client Payment
4. Close Rate
5. Cost Per Qualified Call

#### 1. Net Margin

Net Margin shows how much of your revenue remains as profit after expenses.

**Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue × 100 = Net Margin**

#### 2. Monthly Churn Rate

Monthly Churn Rate shows the percentage of your existing clients that leave during an average month.

**Clients Lost During the Month ÷ Clients at the Beginning of the Month × 100 = Monthly Churn Rate**

#### 3. Monthly Median Client Payment

Monthly Median Client Payment shows the middle monthly payment across your clients.

Arrange every monthly client payment from lowest to highest and select the middle number.

If there are two middle numbers, calculate the average of those two numbers.

The median prevents one unusually large or small client from completely distorting the result.

#### 4. Close Rate

Close Rate shows the percentage of completed Qualified Sales Calls that become clients.

**New Clients Closed ÷ Completed Qualified Sales Calls × 100 = Close Rate**

Do not include:

- No-shows
- Unqualified calls
- Duplicate calls
- Second calls with the same opportunity

We want the percentage of completed, qualified sales opportunities that become clients.

#### 5. Cost Per Qualified Call

Cost Per Qualified Call shows how much it costs to generate one completed sales call with a qualified prospect.

**Total Acquisition Spend ÷ Completed Qualified Sales Calls = Cost Per Qualified Call**

Total Acquisition Spend should include every cost directly required to generate those calls, including:

- Advertising spend
- Outreach expenses
- Lead-generation software
- Appointment setters
- Contractors
- Relevant team salaries
- Other direct acquisition expenses

### Extrapolating Other Metrics

Using only these five Core Metrics, you can extrapolate almost every important metric for your business.

When using a percentage inside these formulas, convert it into a decimal.

For example:

- 25% Close Rate becomes 0.25
- 5% Monthly Churn becomes 0.05
- 20% Net Margin becomes 0.20

### Extrapolating Other Metrics

Using only these five Core Metrics, you can extrapolate almost every important metric for your business.

When using a percentage inside these formulas, convert it into a decimal.

For example:

- 25% Close Rate becomes 0.25
- 5% Monthly Churn becomes 0.05
- 20% Net Margin becomes 0.20

##### Customer Acquisition Cost

Cost Per Qualified Call ÷ Close Rate = Customer Acquisition Cost

##### Calls Required Per Client

1 ÷ Close Rate = Calls Required Per Client

##### Average Client Lifespan

1 ÷ Monthly Churn Rate = Average Client Lifespan in Months

##### Client Lifetime Value

Monthly Median Client Payment ÷ Monthly Churn Rate = Client Lifetime Value

##### Monthly Profit Per Client

Monthly Median Client Payment × Net Margin = Monthly Profit Per Client

##### Lifetime Profit Per Client

Client Lifetime Value × Net Margin = Lifetime Profit Per Client

##### LTV-to-CAC Ratio

Client Lifetime Value ÷ Customer Acquisition Cost = LTV-to-CAC Ratio

##### Profit-to-CAC Ratio

Lifetime Profit Per Client ÷ Customer Acquisition Cost = Profit-to-CAC Ratio

##### CAC Payback Period

Customer Acquisition Cost ÷ Monthly Profit Per Client = CAC Payback Period in Months

##### Expected Lifetime Revenue Per Qualified Call

Close Rate × Client Lifetime Value = Expected Lifetime Revenue Per Qualified Call

##### Expected Lifetime Profit Per Qualified Call

(Close Rate × Lifetime Profit Per Client) − Cost Per Qualified Call = Expected Lifetime Profit Per Qualified Call

##### Break-Even Cost Per Qualified Call

Close Rate × Lifetime Profit Per Client = Break-Even Cost Per Qualified Call

##### Net Profit

Total Revenue − Total Expenses = Net Profit

##### Total Acquisition Spend

Ad Spend + Other Expenses = Total Acquisition Spend

##### Cost Per Call Booked

Total Acquisition Spend ÷ Calls Booked = Cost Per Call Booked

##### Cost Per Call Shown

Total Acquisition Spend ÷ Calls Shown = Cost Per Call Shown

##### Cost Per Outbound Click

Amount Spent ÷ Outbound Clicks = Cost Per Outbound Click

##### Show Rate

Calls Shown ÷ Calls Booked = Show Rate

##### Qualification Rate

Qualified Calls ÷ Calls Shown = Qualification Rate

##### Offer Rate

Offers Sent ÷ Qualified Calls = Offer Rate

##### Offer Close Rate

Closed Won ÷ Offers Sent = Offer Close Rate

##### Required Acquisition Spend

Goal Cost Per Call × Required Call Volume = Required Acquisition Spend

These extrapolated metrics are estimates, but they become extremely useful when the five numbers underneath them are accurate.

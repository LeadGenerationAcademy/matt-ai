### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Input Tracking Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eT95b4OnSVAOaqrRJ5zKZrcBX0ul6EewACqs38u_Z20/edit?usp=sharing).** This gives you the Partnerships, Outreach, Paid Ads, Content, Proof, and Daily Overview tabs, already built and already formatted. [in this skill: assets/templates/input-tracking-template/]
2. **Bookmark it in your Metrics folder, and don't edit the original template.** Keeping the original untouched means you always have a clean copy to start from for future team members.
3. **Fill in the Partnerships tab every day.** The [Partnerships Setup](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b0606dd1?md=d134fd3805a74e55873d563f29ec395e&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) course covers exactly what belongs in each column, so it's not repeated here.
4. **Fill in the Outreach tab every day.** The [Cold Email Setup](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/25ab2241?md=28894163dc4f42708e01b65ed2160e7a) course covers exactly what belongs in each column, so it's not repeated here.
5. **Fill in the Paid Ads tab every day you have ad data.** The [Paid Ads Setup](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=dbb9500dd47c455ea1ac346e625495fd&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) course covers exactly what belongs in each column, so it's not repeated here.
6. **Fill in the Content tab every day.** The [Content Setup](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=28029218da6a4bc9b86b7e2788539d9b&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) course covers exactly what belongs in each column, so it's not repeated here.
7. **Fill in the Proof tab every day.** The [Proof](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/25ab2241?md=28894163dc4f42708e01b65ed2160e7a&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) course covers exactly what belongs in each column, so it's not repeated here.
8. **Check the Column Totals panel on each tab for your running totals.** It sits right next to the header row on every tab, so you never have to scroll to the bottom of the sheet just to see where you stand.
9. **Check the Daily Overview tab if you want everything laid out one row per day instead.** Every column there is a formula pulling from the other tabs, so there's nothing to fill in manually, if a number looks wrong, check that the Date on the source tab is entered correctly, since the formulas key off exact date matches.

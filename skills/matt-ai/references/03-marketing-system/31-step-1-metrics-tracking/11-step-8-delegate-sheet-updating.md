# Step #8: Delegate Sheet Updating

Course: Step #1: Metrics Tracking
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=81deee8641c746f3bcf9e7a9d55435a8

---
You should not be personally collecting and entering every piece of Metrics data forever.

Find someone inside your company who can update the Sheets consistently.

If nobody on your current team can do it, hire a VA from Fiverr or another platform.

This person must be trustworthy because they may have access to sensitive business information.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Assign the Net Margin Sheet to a specific person.** If you're not comfortable showing one person every number, this is often the one to hand to a trusted finance employee.
2. **Assign the Monthly Churn Sheet to a specific person.** A Client Success employee is a natural fit since they're already tracking client status.
3. **Assign the Monthly Median Client Payment Sheet to a specific person.** This usually sits with whoever owns Monthly Churn, since both live on the same Sheet.
4. **Assign the Close Rate Sheet to a specific person.** A Sales employee is a natural fit since they're closest to what's actually happening on calls.
5. **Assign the Cost Per Qualified Call Sheet to a specific person.** A Marketing employee is a natural fit since they own the acquisition spend feeding this number.
6. **Assign the Input Tracking Sheet to a specific person.** Often the same Marketing employee who owns Acquisition Spend, since both track marketing activity.
7. **Assign the Reverse Engineering Sheet to a specific person.** This can be you, since it pulls from every other Sheet and drives the whole review. Whoever ends up with each Sheet should only get access to the accounts and information required for that assignment.
8. **Give them the Updating SOP from [Step #7](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=358a033d86714576b70307e4932c53d1).** This replaces just handing them a Spreadsheet and telling them to figure it out.
9. **Have them watch the video.** The SOP video shows a real update actually being completed, not a theoretical walkthrough.
10. **Have them complete the process using the SOP.** Let them do it without you narrating it live, this is what actually tests whether the SOP alone is enough.
11. **Watch them complete their first update in full.** Watch them find the original data, select the correct date range, export or copy it, clean it, remove duplicates, add it to the Sheet, check the formulas, and confirm the Dashboard updated.
12. **Set a specific update deadline for their Sheet.** For example, Inputs every morning, Sales Calls every Monday, Acquisition Spend every Monday, Client Status and Payments every Monday, Net Margin every Monday. The exact schedule can change, but it can never be "whenever you have time."
13. **Personally review their first several updates.** Check the source data, date ranges, duplicates, categories, client statuses, qualified call statuses, acquisition expenses, formulas, and dashboard totals. Correct problems immediately before they become part of the process.
14. **Fix the SOP if they can't complete the process using only the SOP.** If they still need to ask you what to do after using it, that's a gap in the SOP, not in them. And delegating the data entry doesn't mean you stop looking at the Metrics, you're still responsible for reviewing the numbers, understanding what they mean, and making decisions from them. It's still your business.

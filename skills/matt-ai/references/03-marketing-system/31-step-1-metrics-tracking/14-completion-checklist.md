# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #1: Metrics Tracking
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ba5ef8ff?md=b856522986af4bb1b023fc6e040eb234

---
Complete every item below before moving on.

The entire Metrics Tracking System should be bookmarked, updated, delegated, and reviewed every week.

### Reverse Engineering Sheet

- Made a copy of the Reverse Engineering Sheet Template and bookmarked it in your Metrics folder
- Entered your Actual and Goal numbers for every metric on the sheet
- Selected your Acquisition Source from the dropdown

### Net Margin Sheet

- Made a copy of the Net Margin Tracker Template
- Replaced the placeholder Accounts with your real accounts
- Updated the Category dropdown to match your real income and expense streams
- Confirmed the Currency dropdown covers every currency you actually use
- Backfilled your last 6-12 months of transactions on the Transactions tab
- Checked the Dashboard tab and confirmed Net Margin populated for the month
- Checked the Category Breakdown tab and confirmed your categories populated correctly
- Are logging new transactions on the Transactions tab weekly

### Monthly Churn Sheet

- Made a copy of the Monthly Churn & Average Retainer Tracking Template
- Backfilled your last 6-12 months of clients on the Client Data tab, logging every client's details
- Backfilled your last 6-12 months of payments on the Payments tab, matching each Customer Email exactly to its Client Email
- Checked the Monthly Churn Dashboard tab and confirmed Churn Rate populated for the month
- Are logging new clients and payments weekly

### Close Rate Sheet

- Made a copy of the Close Rate Tracker Template
- Backfilled your last 6-12 months of opportunities on the Sales Call Database tab, logging every opportunity's details
- Checked off each opportunity's milestones as they occurred
- Added a Period Start date on the Dashboard tab for each week or month you backfilled
- Checked the Dashboard tab and confirmed Close Rate populated for the period
- Are logging new opportunities and checking off milestones daily

### Cost Per Qualified Call Sheet

- Made a copy of the Cost Per Call Template
- Backfilled your last 6-12 months of daily numbers on the Paid Ads tab, logging every day's ad performance and spend details
- Backfilled your last 6-12 months of non-ad acquisition costs by category on the Expense tab
- Checked the Dashboard tab and confirmed Cost Per Qualified Call populated for the month
- Are logging new Paid Ads numbers daily
- Are logging Expense tab costs monthly

### Input Tracking Sheet

- Made a copy of the Input Tracking Template and bookmarked it in your Metrics folder
- Deleted or replaced any platform tabs that don't apply to your business, updating the Content Type column for any tab you replaced
- Logged every piece of content's details on its matching platform tab
- Logged all other content in the Other tab, selecting its Content Type from the dropdown
- Checked the Dashboard tab and confirmed your inputs populated for today
- Set up automated logging for each tab where possible
- Are manually logging any non-automated platform every day

### Updating SOPs

- For each Metrics Sheet, recorded yourself completing a real update on video
- Showed exactly where the source data comes from
- Stated the required update frequency
- Documented the rules that protect the data's accuracy
- Showed how to verify the update is correct
- Saved the video and written instructions in your Metrics folder
- Added a direct link to the SOP at the top of that Spreadsheet
- Repeated this process for every Sheet

### Delegating Sheet Updates

- Assigned each Metrics Sheet to a specific person
- Gave them the Updating SOP and had them watch the video
- Had them complete the process using the SOP
- Watched them complete their first update in full
- Set a specific update deadline for their Sheet
- Personally reviewed their first several updates
- Fixed the SOP if they couldn't complete the process using only it

### Weekly Metrics Review

- Created a recurring calendar event called Weekly Metrics Review, scheduled for at least one hour every week at a time you're unlikely to be interrupted
- Added the link to your Metrics folder inside the event
- Reviewed all five Core Metrics and compared each against the previous week, previous month, and your target
- Reviewed every figure on the Reverse Engineering Sheet and compared it against what the business is actually producing
- Reviewed the Input Tracking Sheet across every channel you use
- Identified the single biggest constraint preventing growth
- Recorded the next action, responsible person, and deadline for it
- Completed the review this week, no exceptions

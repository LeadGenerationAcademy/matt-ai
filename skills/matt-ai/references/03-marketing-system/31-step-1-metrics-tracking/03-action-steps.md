The Theory explains that there are Five Core Metrics: Net Margin, Monthly Churn Rate, Monthly Median Client Payment, Close Rate, and Cost Per Qualified Call, and that if these five numbers are accurate you can use them to diagnose problems and extrapolate almost every other important metric in your business.

This module is where you actually build that tracking system.

You will set up your Reverse Engineering Sheet and the underlying Net Margin, Monthly Churn, Close Rate, Cost Per Call, and Input Tracking Sheets that feed it, then create SOPs, delegate the updating, and schedule a recurring review so the numbers stay accurate and actually get looked at.

Do not let one of these Sheets go stale or get set up incorrectly, since every one of the five Core Metrics depends on the underlying data being accurate, and every other number you extrapolate is built on top of them.

### Table of Contents

1. **Reverse Engineering Sheet:** Fill in your five Core Metrics to calculate exactly what must happen, in clients, qualified calls, and acquisition spend, to hit your revenue and profit goals.
2. **Net Margin Sheet:** Log every transaction from your business accounts so you always know your real Net Profit and Net Margin.
3. **Monthly Churn Sheet:** Log every client, active and churned, along with their payments, to track how long clients stay and how many churn every month.
4. **Close Rate Sheet:** Log every sales opportunity from Application through Closed Won or Closed Lost to calculate your real Close Rate.
5. **Cost Per Call Sheet:** Log every acquisition expense to calculate what you are paying for booked, shown, and completed Qualified Sales Calls.
6. **Input Tracking Sheet:** Log how much content, outreach, advertising, and other growth activity you actually complete every day, week, and month.
7. **Updating SOPs:** Record a simple, repeatable process that another person can follow to update every Metrics Sheet without guessing or breaking anything.
8. **Delegate Sheet Updating:** Assign responsibility for updating every Metrics Sheet so the information stays current without depending on you to enter it personally.
9. **Schedule Weekly Metric Review:** Schedule and complete a Weekly Metrics Review that keeps you aware of what is happening throughout the business.

### Framework & Criteria

1. Log every transaction from your business bank accounts, payment processors, and credit cards on the Transactions tab.
2. Only log real cash. Don't count signed contracts, unpaid invoices, or money still sitting with a processor. If it's not in your account yet, it hasn't happened yet.
3. Set Type to Transfer for money moving between your own accounts. Transfers don't count as revenue or expense, or you'll count the same money twice.
4. Pick a real Category for every transaction so you can see where the money is actually going. Don't dump everything into a catch-all.
5. Check the Dashboard tab. Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit, and Net Margin pull automatically from your Transactions tab, by month.
6. Check the Category Breakdown tab. Every Category pulls automatically by month, so you can see exactly where revenue is coming from and where expenses are going.
7. Update the Transactions tab at least monthly, weekly if you can.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Net Margin Tracker Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14bM97ysiCnIcbXezkrXs1ZbMrpy_NIb0yl6Pi8k7kGU/edit?usp=sharing).** This gives you the Transactions, Dashboard, and Category Breakdown tabs, color-coded so editable cells and auto-calculated cells are easy to tell apart at a glance. [in this skill: assets/templates/net-margin-tracker-template/]
2. **In the transactions tab, replace the placeholder Accounts with the real accounts you use.** Pull from every business bank account, payment processor, and credit card, not just your main checking account.
3. **In the transactions tab, update the Category dropdown to match your real income and expense streams.** Use real categories like Client Revenue, Payroll, Contractors, Advertising, Software, Processing Fees, Office, Travel, Legal, Accounting, Refunds, and Taxes. If you add a new category, add a matching row on the Category Breakdown tab or it won't show up.
4. **In the transactions tab, confirm your Currency dropdown covers every currency you actually use.** Currency and Conversion Rate default to USD and 1. Conversion Rate does not calculate itself, so enter it manually for any transaction in another currency, matching the date of that transaction.
5. **In the transactions tab, backfill your last 6-12 months of transactions.** Only count real cash that actually hit or left an account, never signed contracts, unpaid invoices, expected revenue, or payouts still sitting with a processor. To backfill faster, export your raw transactions and give ChatGPT your exact column headers, then ask it to format the data into those columns. Review everything before it goes in the Sheet.
6. **Check your Net Margin on the Dashboard tab.** Net Profit = Total Revenue − Total Expenses. Net Margin = Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue. Look at whether your Net Margin is holding steady month to month or dropping, and put your most recent month into Actual Net Margin on your Reverse Engineering Sheet.
7. **Check where your money is going on the Category Breakdown tab.** Revenue categories show as positive, expense categories show as negative, for the month and the year. Look at your biggest expense categories first, that's where you'll find the waste.
8. **Fix any number that looks wrong on the Transactions tab.** The Dashboard and Category Breakdown are auto-calculated, so the fix is never on those tabs. Go back to the Transactions tab and find the bad entry, like a missing Type or a transfer counted as revenue.
9. **Log new transactions on the Transactions tab weekly.** Staying on top of this weekly makes it much harder for someone to steal from the company. You'll notice unexplained expenses, duplicate charges, missing deposits, and unusual payments much faster because you're actually watching it.

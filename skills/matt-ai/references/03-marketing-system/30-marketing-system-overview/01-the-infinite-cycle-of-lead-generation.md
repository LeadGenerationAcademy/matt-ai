I call this the Infinite Cycle of Lead Generation. You are on the positive or negative side of this at all times.

It explains so much about how you feel in your business and why people behave the way they do. It is much easier to hold your price and turn down a bad client when you have other people to speak to. If you have barely any leads and bills to pay, you feel that pressure on every call. You start accepting things you know you shouldn’t, and then you have to spend your time dealing with them.

After a while, you are stressed, working all the time, and still need more money. You can follow the decisions backwards and see how you ended up there.

You are on the positive or negative side of this cycle at all times. If you don't specifically get yourself on the positive time by generating a fuckload of leads, you are usually on the negative side by default.

##### The Positive Cycle

More leads → you become less needy → selling becomes easier → you can turn away nightmare clients → you bring in more good clients → more referrals → more leads.

That growing demand also lets you raise your prices → more margin → you can afford better hires → less of your time goes into fulfillment → you have more time and money to reinvest in growth → more leads.

Those new leads feed back into the beginning, and the cycle continues.

##### The Negative Cycle

Fewer leads → you become more needy → selling becomes harder → you accept nightmare clients because you need the money → fewer good clients → fewer referrals → fewer leads.

With less demand, you feel pressure to lower your prices → less margin → you struggle to afford the help you need → more of your time goes into fulfillment and correcting mistakes → less time and money to reinvest in growth → fewer leads.

You arrive at the next sales call even more dependent on making the sale, and the cycle continues.

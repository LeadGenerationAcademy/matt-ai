Leverage means getting more out than you put in.

If you spend an hour explaining something to one person, you have reached one person. If you record that explanation, thousands of people can watch it without you spending another hour with each of them.

Leverage is not a yes or no. It is a question of how much. The same recording can reach ten people or ten thousand. You did the work once, but what you get out of that work can keep increasing.

This funnel builds leverage in four ways:

- Media: We create content that can keep being watched, read, and shared.
- Capital: We use paid ads to put that content and our offers in front of more people.
- Labor: We hire and train people to do the work, nurture leads, and serve clients.
- Code: We use AI, automations, and workflows to handle work that would otherwise need to be done manually.

These work together. You can put more money behind content you already made. Your software can handle the additional leads, and trained people can take over work you previously did yourself.

That is what we are aiming for with unlimited scale: your personal time stops being the ceiling on how much the business can do.

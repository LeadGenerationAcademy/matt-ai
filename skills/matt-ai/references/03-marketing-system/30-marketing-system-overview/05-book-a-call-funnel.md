We will need a book a call funnel (lol). That is a fundamental piece of marketing for any service business, and it is truly amazing how badly people screw this up. There will be multiple courses later about how to do this.

For now, I just want you to understand what you are looking at. Here are the different parts and what they do.

- Paid Ads: The ads people see that send them into your funnel.
- Content: Your videos, posts, and other material that help people find you and learn about what you do.
- Opt-In Page: Where someone enters their contact details to get your free resource.
- Lead Magnet: The free resource you give them.
- Welcome Flow: The first emails they receive after signing up.
- Sales Page: The page that explains your offer and gives someone a reason to book a call.
- VSL: Your video sales letter. A video on the sales page that explains and sells your offer.
- Application Form: The questions someone answers so you can determine whether they are a fit before booking.
- Downsell: A smaller or less expensive offer for someone who is not a fit for your main offer.
- Book A Call: Where they choose an available time on your calendar.
- Thank-You Page: The page they see after booking, with instructions on what to do next.
- Breakout Videos: Separate videos that explain particular parts of your offer or answer common questions.
- Pre-Call Flow: The messages they receive before the appointment to help them prepare and remember to attend.
- Selfie Video: A short personal video you send to someone who has booked.
- Retargeting Ads: Ads shown to people who have already interacted with you. Here, we use them to stay in front of people before their calls.
- Manual Reach-Out: Personally contacting someone before their appointment.
- Sales Call: The conversation where you learn about their situation, explain how you can help, and try to make the sale.
- Book Next Meeting: Arranging another conversation when you need one.
- Follow-Up Call: That next conversation, where you continue from what was discussed.
- Follow-Up Flow: The messages sent after the sales call while someone is still deciding.
- Close: When they agree to buy and complete the purchase.
- Onboarding: Getting the customer set up so you can begin delivering the service.

Your company should still have its own profiles.

These profiles serve a different purpose. They should answer questions, display proof, and make the company look established when someone searches for it.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create Or Claim Your Company [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) Profile.** When someone searches for your company, these profiles should appear, if they click on one, they should immediately find proof or receive an answer to an important question.
2. **Create Or Claim Your Company [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/) Profile.** This is one of the company platforms worth claiming even if it's not your primary posting channel.
3. **Create Or Claim Your Company [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) Profile.** This is one of the company platforms worth claiming even if it's not your primary posting channel.
4. **Create Or Claim Your Company [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/) Profile.** This is one of the company platforms worth claiming even if it's not your primary posting channel.
5. **Create Or Claim Your Company [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/) Profile.** This is one of the company platforms worth claiming even if it's not your primary posting channel.
6. **Create Or Claim Your Company [X Or Twitter](https://x.com/) Profile.** This is one of the company platforms worth claiming even if it's not your primary posting channel.
7. **Set Up Your Company Newsletter.** This gives the company its own owned, algorithm-independent channel separate from your personal one.
8. **Add Your Company Name, Logo, Description, Website, Contact Information, And Branding To Every Profile.** This keeps every company profile looking like the same real, established business.
9. **Publish FAQ Responses And Answers To Common Objections On Your Company Profiles.** This preempts hesitation before it turns a prospect away.
10. **Publish Customer Testimonials And Video Testimonials On Your Company Profiles.** This gives prospects proof from other customers, not just company claims.
11. **Publish Screenshots Of Results And Case Studies On Your Company Profiles.** This shows the full before-and-after journey, not just an isolated claim.
12. **Publish Customer Interviews And Product Demonstrations On Your Company Profiles.** This shows prospects both real customer experiences and the product itself in action.
13. **Publish Explanations Of How Your Service Works On Your Company Profiles.** This answers one of the core questions a prospect has before buying.
14. **Confirm Every Profile Is Complete And Consistent Before Moving On.** An unfinished or inconsistent profile can look worse than not having one at all.

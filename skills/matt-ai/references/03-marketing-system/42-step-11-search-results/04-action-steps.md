The Theory explains why you need to show up when someone searches for your personal name or your company name, since prospects who see your ads, content, or outreach without immediately acting will often go search for you before deciding whether to trust you.

This module is where you actually build that search presence.

You will claim high-authority profiles and review websites, build out your personal and company websites, set up personal and company social profiles, and hire a Proof Master to keep collecting and posting proof across all of it.

Do not leave any profile unfinished or inconsistent, since a half-completed or mismatched profile can make you look less credible than having no profile there at all.

### Table of Contents

1. **High-Domain Authority Websites:** Create or claim profiles on established, high-ranking websites and fill each one out completely and consistently with your name, company, description, and proof.
2. **Review Websites:** Create or claim profiles on Clutch, UpCity, GoodFirms, and Trustpilot, then build review requests into your project completion, customer success, account management, and offboarding processes.
3. **Your Personal Website/Name:** Buy the cleanest domain variation of your name and build a personal website with your biography, proof, and content so it becomes the top search result for your name.
4. **Your Company Website/Name:** Review every important page on your company website for clear on-page SEO, then set up Google Search Console and Analytics and submit your sitemap so Google can properly index it.
5. **Your Personal Socials:** Create or claim your personal profiles across YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X, and a newsletter, and publish your primary content through them.
6. **Your Company Socials:** Create or claim your company profiles across the same platforms and publish FAQs, testimonials, case studies, and demonstrations that answer prospects' questions before they buy.

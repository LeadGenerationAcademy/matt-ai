People connect more strongly with people than companies.

Your primary content should be published through your personal profiles.

These profiles belong to you and remain valuable even if you close your company, change its name, or sell it.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create Or Claim Your Personal [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) Profile.** Your company can change, your personal name and reputation remain with you, build the audience around yourself first.
2. **Create Or Claim Your Personal [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/) Profile.** This is one of the personal platforms worth claiming early, even if you're not yet posting there consistently.
3. **Create Or Claim Your Personal [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) Profile.** This is one of the personal platforms worth claiming early, even if you're not yet posting there consistently.
4. **Create Or Claim Your Personal [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/) Profile.** This is one of the personal platforms worth claiming early, even if you're not yet posting there consistently.
5. **Create Or Claim Your Personal [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/) Profile.** This is one of the personal platforms worth claiming early, even if you're not yet posting there consistently.
6. **Create Or Claim Your Personal [X Or Twitter](http://x.com/) Profile.** This is one of the personal platforms worth claiming early, even if you're not yet posting there consistently.
7. **Set Up Your Personal Email Newsletter.** This is your most owned, algorithm-independent personal channel.
8. **Add Your Name, Headshot, Biography, Website, Company, And Contact Information To Every Profile.** Make it obvious that every profile belongs to the same person, use the same name, headshot, biography, website, company, and contact information everywhere.
9. **Publish Your Long-Form And Short-Form Videos Personally.** This is your primary content, published through the profiles that belong to you personally.
10. **Publish Your Written Posts, Frameworks, And Opinions Personally.** This keeps your personal profiles the main home for your thinking and positioning.
11. **Publish Your Lessons And Case Studies Personally.** This gives your personal profiles proof and educational value, not just video and written content.
12. **Publish Your Newsletter Issues Personally.** This keeps your owned audience fed directly from your personal channel.
13. **Confirm Every Profile Is Complete And Consistent Before Moving On.** An unfinished or inconsistent profile can look worse than not having one at all.

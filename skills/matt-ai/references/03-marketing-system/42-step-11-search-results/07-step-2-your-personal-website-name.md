You should always own a domain containing some variation of your name. This protects your name and gives you a permanent website that you control. You don't want someone else buying the best domain for your name.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose And Purchase The Cleanest Available Domain Variation Of Your Name.** Try [FirstNameLastName.com](http://FirstNameLastName.com), [FirstNameMiddleInitialLastName.com](http://FirstNameMiddleInitialLastName.com), [FirstNameMiddleNameLastName.com](http://FirstNameMiddleNameLastName.com), and [FirstNameLastNameOfficial.com](http://FirstNameLastNameOfficial.com) first. Matthew Larsen is a relatively common name, so mine is [MatthewBLarsen.com](http://MatthewBLarsen.com). It's insane how bad it is when someone else owns the website to your name, because they basically control your name and your reputation.
2. **Purchase Other Variations Of Your Name And Redirect Them To Your Primary Website.** I would always try to buy every variation you can. Redirecting them keeps all of that traffic pointed at the one site you actually maintain.
3. **Build Your Personal Website.** It should make it immediately clear who you are, what you do, and why someone should trust you. A domain with nothing on it does very little for you, so fill it out.
4. **Add Your Full Name, Professional Biography, And Contact Information To The Website.** This is the basic information every visitor needs first, plus a direct way to reach you.
5. **Set Up A Professional Email Address And Your Email Newsletter On Your Personal Domain.** This reinforces that the domain belongs to you and ties your owned audience to it. I send my newsletter from mine, and it really helps.
6. **Record And Add A Video Introducing Yourself.** This lets visitors see and hear you directly.
7. **Add Testimonials, Case Studies, Customer Results, Media Appearances, Interviews, And Articles To The Website.** This is proof from customers and other credible sources, beyond your own claims about yourself.
8. **Add Links To Your Important Profiles And Companies.** This connects your personal website to the rest of your online presence.
9. **Decide Whether A Review-Specific Property Makes Sense For Your Situation.** This is a website or YouTube channel built around searches like "[Your Name] Reviews." If your business relies heavily on SEO, don't do this carelessly, because thin or duplicated websites can create more problems than they solve.
10. **Search Your Full Name And Its Variations In An Incognito Browser.** Search your name, then "[Your Name] Reviews," "[Your Name] Company," "[Your Name] Industry," and "[Your Name] Scam." This shows you what a prospect actually sees when they research you.
11. **Confirm Your Personal Website Ranks As One Of The Top Results.** Whether your name is common or unusual, the goal is the same. Fill your name's search results with websites, videos, profiles, proof, and content connected to you.

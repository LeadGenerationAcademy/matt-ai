You and your company need to appear when someone searches for either name. Not showing up at all is bad, it makes you look new, unknown, or possibly not real. The fastest place to start is by creating or claiming profiles on established websites that already rank well in Google.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **List The Major General Profiles And The Ones Most Relevant To Your Industry.** The big general ones are sites like Gumroad, Skool, Whop, blogging platforms, and every social media platform. Then add the directories and associations your ICP or peers would recognize in your industry.
2. **Create Or Claim Every Profile From Your List.** So many people will see your ad or get your outreach, do nothing, then Google you and see what they find. Claiming an existing listing also stops someone else from controlling how your business appears there.
3. **Add Your Name, Company Name, Description, Website, Address, Phone Number, Logo, And Headshot To Every Profile.** Use the exact same information everywhere. Incomplete or inconsistent profiles can create more confusion than having no profile at all.
4. **Add A Clear Description Of What You Sell, Proof, And Answers To Common Questions Wherever The Platform Allows It.** Proof means testimonials, case studies, and photographs. This gives visitors a real reason to trust you beyond the basic business information.
5. **Ask Satisfied Customers For Honest Reviews On The Platforms That Support Them.** If you're afraid of honest reviews, it's probably because you know what you're selling isn't good enough. Improve what you sell and build a search presence you're proud for people to find.
6. **Confirm Every Profile Is Complete And Consistent Before Moving On.** An unfinished or inconsistent profile can look worse than not having one at all.

### Completion Audit

- Did you list the major general profiles and the ones most relevant to your industry?
- Did you create or claim every profile from your list?
- Did you add your name, company name, description, website, address, phone number, logo, and headshot to every profile?
- Did you add a clear description of what you sell, proof, and answers to common questions wherever the platform allows it?
- Did you ask satisfied customers for honest reviews on the platforms that support them?
- Is every profile complete and consistent?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Review Your Homepage, About Page, Service Pages, Product Pages, Case Studies, And Contact Page.** Every page should clearly explain what it's about, using the language your customers would actually use when searching for it. The About page should confirm your credibility, and the Contact page should give visitors a working way to reach you.
2. **Check The Page Title, Meta Description, Headline, URL, Copy, Images, And Internal Links On Every Page.** These are what decide how each page appears in search results. Some company websites are harder to rank than others depending on the keywords, but you want as many of your own pages ranking as possible.
3. **Send Screenshots Of Each Page And Its Metadata To ChatGPT Or Claude And Ask It To Review The On-Page SEO.** Ask it to find anything missing, unclear, duplicated, or incorrectly configured.
4. **Review Every AI Recommendation, Then Publish The Changes You Agree With.** Don't blindly accept every recommendation. Only the changes you actually reviewed should go live.
5. **Set Up [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) And [Google Analytics](https://developers.google.com/analytics), And Verify Ownership Of Your Domain.** Search Console tells you how your site is performing in search, and Analytics shows you how visitors behave on it. Make sure both are connected to the correct domain.
6. **Find Your Website's Sitemap, Submit It Through Google Search Console, And Confirm Google Can Read It.** The sitemap is the file that tells Google which pages on your site exist. Search Console should show it was successfully processed.
7. **Check Your Important Pages Using The URL Inspection Tool.** This lets you verify each page's indexing status directly instead of assuming the sitemap covers it.
8. **Search Your Company Name In An Incognito Browser And Confirm Everything Appears Correctly.** Your official website, title, and description should all show up, with the correct pages beneath it and no broken or outdated results.

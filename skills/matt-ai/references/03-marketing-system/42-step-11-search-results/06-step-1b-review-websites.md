Create profiles on third-party review websites so prospects find independent proof when they search for your company.

### Sign up

- [Clutch](https://clutch.co/)
- [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/)
- [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create Or Claim Your [Clutch](https://clutch.co/), [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/), And [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/) Profiles.** If you're terrified to ask for reviews, it's probably because what you're selling isn't good enough. You don't have to do this right away, but fix your service until you're not scared of reviews.
2. **Add Your Company Name, Logo, Website, Location, Contact Information, And Company Description To Every Profile.** This is the basic information a visitor needs to know who you are and how to reach you.
3. **Add Your Services, Industries Served, Customer Type, And Pricing To Every Profile.** This helps a visitor quickly confirm whether you're relevant to them and sets expectations before they reach out.
4. **Add Case Studies And Portfolio Examples To Every Profile.** This gives visitors concrete proof beyond the reviews themselves.
5. **Create A Direct Review Link For Every Profile.** This is the link you send customers when you ask for a review. Never write reviews for customers or purchase fake reviews.
6. **Add A Review Request To Your Project Completion, Customer Success, Account Management, And Offboarding Processes.** Don't collect five reviews and stop. Every customer should automatically get a request after reaching a successful result.
7. **Ask Satisfied Customers To Leave An Honest Review.** Every review should explain what problem the customer had, why they hired you, what you did, what result they received, and what it was like working with you.
8. **Respond Professionally To Every Positive And Negative Review.** This shows prospects you're actually engaged with your customers.
9. **Confirm Every Profile Is Complete And Consistent Before Moving On.** An unfinished or inconsistent profile can look worse than not having one at all.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create or claim your Clutch, GoodFirms, and Trustpilot profiles?
- Did you add your company name, logo, website, location, contact information, and company description to every profile?
- Did you add your services, industries served, customer type, and pricing to every profile?
- Did you add case studies and portfolio examples to every profile?
- Did you create a direct review link for every profile?
- Did you add a review request to your Project Completion, Customer Success, Account Management, and Offboarding Processes?
- Did you ask satisfied customers to leave an honest review?
- Did you respond professionally to every positive and negative review?
- Is every profile complete and consistent?

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Banner:** confirmation message telling them their lead magnet is on its way to their inbox, and to watch the video below if they want [X Result].
2. **Headline:** encourage them to watch the VSL below and get [X Result].
3. **Subheadline:** qualify who this is for (your ICP).
4. **VSL** (with thumbnail + captions): who this doesn't work for, who this has worked for, how this works, why this works, next steps.
5. **Checklist:** what they'll get from the call.
6. **Testimonials:** video testimonials with name and role, text testimonials with name and role.
7. **CTA section:** headline that pushes them to book the call, the same VSL as above the fold, a subheadline telling them to book with you/your closer, and a CTA button.
8. **Footer:** logo, star rating, Privacy/Terms of Service/Contact Us links, and consent and results disclaimer text.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Webflow](https://webflow.com/signup).** Skip this if you already have a Webflow account from setting up a previous page in this funnel.
2. **Make a copy of the [Opt-In Confirmation Page Webflow Project](https://preview.webflow.com/preview/leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28?utm_medium=preview_link&utm_source=designer&utm_content=leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28&preview=70d6f83c609c7159888f9324c39b3239&pageId=6a870f0282cb75bab077f719&workflow=preview).** This gives you the banner, VSL, checklist, testimonials, CTA, and footer sections already built. You're filling in content, not designing the page. If you've already made a copy of the shared project for a previous page, just navigate to this page inside it instead of making another copy.
3. **Add Domain To Webflow.** Skip this if your domain is already connected from a previous page in this funnel.
4. **Decide What Your Low-Commitment Call Offer Is.** This page is the bridge between someone who wants your lead magnet and someone who wants your service. Give them a very low-risk way to get on the phone, like a 5, 10, or 15 minute call, or something custom you can do for them, like redesigning their above the fold.
5. **Update Banner To Confirm Their Lead Magnet Is On Its Way.** Tell them the lead magnet is on its way to their inbox, and to watch the video below if they want [X Result]. Make it obvious they actually got it, or they'll be confused about what just happened.
6. **Update Headline To Encourage Them To Watch The VSL To Get X Result.** Name the actual result the VSL helps them get. Someone who just downloaded your lead magnet may think you only make content, with no idea you can do the work for them.
7. **Update Subheadline To Qualify Who This Is For.** Name your ICP. The right person should recognize themselves, and everyone else should know this isn't for them.
8. **Record And Add Your VSL With A Thumbnail.** This can be a miniature VSL made specifically for the call offer you decided on above. Cover who this doesn't work for, who it has worked for, how it works, why it works, and the next step to book a call, in that order. Add captions, and make the thumbnail give the right person a clear reason to press play.
9. **Update Checklist To What They'll Get From The Call.** List what they'll actually walk away with from the call, so they know what booking gets them.
10. **Add Your Video Testimonials.** Include each person's name and role. Most people won't press play, so put the strongest result in the thumbnail.
11. **Add Your Text Testimonials.** Include each person's name and role, and lead each one with its strongest result.
12. **Update CTA Headline To Push Them To Book The Call.** Make the ask direct. By this point they've seen the VSL and the proof, and they should know exactly where to go if they want to talk.
13. **Add Your VSL To The CTA Section.** Use the exact same VSL from the top of the page. This gives anyone who scrolled past it a second chance to watch.
14. **Update CTA Subheadline To Tell Them To Book With You/Your Closer.** Name who they'll actually be speaking with, so they know who to expect when they join the call.
15. **Update CTA Button Link To Your Booking Page.** This is your calendar link. Click the button yourself before sending traffic and make sure it lands on the right page. A broken booking link kills the entire page's purpose.
16. **Add Logo And Star Rating To Footer.** Use the same accurate star rating and client count from your Opt-In Page so the numbers stay consistent across your funnel.
17. **Update Footer Links.** Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Contact Us all need to actually work, not just be placeholders.
18. **Update Disclaimer Text.** Include both your consent and results disclaimer text.

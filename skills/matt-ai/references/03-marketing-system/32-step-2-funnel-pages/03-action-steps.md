The Theory covers every page in the funnel, from the opt-in through the DQ page, and how traffic moves between them depending on where it came from.

This section is where you actually build every one of those pages.

You'll customize the Opt-In Page and its confirmation page, build out the Sales Page above and below the fold, set up the Call Booked Page and its confirmation page, and optionally add a DQ Page for anyone who doesn't qualify.

### Table of Contents

1. **Opt In Page:** Customize the Opt-In Page template with your branding, ICP callout, lead magnet name, and form so you capture the lead in exchange for your lead magnet.
2. **Opt In Confirmation Page:** Customize the confirmation page with your VSL, checklist, and testimonials so the lead knows their lead magnet is on the way and books a call for your main service.
3. **Sales Page Above The Fold:** Customize the top of your Sales Page with your headline, subheadline, and VSL so visitors watch your VSL and book a call.
4. **Rest Of Sales Page:** Fill in the logo bar, testimonials, case studies, reviews, and client wins below the fold so enough proof stacks up that the result feels achievable and they book a call.
5. **Call Booked Page:** Customize the headline, subheadline, and Calendly embed so qualified leads who land here book a call with minimal friction.
6. **Call Booked Confirmation Page:** Add your VSL, FAQ videos, reviews, and client wins so leads are educated before the call and actually show up.
7. **DQ Page (Optional):** Customize the headline, subheadline, and CTA to point unqualified leads toward your low-end offer instead of losing them entirely.

# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #2: Funnel Pages
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=4718bf0afa5b4d03876de97499526ee4

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Webflow Setup

- Signed up for Webflow
- Added your domain to Webflow

### Opt-In Page

- Downloaded the Opt-In Page Webflow Project
- Added your logo to the nav bar
- Updated the star rating to yours
- Updated the ICP callout to yours
- Updated the headline to your lead magnet name
- Updated the subheadline to what your lead magnet is
- Updated the CTA button if your lead magnet isn't a download
- Connected the form to your email or CRM

### Opt-In Confirmation Page

- Downloaded the Opt-In Confirmation Page Webflow Project
- Updated the banner to confirm their lead magnet is on its way
- Updated the headline to encourage them to watch the VSL for their result
- Updated the subheadline to qualify who this is for
- Recorded and added your VSL with a thumbnail
- Updated the checklist to what they'll get from the call
- Added your video testimonials
- Added your text testimonials
- Updated the CTA headline to push them to book the call
- Added your VSL to the CTA section
- Updated the CTA subheadline to tell them who they'll be booking with
- Updated the CTA button link to your booking page
- Added your logo and star rating to the footer
- Updated the footer links and disclaimer text

### Sales Page (Above The Fold)

- Downloaded the Sales Page Webflow Project
- Added your logo to the nav bar
- Updated the star rating to yours
- Updated the ICP callout to yours
- Updated the headline to your result in three lines or less
- Updated the subheadline to how you get them that result, in three lines or less
- Recorded and added your VSL with a thumbnail and captions
- Updated the CTA button link to your booking page

### Sales Page (Rest Of Page)

- Updated the logo bar header with your real client/agency count
- Added all your client logos
- Added your video testimonials to the Hear It From Our Clients section
- Updated the first CTA button link to your booking page
- Added your video testimonials to the What Our Clients Have To Say grid
- Added your case studies, each linked to its one-pager PDF
- Added your written testimonials under each category section
- Linked each written testimonial to its original review
- Added your Trustpilot reviews
- Updated the second CTA button link to your booking page
- Added as many client win screenshots as possible
- Added your logo and star rating to the footer, or copied it from a previous page
- Updated the footer links and disclaimer text

### Call Booked Page

- Downloaded the Call Booked Page Webflow Project
- Added your logo to the nav bar
- Updated the headline telling them to book a call
- Updated the subheadline with genuine scarcity or urgency
- Connected your Calendly embed so it only allows booking within a 72 hour window

### Call Booked Confirmation Page

- Downloaded the Call Booked Confirmation Page Webflow Project
- Added your logo to the nav bar
- Updated the star rating to yours
- Updated the ICP callout to yours
- Updated the headline to confirm the booking and point to the VSL
- Updated the subheadline to what the VSL answers
- Recorded and added your "Watch This First" VSL covering your top questions and objections
- Added the screenshot for replying to your confirmation text
- Added or used the template screenshots for accepting the calendar invitation
- Recorded your Top 5 FAQ videos with thumbnails
- Added your Trustpilot reviews
- Added as many client win screenshots as you have
- Updated the footer links and disclaimer text

### DQ Page (Optional)

- Downloaded the DQ Page Webflow Project
- Added your logo to the nav bar
- Updated the star rating to yours
- Updated the ICP callout to yours
- Updated the headline to point to your low-end offer
- Updated the subheadline to elaborate on your low-end offer
- Updated the CTA button to your low-end offer
- Added your logo and star rating to the footer, or copied it from a previous page
- Updated the footer links and disclaimer text

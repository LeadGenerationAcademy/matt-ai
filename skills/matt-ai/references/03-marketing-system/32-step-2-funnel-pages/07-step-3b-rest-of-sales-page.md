### Framework & Criteria

There are SO many ways to make a sales page that I do not want to box you in. However, here are some general guidelines as to what should be on there:

1. Above the fold
2. Logos
3. Sales material (this could be one thing or 10 things such as how it works, sales letter, before vs. after, timeline, etc)
4. Proof

The ONLY requirement as far as I am concerned is that someone should be able to get everything they need to book a call WITHOUT having to watch even a second of your VSL. This is SO important, especially as you go to higher levels of ICP.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Your Existing [Sales Page Webflow Project](https://preview.webflow.com/preview/leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28?utm_medium=preview_link&utm_source=designer&utm_content=leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28&preview=70d6f83c609c7159888f9324c39b3239&workflow=preview) Page.** This is the same page you built the above-the-fold section on in Step #3A, and you're continuing below the fold. Everything down here should give someone what they need to book a call without watching a second of your VSL.
2. **In the Logo Section, update the header text with your real client/agency count.** Use a real, specific number, not a rounded-up guess.
3. **In the Logo Section, add all your client logos.** Put the most impressive and relevant logos first.
4. **In the Hear It From Our Clients section, add your video testimonials.** Include each person's First Name and Industry. Most people won't press play, so put the strongest result, ideally a dollar figure, in the thumbnail.
5. **Update the first CTA button link to your booking page.** Click the button yourself before sending traffic and make sure it lands on the right page.
6. **In the What Our Clients Have To Say section, add your video testimonials to the grid.** This is a separate testimonial section from Hear It From Our Clients above, so pull in a different set of videos rather than reusing the same ones.
7. **In the Read Our Case Studies section, add your case studies and link each to its one-pager PDF.** Pair each with a client logo, name, and a specific metric increase. Each case study should cover the result, how long it took, and the details of the process, without revealing anything confidential.
8. **In each Written Testimonial Category section, add your written testimonials.** The template has 4 category sections built in. Group your testimonials by the objection each one overcomes, and put the strongest one first in each group.
9. **In each Written Testimonial Category section, link each testimonial to its original review.** This lets them verify it on a third-party site you don't control, instead of just taking a quote you could have written yourself.
10. **In the Our Client Reviews section, add your Trustpilot reviews.** Third-party reviews carry more weight than testimonials on your own site, because visitors know you can't edit or remove them.
11. **Update the second CTA button link to your booking page.** Click this button yourself too and make sure it lands on the right page.
12. **In the Client Wins section, add as many client win screenshots as possible.** Keep each screenshot in its original format, like Slack, iMessage or email, and highlight the result. This section works best in volume, so add as many real screenshots as you have.
13. **In the Footer, add your logo and star rating, or copy your footer from a previous page.** If you've already built a Footer section on a previous page in this funnel, copy that whole section here instead of rebuilding it.
14. **In the Footer, update the links and disclaimer text.** Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Contact links all need to actually work, and the disclaimer text should include both your consent and results disclaimers.

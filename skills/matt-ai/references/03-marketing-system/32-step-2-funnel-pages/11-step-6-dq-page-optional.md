This is the page leads land on after they fill out your qualifying form and don't qualify for your main offer. Keep it simple, and point the CTA toward a low-end offer instead of turning them away entirely.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Nav bar:** with your logo.
2. **Star rating:** X.X rated by over X+ clients.
3. **Banner:** with your ICP call out.
4. **Headline:** pointing to your low-end offer.
5. **Subheadline:** elaborating more on your low-end offer.
6. **CTA button:** your low-end offer.
7. **Footer:** logo, star rating, Privacy/Terms of Service/Contact Us links, and consent and results disclaimer text.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Decide What You'll Offer People Who Don't Qualify.** If you have nothing to offer them, this page is pointless, so send them to a simple end screen and skip the rest of these steps. The best option is usually a course teaching your service. When someone fails my application, I send them to a daily automated webinar pitching a $5K course, and it brings in one or two sales a day.
2. **Sign up for [Webflow](https://webflow.com/signup).** Skip this if you already have a Webflow account from setting up a previous page in this funnel.
3. **Make a copy of the [DQ Page Webflow Project](https://preview.webflow.com/preview/leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28?utm_medium=preview_link&utm_source=designer&utm_content=leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28&preview=70d6f83c609c7159888f9324c39b3239&pageId=6a87ce6ee152d76d4c9eb783&workflow=preview).** If you've already made a copy of the shared project for a previous page, just navigate to this page inside it instead of making another copy.
4. **Add Domain To Webflow.** Skip this if your domain is already connected from a previous page in this funnel.
5. **Add Logo To Nav Bar.** Use the same logo and branding as the rest of your funnel.
6. **Update Star Rating To Yours.** Keep it accurate and matching what you've used on your other pages.
7. **Update ICP Callout To Yours.** This page is only for leads who filled out your qualifying form and didn't qualify for your main offer, so the callout should reflect who actually lands here.
8. **Update Headline To Point To Your Low-End Offer.** This keeps you from losing them entirely just because they didn't qualify for the main offer. Build it the same way you built your sales page above the fold.
9. **Update Subheadline To Elaborate More On Your Low-End Offer.** Give them enough context to understand why this offer still fits them.
10. **Update CTA Button To Your Low-End Offer.** Point the action toward this offer instead of a dead end.
11. **Add Your Logo And Star Rating To The Footer, Or Copy It From A Previous Page.** If you've already built a Footer section on a previous page in this funnel, copy that whole section here instead of rebuilding it.
12. **Update Footer Links And Disclaimer Text.** Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Contact links all need to actually work, and the disclaimer text should include both your consent and results disclaimers.

Your application is the qualifying form someone fills out before they get access to your calendar.

It checks whether they have the budget, authority, need and timing for your offer, and it collects the information you will actually use on the sales call.

A full calendar does nothing for you if the people sitting on it cannot buy.

![image](https://assets.skool.com/f/1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce/051cf08271fc4b00b302cf08667e58fd63bdddab99844fdf9a07b9d275629019.png)

### Framework & Criteria

- At least four DQ questions
  - Budget
  - Authority
  - Need
  - Start Date
- At least four questions that help with sales process
- Contact information
  - Name
  - Email
  - Phone
  - Website

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Build Your Form In Typeform Or Your Website Builder.** Link it to, or embed it in, the call to action button on your sales page. Only qualified people should be able to book a call. Every booking teaches your ad platform's pixel who to find more of, so letting unqualified people book gets you more unqualified leads.
2. **Write Your Budget Question.** Find out whether they can afford your offer before they get on a call. Decide which answers qualify before you start sending people through the form.
3. **Write Your Authority Question.** Find out whether the person filling this out can actually say yes, with something like "Are you the business owner?"
4. **Write Your Need Question.** Find out whether they have the problem your offer solves right now. Anyone who answers "Just browsing" shouldn't get on your calendar.
5. **Write Your Start Date Question.** Someone who wants to start this month and someone looking six months out are two different conversations. If they want to start next quarter, don't let them book.
6. **Write At Least Four Questions That Help With Your Sales Process.** Your closer uses these answers to prepare for the call. At my insurance agency, we ask "Have you bought leads before?", "Do you have a CRM?" and "How many sales reps do you have?"
7. **Add Your Name, Email, Phone And Website Fields.** Your team needs all four to reach them and research their business before the call.
8. **Send Qualified Applicants To Your Call Booked Page.** They've already shown they're interested by completing the form, so get them to the calendar with as little friction as possible.
9. **Send Unqualified Applicants To Your DQ Page.** Point them at your low-end offer, or a simple end screen if you don't have one.

### Completion Audit

- Did you build your form in Typeform or your website builder?
- Did you write your budget question?
- Did you write your authority question?
- Did you write your need question?
- Did you write your start date question?
- Did you write at least four questions that help with your sales process?
- Did you add your name, email, phone and website fields?
- Are qualified applicants sent to your Call Booked page?
- Are unqualified applicants sent to your DQ page?

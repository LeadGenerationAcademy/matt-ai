### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Webflow](https://webflow.com/signup).** Skip this if you already have a Webflow account from setting up a previous page in this funnel.
2. **Make a copy of the [Call Booked Confirmation Page Webflow Project](https://preview.webflow.com/preview/leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28?utm_medium=preview_link&utm_source=designer&utm_content=leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28&preview=70d6f83c609c7159888f9324c39b3239&pageId=6a870f0282cb75bab077f70e&workflow=preview).** If you've already made a copy of the shared project for a previous page, just navigate to this page inside it instead of making another copy.
3. **Add Domain To Webflow.** Skip this if your domain is already connected from a previous page in this funnel.
4. **Add Logo To Nav Bar.** Use the same logo and branding as the pages they've already seen, so booking the call feels like the same process continuing.
5. **Update Star Rating To Yours.** Use the same rating and client count as your other pages, in the same format, like "4.8 rated by over 550+ clients."
6. **Update ICP Callout To Yours.** Use the same callout as your sales page, like "For Agency Owners." Keeping the name and branding consistent with the pages they've already seen makes the process feel continuous.
7. **Update Headline To Confirm The Booking And Point To The VSL.** Something like "You're In! Watch This Video For More Information." The first thing they need to see is that the call is booked. They shouldn't have to wonder whether the booking worked before you ask them to do anything else.
8. **Update Subheadline To What The VSL Answers.** Something like "There are 6-12 questions that we get all the time. Our founder [Your Name] made this video answering all of them." This tells them exactly why the video is worth watching.
9. **Record And Add Your Watch This First VSL.** Answer the top 6-12 questions and objections you'd otherwise have to answer live on the call. The number one reason people don't show up is that they don't have enough context or information to make the call worth it. When they arrive with those answers, the call becomes you answering specific questions and fitting the offer to their situation. Add a line under the video telling them there are three steps to finish below.
10. **Give Them A Reason To Act Now In The VSL.** Every time I announce a price increase, I get up to 20 times as many sales from people who were stuck in my sales process with no reason to act. You can't announce a price increase every time, and finding your own reason why now takes real thought.
11. **Add Screenshot For Step #1: Reply To Our Text.** Tell them you'll send a text shortly, and to look for it and reply if they can. Use a screenshot of your own confirmation text with a reply underneath, like the template, or a screenshot of your email if you email them instead. A reply confirms their phone number is real, lets you gauge their intentions, and gives your team a conversation to start from.
12. **Add Or Use Template Screenshots For Step #2: Accept The Invitation.** The calendar invite doesn't always land in their inbox, and sometimes it won't show up automatically at all. If they don't accept it, they'll forget, and there's almost no chance they show up. The screenshots show them how to find it, press the "I Know The Sender" button to add it to their calendar, and click Yes.
13. **Record Your Top 5 FAQ Videos With Thumbnails.** Pick the questions that come up most on your sales calls, and make each one a 2-5 minute video answering just that question. Put the question in big bold text at the top of each thumbnail, like "What Do I Get?"
14. **Add Your Trustpilot Reviews.** Try to use different proof here than on your sales page. If you don't have a lot of other proof, the same proof is fine, and you can just copy that whole section here and delete the placeholder.
15. **Add As Many Client Win Screenshots As You Have.** Copy this section from a previous page if you've already built it. This section works best in volume.
16. **Update Footer Links.** Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Contact links all need to actually work, not just be placeholders.
17. **Update Disclaimer Text.** Include both your consent and results disclaimer text.

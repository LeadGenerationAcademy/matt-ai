Your opt-in page is where a lead lands after they click a link to get your lead magnet. It should be very simple: you capture their contact info, and they get your lead magnet in return.

### Framework & Criteria

- Banner with name matching Facebook/Instagram Page
- Star rating
- High-constrast ICP call out
- Headline (desired result)
- Subheadline (how they will get that result)
- Form with name, email, phone
- Submit button

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Webflow](https://webflow.com/signup).** You need an account to duplicate and host the project, even if you just want to preview it first.
2. **Make a copy of the [Opt-In Page Webflow Project](https://preview.webflow.com/preview/leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28?utm_medium=preview_link&utm_source=designer&utm_content=leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28&preview=70d6f83c609c7159888f9324c39b3239&pageId=6a870f0282cb75bab077f717&workflow=preview).** If you don't want to use Webflow, publish the template and use a scraper tool to copy it into whatever builder you use.
3. **Add Domain To Webflow.** This puts the page on your own domain instead of a Webflow staging address.
4. **Add Logo To Nav Bar.** Use the same name and branding as the account people clicked from. If the page suddenly looks like a different business, you're making them figure out whether they're in the right place.
5. **Update Star Rating To Yours.** Use your real rating and client count, in the same "X.X rated by over X+ clients" format as the template. It gives them an early sign that other people have already worked with you.
6. **Update ICP Callout To Yours.** This is the short line that names who the lead magnet is for, like "For Agency Owners." It helps the right person recognize themselves. A lead magnet that appeals to everyone fills your list with people who will never buy your offer.
7. **Update Headline To Your Lead Magnet Name.** Use the same name and promise your content used when it told them about the resource. Calling it a free guide does very little on its own, so the headline should also make clear the problem it solves or the result it helps them get.
8. **Update Subheadline To What Your Lead Magnet Is.** Explain what's inside and how they can use it. If your traffic only comes from content, this can be the most basic page ever, because your content already sold them on the opt-in. If you ever run paid ads to this page, which I don't recommend for most people until you're well into the full system, you'll need to put more on it.
9. **Update CTA Button If Your Lead Magnet Is Not A Download.** When it is a download, something like "Download Your Free [Lead Magnet Name]" works. Otherwise, change it to "Get Access" or "Watch Now" depending on what the lead magnet actually is. The button needs to be easy to find and tap on a phone.
10. **Connect Form To Your Email/CRM.** The resource needs to be delivered immediately after they submit, and your Welcome Flow should start. If you already have a form in your CRM or email tool that you'd rather use, delete the Webflow form and embed yours.
11. **Set The Form Redirect URL To Your Opt In Confirmation Page.** In the form settings, set the redirect URL to your thank you page URL. After someone opts in, they should land on the page you build in Step #2: Opt In Confirmation Page.
12. **Publish The Page.** Then submit the form yourself before sending any traffic. Check that the resource arrives, your Welcome Flow starts, and you land on your Opt In Confirmation Page.
13. **Point All Your Content To This Page.** This is where you build your list. Mine is my 12 free trainings, and the link is on my YouTube, my Twitter, my LinkedIn, everything.

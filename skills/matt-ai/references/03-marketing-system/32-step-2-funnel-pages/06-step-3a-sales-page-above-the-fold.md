### Framework

- Banner with name matching Facebook/Instagram Page
- Star rating
- High-constrast ICP call out
- Headline (desired result)
- Subheadline (how they will get that result)
- VSL with thumbnail
- Submit button

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Webflow](https://webflow.com/signup).** Skip this if you already have a Webflow account from setting up a previous page in this funnel.
2. **Make a copy of the [Sales Page Webflow Project](https://preview.webflow.com/preview/leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28?utm_medium=preview_link&utm_source=designer&utm_content=leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28&preview=70d6f83c609c7159888f9324c39b3239&workflow=preview).** If you've already made a copy of the shared project for a previous page, just navigate to this page inside it instead of making another copy.
3. **Add Domain To Webflow.** Skip this if your domain is already connected from a previous page in this funnel.
4. **Add Logo To Nav Bar.** Use the same name and branding as the page and account used in whatever ad or content brought them here. Your ad and this page should have the same colors, the same font, and the same language. If it looks like a different business, you're making them figure out whether they're in the right place.
5. **Update Star Rating To Yours.** Use your real rating and client count, like "4.8 rated by over 5,000+ clients." It's a subtle form of social proof that gives them an early sign other people have already worked with you.
6. **Update ICP Callout To Yours.** This is the high-contrast line that names who the offer is for, like "For Annuities Advisors." If you look at heat maps, upwards of 70% of people do not scroll down at all, so the right person needs to recognize themselves here.
7. **Update Headline To Your Result In Three Lines Or Less.** Match it to your ad headline, and state a result they want in language they understand, like "Exclusive Annuity Leads Delivered Straight To Your CRM." A lot of people run a specific ad like "We will generate exclusive and qualified annuity leads and sync them directly to your CRM," then use a headline like "Scale Your Business." Nobody can consciously say what's wrong with that, but it feels off.
8. **Update Subheadline To How You Get Them The Result In Three Lines Or Less.** Elaborate on how they'll get the result in your headline, like "We use Meta ads to generate annuity leads in real time. We'll sync them to your CRM, all you have to do is close." It should add something useful to the headline.
9. **Record And Add Your VSL With A Thumbnail And Captions.** Show proof in the first 5 seconds, explain the full offer within 15 seconds, and show a second form of proof within those same 15 seconds. Then answer your FAQs in order of importance. Your ad, this section, and the first 15 seconds of your VSL should work together as one unit.
10. **Update CTA Button Link To Your Application Form.** Change the button text to something like "Book A Call," "Submit Application," or "Apply Now." Keep it above the fold too, so someone who's already interested can take the next step without scrolling. Test the link yourself before sending traffic.
11. **Add A Small Objection Handling Line (Optional).** You can use a small line above the fold to overcome one objection, like "PS: You'll get your first leads within 48 hours."
12. **Publish And Check The Page On Your Phone.** The mobile version matters most. Everything from the logo down to the button, including the whole VSL, needs to fit above the fold. The template is only a starting point, and you may need to reduce padding or font sizes to get there.

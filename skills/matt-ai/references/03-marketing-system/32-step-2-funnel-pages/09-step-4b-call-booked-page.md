This is the page qualified leads land on after they fill out your qualifying form. You want to keep this page simple, since they've already shown they are interested by filling out your form, and you want the least friction now for them to book a call.

### Framework & Criteria

- No more than 72h booking window (until you are at massive scale, then 7d)
- As many slots as possible
- Slots as soon as possible

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Webflow](http://webflow.com/signup).** Skip this if you already have a Webflow account from setting up a previous page in this funnel.
2. **Make a copy of the [Call Booked Page Webflow Project](https://preview.webflow.com/preview/leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28?utm_medium=preview_link&utm_source=designer&utm_content=leadgenerationacademy-4a091e328ae761c28&preview=70d6f83c609c7159888f9324c39b3239&pageId=6a87d0656e1d53a3f59dd23b&workflow=preview).** If you've already made a copy of the shared project for a previous page, just navigate to this page inside it instead of making another copy.
3. **Add Domain To Webflow.** Skip this if your domain is already connected from a previous page in this funnel.
4. **Add Logo To Nav Bar.** Use the same logo and branding as every other page they've seen in the funnel, so it feels like the same process continuing.
5. **Update Headline.** Tell them directly to book a call. They've already filled out your qualifying form and passed your questions, so the only job left on this page is picking a time.
6. **Update Subheadline With Genuine Scarcity Or Urgency.** I love a bit of subtle scarcity and urgency here, as long as it's genuine and doesn't look fake. Do not do this if you are not going to do it right.
7. **Connect Your Calendly Embed So It Only Allows Booking Within A 72 Hour Window.** Once you're at massive scale, you can extend this to 7 days. People book while the problem feels relevant, and the longer the gap before the call, the more time they have to get busy, forget, or lose interest.
8. **Open As Many Slots As Possible, As Soon As Possible.** A 72-hour window does nothing if there are barely any times available inside it. Look at the calendar the way someone applying today would see it, and make sure there are enough times they can actually attend.

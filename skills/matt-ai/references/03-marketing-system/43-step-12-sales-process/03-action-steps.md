# Action Steps

Course: Step #12: Sales Process
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=0cf4cc4605ba42499e3a3fba2149d7be

---
The Theory explains that once you have a sales team and it's no longer just you taking every call, you need a repeatable system instead of your own personal knowledge and improvisation, a CRM that's actually maintained, a standardized Call Plan, fast follow-through after someone pays, and an onboarding flow that gets them a quick win before buyer's remorse sets in.

This module is where you actually build that system.

You will set up your CRM's deal stages and statuses, hire a CRM Champion to keep it maintained, build your Sales Call Plan covering the Intro, Five Questions, Demo, Objection Handling, and Closing, reuse your Offer Presentation as the Demo, prepare your One Pager, Contract, and Payment templates so you can send them within 5 minutes of closing, then build the onboarding flow that automatically follows and delivers five quick wins in the first 24 hours.

Do not let CRM updates slide once you have a sales team, wing your calls instead of running the same plan every time, or delay sending payment info after someone closes, since a messy CRM, an inconsistent call, or a slow invoice can undo a sale you already won.

### Table of Contents

1. **CRM:** Set up your CRM with the 9 deal stages and 5 statuses you need to track every prospect and client through your sales process.
2. **Hire CRM Champion:** Hire a low-cost VA whose only job is keeping your sales team compliant on CRM updates.
3. **Sales Call Plan:** Write your Intro, Five Questions, Demo, Objection Handling, and Closing into one standardized plan every rep follows on every call.
4. **Sales Call Presentation:** Reuse your Offer Presentation as the Demo portion of your Sales Call Plan, trimmed to 5-7 minutes.
5. **One Pager + Contract + Payment:** Prepare your One Pager, Contract Template, and Payment Template so you can send everything to a closed client within 5 minutes of the call.
6. **Client Onboarding Flow:** Build an automatic onboarding flow that starts the moment someone pays, delivers daily then weekly emails, and gets them five quick wins in the first 24 hours.

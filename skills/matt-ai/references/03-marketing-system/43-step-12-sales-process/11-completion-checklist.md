Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### CRM Setup

- You have chosen a CRM.
- You have added the nine deal stages:
  - Application Submitted
  - Call Booked
  - Call Shown
  - Offer Sent
  - Call Completed
  - Closed Won
  - Closed Lost
  - Unqualified
  - No Show
- You have added the five statuses:
  - Opt In
  - Prospect
  - Active Client
  - Churned Client
  - Unqualified

### CRM Champion

- You have hired a CRM Champion, a VA paid around $5 per hour.
- You have them asking your sales team whether they updated the CRM and what they need.
- You have optionally had them handle project management in their free time.

### Sales Call Plan

- You have made a copy of the Sales Call Plan Template.
- You have written your Intro.
- You are using the five questions exactly as written.
- You have written your Demo, Objection Handling, and Closing/Next Steps.
- You have copied every section into the Your Full Sales Call Plan tab.

### Sales Call Presentation

- You have answered the eight questions in the Framework & Criteria using your Offer Presentation.
- You have trimmed it down to 5-7 minutes and added some graphics.
- You are using it as the Demo portion of your Sales Call Plan.

### One Pager + Contract + Payment

- You have filled in your Payment One-Pager, including a 90-day timeline broken into specific milestones, shortened if you sell an intro offer.
- You have filled in the block at the top of your Contract Template.
- You have created a Payment Link or Invoice Template you can clone and send quickly.
- You have filled in the canned message for email and text, with all three formatted nicely.
- You have saved the canned message somewhere you can reach from your phone.
- You are sending all three to every closed client by email and text within 5 minutes of the call.

### Client Onboarding Flow

- You have set your payment link to redirect to your onboarding form.
- You have built email and SMS automations triggered by a "New Client" tag, sending one email per day for seven days, then three per week for three weeks.
- You have connected Stripe to your email and SMS platforms through Zapier, so every payment applies the "New Client" tag.
- You have set every onboarding form submission to post in your Slack.
- You are adding every new client to your Slack and sending them their Onboarding Package there.
- You have written five Quick Wins that can each be completed within 24 hours, and you are sending them in Slack.
- You are running an Onboarding Call that sets Objectives, Key Results, and Assignments with a date, time required, and priority.
- You have set up the Morning Update, the Evening Update, and the 30 Minute Weekly Meeting.
- You have tested the whole flow by paying through your own payment link.

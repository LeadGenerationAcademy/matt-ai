### Framework & Criteria

1. **One Pager:** Explains everything they need to know and what happens after they pay. Includes a timeline of objectives for their first 90 days, shortened for an intro offer, broken into specific milestones, for example: after 24 hours do X, after 48 hours Y is complete, after 7 days Z happens.
2. **Contract Template:** Something you can clone and send quickly.
3. **Payment Link Or Invoice Template:** Something you can clone and send quickly.
4. **Canned Message:** Sent through both email and text. Includes the One Pager, Contract, and Payment Link, formatted nicely.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Payment One-Pager Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/143IoykvR5s_4i1f0ywM3617zg6URRHtchg1APbAFDI4/edit?tab=t.0).** This is what every closed client gets sent, so save your own copy before you fill anything in. The one-pager lays out exactly what they can expect, which minimizes their final concerns before they pay. [in this skill: assets/templates/canned-message-template/]
2. **Fill In What You're Getting.** This is everything they need to know about what they just bought, written plainly. They already said yes, so there is nothing left to sell here.
3. **Fill In What Happens After You Pay.** Name the first thing that fires the moment the payment clears and who does it. This is what reduces their uncertainty about what happens next, which makes it more likely they actually pay.
4. **Fill In Your First 90 Days With Specific Milestones.** After 24 hours do X, after 48 hours Y is complete, after 7 days Z happens. Specific checkpoints make the timeline feel real and trackable rather than abstract.
5. **Shorten That Timeline If You're Selling An Intro Offer.** An intro offer moves faster, so a full 90-day arc would feel mismatched to its scope and price.
6. **Go To The [Contract Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/143IoykvR5s_4i1f0ywM3617zg6URRHtchg1APbAFDI4/edit?tab=t.fqk2ihfkco58) Tab And Fill In The Block At The Top.** It's the same document you already copied, just a different tab, so there's no need for a second copy. Every field that changes from client to client sits in that one block, and the clauses underneath it stay the same every time. That's what lets you clone the contract and have it ready for them to sign in one second. [in this skill: assets/templates/canned-message-template/]
7. **Create A Payment Link Or Invoice Template You Can Clone And Send Quickly.** Ideally this is one standardized payment link you send to everyone. If you use invoices, build a simple one yourself, or just use Stripe's default invoice template. All that matters is you can clone it and send it in seconds.
8. **Fill In The [Canned Message Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/143IoykvR5s_4i1f0ywM3617zg6URRHtchg1APbAFDI4/edit?tab=t.pp59oqy5p5y3).** Same document again, another tab. It holds both the email version and the text version, so fill in both. [in this skill: assets/templates/canned-message-template/]
9. **Format The One Pager, Contract, And Payment Link Nicely Inside That Message.** Shorten the links for the text version, since a raw Google Docs link arrives as a wall of characters and makes the whole message look sloppy.
10. **Save The Canned Message Somewhere You Can Reach From Your Phone.** Your Apple Notes works. This is what lets you text and email it right after the call, no matter where you are.
11. **Send The One Pager, Contract, And Payment Link To Every Closed Client Within 5 Minutes Of The Call.** Send it by both email and text. Regardless of where you are, at your computer, on your phone, on an airplane, scuba diving, or at dinner, this is the standard to hold yourself to. If you're not this fast, you're leaving money on the table you already earned, and you're not ready to convert people at scale.

### Framework & Criteria

1. What are you trying to do? Articulate your objectives using absolutely no jargon.
2. How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?
3. What is new in your approach and why do you think it will be successful?
4. Who cares? If you are successful, what difference will it make?
5. What are the risks?
6. How much will it cost?
7. How long will it take?
8. What are the mid-term and final “exams” to check for success?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Your Offer Presentation From The [Step #3 Offers Module](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=ffbf64825d78400a8fcaa22927cea445&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce).** You don't need to build a separate presentation for your Sales Call, reusing this one keeps your messaging consistent between your Offer and your sales process. If you already have a sales presentation you like, that's fine too.
2. **Answer The Eight Questions In The Framework & Criteria.** These questions come from one of DARPA's directors back in the day, and I think they make an amazing sales presentation framework. Build it picturing the moment on a call where you tell someone they sound like a good fit and ask if you can share your screen. Keep it bullet-point heavy. You'll be talking one-on-one over a screen share, so the bullets are anchor points for the conversation, and you won't be reading off the screen.
3. **Trim It Down To 5-7 Minutes.** This needs to fit inside a 30-minute call alongside your Intro, Five Questions, Objection Handling, and Closing, so it can't run long.
4. **Add Some Graphics.** A plain template looks pretty sterile on a screen share, and a few graphics make your presentation look less like one.
5. **Use It As The Demo Portion Of Your Sales Call Plan.** End it with the same transition I use, asking if this is what they expected and how they'd rate it from 1-10, which flows directly into Objection Handling.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your Offer Presentation from the Step #3 Offers Module?
- Did you answer the eight questions in the Framework & Criteria?
- Did you trim it down to 5-7 minutes?
- Did you add some graphics?
- Are you using it as the Demo portion of your Sales Call Plan?

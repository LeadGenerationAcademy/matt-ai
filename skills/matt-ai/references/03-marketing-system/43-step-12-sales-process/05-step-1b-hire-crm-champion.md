Once you have a sales team and it's no longer just you taking every call, CRM compliance becomes a real problem: reps get busy, and updating the CRM is the first thing that gets skipped.

The fix is hiring a CRM Champion, a low-cost VA whose entire job is chasing your sales team down and making sure the CRM actually gets updated. It's one of the cheapest hires you can make, and it pays for itself many times over in improved CRM compliance.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Hire A CRM Champion:** A VA paid around $5 per hour.
2. **Core Job:** Have them do nothing except ask your sales team whether they updated the CRM and what they need, if anything.
3. **Optional Expansion:** Optionally, have this person also double as your project management champion in their free time.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Search VA Platforms Like [eVirtualAssistants](https://www.evirtualassistants.com/hire-virtual-assistant/virtual-assistants/60) For Candidates.** This gives you a starting point to find candidates quickly rather than building a hiring process from scratch.
2. **Hire A CRM Champion, A VA Paid Around $5 Per Hour.** This is one of the smartest investments possible for your sales team, it doesn't cost much compared to how much it improves compliance in terms of CRM use.
3. **Have Them Ask Your Sales Team Whether They Updated The CRM.** This is their core job, chasing this down consistently is exactly what most sales teams fail to do on their own once a founder isn't taking every call personally.
4. **Have Them Ask Your Sales Team What They Need, If Anything.** Beyond just checking compliance, this creates a feedback loop so any blockers a rep has get surfaced instead of silently stalling a deal.
5. **Optionally, Have Them Also Handle Project Management In Their Free Time.** Use their remaining free time for this since the CRM-chasing role alone likely won't fill a full workday.

### Completion Audit

- Did you search VA platforms for candidates?
- Did you hire a CRM Champion, a VA paid around $5 per hour?
- Are they asking your sales team whether they updated the CRM?
- Are they asking your sales team what they need, if anything?
- Are they optionally handling project management in their free time?

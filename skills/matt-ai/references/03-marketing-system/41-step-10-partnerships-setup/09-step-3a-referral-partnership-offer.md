A referral partner is the same person as a white label partner. The only thing that changes is the arrangement. They hand you the client, you do the work under your own name, and they take a commission.

Some of them will prefer it this way. They might not have enough need for it, they might not want to deal with it, or they might only have a few clients so the volume never makes sense.

You get warm leads you didn't have to advertise for or chase down with outreach. They get paid. That's the whole trade.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Commission:** What you pay them per sale, paid every time, whether they asked for one or not.
2. **Credibility:** A clear explanation of why you won't embarrass them, since they are handing you a client relationship they built.
3. **Special Offer:** A free introductory offer for their clients that makes the referral easy to make and carries no risk on the client's end.

### Example

**Offer:** Landing pages for Shopify ecom brands

**Commission:** $500 per referred sale, paid every time, whether they asked for it or not.

**Credibility:** 77 agencies refer to us. 200+ pages shipped a month.

**Special Offer:** We audit one of their client's pages for free and send back the above-the-fold section redesigned. Their client decides from there, so nobody is buying on the partner's word.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take your existing client offer as the base.** You are not building something new. This is the same offer you already sell. The difference from white label is that they refer you to their client instead of putting their own name on your work.
2. **Decide what commission you will pay per referred sale.** This is the part that makes the whole thing work. They got you a client without you spending anything on advertising or outreach, and the commission is what makes them want to do it again.
3. **Write an explanation of why you won't embarrass them.** This is almost always the main objection and it is a serious one. Answer it with case studies, testimonials, reviews, and your actual portfolio. The more proof you have, the easier this gets.
4. **Create a free introductory offer their clients can accept with no risk.** An audit or one free piece of work. It should not take you long to produce, and it should be easy enough that the partner can hand it to their client with no risk on their client's end.
5. **Pay the commission every time, whether they asked for one or not.** They got you a client. They want to be compensated even when they act like they don't. Send it, and they will send you a lot more people.

### Completion Audit

- Did you take your existing client offer as the base?
- Did you decide what commission you will pay per referred sale?
- Did you write an explanation of why you won't embarrass them?
- Did you create a free introductory offer their clients can accept with no risk?
- Did you commit to paying the commission every time, whether they asked for one or not?

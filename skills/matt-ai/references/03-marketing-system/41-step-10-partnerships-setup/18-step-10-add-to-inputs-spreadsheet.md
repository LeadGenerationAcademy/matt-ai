Fill this out in your spreadsheet so you know what's being tracked and what's not. The input spreadsheet is very important because a lot of the time, bad metrics don't include inputs, and that masks the fact that you're not doing anything. Remember, cause and effect: there's no cause, there's no effect.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the Partnerships tab of your [Input Tracking Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eT95b4OnSVAOaqrRJ5zKZrcBX0ul6EewACqs38u_Z20/edit?gid=303155588#gid=303155588).** This is where your partnership outreach gets tracked alongside the rest of your inputs. [in this skill: assets/templates/input-tracking-template/]
2. **Fill in the Date.** You need to know exactly which day each day's outreach happened.
3. **Fill in Emails Sent, Calls Made, LinkedIn DMs Sent, Twitter/X DMs Sent, and Instagram DMs Sent.** Each channel has its own column. Together they show how much partnership outreach actually went out that day.
4. **Fill in Other Outreach Sent #1 if used, or replace it with your actual platform name.** This catches any channel that isn't already listed. If you don't use another channel, remove the column.
5. **Fill in Notes if needed.** This is where you keep anything worth remembering about that day's outreach. Total Outreach Sent calculates on its own once the row is filled in.
6. **Repeat this every day.** Bad metrics often leave out inputs entirely, and that hides the fact that nothing is being done. No cause, no effect.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Partnerships tab of your Input Tracking Sheet?
- Did you fill in the Date?
- Did you fill in Emails Sent, Calls Made, LinkedIn DMs Sent, Twitter/X DMs Sent, and Instagram DMs Sent?
- Did you fill in Other Outreach Sent #1 if used, or replace it with your actual platform name?
- Did you fill in Notes if needed?
- Are you repeating this every day?

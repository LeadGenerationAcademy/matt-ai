### Framework & Criteria

1. **Result / Dream Outcome / Headline:** The result their client gets, in one line.
2. **Your Details:** Your name, company name, website and YouTube channel.
3. **Offer Summary:** What you do and how the arrangement works. Your name never appears, it ships under theirs.
4. **What's In It For You:** Beyond the margin. What it does for their retention, their case studies and the price they can charge.
5. **Timeline:** Time to first value, then full delivery.
6. **Cost:** What they pay you, and what they can charge their client.
7. **Guarantee:** Your risk reversal, plus the risk-free offer their client can try before anyone commits.
8. **Who We Have Worked With + When + Result + Timeframe:** Ten case studies. Who, when, what result, how long it took, every line linked to the actual case study.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Whitelabel Partnership One-Pager Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Wy1Ha1IQeAfgxetjSsUkkmPSQ01sRXgWbcpRaJhyzw0/edit?usp=sharing) and go to the White-Label One-Pager Template tab.** This is where you condense the deck from the previous step onto a single page you can send the moment a partner shows interest. The white-label, referral and media tabs all have different fields. [in this skill: assets/templates/media-partnership-one-pager-template/]
2. **Write your Result / Dream Outcome / Headline.** Keep it to one line about the result their client gets. This is the Dream Outcome part of the Value Formula.
3. **Fill in your name, company name, website and YouTube, and hyperlink your website and YouTube channel.** This block sits on the left of the header. A one-pager with dead text where a link should be is doing half a job.
4. **Write the Offer Summary.** Say what you do and how the arrangement works. Your name never appears, and their client never knows you exist.
5. **Write What's In It For You.** Cover what you do for them beyond the margin, like better client retention and a higher price they can charge. Pull this from the What's In It For Them section of your deck.
6. **Write the Timeline.** Put time to first value first, then full delivery. This is the Time Delay part of the Value Formula, so shorter is better.
7. **Write the Cost.** Put what they pay you, and what they can charge their client on top of it.
8. **Write the Guarantee.** Include your risk reversal, plus the free offer their client can try before anyone commits. They are the one putting their name on the line.
9. **Add ten case studies with who, when, the result and the timeframe, and hyperlink every one to the actual case study.** This answers how they know you will not embarrass them. They should be able to check any claim on the page in one click.
10. **Confirm it still fits on one page.** If it has run onto a second page, cut until it does not.
11. **Record a three to five minute Loom walking through the one-pager.** Share your screen and go through it the same way you would walk a partner through it live. It is meant to be quick.
12. **Upload the Loom and confirm that anyone with the link can watch it.** Don't spend hours editing it. A restricted link defeats the whole purpose of having this ready to send.
13. **Link the one-pager and the Loom together.** Put the Loom link on the one-pager and the one-pager link in the Loom.
14. **Save both somewhere you and your team can access immediately.** The next time someone asks about this partnership, you should be able to send both within seconds.

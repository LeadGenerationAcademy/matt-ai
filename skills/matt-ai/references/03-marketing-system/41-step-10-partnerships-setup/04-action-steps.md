# Action Steps

Course: Step #10: Partnerships Setup
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b0606dd1?md=8fe11d6ae2d54e24844b892df10ab979

---
The Theory explains what partnerships are, the three types, and why a great offer is what actually gets you partners instead of a referral fee.

This course is where you actually build them.

You will brainstorm your partner categories, then build the offer, deck, and one-pager for both white label and referral, and put together your media partnership package.

From there you build and enrich the Dream 400 list, run omni-channel outreach against it, route every response into Slack, hand off the inbox, and log your outreach every day.

#### Table of Contents

1. **Partnerships Brainstorm:** List at least 20 white label and referral partner categories and at least 10 types of media partnerships.
2. **Whitelabel Partnership Offer:** Take your existing client offer, price it 25% off, and build the credibility and risk-free introduction around it.
3. **Whitelabel Partnership Deck:** Build an 11-page deck covering everything from who you are through the offer itself, favoring clarity over salesmanship.
4. **Whitelabel One Pager + Loom:** Condense that deck onto one page and record a three-minute Loom walking through it.
5. **Referral Partnership Offer:** Take the same client offer, decide the commission you pay per sale, and build the credibility and free introductory offer around it.
6. **Referral Partnership Deck:** Build the same 11-page deck for partners who want to refer you instead of putting their own name on your work.
7. **Referral One Pager + Loom:** Condense the referral deck onto one page and record a three-minute Loom walking through it.
8. **Media Partnership Package:** Put together your story, your portfolio, the top 10 things you would talk about, and a five minute video of yourself.
9. **Build Dream 400 Lists:** Fill your categories out into 300 named white label or referral partners and 100 media partners.
10. **Enrich Dream 400 Lists:** Find each founder's email, LinkedIn, phone, Instagram, and Twitter, then clean the data before you run anything against it.
11. **Setup Prospecting Campaigns:** Send 40 emails, 40 calls, 40 LinkedIn messages, and 40 Twitter messages per day so you work through the full list every 10 working days.
12. **Setup Partnership Integrations:** Set up partnership call booking and route notifications from every outreach channel into Slack so nothing slips through.
13. **Delegate Inbox Management:** Run outreach yourself for the first two weeks, then hand the inbox to a VA or team member who passes real responses back to you.
14. **Add To Inputs Spreadsheet:** Log your outreach volume across every channel, every day.

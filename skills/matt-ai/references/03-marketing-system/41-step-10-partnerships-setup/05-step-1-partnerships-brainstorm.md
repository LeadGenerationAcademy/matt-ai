Make a list of at least 20 white label/referral partner categories and at least 10 types of media partnerships.

At this step, you have to think about the grouping. For example, the 20 referral/white-label categories are different agencies that provide different services that are competitive with yours, they're adjacent. Find at least 20 of those and 10 types of media partners, then write down the list and save it.

That's all you need to do for now, think of the broad categories. In the Dream 400 List in Step #5, we'll fill these out. Save it somewhere in your Apple Notes, in your workspace, or wherever.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **List at least 20 categories of businesses that sell to the same customers you do, but sell a different service.** These are your white label and referral partners. A landing page agency for ecom brands, for example, would list paid ads agencies, email agencies and SEO agencies for ecom brands. If there is any possibility a category could be a good fit, add it.
2. **List at least 10 categories of media that already reach the audience you sell to.** Think blogs, newsletters, podcasts, YouTube channels, events and communities. You give them content, like a guest post or a podcast episode, and in return you get in front of their audience.
3. **Save the list somewhere you can find it again, such as Apple Notes or your workspace.** You will turn these categories into real names when you build your Dream 400 Lists in Step #5.

### Completion Audit

- Did you list at least 20 categories of businesses that sell to the same customers you do, but sell a different service?
- Did you list at least 10 categories of media that already reach the audience you sell to?
- Did you save the list somewhere you can find it again?

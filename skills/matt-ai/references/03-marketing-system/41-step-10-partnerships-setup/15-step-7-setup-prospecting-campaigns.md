Now what you're going to do is try to hit all of these channels: Email, Phone, LinkedIn, and Twitter (or whatever else makes sense). You want a coordinated omni-channel strategy.

When you're first getting started, it's good enough to run this in a spreadsheet. Once you've had early success and it's proven out, you can build something more advanced later, but for now, keep it all on a spreadsheet.

You have 400 partners on your list. You can send:

- 40 emails per day
- 40 cold calls per day
- 40 LinkedIn messages per day
- 40 Twitter messages per day

Work through the list every 10 working days, meaning Monday to Friday, every two weeks.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the Dream 400 Lists spreadsheet you enriched in the previous step.** A spreadsheet is good enough when you're first getting started. Once it has proven out, you can build something more advanced.
2. **Set up your email, phone, LinkedIn and Twitter outreach.** You want a coordinated omni-channel strategy hitting every partner on all four. Swap Twitter for another channel if that makes more sense for your list.
3. **Contact up to 40 partners per day on each channel.** That's 40 emails, 40 cold calls, 40 LinkedIn messages and 40 Twitter messages every day.
4. **Check off Email Sent, Call Made, LinkedIn Sent and Twitter Sent with the date every time you reach someone.** This is how you always know who has been contacted on which channel and who hasn't.
5. **Log anything worth remembering in Outreach Notes.** This is where you keep context on a specific conversation or response.
6. **Work through the full list of 400 every 10 working days.** That's Monday to Friday, every two weeks, so every partner gets contacted on the same cycle.

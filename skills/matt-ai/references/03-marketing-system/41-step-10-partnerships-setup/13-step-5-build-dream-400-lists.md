This is the step we talked about in Step #1.

You got 20 categories for white label and referral partners, and 10 buckets for media partners. Now you're going to build those out into a full Dream 400 list: 300 ideal partners that could be either white label or referral, and 100 media partners, all going into a spreadsheet.

Pick these partners carefully, more carefully than you'd pick your clients. This is a power law situation, where 1% or 2% of your partners will end up driving 97% of your actual revenue.

A lot of these will just be "yeah, great, we're partners" and nothing ever comes from it. That's fine, still worth doing.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Dream 400 Lists Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1w2ilWqaXZWpux4q0Slg1ljMt4LC1SnQvBH2UmP9VJfs/edit?usp=sharing).** This is where the categories from Step #1 turn into 400 real partners you can reach out to. It has one tab for white label and referral partners and one tab for media partners. [in this skill: assets/templates/dream-400-lists-template/]
2. **Pull up your list of categories from Step #1.** These are the 20 white label/referral categories and the 10 media categories you already brainstormed.
3. **Pick each partner more carefully than you would pick a client.** This is a power law situation, where 1% or 2% of your partners end up driving 97% of your revenue. A lot of the others will say "yeah, great, we're partners" and nothing will come of it, and that's fine.
4. **Fill in the Company Name for 300 white label or referral partners on the White Label / Referral tab.** Spread them across the 20 categories from Step #1.
5. **Fill in the Company Name for 100 media partners on the Media Partners tab.** Spread them across your 10 media categories.
6. **Leave the remaining columns blank for now.** The contact columns get filled in during Step #6, and the outreach columns get dated once you start your campaigns in Step #7.

### Completion Audit

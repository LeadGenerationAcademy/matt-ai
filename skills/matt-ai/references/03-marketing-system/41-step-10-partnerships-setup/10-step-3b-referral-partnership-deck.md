---
You're going to want to make about an 11-page deck with this offer. We've provided a template below.

You shouldn't have to sell this with the same degree of intensity that you sell clients. Tell them who, what, when, where, why, how, the timeline, how it works, what's in it for them, how you're not going to embarrass them, and the offer.

You should be good. I would definitely go on the side of clarity in this over salesmanship or marketing.

This deck is very close to the white label one, but you have to decide whether the partner wants a white label or a referral relationship before you send it.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Who:** Who you are.
2. **What:** What you're offering.
3. **When:** When this applies or takes place.
4. **Where:** Where this applies or takes place.
5. **Why:** Why this partnership makes sense.
6. **How:** How this works at a high level.
7. **Timeline:** The timeline involved.
8. **How It Works:** The mechanics of how it actually works.
9. **What's In It For Them:** What's in it for them specifically.
10. **How You're Not Going To Embarrass Them:** Why they can trust you not to embarrass them.
11. **The Offer:** The actual offer itself.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Confirm the partner wants a referral relationship.** This deck is very close to the white label one. Decide which arrangement the partner actually wants before you send anything.
2. **Make a copy of the [Referral Partnership Deck Template](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1L_gY7figHSZeBMCz5Z2dT6G1wpgUbbgE4KqiBXgStX4/edit?usp=sharing).** This is where you turn the referral offer from Step #3A into the deck you send to partners. Use the Referral Partnership Deck Example whenever you get stuck. [in this skill: assets/templates/referral-partnership-deck-template/]
3. **Write the Who section.** Say who you are, what your company does, and how many partners already refer to you.
4. **Write the What section.** Explain exactly what you do for the clients they send you. They make the intro, and the work goes out under your name.
5. **Write the When section.** List the situations where they should introduce you to one of their clients. This makes it easy for them to spot a referral when it comes up.
6. **Write the Where section.** Describe which of their clients this is built for. This tells the partner exactly who to introduce you to.
7. **Write the Why section.** Explain the problem their clients have that you solve, and how that problem affects the partner.
8. **Write the How section.** Give a high-level view of who does what. They make the introduction, the client becomes yours, and there is no work on their end after that.
9. **Write the Timeline section.** Lay out the steps from their introduction to your free offer, then to full delivery.
10. **Write the How It Works section.** Go through the mechanics step by step: how they make the intro, what happens after, and when and how you pay their commission.
11. **Write the What's In It For Them section.** Cover the commission they get per sale, and what referring you does for their own client relationship.
12. **Write the How You're Not Going To Embarrass Them section.** Use the explanation you wrote in Step #3A. They are handing you a client relationship they built, so show them case studies, testimonials and reviews.
13. **Write The Offer section.** State the commission per sale and the free introductory offer their client can accept with no risk.
14. **Favor clarity over salesmanship throughout the deck.** You shouldn't have to sell a partner with the same degree of intensity you sell clients. Rewrite anything that reads like marketing into a plain explanation.

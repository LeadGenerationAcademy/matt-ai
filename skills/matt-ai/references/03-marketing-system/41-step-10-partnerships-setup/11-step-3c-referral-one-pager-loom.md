### Framework & Criteria

1. **Result / Dream Outcome / Headline:** The result their client gets, in one line.
2. **Your Details:** Your name, company name, website and YouTube channel.
3. **Offer Summary:** What you do and how the arrangement works. They make the intro, the client becomes yours, and there is no work on their end after that.
4. **What's In It For You:** The fee per sale, plus what it does for their client relationship and their own results.
5. **Timeline:** Intro, then time to first value, then full delivery.
6. **Cost:** What their client pays, and what you pay them per sale.
7. **Guarantee:** Your risk reversal, plus the risk-free offer their client can try before anyone commits.
8. **Who We Have Worked With + When + Result + Timeframe:** Ten case studies. Who, when, what result, how long it took, every line linked to the actual case study.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Referral Partnership One-Pager Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Wy1Ha1IQeAfgxetjSsUkkmPSQ01sRXgWbcpRaJhyzw0/edit?usp=sharing) and go to the Referral One-Pager Template tab.** This is where you condense the deck from the previous step onto a single page you can send the moment a partner shows interest. The white-label, referral and media tabs all have different fields. [in this skill: assets/templates/media-partnership-one-pager-template/]
2. **Write your Result / Dream Outcome / Headline.** Keep it to one line about the result their client gets.
3. **Fill in your name, company name, website and YouTube, and hyperlink your website and YouTube channel.** This block sits on the left of the header. A one-pager with dead text where a link should be is doing half a job.
4. **Write the Offer Summary.** Say what you do and how the arrangement works. They make the intro, the client becomes yours, and nothing is expected from them after that.
5. **Write What's In It For You.** Put the fee they get per sale, plus what referring you does for their own client relationship and their results.
6. **Write the Timeline.** Start at the intro, then time to first value, then full delivery. Shorter is better.
7. **Write the Cost.** Put what their client pays you, and what you pay them per sale.
8. **Write the Guarantee.** Include your risk reversal, plus the free offer their client can try before anyone commits. They made the introduction on their own name.
9. **Add ten case studies with who, when, the result and the timeframe, and hyperlink every one to the actual case study.** This answers how they know you will not embarrass them. They should be able to check any claim on the page in one click.
10. **Confirm it still fits on one page.** If it has run onto a second page, cut until it does not.
11. **Record a three to five minute Loom walking through the one-pager.** Share your screen and go through it the same way you would walk a partner through it live. It is meant to be quick.
12. **Upload the Loom and confirm that anyone with the link can watch it.** Don't spend hours editing it. A restricted link defeats the whole purpose of having this ready to send.
13. **Link the one-pager and the Loom together.** Put the Loom link on the one-pager and the one-pager link in the Loom.
14. **Save both somewhere you and your team can access immediately.** The next time someone asks about this partnership, you should be able to send both within seconds.

# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #10: Partnerships Setup
System: Marketing System

Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

#### Partnerships Brainstorm

- Listed at least 20 white label and referral partner categories
- Listed at least 10 types of media partnerships
- Saved the list somewhere you can find it again in Step #5

### Whitelabel Partnership Assets

- Confirmed your offer actually makes sense to white-label
- Priced your existing client offer 25% off for partners
- Wrote your explanation of why you won't embarrass them
- Built a risk-free way for partners to introduce you to their clients
- Made a copy of the Whitelabel Partnership Deck Template and wrote all 11 sections
- Made a copy of the Whitelabel Partnership One-Pager Template and condensed all 11 sections onto one page
- Recorded a Loom under three minutes walking through the one-pager
- Confirmed anyone with the link can watch it, linked it to the one-pager, and saved both somewhere you and your team can access immediately

### Referral Partnership Assets

- Decided what commission you pay per referred sale
- Wrote your explanation of why you won't embarrass them
- Created a free introductory offer their clients can accept with no risk
- Committed to paying the commission every time, whether they asked for one or not
- Made a copy of the Referral Partnership Deck Template and wrote all 11 sections
- Made a copy of the Referral Partnership One-Pager Template and condensed all 11 sections onto one page
- Recorded a Loom under three minutes walking through the one-pager
- Confirmed anyone with the link can watch it, linked it to the one-pager, and saved both somewhere you and your team can access immediately

### Media Partnership Package

- Wrote your story
- Put together your portfolio of past media appearances
- Wrote down the top 10 things you would talk about
- Recorded a five minute video of yourself
- Decided whether you are willing to pay to be featured

### Dream 400 Lists

- Made a copy of the Dream 400 Lists Template
- Filled in 300 ideal white label or referral partners and 100 ideal media partners
- Found each founder's email, LinkedIn, phone, Instagram, and Twitter
- Verified the data through Million Verifier, a VA, or both

### Prospecting Campaigns

- Set up outreach on email, phone, LinkedIn, and Twitter
- Logged every send per prospect with the date
- Set a cadence that works through the full list of 400 every 10 working days

### Responses & Inbox Management

- Set up a call booking system for partnership calls
- Created a "Partnership Outreach" label and built the Zap that sends those emails to Slack
- Got LinkedIn, Twitter, and any other channel's notifications into Slack
- Ran outreach yourself for the first two weeks
- Handed the inbox to a VA or team member who passes real responses back to you

### Outreach Tracking

- Opened the Partnerships tab of your inputs spreadsheet
- Logged emails, calls, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Instagram outreach for the day
- Confirmed Total Outreach Sent calculates correctly
- Doing this every day

After you have these people, you're going to try to enrich them. You should already have the company name from Step #5. Now you want to find:

- Founder names
- Founder email addresses
- Founder LinkedIn
- Founder phone number
- Founder Instagram
- Founder Twitter
- Company websites
- Anything else you can find on them

You can enrich them using [clay.com](http://clay.com), or a lot of other things, whatever gets the job done. Get everything into that same spreadsheet. Then clean it, either in Million Verifier, some other verification method, having your VA check that it's legit, or a combination of all of the above.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the Dream 400 Lists spreadsheet from the previous step.** You already have the company names, and now you're filling in the contact columns on both tabs.
2. **Use [Clay](http://clay.com/) or another tool to find each founder's name, email, LinkedIn, phone number, Instagram and Twitter, plus the company website.** You have the company, and now you need the actual person you're reaching out to on every channel. Clay is the go-to, but use whatever gets the job done.
3. **Add anything else you find to the additional info columns.** Anything that helps you reach them or makes the outreach more personal is worth keeping.
4. **Verify the data using [Million Verifier](https://www.millionverifier.com/), your VA, or both.** Bad data wastes your outreach, so clean it before you move on.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Dream 400 Lists spreadsheet from the previous step?
- Did you use Clay or another tool to find each founder's name, email, LinkedIn, phone number, Instagram and Twitter, plus the company website?
- Did you add anything else you found to the additional info columns?
- Did you verify the data using Million Verifier, your VA, or both?

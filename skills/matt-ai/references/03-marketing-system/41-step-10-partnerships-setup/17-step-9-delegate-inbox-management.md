# Step #9: Delegate Inbox Management

Course: Step #10: Partnerships Setup
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b0606dd1?md=eed1dd42d151408bb23681744e0c4c8a

After the initial learning phase, once you've done it yourself for two weeks, you should delegate inbox management. That means having a virtual assistant or someone on your team managing the inbox and handling outreach. If there's a response, they pass it over to you.

### Prerequisites

- [Step #7: Setup Prospecting Campaigns](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b0606dd1?md=c51fd4fc00d843e3baa1aa2f2b146bad) (Current Course)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Run the outreach yourself for the first two weeks.** This is your learning phase. You need to see what's working before you hand it off to anyone.
2. **Assign a VA or someone on your team to manage the inbox.** Once the two weeks are up, this should not be sitting on your plate anymore.
3. **Have them take over sending outreach and managing responses.** They run the day to day of the campaigns you set up in Step #7.
4. **Have them pass any response over to you.** You still handle the actual conversations with partners, and they manage the volume around it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you run the outreach yourself for the first two weeks?
- Is a VA or someone on your team assigned to manage the inbox?
- Are they sending outreach and managing responses?
- Are they passing any response over to you?

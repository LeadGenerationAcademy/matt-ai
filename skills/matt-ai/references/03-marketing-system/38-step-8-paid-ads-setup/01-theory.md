### Put The Pixel Everywhere

For now, you need to install the pixel on your website. You should put it in the <head> on every section. The easiest way is usually to do it in your sitewide settings.

There are situations where you would not want the pixel on every page, but those will be talked about later. For now, especially if you are already not successful with ads, just put it everywhere.

### The Bare Bones Way To Do Attribution

Attribution can get funky as you scale up, especially if you have other traffic sources. Instead of some complicated or custom solution, the most bare bones way to do attribution is to clone your calendar event and put it [yourlink.com/fb](http://yourlink.com/fb) or something.

That way, the only people who book on this calendar will be from Facebook Ads. It is not perfect, as someone may see your ad, click on your website instead and schedule differently, but it is fine for now, and will be more than enough for our purposes.

You do not need to invest in some insane traffic attribution system until you are already established and spending $1,000+ per day in my opinion. For most people, that will be very far away.

### How Facebook Knows A Conversion Happened

You need to have a thank you page created for your website. You do not need to design it or do anything yet. That will come later. But, it needs to exist for us to set up pixel tracking properly.

Basically, when someone books a call, they will be redirected to this page, and we will set up the pixel to fire when they do. That is how Facebook will know a conversion happened.

This course is about getting people to book calls with Facebook Ads. That is an important distinction when we are setting up the Conversion Event.

In most cases, we will want to use Schedule as the event (ie. schedule a call). We do NOT want to use something such as Lead as we will get much lower intent leads that way.

This stuff actually matters quite a bit. By setting the proper conversion event, you are telling facebook what you want to happen.

For example, I almost NEVER get any ecommerce products on my Instagram feed (that would be the Purchase event) because I never buy any ecommerce products in the past. However, I get book a call ads (schedule) all the time because I frequently book calls from Facebook.

### Use Standard Events, Not Custom Events

This really matters a lot. Do NOT use custom events. Use standard events.

When you use a Standard Event, Facebook knows what that means and then they use every data point from your ad account and EVERY other ad account on Facebook.

When you use a custom event in comparison, Facebook doesn't really know what you are going for. It is an algorithm. When you use a custom event, Facebook only uses the data points from your ad account (and not others).

Standard events will always lead to a better result.

### The Conversions API Can Wait

You probably should set up the conversions api later. When you are just starting out, pixel tracking will be fine anyways.

You should find someone on Fiverr to do this as it is a $15/hour task, not something important.

### Facebook's Default Columns Are Not Good Enough

You will not want to rely on any of the standard columns that Facebook gives you. You will want to make your own.

### Get Notified When Someone Books

You will want to have notifications to your phone and/or Slack when someone books a call so you know and can reach out right away.

### Then You Start Phase #1

Our first goal is obviously to get a client by any means necessary, as long as we don't go to jail or ruin our reputation. Your first client from paid ads will shatter your self-limiting beliefs forever. This is a transformational moment.

We want to get 10 clients. When we get 10, we know we are onto something. One or two could be luck or a fluke. If we get 10, it won't be because of luck.

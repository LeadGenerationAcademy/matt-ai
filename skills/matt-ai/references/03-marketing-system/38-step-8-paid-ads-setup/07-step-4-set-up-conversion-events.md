### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open [Events Manager](https://eventsmanager.facebook.com/events_manager2).** You can also get here by opening your Business Manager and clicking "Events Manager" in the left navigation bar.
2. **Click "Datasets".** This gives you every pixel sitting inside your Business Manager.
3. **Select the pixel you installed in [Step #2: Installing The Pixel](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=27ec205cd1974f08be8b7c354515f350).** This is the same pixel you put in the head of every page on your website.
4. **Click "Add events".** This button sits just above the Events list on your dataset.
5. **Click "Use existing integration".** This adds new events by updating your Meta Pixel setup instead of connecting a new source.
6. **Click "Meta Pixel".** This is the pixel you set up in [Step #2: Installing The Pixel](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=27ec205cd1974f08be8b7c354515f350), and it should show as Active.
7. **Enter your [Call Booked Confirmation Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=c1c3747425614c5596ed450c09f645d3) URL.** This is the thank you page people land on right after they have booked a call, and you should already have built it in the Funnel Pages course.
8. **Click "Add events".** Facebook will open that URL in a new tab in your browser, and the Meta Event Setup Tool will load on the page, most likely in the top left of your screen. If your pixel is not set up, or not set up correctly, Meta will close the tab straight away and tell you there is no Meta Pixel.
9. **Click "Track a URL".** This tells Facebook that landing on a specific page counts as the event, rather than clicking a specific button on the page.
10. **Click "Select an event".** This opens the dropdown of events you can choose from. This really matters a lot. Do NOT use custom events. Use standard events. When you use a Standard Event, Facebook knows what that means and then they use every data point from your ad account and EVERY other ad account on Facebook. When you use a custom event in comparison, Facebook doesn't really know what you are going for. It is an algorithm. When you use a custom event, Facebook only uses the data points from your ad account (and not others). Standard events will always lead to a better result.
11. **Scroll down and click "Schedule".** In most cases, we will want to use Schedule as the event (ie. schedule a call). We do NOT want to use something such as Lead as we will get much lower intent leads that way. This stuff actually matters quite a bit. By setting the proper conversion event, you are telling facebook what you want to happen. For example, I almost NEVER get any ecommerce products on my Instagram feed (that would be the Purchase event) because I never buy any ecommerce products in the past. However, I get book a call ads (schedule) all the time because I frequently book calls from Facebook.
12. **Click "Confirm".** Schedule should then show under "Events on this page" with your URL underneath it. That is how you know the event is set up correctly.
13. **Click "Finish setup".** This saves the event to your pixel.

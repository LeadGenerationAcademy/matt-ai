### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open [Ads Manager](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/).** Everything on this page happens in here.
2. **Click "Columns".** This sits above the table on the right hand side, next to Breakdown.
3. **Click "Customize columns".** This is the button at the bottom of that dropdown.
4. **Remove every column in the right hand panel.** Press the X next to each one to remove it. You will not want to rely on any of the standard columns that Facebook gives you. You will want to make your own. The top row is your campaign, ad set or ad name and cannot be removed, which is the Name column in the list above.
5. **Search for each column in the list above and add it.** Use the search bar at the top of the panel and do them one at a time. The four marked Custom are not in Facebook's list, so leave those for now.
6. **Click the "Custom" tab.** This is where you build the four metrics Facebook does not give you.
7. **Click "Create custom metric".** This opens the formula builder.
8. **Type "Applications submitted" into the formula field.** Facebook will show the matching metrics underneath as you type, and you pick the one that says Applications submitted.
9. **Click the division sign.** This is the ÷ button in the row of operators above the formula field.
10. **Type "Outbound clicks" into the formula field.** Your formula should now read Applications submitted ÷ Outbound clicks.
11. **Name the metric "Application Rate".** Use the exact name from the list above so your columns match.
12. **Change the format to "Percentage".** It will default to Numeric, but all four of your custom metrics should be percentages.
13. **Click "Create and add another".** This saves Application Rate and hands you a blank formula for the next one.
14. **Build Booked Call Rate.** The formula is Website appointments scheduled ÷ Outbound clicks, and the format is Percentage.
15. **Build Hook Rate.** The formula is 3-second video plays ÷ Impressions, and the format is Percentage.
16. **Build ThruPlay Rate, then click "Create metric".** The formula is ThruPlays ÷ Impressions, and the format is Percentage.
17. **Tick the box next to all four of your custom metrics.** Creating a custom metric does not select it, so none of them will show up in your columns until you tick them yourself.
18. **Drag your columns into the order in the list above.** The right hand panel is drag and drop, and the order there is the order they appear in your table.
19. **Save this as a preset.** Click the arrow next to "Save" and give it a name so you can apply the same columns again without rebuilding all of this. Reading these columns comes later, in the Phase #1 Facebook Ads System course.

# Action Steps

Course: Step #8: Paid Ads Setup
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=93be27f96fc5421ca009ba4cb85f8cd5

---
The Theory explains that none of this course is strategy. It is setup, and why every piece of it has to exist before you spend a dollar.

This course is where you build the ad account, the tracking and the reporting.

You work through it in order, because almost every step depends on the one before it. By the end, your ad account is live and everything that happens inside it is tracked.

Do not move into Phase #1 with any of this half finished, apart from the Conversions API, which you can leave until later.

### Table of Contents

1. **Setup Meta Ads:** Get your Business Manager, ad account, pixel, Instagram account, Facebook page and payment method created and connected so you are ready to launch ads.
2. **Installing The Pixel:** Copy your pixel code out of Events Manager, install it in the head of every page on your site, and confirm it is firing on the live version.
3. **Clone Calendar Tool:** Duplicate your booking calendar and give the copy an /fb slug so the only people who book on it came from Facebook Ads.
4. **Set Up Conversion Events:** Use the Meta Event Setup Tool to fire a Schedule event when someone lands on your Call Booked Confirmation Page.
5. **Set up Facebook CAPI:** Leave the Conversions API for later and find someone on Fiverr to handle it.
6. **Metrics To Track:** Read through every metric you will be tracking before you build anything in Ads Manager.
7. **Create Your Columns:** Strip out Facebook's default columns and rebuild the table with your own, including the four custom metrics you have to create yourself.
8. **Set up Slack Notifications:** Build the Zapier automation that posts to Slack the moment someone books on your Facebook calendar.
9. **Start Phase #1 Of Facebook Ads System:** Work through the Completion Checklist, then move into Phase #1 and go after your first 10 clients.

### Framework & Criteria

1. Name
2. Delivery
3. CPM
4. CTR (all)
5. Outbound CTR
6. Outbound Clicks
7. Cost Per Outbound Click
8. Results
9. Cost Per Result
10. Application Rate (Custom: Applications submitted ÷ Outbound clicks)
11. Booked Call Rate (Custom: Website appointments scheduled ÷ Outbound clicks)
12. Hook Rate (Custom: 3-second video plays ÷ Impressions)
13. ThruPlay Rate (Custom: ThruPlays ÷ Impressions)
14. Amount Spend
15. Budget
16. Landing Page Views
17. Outbound Click/Landing Page View Rate (Custom: Outbound clicks ÷ Landing page views)
18. Quality Ranking
19. Engagement Ranking
20. Conversion Ranking

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Read through every metric in the list above.** These are the metrics we will want to track. You will not want to rely on any of the standard columns that Facebook gives you. You will want to make your own.
2. **Continue to Step #7: Create Your Columns.** That is where you build the columns in your ad account to track many of these. Learning how to actually read them comes in the Phase #1 Facebook Ads System course, which is what you should be going through once you are finished with this one.

### Completion Audit

- Did you read through every metric in the list above?
- Did you continue to Step #7: Create Your Columns?

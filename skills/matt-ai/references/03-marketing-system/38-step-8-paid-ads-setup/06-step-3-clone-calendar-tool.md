Attribution can get funky as you scale up, especially if you have other traffic sources. Instead of some complicated or custom solution, the most bare bones way to do attribution is to clone your calendar event and put it [yourlink.com/fb](http://yourlink.com/fb) or something.

That way, the only people who book on this calendar will be from Facebook Ads. It is not perfect, as someone may see your ad, click on your website instead and schedule differently, but it is fine for now, and will be more than enough for our purposes.

You do not need to invest in some insane traffic attribution system until you are already established and spending $1,000+ per day in my opinion. For most people, that will be very far away.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Calendly](https://calendly.com/), or go to your Calendly if you already have an account.** This is whatever people currently book on when they schedule a call with you.
2. **Duplicate the meeting people book on now.** Click the three dots on that meeting, then click "Duplicate".
3. **Click "More options".** This sits at the bottom of the panel on the right, next to "Save changes".
4. **Click "Booking page options".** This is where you can edit the slug for your event.
5. **Put "fb" in the slug field.** The slug is the last part of your booking link, so the link will end in /fb once you have changed it.
6. **Click "Save changes".** The new slug does not apply to your booking link until you save it.
7. **Copy the link to that meeting and book a call on it.** Paste it into a tab first and it should show [yourlink.com/fb](http://yourlink.com/fb). Booking on it confirms it lands in your calendar the same way the original does.

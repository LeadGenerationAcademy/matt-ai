### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a [Business Manager](https://www.facebook.com/business/tools/meta-business-suite).** This process always changes, so Google and ChatGPT will be your friend here more than a fixed set of instructions. Everything else on this page gets built underneath it.
2. **Make an ad account.** This is where your campaigns, budgets and billing will actually live.
  - Open Settings and choose "Business settings", not "Ad account settings"
  - Click "Ad accounts" under Accounts
  - Click "Add" in the top right
  - Click "Create a new ad account"
  - Follow the steps on screen to name it and set your time zone and currency
  - Click your new ad account once it appears in the list
  - Click "Open in Ads Manager" in the top right
3. **Make a pixel.** This is what lets Meta track conversions and results back to your ads.
  - Go to "Business settings" and click "Data Sources"
  - Click "Add" in the top right
  - Give the dataset a name
  - Select any categories that apply to your business
4. **Connect your Instagram account to your ad account and Business Manager.** You should already have set this profile up in the [Instagram Profile](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=e167f3d81da64b10b8343c377c61fc50) step of the Content Setup course. Connecting it is what lets you run ads that appear as coming from your actual Instagram account.
5. **Connect your Facebook page to your ad account and Business Manager.** You should already have set this page up in the [Facebook Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=1e16472a7f024daeb57239363b4eccce) step of the Content Setup course. Connecting it is what lets you run ads that appear as coming from your actual Facebook page.
6. **Connect your pixel to your ad account and Business Manager.** Once you have named the dataset and picked your categories, Meta will prompt you to choose the ad account you want to connect it to. Choose the ad account you just created. This is what lets your ad account actually see the conversion data your pixel collects.
7. **Put in a payment method.** Ads cannot run without a valid payment method attached.
  - Go to "Business settings" and click "Billing & payments"
  - Click "Payment methods"
  - Click "Add payment method" under your ad account
  - Add your card
8. **Bookmark your ad account to your browser.** Your ad account is a nightmare to get back to, which is why bookmarking it matters.
9. **Confirm your funnel is finished, since that's where you'll be sending traffic.** Every page in the [Funnel Pages](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=68a8c2b7f11e41b1996f9f6d67084446) course should already be built, apart from the DQ page, which is optional. There is no reason to launch ads toward a funnel that isn't ready to receive them.

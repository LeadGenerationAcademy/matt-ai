### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Click the Workflows tab on the left side of [Calendly](https://calendly.com/app/workflows), then click New Workflow.** This is where every automated reminder and notification for your bookings lives. Starting a new workflow from a template saves you from building one from scratch.
2. **Choose the Text reminder to host template.** This template already has the Send text to host action built in, so you're only adjusting the trigger.
3. **Choose your sales call event type, then click Apply.** This is the event that takes your sales calls.
4. **Click Edit on the When this happens container, change it to New event is booked, select Immediately when new event is booked, then click Done.** The template defaults to Before event starts. Changing it makes the text fire the instant a call gets booked, rather than before the call starts or on a delay.
5. **Click Edit on Send text to host and verify your phone number, then click Save on the workflow.** Your number is most likely already confirmed, but check that it's correct. Don't change anything else in this window. Saving is what turns the workflow on.
6. **Schedule a test meeting on your own booking page, then confirm you receive the text the moment it's booked.** Go through the exact process a real prospect would use to book a call. This proves the workflow fires immediately rather than on a delay.
7. **If it works, leave the workflow active. If not, ask ChatGPT or Claude to diagnose the problem.** Don't leave it half-tested. Either confirm it's working or get the failure diagnosed before moving on.

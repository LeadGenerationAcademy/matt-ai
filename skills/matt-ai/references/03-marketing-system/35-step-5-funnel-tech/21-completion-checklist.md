### Lead Magnet Automations & Delivery

- Automation set up to tag and deliver your Lead Magnet Delivery Email the moment someone opts in
- Automation set up to notify your team in Slack the moment someone opts in

### Application Automations

- Automation set up to tag and enroll new opt-ins or applications into your Welcome Flow
- Automation set up to notify your team in Slack the moment someone submits an application

### Call Booked Automations

- Automation set up to notify your team in Slack with the prospect's full context the moment a call is booked
- Calendly Workflow set up to text you immediately when a call is booked
- Automation set up to tag and enroll booked prospects into your Pre-Call Flow

### Call Follow Up Automations

- Automation set up to tag Closed Lost prospects from their real call summary and enroll them into your Follow Up Flow
- Automation set up to post the full call summary into Slack
- Automation set up to enroll Closed Lost prospects into your 90, 180, and 365 Day Winback sequences
- Automation set up to tag and enroll No Show leads into your No Show Flow when no real call summary exists
- Automation set up to mark clients Closed Won, tag them, and enroll them into your Onboarding Flow the moment they pay
- Automation set up to notify your team in Slack the moment a new payment comes through
- Automation set up to notify your team in Slack the moment a client submits their onboarding form

### CRM & Database

- CRM connected to every tool in your funnel, natively wherever a connection exists
- Confirmed new contacts are created in your CRM without duplicating
- Automation set up to send every opportunity from your CRM into your customer database, if you keep one separately

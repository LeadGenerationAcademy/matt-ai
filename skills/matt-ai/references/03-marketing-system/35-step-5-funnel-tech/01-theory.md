Every piece of this module exists for one reason: nothing about a prospect's journey should depend on someone remembering to do something manually. The moment a lead touches any part of your funnel, from opting in to becoming a client, an automation should already be moving them to the next step and letting the right person know it happened.

### Lead magnet delivery automations & integrations

The first automation in this module has to exist before anything else does. Someone opts in expecting to get what you promised them immediately, not whenever you happen to check your form submissions.

This section covers two things:

1. Delivery: tagging is what makes this possible. The moment someone opts in they get marked, and that tag is what fires the actual delivery email. No tag, no email, no matter how good the email itself is.
2. Notification: a fresh opt-in is the warmest a lead will ever be. If your team can see it land in real time, someone can reach out personally while that interest is still fresh instead of a week later when it's gone cold.

### Application automations & integrations

An application carries more intent than a simple opt-in, someone took the time to answer real questions about their business, which means they deserve two things happening at once:

- Nurture: they need to start receiving your Welcome Flow so the relationship keeps building while they wait to hear from you.
- Visibility: your team needs to see that application the second it comes in, because a higher-intent lead sitting unread in a form dashboard is the easiest kind of lead to lose.

Both of these run off the same core idea from the Lead Magnet automation, tag on submission, notify on submission, just with an application form for tools further down.

### Call booked automations & integrations

A booked call is the highest-value action in the whole funnel, someone put actual time on your calendar, and this is the point where speed decides how much of that intent survives to the actual call.

- Slack notification: whoever picks up the call already knows who they're talking to, not scrambling to figure out the prospect's context while the call is starting.
- SMS notification: you personally know the instant it happens, because fast, personal outreach right after a booking is what keeps a call from turning into a no-show.
- Pre-Call Flow: keeps that same energy alive automatically between the booking and the call itself, so the prospect shows up warm instead of having forgotten why they booked in the first place.

### Call follow up automations & integrations

Every call ends one of three ways, and none of those outcomes should ever be left for a human to manually route. Your Call Summary software gives you the real data from what actually happened on the call, and that single piece of data is what this whole section branches off of.

1. Closed Lost: if a real summary exists and the deal didn't close, the prospect gets tagged Closed Lost, dropped into your Follow Up Flow, and layered into a 90, 180, and 365 day Winback sequence so they keep hearing from you long after the call ends, since a "no" today isn't a "no" forever.
2. No Show: if no real summary exists, that almost always means the call never actually happened, so that lead gets tagged No Show and routed into a completely different sequence built to get them back on the calendar instead of trying to sell them on a call they never took.
3. Closed Won: if the outcome is a payment instead, none of the losing-path automations matter anymore. The CRM gets marked Closed Won, the client gets tagged, your team gets notified in Slack, and the Onboarding Flow starts automatically. The onboarding submission notification closes the loop, the moment that new client finishes the form on their side, your team knows to actually start the work on yours.

### CRM automations & integrations

Your CRM is the one system everything else in this module points back to, every tag, every status change, every automation in this section either reads from it or writes to it.

- Connection: it needs to be connected to every tool in your funnel first, natively wherever possible, through an automation platform everywhere else, before any of the other automations in this module can be trusted to work correctly.
- Database: this is the one exception that only applies to some of you. If you keep a separate customer database outside your CRM, Airtable, Google Sheets, or your own app, this connects your CRM directly to it so that data lives in two places instead of one. If your CRM is already the only place you store customer data, this step does nothing for you, and that's fine, skip it.

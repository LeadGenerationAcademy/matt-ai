# Action Steps

Course: Step #5: Funnel Tech
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=fba5d4721c334127acdea8000095bf0d

---
The Theory explains that every stage of your funnel, from a lead magnet opt-in through a client submitting their onboarding form, needs to move to the next step and notify the right person automatically instead of depending on someone remembering to do it manually.

This module is where you actually build that entire system.

You will connect your CRM to every tool in your funnel, then build the automations that deliver and announce lead magnets, nurture and announce applications, notify and warm up booked calls, route every call outcome into its correct follow-up path, and confirm payment and onboarding the moment a client actually joins.

Do not publish any automation without testing it end to end first. If an automation breaks somewhere without you knowing it, it'll be days or weeks before you realize it, and by that point you'll have lost tens if not hundreds of qualified leads.

### Table of Contents

1. **Lead Magnet:** Automatically tag and deliver your lead magnet to anyone who opts in.
2. **CRM:** Connect your CRM to every tool in your funnel so it becomes the central record of where each contact stands.
3. **Opt In > Slack Notification:** Notify your team in Slack the moment someone opts into your lead magnet.
4. **Database:** Automatically send every opportunity from your CRM into your customer database if you keep one separately.
5. **Application/Opt In > Welcome Flow:** Automatically enroll every new opt-in or application into your Welcome Flow.
6. **Application > Slack Notification:** Notify your team in Slack the moment someone submits your application.
7. **Call Booked > Slack Notification:** Send your team a Slack notification with full prospect context the moment a call is booked.
8. **Call Booked > SMS Notification:** Text yourself instantly when a call is booked so you can appointment-set fast.
9. **Call Booked > Pre Call Flow:** Automatically enroll every booked prospect into your Pre-Call Flow.
10. **Call Summary > Follow Up Flow:** Tag a prospect Closed Lost from their real call summary and enroll them into your Follow Up Flow.
11. **Call Summary > Slack Notification:** Post the full call summary into Slack the moment a real call is recorded.
12. **Call Summary > Winback Flow:** Enroll every Closed Lost prospect into a 90, 180, and 365 day Winback sequence.
13. **No Call Summary > No Show Flow:** Tag a lead No Show when no real call summary exists and enroll them into your No Show Flow.
14. **Payment > Onboarding Flow:** Mark a client Closed Won and automatically start their Onboarding Flow the moment they pay.
15. **Payment > Slack Notification:** Notify your team in Slack the moment a new payment comes through.
16. **Onboarding Submit > Slack Notification:** Notify your team in Slack the moment a new client submits their onboarding form.

### Phase #1: Clicks

#### Content

- You have a Beehiiv newsletter named after you, on your personal domain and showing Live.
- You have branded your newsletter with your name, one-line description, logo, thumbnail, language and timezone.
- You have built, published and tested your Newsletter Home Page.
- You have built, published and tested your Newsletter Subscribe Page.
- You have switched Instagram to a professional Creator account and connected it to Meta Business Manager.
- You have set up your Instagram profile with your headshot, One-Liner bio and YouTube link.
- You have four Instagram highlights (Clients, Wins, Testimonials and Reviews) with matching covers, filled with proof.
- You have set your Facebook page to public, with your profile picture, cover photo, One-Liner bio, Lead Magnet link and call-to-action button.
- You have connected your Facebook page to Instagram and Business Manager.
- You have set up your LinkedIn personal profile with your picture, cover photo, One-Liner headline, About section and Lead Magnet link.
- You have created your LinkedIn Company Page and added it to the Experience section of your personal profile.
- You have set up Twitter/X with your name, picture, cover photo, One-Liner bio, Lead Magnet link and a pinned post.
- You have set up your YouTube channel with your name, profile picture, banner, description and Lead Magnet link.
- You have your YouTube recording setup: Sony ZV10EF, Sigma 16mm lens, Shure MV7, OBS and Auphonic.
- You have a Content Tracking Spreadsheet updating automatically every day.
- You have the Content tab of your inputs spreadsheet filled in every day.

#### Paid Ads

- You have created your Business Manager, ad account and pixel.
- You have connected your Instagram, Facebook page and pixel to your ad account and Business Manager.
- You have added a payment method and bookmarked your ad account in your browser.
- You have installed your pixel on every page of your site, and it is receiving events.
- You have a separate Calendly meeting with the "fb" slug for Facebook ad traffic.
- You have a Schedule conversion event tracking your Call Booked Confirmation Page.
- You have had someone on Fiverr set up your Facebook CAPI (this can wait until later).
- You have saved an Ads Manager column preset with Application Rate, Booked Call Rate, Hook Rate and ThruPlay Rate as custom metrics.
- You have every call booked through your Facebook calendar sent to Slack.

#### Outreach

- You have bought 30 .com cold email domains, authenticated them with SMTP, DMARC, DKIM and SPF, and redirected them to your main domain.
- You have attached 30 inboxes to your domains.
- You have set up your cold email tool on the plan around $97 per month.
- You have warmed up every inbox for 14 to 30 days.
- You have set sending to 9:00 a.m. ET, Monday to Friday, with stop conditions and your 3-part campaign set up.
- You have interested leads sent to Slack with your appointment setter tagged, and synced to your CRM with a "cold email" tag.
- You have exported your cold email list, with at least 80% of a sample matching your ICP.
- You have written your three cold emails.
- You have added spintax to every word of all three emails.
- You have your cold email activity filled in on your inputs spreadsheet every day.
- You have built your Dream 100 List with contact details and socials, and you work it 30 to 60 minutes a day.
- You have written your Dream 100 copy with who you help, the clients you've worked with and an offer to send your One-Pager.
- You have a VA or team member managing the outreach inbox and passing replies to whoever is doing the calling.

### Phase #2: Calls

#### Pages

- You have built your Opt In Page with your logo, star rating, ICP callout and Lead Magnet headline, with the form connected to your email platform or CRM.
- You have built your Opt In Confirmation Page with a banner confirming the Lead Magnet is on its way, your VSL, testimonials and a CTA linked to your booking page.
- You have built your Sales Page Above The Fold with your logo, star rating, ICP callout, headline, subheadline, VSL and a CTA button linked to your booking page.
- You have added client logos, testimonials, case studies, reviews and client win screenshots to the Rest Of Sales Page.
- You have written your application's budget, authority, need and start date questions, plus at least four questions that help your sales process.
- You have added name, email, phone and website fields to your application.
- You have qualified applicants sent to your Call Booked Page and unqualified applicants sent to your DQ Page.
- You have a Calendly embed on your Call Booked Page that only allows booking within a 72 hour window.
- You have built your DQ Page with a headline, subheadline and CTA button pointing to your low-end offer (optional).

#### Lead Magnet

- You have written every step of your service out in detail in your Lead Magnet, with one image, one example and an action checklist per idea.
- You have written your Lead Magnet Delivery Email with a "[REPLY REQUIRED]" subject line, the primary benefit, the Lead Magnet link and a reply prompt.
- You have a phone number field and SMS consent on your opt-in form, with the Lead Magnet link sent by text.
- You have every opt-in sent to Beehiiv through Zapier and sent the Lead Magnet Delivery Email.

#### Emails & SMS

- You have written your Welcome Flow: four first-hour emails, 30 daily FAQ emails and 4 weekly SMS messages, stopping the moment someone books.
- You have every opt-in and application tagged in your email platform and enrolled in the Welcome Flow.
- You have your Application Confirmation SMS ready for your appointment setter to send manually, with your Loom and One-Pager, for applicants who don't book within five minutes.
- You have your Application Appointment Setting Message sending automatically by SMS with your name, company, Loom and One-Pager when someone applies without booking.

#### Videos

- You have recorded your VSL with proof in the first five seconds and your full Offer explained in the first 15 seconds, with subtitles and a thumbnail.

#### Notifications

- You have every opt-in sent to its own Slack channel.
- You have every application sent to its own Slack channel.

### Phase #3: Shows

#### Pages

- You have built your Thank You Page with a headline confirming the booking and steps to reply to your text and accept the calendar invitation.
- You have added your Trustpilot reviews and client win screenshots to your Thank You Page.

#### Videos

- You have recorded your Thank You Page Main VSL, two to five minutes long, answering the six to 12 biggest FAQs and objections people have after booking.
- You have recorded three to five Thank You Page FAQ Videos, 2 to 5 minutes each, with the question on every thumbnail.
- You have recorded your Pre-Call Selfie Video, a natural, unedited phone video introducing you and your company, and you send it manually whenever someone books.
- You have a One-Pager that fits inside one iPhone screenshot, saved as a PDF, an image and a link.
- You have recorded your 3-Minute Information Loom talking through your One-Pager in under three minutes, with the One-Pager and Calendly links inside.
- You have recorded 30 to 50 Retargeting/FAQ Videos under 60 seconds, each answering one question, and posted them to every company profile.
- You have your FAQ videos running as retargeting ads to people who applied or booked, at around $5 to $20 per day.

#### Emails & SMS

- You have your Call Book Confirmation SMS sending automatically with your company name, Loom and One-Pager the moment someone books.
- You have an email and SMS confirmation going to the prospect, and a separate notification going to your closer.
- You have email and SMS reminders going out 24 hours, 4 hours and 1 hour before the call.
- You have written your Pre-Call Email Flow: up to 18 emails, one every four hours after booking, stopping when the call begins.
- You have your Call Booked Appointment Setting Message ready to send manually whenever someone books, offering the booked time, an earlier call or texting.
- You have written your No Show Flow: 24 emails over eight weeks, stopping the moment they rebook.

#### Notifications & Automations

- You have every booked call sent to its own Slack channel.
- You have a text sent to you the moment a call is booked.
- You have every booked call tagged "Call Booked" and enrolled in the Pre-Call Flow.
- You have every call with no call summary tagged "No Show" and enrolled in the No Show Flow.

### Phase #4: Closes

#### Sales Call

- You have written out your Sales Call Plan's intro, five questions, demo, objection handling and closing/next steps.
- You have trimmed your Offer Presentation to 5 to 7 minutes and use it as your demo.

#### Closing

- You have a Payment One-Pager with what they're getting, what happens after they pay and their first 90 days with specific milestones.
- You have contract and payment link templates, sent with the One-Pager within 5 minutes of the call.
- You have your payment link redirecting to your onboarding form after payment.
- You have your Onboarding Package sent in Slack with five Quick Wins that can be done within 24 hours.
- You have booked the Onboarding Call, with Objectives broken into Key Results and Assignments.
- You have set up the Morning and Evening Updates and booked a 30 Minute Weekly Meeting.

#### Emails

- You have written your Post-Call Follow Up Flow with case studies, insights and FAQs sent from your closer, stopping if they reply, book, buy or ask to stop.
- You have written your 90 Day Winback Flow: 9 emails over 30 days, starting 90 days after the Follow Up Flow begins.
- You have written your 180 Day Winback Flow: 9 emails over 30 days, starting 180 days after the Follow Up Flow begins.
- You have written your 365 Day Winback Flow: 9 emails over 30 days, starting 365 days after the Follow Up Flow begins.

#### Notifications & Automations

- You have every completed call tagged "Closed Lost" and enrolled in the Follow Up Flow.
- You have every call summary sent to its own Slack channel.
- You have every completed call tagged for the 90, 180 and 365 Day Winback Flows.
- You have every payment tagged "New Client" and enrolled in your Onboarding Flow.
- You have every payment sent to its own Slack channel.
- You have every onboarding form submission sent to its own Slack channel.

Do you have the following set up:

### Sales Call

- **[Sales Call Plan](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=0ffffe13343d433ea57c90f4f4562b19):** Intro, five questions, demo, objection handling and closing/next steps written out
- **[Sales Call Presentation](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=798b4bf06e1a4581b174f13c5c253fa1):** Your Offer Presentation trimmed to 5 to 7 minutes and used as your demo

### Closing

- **[Payment One-Pager](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=dd1106722d2e470998c85c56e60883fb):** What they're getting, what happens after they pay and their first 90 days with specific milestones
- **[Contract And Payment](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=dd1106722d2e470998c85c56e60883fb):** Contract and payment link templates, sent with the One-Pager within 5 minutes of the call
- **[Onboarding Form](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=ea36b02951b246a7bd933a6e55267934):** Your payment link redirecting to your onboarding form after payment
- **[Onboarding Package](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=ea36b02951b246a7bd933a6e55267934):** Sent in Slack with five Quick Wins that can be done within 24 hours
- **[Onboarding Call](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=ea36b02951b246a7bd933a6e55267934):** Booked, with Objectives broken into Key Results and Assignments
- **[Client Reporting](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=ea36b02951b246a7bd933a6e55267934):** Morning and Evening Updates set up and a 30 Minute Weekly Meeting booked

### Emails

- **[Post-Call Follow Up Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=1664da623aa544a2b921c81d2c771598):** Case studies, insights and FAQs sent from your closer, stopping if they reply, book, buy or ask to stop
- **[90 Day Winback Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=aee0e15afb6044ff9f0bd585e8965856):** 9 emails over 30 days, starting 90 days after the Follow Up Flow begins
- **[180 Day Winback Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=7cd8b49a45d1414592d1b01cc59ec238):** 9 emails over 30 days, starting 180 days after the Follow Up Flow begins
- **[365 Day Winback Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=b5b74b411c94404ebf78d75d20cdfe38):** 9 emails over 30 days, starting 365 days after the Follow Up Flow begins

### Notifications & Automations

- **[Follow Up Flow Automation](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=bf942b1fff484338a83be3111614d580):** Every completed call tagged "Closed Lost" and enrolled in the Follow Up Flow
- **[Call Summary Slack Notification](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=4d84eae5e11647a690c32ffead493a6c):** Every call summary sent to its own Slack channel
- **[Winback Automation](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=0b55d0b2480449399dccb11464e780ed):** Every completed call tagged for the 90, 180 and 365 Day Winback Flows
- **[Onboarding Flow Automation](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=3137dd50589c4e95a3ee25e3309174ea):** Every payment tagged "New Client" and enrolled in your Onboarding Flow
- **[Payment Slack Notification](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=5d072c8cf7344461a56f6fdcef389621):** Every payment sent to its own Slack channel
- **[Onboarding Slack Notification](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=68629028d1bb48e1a29d3dddcb798e5c):** Every onboarding form submission sent to its own Slack channel

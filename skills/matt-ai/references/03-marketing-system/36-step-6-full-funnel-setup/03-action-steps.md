The Theory explains what every piece of your funnel does, from the moment someone clicks through to the moment they pay you.

This course is where you make sure your full funnel is set up, from the first page someone lands on to the winback flows that bring back prospects who didn't buy.

You will set up everything in Booking Calls to increase your booked call rate, everything in Pre-Call to increase your show rate, and everything in Post-Call to increase your close rate.

Do not skip a piece because it seems small. If any part of your funnel falls apart, you do not get the client.

- **Booking Calls:** Set up every page in this step to increase your booked call rate, by giving every person who clicks enough information and enough reason to book a call.
- **Pre-Call:** Set up every page in this step to increase your show rate, so prospects remember the call, still want to have it, and show up already understanding your offer.
- **Post-Call:** Set up every page in this step to increase your close rate, so every sales call finishes with either a sale or a clear next step.

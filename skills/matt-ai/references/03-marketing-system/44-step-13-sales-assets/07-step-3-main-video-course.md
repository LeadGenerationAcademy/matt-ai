### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Start from the Lead Magnet you already built.** This is the video version of it, so the subject and the material are already decided. You are turning what they read into something they watch.
2. **Build the presentation you will teach from.** One image and one idea per slide for maximum readability. Put a lot of effort into it, since it doubles as internal training and it is also what you will offer as a free download.
3. **Structure the presentation around theory, frameworks, examples, and action steps.** Go in-depth on all four. By the end they should have everything they need to actually go and do it.
4. **Open by stating what the course covers and how long it runs.** Tell them how many hours it is and what they will learn, before you start teaching anything.
5. **Introduce yourself right after that.** Assume they have never watched anything of yours before. Your name, where you are, how long you have been in this space, and what you have built or sold in that time.
6. **Set up screen share with your camera on.** Screen for the presentation, camera for you. That is the whole production requirement.
7. **Record it in one go with no teleprompter and no editor.** This is the part your competitors cannot copy. Anyone can script five minutes. Nobody can talk intelligently about their own craft for hours without hiding behind a teleprompter and editing.
8. **Go deep enough that it runs hours rather than minutes.** Four hours, eight hours, whatever. The point is not the length. The point is that you know enough to keep going.
9. **Offer the presentation as a free download in the video, the description, and the pinned comment.** Say it out loud while you are going through it and put the link in both places. It goes straight to their email, which is how a viewer becomes a lead.
10. **Upload it to YouTube.** These rank in search and get views forever, so it keeps working as a nurture tool for anyone who finds you through ads, other content, or outreach.
11. **Use the same video for internal training and recruiting.** People who watch it view you as an expert and are already bought into your philosophy, and that applies to people you hire as much as people who buy.

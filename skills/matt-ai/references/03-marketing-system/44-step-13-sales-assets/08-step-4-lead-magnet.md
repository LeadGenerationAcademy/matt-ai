# Step #4: Lead Magnet

Course: Step #13: Sales Assets
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/2818ecce?md=2f86bd19d6614f27a2ea25274049c68e

---
Before anyone buys from you, they have no idea how good or bad your service is. All they have to judge you on is your free stuff. If your free stuff is bad, or you have none at all, they assume your service is bad. If it's great, they assume your service is great.

So your Lead Magnet isn't a box to tick. It's one of your products, and they're paying in time instead of money. It's the information-only side of your core service, and if they like what they see, they're more likely to come and ask about the paid version.

Which is why it has to be your Magnum Opus. Every piece, part and step of what you do, in extreme detail, with nothing held back.

![image](https://cdn.gamma.app/5xhi0djo80t9p7f/d95cfcdc07c740a6a9dcfefcc56a4dc8/original/image.png)

### Framework & Criteria

- Lay out your entire service in terms of steps or different parts that it takes to be successful
- Follow the VAR formula
  - Valuable: Even though it is free, would you pay $100 for it?
  - Actionable: Things they can do within 24 hours to get a concrete improvement
  - Readable: One idea + image per slide
- Each slide (or idea if there are multiple slides in a larger idea) should follow:
  - Theory
  - Example(s)
  - Action steps

### Step-By-Step Instructions

1. **Break your service down into every step involved from start to finish.** Take your overall service from the very start to the very finish. This comes with the added benefit that you can use it internally to train your employees, since everything they need will be included.
2. **Write out exactly what to do for every step in extreme detail, with nothing held back.** People worry about giving it all away and say that if they teach everything they know, nobody will hire them. That is the opposite of the truth. Busy, successful people do not want to do the service themselves, and in many cases do not even want to know how. They just want to know that you know how.
3. **Separate every new idea onto its own slide or headline.** Huge walls of text with no clear separation of ideas are what make something hard to read, and the more they consume, the greater the chance they become a paying client.
4. **Add one image per idea.** No examples, frameworks or images is another thing that makes a Lead Magnet hard to get through.
5. **Add one example per idea.** This can also be the image part if the image serves as an example too.
6. **Add a summary checklist of action steps for each idea.** This also benefits the Actionable part of the VAR Rule. A good rule of thumb is that the checklist should be so good that people could do whatever you are telling them to do using only the checklist, with no other external resources or explanations.
7. **Ask yourself if you would honestly pay $100 for it.** Seriously, no joke or word of a lie. If the answer is not a clear yes then it is a no by default, and if you have to think about it, then no. You can also ask your network and peers to look at it and tell you what they think.
8. **Ask yourself if there is a clear action they could take within the next 24 hours.** If they consume any part of it, there should be a clear action or set of actions that would help their business. If not, it needs to be reworked.
9. **Confirm your Lead Magnet is the information-only side of your core service.** There is no point identifying people interested in a subject, demonstrating the quality of your thinking, establishing yourself as an authority and building trust, all for a topic completely unrelated to the service your business actually provides.
10. **Break your Magnum Opus into 10-20 smaller Lead Magnets.** Once every slide represents your best work, each one becomes modular and standalone, so you can copy and paste a unique combination of slides without having to rewrite everything all the time.

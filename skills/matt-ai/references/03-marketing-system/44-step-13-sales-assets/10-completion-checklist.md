### Main Video Course

- Started from the Lead Magnet you already built
- Built the presentation you teach from
- Structured the presentation around theory, frameworks, examples and action steps
- Opened by stating what the course covers and how long it runs
- Introduced yourself right after that

### Lead Magnet

- Broke your service down into every step involved from start to finish
- Wrote out exactly what to do for every step in extreme detail, with nothing held back
- Separated every new idea onto its own slide or headline
- Added one image per idea
- Added one example per idea
- Added a summary checklist of action steps for each idea
- Confirmed you would honestly pay $100 for it
- Confirmed there is a clear action they could take within the next 24 hours
- Confirmed it is the information-only side of your core service
- Broke your Magnum Opus into 10 to 20 smaller Lead Magnets

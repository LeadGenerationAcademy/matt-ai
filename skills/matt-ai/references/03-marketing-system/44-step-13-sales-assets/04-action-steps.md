# Action Steps

Course: Step #13: Sales Assets
System: Marketing System

The Theory explains that great content is proof you actually know what you are talking about, and that there are three monster content assets worth making: a full course, an in-depth audit or case study, and a complete walkthrough of you doing the work.

This course is where you actually record them.

You will record a case study built around a single result, an audit or rebuild where you do the work on screen, and a full course taught from a presentation, then write the Lead Magnet that the course is the video version of.

### Table of Contents

1. **Main Video Case Study:** Record one result in enough depth that nobody can question whether it happened, with the real numbers throughout and what didn't work said out loud.
2. **Main Video Audit/Rebuild:** Go through what a business already has piece by piece, or build one from scratch on screen, doing the work in the actual tools instead of talking about it.
3. **Main Video Course:** Teach one subject all the way through from a presentation, and offer that presentation as a free download in the video, the description and the pinned comment.
4. **Lead Magnet:** Write out every step of your service in extreme detail with nothing held back, then break the Magnum Opus into 10 to 20 smaller lead magnets.

Someone looking at your business has one doubt a testimonial can't kill. They're sitting there thinking okay, I see he has done it for that business, but will it work for me?

A case study answers it. One clear story, told in enough depth that nobody can question whether it happened. Great content is proof you actually know what you are talking about, and this is the version of that proof built around a single result.

It lives on YouTube and does the work for you from then on. Anyone who comes in through your ads is eventually going to find it.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Pick the result you have the most documentation for.** It can be a client or it can be your own business. What decides it is how much you can actually show, not how impressive the number sounds.
2. **Decide whether you are covering the whole engagement or one part of it.** Either works. Start to finish, or one part of it gone into properly.
3. **Gather everything you have on that result before you record.** Links, screenshots, numbers, the assets themselves. Have it all open before you start.
4. **Set up screen share with your camera on.** Screen for the work, camera for you. That is the whole production requirement.
5. **Record it in one go with no teleprompter and no editor.** This is the part your competitors cannot copy. Anyone can script five minutes.
6. **State your relationship to the result in the first few minutes.** Whether it is your client, your partner, or your own business, and how you get paid on it. In the finance agency case study that is a partnership paid 30% of net profits quarterly, said out loud before anything else.
7. **Lay out the problems before you show anything you built.** The finance agency case study goes through the four problems every agency faces before it shows a single piece of the funnel. Problems first, then the build that answered them.
8. **Give the real numbers the whole way through.** Cost per lead, price of the intro offer, margin, ad spend, close rate at seven days and at thirty days, average days from call to close. Specifics are what stop it sounding like a story.
9. **Say what didn't work.** The finance agency case study names the short form video they never made, the referral and media partnerships they do badly, and the two ad funnels they turned back off after those wouldn't scale.
10. **Make it extremely deep.** The point is not the length. The point is that you know enough to talk intelligently about your craft.
11. **Upload it to YouTube.** These barely bring in new viewers on their own. What they do is get watched by the people who already found you through your ads, content, or outreach, before they book.

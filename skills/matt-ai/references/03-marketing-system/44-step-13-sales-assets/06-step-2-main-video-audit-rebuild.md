---
A case study shows a result that already happened. An audit or rebuild shows the work.

The audit is you going through what a business already has, piece by piece. The rebuild is you building one from scratch on screen, the way you would build it if it were yours. Same asset, two shapes.

Great content is proof you actually know what you are talking about. An audit or rebuild is the version of that proof where you are in the tools on screen, making the calls in real time, with no editor cleaning it up afterwards.

It lives on YouTube and does the work for you from then on. Anyone who comes in through your ads is eventually going to find it.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Decide whether you are auditing what already exists or rebuilding it from scratch.** The audit is you going through what they actually have, like their ad library frame by frame. The rebuild is you building a business from scratch on screen, the way you would build it if it were yours.
2. **Gather the assets or set the assumptions before you record.** For an audit that means their ads, their funnel, and their pages open and ready. For a rebuild you have to make some form of assumptions, so decide the margin, the churn, the average client payment, and the close rate before you start.
3. **Set up screen share with your camera on.** Screen for the work, camera for you. That is the whole production requirement.
4. **Record it in one go with no teleprompter and no editor.** This is the part your competitors cannot copy. It also means the mistakes stay in. In the $10 million agency video the camera overheats, the slides get formatted wrong, and the software does something stupid, and it keeps going anyway.
5. **State your credentials in the first few minutes.** Assume they have never seen anything of yours. In the $10 million agency video that is 550 agencies worked with since the start of 2024, 66 of them past 5 million per year, and an agency grown to 900K per month and sold in 2023.
6. **Point them to your other proof while you are there.** The $10 million agency video names two case studies on the same channel, 113K to 1.8 million per month and 61K to 379K per month. The case study you built in Step #1 is what you point at here.
7. **Say upfront what you are covering and what you are not.** State the agenda, then state the limits. The $10 million agency video says plainly that it is high level and that actually building the whole thing out on camera would take 20 to 30 hours even with AI.
8. **Build from the ground up in the order it would actually get built.** Economics, then strategy, then messaging, then offers, then the sales process, then the bottom, middle, and top of the funnel. You would not put the roof on a house before the foundation, the walls, and the frame.
9. **Do the work in the actual tools on screen.** The $10 million agency video runs the numbers through the reverse engineering calculator, then builds the ICP language and the 25 ad angles live in ChatGPT and a slide deck.
10. **Run the real numbers.** In the $10 million agency video that is a 30% net margin, 10% monthly churn, $10,000 average client payment, a 20% close rate, and $400 per qualified call, which works out to 100 clients, 500 calls, and $548 per day in ad spend.
11. **Say why you are rejecting the options you reject.** The $10 million agency video rules out the digital product, rules out the webinar funnel, and decides against hiring a closer, and gives the reason for each one.
12. **Make it extremely deep.** The point is not the length. The point is that you know enough to talk intelligently about your craft.
13. **Upload it to YouTube.** These barely bring in new viewers on their own. What they do is get watched by the people who already found you through your ads, content, or outreach, before they book.

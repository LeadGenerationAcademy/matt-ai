### AI Prompt:

Using only the AI Company Context Document below, write the complete 34-email Welcome Flow.

This flow is for prospects who opted into a lead magnet or completed an application but did not book a call.

Use this schedule:

- Email 1 (after five minutes): Deliver the lead magnet and explain what the company does
- Email 2 (after one hour): Explain how the company does it
- Email 3 (after two hours): Show proof
- Email 4 (after three hours): Present the offer with a direct CTA
- Emails 5–34: One email per day for 30 days, each answering one FAQ or objection

Arrange the 30 FAQs and objections by importance because prospects who book a call will leave this flow early.

For each email:

- Stick to the AI Company Context Document 100%.
- Do not invent, assume, or add information.
- Preserve the author's tone.
- Write between 300 and 1,000 words.
- Communicate one main idea.
- Include concrete proof whenever it is available.
- For Emails 5–34, answer one FAQ or objection and include concrete proof.
- Change the introduction, ending, and sign-off throughout the series.
- Never use the same introduction or ending in consecutive emails.
- Keep the language serious and professional.
- Give them useful information instead of only asking them to book.
- Treat all 34 emails as one connected series.
- Make every email understandable if they missed the previous emails.
- End every email with a clear CTA and link to book a call.
- Follow the Global Copywriting Rules.
- If essential information is missing, ask for it instead of inventing it.

For each email, provide:

1. Email number
2. Send time
3. Purpose
4. Subject line
5. Preview text
6. Finished email

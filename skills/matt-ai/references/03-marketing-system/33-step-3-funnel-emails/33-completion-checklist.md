### Platform Setup

- Chose an Email Platform that supports automated Flows, triggers, delays, contact lists or tags, and Flow suppression
- Confirmed your Email Platform supports personalization, reply handling, a dedicated sending domain, webhooks or integrations, and performance reporting
- Got a business SMS number
- Registered for A2P 10DLC if applicable
- Added SMS consent language to your Forms
- Set up STOP and opt-out handling
- Connected your CRM to track every stage of the funnel, from Lead Magnet requests through Purchases and Non-Purchases
- Created your Flow Rules so only one primary Flow controls the next step at a time
- Submitted every Form yourself and confirmed the contact appears in the CRM
- Confirmed the correct Email Flow begins
- Confirmed the correct SMS Flow begins
- Confirmed personalization works
- Confirmed replies reach a monitored inbox
- Confirmed opt-outs work
- Confirmed booking a call stops the Welcome Flow

### Email Domain Setup

- Created a dedicated sending subdomain using your company domain
- Connected the subdomain inside your Email Platform
- Routed Funnel Emails through your company sending domain
- Routed your Newsletter through your personal domain
- Added the SPF record your Email Platform provided
- Added and verified every DKIM record your Email Platform provided
- Added the DMARC record your Email Platform recommended
- Set your sending address to a real inbox that can receive replies
- Confirmed your Email Platform shows the domain as verified
- Confirmed SPF, DKIM, and DMARC all pass
- Confirmed replies reach the correct inbox
- Confirmed unsubscribe handling works
- Started sending only to people actively entering your Funnel

### AI Company Context & Writing Rules

- Filled out every section of the AI Company Context Document
- Ranked your Top 25 Problems, Circumstances, and Outcomes into your Top 25 Ad Angles
- Downloaded the completed document
- Are attaching the document to every new AI chat when writing copy
- Made a copy of the Global Writing Rules, or confirmed you already had one
- Pasted the Global Writing Rules into every existing AI project
- Pasted the Global Writing Rules into every new AI project going forward
- Are updating the Global Writing Rules as you use them

### Lead Magnet Delivery

- Wrote your Lead Magnet Delivery Email with the "[REPLY REQUIRED]" subject line, a greeting, the lead magnet's benefit, the link, a reply prompt, and a sign-off
- Added the email to your Lead Magnet Delivery automation
- Tested it by submitting the Opt-In Form yourself and confirmed you received it
- Added a Phone Number field and SMS consent language to the Opt-In Form
- Confirmed your system records phone number, consent, date and time, and form together
- Set up STOP and opt-out handling for SMS
- Wrote your Lead Magnet Delivery SMS with the recipient's first name, the lead magnet name, a reliable shortened link, and a note that it was also sent by email
- Added the SMS to your Lead Magnet Delivery automation
- Tested it by submitting the Opt-In Form with your own phone number and confirmed both the SMS and Email arrived

### Welcome Flow

- Made a copy of the Welcome Flow Template
- Exported and ranked your top 30 FAQs and objections, with concrete proof for each
- Wrote the four Hour emails: What We Do, How It Works, Proof, and Call To Action
- Wrote all 30 daily FAQ emails
- Wrote the 4 weekly Welcome SMS messages
- Set the trigger to begin on a Lead Magnet request, Opt-In, or incomplete Application, delayed by five minutes
- Set the suppression rule to stop the Flow the moment someone books a call
- Added the Flow to your Email Platform
- Tested the Flow across every scenario: a Lead Magnet Opt-In, another Opt-In, an Applicant who doesn't book, and an Applicant who books immediately

### Pre-Call Flow

- Made a copy of the Pre-Call Flow Template
- Exported and ranked your top 18 FAQs and objections
- Wrote all 18 emails, each covering one FAQ or objection in the required format
- Made sure each email includes an example, a checklist, and a P.S. linking to your One-Pager and Loom video
- Set the Flow to send one email every four hours after booking
- Set the Flow to stop the moment the call begins or the prospect unsubscribes
- Tested the Flow end to end
- Confirmed every link works before launching

### Call Confirmations & Reminders

- Built your Confirmation Email with the call date, time, time zone, closer's name, One-Pager link, and Loom link
- Built your Confirmation SMS with the same information
- Set both to send immediately after booking
- Built a separate Closer Notification with the prospect's name, company, website, email, phone number, call date and time, and Application/CRM link
- Set the Closer Notification to send immediately after booking
- Tested all notifications end to end and confirmed every link works
- Turned on the built-in Email and SMS reminders inside your Scheduling Platform
- Set reminders for 24 hours, 4 hours, and 1 hour before the call
- Confirmed the prospect's name, closer's name, and call date/time/time zone populate in every reminder
- Removed any meeting links from the reminders
- Confirmed reminders automatically update on reschedule and stop on cancellation
- Tested the full reminder sequence end to end

### No-Show Flow

- Made a copy of the No Show Flow Template
- Chose and ranked your 24 most important FAQs and objections
- Reused your strongest Pre-Call Flow content, reframed for someone who already booked but didn't attend
- Wrote all 24 emails on the correct schedule (daily for two weeks, three times a week for two weeks, then weekly for four weeks)
- Made sure each email references the missed call and varies its beginning, ending, and sign-off
- Set the Flow to remove someone the moment they rebook a call
- Set the Flow to end after eight weeks if they don't rebook
- Tested the Flow end to end
- Confirmed every link works before launching

### Post-Call Follow-Up Flow

- Made a copy of the Follow-Up Flow Template
- Chose your biggest case study, transformation, and industry insight
- Chose your three most important FAQs
- Chose seven additional case studies and seven additional industry insights
- Created the emails as CRM templates or snippets, each referencing the sales call and something specific the prospect mentioned
- Set the emails to send from the closer's address with replies routed back to them
- Set the Flow to stop immediately if the prospect replies, books, purchases, or asks to stop
- Tested the Flow end to end
- Confirmed every link works before launching

### Winback Flows (90/180/365 Day)

- Made a copy of each Winback Flow Template
- Wrote the company reintroduction, personal reintroduction, and offer/hard pitch for each Flow
- Chose three case studies and three insights for each Flow, keeping every Flow's proof distinct from the other two
- Built or confirmed your One-Pager and Loom video
- Added both links to every email in each Flow
- Set each Flow to trigger at the correct interval (90, 180, or 365 days) after the original Follow-Up Flow begins
- Set each Flow to remove someone immediately if they become a customer
- Set each Flow to end after 30 days
- Tested each Flow end to end
- Confirmed every link works before launching

- Built your One-Pager with the key information, a Loom video, and the call-booking link
- Wrote your Application Confirmation SMS template and saved it on the appointment setter's phone
- Created a Zapier notification for applications that don't book within five minutes
- Confirmed the appointment setter sends the Application Confirmation SMS manually
- Wrote your automated Application Appointment-Setting Message with your name, company, and One-Pager link
- Connected the Application Appointment-Setting Message to your application automation
- Wrote your Call Book Confirmation SMS with your company name and One-Pager link
- Connected the Call Book Confirmation SMS to your call-booked automation
- Wrote your Call Booked Appointment Setting Message template with the prospect's name, your name, your company, and the booked call date and time
- Confirmed you're sending the Call Booked Appointment Setting Message manually whenever someone books
- Tested each message and confirmed every link works

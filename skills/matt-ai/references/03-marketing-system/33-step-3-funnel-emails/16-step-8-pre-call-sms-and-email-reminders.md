Use the built-in Email and SMS reminders inside Calendly or whichever Scheduling Platform you use, set at three specific times before the call, so the prospect actually shows up.

### Sign Up

- [Calendly](https://calendly.com/)

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Reminder Times (Email And SMS, Both):**
  1. 24 hours before the call
  2. 4 hours before the call
  3. 1 hour before the call
2. **Reminder Content:**
  1. Prospect's first name
  2. Closer's name
  3. Call date
  4. Call time
  5. Time Zone
3. **No Links:** No meeting links included, the prospect already has that in their Calendar invitation.
4. **Automatic Updates:** Reminders automatically update on reschedule and stop on cancellation.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Calendly Or Your Scheduling Platform.** You don't need to build these reminders separately, use the reminders that already come with your platform. They already have good deliverability, send automatically, and don't cost anything extra.
2. **Open Your Sales Call Event.** Click the three dots on the event and click Edit, or just click the event itself. Both open the same settings.
3. **Click More Options.** It's at the bottom of the settings panel, and it opens the rest of the event's settings.
4. **Click Notifications And Workflows.** This is where Calendly keeps the reminders it sends before a call.
5. **Turn On Email Reminders And Text Reminders.** If either one says Off, click its three dots and switch it On. Sending both means they see the reminder whether they're checking their inbox or their phone. Both start at 24 hours before the call, which puts the call back in front of them the day before. Someone who booked three days ago may have barely thought about it since.
6. **Click The Three Dots Next To Email Reminders And Click Edit.** This opens the email reminder, where you'll add the other two times.
7. **Add A 4 Hour Reminder.** Scroll down to Timing, press Add Another Reminder, and change it to 4 hours before the event. This brings the call back to their attention as it gets closer.
8. **Add A 1 Hour Reminder.** Press Add Another Reminder again and change it to 1 hour before the event. This is the final reminder they'll receive, don't add anything closer than this. A lot of people send one 5 or 15 minutes before, but if someone is in a meeting, at the gym, or doesn't have their phone in their hand, there isn't much they can do about it by then. One hour before has always led to the best show rate.
9. **Click Save And Close.** Email Reminders should now show 24 hours, 4 hours, and 1 hour before the event.
10. **Add The Same 4 Hour And 1 Hour Reminders To Text Reminders.** Click the three dots next to Text Reminders, click Edit, press Add Another Reminder twice, change them to 4 hours and 1 hour, then click Save And Close. Text Reminders should now show the same three times as your email.
11. **Click Save Changes.** Nothing you changed on the event is kept until you do.
12. **Test The Full Reminder Sequence End To End.** Book a call yourself and check that the 24-hour, 4-hour, and 1-hour reminders all send by both Email and SMS, with the right name, date, time, time zone, and a working meeting link. Then reschedule the test call to confirm every reminder updates, and cancel it to confirm they all stop. Don't launch until every reminder fires at the correct time.

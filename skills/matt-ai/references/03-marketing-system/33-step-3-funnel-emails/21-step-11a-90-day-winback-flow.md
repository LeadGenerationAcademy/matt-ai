# Step #11A: 90 Day Winback Flow

Course: Step #3: Funnel Emails

The 90 Day Winback Flow reactivates prospects 90 days after their original Sales Call. Most people will not buy and pay in full right on the call, so you need a system that keeps following up long after the call is over.

A lot can change in 90 days. Their circumstances may be different, and your company should have new information, proof, and insights to share since the last time you spoke.

The first Emails say directly that it has been 90 days since the call and reintroduce both you and the company. Three months is long enough that the prospect will not know what you are talking about unless you tell them.

Everything you send has to be genuinely new. Repeating case studies and insights they already received makes it look like nothing has happened at your company since the call.

The objective is to restart the conversation and get them to book another call.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Trigger:** 90 days pass after the prospect entered the original Follow-Up Flow, without becoming a customer.
2. **Complete 9-Email Schedule (Over 30 Days):**
  1. Day 1: Reintroduce the company
  2. Day 2: Reintroduce yourself
  3. Day 3: Hard pitch, offer, and CTA
  4. Nearest Monday: Case study
  5. Following Thursday: Insight
  6. Following Monday: Case study
  7. Following Thursday: Insight
  8. Following Monday: Case study
  9. Following Thursday: Insight
3. **Time Reference:** The first three Emails state directly that it has been 90 days since the original Sales Call.
4. **Reintroduction:** The first two Emails reintroduce the company and the sender by name, since the prospect has not heard from you in 90 days.
5. **New Information:** Every case study and insight is new information the prospect has not already received in an earlier Flow.
6. **Distinct Proof:** Case studies and insights must be different from the 180 Day and 365 Day Winback Flows.
7. **Every Email Includes:** A link to a One-Pager (with the call-booking link) and a three-minute Loom reviewing the One-Pager.
8. **Tone:** Every Email is as short and simple as possible, written like something manually sent to one person, not a Marketing Email.
9. **Stop Condition:** Remove the prospect from this Flow immediately if they become a customer.
10. **End Condition:** The campaign ends after 30 days.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [90 Day Winback Flow Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.e94x0pqay5sq), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** The link opens directly on the 90 Day Winback Flow tab. Use that as your starting structure instead of building the sequence from scratch. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Write A Simple Explanation Of The Company.** This becomes Day 1's reintroduction. A lot of people who took the call and didn't buy just weren't ready at the time. Don't assume they remember details about you, a lot can change for a prospect in 90 days.
3. **Write A Simple Introduction To Yourself.** This runs on Day 2. Use your name and the company name together, the way you would if you were reintroducing yourself to someone you met once three months ago.
4. **State In The First Three Emails That It Has Been 90 Days Since The Original Sales Call.** Say it directly in the opening line. Without it the prospect has no idea what you're following up on, and the reintroduction reads like cold outreach instead of a continuation.
5. **Write The Offer And Hard Pitch.** This runs on Day 3, and it's the one Email in this sequence allowed to directly push the offer with a CTA.
6. **Choose Three Case Studies Not Used In The 180 Day Or 365 Day Winback Flows.** These fill the three Monday slots spread across the campaign's three weeks. A prospect could receive all three Flows over time, so repeating proof makes the company look like it has nothing new to say.
7. **Choose Three Insights Not Used In The 180 Day Or 365 Day Winback Flows.** Same reasoning as the case studies, these fill the three Thursday slots alongside them.
8. **Confirm Every Case Study And Insight Is New Information To The Prospect.** Check it against what they already received in the Pre-Call and Post-Call Follow-Up Flows, not just the other Winback Flows. The whole premise of this Flow is that something has changed since you last spoke.
9. **Keep A Record Of Which Case Studies And Insights You Used In This Flow.** You'll need it when you build the 180 Day and 365 Day Winback Flows, since nothing can repeat across the three.
10. **Use The One-Pager From [Step #7: Create One-Pager](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=47264b77776f422e889dcffc1b21198c).** Make sure the Next Step on it is your call-booking link, since that's how they book from these Emails.
11. **Use The Loom From [Step #8: Create 3-Min Loom + Message](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=f7792b2c54204b4e9edaafc958d784c4).** This is the three-minute Loom where you walk through your One-Pager.
12. **Add The One-Pager And Loom Links To Every Email.** Every single Email in the sequence needs both links, not just the opening or closing ones.
13. **Set The Flow To Trigger 90 Days After Their Original Sales Call.** It only fires if they never became a customer. Your sales reps won't consistently follow up 90 days later, even with a sales manager, so this needs to run automatically. It won't win back most people, but it gets you a few percent more customers for almost no extra work.
14. **Set The Flow To Remove Someone Immediately If They Become A Customer.** Don't let them keep receiving Winback Emails after they've already purchased.
15. **Set The Flow To End After 30 Days.** Don't let the sequence continue past the ninth Email if they haven't responded.
16. **Test The Flow End To End.** Run it against yourself and check that it triggers 90 days after the original Sales Call, the Emails send on the correct day and week schedule, the first three Emails mention the 90 days and reintroduce you and the company, no case study or insight overlaps with the 180 Day Or 365 Day Winback Flows, becoming a customer removes you straight away, and the Flow ends after 30 days. Check the One-Pager and Loom links across all 9 Emails before launching.

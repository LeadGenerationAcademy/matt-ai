# Step #9A: No Show Flow

Video: this lesson has a video in Skool (about 3:22). Transcript is at the bottom of this file.

A percentage of prospects will not show up for their scheduled Sales Call.

These people normally fall through the cracks because nobody consistently follows up with them.

The No Show Flow automates that follow-up and gives them another opportunity to book.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Trigger:** Someone is marked as a no-show inside the CRM, or the call recording comes in under five minutes.
2. **Schedule (24 Emails Over 8 Weeks):**
  1. Week 1: One Email per day (Emails 1-7)
  2. Week 2: One Email per day (Emails 8-14)
  3. Week 3: Three Emails (Emails 15-17)
  4. Week 4: Three Emails (Emails 18-20)
  5. Week 5: One Email (Email 21)
  6. Week 6: One Email (Email 22)
  7. Week 7: One Email (Email 23)
  8. Week 8: One Email (Email 24)
3. **Each Email Must:**
  1. Answer one FAQ or objection
  2. Include concrete proof
  3. Stay between 300 and 1,000 words
  4. Reference the missed call near the beginning
  5. Reference the missed call again when asking them to rebook
  6. Change the beginning, ending, and sign-off from the previous Email
  7. Include a clear CTA and link to book another call
4. **One Connected Series:** Treat all 24 Emails as one connected series, and keep the language serious and professional, don't shame them for missing the call.
5. **Rebooking Rule:** If they book another call, remove them from this Flow and place them into the Pre-Call Flow.
6. **End Condition:** If they don't book, the Flow ends after eight weeks.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [No Show Flow Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.phzfjbugubw4), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Navigate to the No Show Flow tab inside it and use that as your starting structure instead of building the sequence from scratch. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Choose The 24 Most Important FAQs And Objections From Your AI Company Context Document And Research.** It's best to write this manually, but if you don't want to, [Step #9B](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=f0fed1e529634d6aadad9e361d563e2d) has an AI prompt that can get you 80-90% of the way there. This Flow matters, but it isn't as foundational as your Welcome or Pre-Call Flows, so you don't need to put as much effort into it.
3. **Arrange Them By Importance.** Put the questions and proof most likely to restore their interest first. You don't know why they missed the call. They may have forgotten, or they may have been unsure about the offer.
4. **Reuse The Strongest Information From The Pre-Call Flow.** You already did the work identifying your best FAQs, proof, and explanations there, don't start from zero here.
5. **Present It Differently For Someone Who Already Booked But Did Not Attend.** This person already showed real intent by booking once, so the framing should acknowledge that instead of treating them like a brand-new lead. Before the call, you were preparing them to attend. Now you're giving them a reason to come back to the conversation.
6. **Write Each Email Around One FAQ Or Objection.** Keep each one between 300 and 1,000 words. Remind them what they wanted help with, answer the question, and explain why speaking with you could still be useful.
7. **Include Concrete Proof With Every Answer.** Use a real testimonial, result, or case study for each FAQ, not just an explanation on its own.
8. **Reference The Missed Call Near The Beginning Of Each Email.** For example, "Hey [First Name], [Your Name] here from [Company]. We had a call scheduled recently but were not able to connect." Keep it to a line, since it only gives context for why you're emailing, and never shame them for missing the call. Don't repeat the same opening in consecutive Emails.
9. **End Every Email By Referencing The Missed Call Again And Linking To Rebook.** For example, "Since we did not get to connect, you can choose another time here: [Insert Link]." Every single Email needs its own path back to booking. Don't repeat the same closing in consecutive Emails either.
10. **Vary The Beginning, Ending, And Sign-Off Across The Series.** Rotate through several different openings and closings so no two consecutive Emails read the same way.
11. **Write A Subject Line And Preview Text For Every Email.** These need to give them a clear reason to open the email and come back to the conversation.
12. **Schedule One Email Per Day For The First Two Weeks.** This covers Emails 1 through 14, the most aggressive stretch of the sequence while their intent is still freshest.
13. **Schedule Three Emails Per Week For Weeks 3 And 4.** This covers Emails 15 through 20, tapering the frequency down from daily.
14. **Schedule One Email Per Week For Weeks 5 Through 8.** This covers Emails 21 through 24, the slowest stretch, for prospects who need more time before they're ready to rebook.
15. **Set The Flow To Trigger When Someone Is Marked As A No-Show Or Their Call Recording Comes In Under 10 Minutes.** A recording under 10 minutes usually means your closer joined, they never showed up, and your closer left. You can change the 10 minutes to whatever suits you, but it's better if this triggers automatically, since these are the people who fall through the cracks. Make sure someone who moved, canceled, or already had the conversation another way never gets this Flow.
16. **Set The Flow To Remove Someone The Moment They Rebook A Call.** They move into the Pre-Call Flow immediately once they rebook, they should never receive a No Show Email after that.
17. **Have Someone Handle Every Reply.** If they buy or ask you to stop, take them out of the Flow.
18. **Set The Flow To End After Eight Weeks If They Don't Rebook.** Don't let the sequence continue past the 24th Email. After that, they go back into your marketing, like your newsletter, retargeting ads, and content.
19. **Test The Flow End To End.** Mark yourself as a no-show and check that the Flow triggers from both a CRM no-show mark and a recording under 10 minutes, the Emails send on the daily, then three-times-weekly, then weekly schedule, no two Emails in a row start or end the same way, rebooking removes you straight away, and the Flow ends after eight weeks. Check the rebooking link across all 24 Emails before launching.

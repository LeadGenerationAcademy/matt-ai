The AI Company Context Document is for you to fill out once, and then give to AI, like ChatGPT and Claude, so it fully understands who you are, what you do, and who you're targeting.

This makes sure AI writes copy for your ads, landing pages, and email flows that's actually true to your business and sounds like it's coming from you.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Ideal Customer Profile:** Define your ICP using fields like gender, age range, marital status, kids, homeowner status, household income and net worth, job title, tech stack, industry, revenue range, and employee count, so AI can write copy that resonates with your ICP.
2. **Offer:** Define your offer clearly and with detail, including your offer, benefits, deliverables, timeline and process, guarantee, and call to action type, so AI doesn't write an offer that isn't real.
3. **Proof:** Write the proof of results you've gotten for past clients, up to 10 entries with client name and result, that AI can use when writing copy.
4. **Likes & Dislikes:** Write the likes and dislikes of your ICP, found through your research, so AI can write messaging better aligned to your ICP.
5. **Language:** Write the language your ICP uses, found through your research, so AI can write messaging better aligned to your ICP.
6. **Insights:** Write useful insights of any category related to your business that you'd want AI to know and potentially write about.
7. **Top FAQs:** List the top 10 most frequently asked questions or objections framed as questions, along with your response to each, so AI can write answers in your tone.
8. **Case Studies:** Write up to 10 case studies you've done on past clients, each with a headline and full text, that AI can use when writing copy.
9. **Testimonials:** Write up to 10 testimonials you've gotten from past clients, each with a headline and full text, that AI can use when writing copy.
10. **Source Material:** Copy and paste a transcript of you talking, from a YouTube video, podcast, or multiple short-form videos if you don't have a long-form one, aiming for 2000-4000 words, so AI can write copy that actually sounds like you.
11. **Tech Stack:** List up to 10 tools your ICP uses, with tool name, type, link, and notes, so AI can write copy that resonates more with your ICP.
12. **Problems, Circumstances, Outcomes:** List the top 25 problems, circumstances, and outcomes of your ICP, found through your research, since these make up the majority of how aligned the AI's copy will be to your ICP.
13. **Top 25 Ad Angles:** Take your top 25 problems, circumstances, or outcomes, rank them from most mentioned to least, and use these as your top 25 angles to test first.
14. **Admin:** List additional context AI can use whenever needed, including images, company information, links, and style details like fonts and colors.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [AI Company Context Document Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.g22gidt8kpvl).** This is a one-time document; once it's filled out, you'll reuse the same copy for every future AI chat rather than remaking it per project. Put real effort into it. The better this document is, the better every email flow comes out and the less time you'll spend editing them. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Finish Your ICP Research.** Have this ready before you fill anything in, since it covers 12 distinct fields plus additional notes, things like age range, household income, net worth, job title, and industry. If you already did the ICP exercise, you can copy and paste your answers straight in.
3. **Finish Writing Out Your Offer.** Have your offer, benefits, deliverables, timeline and process, guarantee, and call to action type all written out ahead of time so you're not drafting them inside the document itself.
4. **Pull Up Your Past Proof.** Gather real client results and metrics related to your offer; the document has room for up to 10 entries.
5. **Pull Up Your Case Studies.** Gather any case studies you've written on past clients, up to 10. These are one of the most important parts of the document, since AI uses them as proof when it writes your copy.
6. **Pull Up Your Testimonials.** Gather real testimonials from past clients, up to 10. These are just as important as your case studies.
7. **Fill Out The Ideal Customer Profile Section.** Use your research to complete each field, and put a dash for any field that's irrelevant to your business instead of leaving it blank. If you sell B2B, you'll mostly use gender, age range, job title, revenue, tech stack, and industry. If you sell B2C, marital status, kids, homeowner status, household income, and net worth matter more.
8. **Fill Out The Offer Section.** Use what you wrote out above so AI has an accurate, specific offer to reference instead of inventing one.
9. **Fill Out The Proof Section.** For each entry, include the client's name and the specific result. Write these out yourself so every name and number is exactly right.
10. **Fill Out The Likes & Dislikes Section.** List up to 10 likes and 10 dislikes of your ICP found through your research, so AI can write messaging better aligned to them.
11. **Fill Out The Language Section.** List up to 10 phrases or terms your ICP actually uses, found through your research. Include their acronyms, their lingo, and the different ways they describe their situation.
12. **Fill Out The Insights Section.** List up to 10 useful insights about your business or industry that you'd want AI to know and potentially write about. These should be the things you'd want someone to know before they buy from you.
13. **Fill Out The Top FAQs Section.** List your top 10 most frequently asked questions or objections, framed as questions, along with your response to each. You'll expand these to 30 in the next step.
14. **Fill Out The Case Studies Section.** For each one, include a headline and the full text. Paste the whole case study so AI has the real details to pull from.
15. **Fill Out The Testimonials Section.** For each one, include a headline and the full text. Paste the whole testimonial, the same way you did with your case studies.
16. **Fill Out The Source Material Section.** Paste a transcript of you talking, whether a long-form video, podcast, or multiple short-form videos, aiming for 2000-4000 words so AI can sound like you. Paste it raw, exactly how the transcript comes out, since that's what makes the writing sound like you.
17. **Fill Out The Tech Stack Section.** List up to 10 tools your ICP uses, with tool name, type, link, and notes for each. This only matters about 20% of the time, so if your ICP doesn't use a common set of tools, you can leave it.
18. **Fill Out The Problems, Circumstances, Outcomes Section.** List your ICP's top 25 problems, top 25 circumstances, and top 25 outcomes found through your research; this section makes up the majority of how aligned the AI's copy will be.
19. **Rank Your Top 25 Problems, Circumstances, And Outcomes To Create Your Top 25 Ad Angles.** Rank them from most mentioned to least in your research, and use that ranked list as the angles you test first.
20. **Fill Out The Admin Section.** Include your company logo and founder photo, company name and founder info, key links like your booking page and lead magnet, and your brand style details like fonts and colors.
21. **Download The Completed Document.** Save it somewhere you can quickly attach it from, since you'll be using it repeatedly.
22. **Attach The Document To Every New AI Chat When Writing Copy.** This is what keeps the AI grounded in real information about your business instead of inventing facts, stories, or claims.

Most people won't book a call or buy the first time they enter your Funnel. The Welcome Flow brings them back, introduces what you do, builds trust, answers their questions, and repeatedly gives them a chance to take the next step.

### Framework & Criteria

- Four emails in first three hours
  - Delivery email immediately
  - What we do (1 hour)
  - How we do it (2 hours)
  - Hard pitch + CTA (3 hours)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Welcome Flow Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.2nlb9lkvfm18), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Navigate to the Welcome Flow tab inside it and use that as your starting structure instead of building the sequence from scratch. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Pull Your Top 30 FAQs From [Step #1B: Think Of Your Top 30 FAQs](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=04cebeffa75f415797f223580e3a67a2).** You already ranked them and wrote bullet-point answers under each one. Keep them in order of importance, since this becomes the order you answer them across the 30 days.
3. **Find Concrete Proof For Each Answer.** Use a real testimonial, screenshot, or case study wherever possible, since several of the 30 days are built specifically around proof and case studies rather than just an explanation.
4. **Use The Lead Magnet Delivery Email From [Step #3: Lead Magnet Delivery Email](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=e7de308e5f8c4736956a330eb1f91c4c).** If they came in through your Opt-In Page, this goes out immediately, before the four emails below. It's what earns the initial open, click, and reply that the rest of the Welcome Flow builds on.
5. **Write The Hour #1 Email: What We Do.** Explain who you help, the primary problem you solve, and the outcome you help them achieve. The four emails in the first four hours matter the most. People are never more interested than the second they opt in or apply, and it drops off fast from there.
6. **Write The Hour #2 Email: How It Works.** Explain how your Offer works, what they receive, why the process works, and how quickly they receive value.
7. **Write The Hour #3 Email: Proof.** Share your strongest customer result, testimonial, case study, or screenshot, ideally from someone matching the same level of ICP you're targeting.
8. **Write The Hour #4 Email: Call To Action.** Explain why they should take the next step and directly ask them to book a call. These first four emails usually get 50-70% open rates with very few unsubscribes, because you're explaining the exact thing they just signed up for.
9. **Write All 30 Daily FAQ Emails.** Answer one FAQ or objection per email, in the order you ranked them. Each person usually only has one to three objections stopping them, so answering a different one every day means you eventually hit theirs. Mix in personal stories, customer stories, case studies, "How to Do X" content, and common mistakes so it doesn't feel like the same Email rewritten every day, and give every Email a link to book a call. It's best to write these manually, but if you don't want to, [Step #5B](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=023d02c788bf4cdc85b1dbace2765943) has an AI prompt that can get you 80-90% of the way there.
10. **Write Your Weekly Welcome SMS Messages (Optional).** If you send them, schedule them for Days 3, 10, 17, and 24, and never more than once a week. Point each one to that week's most important Email, or link your best YouTube video or case study. Keep every SMS short and direct.
11. **Set The Trigger To Begin On Any Opt-In Or An Application Without A Booked Call, Delayed By Five Minutes.** Any opt-in counts, anything gated where they have to submit their email. The five-minute delay gives them a chance to book a call first and enter the Pre-Call Flow instead, since someone who already booked should never enter this Flow.
12. **Set The Suppression Rule To Stop The Flow The Moment Someone Books A Call.** They should never resume the Welcome Flow later. After booking, they move through the Pre-Call Flow, then the No-Show Flow if applicable, then Post-Call Follow-Up if they attend but don't buy, or Customer Onboarding if they purchase. Never run the Welcome Flow, Pre-Call Flow, and Post-Call Follow-Up at the same time.
13. **Add The Flow To Your Email Platform.** Build out the full sequence, Emails, SMS, triggers, and suppression rule, inside the platform you chose in Step #0A.
14. **Test The Flow As A Lead Magnet Opt-In, A Different Opt-In, An Applicant Who Doesn't Book, And An Applicant Who Books Immediately.** For each one, check that the Lead Magnet Delivery Email sends immediately, the four Welcome Emails send in the correct order, the 30 daily Emails begin afterward, SMS never sends more than once a week, every Call to Action works, and booking a call permanently stops the Flow. Don't launch until every trigger and suppression rule works correctly.

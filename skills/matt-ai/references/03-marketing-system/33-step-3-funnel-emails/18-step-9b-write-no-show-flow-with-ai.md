### AI Prompt:

Using only the AI Company Context Document below, write the complete 24-email No-Show Flow.

This flow is for prospects who booked a sales call but did not attend.

Use this schedule:

- Emails 1–14: One email per day for two weeks
- Emails 15–20: Three emails per week for two weeks
- Emails 21–24: One email per week for four weeks

For each email:

- Stick to the AI Company Context Document 100%.
- Do not invent, assume, or add information.
- Preserve the author’s tone.
- Write between 300 and 1,000 words.
- Answer one FAQ or objection.
- Include concrete proof.
- Reference the missed scheduled call near the beginning.
- Reference it again when asking them to rebook.
- Change the beginning, ending, and sign-off throughout the series.
- Never use the same beginning or ending in consecutive emails.
- Keep the language serious, professional, and nonjudgmental.
- Give them useful information instead of merely reminding them that they missed the call.
- Treat all 24 emails as one connected series.
- Make every email understandable if they missed the previous emails.
- End every email with a clear CTA and link to book another call.
- Follow the Global Copywriting Rules.
- If essential information is missing, ask for it instead of inventing it.

For each email, provide:

1. Email number
2. Send time
3. FAQ or objection
4. Subject line
5. Preview text
6. Finished email

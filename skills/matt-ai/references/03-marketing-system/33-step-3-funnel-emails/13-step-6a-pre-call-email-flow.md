The entire point of the Pre-Call Email Flow is to force-feed the prospect useful information before the Sales Call. You want them walking into that call already educated, already trusting you, and already past most of their objections.

### Framework & Criteria

- Find top 18 FAQs
- Write a value-dense email explaining each of them
- Send the emails in order of importance (if the call is booked sooner, they won't see all the emails)
- Each email should have a link to an asset
- Send these every four hours before the call (up to 18 across 72 hours)
- Take them out of this flow when the call happens (automation)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Pre-Call Email Flow Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.mw70bfbp9vk1#heading=h.43cx1wwcrzhw), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Navigate to the Pre-Call Flow tab inside it and use that as your starting structure instead of building the sequence from scratch. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Pick Your Top 18 FAQs And Objections From [Step #1B: Think Of Your Top 30 FAQs](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=04cebeffa75f415797f223580e3a67a2).** Use the actual language your prospects use whenever possible, since this becomes your subject lines later.
3. **Rank Them From Most To Least Important.** People who book closer to the call may only receive the first few Emails, so the most important questions need to go first. Someone who books 16 hours out only sees four Emails, so those four need to be your biggest and best.
4. **Assign One FAQ Or Objection Per Email, Up To 18.** Each Email should tackle exactly one question, never combine multiple FAQs into a single Email.
5. **Write Each Subject Line As The Question Itself.** Put it in quotation marks, like "How Long Will It Take Before I See Results?" using the exact language your prospects use.
6. **Reference The Upcoming Call In The First Line Of Every Email.** For example, "Hey, it is Matt. We have a call coming up, so I wanted to X, Y and Z." This keeps every Email tied back to why they're receiving it.
7. **Write The Introduction For Each Email.** Introduce the question and explain why it matters, then move directly into the problem without lingering.
8. **Introduce The Problem In Each Email.** Explain why it happens, why it matters, what it costs them, why previous attempts may have failed, and what happens if they keep ignoring it.
9. **Introduce How To Solve It In Each Email.** Give them the theory, framework, or process required to understand the solution, without turning it into a vague pitch.
10. **Show An Example In Each Email.** Use a theoretical example, numerical example, personal example, real customer example, case study, or before-and-after comparison, ideally matching the same level of ICP.
11. **Add A Step By Step Checklist In Each Email.** Make each Checklist specific to that Email's FAQ, don't reuse the same generic Checklist across all 18.
12. **Add A Sign Off To Each Email.** Keep it simple, just your name.
13. **Add A P.S. Linking To A One-Pager And A Loom Video.** Use the same one-pager and three-minute Loom you'll also use inside your Confirmation Email and SMS from Step #7. Since these send every four hours, you don't need the same link every time. You can also link your best YouTube video, your lead magnet, or an activation point, a five-minute task they can do before the call to get a result.
14. **Write At Least 500 Words Per Email.** Every paragraph should help the prospect understand the problem, solution, example, or Action Steps, don't pad it just to hit the word count. These are not reminder emails. If they don't learn something from an email, it isn't good enough.
15. **Set The Flow To Send One Email Every Four Hours After Booking.** The first one goes out four hours after booking, after your Confirmation Email and SMS from Step #7. Someone who books 8 hours out might only get 2 Emails, while someone who books a full 72 hours out gets all 18. These usually get 60-80% open rates, because you're giving them the information they asked for by booking a call.
16. **Set The Flow To Stop Permanently The Moment The Call Begins, Or Immediately If They Unsubscribe.** Only send an Email if the scheduled Sales Call hasn't started yet; if the call begins before the next Email is due, don't send it.
17. **Test The Flow End To End.** Book a call yourself at a few different lead times to confirm the cadence and total Email count adjust correctly.
18. **Confirm Every Link Works Before Launching.** Check every One-Pager link and Loom link across all 18 Emails, not just the first one.

You should collect a phone number when someone requests your Lead Magnet, and send the Lead Magnet through SMS in addition to Email. The SMS gives them a second way to access it and makes it substantially more likely that they actually read what they requested.

### Framework

1. **Phone Number And Consent:** Add a Phone Number field with SMS consent language to the Opt-In Form.
2. **Automatic Send:** Send the Delivery SMS automatically immediately after Form submission, not manually.
3. **Reliable Link:** Include the Lead Magnet link using a reliable Link Shortener that doesn't get blocked by mobile carriers.
4. **Mention The Email:** Mention that the Lead Magnet was also sent by Email.
5. **Keep It Short:** Keep the SMS to their first name, the Lead Magnet name, the link, and the Email note.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Lead Magnet Delivery SMS Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.n0exw3ej9tow), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Navigate to the Lead Magnet Delivery SMS tab inside it and use that as your starting structure instead of a blank message. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Add A Phone Number Field To The Opt-In Form.** This needs to be collected at the same time as their Lead Magnet request, not through a separate form or step afterward.
3. **Add SMS Consent Language To The Form.** Keep the compliance explanation short, but make sure it's actually present, since it protects you legally and supports your A2P 10DLC requirements.
4. **Confirm Your System Records Phone Number, Consent, Date And Time, And Form.** All four pieces need to be captured together so you have a complete compliance record, not just the phone number by itself.
5. **Set Up STOP And Opt-Out Handling.** Make sure STOP instructions, opt-outs, and A2P 10DLC requirements are all handled properly before you send a single automated message.
6. **Write The Delivery SMS.** Keep it to their first name, the Lead Magnet name, the Lead Magnet link, and a note that it was also sent by Email.
7. **Mention That You Also Sent It By Email.** This encourages them to look for that email, including in their spam folder, and can push your email open rate toward 80 to 95%. Don't ask them to reply "YES" through SMS, the Reply Trick belongs inside the Email only.
8. **Add The Lead Magnet Link Using A Reliable Link Shortener.** Use one that doesn't get blocked by mobile carriers; a branded short link isn't required, but test it before launch to confirm it opens correctly on mobile, goes directly to the Lead Magnet, loads quickly, and avoids unnecessary redirects. Check your deliverability in your SMS tool too. If it's poor with the link in, take the link out and just tell them to look for your email.
9. **Add The SMS To Your Lead Magnet Delivery Automation.** It should send automatically immediately after the Form is submitted, don't depend on someone from your team manually sending it, since the whole point is reaching them while their interest is still high.
10. **Test By Submitting The Opt-In Form With Your Own Phone Number.** Check that the SMS sends immediately, your first name populates correctly, the correct Lead Magnet is delivered, the shortened link works, and the opt-out process works.
11. **Confirm The SMS And Email Both Arrive.** Don't launch this automation until both channels are confirmed working correctly, since the whole point of adding SMS was to give them a second reliable way to receive it.

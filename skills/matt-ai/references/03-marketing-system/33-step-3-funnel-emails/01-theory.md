Email is arguably the most important part of your Funnel.

Almost nobody converts or does what you want them to do the first time.

People visit your page without applying.

They apply without booking.

They book without showing up.

They attend the call without buying.

Email allows you to bring these people back and continue the conversation instead of permanently losing them.

### Why Email Is So Powerful

Email connects the different stages of your Funnel. It moves people from:

- Lead Magnet to Sales Call
- Application to Call Booked
- Call Booked to Call Shown
- No-Show to Reactivated
- Sales Call to Closed Client

Email is also one of the only platforms where you can communicate with your entire audience at once.

Social media platforms normally show your content to a small percentage of your followers. They can also ban your account, remove your reach, or change the algorithm whenever they want. You own your email list.

Email is also one of the easiest and most convenient places to use direct Calls to Action. You can tell people exactly what to read, watch, download, reply to, or book next.

### Every Flow Has a Purpose

These are not random emails sent because someone told you that email marketing is important.

Every Flow exists to solve a specific leak inside the Funnel.

Together, they bring people back, move them toward the next step, increase Show Rate, increase Close Rate, and recover opportunities that would otherwise disappear.

### Platform Setup

Platform Setup connects your Email Platform, your SMS Platform, and your CRM into one system before you build any Flow.

The CRM becomes the record of what every person has done: requested a Lead Magnet, started an Application, booked a call, missed a call, bought, or did not buy.

Only one primary Flow should ever control what happens next for a given person. A booked call should permanently stop the Welcome Flow and start the Pre-Call Flow.

Submit every Form yourself before building anything else, and confirm the contact appears in the CRM, the correct Flow begins, and booking a call stops the Welcome Flow.

### Email/Domain Setup

Your Funnel Emails should be sent using your company domain. Your newsletter is closer to regular content and should be sent using your personal domain.

Create a dedicated sending subdomain and configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC before sending automated Emails. Send from a real address that can receive replies.

Start by sending only to people actively entering your Funnel, and let volume increase from there.

### Email Platforms

You can use whichever email platform works for your business.

If you want something inexpensive and simple, use Kit.

If you can afford HubSpot or already use it as your CRM, use HubSpot.

The specific platform matters substantially less than correctly building the Flows and connecting them to the rest of your Funnel.

### SMS Is Part of the System

For this Course, we will consider SMS part of the Email System. SMS should accompany every major Flow, but it should be used substantially less frequently than email.

You will need a business SMS number to take full advantage of this. Some platforms have basic SMS reminders built in. For example, Calendly can handle certain appointment reminders. Other systems will require your own registered A2P 10DLC number.

Begin that registration process as soon as possible because approval can take time.

### Use SMS at a Lower Frequency

SMS should support each Flow, but it should not match the Email frequency. Use SMS for the most important moments:

- Delivering something they requested
- Confirming an Application or booking
- Reminding them about an upcoming call
- Reactivating a No-Show
- Following up after a Sales Call
- Asking for a direct response

Email can provide the full explanation. SMS should normally be shorter, more direct, and less frequent.

### AI Company Context Document

The AI Company Context Document is a document you fill out once and give to AI so it understands your business, your offer, and who you are targeting.

It makes sure AI writes copy for your ads, landing pages, and email Flows that is accurate to your business and sounds like you.

It includes your Ideal Customer Profile, your Offer, your Proof, your Likes and Dislikes, and a real transcript of you talking.

Attach the completed document to every new AI chat when writing copy.

### Global Writing Rules For Projects

The Global Writing Rules apply to every type of copy you write, including ad copy, landing page copy, and email Flow copy. Paste them into every existing AI Project and every new Project you create going forward.

Every Flow in this Course also has a corresponding "B" step containing an AI prompt you can use, alongside your completed AI Company Context Document, to write that Flow for you.

It is best to write your Flows manually. If you do not want to, the AI prompt can get you 80-90% of the way there.

### Lead Magnet Delivery and Welcome Flow

The Lead Magnet Delivery Email gives the person what they requested. The Welcome Flow then introduces you, builds trust, delivers value, and repeatedly gives them an opportunity to take the next step. The Welcome Flow should send:

- Four emails during the first four hours
- One email per day for the following 30 days

This frequency is intentionally aggressive. They just requested something from you, which means their interest is currently at its highest point.

### Pre-Call Flow

The Pre-Call Flow begins after someone books and continues until the Sales Call takes place. Its purpose is to:

- Increase Show Rate
- Build trust
- Present proof
- Answer objections
- Explain what will happen on the call
- Make the prospect more prepared to buy

Send one email approximately every four hours, with a maximum of 18 total emails. The number they actually receive depends on how much time exists between booking and the Sales Call. Someone who books a call for tomorrow will receive fewer emails than someone who books a call for next week.

### Pre-Call Email & SMS Confirmation

Pre-Call Email & SMS Confirmation sends immediately after someone books a Sales Call. It confirms the call date, time, Time Zone, and Closer's name, and includes a link to the One-Pager and Loom video.

A separate Closer Notification sends at the same time, with the prospect's name, company, website, contact information, call time, and a link to their Application or CRM record.

Both notifications should fire immediately after the call is booked, not on a delay.

### Pre-Call SMS & Email Reminders

Pre-Call SMS & Email Reminders use the built-in reminder system inside Calendly or your Scheduling Platform. Reminders send at 24 hours, 4 hours, and 1 hour before the call, by both Email and SMS.

Each reminder includes the prospect's first name, the Closer's name, the call date, time, and Time Zone. No meeting link is included, since the prospect already has that in their Calendar invitation.

Reminders update automatically on reschedule and stop automatically on cancellation.

### No-Show Flow

The No-Show Flow reactivates people who booked a call but did not attend. These people already applied, selected a time, and demonstrated meaningful intent. Do not permanently throw away the opportunity because they missed one appointment. Use email and SMS to get them to reschedule.

### Short-Term Post-Call Follow-Up

The Short-Term Post-Call Follow-Up Flow is for qualified prospects who attended the call but did not buy. It should contain 18 emails across approximately eight weeks:

- One email per day for the first three days
- Three emails during the following week
- Two emails per week for the following six weeks

This Flow should continue:

- Answering objections
- Presenting proof
- Clarifying the Offer
- Explaining the cost of doing nothing
- Giving them opportunities to reply
- Asking them to take the next step

We will use a separate Framework to create the actual emails.

### Long-Term Post-Call Follow-Up

Some qualified prospects will not be ready during the Short-Term Follow-Up period. That does not mean they will never become ready. After the Short-Term Flow ends, recycle them into your regular Marketing. Then trigger another two-week Follow-Up sequence:

- At 90 days
- At 180 days
- At 365 days

Their circumstances, urgency, budget, or priorities may have changed. The opportunity is not dead simply because they did not buy during the original Sales Process.

### Application Abandonment Flow

The Application Abandonment Flow brings back people who begin the process but do not finish booking a call. These people showed clear intent. Do not simply allow them to disappear because they became distracted, changed tabs, or decided to finish later.

### Application Confirmation SMS

Application Confirmation SMS is sent manually by the appointment setter to someone who completed an Application but did not book a call.

It introduces the sender and company, acknowledges that the Application was received, and links to a One-Pager with the important information, a short Loom, and the booking link.

This message is meant to feel like it was written and sent by a real person, because it was.

### Application Appointment Setting Message

Application Appointment Setting Message is the automated version of the same message, sent to prospects who submitted an Application but did not book a call.

It introduces the sender and company, asks if they want to book a call, and links to the same One-Pager.

This one requires no manual effort once it is set up, so it reaches every applicant the manual message might miss.

### Call Book Confirmation SMS

Call Book Confirmation SMS is an official, automated message sent by the company the moment someone books a call. It confirms the call and links to the One-Pager with the important information and the Loom video.

Keep it short, since shorter automated messages perform better for deliverability.

### Call Booked Appointment Setting Message

Call Booked Appointment Setting Message is a message sent preferably by hand once someone books a call. It acknowledges the specific date and time they booked, and offers three options:

1. Take the call now
2. Keep the booked time
3. Go back and forth by SMS with any questions first.

Giving the prospect a choice like this increases how many of them actually connect with you before the call happens.

### Automations

Automations connects your CRM, Email tool, calendar, and SMS tool through Zapier or an equivalent platform. Every Flow in this module needs its own trigger and its own exit condition, so only one primary Flow ever controls what happens next.

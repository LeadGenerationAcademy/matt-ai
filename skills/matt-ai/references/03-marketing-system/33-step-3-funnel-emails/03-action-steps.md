# Action Steps

Course: Step #3: Funnel Emails
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=9e4b8575ebcd4e3a9c6c7bdd9384d467

---
The Theory explains why Email is the connective tissue of your funnel, bringing back the people who don't convert the first time and moving them from Lead Magnet to Sales Call, Application to Call Booked, Call Booked to Call Shown, No-Show to Reactivated, and Sales Call to Closed Client, with SMS supporting every major Flow at a lower frequency.

This module is where you actually build that entire system.

You will set up your Email and SMS platforms and sending domain, fill out your AI Company Context Document and Global Writing Rules, then write and automate the Lead Magnet Delivery, Welcome, Pre-Call, No-Show, Post-Call Follow-Up, and 90/180/365 Day Winback Flows, along with the confirmation and appointment-setting messages that connect them, and finally tie every Flow together through your automation platform.

Do not launch any Flow without testing its triggers and suppression rules, letting two Flows run on the same person at once, or forgetting that reused proof across your Winback Flows will make your follow-up look repetitive instead of genuinely new.

### Table of Contents

1. **Platform Setup:** Choose an Email and SMS platform, connect it to your CRM, and test the connection so it can automatically track and trigger the right Flow at each stage of your funnel.
2. **Email/Domain Setup:** Create a dedicated sending subdomain and configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC so your Funnel Emails are properly authenticated before you send any.
3. **AI Company Context Document:** Fill out one document covering your ICP, offer, proof, language, and research so AI can write copy that's accurate to your business and sounds like you.
4. **Global Writing Rules For Projects:** Paste the Global Writing Rules into every AI project so all your copy, ads, landing pages, and emails follow the same standards.
5. **Lead Magnet Delivery Email:** Write and automate the email that delivers your lead magnet, states its benefit, and asks for a reply to start the Deliverability Flywheel.
6. **Lead Magnet Delivery SMS:** Add a phone number field with SMS consent and automate a short text that delivers the lead magnet alongside the email.
7. **Welcome Flow:** Write the four first-hour emails, 30 daily FAQ emails, and weekly SMS messages that turn new opt-ins and incomplete applications into booked calls.
8. **Write Welcome Flow With AI:** Use the AI prompt with your Company Context Document to draft the Welcome Flow's emails if you'd rather not write them manually.
9. **Pre-Call Email Flow:** Write up to 18 emails, one every four hours after booking, each answering one FAQ or objection so the prospect arrives at the call already educated.
10. **Write Pre-Call Email Flow With AI:** Use the AI prompt with your Company Context Document to draft the Pre-Call Flow's emails if you'd rather not write them manually.
11. **Pre-Call Email & SMS Confirmation:** Automate a confirmation to the prospect with the call details and a separate notification to the closer so both sides show up prepared.
12. **Pre-Call SMS & Email Reminders:** Set reminders at 24 hours, 4 hours, and 1 hour before the call so the prospect actually shows up.
13. **No Show Flow:** Write a 24-email series over eight weeks that keeps giving no-show prospects useful information and another chance to rebook.
14. **Write No Show Flow With AI:** Use the AI prompt with your Company Context Document to draft the No Show Flow's emails if you'd rather not write them manually.
15. **Post-Call Follow Up Flow:** Write 18 short, personal follow-up emails across about eight weeks for qualified prospects who attended the call but didn't buy.
16. **Write Post-Call Follow Up Flow With AI:** Use the AI prompt with your Company Context Document to draft the Follow-Up Flow's emails if you'd rather not write them manually.
17. **90 Day Winback Flow:** Write a 9-email, 30-day campaign that reactivates prospects 90 days after their original call if they never became a customer.
18. **Write 90 Day Winback Flow With AI:** Use the AI prompt with your Company Context Document to draft the 90 Day Winback Flow's emails if you'd rather not write them manually.
19. **180 Day Winback Flow:** Write a 9-email, 30-day campaign using different proof than the 90 and 365 Day Winback Flows to reactivate prospects 180 days after their original call.
20. **Write 180 Day Winback Flow With AI:** Use the AI prompt with your Company Context Document to draft the 180 Day Winback Flow's emails if you'd rather not write them manually.
21. **365 Day Winback Flow:** Write a 9-email, 30-day campaign using different proof than the 90 and 180 Day Winback Flows to reactivate prospects 365 days after their original call.
22. **Write 365 Day Winback Flow With AI:** Use the AI prompt with your Company Context Document to draft the 365 Day Winback Flow's emails if you'd rather not write them manually.
23. **Application Confirmation SMS:** Build a One-Pager and have your appointment setter manually send applicants who didn't book a short message linking them to it.
24. **Application Appointment Setting Message:** Automate a short SMS to applicants who haven't booked, introducing yourself and linking to your One-Pager.
25. **Call Book Confirmation SMS:** Automate a short official message confirming the call and linking to your One-Pager the moment someone books.
26. **Call Booked Appointment Setting Message:** Send a manual message right after booking that gives the prospect three flexible ways to actually connect with you.
27. **Automations:** Connect Zapier to your CRM, email, calendar, and SMS tools so every Flow triggers, stops, and hands off correctly as a prospect moves through your funnel.

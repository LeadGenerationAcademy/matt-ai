Note: This would be manually sent whereas the previous call booked appointment setting confirmation message in automated.

Send this preferably manual message via SMS to prospects who have booked a call with you.

You can fill out this template within 30 seconds, no AI needed.

### Framework & Criteria

- Make sure you say your name and the company name in the text
- Make sure you reference the call they booked in the text
- 100% of the information should be in the text. No multi-step. No hiding your intentions
- Send text message with your name in it so it shows up Maybe: Matthew Larsen
- Call twice to get through many Do Not Disturb settings
- Try to get the call pushed up if possible so it happens sooner/now

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Call Booked Appointment Setting Message Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Use this copy to fill in your version instead of starting from a blank message. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Add Your Full Name And Company Name To The Template.** These stay the same every time you send it. Send it from a number saved under your name, so it shows up on their phone as something like "Matthew Larsen."
3. **Save The Template Somewhere Easy To Access.** You're sending this manually after every booking, so it needs to be quick to find, unlike the automated messages in Steps #14 and #16.
4. **Make Someone Responsible For Sending It Within Five Minutes Of Every Booking.** They've just been looking at your offer and deciding to speak with you. Whoever owns this needs a way to see new bookings the moment they come in.
5. **Add The Prospect's Name Before You Send It.** This is the one field that changes with every single booking.
6. **Change "I Can Phone You In (Insert Time)" To A Time You Can Actually Offer.** Something like 15 minutes from now, or whenever your schedule is free. The earlier time needs to be real, and someone needs to be ready to take that call.
7. **Keep All The Information In The First Text.** Your name, your company, the call they booked, and their three options all go in one message. Don't send a vague opener and wait for a reply before explaining who you are.
8. **Send The Message Manually.** It makes the prospect feel like a real person is already engaging with them before the call even happens.
9. **Call Them Twice In A Row.** Calling twice gets you through a lot of Do Not Disturb settings.
10. **Respond Based On Whichever Option The Prospect Chooses.** A lot of them will rather text, and a lot will take the call right now, which is better if you can do it. If they agree to an earlier call, update the appointment and make sure your team knows.
11. **Send Your Selfie Video With It Once You've Recorded It In [Step #3: Pre-Call Selfie Video](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/7c941b44?md=716352bf9173466e8944758007520976).** You'll record this in the Funnel Videos Course, which is the next course after this one. It puts a face to your name before the call.
12. **Submit A Test Booking And Confirm The Template Works.** Check that you get notified of the booking fast enough to send the message within five minutes, and that the name and phone-back time are filled in correctly. Don't launch until you can send this quickly after every booking.

# Step #10A: Post-Call Follow Up Flow

Course: Step #3: Funnel Emails
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=1664da623aa544a2b921c81d2c771598

---
Post-Call Follow-Up should feel fundamentally different from the Welcome and Pre-Call Flows. These Emails should not look or sound like Marketing Emails. They should look like the closer remembered the conversation, thought about what the prospect said, and personally followed up with something useful.

### Framework & Criteria

- **Day 1:** Biggest Case Study (Dollar Value)
- **Day 2:** Biggest Case Study (As A % Improvement)
- **Day 3:** Biggest Industry Insight
- **Following Monday:** Biggest FAQ 1
- **Following Wednesday:** Biggest FAQ 2
- **Following Friday:** Biggest FAQ 3
- **Monday:** Case Study
- **Thursday:** Industry Insight
- **Monday:** Case Study
- **Thursday:** Industry Insight

(Repeat Monday + Thursday For 8 More Weeks For 10 Total)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Post-Call Follow Up Flow Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.bnuz4agbvw94), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Navigate to the Follow Up Flow tab inside it and use that as your starting structure instead of building the sequence from scratch. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Send The Information And Next Step You Agreed On The Call Within Two Minutes.** This is separate from the email sequence below. If another conversation is needed, book it while you're still on the call.
3. **Choose Your Biggest Case Study By Dollar Value.** This opens the sequence on Day 1, so lead with your single strongest available proof.
4. **Choose Your Biggest Case Study By Percentage Improvement.** This runs on Day 2, use the most dramatic before-and-after result you have.
5. **Choose Your Biggest Industry Insight.** This runs on Day 3, something genuinely useful that showcases your expertise.
6. **Choose Your Three Most Important FAQs.** These fill the following Monday, Wednesday, and Friday slots right after the first three days.
7. **Choose Ten More Case Studies.** Starting the Monday after that, these fill the Monday slot of each of the next ten weeks.
8. **Choose Ten More Industry Insights.** These fill the Thursday slot of each of those same ten weeks, alongside the case studies.
9. **Create The Emails As CRM Templates Or Snippets.** Whenever possible, have the closer send these manually: select the correct template, add a reference to the Sales Call, personalize the example or insight, and send it from inside the CRM. If your Sales Team won't consistently do this manually, automate it as a Flow instead, since people forget to follow up far too often. Automated Follow-Up is better than Follow-Up that never happens.
10. **Reference The Sales Call Near The Beginning Of Each Email.** For example, "Hey [First Name], it's [Closer Name] following up from our call."
11. **Reference Something Specific The Prospect Mentioned In Each Email.** Their main problem, current circumstances, desired outcome, something they already tried, an objection, a deadline, or a question, so it reads like a real person following up from a real conversation.
12. **Include One Valuable Idea, Example, Or Piece Of Proof Per Email.** Keep these Emails substantially shorter than the Welcome and Pre-Call Emails, plain text, and written like something you'd actually type to one person, no giant walls of text or corporate-sounding language.
13. **Include One Simple Question Or Next Step Per Email.** Use something like "Does this look similar to what you are experiencing?" The goal is to restart the conversation, not push another sale directly.
14. **Set The Emails To Send From The Closer's Email Address.** Use your CRM's Contact Owner or Deal Owner to determine which closer sends each set of Emails; if your system can't send from each closer's address automatically, use a monitored company team Email as the fallback, never a no-reply, Newsletter, or unmonitored Marketing address.
15. **Set Replies To Route Directly To The Closer.** Every reply needs to reach someone who can actually continue the Sales Process.
16. **Set The Flow To Stop Immediately If The Prospect Replies, Books, Purchases, Or Asks To Stop.** Once they reply, the closer should take over manually, automation gets the conversation restarted, but a real person should continue it.
17. **Test The Flow End To End.** Run it against yourself and check that the Emails send on the correct day and week schedule, they send from the correct closer's address or the monitored fallback, replies reach the right closer, and the Flow stops immediately on a reply, booking, purchase, or opt-out request. Check the booking link across all 26 Emails before launching.

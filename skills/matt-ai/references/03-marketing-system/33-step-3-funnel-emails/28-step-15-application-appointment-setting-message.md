### Framework & Criteria

1. **Trigger:** Someone submits an application but does not book a call.
2. **Sender:** Sent automatically.
3. **Message Includes:**
  1. Introduce the sender and company
  2. Ask if they want to book a call
  3. Link to the Loom
  4. Link to the One-Pager

**Use This Template**

Hey, it is [Your Name] with [Company]. I am looking over your application now. Do you want to book a call so we can show you what we do?

Here is a short Loom and a one-pager with everything you need, including a link to book your call:

Loom: [Loom Link]

One-Pager: [One-Pager Link]

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Application Appointment-Setting Message Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.yd4lu12omo35), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Use this copy to fill in your version instead of starting from a blank message. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Add Your Name And Company Name To The Template.** These are the only two fields, which is what keeps this under 30 seconds to send.
3. **Add The Same One-Pager And Loom Links From [Step #14: Application Confirmation SMS](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=2661a24d0b3d4098bec71b0f287601d1).** Don't create new ones just for this message.
4. **Save The Template On The Appointment Setter's Phone.** This keeps it fast to send the moment they get notified, instead of hunting for the wording each time.
5. **Create A Notification In Zapier For Applications That Don't Book Within Five Minutes.** This is what tells your appointment setter that someone applied but didn't book. Don't rely on them checking manually.
6. **Send The Message Manually As Soon As You Get Notified.** The application gives you a chance to reach them while the offer is still on their mind. It should read like a short message the appointment setter typed themselves, not an automated notification.
7. **Respond To Whatever They Reply With.** Answer their question, explain the part of the offer they didn't understand, or help them find a time.
8. **Move The Prospect Into The Pre-Call Flow If They Book.** This is the handoff point once this message succeeds.
9. **Submit A Test Application And Confirm The Notification Works.** Submit one without booking and check your appointment setter gets notified after five minutes. Then submit another and book within five minutes, and check no notification goes out. Don't launch until the notification works every time.

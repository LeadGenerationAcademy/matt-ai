### Framework & Criteria

1. **Trigger:** Someone completes an application and does not book a call within five minutes.
2. **Sender:** Sent manually by the appointment setter, using the template.
3. **Message Includes:**
  1. Introduce the sender and company
  2. Acknowledge that the application was received
  3. Link to the Loom
  4. Link to the One-Pager
4. **One-Pager Includes:**
  1. Important information on everything they need to know
  2. The call-booking link

**Use This Template**

Hey [First Name], [Your Name] here from [Company]. I saw your application come through.

Here is a short Loom and a one-pager with everything you need, including a link to book your call:

Loom: [Loom Link]

One-Pager: [One-Pager Link]

Let me know if you have any questions.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Application Confirmation SMS Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.gdxru5bw5hxc), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Use this copy to write your version instead of starting from a blank message. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Add Your Name And Company Name To The Template.** These are the only two fields you fill in yourself. Their first name should pull in automatically from their application.
3. **Add The One-Pager From [Step #7: Create One-Pager](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=47264b77776f422e889dcffc1b21198c) To The Template.** This becomes the single link every appointment-setting and confirmation message in this course points to, so use the same One-Pager everywhere.
4. **Confirm The Next Step On Your One-Pager Is Your Call-Booking Link.** This is how they book straight from the message, so make sure it's easy to find on the page.
5. **Add The Loom From [Step #5: 3-Minute Information Loom](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/7c941b44?md=d1021fdff9e648979fb762d4ee8ac133) To The Template.** You'll record this in the Funnel Videos Course, which is the next course after this one. It walks them through your One-Pager in under three minutes, so add the link once it's recorded.
6. **Add The Message To Your SMS Tool.** Load the finished message into whatever platform will actually send it.
7. **Set The Message To Send Automatically Five Minutes After Someone Submits An Application.** Nobody on your team sends this one. The five minutes gives them a chance to book a call first.
8. **Set The Message To Skip Anyone Who Books Within Those Five Minutes.** They'll get your Call Book Confirmation SMS from Step #16 instead, so nobody receives both.
9. **Submit A Test Application And Confirm It Sends Correctly.** Submit one without booking and check the message arrives after five minutes with your name, your company, and a working One-Pager link. Then submit another and book within five minutes, and check this message doesn't send. Test the Loom link once you've added it in the Funnel Videos Course.

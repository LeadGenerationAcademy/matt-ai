### AI Prompt:

Using only the AI Company Context Document below, write the complete nine-email 90-Day Winback Flow.

This flow begins 90 days after a prospect completes a sales call but does not buy.

Use this schedule:

- Day 1: Reference that it has been 90 days and reintroduce the company
- Day 2: Reintroduce the sender
- Day 3: Present the offer with a hard pitch and CTA
- The nearest Monday: Case study
- Thursday: Insight
- Monday: Case study
- Thursday: Insight
- Monday: Case study
- Thursday: Insight

Every email must:

- Look like a short email manually written to one person.
- Be as simple as possible.
- Avoid marketing language, long explanations, and complicated formatting.
- Stick to the AI Company Context Document 100%.
- Never invent facts, proof, results, stories, or claims.
- Preserve the author’s tone.
- Communicate one main idea.
- Use plain English and short paragraphs.
- Change the introduction and sign-off throughout the series.
- Include the one-pager and Loom links from the document.
- Treat all nine emails as one connected series.
- Follow the Global Copywriting Rules.
- Ask for missing information instead of inventing it.

For each email, provide:

1. Email number
2. Send day
3. Purpose
4. Subject line
5. Preview text
6. Finished email

You need both an Email Platform and an SMS Platform before creating any of the Flows in this Course. The exact software matters less than whether it can send everything automatically, connect to your CRM, and stop one Flow when another one begins. ### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose Your Email Platform.** This is the foundation every Email Flow in this module runs on, so pick one before building anything else. I'd recommend [Kit.com](http://Kit.com) if you don't have one already, but any platform that meets the requirements below works. The easiest setup is sending both your email and your SMS through the CRM you already use, like HubSpot. Whatever you pick, deliverability matters more than the platform itself, and you'll set that up in the next step.
2. **Confirm Your Email Platform Supports Automated Flows, Triggers, Delays, Contact Lists Or Tags, And Flow Suppression.** These five features let you build sequences that fire based on someone's actions, wait the right amount of time between messages, segment contacts, and stop someone from receiving two conflicting Flows at once.
3. **Confirm Your Email Platform Supports Personalization, Reply Handling, Dedicated Sending Domains, Webhooks Or Integrations, And Performance Reporting.** Personalization and a monitored reply inbox make your emails feel one-to-one, a dedicated sending domain protects your deliverability, and webhooks or integrations are what let this platform actually talk to your CRM and calendar tools.
4. **Choose Your SMS Platform.** Every SMS Flow runs on this one, and it's separate software from your Email Platform. I'd recommend Roezan if you don't have one already. You need both email and SMS for the Flows in this course to work.
5. **Get A Business SMS Number.** This is separate from your personal number and is what all of your automated SMS messages will send from.
6. **Register For A2P 10DLC If Applicable.** This is the carrier registration process for sending business text messages in the US; skipping it can get your messages blocked or filtered by carriers. It's very easy to do inside Roezan.
7. **Add SMS Consent To Your Forms.** Every Form that collects a phone number needs explicit consent language so people are opted in to receive texts before you send any.
8. **Set Up STOP And Opt-Out Handling.** Anyone who replies STOP or opts out needs to be automatically removed from future SMS sends. Text STOP to your own number to confirm it works.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose your Email Platform?
- Does your Email Platform support Automated Flows, Triggers, Delays, Contact Lists Or Tags, And Flow Suppression?
- Does your Email Platform support Personalization, Reply Handling, Dedicated Sending Domains, Webhooks Or Integrations, And Performance Reporting?
- Did you choose your SMS Platform?
- Did you get a business SMS number?
- Did you register for A2P 10DLC if applicable?
- Did you add SMS consent to your Forms?
- Did you set up STOP and opt-out handling?

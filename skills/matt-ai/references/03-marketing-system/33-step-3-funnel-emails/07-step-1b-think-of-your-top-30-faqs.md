### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Top 30 FAQs Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.ykft664sm5qp).** This is where you will list all your questions and write the answers for them. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Pull Your Questions From The [Messaging Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=28f95017c8884266886d6d4e55ebd191) And Your AI Company Context Document.** If you do not have 30 between them, go back through your sales calls and your online research and find the rest.
3. **Cut It Down To Your Top 30.** Keep the ones you get asked most and drop the rest. An objection counts, just write it as a question first.
4. **Write Your Answer Under Each Question.** Put each question where the template says [Question], then bullet point your answer underneath. You are writing these now so you can either paste them into AI or use them yourself when you write your email flows.

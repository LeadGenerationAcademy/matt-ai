Your Lead Magnet Delivery Email needs to accomplish four things:

- Get delivered
- Get opened
- Get clicked
- Get a reply

You want them to read the Lead Magnet because it pushes them further into your Sales Process, and you want the reply because it creates a positive Deliverability Flywheel for your future emails.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Subject Line:** Use "[REPLY REQUIRED] Your Free [Lead Magnet Name]." This primes them to reply before they even open the email, and including the Lead Magnet name makes it immediately recognizable as exactly what they requested.
2. **Greeting:** Start the email with a greeting and introduce yourself and your company.
3. **Link And Benefit:** Put the Lead Magnet link directly inside the email and clearly state the primary benefit of downloading and using it. Do not make them search for the link.
4. **Reply Prompt:** Ask them to reply with something extremely simple, like Reply "YES" so I know you received it. Keep the entire email short so this is impossible to miss.
5. **Sign Off:** Sign off with "Thank You," followed by your name.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make A Copy Of The [Lead Magnet Delivery Email Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vUKmnyABEi6_vFubefRIL7BJ56gbxd_mYA4V8F-hfLo/edit?tab=t.0), Or Skip If You Already Made One For A Previous Step.** Navigate to the Lead Magnet Delivery Email tab inside it and use that as your starting structure instead of a blank email. This email is written from the template, not with AI. [in this skill: assets/templates/welcome-flow-template/]
2. **Write Your Subject Line With "[REPLY REQUIRED]" And Your Lead Magnet Name.** This primes them to reply before they even open the email, and naming the Lead Magnet makes the email immediately recognizable as exactly what they requested. Your open rate should be 70% or higher. If it's closer to 40 or 50%, try taking the word "Free" out, since it can trigger spam filters.
3. **Add Your Greeting, Your Name, And Your Company.** Keep it brief, and tell them they're getting this because they signed up on your website. That's just enough to make the email feel personal before getting straight to the Lead Magnet.
4. **State The Primary Benefit Of The Lead Magnet.** Tell them what they'll get out of it in one clear sentence rather than describing the Lead Magnet itself.
5. **Add The Lead Magnet Link.** Put it directly inside the email as an obvious link, so they don't have to search for it or scroll past unrelated content to find it.
6. **Add The Reply Prompt.** Ask them to reply with something extremely simple, like "Please reply YES to let me know you have received this. If you don't, I will email you again to confirm." Once someone replies, you're a trusted sender, so nearly every future email you send them lands in their inbox. Replies also tell Google and Microsoft to trust your domain, which helps your emails reach everyone else's inbox too.
7. **Sign Off With Your Name.** Use "Thanks," followed by your name.
8. **Keep The Whole Email As Bare Bones As The Example.** A long email makes them less likely to click the link, read the whole thing, or see that they should be replying.
9. **Add The Email To Your Lead Magnet Delivery Automation.** This should trigger automatically the moment someone submits your Opt-In Form, not depend on anyone manually sending it.
10. **Test By Submitting The Opt-In Form Yourself.** Go through the exact process a real prospect would use.
11. **Confirm You Received The Lead Magnet In Your Inbox.** Check that the email arrived, the link works, the benefit statement reads correctly, and the Reply Prompt is visible without scrolling.

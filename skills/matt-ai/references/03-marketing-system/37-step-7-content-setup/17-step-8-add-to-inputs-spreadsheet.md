### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Give your tracking person the links to everything you post.** This is usually the person you've hired to update your tracking. They need the links to every piece of content so they can count it and put it in.
2. **Open the Content tab of your [Input Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eT95b4OnSVAOaqrRJ5zKZrcBX0ul6EewACqs38u_Z20/edit?gid=945673274#gid=945673274).** This is where your publishing volume gets tracked alongside the rest of your inputs. [in this skill: assets/templates/input-tracking-template/]
3. **Fill in the Date.** You need to know exactly which day this content went out.
4. **Fill in the number posted in every platform column.** Newsletters Sent, Instagram Posts, Instagram Reels, Instagram Stories, Facebook Posts, Facebook Stories, LinkedIn Posts, Twitter Posts, YouTube Videos, and YouTube Community Posts each get their own count, taken from the links your tracking person has. Total Content Published adds these up on its own once the row is filled in.
5. **Fill in Notes.** This is where you capture anything worth remembering about that day's content.
6. **Have your tracking person put it in each week.** This is very, very important. Every piece of content you post needs to make it into this Sheet, or you lose the record of when you were actually posting and when you were not.

### Completion Audit

- Did you give your tracking person the links to everything you post?
- Did you open the Content tab of your Input Tracking Sheet?
- Did you fill in the Date?
- Did you fill in the number posted in every platform column?
- Did you fill in Notes?
- Is your tracking person putting it in each week?

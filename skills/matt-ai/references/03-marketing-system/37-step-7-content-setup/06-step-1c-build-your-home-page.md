In this step you will be building a simple home page for your newsletter using Beehiiv's native website builder.

You should be able to finish this home page within 10-15 minutes by just following the steps below.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to** **[Website](https://app.beehiiv.com/website_builder_v2), then Builder.** Website sits in the left navigation bar inside Beehiiv. Once you land on the Website tab, there will be a second navigation bar, click the Builder tab.
2. **Select Simple Blog and click Next.** Beehiiv gives you four options on this screen. Simple Blog is the one you want. Next is in the top right corner, and every screen after this one works the same way.
3. **Pick the Flow template and click Next.** Beehiiv changes their template library over time, so your screen may not match this one exactly. Find the one called Flow. It is the cleanest of them, which means the least for you to strip out later.
4. **Set your brand colors and fonts.** Two screens back to back, both edited from the left side panel. Use the same colors and fonts you already use everywhere else. Do not invent a new palette for your newsletter.
5. **Review your theme and click Start Building.** This screen changes nothing, it only shows you what you picked. Look at the colors and fonts one more time before moving on.
6. **Choose "Explore the editor, publish later".** Beehiiv asks whether you want to publish now or keep editing. Pick the second one, then click Continue. Nobody is visiting your site in the next ten minutes.
7. **Go through the onboarding instead of skipping it.** There is a Skip Onboarding button in the top right. Do not press it. Click Next and actually pay attention. The editor is simple enough, but I am not walking you through every panel in it when Beehiiv already does.
8. **Double-click the top nav bar and delete Archive and Subscribe.** Double-clicking opens the global navbar editor. Delete both links out of the middle. Your left side keeps your profile picture and the search icon and nothing else.
9. **Change Login to "Sign Up" and set its action to Sign Up Modal.** Click Login on the right side of the navbar. Change the Label field to "Sign Up", then under Click Action open the Action dropdown and pick Sign Up Modal. It defaults to Login Modal, which does nothing for someone who has never been to your site.
10. **Delete the socials bar.** Back in the main editor, it is the thin row sitting directly under your subtitle. You have no social data synced, so right now it renders as an empty strip.
11. **Change the headline to "[Your Full Name] Newsletter".** The big centered text near the top. Mine reads "Matthew Larsen Newsletter".
12. **Change the subtitle to your one-line description.** Use the exact line you already put into General Info in the previous step. The same line everywhere, not a new version of it.
13. **Rewrite the subscribe container's headline and subtitle.** The headline becomes "Subscribe to our free newsletter:" and the line under it becomes "(P.S. You will get our free [Your Lead Magnet] when you sign up)" with your actual lead magnet's name swapped into the bracket.
14. **Change the email input placeholder to "Enter your email...".** Click the subscribe form, then in the right panel click Input, not Button. The placeholder field is where you type this. Leave the rest of the form exactly as Beehiiv built it.
15. **Set the section background and text colors to your brand.** Click the section holding the subscribe container, not the container itself. Change the background to whatever fits your brand, then change the text color if the new background makes anything hard to read.
16. **Delete the post carousel under your subscribe section.** This is the row of blurred cards that says "No posts synced". It takes three deletes, not one. Delete the block, then delete the container it sat in, then delete the section that container sat in. Beehiiv nests these, and stopping after the first delete leaves empty boxes on your page.
17. **Set your post archive to five posts starting from post one.** Further down is your actual post list. Click it and the Post Selection screen opens. Set "Post count" to 5 and "Start from post" to 1. Leave "Filter by category" and "Filter by audience" on All.
18. **Turn the footer off.** Click the footer at the bottom of the page. On the right side you will see "Show on Page". Set it to No. It is a pointless section on a page this short.
19. **Publish your site.** Click Publish in the top right corner. A panel drops down, then click Publish to live site at the bottom of it.
20. **Copy your link and test the live site.** The "Your site is live!" pop-up gives you your URL with a Copy button next to it. Copy it, open a new tab, and paste it in. Look at the whole page, submit the subscribe form yourself, then open the same link on your phone and look at it again.

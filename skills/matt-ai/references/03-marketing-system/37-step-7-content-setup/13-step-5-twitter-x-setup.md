A lot of people will look at your X profile after discovering you somewhere else. Your profile needs to immediately confirm what you do, who you help, and where they should go next. Do not treat it as an empty account that only exists so you can repost content.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open** [X](https://x.com/) **and go to your profile.** Open [X.com](http://X.com) on your computer and click Profile in the left sidebar. If you do not have an X account yet, make one first.
2. **Set your username as close to your real name as possible.** Try your full name first. If it is taken, work through variations until one is free.
3. **Reuse your Instagram profile picture.** Click Edit profile, then click the camera icon over your current profile photo. Select the image from your computer, keep your face centered, and click Apply. Click Save in the top right.
4. **Add a professional cover photo that says who you help and what you offer.** Click Edit profile, then click the camera icon over your header photo and upload the image from your computer. Reposition it so the important words sit near the middle, then click Apply. Click Save in the top right.
5. **Write your bio using your One-Liner, your results, and a DM call to action.** Your bio needs to include the One-Liner you already created in the [Offers Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=c36801f65bf34b8cab3adc59bc86bcea&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce), your results, like how many clients you have helped, and a "DM me ___ to ___" call to action. Example: "I help high-performing men and women lose 10-50 lbs, gain strength and improve longevity | 1,200+ clients | 10,000lbs+ lost | DM me 'READY' to work with me."
6. **Add your Lead Magnet as your website link.** Click Edit profile and scroll down to the Website field. Paste your Lead Magnet link and click Save.
7. **Pin a post that is helpful or interesting to your ICP.** You should have a good idea of what would be interesting or helpful to your ICP by going through the [Messaging Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=28f95017c8884266886d6d4e55ebd191&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce).
8. **Open your profile on your phone and look at it as a stranger.** Someone should be able to understand what you do, who it is for, and that you know what you are talking about, within about a second of landing on it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open X and go to your profile?
- Is your username as close to your real name as possible?

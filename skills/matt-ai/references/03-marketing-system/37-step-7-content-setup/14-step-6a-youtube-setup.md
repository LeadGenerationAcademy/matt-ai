YouTube is the most important part of this entire Content System.

Almost all of your content should eventually move people toward your long-form YouTube videos because nothing else nurtures people at the same speed or depth.

Your channel needs to immediately communicate what you do, who your ICP is, and why someone should keep watching.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open YouTube on a computer and go to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/).** Sign in to YouTube, click your profile icon in the top right corner, and select YouTube Studio.
2. **Set your channel name as close to your real name as possible.** Click Customization in the left menu, then the Basic info tab. Type your name into the Name box and click Publish in the top right.
3. **Use a profile picture that makes it clear what you do and shows social proof.** Click Customization, then the Branding tab. Click Change next to your current profile picture, pick the image from your computer, and click Done. Click Publish in the top right.
4. **Add a banner that makes your [ICP](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/3dc179b5?md=56bdb09f179e46d6ba2564594734a08d&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) and [offer](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=d54eaf7d28c34779aaccf76172e0f72a&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) clear.** Click Customization, then the Branding tab. Click Change next to Banner image, select the image from your computer, and click Done. Click Publish in the top right. Use an "I help..." format. Example: "I create long courses to help business owners generate more leads."
5. **Write your description saying what your offer is and the main result.** Click Customization, then the Basic info tab. Type your description into the Description box and click Publish in the top right. You can just use the same text you have in your banner if you want.
6. **Add your Lead Magnet as your channel link.** Paste your Lead Magnet link, including https://, or it will not be clickable.
7. **Open your channel on your phone and look at it as a stranger.** Someone should be able to understand what you do, who it is for, and that you know what you are talking about, within about a second of landing on it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open YouTube on a computer and go to YouTube Studio?
- Is your channel name as close to your real name as possible?
- Does your profile picture make it clear what you do and show social proof?
- Does your banner make your ICP and offer clear?
- Did you write your description saying what your offer is and the main result?
- Did you add your Lead Magnet as your channel link?
- Did you open your channel on your phone and look at it as a stranger?

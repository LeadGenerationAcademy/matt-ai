We need to look like we know what we are doing.

Content is probably the most important part of our entire funnel because it shows up everywhere.

It gets attention, builds trust, nurtures prospects, helps close sales, and even improves retention after someone becomes a customer.

### Goal

Build a content presence that makes people instantly understand:

- What you do
- Who your ICP is
- What problem you solve
- Why they should trust you

Everything should be tailored toward the exact ICP you are trying to attract.

### Content Supports the Entire Funnel

Content works at every stage of the funnel.

At the Top of Funnel, it gets attention and introduces new people to you.

At the Middle of Funnel, it nurtures people, demonstrates your expertise, and builds trust.

At the Bottom of Funnel, it answers questions, overcomes objections, provides proof, and helps prospects convert.

Content even helps with retention.

Nobody watches your content more than your paying clients.

The more useful content they consume, the more bought in they become and the less likely they are to churn.

Content is not one small part of the funnel.

It supports the entire thing.

### Content Is Media Leverage

Content is a form of media leverage.

You put in the work once and continue receiving value from it afterward.

You can create one piece of content and have dozens, hundreds, thousands, or even millions of people consume it.

You do not need to personally repeat the same explanation to every person.

The content does it for you.

That is tremendous leverage.

### Make Your Positioning Obvious

When someone clicks your profile, they should immediately understand what you do and who you help.

They should not need to scroll through 100 posts or watch five videos to figure it out.

Your profile, biography, headlines, topics, examples, proof, and CTAs should all point toward the same ICP and the same general area of expertise.

Everything should feel unified.

### Occupy One Word in Their Mind

Bonus points if you can eventually occupy one word or category inside your ICP’s mind.

For example:

- Matthew Larsen = Agency
- Alex Hormozi = Business
- Ed Lawrence = YouTube
- Devin Jatho = Short-Form Video

When someone thinks about your category, problem, or service, you want them to immediately think about you.

That only happens when your positioning and content remain extremely consistent for a long time.

### Example

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy the Sony ZV10EF.** This is a good camera and it is the one to get. You want your face in these videos as much as possible, so you will be using it constantly.
2. **Buy the Sigma 16mm lens.** The camera needs a lens and this is the one that goes with it.
3. **Buy the Shure MV7.** This is a good mic. Sound quality matters a lot more than video quality, so this is the piece that carries the most weight.
4. **Download OBS.** This is the standard screen recording software people use to record actual videos. Loom is more for the shorter stuff, OBS is for longer videos. It makes it easy to share your screen while also using your camera, which is what most of these videos will be, screensharing and teaching a topic with your face in it. It is standard, easy to use, and free.
5. **Download Auphonic.** This is what cleans up the audio after you record. You come out sounding better and higher quality without doing anything yourself.
6. **Run every video through Auphonic before you upload it.** Every video, not just the ones you think need it. Since sound quality is the part that matters most, this is the last thing that happens before anything goes up.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy the Sony ZV10EF?

Your Instagram profile needs to look professional if you post content or run paid advertisements. A lot of people will see your advertisement, click your profile, and look around before doing anything else. If your profile is confusing, empty, or looks like shit, you will lose them.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Instagram and go to Account type and tools.** Open the Instagram app on your phone. Tap your profile picture in the bottom right corner, then tap the three lines in the top right corner. Scroll down to the "For professionals" section and tap Account type and tools.
2. **Tap Switch to professional account.** It is the first option on the screen, sitting under Account type. This converts the account you are already using. If you do not want to touch that account, make a brand new Instagram account and run these steps on that one instead.
3. **Pick the category that best describes you.** Instagram gives you a list to choose from. Pick the one that actually matches what you post about.
4. **Select Creator, then tap Next.** Instagram asks what type of professional you are and gives you two options to pick from. Creator should already be selected by default, so confirm it is before you tap Next at the bottom of the screen.
5. **Close the setup checklist and check your profile.** Instagram drops you onto a "Set Up Your Professional Account" checklist. Tap the X in the top right corner. Back on your profile you should now see Professional dashboard sitting under your bio, which is how you know the switch went through. Only you can see it.
6. **Open your [Instagram accounts settings](https://business.facebook.com/latest/settings/instagram_account) in Meta Business Manager.** The link takes you straight there. If you would rather find it yourself, open Meta Business Manager, click Settings in the bottom left, and Instagram accounts sits under the Accounts section.
7. **Click Add in the top right.** A warning appears saying only Instagram professional accounts can be added to a business portfolio. You already handled that in the steps above, so click Add Instagram account.
8. **Log into the Instagram account you just switched.** If you are already logged into that account on this computer, click "Log in as [your username]". If the screen shows the wrong account, click Switch accounts first and log into the right one.
9. **Set your username as close to your real name as possible.** Try your full name first. If it is taken, work through variations until one is free. Do not make people decode a clever username to figure out who you are.
10. **Use a clean headshot, kept centered.** Instagram crops your profile picture into a circle, so your face needs to sit in the middle of the image or it gets cut off.
11. **Write your bio using your One-Liner.** Your bio is two lines. The first line is the One-Liner you already created in the [Offers Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=c36801f65bf34b8cab3adc59bc86bcea), and the second line points them toward your YouTube: "[Your One-Liner]" "Watch My YouTube (link below)" Mine reads "Lead Generation" on that first line.
12. **Add your YouTube URL as your link.** Tap your profile picture in the bottom right corner, tap Edit profile, tap Links, then tap Add link. Paste your YouTube URL into the URL field. Leave the Title field empty, the URL on its own is enough.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open Account type and tools?
- Did you tap Switch to professional account?
- Did you pick the category that best describes you?
- Did you select Creator and tap Next?
- Did you close the setup checklist and confirm Professional dashboard shows on your profile?
- Did you open your Instagram accounts settings in Meta Business Manager?
- Did you click Add in the top right?
- Did you log into the Instagram account you just switched?
- Is your username as close to your real name as possible?
- Are you using a clean headshot, kept centered?
- Did you write your bio using your One-Liner?
- Did you add your YouTube URL as your link?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make four matching highlight covers at 1080 × 1920 pixels.** You need one for Clients, one for Wins, one for Testimonials, and one for Reviews. Keep the icon and anything else important centered in the image, since Instagram crops the cover down into a small circle on your profile.
2. **Open Instagram, tap the plus button in the middle of the bottom navigation bar, then select STORY.** Once the create screen has opened, you will see POST, STORY, REEL, and LIVE running along the bottom of it. STORY is the second one across.
3. **Tap the gallery icon in the bottom left corner and pick one of your covers.** That opens your camera roll. Check the image sits centered on the screen before you move on, then tap the arrow in the bottom right corner.
4. **Share it to Your story.** On the Share screen, Your story should already be selected. Tap Share at the bottom.
5. **Tap "Add to highlights".** The screen that follows is called "Also share to", and "Add to highlights" sits directly under the search bar.
6. **Name the highlight, tap Add, then tap Done.** Type "Clients", "Wins", "Testimonials", or "Reviews" depending on which cover you just posted. Add creates the highlight, and Done closes the screen.
7. **Repeat steps 2 through 6 for your other three covers.** You end up with four highlights, each one named, with its cover image sitting as the first slide inside it.
8. **Fill your Clients highlight with client logos.** These are the logos you should have already collected in the [Logos](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=f6782fc7ab3f46a0acb86affe1259a08) step of the Proof course. One logo per story, on a plain background. Post each one and add it to the Clients highlight the same way you added the cover.
9. **Fill your Wins highlight with screenshots of client wins.** These are the same screenshots you should have already collected in the [Screenshots](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=45dcae32230a4c3b9786f38d20654ad1) step of the Proof course. Post them one at a time and add each one to the Wins highlight.
10. **Fill your Testimonials highlight with your video testimonials.** These are the videos you should have already collected in the [Video Testimonials](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e0a27db4?md=23ae4e987fb949748e8e5ace9518b376) step of the Proof course. Post each one and add it to the Testimonials highlight.
11. **Fill your Reviews highlight with screenshots of third-party reviews.** Reviews from somewhere you do not control, like Trustpilot, screenshotted straight off the site. Post each one and add it to the Reviews highlight. If you do not have any reviews yet, leave this highlight alone for now, since you will be setting those up in the Search Results course.
12. **Open your profile and check all four highlights are showing.** Tap your profile picture in the bottom right corner. The four circles should be sitting under your bio with their covers and names on them.
13. **Keep adding to your highlights as you collect more proof.** This should not be something you add to once and never again. This is a continuous process of adding new client wins, testimonials, and reviews into their matching highlight.

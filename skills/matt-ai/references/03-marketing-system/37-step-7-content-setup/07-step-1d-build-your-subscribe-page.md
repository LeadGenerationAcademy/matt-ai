In this step you will be building a simple subscribe page for your newsletter using Beehiiv's native website builder.

You should be able to finish this subscribe page within 10-15 minutes by just following the steps below.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to** **[Website](https://app.beehiiv.com/website_builder_v2), then Builder.** Same starting point as the last step. Website sits in the left navigation bar inside Beehiiv, and Builder is in the second navigation bar that appears.
2. **Click Pages, then Subscribe.** Pages is the icon on the far left side of the editor. Clicking it opens your page list, and Subscribe sits under Default pages. What opens is the default subscribe page Beehiiv built for you.
3. **Hide the top nav bar.** Click the nav bar at the top of the page. On the right side you will see "Show on Page". Set it to No.
4. **Hide the footer.** Same thing at the bottom of the page. Click the footer, find "Show on Page" on the right side, set it to No.
5. **Write your headline.** Treat it like a sales page headline for your newsletter. It has to be related to what you actually post about. Mine reads "Lock In & Grow Your Business Faster Than Ever Before" because I post about lead generation and about locking in personally. Set it somewhere around 30 to 36 px. Font weight is your call, though a headline is usually heavy. Keep it to two lines on desktop and three lines maximum in mobile view.
6. **Write your subheadline.** Start it with "This free newsletter sends you", then say what you send and how often. Mine reads "This free newsletter sends you business tactics and strategies every week". Set it to 18 px on both desktop and mobile and leave the font weight on default.
7. **Leave the ratings line off until you actually have ratings.** Above the headline is where a line like "Rated 4.83/5 By 66,000+ Readers" goes. On day one you have no readers and no ratings. Come back and add it once you do.
8. **Set your subscribe form's fonts and button color.** Leave the placeholder on "Email". Click the form, then in the right panel open Input and Button and make sure both are using your font. Button is also where you change the background color to match your brand. Nothing else on this form needs touching.
9. **Add a text block under your subscribe form.** Click the plus button in the top left of the editor, which is Insert new block. Open Content blocks and drag Text underneath your subscribe form. Paste this line into it: "(No spam. Unsubscribe at any time)". Then set its alignment to center in the right panel.
10. **Set the section gap to 0.** Click the section background and Gap appears in the right panel. Gap is the default spacing Beehiiv puts between every element inside that section. Setting it to 0 hands the spacing back to you, and from there you can adjust the padding between everything yourself.
11. **Give the no spam line 16px of top padding.** This is what puts 16px between your subscribe form and the line underneath it. With that text block selected, find the Padding section on the right side, the one split into four boxes. The top left box is your top padding. Set it to 16. If you skipped Step 10 and the section gap is still on, you will just end up with far too much space here.
12. **Publish your page.** Click Publish in the top right corner, then click Publish to live site.
13. **Test your page.** Open your live subscribe page and enter an email address you actually own, not the one you signed up to Beehiiv with. Then go to your Subscribers tab in Beehiiv and confirm that email is sitting in there.

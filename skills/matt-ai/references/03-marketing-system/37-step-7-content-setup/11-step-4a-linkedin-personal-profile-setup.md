LinkedIn is very important because so many people will look at your profile after they learn about you somewhere else. They may see your advertisement, receive your cold email, watch your video, hear about you from a referral, and then immediately search for you on LinkedIn. Your profile needs to confirm that you are real, qualified, and relevant to them.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open LinkedIn on a computer and go to your profile.** Click the Me icon at the top of your LinkedIn homepage, then click View Profile.
2. **Reuse your Instagram profile picture.** Same headshot, kept centered. LinkedIn crops your profile picture into a circle, so your face needs to sit in the middle of the image or it gets cut off.
3. **Add a cover photo that says who you help and what you offer.** Click the camera icon in the upper right of your intro section and upload the image from your computer. Crop and reposition it so the important words sit near the middle, then click Apply.
4. **Write your headline using your [One-Liner](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=c36801f65bf34b8cab3adc59bc86bcea) and social proof.** Click the pencil icon in your intro section and find the Headline field. Your headline needs to include the One-Liner you already created in the Offers Course and social proof, like how many clients you have helped.
5. **Write your About section.** Scroll down to the About section and click the pencil icon in its top right corner. Write who you are and what you have accomplished, with real numbers wherever you can. Click Save.
6. **Add your Lead Magnet as your website link.** Click Contact info in your intro section, then click the pencil icon in the pop-up. Click Add website, paste your Lead Magnet link, and click Save.
7. **Open your profile on your phone and look at it as a stranger.** Someone should be able to understand what you do, who it is for, and that you know what you are talking about, within about a second of landing on it.

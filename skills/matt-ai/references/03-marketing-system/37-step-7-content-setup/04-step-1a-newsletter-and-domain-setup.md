Your newsletter gives you a direct way to reach your audience without depending on a social-media algorithm. Build it on your own name and your own domain now, and you will not have to tear it down and rebuild it later.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create your newsletter using** **[Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/).** Sign up and create your Beehiiv account. Every step below happens inside Beehiiv.
2. **Name the newsletter after yourself, not your company.** Your company may shut down, change its name, or get sold. Your name stays your name, which is the entire reason the newsletter gets built on it.
3. **Go to Settings, then** **[Domains](https://app.beehiiv.com/settings/publication/domain).** This is where every domain and sending address for your publication is handled.
4. **Add your personal domain.** This is your name, not your company's name. If you do not own one yet, buy [yourname.com](http://yourname.com) on GoDaddy before going any further.
5. **Complete Verify Ownership.** Beehiiv hands you a DNS record that proves the domain is yours. Add it inside your domain provider (e.g. GoDaddy), then come back and click Verify. If you have never added a DNS record before, paste the record and the name of your provider into ChatGPT and have it walk you through exactly where it goes.
6. **Complete Connect Domains.** Beehiiv gives you another set of records, this time for your email domain. Add every single one of them, not just the first, then click Verify.
7. **Complete Branded Link Setup.** Same process one more time, records to add and one to update. This is what makes the links inside your emails run through your own domain instead of a third party's.
8. **Set your email username to newsletter@ your domain.** This is the address subscribers see and reply to. Beehiiv puts sending on a mail. subdomain automatically, so the finished address reads as [newsletter@mail.yourname.com](mailto:newsletter@mail.yourname.com).
9. **Confirm your domain shows Live.** Beehiiv marks it Live once every record resolves. Depending on your provider this takes anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours. Do not start on branding until it says Live.

### Completion Audit

Your Facebook page needs to look professional if you run paid advertisements.

A meaningful amount of your Meta advertising spend may appear on Facebook, and a lot of people will see your advertisement, click your page, and look around before visiting your Funnel.

If your page is confusing, empty, or looks unprofessional, you will lose them.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Facebook on a computer and go to your personal Facebook page.** Log in to [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/), click your profile picture in the top right corner, then click your name. You should already have a page under your own name. You are not creating anything new here, you are fixing up the one you already have.
2. **Make sure your Facebook page is public.** Click your profile picture in the top right corner, select Settings & privacy, then click Settings. In the left sidebar, click General, find Page Visibility, and click Edit. Change it to Page Published and save.
3. **Reuse your Instagram profile picture.** It needs to look professional, with your face kept centered, since Facebook crops your profile picture into a circle. Click the camera icon on your current profile picture, select Update profile picture, then click Upload photo and pick the image from your computer. Click Save.
4. **Add a professional cover photo that says who you help and what you offer.** Hover over your current cover photo and click Edit cover photo in the bottom right corner. Choose Upload Photo and pick the image from your computer. Drag it so the important words sit near the middle, then click Save changes.
5. **Write your bio using your One-Liner and a call to action.** In the Intro section on the left, click the pencil icon next to your bio. Your bio needs to include the One-Liner you already created in the [Offers Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=c36801f65bf34b8cab3adc59bc86bcea&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce) and a call to action. Click Save.
6. **Add your Lead Magnet as your website link.** Click About under your page name, then select Contact and basic info. Find the Website section and click Add a website. Paste your Lead Magnet link and click Save.
7. **Add a call-to-action button to your Page.** Click the three dots near the top of your Page, then click Add action button. Select your button, click Next, follow the steps on screen, and click Save.
8. **Connect your Facebook page to your Instagram account.** Go to Settings in the left-hand menu, click Linked accounts, then select Instagram. Click Connect account and log in with your Instagram credentials.
9. **Connect your Facebook page to Meta Business Manager.** Open Meta Business Manager, click Settings in the bottom left, then click Pages under the Accounts section. Click Add in the top right, then click "Add an existing Facebook Page" and follow the steps on screen. This is the same screen you used for your Instagram account, just a different tab.
10. **Open your Page on your phone and look at it as a stranger.** Someone should be able to understand what you do, who it is for, and that you know what you are talking about, within about a second of landing on it.

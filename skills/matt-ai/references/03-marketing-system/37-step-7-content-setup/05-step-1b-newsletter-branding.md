### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go to Settings, then** **[General Info tab](https://app.beehiiv.com/settings/publication/general).** Every branding field for your publication sits on this one screen.
2. **Set your publication name to your full name.** Not your company name. The newsletter is built on you, which is the entire reason you set the domain up the way you did in Step #1.
3. **Write your one-line description.** One sentence telling someone what they get and how often they get it. Mine reads "Three ideas per week on how to generate more leads." Keep it that plain. Nobody is impressed by a clever description.
4. **Upload your profile picture as the publication logo.** This is a personal newsletter, so the logo is your face. Beehiiv recommends 800x800 px, and the same image becomes your favicon and mobile app logo.
5. **Upload your default thumbnail preview.** Use your profile picture again on a plain background, your full name underneath it, and the word Newsletter below that. Beehiiv recommends 1200x630 px. Build something else if you want, this is just for simplicity's sake.
6. **Choose your publication tags or leave them empty.** Beehiiv lets you pick up to three. Empty is the default. This will not affect your newsletter in any sort of way.
7. **Set your default language.** English for almost everybody here.
8. **Set your default timezone.** Select the time zone that you live in. Every scheduled send will be based off this timezone.

### Completion Audit

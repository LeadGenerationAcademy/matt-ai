# Action Steps

Course: Step #7: Content Setup
System: Marketing System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=58511e61d7864568b65e6002ce3f41b5

---
The Theory explains that content is probably the most important part of your entire funnel because it shows up everywhere, getting attention at the top, nurturing people in the middle, helping them convert at the bottom, and keeping clients bought in long after they buy.

This course is where you actually set up every account and tool your content runs on.

You will set up your newsletter on your own name and domain, then set up every personal profile across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and YouTube, so anyone who finds you somewhere else can immediately see what you do and who you help.

From there you buy the gear you record with, set up your Content Tracking Spreadsheet, and start logging the content you actually post in your Inputs Spreadsheet.

### Table of Contents

1. **Newsletter Setup:** Set up your Beehiiv newsletter on your own name and domain so you have a direct way to reach your audience without depending on a social-media algorithm.
2. **Instagram Setup:** Make your Instagram profile look professional, since a lot of people will see your advertisement, click your profile, and look around before doing anything else.
3. **Facebook Setup:** Make your Facebook page look professional, since a meaningful amount of your Meta advertising spend may appear on Facebook and people will click your page before visiting your Funnel.
4. **LinkedIn Setup:** Set up your LinkedIn so anyone who searches for you after seeing your advertisement, cold email, video, or referral can confirm you are real, qualified, and relevant to them.
5. **Twitter/X Setup:** Set up your X profile so it immediately confirms what you do, who you help, and where they should go next.
6. **YouTube Setup:** Set up your YouTube channel and recording gear, since almost all of your content should eventually move people toward your long-form YouTube videos.
7. **Content Tracking Spreadsheet:** Set up your Content Tracking Spreadsheet so every piece of content from every personal account sits in one organized place and updates automatically once per day.
8. **Add To Inputs Spreadsheet:** Log every piece of content you post in your Inputs Spreadsheet so you can hold yourself accountable to when you are actually posting content and when you are not.

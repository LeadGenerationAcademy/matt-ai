### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open LinkedIn on a computer and click For Business.** Sign in to your personal LinkedIn account, since you need one to manage a Company Page. The For Business grid icon sits in the top right corner of the home page.
2. **Click Create a Company Page.** It is at the bottom of the For Business menu. LinkedIn then walks you through a form, and steps 3 to 9 follow that form in order.
3. **Select Company.** LinkedIn gives you three options: Company, Showcase page, and Educational institution. Select "Company".
4. **Add your company name.** Use your actual business name.
5. **Add your website.** Add your website URL here.
6. **Add your industry.** Pick the industry that best matches what your company does.
7. **Add your company size.** Pick the employee range that matches how many people work in your company.
8. **Add your company logo.** Upload your company logo in square ratio.
9. **Check the verification box and click Create Page.** The box confirms you have the right to act for the business.
10. **Add a cover photo that says who you help and what you offer.** Click the camera icon in the upper right of your Page's intro section and upload the image from your computer. Crop and reposition it so the important words sit near the middle, then click Apply.
11. **Write your Overview section.** Click Edit page near the top right of your page header, scroll down to the Overview section, and paste your text into the About description field. Keep it simple by just writing who your company serves (your [ICP](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/3dc179b5?md=56bdb09f179e46d6ba2564594734a08d)) and what you do (your [offer](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=d54eaf7d28c34779aaccf76172e0f72a)).
12. **Add your company to the Experience section of your personal profile.** Click your profile picture in the top right, then click View Profile. Scroll down to the Experience section and click the pencil next to your current job, or the plus to add a new one. Type your company name and select it from the dropdown, or it will not connect. Add your title and dates, then click Save.
13. **Open your Company Page on your phone and look at it as a stranger.** Someone should be able to understand what your company does, who it is for, and that you know what you are talking about, within about a second of landing on it.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Ad Set Name:** The headline this ad set is testing.
2. **Conversion Location:** Website only.
3. **Performance Goal:** Maximize number of leads.
4. **Pixel / Dataset:** Your pixel.
5. **Conversion Event:** Schedule.
6. **Ad Set Budget:** Your daily budget divided by your number of ad sets.
7. **Start Date & Schedule:** The next day at midnight.
8. **Audience:** Broad targeting, with your location, age capped at 59 and gender only if relevant.
9. **Placements:** Facebook and Instagram only, on feeds, stories and reels.

### Example

Offer: Annuities Leads

- Ad Set Name: Leads That Actually Pick Up The Phone
- Conversion Location: Website
- Performance Goal: Maximize number of leads
- Dataset: Lead Generation Academy
- Conversion Event: Schedule
- Bid Strategy: Highest volume
- Attribution Model: Standard
- Budget Strategy: Daily budget, $66.66 ($200 per day across three ad sets)
- Start Date: Tomorrow at 12:00 AM
- Audience: Advantage+ off
- Locations: United States
- Age: 18 - 59
- Gender: All genders
- Detailed Targeting: All demographics, interests and behaviors
- Languages: English (US) or English (UK)
- Platforms: Facebook and Instagram
- Placements: Advantage+ off, feeds, stories and reels only (10 included, 12 excluded)
- Allow Limited Spending To Excluded Placements: Unchecked

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Click the ad set under your campaign in the left panel.** Set up one ad set properly first. You will duplicate it later instead of building every ad set from scratch.
2. **Name the ad set after the headline it tests.** I test by angle, so each ad set gets one of my headlines, like "Leads That Actually Pick Up The Phone" or "Qualified, Exclusive". Beyond that, name it whatever you like.
3. **Choose "Website" as the conversion location.** It will often default to website and instant forms. Make sure it is website only.
4. **Pick "Maximize number of leads" as the performance goal.** This is what we are using right now.
5. **Select your pixel as the dataset.** If your pixel is not showing up, it is usually because it is not connected to your ad account properly, which is covered in Step #1: Setup Meta Ads in the Paid Ads Setup Course.
6. **Make "Schedule" your conversion event.** This should be the Schedule event you set up in Step #4: Set Up Conversion Events in the Paid Ads Setup Course. It may show a warning if you do not have any conversions yet, and you can ignore it.
7. **Leave the bid strategy on "Highest volume" and the attribution model on "Standard".** Right now, this is what we are going to use.
8. **Enter your daily budget.** Leave the budget strategy on "Daily budget", then divide what you want to spend per day by the number of ad sets. If I want to spend $200 per day across three ad sets, each one gets $66.66. Keep as few ad sets as possible, since Facebook wants about 50 conversions per ad set per week.
9. **Schedule the start date for midnight tomorrow.** It is very clean, and it does not waste your budget on whatever is left of today.
10. **Keep your audience on "Advantage+ off".** Facebook will try to turn this on and use your age and gender as suggestions only. Do not let it, and do not click "Switch to recommended setup".
11. **Add your location.** For most people, this is just your country. If you are only licensed in one state, you can target that state, a city, a dropped pin or a list of zip codes instead.
12. **Cap the age at 59.** It defaults to 65+, and 65+ could also mean 80 year olds, who are not as likely to convert. Unless you have a real reason from your ICP, cap it at 59. If you declared a Special Ad Category in the previous step, the age range will be locked.
13. **Narrow the gender only if it is relevant to your ICP.** If you know from your customer data that you sell predominantly to men or to women, this is where you use it.
14. **Search for the language your ads are in.** In America, a lot of people speak Spanish. If your ads and everything else are in English and they do not speak English, that is a big problem.
15. **Leave detailed targeting on "All demographics, interests and behaviors".** Detailed targeting has not been accurate since the iOS 14 update in 2021. If you target people who like Ford trucks, a third actually like them, a third just hate on them in the comments and a third are mistagged, so you end up narrowing your audience more than anything else.
16. **Uncheck every platform except Facebook and Instagram.** Make sure Placements shows "Advantage+ off", then click "Show settings" under Account controls. Facebook tries to hide this from you.
17. **Remove every placement except feeds, stories and reels.** Uncheck marketplace, Audience Network and the rest of that crap. If you skip this, 99% of your clicks will go to Audience Network, and you will think you have a 20% click-through rate at 1 cent per click when none of it will ever convert. Leave "Allow limited spending to excluded placements" unchecked, and do not click "Include all available placements (recommended)" at the bottom.

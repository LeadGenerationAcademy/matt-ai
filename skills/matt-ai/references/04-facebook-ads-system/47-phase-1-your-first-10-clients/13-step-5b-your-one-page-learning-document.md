### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Your First 10 Client Master Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1y-NQrGDhoSezgBSJpMP-UV_CMTVpZirFv6QI1nI7Je8/edit?tab=t.wf7qn2b6xoj3).** In this document, you'll find the One-Page Learning Document as its own tab. This is the one document that stays on one page, and it gives you a quick way to see what you have learned and what you need to work on next. [in this skill: assets/templates/data-and-benchmarks/]
2. **Review the recording after each sales call.** Your call recordings get set up in Step #6C: Phase #4: Closes Plan + Assets. Everything you fill in below comes from these recordings. Use their actual language.
3. **Fill in Learnings On People Who Bought.** Who is buying, what do they actually want and why did they buy?
4. **Fill in Learnings On People Who Didn't Buy.** Who is booking but not buying, and why did they not buy?
5. **Fill in Questions & Objections That Keep Coming Up.** Only the ones that keep repeating. If the same objection keeps coming up, make an email around it, put it in your sales presentation or answer it in your VSL.
6. **Fill in What People Respond To Or Misunderstand About The Offer.** Both halves matter, what lands as well as what does not. If people keep misunderstanding something, change how you explain it.
7. **Fill in What You Need To Change In The Ads.** These are the 15 static ads you made in Step #2C.
8. **Fill in What You Need To Change In The Funnel.** Your VSL and pre-call material sit here.
9. **Fill in What You Need To Change In The Sales Process.** Your sales presentation sits here.
10. **Update the same document after every call.** Keep the useful patterns and replace assumptions with what you are actually hearing. The point is to learn as fast as possible.

# Completion Checklist

Course: Phase #1: Your First 10 Clients
System: Facebook Ads System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/988cd10a?md=8ab0ca9ce698455ead0ad9f476e0dbe4

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Setup

- You have finished the Paid Ads Setup Course.
- You have finished the Content Setup Course.
- You have finished the Search Results Course.
- You have set up the Call Booked > SMS Notification for your Facebook calendar event.

### Ad Creatives

- You have written five different ways to say your offer.
- You have created three static image ad designs that make the ICP and offer instantly clear, use high contrast and readable text, address roughly three objections and include a clear CTA.
- You have put all five headlines into all three designs, for 15 total ads.
- You have checked the finished ads against your above the fold and VSL.

### Media Buying

- You have set up your campaign level with the Leads objective and an ad set budget.
- You have set up your ad set level with Schedule as the conversion event, broad targeting, age capped at 59, and Facebook and Instagram placements only.
- You have set up your ad level with your sales page link, every creative enhancement turned off and your URL parameters added.
- You have duplicated your ads and ad sets so all 15 ads are uploaded.

### Launching

- You have removed your application, or loosened it if you chose to keep it.
- You have made a copy of the Metrics Tracking Template.
- You have made a copy of the Your First 10 Client Master Document and set up your One-Page Learning Document.
- You have gone through your whole funnel on your phone and made a test booking.
- You have confirmed your booking redirects to your thank you page and fires the Schedule event.
- You have your thank you page, reminders, pre-call flow, selfie video, Loom, One-Pager and appointment-setting message ready.
- You have your sales call plan, sales call presentation, follow-up flow, contract, One-Pager and payment link ready.
- You have launched your campaign at $100 or $200 per day, and your ads are approved and running.

### Decision Making

- You have checked your gender, age, platform and region breakdowns after 72 hours.
- You are taking every call yourself.
- You are reviewing every call recording and updating your One-Page Learning Document.
- You are filling in your Metrics Tracking Template every day.
- You have increased your budget to at least a few hundred dollars per day after your first close or your first five qualified shown calls.
- You have compared your numbers against the benchmarks and used If This, Then That to fix whatever is off.
- You have 10 clients from paid ads.

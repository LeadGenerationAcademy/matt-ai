### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your Ads Manager.** You can do this anywhere from 24 to 72 hours after launching. What you are looking for is whether your ads are accidentally sending to the wrong stuff, meaning you messed up the setup.
2. **Click the date button in the top right.** It will always default to the last 30 days.
3. **Set a custom 72 hour range.** There is no 72 hour preset, so you set it yourself using the start date and the date you are on now. You can click the two dates on the calendar or type them into the date fields.
4. **Click the Breakdown button in the top right.** It sits right next to Columns. Clicking it opens a dropdown holding every breakdown from steps 5 to 8.
5. **Check the Gender breakdown.** This should match the gender you set in your targeting in Step #3B.
6. **Check the Age breakdown.** You should not see any age outside the range you set. With your age capped at 59, Facebook shows the top bracket as 55-64, but you should never see 65+. One click outside your range does not really count.
7. **Check the Platform breakdown.** You should only see Facebook and Instagram. If you see Messenger or Audience Network, that is no good.
8. **Check the Region breakdown, if relevant.** This only matters if your business targets a specific area.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your Ads Manager?
- Did you click the date button in the top right?
- Did you set a custom 72 hour range?
- Did you click the Breakdown button in the top right?
- Did you check the Gender breakdown?
- Did you check the Age breakdown?
- Did you check the Platform breakdown?
- Did you check the Region breakdown, if relevant?

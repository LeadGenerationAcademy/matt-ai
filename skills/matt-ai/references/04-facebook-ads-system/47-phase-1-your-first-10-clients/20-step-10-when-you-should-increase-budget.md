### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep your budget at $100 or $200 per day until you have traction.** I do not think it is necessary to increase the budget before this. If it is not going to work at $100 or $200 per day, it is not going to work at $500 per day or $1,000. It only gets worse over time.
2. **Increase after your first close.** As soon as you get your first close, this is working at least to some degree.
3. **Or do it after your first five qualified shown calls, if you feel confident.** This is for when you have not closed yet because there is a sales cycle, and you want to speed things up. Those five calls need to be qualified people who actually attended, even if they have not closed yet. Five bookings do not mean the same thing.
4. **Raise your spend to at least a few hundred dollars per day.** This is my recommendation once you have traction. You don't have to.

### Completion Audit

- Did you keep your budget at $100 or $200 per day until you had traction?
- Did you increase after your first close?
- Or did you do it after your first five qualified shown calls?
- Did you raise your spend to at least a few hundred dollars per day?

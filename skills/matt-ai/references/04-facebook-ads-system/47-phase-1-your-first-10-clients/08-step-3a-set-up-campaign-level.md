### Framework & Criteria

1. **Campaign Name:** Campaign #1, or whatever you like.
2. **Buying Type:** Auction.
3. **Campaign Objective:** Leads.
4. **Budget Strategy:** Ad set budget.
5. **Campaign Budget:** Only if you use campaign budget, which you will not while testing.
6. **Special Ad Categories:** Only if you sell financial products, employment, housing or social issues.

### Example

Offer: Annuities Leads

- Campaign Name: Campaign #1
- Buying Type: Auction
- Campaign Objective: Leads
- Budget Strategy: Ad set budget
- Special Ad Categories: Financial products and services, since annuities are a type of insurance

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open [Ads Manager](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/adsmanager).** Media buying is more about avoiding mistakes than doing anything crazy.
2. **Click "Create".** This is the green button on the top left of your Campaigns table.
3. **Leave the buying type on "Auction".** It is already set to Auction by default. Never pick Reservation.
4. **Select "Leads".** If you are running ads to book calls, it is always Leads. Sales is really for ecommerce, and everything else has no place in this phase.
5. **Press "Continue".** This takes you into the campaign editor, where you will also build your ad sets and ads.
6. **Name the campaign "Campaign #1".** You can name it whatever you like. A naming convention is not going to make your ads convert.
7. **Choose "Ad set budget" under Budget strategy.** Campaign budget lets Facebook push spend to whichever ad set it thinks is best, which makes sense once everything is proven. When you are testing, you want each ad set to get the amount you give it.
8. **Declare a Special Ad Category only if you sell financial products, employment, housing or social issues.** Your ads often will not get approved without it, but it stops you from choosing gender and age range. If you can get away with it, leave it blank.

### Framework & Criteria

#### 1. Clicks

Look in your ad account.

##### Benchmarks

- CPM → $25-70
- Outbound CTR → 1-2%+

##### Improvements

- Better hooks
- Better bodies
- More proof, faster
- Clearer ICP + offer, faster
- Cleaner ad design
- Cleaner visual hierarchy

#### 2. Calls

Compare these to your number of outbound clicks.

##### Benchmarks

- Application Rate → 6-10%
- Booked Call Rate → 3-5%+

##### Improvements

- Better above the fold
- Cleaner visual hierarchy
- Better VSL
- Greater congruency
- Better rest of sales page
- More proof
- More calendar spots available
- More calendar spots, sooner

#### 3. Shows

Compare shown calls to calls booked.

##### Benchmarks

- Show Rate → 50-70%+

##### Improvements

- Shorter maximum booking time
- Better pre-call email flow
- Clearer thank you page
- Better selfie video
- Faster speed to lead
- Better pre-call assets
- More email and SMS reminders
- Better email deliverability for the calendar invite

#### 4. Closes

Compare closed calls to calls booked. At this stage we can switch to live calls later.

##### Benchmarks

- Close Rate → 15-30%

##### Improvements

- Better show rate
- Better pre-call framing
- Better introduction
- Better questions
- Better demo
- Better objection handling
- Better ways to ask for the close
- Book a meeting from a meeting
- More proof
- Better follow up flow
- More consistent follow up flow
- Clearer proposal, payment link, contract, next steps

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Data & Benchmarks tab](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1y-NQrGDhoSezgBSJpMP-UV_CMTVpZirFv6QI1nI7Je8/edit?tab=t.3nqwvifky9uf).** This is in the same Your First 10 Client Master Document you made a copy of in Step #5B. These benchmarks are specific to this phase. They are the barebones ones, and we do not need to overcomplicate it at this phase. [in this skill: assets/templates/data-and-benchmarks/]
2. **Pull up your copy of the Metrics Tracking Template from Step #5A.** These are the numbers you have been tracking since launch, and this is where you use them to make your decisions.
3. **Start with your CPA.** Your CPA is your cost per acquisition. If CPA is acceptable, nothing else matters. Every other metric could be absolute shit.
4. **If your CPA is not acceptable, use the metrics to troubleshoot.** This Golden Rule stays true throughout every phase of scaling.
5. **Check your CPM in your ad account.** You want $25-70. If you are in an exceptionally crowded niche, something like $125 might be acceptable. If it is $250 or $300, something is probably wrong. The same goes for a $4 CPM.
6. **Check your Outbound CTR.** You want 1-2%. Anything under 1% a lot of the time means your ads are not resonating enough. Anything over 2% is usually a function of your CPM being very high, because the higher your CPM, the more your ads go only to the most qualified people.
7. **Compare your calls to your number of outbound clicks.** You want a 6-10% Application Rate if you kept the application in Step #4, or once you add it back in. You want a 3-5% Booked Call Rate. Your booked call rate is really a function of your application rate, and a lot of the time it will be 50-70% of it.
8. **Look at how many of your calls are qualified.** You want 80-90%. It will never be perfect, but anything less than that at this phase is probably not good.
9. **Compare your shown calls to your calls booked.** You want 50-70%. If it was 100% that would be phenomenal, but 70% is very realistic. A lot of B2C and mass market offers will typically skew lower.
10. **Compare your closed calls to your calls booked.** You want 15-30%. When you have a sales team, you usually base close rate on live calls to judge their sales ability. Comparing to calls booked also takes in your show rate, and since you are the one taking all these calls and you want your show rate up anyway, we do it this way.
11. **Pick your fixes from the Improvements list for whichever phase is off.** Each phase in the Framework & Criteria above has its own list. The next step, If This, Then That, tells you exactly what to do for each metric.

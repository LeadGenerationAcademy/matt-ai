# Action Steps

Course: Phase #1: Your First 10 Clients
System: Facebook Ads System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/988cd10a?md=f3b3f94d5cb348168d4967e055329b6d

---
The Theory explains that Facebook ads are only one part of the process. The ad gets the click, but someone still needs to book, show up and buy, and if any phase falls apart, you do not get the client.

This course is where you go and get your first 10 clients from paid ads.

You will confirm your setup is finished, make 15 direct-offer static image ads, build your campaign, remove your application, set up your Metrics Tracking Template and One-Page Learning Document, check every phase of your funnel, and launch. Then you will check your delivery, learn from every call, increase your budget once you have traction and use your numbers to decide what to fix until you have 10 clients.

Do not launch with any part of your funnel half finished, and do not increase your budget before your first close or your first five qualified shown calls.

### Table of Contents

1. **Make Sure You Have These Set Up:** Confirm your Paid Ads Setup, Content Setup and Search Results courses are finished so Facebook can track bookings and your profiles look professional when people look you up.
2. **Create 5 Static Image Direct Offer Ads:** Write five different ways to say the same offer, because you do not know yet which one your ICP will resonate with.
3. **Create 3 Static Image Ad Designs:** Build three designs that make the ICP and offer instantly clear, stand out in the feed, address roughly three objections and include a clear CTA.
4. **This Will Make 15 Total Ads:** Put all five headlines into all three designs and check the finished ads against your above the fold and VSL so the page delivers what the ad promises.
5. **Set Up Campaign Level:** Create a Leads campaign on an ad set budget so each ad set gets the amount you give it while you test.
6. **Set Up Ad Set Level:** Optimize for the Schedule event with broad targeting, capped at age 59, on Facebook and Instagram only.
7. **Set Up Ad Level:** Upload your ads, point them to your sales page, turn off every creative enhancement and add your URL parameters.
8. **Remove Application (Optional):** Send people straight to the calendar so you get experience quickly with as little friction as possible.
9. **Metrics Tracking Setup:** Log your ad spend, clicks, calls, shows, clients and cash every day so you know exactly where the problem is when something does not work.
10. **Your One-Page Learning Document:** Keep one page of what you learn from every call so you can change your ads, funnel and sales process.
11. **Phase #2: Calls Checklist:** Go through your whole funnel on your phone and make a test booking to confirm the ad, page, VSL, calendar and Schedule event all work.
12. **Phase #3: Shows Plan + Assets:** Have your thank you page, reminders, pre-call flow, selfie video, Loom, One-Pager and appointment-setting message ready before the first person books.
13. **Phase #4: Closes Plan + Assets:** Take every call yourself, with pre-call research, your sales call plan and presentation, and your follow-up, contract and payment link ready.
14. **Upload & Schedule Your Campaigns:** Check your ads, audience, conversion event, budget and start time, then launch at $100 or $200 per day.
15. **Check Breakdown After 72 Hours:** Make sure your ads are not accidentally going to the wrong genders, ages, platforms or locations.
16. **Refine Your Information After Each Call:** Review every recording and update your funnel or sales process so the next call is better than the last.
17. **When You Should Increase Budget:** Raise your spend to at least a few hundred dollars per day after your first close or your first five qualified shown calls.
18. **How To Read The Data & Benchmarks:** Start with your CPA, then compare each phase against its benchmarks to find where things are falling apart.
19. **If This, Then That:** Match whatever metric is off to the exact fix for it, and revisit your offer if every metric is poor.
20. **Continue Until You Have 10 Clients:** Keep taking the calls, learning from the recordings and improving your ads, funnel and sales process until you have 10 clients from paid ads.

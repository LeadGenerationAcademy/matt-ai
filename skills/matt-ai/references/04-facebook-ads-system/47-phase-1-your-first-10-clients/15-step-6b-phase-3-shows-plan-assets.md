### Framework & Criteria

1. **Thank-You Page:** Confirms the booking and tells them to accept the calendar invitation, reply to your text and watch the material.
2. **Pre-Call Reminders:** Calendly's built-in email and SMS reminders at 24 hours, four hours and one hour before the call. Just turn them on.
3. **Pre-Call Flow:** Answers the biggest questions and objections. Put the most important ones first. Someone booking for later today will not see the whole flow.
4. **Selfie Video:** Under 60 seconds, introducing the person they will speak with.
5. **Loom + One-Pager:** Ready to send so they can learn more about the offer before the call.
6. **Appointment-Setting Message:** Your name, company and the appointment they booked. Send it with the selfie video and preparation material as quickly as possible.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your thank-you page.** It should already confirm the booking and tell them to accept the calendar invitation, reply to your text and watch the material. Booking does not guarantee they will show up. This page helps them confirm the appointment and gives them something useful to do while their interest is still high.
2. **Turn on Calendly's built-in email and SMS reminders.** Set them for 24 hours, four hours and one hour before the call. Just turn them on. Someone can be interested in your offer and still miss the call because they forgot or could not find the link.
3. **Confirm your pre-call flow is running.** Each email answers one of the biggest questions or objections, which keeps them learning about the offer while they wait and gives them more reason to attend.
4. **Put your most important questions and objections first.** Someone booking for later today will not see the whole flow. Someone who books three days ahead sees more emails than someone who books a call for tonight.
5. **Keep your selfie video under 60 seconds.** It introduces you as the person they will speak with. It gives them some contact with you before the appointment, which matters when they came through a cold ad and barely know who you are.
6. **Have your Loom and One-Pager ready to send.** This is so they can learn more about the offer before the call. Send both together with your personal reach-out so they arrive at the call understanding what you offer and why they might want your help.
7. **Write your appointment-setting message.** Say your name, your company and the appointment they booked. Put all of the information in the text itself, with no hidden intentions and no multi-step back and forth.
8. **Send the message with your selfie video, Loom and One-Pager as soon as someone books.** Your call booked notification tells you the moment it happens. Sometimes someone is ready to talk now, and there is no reason to leave them waiting for an appointment several days away.
9. **Check that the emails, texts and links work on your test booking.** Go back to the test booking you made in the previous step. Have all of this ready before the first person books.

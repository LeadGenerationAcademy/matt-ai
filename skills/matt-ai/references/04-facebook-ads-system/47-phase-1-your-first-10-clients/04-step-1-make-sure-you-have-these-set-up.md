Make sure you have the following set up before moving forward with this course:

### Paid Ads

- **[Meta Ads](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=578291ccdd0a4151a19948a3acc6fba3):** Business Manager, ad account and pixel made, with your Instagram, Facebook page and payment method connected
- **[Pixel](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=27ec205cd1974f08be8b7c354515f350):** Installed in the head of every page, published and showing as Active
- **[Conversion Event](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=b5d27feaff3b4356a2d96300d6901c34):** Schedule set up on your Call Booked Confirmation Page URL
- **[Columns](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=c313b114671b4c758749e96d439a01c5):** Custom columns built, including Application Rate, Booked Call Rate, Hook Rate and ThruPlay Rate, and saved as a preset

### Content

- **[Instagram Profile](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=e167f3d81da64b10b8343c377c61fc50):** Professional Creator account connected to Business Manager, with a centered headshot, One-Liner bio and YouTube link
- **[Instagram Highlights](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=45622a789fc445969ebccc39521549e3):** Clients, Wins, Testimonials and Reviews, each with a matching cover
- **[Facebook Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=1e16472a7f024daeb57239363b4eccce):** Public, with a cover photo saying who you help and what you offer, One-Liner bio, Lead Magnet link and call-to-action button
- **[LinkedIn Personal Profile](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=c1d44363d5594f1d9f2317a20d7137ac):** One-Liner headline with social proof, About section, cover photo and Lead Magnet link
- **[LinkedIn Company Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=3053218e47734a15a1bd2767b360c430):** Logo, cover photo and Overview filled in, and added to the Experience section of your personal profile
- **[Twitter/X](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=8d85849a84ef418da61d5134c46e6bf5):** One-Liner bio with a DM call to action, Lead Magnet link and a pinned post your ICP would find useful
- **[YouTube Channel](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/1df452b8?md=00f0e6821a884b189f99b269f034d60b):** Banner making your ICP and offer clear, a description with your offer and main result, and your Lead Magnet link

### Search Results

- **[High-Domain Authority Websites](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5ab376c5?md=9038e237298f490aa995fb59e2e0c44e):** Every general and industry profile created or claimed, complete and consistent
- **[Review Websites](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5ab376c5?md=c3bcdca219ad4269bbf7e77939523c15):** Clutch, GoodFirms and Trustpilot claimed, with review requests built into your processes
- **[Personal Website](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5ab376c5?md=c5bbfd126f4d4e788dbd9823bc59f502):** Your name as the domain, with your bio, results and contact information, ranking as a top result when someone searches you
- **[Company Website](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5ab376c5?md=938edd80b0b44608987a1f789b558ff8):** On-page SEO reviewed, Google Search Console set up and your sitemap submitted
- **[Personal Socials](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5ab376c5?md=907eff336d7f4ecc95889aa1e1270cc3):** Every personal profile claimed, with your videos, posts and case studies published under your name
- **[Company Socials](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5ab376c5?md=6a20bf43ffca4507b075044c9bdc6d75):** FAQ answers, testimonials and client results published on every company profile

### Bookings & Notifications

- **[Facebook Calendar](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=bc29b4542cff4e9095e09f832790dd69):** Your Calendly meeting duplicated with "fb" as the slug, and a test call booked on it
- **[Slack Notification](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=7db362a1942c4264bcf436ffac94f63a):** Every booking on your /fb calendar posted to a Slack channel with their name, email and phone number
- **[SMS Notification](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/0a453095?md=2969a3a4e07a424c9c16baf78b20c261):** A text to your phone the moment someone books on your /fb calendar

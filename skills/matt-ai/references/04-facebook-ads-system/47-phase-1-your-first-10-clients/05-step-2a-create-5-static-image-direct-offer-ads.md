### Example

Offer: Annuities Leads

- Qualified, Exclusive Annuity Leads Delivered Straight To Your CRM
- Get Annuity Leads Without Paying Agency Retainers Or Ad Spend
- Get Annuity Leads That Actually Answer The Phone
- Stop Competing With Other Advisors For The Same Annuity Leads
- Consistent, Affordable Annuity Leads On A Pay Per Call Basis

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go through everything that makes your offer good.** You know all of it. Someone seeing your ad for the first time does not, and they cannot read your mind. If you get results quickly, do all the work for them, include something valuable or have great proof, SAY IT. Do not assume something is obvious just because it is obvious to you.
2. **Write five different ways to say your offer.** The example above shows what this looks like for one offer. The ICP is financial advisors, the offer is annuity leads, and there are five different ways to phrase it. We do not know what people are going to resonate with yet, because we have limited or no history of what works and what does not.
3. **Keep it essentially the same offer across all five.** They all appeal to the same ICP, but some might appeal more than others. You have to throw some stuff out there and see what sticks.
4. **Treat these five as your headlines.** In Step #2C you put each of these five headlines into all three of your static ad designs, keeping everything else the same and only changing the headline.

### Completion Audit

- Did you go through everything that makes your offer good?
- Did you write five different ways to say your offer?
- Is it essentially the same offer across all five?
- Are all five written as headlines you can drop into a design?

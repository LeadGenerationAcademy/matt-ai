### Framework & Criteria

1. **Ad Spend:** What you spent on your ads.
2. **Outbound Clicks:** The clicks from your ads to your sales page.
3. **Calls Booked:** Everyone who booked a call.
4. **Calls Attended:** Everyone who actually showed up.
5. **Qualified Calls Attended:** Only the people who showed up and can buy.
6. **Clients Acquired:** Everyone who became a client.
7. **Cash Collected:** The money you actually collected.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Metrics Tracking Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dl4T891VA6cECaJx1C3jE6BF6hkA5hJkofzAN5vxFEQ/edit?usp=sharing).** This is where you track what happens after you spend money on ads, one row per day. Otherwise, when something does not work, you will have no idea where the problem is. [in this skill: assets/templates/metrics-tracking-template/]
2. **Open the tab for the current month.** Every month of 2026 has its own tab, with the dates already filled in.
3. **Enter your ad spend and outbound clicks for each day.** Both numbers come straight from Ads Manager.
4. **Enter your calls booked and calls attended for each day.** Calls booked is everyone who booked a call. Calls attended is only the people who actually showed up.
5. **Enter your qualified calls attended separately.** Ten calls with people who can buy is very different from ten calls with people who cannot.
6. **Enter your clients acquired and cash collected for each day.** Cash collected is the money you actually collected, not what someone agreed to pay later.
7. **Check your numbers in the Week and Month rows.** The purple Week row and the red Month row add up your numbers, then calculate your cost per click, cost per booked call, show rate, close rate and cost per client from those totals. Close rate is clients acquired divided by calls booked. You will use these numbers to make your decisions later in this course.
8. **Check the Dashboard tab.** It pulls every month's Month row into one place, so you can compare your numbers month to month without clicking into each tab.
9. **Update the spreadsheet every day.** Keep it updated, or the numbers you make your decisions from will be wrong.

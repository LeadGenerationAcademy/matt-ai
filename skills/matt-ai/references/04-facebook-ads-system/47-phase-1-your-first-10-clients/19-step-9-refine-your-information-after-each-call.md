### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Review the recording after each call.** This is an ongoing process. Every call you take, you want the next one to be better and better.
2. **Note what the person wanted.** Use their actual language, and put it in your One-Page Learning Document from Step #5B.
3. **Note what they understood or misunderstood about the offer.** If the same misunderstanding keeps coming up, improve how you explain the offer.
4. **Note the questions they asked.** These go under Questions & Objections That Keep Coming Up in your learning document.
5. **Note the objections they raised.** If people repeatedly raise the same objection, address it in your VSL, pre-call material or sales presentation.
6. **Note the reasons they gave for buying or not buying.** These go under Learnings On People Who Bought and Learnings On People Who Didn't Buy in your learning document.
7. **Update the relevant part of your funnel or sales process.** Use the recordings to improve what the next person sees and hears.

### Completion Audit

- Did you review the recording after each call?
- Did you note what the person wanted?
- Did you note what they understood or misunderstood about the offer?
- Did you note the questions they asked?
- Did you note the objections they raised?
- Did you note the reasons they gave for buying or not buying?
- Did you update the relevant part of your funnel or sales process?

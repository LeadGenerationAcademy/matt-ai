### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Put your five headlines into each of your three designs.** These are the five ways of saying your offer from Step #2A, dropped into the three designs you made in Step #2B. Design 1 gets five headline versions, Design 2 gets five and Design 3 gets five.
2. **Keep everything else the same within each design and only change the headline.** The ICP callout, the three objections and the CTA all stay identical across the five versions of that design. Five headlines × three designs = 15 ads.
3. **Look at the finished ads alongside your above the fold section and VSL.** Treat these as one unit. Ideally, your static image ad and sales page combine to make it clear who it is for and what the offer is, so your VSL can get into proof and explanation as fast as possible.
4. **Make sure the page delivers the same offer the ad promises.** They all need to be congruent and work as part of the same unit. Some of these ads may work for you right away and some may not. You will upload them when you set up your ad level in Step #3C.

### Completion Audit

- Did you put your five headlines into each of your three designs?
- Within each design, did you keep everything else the same and only change the headline?
- Did you look at the finished ads alongside your above the fold section and VSL?
- Does the page deliver the same offer the ad promises?

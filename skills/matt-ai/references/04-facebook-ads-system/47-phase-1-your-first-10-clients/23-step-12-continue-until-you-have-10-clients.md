### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Stay in this phase until you have 10 clients from paid ads.** I have no idea how long that will take you. It could be one week on the fast end, one month or two months. It depends on you, your skill and your budget.
2. **Keep taking the calls.** These are still yours to take, same as Step #6C.
3. **Keep learning from the recordings.** This is the review from Step #9, running on every call for as long as this phase lasts.
4. **Keep making more ads.** Some of your ads may work right away and some may not.
5. **Keep improving the ads, funnel and sales process.** These are the same three change sections in your One-Page Learning Document.
6. **Confirm you have the fulfillment capacity before the next phase.** Ten clients is what lets you move on, and your ability to serve them is the second thing that has to be true.
7. **Have at least 90x your daily ad spend in the bank before the next phase.** Ninety times whatever you are spending per day, which goes up as soon as you raise the budget in Step #10.

### Completion Audit

- Did you stay in this phase until you had 10 clients from paid ads?

- Do you have at least 90x your daily ad spend in the bank?

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Ad Name:** The design this ad uses.
2. **Facebook Page + Instagram Account:** The page and profile you want the ad to come from.
3. **Ad Format:** Single media.
4. **Upload Creative:** The ad you made in Canva.
5. **Primary Text:** Your ad copy.
6. **Headline:** The same headline as your ad and sales page.
7. **Description:** Leave it blank.
8. **Website URL:** Your sales page.
9. **Call-To-Action Button:** Learn more.
10. **Creative Enhancements:** All off.
11. **Tracking:** URL parameters for campaign, ad set, ad ID and placement.
12. **Check Previews:** Make sure it looks good and stands out in the feed.

### Example

Offer: Annuities Leads

- Ad Name: Underline
- Destination: Website
- Website URL: [https://leadgenerationacademy.ai/](https://leadgenerationacademy.ai/)
- Browser Add-Ons: None
- Media: The Underline design
- Format Display Options: Single media
- Primary Text: Financial Advisors: We will generate exclusive and qualified annuities leads and sync them directly to your CRM...
- Headline: Exclusive, Qualified Annuity Leads
- Description: Blank
- Call To Action: Learn more
- Advantage+ Creative Enhancements: All off
- Essential Enhancements: All off
- Advantage+ Creative Translation: Off
- Languages: Off
- URL Parameters: utm_source={{[campaign.id](http://campaign.id)}}&utm_medium={{[adset.id](http://adset.id)}}&utm_campaign={{[ad.id](http://ad.id)}}&utm_content={{placement}}

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Click the first ad under your ad set in the left panel.** Set up this one ad perfectly first. Then you can easily duplicate it.
2. **Name the ad after its design.** For example, "Underline Ad", "Cloud Ad" or "Big Letter Ad". You can use a naming convention if you like.
3. **Choose your Facebook Page and Instagram account.** Make sure the ad is going out from whichever Facebook page and Instagram profile you want.
4. **Enter your sales page link as the website URL.** Leave the destination on "Website". Make sure the link is going to the proper page.
5. **Click "None" for browser add-ons.** It may be set to "Instant form (suggested)". Just click none.
6. **Upload your ad from Canva under Media.** Download it from Canva first. Facebook will show you a bunch of other options here, like AI-generated images. Just ignore them.
7. **Make sure format display options is set to "Single media".** The ads you made in Canva are single static images.
8. **Paste your ad copy into the primary text.** All of the results are going to come from your creative itself, so this does not matter that much. Move the text around so the first line reads fully in the preview, like "Financial Advisors: We will generate exclusive and qualified annuities leads".
9. **Use the same headline as your ad and sales page.** The headline only shows on Facebook. Just check what your ad and sales page say, like "Exclusive, Qualified Annuity Leads".
10. **Leave the description blank.** The description is pretty pointless. You do not need it.
11. **Set the call to action to "Learn more".** It does not really matter that much. Facebook might auto-select something, and you can just click off of it.
12. **Turn off every creative enhancement.** This means both Advantage+ creative enhancements and Essential enhancements. Facebook will turn a bunch of this crap on. Always say no. If it is on, click it off.
13. **Keep translation off.** Advantage+ creative translation and Languages should both show off. They only translate your text, and they do not translate the creative. Only turn them on if you have a real reason to translate your ad.
14. **Add your URL parameters under Tracking.** Copy "utm_source={{[campaign.id](http://campaign.id)}}&utm_medium={{[adset.id](http://adset.id)}}&utm_campaign={{[ad.id](http://ad.id)}}&utm_content={{placement}}" into the URL parameters field. Every ad click will carry the campaign, ad set, ad ID and placement, which helps your tracking and feeds back to Facebook a bit better. You can also sort by UTM in your CRM and see exactly which ad led to the result.
15. **Check the preview.** Look at the Ad preview on the right and make sure it looks good. Your ad should stand out in the feed with its contrasting colors, enough that you can imagine yourself stopping at it.
16. **Duplicate the ad for your other designs.** I duplicate it twice, swap in the other two designs and rename them "Cloud Ad" and "Big Letter".
17. **Copy this ad set for each of your other headlines.** Rename each copy after its headline and swap each ad's creative for that headline's version. With 15 ads, that is five ad sets with three ads each, or three ad sets with five ads each.

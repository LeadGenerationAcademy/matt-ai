### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Check whether you have ever run paid ads to a sales call before.** If you have never done this before, getting experience quickly is extremely valuable, and it outweighs the negatives of removing the application. If you have done this before, you can leave your application as it is.
2. **Remove your application and send people straight to the calendar.** Normally, we use the application to stop unqualified people from booking, so we don't get garbage leads caught up in our pixel data and ruin our optimization. That remains true. This phase is the one exception, and for now we want as little friction as possible. Even unqualified calls can teach you what people think you sell, what they want and what questions they have.
3. **Loosen your application instead if you would rather keep it.** You don't have to remove it, but it is recommended. We may not completely remove the application, or we may do so, but we should not make it tough or strict.
4. **Add the application back after your first 10 clients.** We can always add more friction later. Until then, you will learn what to expect, adjust to selling to cold traffic, and quickly find out what the questions and objections are so you can tailor your sales call plan and presentation.

### Completion Audit

- Did you check whether you have ever run paid ads to a sales call before?
- Did you remove your application and send people straight to the calendar?
- If you kept your application, did you loosen it?
- Are you waiting until after your first 10 clients to add the application back?

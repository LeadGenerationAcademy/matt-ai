### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Canva](https://www.canva.com/).** The Static Ad Template is made in Canva, so you need an account to be able to edit this template. Skip this if you already have a Canva account.
2. **Make a copy of the [Static Ad Template](https://canva.link/gkp2rrf8pmvgf2t) or create your own design.** If you already made a copy during the paid ads setup course, use that one instead of making a new one. You do not have to use the template at all, you can also just create your own designs.
3. **Create three designs using the static image framework.** I like to do at least three designs. Your first can be pretty simple, your second a bit different, and your third a bit different still. The framework is the four things below, and all four need to be true in every one of the three designs.
4. **Make the ICP and offer instantly clear.** They know what the offer is and who it is for within one second. Literally instantly. In my example, that is financial advisors and annuity leads.
5. **Use high contrast and readable text.** The ad stands out in the feed when they are scrolling Instagram, and the important information is easy to read.
6. **Address roughly three important objections.** These can also be three pieces of detail. Use your FAQ and objection research to address the things most likely to stop them from clicking.
7. **Include a clear CTA.** Tell them what to do next. With all ads, but especially static images, we should not be trying to sell our entire service inside the ad. That is what our page, VSL and sales process are for.

### Completion Audit

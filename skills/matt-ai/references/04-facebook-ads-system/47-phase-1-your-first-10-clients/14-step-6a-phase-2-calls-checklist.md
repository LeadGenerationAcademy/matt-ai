### Framework & Criteria

1. **Ad + Above-The-Fold + VSL:** All explain the same offer to the same ICP.
2. **Above-The-Fold:** The headline, VSL and CTA are easy to find on mobile.
3. **VSL:** Plays properly and explains the offer.
4. **Rest Of Sales Page:** Someone can understand what you sell without watching the video.
5. **Booking:** The CTA takes them straight to the calendar.
6. **Availability:** Plenty of slots, as soon as possible, with a booking window of no more than 72 hours.
7. **Confirmation:** Booking sends them to the thank-you page and triggers the Schedule event.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Pull up your sales page on your phone.** They have never heard of you before, and this page is the first thing they ever see.
2. **Put one of your ads next to your above the fold and the first 15 seconds of your VSL.** Treat these three as one unit. A lot of people have them completely disconnected. Your ad and above the fold should make the ICP and the offer immediately clear, which frees the first 15 seconds of the VSL to show proof right away and explain the offer.
3. **Confirm all three explain the same offer to the same ICP.** The page has to deliver the same offer the ad promises.
4. **Find the headline, VSL and CTA without scrolling.** The above the fold is what they can see on mobile when the page loads. If you look at heat maps, upwards of 70% of people do not scroll down at all, so anything tucked away below it they will never see.
5. **Play the VSL all the way through.** Make sure it plays properly on your phone and explains the offer. Someone coming from a paid ad may have never heard of you before, and the video needs to give them a reason to believe you can help.
6. **Read the rest of your sales page without watching the video.** Someone should be able to understand what you sell without watching the VSL for even one second. Some people just do not want to watch VSLs, and if the information only lives in the video, they will not know how it works and they will not book a call.
7. **Tap the CTA button.** It should take them straight to the calendar. For this phase, the application should already be removed in Step #4: Remove Application (Optional), since we want as little friction as possible.
8. **Look at the available times on your calendar.** You want as many slots as possible, as soon as possible, with a booking window of no more than 72 hours. As soon as possible means someone should be able to book a call a few minutes from now. A calendar with very little availability creates another place for interested people to drop out.
9. **Make a test booking.** Go through the entire thing on your phone and book through the Calendly event you cloned for this funnel. Use that event only in this funnel so you can identify the calls booked through it.
10. **Watch for the redirect to your thank-you page.** Booking should send you straight to your thank-you page. This is where your conversion triggers, and where people are told what to do before the meeting.
11. **Check the Schedule event with Meta Pixel Helper.** Open the thank-you page with the Meta Pixel Helper Chrome extension, which may be called Meta Ads Data Advisor now. You should see Schedule on this page along with PageView. Schedule is the conversion event in your ad sets, and if the two do not match, it will not track.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [If This, Then That tab](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1y-NQrGDhoSezgBSJpMP-UV_CMTVpZirFv6QI1nI7Je8/edit?tab=t.3sd41aphis09).** This tab is in the same Your First 10 Client Master Document as Step #5B and the previous step. [in this skill: assets/templates/data-and-benchmarks/]
2. **Use this table only if your CPA is not acceptable.** If CPA is acceptable, nothing else matters. Every other metric could be absolute shit.
3. **If your CPM is over $100, completely redo or rethink your ads.** If you are in a very, very competitive niche, maybe $150. Past that, your ads probably have a terrible quality ranking. The six ad improvements from the previous step are what you work from.
4. **If your Outbound CTR is under 1%, completely redo or rethink your ads.** Anything under 1% a lot of the time means your ads are not resonating enough.
5. **If your Cost Per Outbound Click is over $10, review the ad improvements.** It is not necessarily a dealbreaker if you are getting calls booked. If your cost per outbound click is like $20 and you are not getting any calls booked, it really is unlikely to work.
6. **If your Application Rate is under 6%, rework the above the fold and VSL.** Make the offer clearer, show relevant proof and make sure the page matches the ad. Then make sure the ads have the right messaging to bring in the relevant people.
7. **If your Application Rate is over 10%, tighten the application and add friction.** This only applies if you are still using the application, since you may have removed it back in Step #4.
8. **If your Close Rate is under 15%, check your show rate first.** Then review pre-call framing, the sales calls and follow-up. Use the recordings to identify what needs work.
9. **If your Close Rate is over 30%, increase your price.** That is a sign you are not charging enough. You never really want your close rate to be more than 30%, because you can just increase your prices and make more margin.
10. **If more than 50% of your calls are unqualified, make the ICP and offer more specific in your ads and on the page.** Realistically, even over 20%. Tighten the application if needed.
11. **If your Show Rate is under 50%, improve pre-call flow, reminders and speed to lead.** The eight show improvements from the previous step are the full list to pull from.
12. **If every single metric is poor and you think you did a good job, revisit your offer.** This only applies once you have spent a few thousand dollars, generated clicks and calls, and REALLY tried to make this work, and it remains a complete battle beyond the normal learning curve and growing pains. Most people give up too soon and blame the offer when the problem is their execution. But the reality is that some offers just are not good, and you may need to try another one.

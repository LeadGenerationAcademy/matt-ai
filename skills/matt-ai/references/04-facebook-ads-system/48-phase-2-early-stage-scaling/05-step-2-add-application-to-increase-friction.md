### Framework & Criteria

- **Application Rate:** 6–10% of outbound clicks.
- **Qualified Call Rate:** At least 90% of the people attending calls fit our qualification criteria.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Add your application back in if you removed it in Phase #1.** We now want more control over who reaches the calendar.
2. **Review the qualification criteria we established earlier.** Make sure the application checks whether they have the budget, authority, need and timing for your offer.
3. **Confirm your application rate is 6-10% of outbound clicks.** If fewer than 6% of outbound clicks become applications, the ad is appealing to the wrong people, the landing page is not giving them enough reason to apply, or the form is too strict. Figure out which one it is before changing everything.
4. **Confirm at least 90% of the people attending calls fit your qualification criteria.** If they do not, make the ICP and offer more specific in the ad and on the page. Then tighten the application where needed.
5. **Look at who is applying if your application rate is above 10%.** A higher application rate is not automatically better. Check whether these are actually the people you want to sell to, and look at application volume and qualification together.

### Completion Audit

- Did you add your application back in if you removed it in Phase #1?
- Does your application check for budget, authority, need and timing?
- Is your application rate 6-10% of outbound clicks?
- Do at least 90% of the people attending calls fit your qualification criteria?
- If your application rate was above 10%, did you look at who is applying?

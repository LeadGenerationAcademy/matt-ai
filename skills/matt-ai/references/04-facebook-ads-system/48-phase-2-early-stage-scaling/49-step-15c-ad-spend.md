### Framework & Criteria

1. **Below 0.75X:** Decrease spend.
2. **0.75X to below 1X:** Keep spend the same.
3. **1X to below 2X:** Increase spend by 20% per week.
4. **2X or more:** Increase spend by 50% per week.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Ad Spend Decision Making Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nqmZGoj9L2_EvhT8U54mRO6i6sVTtxvR4dnuqUfatAM/edit?tab=t.k9qt2r5n7465#heading=h.g2x4fuf1temi).** This is a tab in the same document as your Unit Economics Model Template. This is where you decide what happens to spend each week. [in this skill: assets/templates/format-copywriting-bot-template/]
2. **Review spend weekly using the unit economics model.** The model is Net Upfront Cash ÷ (Acquisition Cost + Fulfillment Cost), and the result tells you which of the four decisions above to make.
3. **Use a measurement period that gives your sales cycle time to play out.** Compare the cash and costs for the same group of customers.
4. **Apply these increases while you have the cash and capacity to support them.** If sales or fulfillment cannot handle more volume, hold or reduce spend while you fix that.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Ad Spend Decision Making Template?
- Did you review spend weekly using the unit economics model?
- Did you use a measurement period that gives your sales cycle time to play out?
- Did you apply these increases while you have the cash and capacity to support them?

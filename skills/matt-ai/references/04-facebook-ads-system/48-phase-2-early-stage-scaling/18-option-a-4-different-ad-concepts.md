### Framework & Criteria

1. **Negative:** Focus on the problem and what happens if they keep dealing with it.
2. **Positive:** Focus on the result they want and what improves when the problem is solved.
3. **Case Study:** Show how a real customer went from having the problem to getting the result.
4. **Story:** Explain the same message through a story rather than presenting the points directly.
5. **How-To / Step-By-Step:** Walk through the steps involved in solving the problem.
6. **Example:** Show a specific example so the buyer can see what you mean.

### Example

**Original**

We turn one hour of your raw footage into 20 ready-to-post short-form videos within seven days, with all editing and captions included.

**Negative**

That hour of content sitting on your hard drive is doing nothing for your business. You already recorded it. Send it to us, and we'll turn it into 20 finished videos within seven days.

**Positive**

Get 20 pieces of content out of the next hour you record. We'll handle the editing and captions, so next week you have finished videos ready to publish without spending your evenings editing them.

**Case Study**

Here's what we made from [Client]'s one-hour interview: 20 standalone videos, all edited, captioned and delivered within seven days. Let me show you the original recording alongside the finished clips.

**Story**

You finish recording an hour of useful content and open the editing software. An afternoon later, you've barely finished the first clip.

Next time, send us the recording. We'll turn it into 20 finished videos within seven days.

**How-To / Step-By-Step**

Start with one hour of footage. Find 20 useful points that each work as a standalone video. Cut the repetition, clean up the edits and add captions.

That is what we do for you, with all 20 videos delivered within seven days.

**Example**

In one interview, you explain what your service costs, answer a common objection and walk through a customer result.

Those can become three separate videos. We do that across the full recording, turning one hour of footage into 20 edited, captioned clips within seven days.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep the same underlying message.** These are ways to take essentially the same ad and frame it differently. We keep the same message, but change how we explain it. That gives us another version of the ad.
2. **Rewrite your ad in each of the six framings.** Use the negative, positive, case study, story, how-to and example framings from the Framework & Criteria above.
3. **Combine each framing with the formats from [Option A #2: Different Ad Formats](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=d2e15a986bbd44459ae87176b91da5ad).** A case study could be delivered as a talking-head video, a Loom screenshare or a set of slides.
4. **Keep the problem and offer consistent.** We are just changing how we communicate them.

### Completion Audit

- Did you keep the same underlying message?
- Did you rewrite your ad in each of the six framings?
- Did you combine each framing with the formats from Option A #2: Different Ad Formats?
- Did you keep the problem and offer consistent?

### Example

The same static ad could become:

- Large text on a plain background.
- A headline over a relevant photo.
- A layout with the message on one side and supporting proof on the other.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep the concept that worked.** We are giving the same message a different visual presentation.
2. **Change the layout, typography, colors or images of your static ad.** Keep the same wording. Only the design changes.
3. **Change the framing, caption style or on-screen information of your video ad.** This covers how the video is shot and how supporting information appears on screen.
4. **Keep the main message consistent.** This is so the design is the only thing we are testing.

### Completion Audit

- Did you keep the concept that worked?
- Did you change the layout, typography, colors or images of your static ad?
- Did you change the framing, caption style or on-screen information of your video ad?
- Did you keep the main message consistent?

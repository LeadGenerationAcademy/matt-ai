### Framework & Criteria

1. **ICP Callout:** Who the ad is for is obvious immediately.
2. **High Contrast:** The ad stands out in the feed, and the important information is easy to read.
3. **Clarity:** Someone understands the message immediately.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Start with the message.** Decide what someone needs to understand immediately, which objections we need to address and what we want them to do next.
2. **Choose a layout that makes those things easy to read.** You can do a lot here: a simple text-based graphic, an Apple Notes-style ad or other layouts that present the same message differently.
3. **Adapt the amount of copy to the layout.** An Apple Notes-style ad can work with a more written explanation. A simple graphic may need a larger headline and much less supporting text.
4. **Check the ICP callout, high contrast and clarity.** These are the most important things in a static image.
5. **Keep the editable templates.** This is so we can produce variations without redesigning everything.

### Completion Audit

- Did you start with the message?
- Did you choose a layout that makes those things easy to read?
- Did you adapt the amount of copy to the layout?
- Did you check the ICP callout, high contrast and clarity?
- Did you keep the editable templates?

### Framework & Criteria

1. **ICP Callout:** Some sort of banner or on-screen text identifying the ICP immediately.
2. **Captions:** Captions underneath the chin.
3. **Pacing:** Unnecessary pauses removed.
4. **Proof:** Proof added throughout the edit.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a real editing workflow for each ad format you chose in [Step #12A: Ad Creative Production System](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=e123c412275c4001a59d8ea5c6047512).** Regardless of the format, the editing "rules" will very much be the same. Build each workflow so it can be reused and improved.
2. **Identify the ICP within 0.1 seconds.** The ICP should already be obvious from the first frame. If the ad is for financial advisors, we can have "Financial Advisors" on screen while the presenter starts speaking. That lets the intended buyer recognize that the ad is relevant before we have explained the entire offer. Keep the callout readable. It should not disappear before someone has had a chance to understand it.
3. **Put captions underneath the chin.** This is where they are easy to read without covering the face. Adjust the placement when we show something else that needs to remain visible. If the captions cover the result or demonstration, the edit is working against the explanation.
4. **Remove long pauses, false starts and dead space.** Keep the delivery natural enough that the person is still easy to follow.
5. **Add proof where it supports the script.** When the presenter mentions a result, show the relevant result. When they explain what the customer receives, show what the customer receives. If they describe a process, show the part of the process that helps someone understand what they mean. In regards to editing, we should focus primarily on adding proof, not making brainrot.
6. **Give the editor the screenshots, testimonials or demonstrations with the script.** The editor should know which asset belongs at each point.
7. **Save the finished edit as a reference.** This also makes it easier to apply AI to parts of the workflow because we have defined the inputs, instructions and expected result.

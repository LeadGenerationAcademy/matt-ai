### Example

ICP: Agency owners between $500k - $5 million per year

1. Getting paid ads to work
2. Adding a second acquisition channel
3. Getting out of founder-led sales

These speak to problems or outcomes that could matter to a substantial part of that market. Each one gives us a different reason the same type of agency owner might want our help.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Find at least 10 angles that could apply to a large portion of your ICP.** These are the potential workhorses, messages that could support substantial spend if they perform. Research helps you identify candidates, but testing tells you which ones actually produce customers.
2. **Expect to end up with at least 4 that actually work.** You do not necessarily expect all 10 to work. We are trying to end up with at least four that actually work and that we can keep producing ads around.

### Completion Audit

- Did you find at least 10 angles that could apply to a large portion of your ICP?
- Do you expect to end up with at least 4 that actually work?

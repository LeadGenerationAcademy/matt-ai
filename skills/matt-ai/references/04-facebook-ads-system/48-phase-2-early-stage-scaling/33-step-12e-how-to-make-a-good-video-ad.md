### Prerequisites

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Within One Second:** They understand what the offer is and who it is for.
2. **Within Five Seconds:** They have seen proof.
3. **Within Fifteen Seconds:** They have the information they need to understand the offer and decide whether to click.
4. **After That:** More proof, answers to FAQs and explanations that help them make the decision.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Show the ICP and offer within one second.** We want the important information at the beginning. Use the visual and the script together. The ICP and offer can already be on screen while the presenter starts speaking. We do not need to say everything in one second for the opening frame to communicate it. Skip the long introduction and get into the offer.
2. **Show proof within five seconds.** This could be a result, a demonstration or an example of the work. Make it clear what the person is looking at. A tiny screenshot with no explanation does not give them much reason to believe us.
3. **Explain everything within fifteen seconds.** Someone should understand what we sell and why it might be relevant to them. They should not have to watch the entire video just to figure out the offer. We can explain more afterward, but the opening needs to give them enough information to decide whether they want to continue or click.
4. **Use the rest of the video for more proof and objections using your [Top 30 FAQs](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=04cebeffa75f415797f223580e3a67a2).** The rest of the video gives people more reasons to believe us and answers the questions they still have. Show more proof, explain how the service works and address the objections that would stop them from moving forward. Use the FAQ and sales call research we already have. Those are the questions our buyers are actually asking.

### Completion Audit

- Did you show the ICP and offer within one second?
- Did you show proof within five seconds?
- Did you explain everything within fifteen seconds?
- Did you use the rest of the video for more proof and objections using your Top 30 FAQs?

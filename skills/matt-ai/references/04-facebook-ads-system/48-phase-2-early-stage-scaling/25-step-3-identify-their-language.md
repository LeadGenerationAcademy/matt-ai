### Example

I did research for auto insurance, and this is what I found:

1. "They were nice during the sale, then ghosted me."
2. "They lowballed my claim."
3. "The claims process took forever."

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Record how people describe the situation.** Those statements tell you what happened and what frustrated the customer. You can build an ad around that language because it describes a situation someone else may immediately recognize.
2. **If five people describe the same problem differently, keep those versions.** They may give you different ways to open an ad.
3. **Count different wordings of the same problem as one angle.** Those are wording variations around one underlying problem. We should not count them as five completely different angles.
4. **Sort your angles into workhorse angles and niche angles.** A workhorse angle could apply to a large portion of our ICP. If it performs, it has the potential to support substantial spend. A niche angle speaks to a more specific problem or circumstance. Fewer people may identify with it, but it can resonate strongly with the people who do. We want both. A smaller audience does not make an angle useless.

### Completion Audit

- Did you record how people describe the situation?
- If five people described the same problem differently, did you keep those versions?
- Did you count different wordings of the same problem as one angle?
- Did you sort your angles into workhorse angles and niche angles?

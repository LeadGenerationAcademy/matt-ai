### Example

One proven angle, with a few variations of every method:

- Angles: 1
- Genders: 2
- Races: 3
- Age ranges: 3
- Ad formats: 5
- Visual designs: 3
- Framing styles: 6
- Keyword variations: 3
- Different ways to phrase the message: 3
- Backgrounds, locations or settings: 3
- Total possible combinations: 43,740

1 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 × 3 × 6 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 43,740.

That is 43,740 theoretical combinations from one angle, using relatively few options in each category. This is obviously an extreme example. You would not need to turn one winner into 43,000+ ads. But it demonstrates there is effectively no upper limit, and any reason you cannot scale your ads is just cope.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Start with one proven ad.** We already know the message works. Now we want to see how many useful variations we can get out of it through different presenters, formats and wording. The underlying problem, offer and reason to buy stay consistent as we change how we present them.
2. **Expand your proven ad through different people presenting it in [Option A #1: Different Avatars In The Ad](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=b1582d8395bf476fbb002efb59ee9407).** Have different people recreate the same ad. This gives us a way to see whether the same message resonates with other people in the market.
3. **Expand your proven ad through different creative formats in [Option A #2: Different Ad Formats In Terms Of Format](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=d2e15a986bbd44459ae87176b91da5ad).** Take the message that worked and adapt it to another format. We will need to adapt the delivery to the format, but we should still be able to recognize the original angle and offer.
4. **Expand your proven ad through different designs in [Option A #3: Different Ad Formats In Terms Of Design](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=04904c5c7cc84265a18ea4b8e5be33c9).** Keep the concept and change the design. We are giving the same message a different visual presentation.
5. **Expand your proven ad through different concepts in [Option A #4: Different Ad Formats In Terms Of Concept](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=cce18101ad4a48389f870820f3dd0b24).** Take essentially the same ad and frame it differently. The problem and offer stay consistent. We are changing how we communicate them.
6. **Expand your proven ad through different words that make it relevant to the intended audience in [Option A #5: Different Keywords](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=78a855528a34470ba433ca02f7e5d502).** Sometimes you can keep almost the entire script and change a few words. The person watching recognizes the language used in their business.
7. **Expand your proven ad through different ways of describing the same thing in [Option A #6: Different Ways To Describe The Same Thing](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=4a5b2138d7f441cd8e544b9fc6bc06a6).** Change how we phrase the message while keeping the meaning. We are still speaking to the same buyer about the same problem.
8. **Expand your proven ad through different backgrounds, locations and settings in [Option A #7: Different Backgrounds/Locations/Settings](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=64624373328041a58e2fa765f3810078).** Have the presenter deliver the same ad in different settings. We can keep the script and offer consistent while changing where it is filmed and what the viewer sees around them.
9. **Test the combinations that fit your offer and audience.** We are keeping the same offer and ICP throughout, so the keyword and wording variations still need to fit that customer. Some combinations will overlap or make no sense. We work through the ones that fit the offer and audience, then test them. This is how much we can get out of a message that already works.

### Completion Audit

- Did you start with one proven ad?
- Did you expand your proven ad through different people presenting it?
- Did you expand your proven ad through different creative formats?
- Did you expand your proven ad through different designs?
- Did you expand your proven ad through different concepts?
- Did you expand your proven ad through different words that make it relevant to the intended audience?
- Did you expand your proven ad through different ways of describing the same thing?
- Did you expand your proven ad through different backgrounds, locations and settings?
- Did you test the combinations that fit your offer and audience?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Decide what you want the buyer to understand before recording.** This can show the actual deliverable, account or workflow. We can also use supporting slides where they help explain what someone is seeing.
2. **Have the relevant screens ready and know the order you will show them in.** This keeps the demonstration easy to follow.
3. **Record a screenshare going through a demonstration of the offer.** Explain what they are looking at, what they would receive and how it relates to the result they want.
4. **Have the editor remove unnecessary navigation.** The editor should also highlight the details that would be difficult to follow at normal screen size.
5. **Keep the demonstration outline.** This is so we can repeat the format with another example or angle.
6. **Expand within your five formats using different presenters from [Option A #1: Different Avatars In The Ad](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=b1582d8395bf476fbb002efb59ee9407).** You can keep the same script and have different people deliver it. Their appearance, voice, personality and delivery give us different versions of the ad without needing to rewrite the message.
7. **Expand within your five formats using different framing from [Option A #4: Different Ad Concepts](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=cce18101ad4a48389f870820f3dd0b24).** Take that same message and explain it through the problem, the desired result, a case study, a story, a how-to or an example.
8. **Combine presenters and framing.** One proven message presented by three people in three different framings gives us nine versions within a single video format. Keep the ICP, offer and underlying message consistent while changing how we communicate them.

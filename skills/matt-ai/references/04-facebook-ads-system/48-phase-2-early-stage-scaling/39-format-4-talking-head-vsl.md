### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write the original script manually.** This is the classic style of ad where someone speaks directly to the camera and explains the offer. We call out the ICP, explain what we sell, show why they should believe us and answer the questions most likely to stop them from taking the next step.
2. **Use the opening structure from [Step #12E: How To Make A Good Video Ad](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=7c743f901d1c4d868c8351d5406c269f).** That is immediate clarity, proof within the first five seconds and enough information to understand the offer within fifteen seconds.
3. **Explain the process, show more proof and address the relevant FAQs after the opening.** The rest of the video gives people more reasons to believe us.
4. **Give the editor instructions for what should appear while each point is being made.** The presenter delivers the explanation, and the edit shows the evidence supporting it.

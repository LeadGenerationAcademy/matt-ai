### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Start with your sales call recordings in the [Messaging Research From Calls Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pvUnPwUhN16OCTgx1pr7Pa0b9WRpulS6tHW_jt_xX0s/edit?gid=2135564468#gid=2135564468) you made in [Step #2A: Messaging Research From Calls](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=72137c7f43c24d3bb594a6bde9ddb80b).** Look for repeated problems, objections and explanations of why someone wants the result. Pay attention to what was happening in their business that made them book the call. [in this skill: assets/templates/messaging-audit-template/]
2. **Review the places where your ICP discusses these things in the [Messaging Research Via Internet Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pvUnPwUhN16OCTgx1pr7Pa0b9WRpulS6tHW_jt_xX0s/edit?gid=998399835#gid=998399835) you made in [Step #2B: Messaging Research Via Internet](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=913451d7c5ce45c9ae9cd0639cda8481).** These are customer reviews, Reddit, forums and relevant communities. [in this skill: assets/templates/messaging-audit-template/]
3. **Add what you find to the same research document.** You can use AI to organize the material, but keep the original wording. We want to know what these people actually said.

### Completion Audit

- Did you start with your sales call recordings in the Messaging Research From Calls Template?
- Did you review the places where your ICP discusses these things in the Messaging Research Via Internet Template?
- Did you add what you find to the same research document?

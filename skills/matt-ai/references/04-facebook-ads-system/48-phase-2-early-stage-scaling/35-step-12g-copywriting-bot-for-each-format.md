### Framework & Criteria

1. **Static Image:** Ask for the headline, supporting statements, CTA and proof to feature.
2. **UGC:** Ask for a spoken script with on-screen text and proof instructions.
3. **Loom:** Ask for the demonstration sequence and what to explain while showing each part.
4. **Slides:** Ask for the slide sequence, slide copy and supporting visuals.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Format Copywriting Bot Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nqmZGoj9L2_EvhT8U54mRO6i6sVTtxvR4dnuqUfatAM/edit?tab=t.ob9ny0mk92xm).** This is where you give the copywriting bot everything it needs to adapt your original ad into one format, along with exactly what to produce and the boundaries it has to stay inside. [in this skill: assets/templates/format-copywriting-bot-template/]
2. **Feed the bot the full context.** This is the original ad or selected angle, the ICP and offer, the above-the-fold section, the VSL including its transcript, the relevant proof and examples of the format we want. Include the visuals as well as the words where available. The bot needs to understand what is shown, what is said and what happens after the click.
3. **Tell it exactly what to produce for the format.** "Turn this into an ad" leaves too much unspecified. The format changes the output. The ICP, offer, claims and conditions should remain consistent.
4. **Give it clear boundaries.** Tell it what to keep the same, what to write and what it is not allowed to add. For example: "Do not add claims, results or terms that are not in the source."
5. **Review the output against the original ad and destination page before sending it into production.** This is how you catch anything the bot changed that it should not have.
6. **Update the bot's instructions and examples when you repeatedly make the same correction.** That is how the workflow becomes quicker and more consistent over time.

### Completion Audit

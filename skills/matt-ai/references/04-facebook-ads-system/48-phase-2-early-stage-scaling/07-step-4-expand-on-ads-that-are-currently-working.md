### Framework & Criteria

**Ad Creation Checklist**

1. **ICP Callout:** Call out the ICP in a banner at the top or in a popup.
2. **Instant Clarity:** Make it immediately clear who the offer is for and what it is. You can do this with your script or with your editing.
3. **Hook First:** Start with the hook. Skip the introduction and get straight into the ad.
4. **Lighting:** If filming on your phone, film facing a window for natural light. Avoid having the window behind you.
5. **Delivery:** Look into the camera and speak conversationally. It should sound like you are talking to one person.
6. **Proof On Screen:** Show the actual testimonials, results or other proof on screen when you refer to them.
7. **Subtitles:** Put subtitles below your chin. Check them for mistakes.
8. **On-Screen Text:** Make on-screen text large enough to read on a phone without covering your face or the proof. Also make sure it is not cut off anywhere when it is eventually posted on Instagram or Facebook.
9. **Minimal Editing:** Keep editing minimal. You need to learn how to be compelling on camera.
10. **Phone Check:** Watch the finished ad on your phone, both with sound and on mute.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Copywriting Bot Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1md-ADSSRPDTc9Ob4_oOF4_rrgMj-L_5iQXAsNBh52F4/edit?usp=sharing).** This is where you write your angle versions and bodies, and its instructions have the copywriting bot combine them into complete scripts. Have the copywriting bot write the scripts. Then you film them. [in this skill: assets/templates/copywriting-bot-template/]
2. **Write 10 versions of your winning ad angle.** Even inside your winning ad angle, there are quite a few different ways to say the same thing. Each version will literally be like one sentence. Even though they are saying something very similar, the ads can feel quite different, and one version might appeal to a part of your ICP that another version does not. Keep the idea that worked. Change how you say it. The rest of our strategy will take time to set up, and this gives us something to work on immediately, using ads that have already worked, while we build the rest. Write them in the "My 10 Angle Versions" section of the Copywriting Bot Template.
3. **Write three versions of your winning ad body.** This is where you explain the offer, show proof and answer their questions, in about 100 words, maybe a bit more. Keep the same offer, but vary how you explain it. You can use different proof, answer different objections or change the order you go through things. Write them in the "My Three Bodies" section of the Copywriting Bot Template.
4. **Paste the filled-in Copywriting Bot Template into your copywriting bot, like ChatGPT or Claude.** Each body should work with the 10 versions of your angle, with small edits where needed. That gives us 30 complete scripts, labelled by the angle version and body used.
5. **Review any combination the bot flagged.** The bot is told not to rewrite a combination that does not work without substantial changes. It flags it, explains the issue and keeps any problems or suggested changes separate from the scripts.
6. **Decide who is going to film the ads.** My preference is you. It is your business, and you need to learn how to speak on camera and make compelling ads. If you are completely against this or want help producing more, the second option is hiring actors on Fiverr or Upwork, which costs money. The final option is AI, although by the time you fuck around with it, you may have spent longer than it would have taken to film the ad yourself. Most of the reasons not to do it yourself are complete cope. There are no tricks or hacks to getting comfortable on camera. You need to practice.
7. **Choose five different setups to film in.** One of the easiest ways to get more ads quickly is to film those scripts in different settings. You could wear different outfits. You could be in different locations. Some could be outside. Some could be in your studio. You could even have different people reading the scripts.
8. **Film all 30 scripts in each of the five setups.** This turns your winning static image ads into equivalent talking head videos. Keep the offer and the message that worked, and use the video to explain the offer, show proof and address the questions someone might have. This gives us another format to test while we develop more messaging. Even with the same words, the ads will look and feel different. That gives us 150 variations to test, all based on messages that have already worked. The idea is to get the next leg up in your ad account without having to come up with 150 new ideas.
9. **Edit every ad using the Ad Creation Checklist.** Call out the ICP at the top, put subtitles below your chin and show the actual proof on screen when you refer to it. Keep the editing minimal.
10. **Watch every finished ad on your phone with sound and on mute.** You will be amazed at what you look like and your body language when it is on mute.

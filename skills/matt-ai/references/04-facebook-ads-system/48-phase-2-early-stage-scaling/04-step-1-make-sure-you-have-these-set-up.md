Make sure you have the following set up before moving forward with this course:

### Phase #1

- **[10 Clients From Paid Ads](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/988cd10a?md=25c6e8ea67bb4237a201ab80a9b3aecb):** At least 10 clients from Facebook Ads with your current offer or a substantially similar offer

### Calls

- **[Sales Page Above The Fold](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=3feb286fb88041009e34f24ca2d7789c):** ICP called out, a clear offer and result in the headline, and the VSL and CTA button visible on mobile without scrolling
- **[VSL](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/7c941b44?md=79ad1ab3eeda40cdb98e20b6ac1bbb38):** Quickly shows proof, explains what you do and answers the questions most likely to affect their decision to speak with you
- **[Application](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=ef780cefa17b4c27b6e83db128999d5c):** Checks budget, authority, need and timing before someone gets access to your calendar
- **[Rest Of Sales Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=3b6742b4d7514d09aea0a756f39f0cf0):** Explains what they get, how it works and why they should believe it, without them watching the VSL for even one second
- **[Welcome Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=f61ef3a01fe74f08a9919594b977c394):** Delivers what they requested, explains what you do and answers the biggest FAQs with relevant proof
- **[Call Booking Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=6ca6b510654b459e82772aade2c1881f):** As many available slots as possible, appointments as soon as possible and a booking window of no more than 72 hours
- **[Top 30 FAQs](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=04cebeffa75f415797f223580e3a67a2):** Taken from real sales conversations in their own language, most important first, each with a clear answer and proof where you have it

### Shows

- **[Thank You Page](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/94f7e3ec?md=c1c3747425614c5596ed450c09f645d3):** Confirms the call is booked and prompts them to watch the VSL, reply to your text, accept the calendar invitation and watch the FAQ videos
- **[Pre-Call Email & SMS Reminders](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=a75e1776bac74f0da0981d2ed1001472):** Email and SMS reminders turned on for 24 hours, four hours and one hour before the call
- **[Pre-Call Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=3b28b1dd935148b89ef719f783ffe2db):** Each email answers an important question and links to an asset that explains it further, with the most important information first
- **[Selfie Video](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/7c941b44?md=716352bf9173466e8944758007520976):** 60 seconds or less, filmed so it could plausibly be custom-filmed for them and paired with your appointment setting reach out
- **[Appointment Setting Reach Out](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=583975d68bea4e43aca7c6ca049405a2):** A text with your name, your company name and a reference to their booked call, plus two phone calls to get through Do Not Disturb
- **[Loom](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/7c941b44?md=d1021fdff9e648979fb762d4ee8ac133) + [One-Pager](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=47264b77776f422e889dcffc1b21198c):** A one-pager they can read on their phone without scrolling and a Loom walking through it, sent together with your reach out
- **[Retargeting Ads](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/7c941b44?md=2d01eb9f92d04ada8442fb271c180808):** Each ad focused on one real FAQ, objection or piece of proof for people who booked a call but have not attended yet
- **[No Show Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=65979b6ded20449e824f1c461e73b976):** Answers what may still be holding them back, shows relevant proof and gives them a direct link to book another time

### Closes

- **[Pre-Call Research](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/988cd10a?md=ea816de206b0470a8bd07f5f8e42517e):** Five to fifteen minutes on their website, profiles and application, with two or three observations and relevant proof ready
- **[Sales Call Plan](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=0ffffe13343d433ea57c90f4f4562b19):** A repeatable structure from the introduction and questions through the demonstration, objections and close
- **[Sales Call Presentation](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=798b4bf06e1a4581b174f13c5c253fa1):** Explains what you are trying to accomplish, how you will do it, what it costs, how long it takes, the risks and how you will measure success
- **[Follow Up Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=1664da623aa544a2b921c81d2c771598):** Case studies, answers to common questions and industry insights sent over the following weeks to people who did not buy on the call
- **[One Pager + Contract + Payment](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=dd1106722d2e470998c85c56e60883fb):** All three prepared before the call and sent together in one clearly labeled message through email and text

### Sales Assets

- **[Lead Magnet](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/2818ecce?md=2f86bd19d6614f27a2ea25274049c68e):** A masterclass-level written resource that lays out your entire service in steps and follows the VAR formula
- **[6+ Hour Video Course](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/2818ecce?md=9dedc2a8ec3149ccbc9fe688f92cf837):** The lead magnet material taught in enough detail that someone understands what you do and can actually apply it
- **[3+ Hour Audit/Rebuild](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/2818ecce?md=74a5d1b432a04fd7866c6a82e16f284a):** A real business, campaign or piece of work relevant to your ICP, showing what you would change and why
- **[3+ Hour Case Study](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/2818ecce?md=58d6a714b8a74aa0a98237a2de66d1b4):** A real client result relevant to your ICP, from where they started through the results, with the actual evidence

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Turn off all the other ads in the winner's ad set, relabel the ad set as a winner and let it keep running.** Do not immediately add the winner to the scaling campaign. Some of those other ads may not have reached 100 outbound clicks. We are accepting that when we use this grouped testing method.
2. **Add the winning ad to the scaling campaign the next time you are going to change it anyway.** Adding it to the scaling campaign does not mean turning off the original ad set. Leave it running there until or unless it dies. By "dies," I mean it used to work, but now the CPA is no longer acceptable.

### Completion Audit

- Did you turn off all the other ads in the winner's ad set, relabel the ad set as a winner and let it keep running?
- Did you add the winning ad to the scaling campaign the next time you changed it?

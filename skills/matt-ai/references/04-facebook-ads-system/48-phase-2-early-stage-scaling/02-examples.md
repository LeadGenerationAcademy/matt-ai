### Offer Standardization

**Offer Pricing**

- $2,000 acquisition + $1,200 fulfillment = $3,200
- $3,200 × 2 = $6,400 required upfront collection
- Aim for $6,500 or more collected upfront, after fees

**Collecting Three Months Upfront**

- Recurring service at $2,500 per month, with the first three months sold together
- $2,500 × 3 months = $7,500 collected upfront
- 80% gross margin target, so fulfillment can cost 20% of that revenue: $7,500 × 20% = $1,500 to fulfill all three months
- $1,500 fulfillment + $2,000 acquisition = $3,500 total cost
- $7,500 collected ÷ $3,500 total cost = 2.14x before fees

### Different Ad Formats

1. **Static image:** Call out the ICP, explain the offer and tell them how to get started.
2. **Talking-head VSL:** Speak directly to the buyer about their problem, your offer and why they should work with you.
3. **UGC:** Have a creator deliver the script in a casual, phone-filmed style.
4. **Man on the street:** Interview business owners about the problem your offer solves.
5. **Podcast:** Record a conversation around the questions your ideal buyer would ask.
6. **Reddit comment response:** Put a relevant question on screen and answer it.
7. **TikTok comment response:** Use the comment-response layout to introduce the question your ad answers.
8. **Whiteboard/smartboard:** Draw out the problem, the numbers or your process.
9. **Loom screenshare:** Walk through an account, website or workflow and explain what you would improve.
10. **Slides screenshare:** Record yourself presenting a short deck that explains the business problem, your offer, relevant proof and the next step.
11. **Memes:** Build an ad around a frustration your exact ICP recognizes from running their business, then connect it to your offer.
12. **Voiceover with B Roll:** Record your ad script as a voiceover and pair it with footage of the work, team or process you are describing.
13. **Skits:** Act out a situation your ideal buyer deals with in their business, then show how your offer addresses it.
14. **Behind the scenes:** Film your team doing the work and explain how that process produces the result you sell.
15. **Customer testimonial:** Turn a real client's explanation of their business problem, experience and results into an ad for similar businesses.
16. **FAQ response:** Film yourself answering a question that could stop your ideal buyer from booking a call.
17. **Demonstration (if possible):** Demonstrate a specific task your product or service handles so the buyer can see how it would work in their business.
18. **Greenscreen:** Film yourself over an account screenshot, business result or process diagram while using it to explain and support your offer.

### Ads Workflow

**Workflow Example:**

- $3,000 per day in ad spend.
- 80%, or $2,400, goes into one large scaling campaign and ad set containing our proven ads.
- 20%, or $600 per day, goes to testing.
- Using that testing budget for our creative-production target, we want sixty new creatives per week.
- We make those creatives in five different formats and group the tests by format, with one ad set for each.
- Sixty creatives divided across five formats gives us twelve ads per ad set.
- $600 per day divided across five ad sets gives each one $120 per day.

All twelve ads run together inside their group. Facebook decides how to distribute that group's $120.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Pre-Call Preparation:** What to review before speaking with someone.
2. **Call Plan:** The questions, presentation, proof, objections and close. These are the Intro, Five Questions, Demo, Objection Handling and Closing/Next Steps tabs of your Sales Call Plan.
3. **Offer Details:** What we sell, what is included, pricing and payment options.
4. **Follow-Up:** What happens after the call, when and who handles it.
5. **Tracking:** The outcome, cash collected and next action.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Sales Call Plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SoluV8ZhrksZTlpNAfY1qYEensGSo5UJPfbD_-xEWlE/edit?usp=sharing) from [Step #2: Sales Call Plan](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=0ffffe13343d433ea57c90f4f4562b19).** If you have not created it yet, create it now. We already covered the individual pieces of the sales process. Now we need to put them together so someone else can follow them. Start by documenting what is working now. [in this skill: assets/templates/sales-call-plan-template/]
2. **Use your pre-call research from [Step #6C: Phase #4: Closes Plan + Assets](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/988cd10a?md=ea816de206b0470a8bd07f5f8e42517e) as the pre-call preparation.** This is what to review before speaking with someone: their website, relevant profiles and anything they have already told you.
3. **Check your call plan covers the questions, presentation, proof, objections and close.** Once multiple closers are involved, a shared process gives you something consistent to train and evaluate. They still need to understand the customer and handle the conversation, but they should not have to invent the pitch or figure out how to sell the offer from scratch.
4. **Use your standardized offer from [Step #3: Offer Standardization](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=c0c1679875a04e2eaf22d7a01a477b11) as the offer details.** This is what we sell, what is included, pricing and payment options. We want the offer, funnel and sales process to be so good that our closers are as close to cashiers as possible.
5. **Use your [Step #10A: Post-Call Follow Up Flow](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=1664da623aa544a2b921c81d2c771598) and CRM as the follow-up.** This covers what happens after the call, when it happens and who handles it.
6. **Track the outcome and next action of every call in your [CRM](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/b799ebc8?md=1cc8463ece8d482cbaef68c8f16cf7ac).** Log the cash collected in the [Scaling Metrics Tracking Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1AS4hNqUVefgaCe1szr12kwuVLJVB9sUwlrg5EUCAkLk/edit?usp=sharing) you made a copy of in [Step #5: More Sophisticated Metrics Tracking Setup](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=6297cfd3115b49608e1d85f725187dd7). [in this skill: assets/templates/scaling-metrics-tracking-template/]
7. **Add examples of good calls.** A new closer needs to hear what this actually looks like.
8. **Check a new closer can follow the process before handing them leads.** Give them the process, show them good examples and let them practice. That is what makes hiring more closers something we can repeat. If you are the only person who can sell the offer, your calendar becomes the constraint.
9. **Review every closer's calls against the process.** This is how you know what needs coaching. If everyone sells differently, it becomes much harder to understand why one person performs better than another.
10. **Use your recordings to improve the process.** This will change and become more sophisticated over time. As you take more calls, you will find better explanations, recurring objections and places where people get confused. Use that information to improve the process and training.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Watch for the point where something in the business is fundamentally broken.** This phase ends when something in the business is seriously broken, and if you keep scaling, the whole thing will collapse. We are talking about something far beyond needing a better SOP or another closer. You cannot just keep hiring people and plugging them into what you already have. The way you currently operate cannot handle the volume, and adding more people is no longer enough to maintain results, control costs or keep delivery from falling apart.
2. **Stop increasing ad spend when you reach that point.** Hold it where it is, or reduce it to a level you can actually handle while you fix the underlying problem.
3. **Rebuild the parts of the business that are broken.** Parts of the business need to be rebuilt before you can keep growing. This is where we move into [Phase #3: Transitory Scaling](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/787ac3b9?md=da092fa960d34cf28dd482bf4e5b9fef)

### Completion Audit

- Are you watching for the point where something in the business is fundamentally broken?
- Did you stop increasing ad spend when you reached that point?
- Are you rebuilding the parts of the business that are broken?

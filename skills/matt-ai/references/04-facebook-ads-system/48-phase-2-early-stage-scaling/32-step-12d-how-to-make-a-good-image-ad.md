### Framework & Criteria

1. **Instant Clarity:** They know what the offer is and who it is for within one second. Literally instantly.
2. **High Contrast:** The ad stands out in the feed, and the important information is easy to read.
3. **Overcome Roughly Three Objections:** Use your FAQ and objection research to address the things most likely to stop them from clicking.
4. **Clear CTA:** Tell them what to do next.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make the offer and who it is for clear within one second.** Literally instantly.
2. **Use high contrast.** The ad needs to stand out in the feed, and the important information needs to be easy to read.
3. **Overcome roughly three objections using your [Top 30 FAQs](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/ff36840f?md=04cebeffa75f415797f223580e3a67a2).** Use your FAQ and objection research to address the things most likely to stop them from clicking.
4. **Add a clear CTA.** Tell them what to do next. With all ads, but especially static images, we should not be trying to sell our entire service inside the ad. That is what the page, VSL and sales process are for.
5. **Convey enough of the Value Formula from [Step #2: Amplify Your Offer](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/840e7e5f?md=4e90242bfbe24f26b575d3c6e89615c4) to make the next step attractive.** Show the result they want, why they should believe they can get it, how quickly they can get value and how little work it requires from them. Use the offer, proof and supporting statements to communicate those things. We have already done the thinking behind the offer. Now we need to make that value easy to see.

### Completion Audit

- Is the offer and who it is for clear within one second?
- Did you use high contrast?
- Did you overcome roughly three objections using your Top 30 FAQs?
- Did you add a clear CTA?
- Did you convey enough of the Value Formula from Step #2: Amplify Your Offer to make the next step attractive?

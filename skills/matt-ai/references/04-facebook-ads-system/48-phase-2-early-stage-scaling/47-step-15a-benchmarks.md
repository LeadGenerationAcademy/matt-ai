### Framework & Criteria

**From Phase #1:**

**1. Clicks**

Look in your ad account.

Benchmarks

- CPM → $25-70
- Outbound CTR → 1-2%+

Improvements

- Better hooks
- Better bodies
- More proof, faster
- Clearer ICP + offer, faster
- Cleaner ad design
- Cleaner visual hierarchy

**2. Calls**

Compare these to your number of outbound clicks.

Benchmarks

- Application Rate → 6-10%
- Booked Call Rate → 3-5%+

Improvements

- Better above the fold
- Cleaner visual hierarchy
- Better VSL
- Greater congruency
- Better rest of sales page
- More proof
- More calendar spots available
- More calendar spots, sooner

**3. Shows**

Compare shown calls to calls booked.

Benchmarks

- Show Rate → 50-70%+

Improvements

- Shorter maximum booking time
- Better pre-call email flow
- Clearer thank you page
- Better selfie video
- Faster speed to lead
- Better pre-call assets
- More email and SMS reminders
- Better email deliverability for the calendar invite

**4. Closes**

Compare closed calls to calls booked. At this stage we can switch to live calls later.

Benchmarks

- Close Rate → 15-30%

Improvements

- Better show rate
- Better pre-call framing
- Better introduction
- Better questions
- Better demo
- Better objection handling
- Better ways to ask for the close
- Book a meeting from a meeting
- More proof
- Better follow up flow
- More consistent follow up flow
- Clearer proposal, payment link, contract, next steps

**From Phase #2:**

1. **CPM:** $25–70
2. **Outbound CTR:** 1–2%+
3. **Application Rate:** 6–10% of outbound clicks
4. **Booked Call Rate:** 3–5%+ of outbound clicks
5. **Show Rate:** 50–70%+ of booked calls
6. **Qualified Call Rate:** 90%+ of live calls
7. **Close Rate:** 15–30% of live calls

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Data & Benchmarks Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nqmZGoj9L2_EvhT8U54mRO6i6sVTtxvR4dnuqUfatAM/edit?tab=t.94eypbfxxnvu#heading=h.9uulxnrwqoig).** This is a tab in the same document as your Unit Economics Model Template. These are the benchmarks we will use to figure out where things need work. [in this skill: assets/templates/format-copywriting-bot-template/]
2. **Keep using the clicks, calls, shows and closes framework from Phase 1.** In this phase you are adding a clearer view of qualification, fulfillment costs and upfront cash.
3. **Measure your close rate against live calls.** Now that we have closers, we are measuring close rate against calls that actually happened. Keep that separate from show rate. We want to know whether people are showing up and whether the closer can sell to them once they do.
4. **Check your metrics against the Phase #2 benchmarks.** These are the seven numbers in the Framework & Criteria above.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Data & Benchmarks Template?
- Are you still using the clicks, calls, shows and closes framework from Phase 1?
- Are you measuring your close rate against live calls?
- Did you check your metrics against the Phase #2 benchmarks?

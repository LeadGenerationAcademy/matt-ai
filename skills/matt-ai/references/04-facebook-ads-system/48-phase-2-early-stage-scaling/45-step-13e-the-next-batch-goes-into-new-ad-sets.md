### Framework & Criteria

1. **Vertical Scaling:** Group by messaging angle. "Get out of sales" could be one ad set, while "add a second acquisition channel" could be another.
2. **Horizontal Scaling:** Group by the format or presenter demographic you are testing. That could mean separate ad sets for talking-head videos and UGC, or for presenters from different age groups. Keep the proven message consistent across those variations.

### Example

**Workflow Example:**

- $3,000 per day in ad spend.
- 80%, or $2,400, goes into one large scaling campaign and ad set containing our proven ads.
- 20%, or $600 per day, goes to testing.
- Using that testing budget for our creative-production target, we want sixty new creatives per week.
- We make those creatives in five different formats and group the tests by format, with one ad set for each.
- Sixty creatives divided across five formats gives us twelve ads per ad set.
- $600 per day divided across five ad sets gives each one $120 per day.

All twelve ads run together inside their group. Facebook decides how to distribute that group's $120.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Decide what you want to test.** Testing means launching new batches of creatives in new ad sets.
2. **Group the creatives and create the new ad sets.** I recommend grouping each ad set around the main thing you are testing. At lower budgets this is a balancing act. You want as few ad sets as possible, but continually adding new creatives to your main ad sets can disrupt delivery, and many of the new ads may barely spend. Do not put the new batch into the ad set containing the winner you decided to leave running.
3. **Set each ad set's budget, upload the corresponding ads and launch.** The retained winners still count toward our total spend, so account for them when deciding how much budget is available for the next batch. Keep the testing structure simple enough that you can give each test a meaningful budget.
4. **Review the results using [Step #13C: Identifying Winners](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=4508780102844f4ab278030ab05e5301).** Review each ad against your target CPA and sample size, and check whether its booked calls turn into clients at an acceptable cost.
5. **Handle each winner using [Step #13D: What To Do With A Winner](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=59a28b2cde634a63b8f8c27a020fe7d5), then use the results to decide what to produce next.** When we identify a winner, we turn off the other ads in its group, relabel the ad set and leave it running. Repeat this as you produce new batches of creatives.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Reuse the messaging from your [Format #1: Static Image](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=549796d4da9c4825a4933bfd7c9cd5c5) ads.** This is basically a static image ad over B-roll. The main communication still comes from the text, so we can reuse much of the messaging from a static image.
2. **Choose your B-roll footage.** You can use a wide variety of B-roll. Relevant footage is better when we have it, but the background does not need to become a complicated production.
3. **Loop seven or eight seconds of the footage.** This gives us a video version of the message to use in suitable video placements.
4. **Keep the ICP callout and offer readable while the footage moves behind them.** The viewer should not need to watch several loops to piece together the message.
5. **Save the text layout and loop setup.** This is so we can change the copy and footage efficiently.

### Completion Audit

- Did you reuse the messaging from your Format #1: Static Image ads?
- Did you choose your B-roll footage?
- Did you loop seven or eight seconds of the footage?
- Are the ICP callout and offer readable while the footage moves behind them?
- Did you save the text layout and loop setup?

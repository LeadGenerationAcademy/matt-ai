### Example

ICP: Agency owners between $500k - $5 million per year

1. You hired a closer, but you still have to join every sales call.
2. Your main referral partner stopped sending you opportunities.
3. Your ads were working until you increased the budget.
4. You hired a setter, but your calendar is filling with people who cannot afford the offer.
5. You lost a major client and realized how dependent you were on waiting for referrals.

These are specific situations within the same ICP. Someone who has never experienced the situation may ignore the ad. Someone dealing with it right now may feel like you are describing exactly what is happening in their business.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Find at least 40 niche angles.** These are the opposite of your workhorse angles. A lot of the time there are great niche angles that resonate strongly with people who have those particular problems, circumstances and outcomes, but that situation does not apply to most of the market. They have their place and can bring in clients even if the audience for each individual angle is relatively small. They do not necessarily have a huge TAM.
2. **Expect a couple dozen of them to end up working.** Not all 40 are going to work or be successful. We are trying to build a couple dozen useful niche angles over time, and we should not assume the first 40 will get us there.
3. **Keep researching and testing until you have enough that work.** Add new niche angles to your list as you find them, and keep going until you have a couple dozen that bring in clients.

### Completion Audit

- Did you find at least 40 niche angles?
- Do you expect a couple dozen of them to end up working?
- Are you researching and testing until you have enough that work?

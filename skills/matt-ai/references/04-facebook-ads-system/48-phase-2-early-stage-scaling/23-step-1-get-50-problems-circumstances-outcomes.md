### Framework & Criteria

1. **Problems:** Something is going wrong that they want fixed.
2. **Circumstances:** A situation they currently find themselves in, almost always a negative one.
3. **Outcomes:** Something they want to achieve.

### Example

**Problems**

1. Client acquisition rests on a single uncontrollable channel
2. They cannot forecast revenue with any confidence
3. They are the constraint on their own growth

**Circumstances**

1. Most of my clients still come from referrals
2. My revenue swings up and down every month
3. I am wearing every hat in this business

**Outcomes**

1. Get off referral dependency for good
2. Escape the feast-or-famine cycle
3. Stop being the bottleneck in my business

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Build a list of at least 50 problems, circumstances and desired outcomes for the exact customer you want.** Each one gives you a potential angle to test. People inside the same ICP can care about different things. One person may recognize the problem immediately. Another may have already solved it or have a different priority. If we only talk about one problem, we are giving everyone else very little reason to pay attention. That is why you need multiple angles, even when you are selling the same offer to the same type of customer.
2. **Pull what you already have from the [Top 25 PCO Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1s4COJJQxCssA8PMcHL5jKmwJAthZdHQFA2vXywMAVws/edit?tab=t.w3ckyts5xisl) you made in [Step #3: Create Top 25 PCO Document](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/05248c5b?md=4e8e17f34e47499fa2e6477a5b8369ab).** This is where the ICP work from earlier becomes useful. If you have gone through the Messaging course, some of this may already be built. That step has you research and list 25 problems, circumstances and desired outcomes experienced by your specific level of ICP. [in this skill: assets/templates/top-25-pco-template/]
3. **Include a mixture of broad and specific angles.** We want broad things that many people identify with and specific things that matter to a smaller group.
4. **Check who would identify with the angle before turning it into an ad.** Every angle still needs to make sense for the customer you want. An established agency owner may want to add a second acquisition channel or get out of sales. Messaging about getting a first client speaks to someone at a different stage. It also needs to connect to something our offer actually helps them solve.

### Completion Audit

- Did you build a list of at least 50 problems, circumstances and desired outcomes for the exact customer you want?
- Did you pull what you already have from your Top 25 PCO Document?
- Did you include a mixture of broad and specific angles?
- Did you check who would identify with the angle before turning it into an ad?

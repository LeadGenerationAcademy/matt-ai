### Example

1. **Agency owners:** "Want more clients without relying on referrals? We run your ads and bring qualified inquiries directly to you."
2. **Dental practices:** "Want more patients without relying on referrals? We run your ads and bring qualified inquiries directly to you."
3. **Law firms:** "Want more cases without relying on referrals? We run your ads and bring qualified inquiries directly to you."
4. **Roofing companies:** "Want more jobs without relying on referrals? We run your ads and bring qualified inquiries directly to you."
5. **Coaches:** "Want more students without relying on referrals? We run your ads and bring qualified inquiries directly to you."

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep almost the entire script and change a few words.** If you sell lead generation, those words might be "clients," "patients," "cases," "customers," "deals," "retainers," "projects" or "students."
2. **Use the version that fits the industry and customers you have chosen to serve.** The person watching recognizes the language used in their business. "Patients" speaks to a medical provider, "cases" speaks to a law firm. That changes who the ad feels relevant to and can also influence who Facebook shows it to. This kind of goes against the whole standardization and industry thing that is so important in this course, but there are niche examples where it makes sense.

### Completion Audit

- Did you keep almost the entire script and change a few words?
- Did you use the version that fits the industry and customers you have chosen to serve?

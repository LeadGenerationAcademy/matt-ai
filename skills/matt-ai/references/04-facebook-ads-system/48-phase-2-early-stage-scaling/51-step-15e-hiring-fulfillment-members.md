### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Start hiring the next fulfillment member when the team reaches roughly 75% capacity.** They need time to learn the work before you need them handling a full workload.
2. **Keep watching what happens as client volume increases, and stop increasing ad spend if things are beginning to break.** Are you maintaining results? Is delivery taking longer? Are costs going up? If things start to break, hold ad spend where it is until fulfillment can handle the volume.
3. **Make fulfillment easy to repeat using your [Step #8: Fulfillment Framework](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=a8bce73726914145bb25fab87f592275).** We want fulfillment to be so dialed in that someone can come in and follow the process using our templates, training and technology. Document how the work gets done. Give people the assets they need to do it, and update the process when you find a better way. This is why we spent time standardizing the offer. As we keep delivering the same thing, we should get better, faster and less expensive at doing it. That is what makes adding another fulfillment member useful.

### Completion Audit

- Did you start hiring the next fulfillment member when the team reached roughly 75% capacity?
- Are you watching what happens as client volume increases, and did you stop increasing ad spend if things began to break?
- Did you make fulfillment easy to repeat using your fulfillment framework?

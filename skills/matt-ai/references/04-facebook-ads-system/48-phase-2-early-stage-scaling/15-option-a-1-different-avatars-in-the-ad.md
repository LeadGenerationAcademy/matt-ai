### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Have different people recreate the same ad.** I am a 30-year-old white guy, and many of the people who respond to my ads are similar to me.
2. **Start with presenters of different ages, genders and races.** Testing other presenters gives you a way to see whether the same message resonates with other people in the market. You can also change their appearance.
3. **Give them the proven script and have them deliver it naturally.** This is how you start testing other presenters without changing what already works.
4. **Keep the message and offer consistent.** This is how you see how the different presentations perform.

### Completion Audit

- Did you have different people recreate the same ad?
- Did you start with presenters of different ages, genders and races?
- Did you give them the proven script and have them deliver it naturally?
- Did you keep the message and offer consistent?

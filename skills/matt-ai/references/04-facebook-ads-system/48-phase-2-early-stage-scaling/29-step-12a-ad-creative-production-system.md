### Framework & Criteria

Choose the message → Write → Design or record → Edit → Prepare for testing

**Target: 1 Creative/Week Per $10/Day Budget**

- $100 per day: 10 creatives per week.
- $500 per day: 50 creatives per week.
- $1,000 per day: 100 creatives per week.
- $10,000 per day: 1,000 creatives per week.

### Example

A creative means one finished ad variation. Five headlines across three designs gives you 15 creatives.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Build a production system.** Both scaling approaches require one, and whichever approach we use, the ad creation system will be basically the same. For horizontal scaling, we give it the original ad and specify what we want to change. For vertical scaling, we give it the new angle and the relevant material from our PCO research. The presenter still needs a script. The editor still needs footage, proof and instructions. The designer still needs copy and assets. We should be able to use the same people and production processes for both.
2. **Choose five to seven ad formats you have the will to go all in on.** This will depend on your offer, capabilities and what you are actually willing to produce. Think about producing these every week. Can we get the footage? Can someone else edit them? Can we reuse the setup and templates? We want formats we could eventually produce at industrial scale. Five formats is plenty and will allow us to get tons of variety in a cost-efficient and time-efficient way.
3. **Prioritize quantity and use the repetitions and results to improve the quality.** The top advertisers are producing substantially more creative than almost everyone else. They get more chances to succeed because they are testing more angles, formats, presenters and ways of explaining their offers. They also get more practice and more feedback, so they are making more attempts while improving the quality of those attempts. The production system needs to let us do that consistently, every week.
4. **Plan approximately one new creative per week for every $10 in daily ad budget.** Fairly oversimplified, but this is a good production target to keep in mind at this stage. You will need to pound creatives of different types here. A lot of these will come from taking something that already works and applying the horizontal scaling methods. We do not need one hundred completely unrelated ideas.
5. **Expect one to three ads out of every hundred to become winners.** As a rough planning assumption, those one to three ads may get almost all of the spend. This is educated guessing. We use our research, previous winners and understanding of the customer to make better guesses, but we still need to run the ads. That is why we need to produce a lot of creative. 1-3 winners make all of our efforts worth it.
6. **Decide which of those creatives to test based on the budget available.** Production gives you options, testing tells you which ones deserve more spend. When we are first scaling, we can use basically our full budget for this calculation. Once we begin finding our winners, we will break out into scaling vs. testing campaigns, and we will usually base this off our testing budget.
7. **Make the process for each ad repeatable.** Take an ad you understand, break down how it was made and make that process repeatable. What does the presenter need? What does the editor need? Which parts can we template? Which parts still require your judgment? If you have to personally explain everything again for every ad, we have more work to do on the system. The experience and systems-level thinking you get here is even more valuable than the first version of the system you produce.
8. **Improve the instructions, templates and individual steps that keep creating extra work.** The system you build here will change as your volume increases and you figure out better ways to do the work. You will probably have to completely redo parts of it later. That is fine. We need something that works at this stage, and we need the experience of building and running it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you build a production system?
- Did you choose five to seven ad formats you have the will to go all in on?
- Are you prioritizing quantity and using the repetitions and results to improve the quality?
- Did you plan approximately one new creative per week for every $10 in daily ad budget?
- Do you expect one to three ads out of every hundred to become winners?
- Did you decide which of those creatives to test based on the budget available?
- Did you make the process for each ad repeatable?
- Are you improving the instructions, templates and individual steps that keep creating extra work?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Start hiring your next closer when the existing sales team reaches roughly 75% capacity.** You need time to find someone, train them and get them taking calls before everyone else is completely full. Waiting until you have more calls than the team can handle means you are trying to hire while already having a problem.
2. **Lead score your calls.** This is so you know which opportunities are strongest. Use the ICP and qualification criteria you have already established.
3. **Send your best leads to your trained closers.** Those are the people who already know the offer and how to sell it.
4. **Start new closers with lower-scoring calls from the existing team's spillage.** Do not hand a new closer an entire calendar all at once. Let them take calls, review the recordings and work on the parts they are getting wrong.
5. **Increase their volume as they become capable of handling it.** As they improve, you can give them more volume and better opportunities. We want to develop another person who can consistently sell the offer. The handoff should happen gradually.
6. **Keep improving your [Step #7: Sales Framework](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=bcded5657e164fbcb434891190654af0) from the recordings.** We want our sales process to be so dialed in that we can put someone into it, train them and have them follow it. That means having a clear process for the questions, presentation, objections, close and follow-up. The closer should be coming into a sales process you have already built and improved. You will get nowhere if every closer does whatever they want. You need something consistent to train people on and something consistent to evaluate them against.

### Completion Audit

- Did you start hiring your next closer when the existing sales team reached roughly 75% capacity?
- Did you lead score your calls?
- Are you sending your best leads to your trained closers?
- Did you start new closers with lower-scoring calls from the existing team's spillage?
- Are you increasing their volume as they become capable of handling it?
- Are you improving your sales framework from the recordings?

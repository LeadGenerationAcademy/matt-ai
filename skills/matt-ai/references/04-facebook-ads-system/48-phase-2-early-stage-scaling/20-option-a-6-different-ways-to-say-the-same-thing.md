### Example

- "Want more clients without relying on referrals?"
- "Want to get clients without waiting for referrals?"
- "Looking for a way to bring in clients beyond referrals?"

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep the meaning of your proven message.** These are different ways of describing the same thing. We are still speaking to the same buyer about the same problem.
2. **Change how you phrase the message.** This gives us wording variations without needing to change industries.
3. **Keep the underlying problem and desired outcome the same.** Changing the underlying problem or desired outcome would take us into [Option B: Vertical Scaling](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=a3fdf1ee19424ed3a90738d7bc35a8f8), which comes next.

### Completion Audit

- Did you keep the meaning of your proven message?
- Did you change how you phrase the message?
- Did you keep the underlying problem and desired outcome the same?

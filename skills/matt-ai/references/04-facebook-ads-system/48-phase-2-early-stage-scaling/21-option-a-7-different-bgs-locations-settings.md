### Example

- Sitting at a desk.
- Standing in an office.
- In front of a whiteboard.
- Walking outside.
- At the location where the work happens.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose settings that make sense for the business and message.** These give us more ways to present the same proven ad without needing to develop another angle.
2. **Have the presenter deliver the same ad in each setting.** We can keep the script and offer consistent while changing where it is filmed and what the viewer sees around them.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose settings that make sense for the business and message?
- Did you have the presenter deliver the same ad in each setting?

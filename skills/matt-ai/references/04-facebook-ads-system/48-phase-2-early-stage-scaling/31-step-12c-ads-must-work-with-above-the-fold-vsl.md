### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make sure your above the fold continues what the ad started.** The ad creates an expectation. The page and VSL need to continue explaining that same thing.
2. **Clone the landing page for each winning ad angle and match the headlines.** Someone who clicks because of one particular problem should immediately recognize that the page addresses it. We should not make them search through the entire page to find the connection. The offer can stay the same while the headline and opening explanation change.
3. **Check the opening of your VSL supports the reason the person clicked.** It needs to continue from the ad, rather than immediately moving into an unrelated explanation.

### Completion Audit

- Does your above the fold continue what the ad started?
- Did you clone the landing page for each winning ad angle and match the headlines?
- Does the opening of your VSL support the reason the person clicked?

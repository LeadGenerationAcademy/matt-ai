# Action Steps

Course: Phase #2: Early-Stage Scaling
System: Facebook Ads System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=e4098a0e905841d9aab63bf3b613a7c7

---
The Theory explains that the actual Facebook Ads are one of the least important parts of scaling Facebook Ads. Your primary constraint will normally be your unit economics and the business underneath the ads, and every time you double spending, your ads and funnel need to get better to keep the same performance.

This course is where you actually scale from your first 10 clients until your current business model approaches its structural limit.

You will standardize your offer, expand on the ads already working and build the systems underneath them. Then you will choose a scaling model, produce and test creative every week, and use your numbers to decide spend and hiring until something in the business is fundamentally broken.

Do not keep increasing ad spend once sales or fulfillment cannot handle the volume, and do not judge your campaigns by anything other than whether your CPA supports your unit economics.

### Table of Contents

1. **Make Sure You Have These Set Up:** Confirm you have 10 clients from paid ads and every calls, shows, closes and sales asset in place, because the ad only gets someone into the process.
2. **Add Application To Increase Friction:** Put your application back in so you have more control over who reaches the calendar.
3. **Offer Standardization:** Write down the one offer you will sell repeatedly and price it to collect at least 2x your combined acquisition and fulfillment costs upfront.
4. **Expand On Ads That Are Currently Working:** Turn your winning ad into 150 talking-head variations, so you have something to scale immediately while you build the rest.
5. **More Sophisticated Metrics Tracking Setup:** Reverse engineer your goal and track your funnel and money every day so you know what it takes to hit it.
6. **Schedule Meetings:** Review fulfillment, acquisition, unit economics and metrics every week, with a named owner for each part of the business.
7. **Sales Framework:** Put your sales process together so a new closer can be plugged into it without you handling every opportunity.
8. **Fulfillment Framework:** Document, templatize and train every step of delivery so fulfillment gets quicker and cheaper while maintaining results.
9. **Unit Economics Model:** Work out what you collect, what it costs to acquire and fulfill each customer, and whether you hit 2x, 80% gross margin and $250 per hour.
10. **Choose Scaling Model:** Decide whether to begin with horizontal or vertical scaling based on how much trust your offer requires.
11. **Horizontal Scaling:** Expand one proven ad into many versions while the problem, offer and reason to buy stay the same.
12. **Different Avatars In The Ad:** Have different people recreate your proven ad to see whether the message resonates with other people in the market.
13. **Different Ad Formats:** Adapt the message that worked into other formats while keeping the original angle and offer recognizable.
14. **Different Ad Designs:** Give the same message a different visual presentation so the design is the thing being tested.
15. **Different Ad Concepts:** Frame the same message six different ways to get more versions of the ad.
16. **Different Keywords:** Swap a few words so the person watching recognizes the language used in their business.
17. **Different Ways To Say The Same Thing:** Change how you phrase the message while keeping its meaning.
18. **Different BGs/Locations/Settings:** Film the same ad in different settings to present it in more ways without a new angle.
19. **Vertical Scaling:** Develop new messaging around the different things your ICP actually cares about.
20. **Get 50+ Problems, Circumstances, Outcomes:** Build a list of at least 50 potential angles for the exact customer you want.
21. **Analyze Recordings + Research:** Pull what your ICP actually said from your sales calls and the internet into one research document.
22. **Identify Their Language:** Keep how people describe each situation and sort your angles into workhorse and niche angles.
23. **Get 10+ Potential Workhorse Angles:** Find at least 10 angles that could apply to a large portion of your ICP and support substantial spend.
24. **Get 40+ Potential Niche Angles:** Find at least 40 angles that resonate strongly with people in a particular situation.
25. **Hiring & Team:** Hire the people who take work off your plate, from an assistant through to a head of sales.
26. **Ad Creative Production System:** Build a system that produces roughly one creative per week for every $10 per day in budget.
27. **Editing Framework:** Create a reusable editing workflow that calls out the ICP, adds captions, cuts dead space and adds proof.
28. **Ads Must Work With Above The Fold + VSL:** Make sure your page and VSL continue whatever your ad started.
29. **How To Make A Good Image Ad:** Make the offer clear within one second with high contrast, roughly three objections answered and a clear CTA.
30. **How To Make A Good Video Ad:** Get the ICP and offer in within one second, proof within five and everything within fifteen.
31. **Setting Up Format Creation Systems:** Give each format a copywriting framework, an editing or design framework and a copywriting bot.
32. **Copywriting Bot For Each Format:** Set up a bot that adapts your original ad into each format without changing the offer or claims.
33. **Static Image:** Produce static image ads from editable templates with a clear ICP callout, high contrast and clarity.
34. **Looping B Roll:** Put your static image messaging over seven or eight seconds of looping footage.
35. **UGC:** Present the offer in a casual, phone-filmed style that still gets the important details across.
36. **Talking Head VSL:** Explain the offer directly to the camera using the opening structure from your video ads.
37. **Loom + Screenshare:** Record a demonstration of the offer so the buyer can see what they would receive.
38. **Scaling vs. Testing Campaigns:** Split your budget between proven ads and new creatives, and count everything you spend.
39. **ABO Tests:** Test each batch in ABO ad sets grouped by the variable you are testing.
40. **Identifying Winners:** Judge each ad at around 100 outbound clicks by its cost per booked call and whether those calls close.
41. **What To Do With A Winner:** Leave the winner running in its own ad set and add it to the scaling campaign at your next change.
42. **The Next Batch Goes Into New Ad Sets:** Launch every new batch in new ad sets so the testing process keeps repeating.
43. **Winning Ad Loop System:** Turn each winner into new concepts and formats, test them and use what keeps working to make the next batch.
44. **Benchmarks:** Check your metrics against the Phase #2 benchmarks to find where things need work.
45. **If This, Then That:** Match whatever metric is off to the fix for it, starting with your CPA.
46. **Ad Spend:** Increase, hold or decrease spend every week based on your unit economics multiple.
47. **Hiring Closers:** Start hiring at roughly 75% capacity and ramp new closers up with lower-scoring calls.
48. **Hiring Fulfillment Members:** Start hiring at roughly 75% capacity and stop increasing spend if delivery begins to break.
49. **The End Of This Phase:** Stop increasing ad spend once something is fundamentally broken, and rebuild it.

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Assistant:** Hire now. Their primary job is to get you back time.
2. **Editor:** Hire now. Turns your footage into finished video ads.
3. **Designer:** Hire now. Handles static image ads and branding-related work.
4. **Setter:** Hire once you consistently get 8+ applications submitted per day.
5. **Closer:** Hire once you are getting 25+ calls from paid ads per week and are confident you can scale up.
6. **Head Of Fulfillment:** Best if you already have one. Pause during this phase if you don't.
7. **Head Of Sales:** Hire once you pass five closers.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Get at least one person with a brain on your team.** This is someone who can follow the process, figure things out and get work finished without asking you about every little thing. You will still be responsible for this, but the team needs to take stuff off your plate. We have already started building the workflows, SOPs and training. Now we need people who can take responsibility for that work. Some of these responsibilities will still be yours at this stage.
2. **Hire an assistant and give them repeatable tasks using the checklists and training process from [Step #8: Fulfillment Framework](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=a8bce73726914145bb25fab87f592275).** This person will be a jack of all trades. Their primary job is to get you back time. They can help with uploading ads, organizing files, updating metrics and keeping track of what needs to get done. Start with tasks you already know how to do. As they get better, you can give them more responsibility.
3. **Hire an editor and give them the script, footage and examples of what the finished ad should look like.** This person turns your footage into finished video ads. They need to understand the message so their editing supports what we are trying to say. We will build repeatable editing workflows for the formats we choose in Step #12B: Editing Framework.
4. **Hire a designer and give them the copy, assets and references they need.** This person handles static image ads and branding-related work. They should be able to take a proven message and turn it into different visual variations. We want a repeatable process for producing these ads. You can also use ChatGPT for parts of this work. Someone still needs to check the finished design, wording and details before it goes live.
5. **Hire a setter once you consistently get 8+ applications submitted per day, and make sure they know who qualifies, what to say and when to hand someone over to a closer.** This person handles the contact between someone becoming a lead and attending a sales call. That includes responding, following up, confirming appointments and bringing calls forward when someone is ready to talk sooner. We care about qualified people showing up. Filling a calendar with people who cannot buy creates more work for the sales team.
6. **Hire a closer once you are getting 25+ calls from paid ads per week, and train them on the process from [Step #7: Sales Framework](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=bcded5657e164fbcb434891190654af0).** Only hire if you are also confident you can scale up. If that is the case now, hire at this stage. If not, we will come back to it later. They should be following the sales framework we already built: preparation, call plan, presentation, follow-up and tracking. Use your own successful calls as training material, then review their calls so you can see where they need help.
7. **Get a head of fulfillment if you don't already have one, and meet with them in the weekly fulfillment meeting from [Step #6: Schedule Meetings](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=cf345ff3f2894f73a2a5049cabe97764).** You will need to pause during this phase if you don't have one, as eventually, you will not be able to oversee fulfillment and marketing. This person is responsible for making sure the team delivers what we sold. They manage the workload, check the quality, handle delivery problems and train the people doing the work. They should keep improving the workflow, templates and SOPs so fulfillment gets quicker and cheaper while maintaining results. Use the weekly meeting to review fulfillment costs, capacity and where the process needs work.
8. **Hire a head of sales once you pass five closers.** As the sales team grows, someone needs to manage the setters and closers. That means reviewing calls, coaching people, checking follow-up and making sure everyone follows the sales process. They need to know where opportunities are being lost and what the team needs to improve. You may handle this yourself initially. Eventually, we want someone responsible for the sales team so you are not personally managing every rep and every opportunity.

### Completion Audit

- Did you get at least one person with a brain on your team?
- Did you hire an assistant and give them repeatable tasks?
- Did you hire an editor and give them the script, footage and examples of what the finished ad should look like?
- Did you hire a designer and give them the copy, assets and references they need?
- Did you hire a setter once you consistently got 8+ applications submitted per day, and do they know who qualifies, what to say and when to hand someone over?
- Did you hire a closer once you were getting 25+ calls from paid ads per week, and train them on the sales framework?
- Do you have a head of fulfillment, and are you meeting with them weekly?
- Did you hire a head of sales once you passed five closers?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Decide whether your offer is broad and mass-market or more specific and high-ticket.** You are choosing which model to begin with. You will eventually use both in Mature Scaling, which is the advanced phase. But you will often get through this Early-Stage Scaling phase with just one of the two. This determines whether our next work is expanding a proven message or developing additional messaging.
2. **Begin with [Option A: Horizontal Scaling](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=d129dc0d550147e3a18ae6a2eb3c5073) if the offer requires relatively little trust.** Horizontal scaling takes a proven message and changes its presentation, format or audience language. The underlying message stays the same. We are finding more ways to present something that already works. If we already have a message that works for a large number of people, there is a lot we can do with it. We can have different people present it, use different creative formats and change the wording to fit different people in the market. The offer and the main reason to buy stay consistent.
3. **Begin with [Option B: Vertical Scaling](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=a3fdf1ee19424ed3a90738d7bc35a8f8) if the offer requires more explanation and trust.** Vertical scaling develops new messaging around different problems, circumstances and desired outcomes. We are finding additional reasons someone in our ICP would want the offer. People inside the same ICP can care about different things. One person may immediately identify with a problem, while another has already solved it or has a different priority. The ICP stays the same. We are giving more of those people a reason to pay attention because the message speaks to something they actually care about.
4. **Keep the end goal in mind whichever model you choose.** We want a repeatable way to produce more winning ads that bring us the right customers at an acquisition cost that supports the unit economics from [Step #9: Unit Economics Model](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=d8ac5ae3859646b7a8cb60d848653418). As we increase spending, we need enough creative that can continue doing that. We will test the ads, identify what produces customers and use those results to decide what to make next.

### Completion Audit

- Did you decide whether your offer is broad and mass-market or more specific and high-ticket?
- Did you begin with horizontal scaling if the offer requires relatively little trust?
- Did you begin with vertical scaling if the offer requires more explanation and trust?
- Are you keeping the end goal in mind for the model you chose?

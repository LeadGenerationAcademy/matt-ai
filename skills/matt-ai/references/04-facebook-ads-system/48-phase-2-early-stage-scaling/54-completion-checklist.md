# Completion Checklist

Course: Phase #2: Early-Stage Scaling
System: Facebook Ads System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/031026d9?md=c0d8d4f7d50b4a82b19d866ef3bb7d31

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Setup

- You have at least 10 clients from Facebook Ads with your current offer or a substantially similar offer.
- You have your Calls assets set up:
  - Sales Page Above The Fold
  - VSL
  - Application
  - Rest Of Sales Page
  - Welcome Flow
  - Call Booking Page
  - Top 30 FAQs
- You have your Shows assets set up:
  - Thank You Page
  - Pre-Call Email & SMS Reminders
  - Pre-Call Flow
  - Selfie Video
  - Appointment Setting Reach Out
  - Loom + One-Pager
  - Retargeting Ads
  - No Show Flow
- You have your Closes assets set up:
  - Pre-Call Research
  - Sales Call Plan
  - Sales Call Presentation
  - Follow Up Flow
  - One Pager + Contract + Payment
- You have your Sales Assets set up:
  - Lead Magnet
  - 6+ Hour Video Course
  - 3+ Hour Audit/Rebuild
  - 3+ Hour Case Study
- You have added your application back in, and it checks for budget, authority, need and timing.
- You have an application rate of 6-10% of outbound clicks, and at least 90% of the people attending calls fit your qualification criteria.

### Offer Standardization

- You have written down the specific ICP, result and deliverables, scope, delivery timeframe and customer requirements for your offer.
- You have set your price and payment terms to collect at least 2x your combined acquisition and fulfillment costs upfront, after fees.
- You have your sales and fulfillment teams working from the same offer.

### Expanding Your Winning Ads

- You have made a copy of the Copywriting Bot Template.
- You have written 10 versions of your winning ad angle and three versions of your winning ad body.
- You have had your copywriting bot combine them into 30 scripts and reviewed any combination it flagged.
- You have filmed all 30 scripts in five different setups.
- You have edited every ad using the Ad Creation Checklist and watched each one on your phone with sound and on mute.

### Metrics & Meetings

- You have made a copy of the Scaling Reverse Engineering Template and filled in your funnel numbers.
- You have made a copy of the Scaling Metrics Tracking Template and are filling it in once per day.
- You have confirmed your Ads Manager columns are set up.
- You have cloned your landing page and are using a separate calendar event only in this funnel.
- You have scheduled a weekly fulfillment meeting, acquisition meeting, unit economics review and metrics review.
- You have calculated your maximum CAC.
- You have a named owner for each part of the business, and you are assigning the improvements agreed on in each meeting.

### Sales & Fulfillment Frameworks

- You have put together your sales framework with pre-call preparation, call plan, offer details, follow-up and tracking.
- You have added examples of good calls to your sales framework.
- You are reviewing every closer's calls against the process.
- You have listed every micro step from payment to the customer getting the result, with the five workflow details for each.
- You have templatized as many steps as possible and kept your templates, assets and frameworks together.
- You have turned each task into an SOP checklist and trained people using only the checklist.
- You have decided what the customer's first value is and cut how much work the customer has to do.

### Unit Economics Model

- You have made a copy of the Unit Economics Model Spreadsheet Template and entered your cash collected, funnel numbers, salaries, software and other costs.
- You have listed every step in your fulfillment workflow with the time it takes.
- You have checked your unit economics multiple, gross margin and effective hourly rate against the 2X, 80% and $250 targets.
- You are raising your price once your close rate goes above 25%, by 20% every five clients.
- You are replacing your estimates with actual numbers as you get them.

### Scaling Model

- You have chosen whether to begin with horizontal or vertical scaling.
- If you chose horizontal scaling, you have expanded one proven ad through:
  - Different Avatars In The Ad
  - Different Ad Formats
  - Different Ad Designs
  - Different Ad Concepts
  - Different Keywords
  - Different Ways To Say The Same Thing
  - Different BGs/Locations/Settings
- If you chose vertical scaling, you have built a list of at least 50 problems, circumstances and desired outcomes from your sales call recordings and internet research.
- If you chose vertical scaling, you have recorded how people describe each situation and sorted your angles into workhorse and niche angles.
- If you chose vertical scaling, you have at least 10 potential workhorse angles and at least 40 potential niche angles.

### Hiring & Team

- You have at least one person with a brain on your team.
- You have hired an assistant, an editor and a designer.
- You have hired a setter if you consistently get 8+ applications submitted per day.
- You have hired a closer if you are getting 25+ calls from paid ads per week.
- You have a head of fulfillment.
- You have hired a head of sales if you have passed five closers.

### Ad Creative Production

- You have built a production system around five to seven ad formats.
- You are producing approximately one new creative per week for every $10 in daily ad budget.
- You have a real editing workflow for each format that identifies the ICP within 0.1 seconds, puts captions underneath the chin, removes dead space and adds proof.
- You have cloned the landing page for each winning ad angle and matched the headlines.
- You have made your image ads make the offer clear within one second, use high contrast, overcome roughly three objections and have a clear CTA.
- You have made your video ads show the ICP and offer within one second, proof within five seconds and everything within fifteen seconds.
- You have a copywriting framework, an editing or design framework and a copywriting bot for each format.
- You have made a copy of the Format Copywriting Bot Template.
- You are writing the original ads manually every time.
- You are producing ads in your five formats:
  - Static Image
  - Looping B Roll
  - UGC
  - Talking Head VSL
  - Loom + Screenshare

### Ad Testing

- You have a scaling campaign for your already successful ads and a testing campaign for your new creatives.
- You are using ABO for testing, with one ad set per group of a particular variable.
- You are judging each ad at around 100 outbound clicks by its cost per booked call and whether those calls close.
- You are leaving each winner running in its relabeled ad set and adding it to the scaling campaign at your next change.
- You are putting every new batch into new ad sets.
- You are turning each winner into different concepts and ad types, testing them and using what keeps working to produce the next batch.

### Scaling & Decisions

- You have opened the Data & Benchmarks Template and checked your metrics against the Phase #2 benchmarks.
- You have opened the If This, Then That Template and are using it to fix whatever is off.
- You have opened the Ad Spend Decision Making Template and are reviewing spend weekly using your unit economics multiple.
- You are hiring your next closer when the sales team reaches roughly 75% capacity and ramping new closers up with lower-scoring calls.
- You are hiring your next fulfillment member when the team reaches roughly 75% capacity.
- You have stopped increasing ad spend if something in the business is fundamentally broken.

### Framework & Criteria

**Reverse Engineering**

1. **Clients Needed:** Lifetime revenue goal ÷ customer lifetime revenue.
2. **Shows Needed:** Clients needed ÷ close rate, as a percentage of shown calls.
3. **Calls Booked Needed:** Shows needed ÷ show rate, as a percentage of booked calls.
4. **Applications Needed:** Calls booked needed ÷ call-booked rate, as a percentage of applications.
5. **Outbound Clicks Needed:** Applications needed ÷ application rate, as a percentage of outbound clicks.
6. **Total Ad Spend Required:** Outbound clicks needed × cost per outbound click.
7. **Average Daily Ad Spend:** Total ad spend required ÷ 365.

**Unit Economics Model**

1. **Cash Collected:** Cash collected upfront and fees, which give you net cash collected.
2. **Acquisition Costs:** Ad spend, closers, setters, software and miscellaneous, which give you total acquisition costs.
3. **Fulfillment Costs:** Labor, software and miscellaneous, which give you total fulfillment costs.
4. **Calculation:** Net cash collected ÷ (total acquisition costs + total fulfillment costs). We want to collect at least 2x our combined acquisition and fulfillment costs upfront, after fees.

**Metrics To Track**

- Gross revenue
- Gross cash collected
- Net cash collected
- Fulfillment costs
- Fulfillment cost per client
- Acquisition costs
- Acquisition cost per client
- Gross margin
- Net margin
- Median client payment
- Monthly churn rate, if applicable
- Show rate, as a percentage of booked calls
- Close rate, as a percentage of shown calls
- Cost per booked call

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Scaling Reverse Engineering Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1QLc5LhWMv7E21nC7qjLyP5VG8oM3x8t3Pvy_VVuJSjE/edit?usp=sharing).** This works backward from your lifetime revenue goal to show you how many clients, shows, booked calls, applications, outbound clicks and dollars of ad spend it takes to get there. [in this skill: assets/templates/scaling-reverse-engineering-template/]
2. **Enter your lifetime revenue goal in the Actual and Goal columns.** This is the total revenue you want from the customers you acquire, spread over one year of acquisition. It is their expected lifetime revenue, so depending on how long customers stay and when they pay, some of that revenue will come in after the year ends.
3. **Enter your customer lifetime revenue in the Actual and Goal columns.** This is what each customer is worth over their lifetime. The sheet divides your goal by it to give you the number of clients you need.
4. **Enter your close rate as a percentage of shown calls.** Put your current close rate in the Actual column and the close rate you are working toward in the Goal column. Your own model needs to use your actual funnel performance, not the example numbers. The sheet divides the clients needed by this to give you the number of shows you need.
5. **Enter your show rate as a percentage of booked calls.** Fill in both the Actual and Goal columns. The sheet divides the shows needed by this to give you the number of calls you need booked.
6. **Enter your call-booked rate as a percentage of applications.** This is the percentage of applications that book a call. The sheet divides the calls booked needed by this to give you the number of applications you need.
7. **Enter your application rate as a percentage of outbound clicks.** This is the percentage of outbound clicks that apply. The sheet divides the applications needed by this to give you the number of outbound clicks you need.
8. **Enter your cost per outbound click.** The sheet multiplies the outbound clicks needed by this to give you the total ad spend required to hit your goal, then divides it by 365 for your average daily ad spend. It also shows what you would spend in ads per booked call and per acquired customer.
9. **Check the % Difference column for your biggest gaps.** This shows how far your Actual numbers are from your Goal numbers. The biggest gaps show you which part of the funnel needs the most work.
10. **Make a copy of the [Scaling Metrics Tracking Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1AS4hNqUVefgaCe1szr12kwuVLJVB9sUwlrg5EUCAkLk/edit?usp=sharing).** We need to track the money coming in, what it costs to acquire and fulfill each customer, and how the funnel performs. The template calculates every metric on the Metrics To Track list for each month, and the Dashboard tab lets you compare them month to month without clicking into each month's tab. [in this skill: assets/templates/scaling-metrics-tracking-template/]
11. **Fill in the yellow columns once per day.** Log revenue, cash, fees and costs on the day they happen, and take Ad Spend straight from Ads Manager. Enter Active Clients once on the 1st of the month and Median Client Payment once on the last day of the month. Close rate is now measured as a percentage of shown calls, so the template divides Clients Acquired by Calls Attended.
12. **Confirm your Ads Manager columns match [Step #7: Create Your Columns](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=c313b114671b4c758749e96d439a01c5).** Your columns should already be set up from the Paid Ads Setup Course, with Application Rate, Booked Call Rate, Hook Rate and ThruPlay Rate as custom columns.
13. **Clone your landing page and use the separate Calendly event from [Step #3: Clone Calendar Tool](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c0a462fb?md=bc29b4542cff4e9095e09f832790dd69) only in this funnel.** Give the page a separate link, such as /facebook-ads. This gives us a simple way to identify the calls booked through this funnel without needing a complicated attribution system. It is not perfect, since someone could see your ad, Google you and book through another link.

### Example

1. Doing today's work on the new offer is how I build the income that gets my parents out of their mortgage.
2. Finishing my four hours without dragging out the breaks is what lets me close the laptop when my kids get home.
3. Getting up when my alarm goes off is how I start trusting myself when I say I am going to do something.
4. Following my food plan is what stops me avoiding the camera every time my family takes a picture.
5. Finishing the full workout is what lets me keep up with my son as he gets older.
6. Going to bed at the time I chose is what stops my family getting the exhausted version of me every morning.
7. Doing my 2.0 You Hour every day is how I stop spending another five years building a life I never actually chose.
8. Writing my employee a complete brief is how I eventually take a vacation where my wife is not listening to client calls beside the pool.
9. Putting my phone away when my dad calls is me having these conversations while he is still here.
10. Submitting an honest Work Form is how I get evidence behind the things I tell myself about my work ethic.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write down the things you do every day.** The actions may be the same ones already on your Self-Mastery List. Most people have attached almost no meaning to how they live, so their food, sleep, exercise, wake-up time, and work mean very little to them.
2. **Write down what doing each one means for your goals and Life Vision.** Draw the action toward your hopes, dreams, and the 10X version of yourself, so it connects to something you genuinely care about instead of feeling pointless or low-stakes.
3. **Be specific about why following through matters to you.** When you attach genuine meaning to ordinary actions, how you perform them can change dramatically.
4. **Build the first version during your 2.0 You Hour.** This is one of the four lists you will keep returning to as you build the Daily System.
5. **Return to it during your daily reviews.** Especially the one right before you go to bed. You want to review this as much as possible to literally train your behavior.
6. **Update it weekly as your vision develops and you find stronger connections.** Your Life Vision gets built gradually during your daily 2.0 You Hours, so the connections available to you now will get stronger as that document does.

### Completion Audit

- Did you build the first version during your 2.0 You Hour?
- Did you return to it during your daily reviews?
- Did you update it weekly as your vision develops and you find stronger connections?

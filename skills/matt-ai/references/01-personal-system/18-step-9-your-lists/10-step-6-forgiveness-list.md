### Example

1. I forgive myself for the years I spent talking about starting instead of doing anything.
2. I forgive myself for the money I wasted trying to look successful.
3. I forgive myself for letting my health go while pretending I would deal with it later.
4. I forgive myself for the evenings I sat beside my family and spent the whole time reading work messages.
5. I forgive myself for staying in a bad partnership long after I knew it was wrong.
6. I forgive myself for promising to change and then going back to the same habits.
7. I forgive myself for taking my frustration with the business out on people at home.
8. I forgive myself for the decision that failed, which I made honestly with what I knew at the time.
9. I forgive myself for not being where I thought I would be by this age.
10. I forgive my former business partner for breaking our agreement, which I still replay in my head.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write down the things you are still holding against other people or yourself.** Any person, event, or part of yourself that you already know you need to forgive.
2. **Write down what still weighs on you.** Old mistakes, wasted opportunities, and times you know you did not give your full effort.
3. **Expect most of it to be about forgiving yourself.** I have shared this with maybe two dozen people. For roughly 5 to 10%, it was about forgiving others. For 90 to 95%, it was about forgiving themselves.
4. **Work through what you have been carrying.** Your 2.0 You Hour is where this one gets worked on.
5. **Keep the list forever.** This is mostly a one-and-done list, but it does not get deleted once you have worked through it.
6. **Return to anything you still need to forgive.** If an old issue comes up during the day that you need to work through, put it on the list then.

### Completion Audit

- Did you write down the things you are still holding against other people or yourself?
- Did you write down what still weighs on you?
- Did you expect most of it to be about forgiving yourself?
- Did you work through what you have been carrying?
- Did you keep the list forever?
- Did you return to anything you still need to forgive?

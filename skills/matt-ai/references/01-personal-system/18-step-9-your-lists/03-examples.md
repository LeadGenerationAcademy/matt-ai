I've listed ten individual examples for each list. This is just to give you an idea of what each list would look like, but related to you.

### Self Mastery List

1. When I wake up, I immediately go to my computer and start these four hours of work
2. I don't mess around too long between hours. I am back at it after 10-15 minutes
3. I get these four hours of work done by 11am every day
4. I wake up at 5:30am every day to give myself time to accomplish it by 11am
5. When I want to check my phone during a work hour, I leave it alone and get back to the assignment
6. When I finish a work hour, I submit the Work Form right then instead of leaving it until tonight
7. When I want to skip the last exercise, I finish the workout I planned with proper form
8. When I can't be bothered making food, I eat the meal I planned instead of ordering something else
9. When an employee questions my decision, I let him finish before I start defending myself
10. When my bedtime arrives, I close the computer and leave my phone outside the bedroom

### Meaning List

1. Doing today's work on the new offer is how I build the income that gets my parents out of their mortgage.
2. Finishing my four hours without dragging out the breaks is what lets me close the laptop when my kids get home.
3. Getting up when my alarm goes off is how I start trusting myself when I say I am going to do something.
4. Following my food plan is what stops me avoiding the camera every time my family takes a picture.
5. Finishing the full workout is what lets me keep up with my son as he gets older.
6. Going to bed at the time I chose is what stops my family getting the exhausted version of me every morning.
7. Doing my 2.0 You Hour every day is how I stop spending another five years building a life I never actually chose.
8. Writing my employee a complete brief is how I eventually take a vacation where my wife is not listening to client calls beside the pool.
9. Putting my phone away when my dad calls is me having these conversations while he is still here.
10. Submitting an honest Work Form is how I get evidence behind the things I tell myself about my work ethic.

### Thought Habit List

1. When my alarm goes off, I instantly smile and think "yay" and then leap out of bed
2. When I am done my morning routine, I think "I am so excited and lucky to have these four hours to develop the best version of myself and what I can do"
3. Whenever something is not my absolute best or the 10x standard of my previous best, I instantly think "no, that is not good enough, I need to do it better"
4. When the work gets hard, I instantly think "good, this is the part where everybody else quits"
5. When a prospect says no, I think "fine, next one," and I move straight to the next one
6. When I see a competitor doing well, I think "great, so it works, now I know what is possible"
7. When I catch myself reaching for my phone, I instantly think "no" and I put it face down
8. When I miss a workout, I think "one missed, I am not missing two," and I book the next one right there
9. When I finish my four hours, I think "the day is already won and everything after this is extra"
10. When my numbers are down, I think "good, now I know exactly where the problem is," and I go find it

### Joy List

1. Taking my dog to his favorite park and letting him run around.
2. Playing the song I always turn up when it comes on in the car.
3. Getting a coffee and walking outside after my four hours.
4. My daughter running to the door when I get home.
5. Watching the old video that always makes my brother and me laugh.
6. Cooking breakfast with my wife when neither of us is rushing.
7. Calling the friend who makes me laugh about things that have nothing to do with business.
8. Sitting by the water for ten minutes without looking at a screen.
9. Playing basketball with my friends.
10. Looking through photos from the family vacation I actually enjoyed.

### Gratitude List

1. My parents are still alive, and I can pick up the phone and speak to them.
2. I have customers who trust me enough to pay for something I created.
3. My wife stayed beside me during the year the business was struggling.
4. My body allows me to walk, lift weights, and do something about my health.
5. I have control over my calendar, even if I have not been using it properly.
6. My children still want to spend time with me.
7. An employee caught a mistake last week before it reached the customer.
8. I can afford food, a safe place to live, and the basic tools I need to work.
9. I have a friend who tells me the truth when I need to hear it.
10. I have learned skills I can use to earn money even if this particular business does not work out.

### Forgiveness List

1. I forgive myself for the years I spent talking about starting instead of doing anything.
2. I forgive myself for the money I wasted trying to look successful.
3. I forgive myself for letting my health go while pretending I would deal with it later.
4. I forgive myself for the evenings I sat beside my family and spent the whole time reading work messages.
5. I forgive myself for staying in a bad partnership long after I knew it was wrong.
6. I forgive myself for promising to change and then going back to the same habits.
7. I forgive myself for taking my frustration with the business out on people at home.
8. I forgive myself for the decision that failed, which I made honestly with what I knew at the time.
9. I forgive myself for not being where I thought I would be by this age.
10. I forgive my former business partner for breaking our agreement, which I still replay in my head.

### Anti-Worry List

1. Book the dentist appointment I have postponed twice.
2. Send the accountant the receipts he keeps asking for.
3. Renew my passport before it becomes an urgent problem.
4. Cancel the unused software subscription that keeps charging me.
5. Reply to the important email I have been avoiding all week.
6. Book someone to fix the air conditioner that keeps waking me up.
7. Return the package before the refund deadline.
8. Pay the parking ticket sitting on my desk.
9. Replace the damaged charger that only works when the cable is bent.
10. Call the insurance company about the notice I have not dealt with.

### Improvement List

1. Lay my gym clothes out the night before, so the morning has one less thing I can talk myself out of.
2. Put one finished example in every brief I hand an employee, so the work comes back right the first time instead of three rounds later.
3. Record the answer as a short video the first time somebody asks, so I stop explaining the same thing to every new hire.
4. Put the decision we need to make at the top of every meeting agenda, so meetings end with something decided instead of another meeting.
5. Name every client folder the same way, so nobody loses half an hour looking for a file that already exists.
6. Put the three questions customers always ask into the onboarding email, so we stop answering the same thing forty times a month.
7. Move the break timer somewhere I can actually hear it, so a ten minute break stops turning into twenty five.
8. Add every site I keep landing on to the blocker, so focus stops depending on how I feel that afternoon.
9. Set a minimum stock level for the supplies we run out of, so nobody's work stops over printer paper.
10. Cook tomorrow's food while I am already cooking tonight's, so the plan holds on the days I get home late.

### Foundational Causes List

1. Being asleep by 10pm → the four work hours actually happen tomorrow.
2. Keeping my phone out of the bedroom → the first hour of the day belongs to me instead of everybody else.
3. Logging my numbers every day → I find a problem in three days instead of six months.
4. Counting every distraction honestly → I find out most of my lost hours come from one thing I could have removed years ago.
5. Investing a fixed amount every month regardless of what the market does → most of my net worth in twenty years.
6. Writing a proper brief before I hand work over → an employee who runs the function instead of one I supervise forever.
7. Removing a wrong hire in the first month → I stop building the business around his weaknesses for the next year.
8. Saying no to the client I already know is wrong → I keep the six months that client was going to take.
9. Submitting the Work Form after every hour → I end the year with evidence about how I work instead of a story about it.
10. Doing the 2.0 You Hour every day → my Life Vision exists on paper within a month instead of staying a vague idea for another decade.

# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #9: Your Lists
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/596f01c8?md=9775964d352143a5950dd9d60d2aa088

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Reviewed Daily

- Your Self Mastery List has the situations where you get an opportunity to practice self-mastery, with the response you expect from yourself written against each one.
- Your Meaning List has the things you do every day and what doing them means for your goals and Life Vision.
- Your Thought Habit List has the circumstances you regularly face and what you have decided to think when they happen.
- Your Foundational Causes List has the ordinary decisions and repeated behaviours that carry the largest downstream consequences.
- Every entry on all four uses an actual moment you recognize from your own life.

### Used As Needed

- Your Joy List has things that actually make you smile or lift your mood.
- Your Gratitude List has at least ten things you genuinely feel grateful for and actually mean.
- Your Forgiveness List has what you are still holding against other people or yourself.
- Your Anti-Worry List has each unresolved problem paired with the task you can actually do about it.
- Your Improvement List has the 1% improvements you have noticed so far.

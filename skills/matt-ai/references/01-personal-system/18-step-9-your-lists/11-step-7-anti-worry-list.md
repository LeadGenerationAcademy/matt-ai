### Example

1. Book the dentist appointment I have postponed twice.
2. Send the accountant the receipts he keeps asking for.
3. Renew my passport before it becomes an urgent problem.
4. Cancel the unused software subscription that keeps charging me.
5. Reply to the important email I have been avoiding all week.
6. Book someone to fix the air conditioner that keeps waking me up.
7. Return the package before the refund deadline.
8. Pay the parking ticket sitting on my desk.
9. Replace the damaged charger that only works when the cable is bent.
10. Call the insurance company about the notice I have not dealt with.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write down the unresolved things that worry you, no matter how small.** These are the small unresolved problems that continue taking up space in your head.
2. **Write down the task you can actually do about each one.** Make the appointment. Send the email. Submit the form. Replace the broken thing. Cancel the subscription. Whatever the real action is.
3. **Keep adding things as they come up.** When something begins worrying you during the week, add it to the list instead of stopping your work to deal with it.
4. **Work through them during your weekly Anti-Worry Hour.** This list has its own scheduled hour, so it does not get worked during your 2.0 You Hour.
5. **Check them off when they are handled.** The list will keep developing because life keeps producing new things to deal with.

### Completion Audit

- Did you write down the unresolved things that worry you, no matter how small?
- Did you write down the task you can actually do about each one?
- Did you keep adding things as they came up?
- Did you work through them during your weekly Anti-Worry Hour?
- Did you check them off when they were handled?

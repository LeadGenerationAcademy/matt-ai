# Action Steps

Course: Step #9: Your Lists
System: Personal System

---
The Theory explains that your Life Vision gives you something to work toward. The nine lists you created in the Create course covers the ordinary decisions and situations that determine whether you actually get there.

In this course you will build the first version of each of your nine list.

You will write down what is already obvious on every list. Four of them, your Self Mastery, Meaning, Foundational Causes and Thought Habit Lists, get reviewed several times a day and updated weekly. The rest are there for when you need them.

Do not try to finish any of these. Write what comes immediately to mind and let them develop as you keep using the System.

### Table of Contents

1. **Self Mastery List:** The situations where you get an opportunity to practice self-mastery, and what you are going to make yourself do when each one happens.
2. **Meaning List:** The things you do every day and what doing them means for your goals and Life Vision.
3. **Thought Habits List:** The circumstances you regularly face and what you are going to deliberately think when they happen.
4. **Joy List:** The things that reliably make you happy or immediately lift your mood.
5. **Gratitude List:** At least ten things you genuinely feel grateful for and actually mean.
6. **Forgiveness List:** Everything you are still holding against other people or yourself, most of which will be yourself.
7. **Anti Worry List:** The unresolved things that worry you, each one paired with the task you can actually do about it.
8. **Improvement List:** Possible 1% improvements to yourself, your life and your business, written down as you notice them.
9. **Foundational Causes List:** The ordinary decisions that carry tremendous downstream consequences, which is where the loop starts.

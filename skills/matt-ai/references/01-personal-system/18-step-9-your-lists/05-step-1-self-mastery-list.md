### Example

1. When I wake up, I immediately go to my computer and start these four hours of work
2. I don't mess around too long between hours. I am back at it after 10-15 minutes
3. I get these four hours of work done by 11am every day
4. I wake up at 5:30am every day to give myself time to accomplish it by 11am
5. When I want to check my phone during a work hour, I leave it alone and get back to the assignment
6. When I finish a work hour, I submit the Work Form right then instead of leaving it until tonight
7. When I want to skip the last exercise, I finish the workout I planned with proper form
8. When I can't be bothered making food, I eat the meal I planned instead of ordering something else
9. When an employee questions my decision, I let him finish before I start defending myself
10. When my bedtime arrives, I close the computer and leave my phone outside the bedroom

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write down the situations during an ordinary day where you have an opportunity to practice self-mastery.** Self-mastery means gaining control over your thoughts, attention, actions, standards, body, and time, so look at where you keep losing that control or breaking your own standards.
2. **Write down what you are going to make yourself do when each one happens.** This means making yourself do it. Write the actual response, not a general intention to do better in that situation.
3. **Use actual moments you recognize from your life.** Every time you have an opportunity to exercise self-mastery, you should recognize it and follow through, no matter how small the opportunity is.
4. **Build the first version during your 2.0 You Hour.** This is one of the four lists you will keep returning to as you build the Daily System.
5. **Return to it during your daily reviews.** Especially the one right before you go to bed. You want to review this as much as possible to literally train your behavior.
6. **Update it weekly as you encounter new situations and raise your standards.** Add to it during the day too, whenever you notice something you need greater control over.

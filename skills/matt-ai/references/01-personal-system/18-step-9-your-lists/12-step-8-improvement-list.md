### Example

1. Lay my gym clothes out the night before, so the morning has one less thing I can talk myself out of.
2. Put one finished example in every brief I hand an employee, so the work comes back right the first time instead of three rounds later.
3. Record the answer as a short video the first time somebody asks, so I stop explaining the same thing to every new hire.
4. Put the decision we need to make at the top of every meeting agenda, so meetings end with something decided instead of another meeting.
5. Name every client folder the same way, so nobody loses half an hour looking for a file that already exists.
6. Put the three questions customers always ask into the onboarding email, so we stop answering the same thing forty times a month.
7. Move the break timer somewhere I can actually hear it, so a ten minute break stops turning into twenty five.
8. Add every site I keep landing on to the blocker, so focus stops depending on how I feel that afternoon.
9. Set a minimum stock level for the supplies we run out of, so nobody's work stops over printer paper.
10. Cook tomorrow's food while I am already cooking tonight's, so the plan holds on the days I get home late.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write down possible 1% improvements to yourself, your life, and your business.** There are probably thousands you could think of. We are aiming for a huge 2.0 transformation in thirty days, but getting a little better every day is useful too. I like doing both. The idea that you have to choose one is stupid.
2. **Write down things you or your employees could improve as you notice them.** The list will begin filling itself once you start paying attention.
3. **Do not turn every idea into an assignment immediately.** Recording one should take a few seconds. You are writing the thought down so you can return to what you were doing.
4. **Use your 2.0 You Hour to decide which ones are worth doing.** That hour is where this list gets worked on, alongside your Self Mastery List and your Forgiveness List.

### Completion Audit

- Did you write down possible 1% improvements to yourself, your life, and your business?
- Did you write down things you or your employees could improve as you noticed them?
- Did you avoid turning every idea into an assignment immediately?
- Did you use your 2.0 You Hour to decide which ones are worth doing?

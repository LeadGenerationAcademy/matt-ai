### Example

1. Being asleep by 10pm → the four work hours actually happen tomorrow.
2. Keeping my phone out of the bedroom → the first hour of the day belongs to me instead of everybody else.
3. Logging my numbers every day → I find a problem in three days instead of six months.
4. Counting every distraction honestly → I find out most of my lost hours come from one thing I could have removed years ago.
5. Investing a fixed amount every month regardless of what the market does → most of my net worth in twenty years.
6. Writing a proper brief before I hand work over → an employee who runs the function instead of one I supervise forever.
7. Removing a wrong hire in the first month → I stop building the business around his weaknesses for the next year.
8. Saying no to the client I already know is wrong → I keep the six months that client was going to take.
9. Submitting the Work Form after every hour → I end the year with evidence about how I work instead of a story about it.
10. Doing the 2.0 You Hour every day → my Life Vision exists on paper within a month instead of staying a vague idea for another decade.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write down the ordinary moments, causes and decisions that have tremendous downstream effects and consequences.** For every cause, there is an effect. For every effect, there was a cause. Without causes, there are no effects.
2. **Write down the core habits or things you must do on a repeated basis to achieve your life goals.** Only the ones you already know. The rest gets taken care of later.
3. **Start the loop from a cause on this list.** These four lists work as four steps in one process: Foundational Causes, Self Mastery, Meaning, Thought Habit. You choose the cause here, then choose the moment to practice self-mastery, assign meaning to it, and create a thought habit for it.
4. **Return to it as you build the Daily System.** This is one of the four lists you will keep coming back to, and the process repeats forever, becoming more effective every time you review it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you write down the ordinary moments, causes and decisions that have tremendous downstream effects and consequences?
- Did you write down the core habits or things you must do on a repeated basis to achieve your life goals?
- Did you start the loop from a cause on this list?
- Did you return to it as you build the Daily System?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go directly to your scale and weigh yourself.** Do this before anything else. The scale should already be sitting where it belongs from last night's preparation.
2. **Do it under roughly the same conditions every morning.** Same conditions is what makes the data actually useful.
3. **Let the scale send the measurement to the app.** The measurement should go into its app automatically, and it ends up in your [Health Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?usp=sharing) during your Daily 15-Minute Setup. [in this skill: assets/templates/health-tracking-template/]
4. **Get the number and continue.** Do not stare at one measurement and have an emotional breakdown because your weight moved in the wrong direction. We care about the trend.

### Completion Audit

- Did you go directly to your scale and weigh yourself?
- Did you do it under roughly the same conditions as every other morning?
- Did the scale send the measurement to the app?
- Did you get the number and continue?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Read at least 20 pages a day.** This runs across the whole 30 days, not whenever you happen to feel like picking a book up.
2. **Prioritize the two books you bought during Setup.** [10X Is Easier Than 2X](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=10X+Is+Easier+Than+2X), and the [Easy Way](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Allen+Carr+Easyway+books) book related to your number-one addiction. You bought both for a reason.
3. **Read between meetings, while waiting somewhere, during the evening or before bed.** The pages do not need their own scheduled block on the calendar.
4. **Keep everything else you consume inside your Information Diet.** Completing your first four hours does not make the garbage good for you again.

### Completion Audit

- Did you read at least 20 pages today?
- Did you prioritize the two books you bought during Setup?
- Did you read between meetings, while waiting somewhere, during the evening or before bed?
- Is everything else you consume still inside your Information Diet?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose the specific thing you are going to complete before every hour.** Written on your legal pad before anything starts, so there is nothing to work out once the timer is running.
2. **Reset your [Distraction Counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter) to zero.** Every hour gets its own count, which is the only way that number means anything.
3. **Start the [60-minute timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer).** The same 60 minutes every time. Do not shorten the hour because focusing has become uncomfortable.
4. **Stay in your chair until the timer goes off.** This is a personal standard, one of the promises you keep to yourself no matter what mood you are in that day.
5. **Press the [Distraction Counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter) and write down what distracted you every time you get distracted.** The number tells you how many times you lost focus. The note tells you why.
6. **Stay there when the work gets difficult, when you feel tired, and when your brain starts hunting for something unrelated.** All three of those will happen. None of them is a reason to get up.
7. **Let yourself stare at the screen rather than getting up.** When you are starting out you do not have to work if you really do not want to. You just cannot get up, grab your phone, browse random shit, or switch to something easier.

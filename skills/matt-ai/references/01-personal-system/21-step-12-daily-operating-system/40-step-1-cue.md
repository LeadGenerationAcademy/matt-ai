### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat the scheduled hour on your calendar as the cue.** This one happens every day, and it should already be blocked out from Setup.
2. **Set an alarm on your phone too.** This hour can feel like an inconvenience when you have a business to run and a pile of other things to do, so it gets a second cue.
3. **Open your 2.0 folder and begin when the alarm goes off.** This is active work. You should be writing, reading, making decisions and working through things.
4. **Keep the hour where it sits in your day.** Do not wait until you have finished every possible responsibility before deciding whether there is time left to work on yourself.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat the scheduled hour on your calendar as the cue?
- Did you set an alarm on your phone too?
- Did you open your 2.0 folder and begin when the alarm went off?
- Did you keep the hour where it sits in your day?

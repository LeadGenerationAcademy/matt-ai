### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Review your Self Mastery List.** This is to remind yourself how you decided to handle the things you struggle to control, before tomorrow puts them in front of you again.
2. **Review your Meaning List.** This is to remind yourself what your actions are actually for.
3. **Review your Thought Habit List.** This is to remind yourself how you decided to think about the situations you deal with regularly.
4. **Look at your other lists too if you want.** These three stay the priority, because reviewing them as much as possible is what literally trains your behavior.
5. **Make sure you have reviewed all three before you go to bed.** These are the last documents you look at before you sleep.

### Completion Audit

- Did you review your Self Mastery List?
- Did you review your Meaning List?
- Did you review your Thought Habit List?
- Did you look at your other lists if you wanted to?
- Did you review all three before going to bed?

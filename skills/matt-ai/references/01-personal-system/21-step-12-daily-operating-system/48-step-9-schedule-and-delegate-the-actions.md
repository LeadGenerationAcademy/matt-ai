### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take assignments from your 90-day lists.** These are the actions you checkmarked in the last step.
2. **Put three to five tasks on your [War Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?usp=sharing) for each day.** You can schedule tomorrow, plan several days ahead, or update what is already there. You do not have to rebuild tomorrow's plan during every 2.0 Hour. [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
3. **Assign a time to each action.** These are the things you will complete during the rest of your day, since your first four work hours still belong to your 10X Project.
4. **Assign a person to each action.** A lot of the work does not need to be done by you.
5. **Assign a priority to each action.** This decides what gets done first when the day runs short.
6. **Put the delegated assignments on your employees' [War Maps](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?usp=sharing).** Give clear expectations about what needs to be completed and when. When a few people are doing this together, you can get a tremendous amount done. [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
7. **Put repeating actions directly on your calendar.** You should not need to remember to recreate the same task every week.
8. **Attach a cue and a reward to them.** The same way we have done with the other parts of this System.
9. **Choose assignments you can actually finish and treat them as commitments.** These are the things you said would be done before you went to bed. Completing them is a habit, a standard, and part of self-mastery, so do not get comfortable moving them to tomorrow.

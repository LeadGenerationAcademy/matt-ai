### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Check whether your previous assignments were actually completed.** Before you add a single new task. These are the assignments you scheduled on your [War Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?usp=sharing). [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
2. **Check anything you delegated to employees.** Delegated assignments should be sitting on their War Maps too, so those get checked the same way.
3. **Look at the work.** The actual output, not just whether something got marked complete.
4. **Find out why and deal with anything that was not completed.** Otherwise you can spend months making plans while the same unfinished assignments keep getting moved around.

### Completion Audit

- Did you check whether your previous assignments were actually completed?
- Did you check anything you delegated to employees?
- Did you look at the work?
- Did you find out why and deal with anything that was not completed?

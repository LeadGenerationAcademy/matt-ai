### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Add "Rise Immediately" to your Self Mastery List and Meaning List.** Rising immediately is the first opportunity of the day to practice self-mastery.
2. **Get your feet on the floor and stand up instead of lying there negotiating with yourself.** You made the decision the night before. Now you either follow it or begin the day by proving that your own decisions do not matter.
3. **Change what waking up means to you.** You are not waking up because another miserable day has started. You are blessed enough to have another day in front of you and a four-hour project that represents a 10X version of your life and business.
4. **Be happy to get up because you know exactly what you are getting up to do.** It may take time before you naturally feel that way. Until then, get up anyway.

### Completion Audit

- Did you add "Rise Immediately" to your Self Mastery List and Meaning List?
- Did you get your feet on the floor and stand up instead of lying there negotiating with yourself?
- Did you change what waking up means to you?
- Are you happy to get up because you know exactly what you are getting up to do?

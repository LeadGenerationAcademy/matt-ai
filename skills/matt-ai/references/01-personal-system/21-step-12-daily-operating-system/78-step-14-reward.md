### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take these actions as their own reward.** You do not need to give yourself a treat every time you make a good decision. Having greater control over yourself and seeing the results is satisfying on its own.
2. **Take a second to recognize what you did.** You did what you said you would do. You corrected yourself when you caught something wrong. You completed work and have proof of it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you take these actions as their own reward?
- Did you take a second to recognize what you did?

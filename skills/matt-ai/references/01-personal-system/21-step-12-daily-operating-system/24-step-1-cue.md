### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat completing your Daily 15-Minute Setup as the cue.** The setup ends and Hour One starts. There is nothing left to decide at that point.
2. **Start Hour One immediately, with no gap.** There should not be some weird gap where you check your email, walk around, get another coffee and somehow start working 45 minutes later.
3. **Use the cues already sitting around you.** The [sticky note](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes) on the bottom of your computer, the [sticky notes](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes) and documents on the wall behind it, your vision board, the [physical calendar](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physical+wall+calendar) waiting for another X, and your timer and [Distraction Counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter) directly in front of you.
4. **Let your accountability partners work as a cue too.** They are doing their hours at the same time, and you will see their Work Form notifications the same way they see yours.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat completing your Daily 15-Minute Setup as the cue?
- Did you start Hour One immediately, with no gap?
- Did you use the cues already sitting around you?
- Did you let your accountability partners work as a cue too?

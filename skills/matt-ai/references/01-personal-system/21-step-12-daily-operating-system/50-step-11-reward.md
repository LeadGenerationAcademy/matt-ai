### Example

**Same example rewards as First Four Hours Reward:**

- Go to a coffee shop, get a coffee, and chill while watching YouTube
- Go out and get lunch somewhere instead of eating at your desk
- Spend time outside with no phone
- Take time with your wife, kids, or a friend
- Sit in the sauna or take a long shower with nothing scheduled after it
- Watch an episode of a TV show
- Play video games
- Take a nap
- Get a massage
- Go for a swim

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose a substantial reward for completing your 2.0 Hour.** I like to make mine roughly equal to the reward for completing my first four work hours, because this hour is that important.
2. **Finish the hour with something developed, decided, planned, or worked through.** Sitting there looking at the same documents for an hour does not count.
3. **Look at what you worked on.** This is the check on whether the hour actually produced something before you collect anything for it.
4. **Take the reward.** This is what makes completing the hour feel worth repeating.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose a substantial reward for completing your 2.0 Hour?
- Did you finish the hour with something developed, decided, planned, or worked through?
- Did you look at what you worked on?
- Did you take the reward?

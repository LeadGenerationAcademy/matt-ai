### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat a worry, improvement, unresolved issue, or useful idea coming to mind as the cue.** These arrive while you are in the middle of something else.
2. **Write it down in the relevant place while you remember it.** You are recording the thought so you can return to what you were doing.
3. **Put anything that worries you on your Anti-Worry List.** The small unresolved problems that keep taking up space in your head. You deal with them during your weekly Anti-Worry Hour.
4. **Add anything you need greater control over to your Self Mastery List.** The specific situations where you get an opportunity to practice self-mastery, along with what you are going to make yourself do.
5. **Put old issues you need to work through on your Forgiveness List.** Any person, event, or part of yourself you already know you need to forgive.
6. **Put things that make you happy on your Joy List.** The things that actually lift your mood or bring you back to yourself.
7. **Put things you feel grateful for on your Gratitude List.** We forget how good we have it, especially when we spend the whole day looking at what we have not achieved yet.
8. **Put improvements you want to make on your Improvement List.** Anything about yourself, your life, or your business that could be better.
9. **Add actions that could help a one-year goal to that goal's note.** You can organize and schedule it during your 2.0 Hour.
10. **Get back to what you were doing.** The whole thing should take a few seconds.

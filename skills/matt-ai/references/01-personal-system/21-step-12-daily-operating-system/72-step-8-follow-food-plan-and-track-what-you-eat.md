### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat eating or drinking something as the cue.** Food gives you repeated opportunities to practice self-mastery.
2. **Follow the food plan you already made.** Eating similar meals makes the tracking easier, which is another reason the plan is a simple one.
3. **Record what you consume in your [calorie-tracking app](https://www.loseit.com/).** The same app you put your calorie target into during Setup and also in your [Health Tracking spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?usp=sharing). [in this skill: assets/templates/health-tracking-template/]
4. **Scan the barcode, search for the food, or use the photo feature if your app has one.** Whichever is fastest for what is in front of you.
5. **Check that the food and quantity are correct.** A scan or search gets you the food, and the quantity still needs setting.
6. **Do it at the time.** Instead of trying to reconstruct everything you ate at the end of the day.
7. **Be honest about what actually happened.** I am not going to turn this into a diet tangent. Use the plan you established.

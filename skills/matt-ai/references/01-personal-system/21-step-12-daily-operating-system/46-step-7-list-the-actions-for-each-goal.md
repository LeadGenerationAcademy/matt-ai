### Examples

These are some examples of actions. Your list would run much longer than this:

- Film VSL
- Film FAQ Video
- Film Proof Video
- Film Onboarding Video
- Film Cancellation Video
- Sign Up For Kit
- Create Onboarding Flow For Email
- Create Onboarding Flow For SMS
- Sign up for Roezan SMS
- Set up Zapier Onboarding Automation
- Setup Membership Questions
- Setup Webflow Website

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write down everything you could actually do to achieve each one-year goal.** Do this for all four goals separately.
2. **Make every action something that can be scheduled, completed, and checked off.** "Create content" is not an assignment. "Create the thumbnail for this video" is.
3. **Break anything too broad down until you have the actual tasks.** If you could not sit down and complete it, it is still too broad.
4. **Keep these inside the note for the relevant goal.** This gives you somewhere organized to add ideas as they come to you.
5. **Add new ideas as you think of them.** This is a fluid process. You will think of things tomorrow that you did not think of today, and you do not need to rebuild the whole plan every time you have another idea.

### Completion Audit

- Did you write down everything you could actually do to achieve each one-year goal?
- Is every action something that can be scheduled, completed, and checked off?
- Did you break anything too broad down until you had the actual tasks?
- Did you keep these inside the note for the relevant goal?
- Are you adding new ideas as you think of them?

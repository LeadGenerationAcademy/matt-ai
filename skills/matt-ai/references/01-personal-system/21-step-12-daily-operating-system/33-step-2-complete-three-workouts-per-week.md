### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Complete three weight-training sessions every week.** These are the three workouts you saved as templates in your [workout-tracking app](https://www.strong.app/) during Setup.
2. **Spread them throughout the week.** Monday, Wednesday and Friday, or Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. These should already be blocked in your calendar from Setup.
3. **Do approximately six exercises per workout.** Your saved templates should already be built this way.
4. **Complete 15 working sets.** Three sets for the first three exercises and two sets for the remaining three gets you there without turning the workout into a two-hour event.
5. **Aim for eight controlled repetitions per set.** Use strict form on every one of them.
6. **Lower the weight if you cannot complete eight repetitions with proper form.** The form is the standard, so the weight is what moves.
7. **Increase the weight the next time if you complete eight repetitions on the final set.** Especially at the beginning, you should be making noticeable progress almost every week.
8. **Log every exercise, set, repetition and weight in your [workout-tracking app](https://www.strong.app/) while you train.** Do not try to remember everything afterward. Once the workout is complete, the information ends up in your [Health Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?usp=sharing), so over time you see exactly how much stronger you have become instead of how strong you vaguely feel.

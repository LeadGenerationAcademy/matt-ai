- Take a nap

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose a reward for completing each workout.** It needs to be something you genuinely want. A good meal, Starbucks, a sauna, a longer break, or anything else that feels substantial enough to make finishing the workout immediately satisfying.
2. **Make it roughly equal to your four-hour reward.** You can use the same reward as your four-hour reward if that is what you want.
3. **Finish and log the entire workout before you take it.** Going to the gym, completing half the exercises and leaving does not count.
4. **Look at the completed workout, your total tonnage and any personal records.** This is in your [Health Tracking spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?usp=sharing). This is you keeping another promise to yourself and created another piece of physical proof. [in this skill: assets/templates/health-tracking-template/]
5. **Take the reward.** This is what makes completing the workout feel worth repeating.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose a reward for completing each workout?
- Is it roughly equal to your four-hour reward?
- Did you finish and log the entire workout before taking it?
- Did you look at the completed workout, your total tonnage and any personal records?
- Did you take the reward?

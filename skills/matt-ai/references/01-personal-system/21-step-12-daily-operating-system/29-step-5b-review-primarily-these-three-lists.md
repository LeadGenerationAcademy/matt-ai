### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Review your Self Mastery List.** This is to remind yourself how you decided to handle the things you struggle to control, before you face any of them.
2. **Review your Meaning List.** This is to remind yourself what your actions are actually for, so they do not feel pointless while you are doing them.
3. **Review your Thought Habit List.** This is to remind yourself how you decided to think about the situations you deal with regularly, before one of them happens.
4. **Do this after every one of your four hours.** Repetition is what makes these lists change your mind and your behavior, which is the whole reason you keep coming back to them.

### Completion Audit

- Did you review your Self Mastery List?
- Did you review your Meaning List?
- Did you review your Thought Habit List?
- Did you do this after every one of your four hours?

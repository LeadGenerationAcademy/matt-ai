### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat making a decision, speaking, or choosing how to behave as the cue.** These come up all day and none of them are scheduled.
2. **Think, speak and act like the person in your 2.0 vision already exists.** You do not need to wait until you achieve the goals before you keep your word, take your work seriously, or behave with the standards you wrote down. You can do those things today.
3. **Notice it and correct it when the three do not match.** You will catch yourself thinking one way, saying something completely different, and then acting differently again. This is going to take practice.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat making a decision, speaking, or choosing how to behave as the cue?
- Did you think, speak and act like the person in your 2.0 vision already exists?
- Did you notice and correct it when the three did not match?

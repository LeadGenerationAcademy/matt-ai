### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Health Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?gid=2107169354#gid=2107169354).** It should already be pinned in your browser as "H," and today's row sits on the current month's tab. [in this skill: assets/templates/health-tracking-template/]
2. **Enter this morning's weight.** It should already be waiting in your scale's app from the weigh-in you did during the morning routine.
3. **Enter last night's sleep data from your wearable.** Sleep score, total sleep, latency, REM and deep sleep, along with your bedtime and wake up time.
4. **Enter yesterday's food and calorie data.** Planned calories, total calories, fat, protein and carbohydrates, straight out of your calorie tracking app.
5. **Enter yesterday's workout data.** Whether you lifted, your tonnage, any PRs you hit and which split you did.
6. **Enter yesterday's steps, cardio and anything else you track.** Steps, Zone 2 cardio minutes, the treadmill walk, the stretch and your water.
7. **Look at the trend and continue.** Do not analyze every individual number like it represents the future of your entire life.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Health Tracking Spreadsheet?
- Did you enter this morning's weight?
- Did you enter last night's sleep data from your wearable?
- Did you enter yesterday's food and calorie data?
- Did you enter yesterday's workout data?
- Did you enter yesterday's steps, cardio and anything else you track?
- Did you look at the trend and continue?

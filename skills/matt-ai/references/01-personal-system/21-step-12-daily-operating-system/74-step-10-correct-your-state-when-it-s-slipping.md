### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat catching yourself becoming negative, resentful, discouraged, or stuck in self-doubt as the cue.** You will still have difficult feelings and real problems to deal with.
2. **Open your Joy List or Gratitude List.** The things that reliably lift your mood, and the things you genuinely feel grateful for.
3. **Use something from it.** Most of what is on there is something you can actually go and do right then.
4. **Go to your symbol.** It is on your phone background, your computer background, your bathroom mirror and your monitor, so it is already wherever you are.
5. **Remind yourself of the meaning you have given it.** We chose something that naturally resonates with you, and we are repeatedly connecting it with the state you want to return to.
6. **Deal with what needs to be done.** Take a moment to get into a better state first. You do not need to spend the entire day feeding those feelings.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat catching yourself becoming negative, resentful, discouraged, or stuck in self-doubt as the cue?
- Did you open your Joy List or Gratitude List?
- Did you use something from it?
- Did you go to your symbol?
- Did you remind yourself of the meaning you have given it?
- Did you deal with what needs to be done?

### Example

2. Put it on the wall with the others
3. Read the printed documents, vision board and symbol on the wall
4. Submit your Work Form
5. Start the 15-minute between-hour timer
6. Stretch for a few minutes

11. Write your next output on the legal pad: "Finish the landing page headline section"

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create your Work Hour [Sticky Note](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes) when the timer goes off.** Write what hour you completed, what you worked on, when you completed it, and how many distractions you recorded.
2. **Put it on the wall with the others.** The wall fills up over time, and that is the physical proof that you have been doing the work.
3. **Review the documents on the wall.** The printed documents, your vision board and your symbol, which should all be up there from Setup.
4. **Submit your [Work Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1i7ZgFfPYRK6CgJ7BtblZNm2lQR2_QoEnl853HOL15co/copy).** This officially records the hour, updates your spreadsheet and leaderboard, and sends the notification to your accountability partners. Do not save all four for later, because you will not remember everything properly.
5. **Set your 15-minute between-hour [timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer).** The second timer, not the one you just used. This is what stops a 15-minute break becoming a 45-minute one.
6. **Do the between-hour routine you created during Setup.** Clean up your workspace, drink water, walk around, stretch and use the bathroom.
7. **Sit back down when the break timer goes off.** The timer ends the break, not how you happen to feel about going back.
8. **Choose the next output.** The specific thing you are going to complete in the next hour, written on your [legal pad](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=small+yellow+legal+notepads).
9. **Reset the [Distraction Counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter).** Back to zero, so the next hour gets its own number.
10. **Set the [timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer) for 60 minutes.** That starts the next hour, and you repeat all of this until all four are done.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat reaching the end of your day as the cue.** You are done working, and you have completed your responsibilities.
2. **Finish the day properly before you go to bed.** The Night Routine closes the current day and prepares the next one, rather than leaving everything for tomorrow morning.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat reaching the end of your day as the cue?
- Did you finish the day properly before going to bed?

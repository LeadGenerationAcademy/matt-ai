### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Schedule the Anti-Worry Hour on a Wednesday.** A lot of these tasks need another person, business or office to respond. Wait until the weekend and everything may be closed, so the same unresolved task gets carried into another week.
2. **Put it on one of your days away from the gym.** This keeps you from having two separate interruptions in the middle of the same day.
3. **Work directly from the Anti-Worry List.** Make the appointment. Send the email. Submit the form. Replace the broken thing. Make the phone call. Cancel the subscription. Pay whatever needs to be paid.
4. **Handle as many items as possible before the timer ends.** This is not an hour for sitting there and thinking about your worries. The hour is for taking the actions that remove them.
5. **Take the next real action on anything you cannot fully resolve.** Send the message, request the document or schedule the appointment.
6. **Update the list so you know exactly what you are waiting for.** Anything half-finished should say who or what it is waiting on rather than sitting there half crossed off.
7. **Keep adding new unresolved tasks during the week.** The list captures the worry so your mind does not have to keep carrying it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you schedule the Anti-Worry Hour on a Wednesday?

- Did you work directly from the Anti-Worry List?
- Did you handle as many items as possible before the timer ended?
- Did you take the next real action on anything you could not fully resolve?
- Did you update the list so you know exactly what you are waiting for?
- Are you adding new unresolved tasks during the week?

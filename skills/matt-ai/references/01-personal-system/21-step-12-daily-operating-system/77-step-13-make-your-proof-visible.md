### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat completing an action that produces proof as the cue.** A work hour disappears pretty quickly if you finish it and immediately move on.
2. **Turn that proof into something physical or something you can actually see.** A [Sticky Note](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes) on the wall stays there. So does the [Work Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1i7ZgFfPYRK6CgJ7BtblZNm2lQR2_QoEnl853HOL15co/copy), the completed assignment, or the document you developed.
3. **Put your completed work hours on the [Sticky Note](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes) wall.** What hour you completed, what you worked on, when you completed it and how many distractions you recorded.
4. **Submit the [Work Forms](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1i7ZgFfPYRK6CgJ7BtblZNm2lQR2_QoEnl853HOL15co/copy) and let the accountability notifications go out.** Your partners see yours and you see theirs.
5. **Put an X on the [calendar](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physical+wall+calendar) after your first four hours.** The X specifically means you completed the four project hours.
6. **Keep your food and workout records.** Your calorie-tracking app and your workout-tracking app, which both feed your [Health Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?usp=sharing). [in this skill: assets/templates/health-tracking-template/]
7. **Save the actual work produced during your 2.0 Hour.** The vision, the lists and the planning you developed that day.
8. **Check off completed [War Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?usp=sharing) assignments.** The three to five you put on there for the day. [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
9. **Record the results as they come in.** Whatever the work actually produced, recorded at the time.
10. **Look at this stuff.** Do not create all this proof and then ignore it. The wall fills up and your records show the work, and that gives you something real to base your self-image on.

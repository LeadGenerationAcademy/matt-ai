### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Handle whatever you were doing before you found this course.** Run your business. Complete your client work. Respond to messages. Attend meetings. Manage your employees. Put out fires. Spend time with your wife and children. Help your family. Run errands.
2. **Do it after your four hours, never before.** Most of this is reactive and theoretically infinite. There will always be another message, meeting, request, problem or person who needs something from you, and starting there will consume the entire day.
3. **Give the rest of your responsibilities your full attention.** The work that changes your life and business is already finished, so nothing is competing with it now.

### Completion Audit

- Did you handle whatever you were doing before you found this course?
- Did you do it after your four hours rather than before?
- Did you give the rest of your responsibilities your full attention?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take being fully present as the reward.** Your first four hours are done. The 10X Project moved forward, and you kept your promise to yourself.
2. **Give whatever is in front of you your full focus.** When you are running your business, you can focus on the business. When you are with your family, you can actually be with them.
3. **Handle the unexpected without half your mind attached to unfinished work.** You've already finished all the important work for the day.
4. **Stop feeling guilty for handling the rest of your life.** Be completely wherever you are.

### Completion Audit

- Did you take being fully present as the reward?
- Did you give whatever is in front of you your full focus?
- Did you handle the unexpected without half your mind attached to unfinished work?
- Did you stop feeling guilty for handling the rest of your life?

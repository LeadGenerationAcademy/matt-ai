### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take your unified vision and describe an ordinary Wednesday inside it.** This is the Average Wednesday document you left blank during Setup.
2. **Write in the first person, as if it is already your life.** We want to turn the vision into something you can actually experience in your mind.
3. **Start from waking up.** The first question is where you are when you do.
4. **Write where you are.** The place you wake up in that life, which comes out of the unified vision you just built.
5. **Write what you do.** The things that fill an ordinary Wednesday in that life.
6. **Write who is around you.** The people who are there on a normal day in that life.
7. **Write what you are working on.** The work itself, in enough detail that you could picture sitting down to it.
8. **Write how you feel while you are doing it.** The other answers describe the day. This one is what you will be visualizing later.
9. **Put your Life Vision into ChatGPT and have it give you an idea of what an Average Wednesday could look like.** This is how I start mine when the document is blank.
10. **Delete that and write it yourself.** The ChatGPT version helps me get started, but I still need to do the actual thinking.
11. **Keep developing the details.** This will become more vivid over time.
12. **Practice visualizing it until it starts feeling like you are experiencing the day.** That is what you are working toward.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Complete the five-minute stretching routine you created during Setup.** The one already sitting in your [stretching app](https://bend.com/), built around the stiffness and mobility problems you actually have.
2. **Use the same routine instead of searching for new stretches every morning.** The entire reason it was created in advance was to remove that decision.
3. **Move on as soon as you are finished.** Five minutes is enough to make your body feel substantially better, especially when you are lifting weights, walking every morning and sitting at a desk for long periods of time.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat choosing what to work on next as the cue.** Your first four hours belong to your 10X Project, so this covers everything after them.
2. **Look at your [War Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?usp=sharing).** Do not spend an hour deciding what to do when you already made that decision. [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
3. **Complete the assignments you put there.** These are the things you committed to completing before you went to bed.
4. **Check the work you delegated.** The assignments you put on your employees' War Maps, with the completion dates you gave them.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat choosing what to work on next as the cue?
- Did you look at your War Map?
- Did you complete the assignments you put there?
- Did you check the work you delegated?

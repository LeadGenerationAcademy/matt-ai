### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Time Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14t3J3f-tSw_N91AczMsPPPvkLnuwWwAevCEuPEB1esk/edit?usp=sharing).** It should already be pinned in your browser as "T," and yesterday's row sits on the current month's tab. [in this skill: assets/templates/tracking-spreadsheet/]
2. **Enter yesterday's wake-up time.** In h:mm AM/PM format, because a plain number gets read as a number and throws off every efficiency calculation on that row.
3. **Enter the time you completed each work hour.** The time you finished the hour, not the time you started it.
4. **Enter whether you rose immediately.** Yes or No in the Get Up Immediately column, from the dropdown.
5. **Enter any notes required to explain the day.** Free text, and this is where the reason behind a bad number goes.
6. **Look at the result.** Your total hours, consistency and efficiency all calculate on their own once the times are in.
7. **Acknowledge a bad number instead of explaining it away.** If your efficiency was bad, it was bad. Do not create a story about why the number does not count.
8. **Think briefly about what slowed you down and try to improve it today.** Briefly. Then move on to your War Map.

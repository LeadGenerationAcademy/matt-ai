# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #12: Daily Operating System
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/bc7debcc?md=098c7ad2daff41f1af254c7bd187ca81

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Morning

- You started the routine the moment you woke up, without checking your phone or lying there.
- You weighed yourself, drank your 500 mL of water, and completed the 20-minute treadmill walk and five-minute stretch.
- You reviewed your Self Mastery, Meaning and Thought Habit Lists.
- You took your morning reward and sat down to begin the Daily Setup.

### Daily 15-Minute Setup

- All four tabs were open and the 15-minute timer was running.
- Your financial, health and time records were updated.
- You reviewed the three to five assignments on your War Map for today.
- You finished the whole setup inside 15 minutes.
- You started Hour One the moment the setup finished.

### First Four Hours

- You completed four separate 60-minute hours, all of them on your 10X Project.
- Every hour had its output written on the legal pad and the Distraction Counter reset before it started.
- You stayed in the chair until every timer went off.
- You pressed the Distraction Counter and wrote down the cause every time you lost focus.
- A Sticky Note went on the wall and a Work Form was submitted after every hour.
- You reviewed the same three lists after every hour.
- You marked the calendar and took your four-hour reward.

#### Exercise

- On a gym day, the workout was completed and logged straight after your four hours.
- You took the reward you chose for it.

### Anti-Worry Hour

- On its scheduled day, you worked straight off your Anti-Worry List for 60 minutes.
- You took the next real action on anything you could not finish and updated the list.

### 2.0 You Hour

- You checked previous assignments before adding anything new.
- You worked on whatever actually needed attention rather than rebuilding every document.
- The hour produced something developed, decided, planned or worked through.
- You submitted a Work Form for the hour and took your reward.

### Rest Of Day

- Your business, clients, messages and family all got your attention after the four hours rather than before.
- You completed the War Map assignments you committed to for today.
- You read at least 20 pages.

### Night

- Your workspace is clean and tomorrow morning is laid out.
- Your phone is outside the bedroom and the room is cool and dark.
- You reviewed the day and confirmed the work was recorded.
- You reviewed the same three lists before bed.

### All Day Long

- You practiced self-mastery whenever the opportunity came up, and corrected it immediately when you did not.
- You behaved like the person in your 2.0 vision rather than waiting until you achieve the goals.
- You followed your Information Diet and your food plan, recording what you ate at the time.
- Worries, improvements and useful ideas went into the right list as they came up.
- You fixed anything that kept distracting you or making the correct action harder.
- Every completed hour was recorded and every piece of proof went somewhere you can see it.
- You corrected your state when you caught it slipping.

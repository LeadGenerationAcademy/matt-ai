### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat completing your first four work hours as the cue.** The four hours are done and nothing else took them, which is the whole point of protecting the first part of the day.
2. **Complete whatever scheduled block comes immediately afterward.** Depending on the day, that may be your workout, Anti-Worry Hour or 2.0 You Hour.
3. **Return to the chaos once the required blocks are finished.** The Rest Of Day is not necessarily rest. This is where running your business, answering clients, handling emergencies and spending time with your family all go.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat completing your first four work hours as the cue?
- Did you complete whatever scheduled block comes immediately afterward?
- Did you return to the chaos once the required blocks were finished?

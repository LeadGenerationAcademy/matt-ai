### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat the Anti-Worry Hour scheduled on your calendar as the cue.** One hour every week, which should already be blocked out from Setup.
2. **Add anything that starts worrying you during the week to your Anti-Worry List.** Do not stop your work to deal with it, because you will handle it during the Anti-Worry Hour.
3. **Open your Anti-Worry List when the scheduled time arrives.** The list in your Lists folder, holding the small unresolved problems that take up space in your head.
4. **Set a timer for 60 minutes.** Then begin. These tasks are usually not very difficult, you have just been avoiding them.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat the Anti-Worry Hour scheduled on your calendar as the cue?
- Did you add anything that started worrying you during the week to your Anti-Worry List?
- Did you open your Anti-Worry List when the scheduled time arrived?
- Did you set a timer for 60 minutes?

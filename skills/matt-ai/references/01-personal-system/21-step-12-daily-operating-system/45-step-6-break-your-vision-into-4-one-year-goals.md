### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Decide what you are going to accomplish in the next year.** Your life vision might take decades. That is fine, and it gives meaning to what you are doing, but the year in front of you still needs an answer.
2. **Break your vision down into four specific one-year goals.** Each one goes in one of the four One-Year Goal notes in your One Year folder, which you created during Setup.
3. **Do not go beyond four.** You are already running a business and living your life. These need enough attention for you to actually accomplish them.

### Completion Audit

- Did you decide what you are going to accomplish in the next year?
- Did you break your vision down into four specific one-year goals?
- Did you keep it to four?

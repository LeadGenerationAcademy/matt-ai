### Examples

This is an example of each list and what they'll look like as you keep adding to them.

**Self Mastery List**

1. When I wake up, I immediately go to my computer and start these four hours of work.
2. When my alarm goes off at 5:30am, I get up instead of hitting snooze.
3. When I want to stretch the break out, I am back at my desk within 10 to 15 minutes.
4. When I want to check my phone during a work hour, I leave it alone and get back to the assignment.
5. When I finish a work hour, I submit the Work Form right then instead of leaving it until tonight.
6. When something tries to take my morning, I protect the four hours and still finish by 11am.
7. When I want to skip the last exercise, I finish the workout I planned with proper form.
8. When I can't be bothered making food, I eat the meal I planned instead of ordering something else.
9. When an employee questions my decision, I let him finish before I start defending myself.
10. When my bedtime arrives, I close the computer and leave my phone outside the bedroom.

**Meaning List**

1. Doing today's work on the new offer is how I build the income that gets my parents out of their mortgage.
2. Finishing my four hours without dragging out the breaks is what lets me close the laptop when my kids get home.
3. Getting up when my alarm goes off is how I start trusting myself when I say I am going to do something.
4. Following my food plan is what stops me avoiding the camera every time my family takes a picture.
5. Finishing the full workout is what lets me keep up with my son as he gets older.
6. Going to bed at the time I chose is what stops my family getting the exhausted version of me every morning.
7. Doing my 2.0 You Hour every day is how I stop spending another five years building a life I never actually chose.
8. Writing my employee a complete brief is how I eventually take a vacation where my wife is not listening to client calls beside the pool.
9. Putting my phone away when my dad calls is me having these conversations while he is still here.
10. Submitting an honest Work Form is how I get evidence behind the things I tell myself about my work ethic.

**Thought Habit List**

1. When my alarm goes off, I instantly smile and think "yay" and then leap out of bed.
2. When I am done my morning routine, I think "I am so excited and lucky to have these four hours to develop the best version of myself and what I can do."
3. Whenever something is not my absolute best or the 10x standard of my previous best, I instantly think "no, that is not good enough, I need to do it better."
4. When the work gets hard, I instantly think "good, this is the part where everybody else quits."
5. When a prospect says no, I think "fine, next one," and I move straight to the next one.
6. When I see a competitor doing well, I think "great, so it works, now I know what is possible."
7. When I catch myself reaching for my phone, I instantly think "no" and I put it face down.
8. When I miss a workout, I think "one missed, I am not missing two," and I book the next one right there.
9. When I finish my four hours, I think "the day is already won and everything after this is extra."
10. When my numbers are down, I think "good, now I know exactly where the problem is," and I go find it.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Check in with your Self Mastery List when one of the situations on it shows up.** You already decided what you were going to do in that moment, so this is where you go to read it back while the moment is still in front of you.
2. **Check in with your Meaning List when something you committed to starts feeling pointless.** That is the exact moment the list was built for, not the moment after you have already skipped it.
3. **Check in with your Thought Habit List when one of the circumstances on it happens.** You already chose what you were going to think, so go read that instead of repeating whatever thought normally shows up.
4. **Do this whenever the situation comes up, not at a set time.** Your morning, between-hour and night reviews are already scheduled. This one is not, because the situations do not run on a schedule either.
5. **Correct it and keep going if you catch yourself late.** You do not need to wait until tomorrow to start behaving properly again.

### Completion Audit

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take a second to recognize the System is updated.** You know where your money is, you know what happened with your body, and you know how you used your time.
2. **Notice that the day is under control.** You know what must be done today, and nothing important is being hidden from you because you were scared to look at it.
3. **Give yourself credit for keeping another promise to yourself.** You tracked the truth even when the truth was ugly. That is self-mastery.

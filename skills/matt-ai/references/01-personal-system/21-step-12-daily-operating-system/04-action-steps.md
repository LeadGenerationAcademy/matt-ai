# Action Steps

Course: Step #12: Daily Operating System
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/bc7debcc?md=4da101d1d976447c8ba121865e4582d4

---
The Theory explains that the Setup gave you the tools, the environment and the documents. The Daily System is how you keep using them, and it becomes part of how you live.

In this course you will run the whole day, from waking up to going to bed.

You will go through it in order, starting with the morning routine and ending with the night routine, with the standards in All Day Long running through every part of it.

When you catch yourself slipping, correct it and continue. If the same problem keeps happening, work out what is causing it and change that part of the System instead of giving yourself the same motivational speech every morning.

### Table of Contents

##### Morning

1. **Cue:** Waking up is the cue, with no phone, no lying there and no second cue slotted in front of it.
2. **Note: Wake-Up Time:** 7:00 a.m. or earlier for most people, counted backwards from what your morning actually holds.
3. **Rise Immediately:** Feet on the floor instead of lying there negotiating with yourself.
4. **Weigh Yourself:** Straight to the scale under the same conditions every morning.
5. **Drink 500 mL Of Water:** Before you decide you are exhausted, with creatine in it if you take it.
6. **Complete 20-Minute Treadmill Walk:** Zone 2, split between your motivational video and reviewing the life you are building.
7. **Complete Five-Minute Stretch:** The same routine you built during Setup, not a new one every morning.
8. **Review Primarily These Three Lists:** Self Mastery, Meaning and Thought Habit, to prime your brain before the decisions start.
9. **Reward:** The one you chose during Setup, taken only once the whole routine is finished.

##### Daily 15-Minute Setup

1. **Cue:** Finishing the routine and sitting down, with all four tabs opened and the 15-minute timer started.
2. **Add Financial Data:** Today's balances plus your new transactions run through your Financial Tracking project.
3. **Add Health Data:** This morning's weight, last night's sleep, and yesterday's food, workout and steps.
4. **Add Time Data:** Yesterday's wake-up time and hour completion times, with a bad number acknowledged rather than explained away.
5. **Review Today's Three To Five Tasks:** The assignments already scheduled on your War Map during your 2.0 Hours.
6. **Immediately Start The First Four Hours:** Planned output written, counter reset, timer set, start.
7. **Reward:** The System is updated and the day is under control, which is another promise kept.

##### First Four Hours

1. **Cue:** The Daily Setup ending, plus every cue already sitting around your desk.
2. **As Fast As Possible:** Four actual hours as early and as close together as you can get them.
3. **Your 10X Project:** All four hours on the one project, held to a standard 10 times your previous best.
4. **Train Yourself To Literally Sit There:** Stay in the chair until the timer goes off, counting every distraction.
5. **Between-Hour Routine:** Sticky note, Work Form, break timer, then the next hour's output and a reset counter.
6. **Review Primarily These Three Lists:** The same three lists after every one of the four hours.
7. **Reward Upon Completion:** Final sticky note, final Work Form, the X on the calendar and your larger reward.

##### Exercise

1. **Cue:** The scheduled workout on your calendar, sitting immediately after your four work hours.
2. **Complete Three Workouts Per Week:** Six exercises, 15 working sets, eight controlled reps, everything logged while you train.
3. **Reward:** Roughly equal to your four-hour reward, taken only once the whole workout is finished and logged.

##### Anti-Worry Hour

1. **Cue:** The scheduled hour on your calendar, with the list open and a 60-minute timer running.
2. **Complete One Anti-Worry Hour Per Week:** Work straight off the list and take the next real action on anything you cannot finish.
3. **Reward:** Fewer unfinished tasks and less random anxiety following you through the week.

##### 2.0 You Hour

1. **Cue:** The scheduled hour plus an alarm, because this is the one most likely to get pushed.
2. **Check Previous Assignments:** Look at the actual work before you add a single new task.
3. **Develop Your Top Five Pillars:** Keep making the answers more complete instead of rewording the same goal.
4. **Work On Your Unified 2.0 Vision:** Gradually combine the five pillars into one life.
5. **Develop Your Average Wednesday:** An ordinary day inside that vision, written in the first person.
6. **Break Your Vision Into 4 One-Year Goals:** Four and no more, because they need enough attention to actually happen.
7. **List The Actions For Each Goal:** Everything you could do, written as tasks you could sit down and complete.
8. **Choose What Belongs In The Next 90 Days:** Checkmark the actions that can and should happen now, in the right order.
9. **Schedule And Delegate The Actions:** Three to five per day on your War Map, each with a time, a person and a priority.
10. **Review And Work On Your Lists:** Whichever ones actually need your attention, leaving Anti-Worry for its own hour.
11. **Reward:** Substantial, and only once the hour produced something developed, decided or planned.

##### Rest Of Day

1. **Cue:** Your four hours finished, plus whatever scheduled block came immediately after them.
2. **Complete Your Responsibilities:** Everything you were doing before this course, handled after the four hours rather than before.
3. **Read At Least 20 Pages Of The Two Books:** Between meetings, while waiting, in the evening or before bed.
4. **Reward:** Being fully present, with nothing important left waiting on you.

##### Night

1. **Cue:** Reaching the end of your day, with the work and your responsibilities done.
2. **Prepare For The Morning Routine:** Clean the workspace and lay out everything tomorrow morning needs.
3. **Prepare For A Good Sleep:** Phone outside the bedroom, room cool and dark, nothing left that can wake you.
4. **Review Daily Results:** Your hours, Work Forms, distraction totals and Efficiency Ratio, briefly and honestly.
5. **Review Primarily These Three Lists:** The last documents you look at before you sleep.
6. **Reward:** Going to bed with everything already handled.

##### All Day Long

1. **Practice Self Mastery:** Do what you said you would do, and correct it immediately when you do not.
2. **Check In With These Three Lists:** Self Mastery, Meaning and Thought Habit, used in the moment rather than on a schedule.
3. **Pay Attention To Your Thoughts:** Stop feeding a destructive thought once you have noticed it and get back to work.
4. **Think, Speak And Act In Alignment:** Behave like the person in your 2.0 vision already exists.
5. **Practice Autosuggestion:** Talk to yourself as that person, with a deliberate check-in between work hours.
6. **Keep Using Your War Map:** It already decided what you work on next, so stop deciding again.
7. **Keep Improving Your Environment:** Fix whatever keeps distracting you as you notice it.
8. **Follow Your Information Diet:** Every time you pick up your phone or computer, and close any gap you find.
9. **Follow Food Plan & Track What You Eat:** Recorded at the time, honestly, in your calorie-tracking app.
10. **Track Every Work Hour:** Your wake-up time and every completed hour, including your 2.0 Hour.
11. **Correct Your State When It's Slipping:** Joy List, Gratitude List or your symbol, then deal with what needs doing.
12. **Keep Your Energy Up:** Notice what affects how you feel and repeat whatever works.
13. **Update Your Lists As Things Come Up:** A few seconds to write it in the right place, then back to what you were doing.
14. **Make Your Proof Visible:** Turn completed work into something you can see, then actually look at it.
15. **Reward:** These actions are their own reward, so take a second to recognize what you did.

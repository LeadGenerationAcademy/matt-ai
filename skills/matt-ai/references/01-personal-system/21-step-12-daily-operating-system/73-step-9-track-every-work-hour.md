### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Record your wake-up time.** This is what your [Time Tracking spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14t3J3f-tSw_N91AczMsPPPvkLnuwWwAevCEuPEB1esk/edit?usp=sharing) uses to work out your efficiency for the day. [in this skill: assets/templates/tracking-spreadsheet/]
2. **Record the time you complete each work hour throughout the day.** This continues beyond your first four hours. This includes submitting your [Work Forms](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1i7ZgFfPYRK6CgJ7BtblZNm2lQR2_QoEnl853HOL15co/copy) and logging it in your [Time Tracking spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14t3J3f-tSw_N91AczMsPPPvkLnuwWwAevCEuPEB1esk/edit?usp=sharing). [in this skill: assets/templates/tracking-spreadsheet/]
3. **Complete the Work Form after each hour.** Do not submit everything at the end of the day from memory. This is how you see where your time went and what you produced.
4. **Complete a Work Form for your 2.0 Hour too.** Record what you actually worked on. Developing your vision, working through your lists, and organizing your plans are real work, and they should have a record.

### Completion Audit

- Did you record your wake-up time?
- Did you record the time you completed each work hour throughout the day?
- Did you complete the Work Form after each hour?
- Did you complete a Work Form for your 2.0 Hour?

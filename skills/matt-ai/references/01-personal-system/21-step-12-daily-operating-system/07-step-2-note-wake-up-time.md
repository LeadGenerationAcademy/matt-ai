### Example

**Wake-Up Time: 7:00 a.m.**

7:00 to 7:30: Morning Routine

7:30 to 7:45: 15-Minute Daily Setup

7:45 to 8:45: Hour One

8:45 to 8:55: Break

8:55 to 9:55: Hour Two

9:55 to 10:05: Break

10:05 to 11:05: Hour Three

11:05 to 11:15: Break

11:15 to 12:15: Hour Four

Four hours of work finished at 12:15 p.m., 5 hours and 15 minutes after waking.

The same sequence starting at 9:00 a.m. does not finish until 2:15 p.m.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Count backwards from what your morning actually holds.** This routine, the 15-Minute Daily Setup, and four hours of work with breaks. Start at 9:00 a.m. and you have eaten most of a normal workday before touching a single one of your existing responsibilities.
2. **Adjust the time if your life genuinely runs on a different schedule.** Genuinely, as in shift work or a different time zone, not as in you prefer your mornings to start slowly.
3. **Block the morning routine, the Daily Setup and your four work hours in your calendar.** Marked busy, so nobody can drop a meeting into the middle of them and the whole thing is finished before the day starts pulling you in 50 directions.

### Completion Audit

- Did you count backwards from what your morning actually holds?
- Did you set your wake-up time at 7:00 a.m. or earlier?
- Did you adjust the time if your life genuinely runs on a different schedule?
- Did you block the morning routine, the Daily Setup and your four work hours in your calendar?

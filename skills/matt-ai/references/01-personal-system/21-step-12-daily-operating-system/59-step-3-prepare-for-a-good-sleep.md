### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Brush your teeth and complete whatever else you normally do before bed.** None of this replaces what you already do at night.
2. **Put your phone outside the bedroom.** Do not keep it beside the bed and pretend that this time you will have enough self-control to leave it alone.
3. **Make the room cool and dark.** The quality of tomorrow's work will be affected by what you do tonight.
4. **Close the blinds.** Including whatever light comes in before your alarm goes off.
5. **Turn on your white-noise machine if you use one.** Skip this if you don't have one.
6. **Remove anything likely to wake you up.** Anything that beeps, buzzes or lights up during the night.
7. **Read for a little while if you cannot fall asleep.** Do not pick up your phone and blast your brain with garbage until 2:00 a.m.

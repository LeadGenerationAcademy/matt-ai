### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat your internal conversation throughout the day as the cue.** You are already talking to yourself all day, so pay attention to what you are saying.
2. **Talk to yourself as if the person in your 2.0 vision already exists.** Use that conversation to think as that person, with those standards already yours.
3. **Behave accordingly.** Repeating the words only does something when your actions back them up.
4. **Check in with yourself deliberately between work hours.** This happens during the between-hour routine. You do not need an alarm interrupting you halfway through focused work.
5. **Review the relevant parts of your vision.** Whichever part applies to what you are about to sit down and do.
6. **Remind yourself how the person in your 2.0 vision thinks and acts.** Actually pay attention to what you are telling yourself instead of rushing through the words so you can say you did it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat your internal conversation throughout the day as the cue?
- Did you talk to yourself as if the person in your 2.0 vision already exists?
- Did you behave accordingly?
- Did you check in with yourself deliberately between work hours?
- Did you review the relevant parts of your vision?
- Did you remind yourself how the person in your 2.0 vision thinks and acts?

### Night

#### Prepare For Morning Routine

- Desk cleared, garbage out, nothing on it that tomorrow's work does not need
- Water bottle filled, 500 mL, wherever you will be standing when you want it
- Creatine measured out beside it, if you take it
- Running shoes out, somewhere you will walk past
- Socks and workout clothes beside the shoes, in one pile
- Headphones charged and in the same place they are every night
- RENPHO scale where it belongs, so you walk straight to it
- Alarm set, with tomorrow's wake-up time already decided
- Anything else you need left somewhere obvious

#### Prepare For A Good Sleep

- Teeth brushed and whatever else you already do at night, unchanged
- Phone outside the bedroom, not on the nightstand face down
- Room cool
- Blinds closed, including against whatever comes in before the alarm
- White noise machine on, if you use one
- Anything that beeps, buzzes or lights up in the night removed or turned off
- A book within reach in case you cannot fall asleep

8. When I can't be bothered making food, I eat the meal I planned instead of ordering something else
9. When an employee questions my decision, I let him finish before I start defending myself
10. When my bedtime arrives, I close the computer and leave my phone outside the bedroom

### Paying Attention To Your Thoughts

Here are some examples of destructive thoughts:

- Running imaginary arguments in your head

- Resenting people
- Assuming things will fail
- Telling yourself you are not built for this
- Envying people who are doing better than you
- Staying angry about things you cannot change
- Doubting whether any of it will work

### Improving Your Environment

- You keep going back to a site that gets past your blockers -> Add it to your Bad List and block it in Opal, Focus Me or Brick
- You keep forgetting something -> Write it on a Sticky Note and stick it under your monitor
- You are not drinking enough water during the day -> Keep the water bottle on your desk
- You keep picking up your phone during work hours -> Leave it in another room before the timer starts
- You cannot hear the timer go off from where you take your break -> Move the timer somewhere you can hear it
- Someone keeps walking in mid-hour -> Close the door and tell them the hours you are working
- You keep reaching for something on your desk that is not the work -> Take it off the desk
- Your desk is a mess by Hour 3 -> Clear it during the between-hour routine

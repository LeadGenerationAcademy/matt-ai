### Examples

- Go to a coffee shop, get a coffee, and chill while watching YouTube
- Go out and get lunch somewhere instead of eating at your desk
- Spend time outside with no phone
- Take time with your wife, kids, or a friend
- Sit in the sauna or take a long shower with nothing scheduled after it
- Watch an episode of a TV show
- Play video games
- Take a nap
- Get a massage
- Go for a swim

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create the final [Sticky Note](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes).** Same as the other three, so write what hour you completed, what you worked on, when you completed it and how many distractions you recorded.
2. **Submit the [Work Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/u/7/d/1i7ZgFfPYRK6CgJ7BtblZNm2lQR2_QoEnl853HOL15co/copy).** Same ending process as every other hour, which records it and updates your spreadsheet and leaderboard.
3. **Let your accountability partners know that your four hours are complete.** Four finished hours is the part worth telling them about, not another single hour.
4. **Record your final completion time.** This goes into your [Time Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14t3J3f-tSw_N91AczMsPPPvkLnuwWwAevCEuPEB1esk/edit?usp=sharing), which works out your efficiency from your wake up time and the time you finished. [in this skill: assets/templates/tracking-spreadsheet/]
5. **Look at your efficiency ratio.** Tomorrow, try to beat it.
6. **Put a giant X through the day on your [physical calendar](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physical+wall+calendar).** After enough days you end up with a chain you do not want to break.
7. **Take your larger four-hour reward.** You earned it. Take 30 to 60 minutes if you want it.
8. **Go to the gym or move into the rest of your responsibilities.** You already completed the most important work and won the day, so whatever the rest of it turns into does not take that away.

### Completion Audit

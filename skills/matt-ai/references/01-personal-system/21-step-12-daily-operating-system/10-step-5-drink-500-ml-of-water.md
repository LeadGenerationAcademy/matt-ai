### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Drink 500 mL of water as quickly as you reasonably can.** A normal bottle is roughly the right amount, and it should already be filled from last night's preparation.
2. **Get the water into your body before deciding that you are exhausted.** You have probably gone seven to nine hours without drinking anything, which is exactly when crawling back into bed starts sounding reasonable.
3. **Put your [creatine](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=creatine+monohydrate) in the water if you decided to take it.** Creatine is optional. The water is not.

### Completion Audit

- Did you drink 500 mL of water as quickly as you reasonably could?
- Did you get the water into your body before deciding that you were exhausted?
- Did you put your creatine in the water if you decided to take it?

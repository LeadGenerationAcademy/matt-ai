### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Begin the first work hour as soon as the Daily Setup is complete.** Do not finish the Setup and then check your messages, make breakfast, walk around, watch something or take a little break.
2. **Write the intended result of the first hour on your [legal pad](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=small+yellow+legal+notepads).** Exactly what you intend to have finished in the next 60 minutes, written down before anything starts.
3. **Reset the [distraction counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter).** The handheld counter that should be sitting directly in front of you, back to zero before the hour begins.
4. **Set the [timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer) for 60 minutes.** The kitchen timer that should be sitting underneath your monitor.
5. **Start.** Do not sit there thinking about working. Rip it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you begin the first work hour as soon as the Daily Setup was complete?
- Did you write the intended result of the first hour on your legal pad?
- Did you reset the distraction counter?
- Did you set the timer for 60 minutes?
- Did you start?

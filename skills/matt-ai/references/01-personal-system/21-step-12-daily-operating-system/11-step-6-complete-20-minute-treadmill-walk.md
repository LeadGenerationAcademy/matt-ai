### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Complete the 20-minute treadmill walk you prepared during Setup.** Your shoes, socks, workout clothes and headphones should already be out from last night.
2. **Get your heart rate somewhere around Zone 2.** Your wearable will give you a rough idea. For me that is about three miles per hour at a 12% incline, and yours will depend on your current fitness.
3. **Walk hard enough to wake yourself up and create some momentum.** This is not supposed to destroy you before work.
4. **Split the walk between the video and your documents.** Twenty minutes, divided however you want between the two.
5. **Listen to your motivational video while you walk.** The one you chose during Setup, the one that actually does something to you.
6. **Begin reviewing the life you are building.** Your goals and your lists. The purpose is to deliberately change your physical and mental state before the rest of the day begins.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat using your phone or computer as the cue.** This applies every time you pick either one up.
2. **Follow the Information Diet you established during Setup.** Your bad list, your good list, and the four or more layers of blocking you put between yourself and the bad information.
3. **Fix the gap if you find a way around one of your blockers.** Do not spend time figuring out how to defeat something you installed specifically because you wanted help controlling yourself.
4. **Add the site or app to the block list.** Add it to [Opal](https://opalapp.com/) and [FocusMe](https://focusme.com/) on your computer. Add it to [Opal](https://opalapp.com/), [Brick](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/brick-ditch-distractions/id6448794069), and [screen time limit](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e3de926b?md=2f5e4ba6849943c59bea7ebaca162538) on your phone.
5. **Keep using the blockers.** Eventually we want you to become someone who is no longer interested in constantly consuming that crap. Until then, there is no prize for making this harder than it needs to be.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat using your phone or computer as the cue?
- Did you follow the Information Diet you established during Setup?
- Did you fix the gap when you found a way around one of your blockers?
- Did you add the site or app to the block list?
- Are you still using the blockers?

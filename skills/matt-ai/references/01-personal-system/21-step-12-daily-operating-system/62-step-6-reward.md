### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take going to bed as the reward.** Honestly, sleep is one of the best rewards you can receive.
2. **Recognize that everything is already handled.** The day is finished, your workspace is clean, tomorrow morning is prepared, and you have done everything you reasonably can to set yourself up for another successful day.
3. **Go to sleep.** You do not need to lay there thinking about what you forgot or what you need to do when you wake up.

### Completion Audit

- Did you take going to bed as the reward?
- Did you recognize that everything is already handled?
- Did you go to sleep?

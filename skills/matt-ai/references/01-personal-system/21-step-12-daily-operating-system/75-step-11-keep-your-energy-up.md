### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat the gaps between work hours and noticing your energy dropping as the cue.** The between-hour break is the scheduled version of this. The rest happens whenever you catch it.
2. **Pay attention to the things that affect how you feel.** If you keep eating huge meals and then struggling to work, change what you are doing.
3. **Follow your food plan.** You can leave your first main meal until after the four hours if that works for you. It is not a requirement.
4. **Use your breaks to move around.** Walking around and stretching are already part of the between-hour routine you built during Setup.
5. **Take the previous night's sleep seriously.** The quality of tomorrow's work is affected by what you do tonight.
6. **Notice what helps you maintain your energy and repeat it.** The point is to support your ability to work, not to ignore hunger or make yourself feel worse.

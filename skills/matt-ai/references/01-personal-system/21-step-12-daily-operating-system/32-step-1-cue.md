### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat the scheduled workout on your calendar as the cue.** Three sessions a week, already blocked out from Setup, with the workouts already saved in your workout-tracking app.
2. **Schedule the gym immediately after your first four work hours.** You finish the work, submit the final Work Form, cross the day off your calendar, and the gym is the next thing sitting there.
3. **Go directly to the gym.** Do not finish working and sit around for two hours debating whether you feel like training. Your first four hours are done.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat the scheduled workout on your calendar as the cue?
- Did you schedule the gym immediately after your first four work hours?
- Did you go directly to the gym?

### Framework & Criteria

1. **Cue:** Waking up, whether that happens naturally or because of an alarm.
2. **Craving:** The life you are building, the 10X Project you get to work on, and the reward you have chosen for completing the routine.
3. **Response:** Completing every step of the morning routine.
4. **Reward:** The reward you chose, taken at the end of the routine.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat waking up as the cue.** Natural or alarm, it makes no difference. The moment you are awake, the routine has already started.
2. **Do not create another cue after it.** Anything you slot in between waking up and starting becomes the real cue, and then the routine only happens when that thing happens.
3. **Do not check your phone.** Picking it up hands the first decision of your day to whoever messaged you overnight.
4. **Do not lie there.** Twenty minutes of negotiating with yourself and the routine now begins whenever you happen to feel like it.
5. **Start the routine the moment you are awake.** Note your wake-up time, rise, weigh yourself, water, treadmill, stretch, review your lists, reward. In that order, every morning.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat waking up as the cue?
- Did you avoid creating another cue after it?
- Did you avoid checking your phone?
- Did you avoid lying there?
- Did you start the routine the moment you were awake?

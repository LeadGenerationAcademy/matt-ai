### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the five pillars you created during Setup.** I view these as the five biggest accomplishments I genuinely care about achieving in my life. You could call them life goals.
2. **Ask yourself what you would achieve if you could only achieve five things in your entire life.** These should be huge. Much bigger than what you currently feel capable of achieving.
3. **Use the [Goal Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/121OlUONCeMXAQlhiTLywKPTjB8G8KqwY4w9TvYTmXpI/edit?usp=sharing).** The same one from Setup, covering what you want, what it looks like, and what achieving it would actually represent to you. [in this skill: assets/templates/goal-template/]
4. **Make your answers more complete as you understand more about what you actually want.** You are not recreating this document every day, you are continuing the work you already started.
5. **Check that achieving the five would imply a lot of the other things you care about have been achieved too.** Think about everything that would need to happen, everything you would need to learn, and who you would need to become. You should find that a lot of your smaller goals fit inside them.
6. **Actually think about your answers instead of just changing the wording.** Rewording the same goal is not the same as working out whether it is the right goal.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the five pillars you created during Setup?
- Did you ask yourself what you would achieve if you could only achieve five things in your entire life?
- Did you use the Goal Template?

- Did you check that achieving the five would imply a lot of the other things you care about have been achieved too?

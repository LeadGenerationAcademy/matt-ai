### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Go through the possible actions under each goal.** These are the lists you built in the last step, one goal at a time.
2. **Put a checkmark beside the ones that can and should be completed in the next 90 days.** Even one year is a long time to plan in detail. Things change, and doing the work reveals steps you could not have known about beforehand.
3. **Pay attention to the order.** Some things need to happen before others. Some good ideas will have to wait because something else matters more right now.
4. **Keep updating it as you make progress.** You now have a much smaller collection of actions to schedule.

### Completion Audit

- Did you go through the possible actions under each goal?
- Did you put a checkmark beside the ones that can and should be completed in the next 90 days?
- Did you pay attention to the order?
- Are you updating it as you make progress?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat finishing your morning routine and sitting down at your desk as the cue.** There should be almost no thinking required to begin. You finished the routine, you sat down, the setup has started.
2. **Open your [Financial Tracking](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=1818769831#gid=1818769831), [Health Tracking](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?gid=2107169354#gid=2107169354), [Time Tracking](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14t3J3f-tSw_N91AczMsPPPvkLnuwWwAevCEuPEB1esk/edit?gid=52205295#gid=52205295) and [War Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?usp=sharing) tabs immediately.** All four are already pinned in your browser, so this is opening tabs rather than going looking for anything. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/] [in this skill: assets/templates/health-tracking-template/] [in this skill: assets/templates/tracking-spreadsheet/] [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
3. **Start the [15-minute timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer).** This exists to get you into the work. It cannot turn into another hour of moving tasks around and pretending to be productive.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat finishing your morning routine and sitting down at your desk as the cue?
- Did you open your Financial, Health, Time and War Map tabs immediately?
- Did you start the 15-minute timer?

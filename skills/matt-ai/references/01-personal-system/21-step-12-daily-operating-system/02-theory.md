The Setup gave you the tools, environment, and documents. The Daily System is how you keep using them.

This becomes part of how you live. As you notice what works and what needs to change, you improve the System.

### Morning

Your day begins when you wake up. You get out of bed, weigh yourself, drink your water, and complete your 20-minute walk and five-minute stretch.

You also review your core lists so the standards, thoughts, and reasons you have chosen are fresh in your mind. Then you take your morning reward and sit down to begin the Daily Setup.

### Daily 15-Minute Setup

You update your financial, health, and time records, then review the three to five assignments on your War Map for today.

This gives you awareness of what happened and a clear idea of what needs your attention next. When the 15 minutes are finished, you immediately begin your first work hour.

### First Four Hours

You complete four separate 60-minute periods of Deep Work on your most important stuff, as early and as close together as possible.

Each hour has a specific assignment, a timer, and a distraction count. Afterward, you create your Sticky Note, submit your Work Form, and review your core lists. Your recorded hour updates the Leaderboard and notifies your accountability partners.

Between hours, you follow your chosen break routine and use the break timer to tell you when to return.

After all four hours, you mark the calendar and take your larger reward. The Daily Setup and 2.0 You Hour are separate from these four hours.

### Exercise

Three days per week, you complete the workout you scheduled during Setup, ideally after your first four work hours.

You record the workout so you can see your progress and know what to work on next time. Once the workout is complete, you take the reward you chose for it.

### Anti-Worry Hour

Once per week, you work through the practical problems on your Anti-Worry List.

This is when you make the appointments, send the emails, and handle the small unresolved tasks that have been bothering you. During the rest of the week, the list gives you somewhere to capture those things without interrupting your work.

### 2.0 You Hour

Every day, you spend an hour deliberately working on yourself and the life you are creating.

This is where you develop your Life Vision and Average Wednesday, work through your lists, and turn your one-year goals into plans for the next 90 days. You also check previous assignments and schedule or delegate what needs to happen next.

You continue whatever needs attention. You are not rebuilding every document every day.

The hour should produce actual thinking, decisions, or progress. You record it with a Work Form and take your chosen reward.

### Rest Of Day

You handle the rest of your business and personal responsibilities. That includes clients, employees, meetings, messages, appointments, and time with your family.

Your War Map tells you which other assignments you committed to completing. Your reading fits into this part of the day too.

The important work already has protected time, so these responsibilities can receive your attention afterward.

### Night

You briefly review the day, confirm your work was recorded, and prepare what you need for tomorrow morning.

Your workspace is ready, your morning supplies are in place, and your phone stays outside the bedroom. You review your core lists again and go to bed at the time you chose.

What you do tonight affects how tomorrow begins.

### All Day Long

You practice self-mastery whenever the opportunity appears. Your Self Mastery, Meaning, Foundational Causes, and Thought Habit Lists help you remember how you want to respond and why it matters.

You follow your Information Diet and food plan, record completed work, and capture worries or useful ideas in the appropriate place. Your Joy and Gratitude Lists are available when you need them.

When you catch yourself slipping, correct it and continue. If the same problem keeps happening, work out what is causing it and change that part of the System.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Clean your workspace and put everything back where it belongs.** You do not want to begin the next day surrounded by the physical and visual noise left over from today.
2. **Throw away the garbage.** Whatever collected on the desk today goes out tonight.
3. **Remove anything you do not need.** Only what tomorrow's work requires should still be there.
4. **Leave the desk ready for tomorrow.** You should be able to sit down and start rather than tidy up first.
5. **Fill the [water bottle](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=water+bottles).** Done tonight so it is already sitting there in the morning.

1. **Prepare the [creatine](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=creatine+monohydrate) if you use it.** Skip this if you don't use creatine.
2. **Put out your running shoes.** Somewhere you will see them when you get up.
3. **Put out your socks and workout clothes.** Beside the shoes, so it is one thing to pick up.
4. **Prepare your headphones.** Charged and in the same place every night.
5. **Set your alarm.** Decide tomorrow's wake-up time tonight.
6. **Make sure your [scale](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=RENPHO+smart+scale) is where it belongs.** You go straight to it after you rise.
7. **Leave anything else you need somewhere obvious.** You should not wake up and begin searching for shoes, filling water bottles, finding headphones or making decisions. Everything should already be waiting for you.

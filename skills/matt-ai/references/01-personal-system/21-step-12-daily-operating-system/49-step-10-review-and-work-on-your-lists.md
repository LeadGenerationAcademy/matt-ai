### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Work on what actually needs your attention.** You do not need to work through every list every day.
2. **Work on your Self Mastery List.** Look at where you keep losing control or breaking your own standards, then work through what needs to change.
3. **Work on your Forgiveness List.** Any person, event, or part of yourself you already know you need to forgive.
4. **Work on your Improvement List.** Something you wrote down during the day may need a decision, some thought, or an action added to one of your plans. This is when you deal with it.
5. **Use your Joy and Gratitude Lists as needed.** The things that reliably change your state, and the things you genuinely feel grateful for right now.
6. **Leave your Anti-Worry List for its own scheduled hour.** Do not spend this entire session booking appointments and handling errands.

### Completion Audit

- Did you work on what actually needs your attention?
- Did you work on your Self Mastery List?
- Did you work on your Forgiveness List?
- Did you work on your Improvement List?
- Did you use your Joy and Gratitude Lists as needed?
- Did you leave your Anti-Worry List for its own scheduled hour?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Look at what you removed from the list.** Knocking this stuff off your list is its own reward.
2. **Recognize how long some of it had been bothering you compared with how little time it actually required.** These tasks are usually not very difficult. You just keep avoiding them, so they sit in your head for weeks or months.
3. **Close the list and continue with your day.** You finish the hour with fewer unfinished tasks, fewer things occupying the back of your mind and less random anxiety following you through the week.

### Completion Audit

- Did you look at what you removed from the list?
- Did you recognize how long some of it had been bothering you compared with how little time it actually required?
- Did you close the list and continue with your day?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Put all four hours toward your current 10X Project.** The 15-minute setup does not count toward them, and neither does your 2.0 Hour.
2. **Hold it to a 10X standard.** It should be one of the best and most important things you have ever created.
3. **Treat every part of it as something worth doing properly.** There should not be anything inside it that you look at and say, "Whatever. I just needed to get that done."
4. **View it as your masterpiece.** You are putting four hours a day into this, every day, for as long as it takes.
5. **Expect the work to change you as much as the project changes your business.** It should substantially improve your business while forcing you to become a better person in order to finish it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you put all four hours toward your current 10X Project?
- Are you holding it to a 10X standard?
- Are you treating every part of it as something worth doing properly?
- Are you viewing it as your masterpiece?
- Are you expecting the work to change you as much as the project changes your business?

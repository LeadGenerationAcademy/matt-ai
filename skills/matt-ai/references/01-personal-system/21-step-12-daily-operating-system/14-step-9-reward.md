### Examples

- Walk to the coffee shop and get a coffee
- Go for a short walk
- Have a drink you enjoy
- Spend a few minutes on your phone
- Drink a sparkling water
- Watch a short video
- Listen to your favorite song
- Make your first coffee of the day at home
- Sit outside with your drink for ten minutes
- Take a hot shower

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make sure your reward is something you actually enjoy and can repeat every morning.** Something you do not care about will not do the job, and something you cannot have daily will not last the week.
2. **Keep it smaller than the reward for completing all four work hours.** This is the second most substantial recurring reward of the day. The one for the four work hours stays the largest.
3. **Complete the entire morning routine before you take it.** Giving it to yourself early breaks the connection between finishing the routine and getting the reward.
4. **Take the reward you selected during Setup.** This closes the habit loop and teaches your brain that waking up and completing the routine leads to something satisfying.
5. **Begin the 15-Minute Daily Setup.** The morning routine ends here and the Daily Setup starts straight after it.

### Completion Audit

- Is your reward something you actually enjoy and can repeat every morning?
- Is it smaller than the reward for completing all four work hours?
- Did you complete the entire morning routine before taking it?
- Did you take the reward you selected during Setup?
- Did you begin the 15-Minute Daily Setup?

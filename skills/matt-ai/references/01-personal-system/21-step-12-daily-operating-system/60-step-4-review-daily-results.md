### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Briefly review what happened today.** Do not conduct a two-hour investigation into your entire life. Look at the result and be honest.
2. **Look at your completed work hours.** Whether you actually finished all four.
3. **Look at your Work Forms.** The ones you submitted after each hour, automatically logged in your [Accountability Work Form spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ZxEcvABm7l0Bz4Jxs9l-VqMGMHZPLDdhgW-l1sf-NyA/edit?usp=sharing). [in this skill: assets/templates/accountability-work-form-spreadsheet-template/]
4. **Look at your distraction totals.** The number from your Distraction Counter at the end of each hour. This should be automatically logged in your [Accountability Work Form spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ZxEcvABm7l0Bz4Jxs9l-VqMGMHZPLDdhgW-l1sf-NyA/edit?usp=sharing). [in this skill: assets/templates/accountability-work-form-spreadsheet-template/]
5. **Look at your Efficiency Ratio.** Your [Time Tracking spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14t3J3f-tSw_N91AczMsPPPvkLnuwWwAevCEuPEB1esk/edit?usp=sharing) works this out from your wake-up time and the time you finished. [in this skill: assets/templates/tracking-spreadsheet/]
6. **Confirm that everything was recorded properly.** So tomorrow's Daily Setup does not begin with you trying to remember what happened.
7. **Identify what caused it if your efficiency was worse.** Maybe you took too long between hours, checked your phone, allowed someone to interrupt you or waited too long to begin.

### Completion Audit

- Did you briefly review what happened today?
- Did you look at your completed work hours?
- Did you look at your Work Forms?
- Did you look at your distraction totals?
- Did you look at your Efficiency Ratio?
- Did you confirm that everything was recorded properly?
- If your efficiency was worse, did you identify what caused it?

### Examples

- You keep going back to a site that gets past your blockers -> Add it to your Bad List and block it in Opal, Focus Me or Brick
- You keep forgetting something -> Write it on a Sticky Note and stick it under your monitor
- You are not drinking enough water during the day -> Keep the water bottle on your desk
- You keep picking up your phone during work hours -> Leave it in another room before the timer starts
- You cannot hear the timer go off from where you take your break -> Move the timer somewhere you can hear it
- Someone keeps walking in mid-hour -> Close the door and tell them the hours you are working
- You keep reaching for something on your desk that is not the work -> Take it off the desk
- Your desk is a mess by Hour 3 -> Clear it during the between-hour routine

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Treat noticing something repeatedly distracting you or making the correct action harder as the cue.** We did a lot of environment design during Setup, but you are going to discover things that are specific to you.
2. **Work out what specifically needs to change.** Maybe something needs to be blocked. Maybe a reminder needs to be moved somewhere you will actually see it. Maybe you keep leaving something you need in another room.
3. **Fix it as you notice it.** Your environment should become more helpful the longer you use it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you treat noticing something repeatedly distracting you or making the correct action harder as the cue?
- Did you work out what specifically needs to change?
- Did you fix it as you noticed it?

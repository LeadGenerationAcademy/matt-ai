### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Financial Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=1818769831#gid=1818769831).** It should already be pinned in your browser as "F", and the balances go on the Bank Balance tab. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
2. **Record the current balance of every account you included during [Setup](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/aaa118a0?md=3b54b1e617d64fbcb4f0ff4d990a87bb).** Every bank account, credit card, payment processor and financial account, entered on today's row.
3. **Collect the new transactions since your previous update.** Screenshots, copied transaction tables or exported files all work.
4. **Remove account numbers and anything else the AI does not need.** None of it helps the AI sort a transaction, and there is no reason to hand it over.
5. **Send the transactions to your Financial Tracking project.** This is the project you already configured with your currency, your entities, your accounts and your categories.
6. **Have it return them in the correct spreadsheet format.** The same nine columns your Transaction Log uses, in the same order.
7. **Paste them into the current month.** Into the Transaction Log at the bottom of that month's tab, and only across as far as Conversion Rate, because USD Amount works itself out.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Financial Tracking Spreadsheet?
- Did you record the current balance of every account you included during Setup?
- Did you collect the new transactions since your previous update?
- Did you remove account numbers and anything else the AI does not need?
- Did you send the transactions to your Financial Tracking project?
- Did it return them in the correct spreadsheet format?
- Did you paste them into the current month?

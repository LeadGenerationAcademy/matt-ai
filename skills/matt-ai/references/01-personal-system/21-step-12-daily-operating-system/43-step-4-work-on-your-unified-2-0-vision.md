### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Take your five pillars and gradually combine them into one vision.** This is the Life Vision document you left blank during Setup, because the pillars were not developed enough yet.
2. **Look for the overlap between them.** There should be a lot. These are five parts of the same life, and if every goal requires you to live in a completely different way, you have something to work through.
3. **Write about what you achieve, what it means to you, how it feels, and who you become.** Bring everything together so you can understand what the whole thing looks like.
4. **Include the causes you need to create and the standards you need to live by.** Not only what you end up with, but what has to happen and how you have to live to get there.
5. **Keep developing it as the individual pillars become clearer.** That is the 2.0 version of you that you are working toward.

### Completion Audit

- Did you take your five pillars and gradually combine them into one vision?
- Did you look for the overlap between them?
- Did you write about what you achieve, what it means to you, how it feels, and who you become?
- Did you include the causes you need to create and the standards you need to live by?
- Are you developing it further as the individual pillars become clearer?

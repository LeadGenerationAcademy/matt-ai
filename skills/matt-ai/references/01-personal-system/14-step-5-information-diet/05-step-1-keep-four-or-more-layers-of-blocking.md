### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep at least four layers between you and the bad information.** Nothing is truly unblockable. The final goal is to become someone who no longer wants this garbage. Until then, four is the minimum.
2. **Confirm your four phone layers are in place.** Screen Time, the burner iCloud account holding your Screen Time password, [Brick](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/brick-ditch-distractions/id6448794069) and [Opal](https://opalapp.com/). Screen Time gets turned on in Step #5 and the burner account gets created in Step #6. Those two are not done yet.
3. **Confirm your computer layers are in place.** [FocusMe](https://focusme.com/), [Opal](https://opalapp.com/), [Tab Limiter](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tab-limit/djdcpjfmniidpdaalloiipgjphgikejl), [YouTube Feed Blocker](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/block-youtube-feed-homepa/lcpclaffcdiihapebmfgcmmplphbkjmd), [IGPlus](https://weblxapplications.com/en/products/igplus), [News Feed Eradicator](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/news-feed-eradicator/fjcldmjmjhkklehbacihaiopjklihlgg) and [SocialFocus](https://socialfocus.app/) should all be installed from Setup.
4. **Install anything you skipped before moving on.** One blocker is easy to disable when you chimp out. Four usually creates enough friction that your brain eventually stops treating the information as available.

### Completion Audit

- Are you keeping at least four layers between you and the bad information?
- Are your four phone layers in place?
- Are your computer layers in place?
- Did you install anything you skipped?

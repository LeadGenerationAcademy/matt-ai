### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Click the [Tab Limiter](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tab-limit/djdcpjfmniidpdaalloiipgjphgikejl) icon in your browser.** If you pinned it back in Setup it is sitting there already. If not, click the extensions icon and find Tab Limiter in the list.
2. **Change the number in the "Max number of tabs" box to 3.** This is the only setting the extension has. You do not need more than three tabs while working. Anything beyond that is realistically cope.
3. **Press Enter to save it.** Nothing else to confirm. The limit is live from that point.
4. **Open three tabs and confirm a fourth is blocked.** If a fourth opens anyway, the number did not save.

### Completion Audit

- Did you click the Tab Limiter icon in your browser?
- Did you change the number to 3?
- Did you press Enter to save it?
- Did you open three tabs and confirm a fourth was blocked?

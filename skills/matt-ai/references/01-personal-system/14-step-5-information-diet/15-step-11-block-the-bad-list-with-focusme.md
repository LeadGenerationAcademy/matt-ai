### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open [FocusMe](https://focusme.com/) on your computer.** Have your Bad List open next to it so you can work through it.
2. **Click "Add Plan" in the top right.** The app comes with default plans already in there. Ignore them and build your own.
3. **Choose the "Stay Focused" plan type, then click Next.** This is the one that blocks specific websites and applications.
4. **Leave the websites page set to "block some websites."** Blocking all websites is a different job and would stop you working.
5. **Use "Bulk add websites" and paste your Bad List in, one domain per line.** Much faster than adding 50 sites one at a time. You do not need the https:// or the [www](http://www).
6. **Add broader keywords for anything that would be easy to get around.** Switch the target type to Keyword and enter a word like "news." It matches the address and the page title, so it catches the topic wherever it shows up instead of one site at a time.
7. **Set the block method to "Show block page," then click Next.** Closing the tab also works. The block page is more useful because you can see what it caught.
8. **On the applications page, add any app from your Bad List, then click Next.** It blocks no apps by default, so anything you want stopped has to be added here.
9. **Choose Schedule as the timing mode and click Next.** A timer runs out. Your Bad List does not become acceptable at 4pm.
10. **Block out every day, all day, then click Next.** This is a permanent block, not a work session.
11. **Choose "Custom password" on the protection screen and set one.** The other options are Random Characters, None, and Forced Mode. Random Characters only slows you down and None does nothing.
12. **Name the plan and click Done.** Give it a name you will recognize later, since you will end up with more than one plan in here.
13. **Click Start.** Leave the delay off. The delay exists to let you finish what you were doing, and you are not in the middle of anything on your Bad List.
14. **Open every major site and keyword you blocked and confirm they are blocked.** Test the keyword ones specifically. Those are the ones most likely to be set up wrong.
15. **Open any app you blocked and confirm it closes.** FocusMe closes the app rather than showing a block page, so it disappears instead of warning you first.

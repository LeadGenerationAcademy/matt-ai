### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Settings on your phone and tap Screen Time.** Have your Bad List open on something else so you can work through it.
2. **Scroll down to Restrictions and tap "Content & Privacy Restrictions."** It sits well below the usage graph, so keep scrolling past the app limit sections.
3. **Turn on the Content & Privacy Restrictions toggle.** Everything below it stays greyed out until this is on.
4. **Enter your Screen Time passcode when it asks.** If the person you trust holds it, they need to be there for this part.
5. **Tap "App Store, Media, Web, & Games."** Website blocking lives inside here, not anywhere obvious.
6. **Tap "Web Content."** It will say Unrestricted next to it right now.
7. **Select "Limit Adult Websites."** This is Apple's own adult-content restriction and it catches a lot on its own before you add anything.
8. **Under "Never Allow," tap "Add Website."** Do not confuse this with the "Always Allow" box sitting above it.
9. **Type a URL from your Bad List and tap the blue checkmark.** Enter it exactly as written on your list.
10. **Repeat for every URL on your Bad List.** This is the slow part. It is also the part that does the work.
11. **Test the important websites immediately.** Writing them down means nothing if they still open. A URL typed in slightly wrong sits there looking blocked and opens fine.
12. **Go back to Screen Time and tap "App Limits."** Websites are handled. Now the apps on your Bad List.
13. **Tap "Add Limit."** If you already have limits in here from before, leave them. You are adding to the list.
14. **Select every app from your Bad List, then tap Next.** You can tap a whole category to grab everything inside it, or open the category and pick apps one by one.
15. **Set the time to 0 hours and 0 min, Every Day.** Anything above zero gives you a daily allowance, which is not what you want for a bad-list app.
16. **Turn on "Block at End of Limit."** Without this the app keeps working after the limit and just tells you about it.
17. **Tap the checkmark to save it.** Nothing you just set applies until you do.
18. **Open one of the blocked apps and confirm it blocks.** You get one free minute per day before it starts demanding the passcode, which is fine.

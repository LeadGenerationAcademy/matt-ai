### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your Good List.** This is the note you made earlier with everything you deliberately want entering your mind.
2. **Subscribe to the useful YouTube channels on it.** Your home page is blank now, so the Subscriptions tab is how you get to anything worth watching.
3. **Bookmark the websites, blogs, and archives on it.** Work through the list one by one rather than trying to remember them later.
4. **Put those bookmarks in your bookmarks bar where you can see them.** The information you want should be easier to reach than the information you are trying to remove. Bookmarks buried in a folder you never open are no easier to reach than anything else.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your Good List?
- Did you subscribe to the useful YouTube channels on it?
- Did you bookmark the websites, blogs, and archives on it?
- Did you put those bookmarks in your bookmarks bar where you can see them?

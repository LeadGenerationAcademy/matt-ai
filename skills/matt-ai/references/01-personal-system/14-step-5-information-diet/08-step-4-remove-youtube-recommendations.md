### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open [My Google Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/).** Make sure you are signed in to the Google account you actually use for YouTube.
2. **Click the "YouTube History" container.** This opens the settings page for your YouTube history.
3. **Click the "TURN OFF" button.** This is what stops YouTube from recording what you watch.
4. **Click "Pause" in the confirmation pop up.** This stops YouTube saving anything new from this point on.
5. **Click "Delete old activity" in the privacy tip pop up.** Pausing alone leaves everything YouTube already has on you, which is what the home page is built from.
6. **Click "All time."** You are wiping the whole history, not the last hour or the last day.
7. **Click "Delete" to confirm.** A second screen shows you examples of what is about to go. Confirm it there.
8. **Open the YouTube home page and confirm it is basically blank.** Between your history being off and the extensions you installed earlier, there should be no videos on the home page. We are not blocking YouTube because it is useful. We are removing the algorithm so you only watch something when you deliberately search for it.

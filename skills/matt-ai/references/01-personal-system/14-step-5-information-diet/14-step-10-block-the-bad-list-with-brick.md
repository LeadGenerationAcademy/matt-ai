### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Brick app](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/brick-ditch-distractions/id6448794069) on your phone.** Have your Bad List open on something else so you can work through it.
2. **Tap Get Started if you have not paired your brick yet.** There is nothing to charge or plug in. The brick works wirelessly.
3. **Grant the permissions it asks for, including Screen Time access.** Brick cannot block anything without them.
4. **Hold your phone against the brick to pair it.** You will feel a short vibration when it reads.
5. **Tap the mode name to open it.** This is where you choose what gets blocked while you are bricked.
6. **Select every app from your Bad List.** Leave yourself whatever you genuinely need for work, usually messages and mail, and block the rest.
7. **Select every website from your Bad List.** Brick blocks these across Safari, Chrome and any other browser on your phone, so the sites you added to Screen Time get a second layer here.
8. **Save the mode.** The blocks do not exist until the mode is saved, even if the apps are ticked on screen.
9. **Turn on Strict Mode.** Without it you can delete the Brick app mid-session and walk straight past everything you just set up.
10. **Stick the brick somewhere far from where you work.** It is magnetic. The fridge, the front door, another room. The distance is the entire point.
11. **Activate the device by tapping your phone against the brick.** The timer starts and the blocks go live.
12. **Test every major block.** Open every app and website you blocked and confirm they are blocked. You should get Brick's screen telling you to tap your brick, not the app.
13. **Tap your phone against the brick again to unbrick.** Same motion, opposite result.
14. **Do not spend your emergency unbricks.** You get a limited number for the life of the account. Once they are gone the only way out is deleting the app, which wipes your data and can stop your brick working again.

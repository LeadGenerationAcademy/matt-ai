### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open [account.apple.com](http://account.apple.com) in your browser.** Any browser works, on your computer or your phone. This is Apple's official page for managing Apple accounts.
2. **Click "Create Your Apple Account."** On a computer it sits in the top right corner of the page. On a phone, tap the arrow next to "Apple Account" to open the dropdown first, then tap it from there. Either way, ignore the Sign In option. You are making a new account, not getting into an existing one.
3. **Enter a first name and last name.** It does not have to be anything clever. This account exists to hold a password, nothing else.
4. **Choose your country or region from the dropdown.** It usually fills itself in based on where you are, so check it rather than assume it.
5. **Enter a date of birth using the Month, Day and Year dropdowns.** Apple restricts what an account can do if the age is under 18, so do not put in something random.
6. **Enter an email address you are not already using on your Apple account.** Apple will not let one email address hold two accounts, so this has to be a different one from the email on the account already on your phone.
7. **Have the person you trust enter a password and confirm it.** Give the password to your wife, mother, sibling, friend, or business partner. The best version of this is having them sit there and type it in themselves, so you never see it at all.
8. **Enter a phone number.** Apple uses this to verify you, so it needs to be a number you can actually get a message on.
9. **Uncheck the two marketing boxes.** Announcements and Apps, Music, TV, and More are both ticked by default, and leaving them on means Apple emails this account forever.
10. **Type the characters shown in the image.** If you cannot read it, click New Code and Apple gives you a different one.
11. **Click Continue.** Apple checks everything you entered before it moves you on.
12. **Verify the account with the code Apple sends you.** Enter it when prompted and finish the setup.
13. **Open Settings on your phone and tap Screen Time.** This is where the burner account actually gets attached.
14. **Scroll down and tap "Lock Screen Time Settings."** Without this, anything you block later can be unblocked by anyone holding the phone.
15. **Have the person you trust enter a Screen Time passcode and confirm it.** They need to set this one as well. If you know the Screen Time passcode, you can change every block yourself and the burner account has done nothing.
16. **Enter the burner account's email address or phone number when your phone asks for an Apple Account, then tap OK.** This is what it uses if the Screen Time passcode ever needs resetting.
17. **Have the person you trust enter the burner account's password, then tap OK.** Same reason as before. You should not see this one either.
18. **Confirm you cannot get into Screen Time settings on your own.** You should not be able to reset Screen Time yourself, but someone you trust should still be able to help when there is a legitimate reason.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Settings on your phone.** This is the iPhone version. If you are on something else, the names will be different but the feature does the same job.
2. **Scroll down and tap "Screen Time."** Or whatever your phone calls the equivalent feature.
3. **Check whether Screen Time is already on.** If you see a graph of your usage, it is already on and you can move to the next step. If there is no graph anywhere on the page, it is off.
4. **If it is off, tap "App & Website Activity."** Or whatever your phone calls the equivalent.
5. **Tap "Turn on App & Website Activity."** Confirm it if your phone asks you to.
6. **Go back and confirm Screen Time is now on.** Do not expect numbers in the graph straight away. It has nothing to report until your phone has recorded a day of usage.
7. **Leave the actual blocking for later.** We will use Screen Time to block the bad list and make your other blocking tools harder to remove. Your bad list goes in during Step #9.

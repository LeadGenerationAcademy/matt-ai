### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Apple Notes and create a note called "Good List."** Or the same notes app you used to create the other documents.
2. **List the information you deliberately want entering your mind.** This could include CEO interviews, older business books, my YouTube videos, useful business content, and autobiographies of people who have actually done something.
3. **Skip the exact URLs.** You do not need them here the way you did on your Bad List. Decide what you want filling your head instead of allowing the internet to decide for you.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open Apple Notes and create a note called "Good List"?
- Did you list the information you deliberately want entering your mind?
- Did you skip the exact URLs?

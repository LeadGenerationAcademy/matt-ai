A line that always sticks with me goes something like the following:

"After oxygen, content is the thing that we consume most on Earth (even more than food) but unlike the quality of air we breathe, we don't typically put much thought into the quality of content we consume."

This hits hard for me, because I am well aware of this fact.

You may or may not be aware.

It is absolutely insane (in a bad way) how much worse your life is when you have a bad information diet full of porn, news, politics, sports, mindless music, mindless podcasts, streaming and so on.

I would say that most people in this Skool group would not be aware of this as they have never really thought about this concept or tested it before.

I want you to consciously "design' your information diet from now on. That is why we downloaded so many of those blocking apps, Chrome Extensions, ordered the Brick device and so much more.

There are two parts here:

1. Blocking the bad stuff
2. Replacing it with good stuff

At the VERY least, I want you to get rid of the bad stuff. If you replace it with good stuff, that is a great bonus.

You will instantly see how much better your life gets once you do this. it will literally be like magic.

I would go really harsh on this blocking at the start. You will see that you are wasting literally 80%+ of your time on non-sense and then you will see how much time you have in the day when you can no longer go on social media, streaming services, news, and so on. It is insane how much time there is, you, and I and everyone else just waste it.

The key thought here in my opinion is that it really isn't about blocking the stuff. Nothing is truly unblockable. If you want to get around the blocking, you can and you will. That is why we have four layers, to make it just enough friction in our mind that we stop viewing it as a real option.

But in reality, we will use blocking as a tool. The end goal is to become a better version of ourselves who doesn't even crave this shit to begin with. That should be the end goal. Blocking is just something we can utilize while we make this mental transformation and become a much higher, more powerful version of ourselves.

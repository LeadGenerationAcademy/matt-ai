### Your Lists

- Your bad list contains every website, app and source you intended to block.
- It uses exact websites and URLs rather than vague categories.
- Your good list exists and says what you deliberately want entering your mind.

### Blocking

- Every bad website and app on your phone is blocked by Screen Time.
- Every relevant app and website is blocked while Brick is active.
- Brick's Strict Mode is turned on.
- The brick itself is kept far from where you work.
- Every bad website, keyword and app on your computer is blocked by FocusMe.
- The same blocks exist in Opal on your phone and on your computer, built separately on each.
- Your YouTube history is off and the home page is blank.
- A fourth browser tab will not open.
- You cannot change Screen Time without the password.
- Someone you trust controls the burner-account password.

### Access

- Everything on your good list is one click away, with the channels subscribed and the sites bookmarked in your bookmarks bar.
- Nothing on your good list got caught by your blockers.
- Everything required to operate your business still works.
- Everything that failed the first time has been fixed and tested again.

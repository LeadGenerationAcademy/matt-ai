### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Apple Notes and create a note called "Bad List."** Or the same notes app you used to create the other documents.
2. **List every website, app, and source of information that wastes your attention.** Include pornography, social media, streaming, news, politics, and anything else you already know is bad for you.
3. **Use exact websites and URLs instead of vague categories.** "Social media" cannot be pasted into a blocker. The actual URL can.
4. **Include music and entertainment podcasts if you mainly use them to escape your own thoughts.** You already know whether that is what you are doing with them.
5. **Keep going until you cannot think of anything else.** Your list will probably contain more than 50 things. If it is coming up short, ask yourself whether you are being honest about what you actually open during the day.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open Apple Notes and create a note called "Bad List"?
- Did you list every website, app, and source of information that wastes your attention?
- Did you use exact websites and URLs instead of vague categories?
- Did you include music and entertainment podcasts if you mainly use them to escape your own thoughts?
- Did you keep going until you ran out?

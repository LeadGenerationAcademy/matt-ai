### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open [Opal](https://opalapp.com/) on your computer and click "+ New Block."** Have your Bad List open next to it so you can work through it.
2. **Click "Apps Blocked" to open the Select Apps & Websites screen.** This is where everything on your Bad List goes in.
3. **Tick the Common Presets that match your Bad List.** Adult, Social, News, Dating, Gambling, Video and Shopping are all sitting there. Each preset covers the common sites in that category, so ticking one saves you typing out twenty domains.
4. **Type any remaining domain from your Bad List into the website field.** The presets catch the obvious ones. Anything specific to you has to be entered by hand.
5. **Add any app from your Bad List under Apps.** Click the plus icon next to Apps. This blocks any app or game on your Mac, not just browsers.
6. **Click Save.** The blocks are not attached to the session until you do.
7. **Set Difficulty to "Yes, but make it harder."** The three options are "Yes, make it easy," "Yes, but make it harder," and "No way, I'm hardcore." Easy lets you cancel whenever you want, which defeats the point. Hardcore means no breaks and one emergency pass a week, and you can take that one if you want the strongest version.
8. **Turn on "Always On."** This makes it permanent instead of a timed session. If you would rather not go that far yet, set it to cover your working hours instead.
9. **Click "Start Now."** The block goes live from that moment.
10. **Open Opal on your phone and go to My Apps.** The phone version looks nothing like the computer version, so do not go looking for the same screens.
11. **Tap "Never Allowed."** Sessions and schedules run on a timer. Never Allowed is the permanent one, which is where your Bad List belongs.
12. **Tap "Add App or Website" and enter every app and website from your Bad List.** Same list you just worked through on the computer.
13. **Tap the green checkmark to save it.** Nothing you just added applies until you do.
14. **Check that the same apps and websites are blocked on both devices.** Opal does not sync between your phone and your computer, so nothing you did on one shows up on the other. Compare the two lists side by side and fill in whatever you missed.
15. **Open a blocked app and a blocked site on each device.** This is the only way to know it actually took.

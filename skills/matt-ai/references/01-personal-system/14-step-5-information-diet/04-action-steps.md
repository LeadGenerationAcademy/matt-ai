# Action Steps

Course: Step #5: Information Diet
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/e3de926b?md=c041ced61a4d447b9732ce69f23477f8

---
The Theory explains that after oxygen, content is the thing you consume most on earth, even more than food, and almost nobody puts any thought into the quality of it.

In this course you will consciously design your information diet.

You will write your Bad List and your Good List, then block the bad across four layers on your phone and computer, with the passwords held by someone else. Getting rid of the bad is the requirement and the good is a bonus.

Go harsh on the blocking at the start. You are wasting 80% or more of your time on nonsense, and you will not believe how much time is actually in a day once you cannot reach for any of it.

Do not think the blocking is the point. Nothing is truly unblockable. It is a tool you use while you become someone who does not crave this shit in the first place.

### Table of Contents

1. **Keep Four Or More Layers Of Blocking:** Confirm your phone layers and your computer layers are in place, and install anything you skipped.
2. **Create Your Bad List:** Every website, app and source of information that wastes your attention, written out as exact URLs.
3. **Create Your Good List:** The information you deliberately want entering your mind, with no URLs needed.
4. **Remove YouTube Recommendations:** Turn off and delete your YouTube history until the home page is basically blank.
5. **Turn On Screen Time:** Turn on App & Website Activity so Screen Time is ready to block your Bad List later.
6. **Create A Burner iCloud Account:** A separate Apple account with the password held by someone you trust, locked over your Screen Time settings.
7. **Limit Yourself To Three Browser Tabs:** Set Tab Limiter to 3 and confirm a fourth tab is blocked.
8. **Make Your Good List Easy To Access:** Subscribe and bookmark everything on it, with the bookmarks sitting in your bookmarks bar.
9. **Block The Bad List With Screen Time:** Every URL under Never Allow and every app on a zero-minute daily limit.
10. **Block The Bad List With Brick:** Build the mode, turn on Strict Mode, and stick the brick far from where you work.
11. **Block The Bad List With FocusMe:** A permanent Stay Focused plan with broader keywords, locked behind a password you do not hold.
12. **Block The Bad List With Opal:** Presets and remaining domains on your computer and your phone separately, since Opal does not sync between them.

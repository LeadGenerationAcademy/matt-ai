### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Canva](https://canva.com/signup).** If you already have an account, skip this.
2. **Search up your monitor or laptop's screen size in pixels.** Google your exact monitor model or MacBook model plus "screen resolution", you need the exact width and height in pixels before you build anything.
3. **Click Create in Canva.** It's in the top left of your Canva home page.
4. **Click Custom size.** It's listed in the sidebar of the design menu that pops up.
5. **Enter your screen size and create the design.** Type in the width and height you found in step 2, then click to create the design.
6. **Add your symbol to the design.** Drop in the same symbol you already used for your phone and build it out to fill the screen.
7. **Download the design as a PNG.** Click Share, then Download, keep the file type as PNG, quality set to High, and transparent background turned off.
8. **Set it as your desktop background.** Open your settings, type "Wallpaper" into the search bar to find where it lives, then select the file you just downloaded. Check that it fits the screen and stays clearly visible.

### Examples

##### Self Mastery List

1. When I wake up, I immediately go to my computer and start these four hours of work
2. I don't mess around too long between hours. I am back at it after 10-15 minutes
3. I get these four hours of work done by 11am every day

##### Meaning List

1. Doing today's work on the new offer is how I build the income that gets my parents out of their mortgage.
2. Finishing my four hours without dragging out the breaks is what lets me close the laptop when my kids get home.
3. Doing my 2.0 You Hour every day is how I stop spending another five years building a life I never actually chose.

##### Thought Habits List

1. When my alarm goes off, I instantly smile and think "yay" and then leap out of bed
2. When I am done my morning routine, I think "I am so excited and lucky to have these four hours to develop the best version of myself and what I can do"
3. Whenever something is not my absolute best or the 10x standard of my previous best, I instantly think "no, that is not good enough, I need to do it better"

##### Anti-Worry List

1. Book the dentist appointment I have postponed twice.
2. Reply to the important email I have been avoiding all week.
3. Cancel the unused software subscription that keeps charging me.

##### Joy List

1. Taking my dog to his favorite park and letting him run around.
2. Getting a coffee and walking outside after my four hours.
3. My daughter running to the door when I get home.

##### Gratitude List

1. My parents are still alive, and I can pick up the phone and speak to them.
2. I have customers who trust me enough to pay for something I created.
3. My body allows me to walk, lift weights, and do something about my health.

##### Forgiveness List

1. I forgive myself for the years I spent talking about starting instead of doing anything.
2. I forgive myself for letting my health go while pretending I would deal with it later.
3. I forgive my former business partner for breaking our agreement, which I still replay in my head.

##### Improvement List

1. Lay my gym clothes out the night before, so the morning has one less thing I can talk myself out of.
2. Put the decision we need to make at the top of every meeting agenda, so meetings end with something decided instead of another meeting.
3. Record the answer as a short video the first time somebody asks, so I stop explaining the same thing to every new hire.

##### Foundational Causes List

1. Being asleep by 10pm → the four work hours actually happen tomorrow.
2. Keeping my phone out of the bedroom → the first hour of the day belongs to me instead of everybody else.
3. Logging my numbers every day → I find a problem in three days instead of six months.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Add an initial entry to your Self Mastery List.** The circumstances you regularly face and what you are going to make yourself do when they happen.
2. **Add an initial entry to your Meaning List.** The things you do every day and what doing them means for your goals and Life Vision.
3. **Add an initial entry to your Thought Habits List.** The circumstances you regularly face and what you are going to think when they happen, decided in advance instead of whatever thought normally shows up.
4. **Add an initial entry to your Anti-Worry List.** Whatever is unresolved and currently worrying you.
5. **Add an initial entry to your Joy List.** Whatever actually makes you happy.
6. **Add an initial entry to your Gratitude List.** Whatever you genuinely feel grateful for.
7. **Add an initial entry to your Forgiveness List.** Whatever you are still holding against other people or yourself.
8. **Add an initial entry to your Foundational Causes List.** The ordinary habits and decisions that have enormous downstream effects on everything else.

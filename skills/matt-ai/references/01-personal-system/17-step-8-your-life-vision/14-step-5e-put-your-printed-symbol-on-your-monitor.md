### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Print a second copy of the symbol file you downloaded in Step #5D.** Same file, same settings. You already built the design for your bathroom mirror, this is just a second print of it.
2. **Put your printed symbol on or beside your monitor.** Taped to the monitor itself, sat underneath it, or propped up on the desk next to it. Whichever one works with your setup. You'll be sat in front of this monitor for four hours a day, and the symbol belongs in that space.
3. **Sit down in your work position and confirm you can see it without moving.** If you have to lean over or turn your head to find it, move it somewhere you don't. You should be able to see it while working, whenever you need the boost.

### Completion Audit

- Did you print a second copy of your symbol?
- Did you put your symbol on or beside your monitor?
- Can you see it from your work position without moving?

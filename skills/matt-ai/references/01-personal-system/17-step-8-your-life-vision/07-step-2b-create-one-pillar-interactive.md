### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a [New Google Doc](https://docs.new).** The link makes a blank doc the moment you click it. This is scratch space for building one pillar, not where your five pillars live.
2. **Create one pillar along with the video.** One pillar only, taken all the way through the template.
3. **Go deeper than the pillar in the video.** The video is in the previous step and is deliberately shallower than yours should be, because it is being made live and publicly. A pillar you would never want to show anyone is the sign you took it far enough.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a new Google Doc?
- Did you create one pillar along with the video?
- Did you go deeper than the pillar in the video?

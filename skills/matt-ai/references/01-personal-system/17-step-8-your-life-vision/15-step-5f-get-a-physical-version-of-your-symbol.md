### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Check whether your symbol has a real physical version.** Some symbols only exist as images, which is why this step is optional.
2. **Get one if it does.** Only worth doing when the object genuinely exists and means something to you.
3. **Place it somewhere visible.** Same as the phone, the computer, the mirror and the monitor, it only does its job if you actually see it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you check whether your symbol has a real physical version?
- Did you get one if it does?
- Did you place it somewhere visible?

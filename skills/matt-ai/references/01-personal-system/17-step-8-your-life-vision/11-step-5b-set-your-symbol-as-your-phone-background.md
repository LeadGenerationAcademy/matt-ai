### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sign up for [Canva](https://canva.com/signup).** This is free and only takes a minute.
2. **If you have an iPhone, open the [iPhone Wallpaper Template](https://canva.link/hzaemdtfmqyotyr) and make a copy of it.** It has a page built for every iPhone size back to the iPhone 11. Making a copy gives you your own editable version so you're not touching the original.
3. **If you have a Samsung, open the [Samsung Wallpaper Template](https://canva.link/c8a9fyz0a017ah3) and make a copy of it.** It has a page built for every Galaxy size from the S22 up to the S26. Making a copy gives you your own editable version so you're not touching the original.
4. **Find the page for your phone model.** Every phone's page is lined up along the bottom, hover over each one to see its full name if it's cut off, and scroll left and right until you spot yours.
5. **If your phone isn't listed, build a custom-size page for it.**
  1. Scroll all the way to the end of the pages at the bottom.
  2. Click the "+" to add a new page.
  3. Click Resize Page.
  4. Click Custom Size.
  5. Search your exact phone's screen size on Google, enter it under Width and Height, and resize.
6. **Add your symbol to your phone's page.** Drop in the symbol you chose in Step #5A and build it out, the size is already handled by the page.
7. **Download the [Canva app](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/canva-ai-video-photo-editor/id897446215) on your phone.** You'll need it in the next step to grab the file directly onto your device, which is what actually lets you set it as your wallpaper.
8. **Open your design in the Canva app and download your page from there.** Don't use the download icon at the top right of the app, that downloads every page in the file, not just yours.
  1. Make sure your design is open and your phone's page is the one on screen.
  2. Tap the three dots near the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Download.
  4. Confirm the file type is PNG, quality is High, and transparent background is off, these should already be set the way you left them.
  5. Tap Download to save the file to your phone.
9. **Set your symbol as your Home Screen and Lock Screen wallpaper.**
  1. Open Settings and go to Wallpaper, you can search "Wallpaper" if it's easier to find.
  2. Tap Add New Wallpaper.
  3. Tap Photos and select the image you just downloaded from Canva.
  4. Set it for both your Home Screen and your Lock Screen.
  5. On the Home Screen version, turn off the blur, iPhone applies it automatically, and make sure your symbol is centered properly.

# Action Steps

Course: Step #8: Your Life Vision
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/554c839c?md=d3ae436843d1426bb5ef52a713e940af

---
The Theory explains that nothing in life has meaning beyond the meaning you give it. The same assignment feels completely different depending on whether you see it as another thing to get through or as something that builds a life you desperately want.

In this course you will build the first real version of your Life Vision.

You will fill in your five pillars, get a vision board on the wall behind your monitor, put your symbol everywhere you look, and add a first entry to every one of your lists. Most of the other documents stay blank until your pillars are developed enough to build on.

Do not try to finish your vision in this hour. You are getting the first real version down so there is something in your folders to start improving.

### Table of Contents

1. **Set A Timer For One Hour:** One hour to get the first real version down, not the finished one.
2. **Fill In Your Top Five Pillars:** The five things you would achieve if you could only achieve five, set 20 years out and taken through the whole template.
3. **Create One Pillar (Interactive):** Build one pillar along with the video in a scratch doc, and take it deeper than the one shown.
4. **Leave Blank: Life Vision & Avg Wednesday:** Both stay empty until your pillars are developed enough to combine.
5. **Create Your Initial Vision Board:** Six to 18 images representing your pillars, printed in color and placed on the wall behind your monitor.
6. **Choose Your Symbol:** One image, object or idea that represents the person you are becoming, with a real image file of it.
7. **Set Your Symbol As Your Phone Background:** Built in Canva at your phone's exact size and set as both your Home Screen and Lock Screen.
8. **Set Your Symbol As Your Computer BG:** The same symbol built at your exact screen resolution and set as your desktop background.
9. **Put Your Symbol On The Bathroom Mirror:** Printed from the A4 or A3 template and taped up where you see it at the start and end of every day.
10. **Put Your Printed Symbol On Your Monitor:** A second print sitting where you can see it from your work position without moving.
11. **Get A Physical Version Of Your Symbol:** Optional, and only worth doing if the object genuinely exists and means something to you.
12. **Fill In Your Lists:** One initial entry on each of your eight lists.
13. **Create One Loop (Interactive):** Take one cause through the self-mastery moment, the meaning, six orders of effects and a thought habit.
14. **Leave The Rest Blank:** Your One-Year Goals, 90-Day Objectives and War Map all wait until later.

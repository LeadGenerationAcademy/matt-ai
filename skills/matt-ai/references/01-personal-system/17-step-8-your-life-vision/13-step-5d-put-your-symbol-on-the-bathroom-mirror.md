### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [A4 Symbol Template](https://canva.link/ebj1ae2rc6qoipc) or [A3 Symbol Template](https://canva.link/dkw2tddcqe8rwzv) and make a copy of it.** Making a copy gives you your own editable version so you're not touching the original.
2. **Pick portrait or landscape.** Use whichever orientation you want your symbol displayed in.
3. **Copy your symbol into the template.** You already built this symbol for your phone or computer background in the previous steps, so just copy it over and paste it into this template instead of starting from scratch.
4. **Click Share, then Download.** Share is in the top right, and Download is inside that menu.
5. **Confirm the file type is PDF.** This is what your printer software will need to print it correctly.
6. **Leave the size untouched.** The size is already set correctly for the template you picked, don't change it.
7. **Set quality to High.** This keeps the print sharp instead of grainy.
8. **Select only the page you used.** Under Select Pages, choose the specific page you built your design on, unless you want to download and print both a portrait and a landscape version.
9. **Print the design.** Take the downloaded file into whatever printing software you use for your printer and print it from there. Print it in color if you can, it hits a lot harder than black and white.
10. **Tape it to your bathroom mirror.** You will normally see it near the beginning and end of every day.

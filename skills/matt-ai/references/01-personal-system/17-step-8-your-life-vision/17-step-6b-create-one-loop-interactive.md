### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a [New Google Doc](https://docs.new/).** The link makes a blank doc the moment you click it. This is scratch space for building one loop along with the video, not where your lists live.
2. **Choose the cause.** Take one off your Fundamental Causes List.
3. **Choose the moment to practice self-mastery.** The situation in an ordinary day where that cause is either produced or avoided.
4. **Assign meaning to it, positive and negative, then work out six orders of effects.** What following through in that moment means for your goals and Life Vision, and what not following through means. Then take the first effect, what that causes, and what that one causes, six levels down.
5. **Create a thought habit for it.** What you are going to think when that moment shows up, decided now instead of whatever thought normally arrives.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a new Google Doc?
- Did you choose the cause?
- Did you choose the moment to practice self-mastery?
- Did you assign meaning to it, positive and negative, and work out six orders of effects?
- Did you create a thought habit for it?

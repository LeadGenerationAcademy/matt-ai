### Examples

Here are some common symbols and what people usually take them to mean. Yours should mean something more specific to you than any of these:

- **Infinity Symbol:** Unlimited potential
- **Cross:** Faith and sacrifice
- **Yin and Yang:** Balance between two opposing sides of yourself
- **Phoenix:** Rebirth as a new person
- **Lion:** Courage and leadership

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose one image, object, or idea that naturally represents your growth and the person you are becoming.** Mine is an AI image of me carving the 2.0 version of myself out of the old version. Yours could be a cross or something completely different.
2. **Do not choose something because you think it is supposed to be meaningful.** It should resonate with you immediately.
3. **Get an actual image of it.** Search for it, or generate it with AI the way I did with mine. Every step after this one needs a real image file.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose one image, object, or idea that naturally represents your growth and the person you are becoming?
- Did you avoid choosing something because you think it is supposed to be meaningful?

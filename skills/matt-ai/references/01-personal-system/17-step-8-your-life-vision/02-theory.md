Course: Step #8: Your Life Vision
System: Personal System

---
Nothing in life has meaning beyond the meaning we give it. We decide what matters to us and what we want to spend our lives doing.

That is why having a grand Life Vision matters. When you have a future you genuinely want to create, you have a reason to act with more intensity than you ever have before. You understand what the work is for and why it deserves so much of your effort.

The same assignment can feel very different depending on what it means to you. If you see it as another thing you have to get through, you will probably approach it that way. If you understand how it contributes to a life you desperately want, you have a much stronger reason to sit down and do it properly.

Think of your brain as a goal-setting mechanism. You need to give it a clear end goal to work toward. Once you know what you want, you can begin figuring out what needs to happen and which actions will help you get there. You have something to base your decisions on.

This is why your Life Vision needs to be personal. You need to actually care about achieving it. A goal can sound impressive and still mean very little to you. You are the person who will spend years doing the work, so you need to understand why you want the result.

Your five pillars represent the biggest accomplishments you care about achieving in your life. They should be much bigger than what you currently feel capable of doing. Achieving them would require you to learn things, develop new abilities, and become someone you are not yet.

A lot of your smaller goals should fit inside them. You can begin to see how the things you are working on contribute to something you care about much more.

Those pillars also need to make sense together. Everything in your life is connected, and you are going to be the same person living all of it. If achieving one goal requires a way of living that makes another impossible, that is something you need to think through. Bringing them into one vision helps you understand what you actually want your whole life to look like.

That includes who you become. The results you want will require certain causes, and your vision needs to account for the standards you would have to live by to create them. This gives you a clearer idea of what the 2.0 Version of You actually does.

There is also a reason we want you to imagine an ordinary day in that life.

An accomplishment written in a document can still feel distant. Being able to picture where you wake up, who is around you, and what you spend the day doing makes it easier to understand what achieving it would mean to you. Your Average Wednesday gives you a way to experience that future in your mind.

As the vision becomes clearer, you may find that some things matter more than you thought and others matter less. That is useful. You are developing a better understanding of the life you want to spend your time creating.

You then need to take inspired action toward it. The vision gives you a reason to act, while the work creates the causes required to achieve it. When you sit down to work on something important, you should understand how it contributes to the life you have described and why you care enough to follow through.

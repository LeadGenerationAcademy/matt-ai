### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Leave your One-Year Goals blank.** These get built out of your five pillars later, and your pillars are only a first version right now.
2. **Leave your 90-Day Objectives blank.** These come out of your One-Year Goals, so there is nothing to base them on yet.
3. **Leave your War Map blank.** This is the planning document all of it eventually feeds into, and you will develop the whole thing throughout your future 2.0 You Hours.

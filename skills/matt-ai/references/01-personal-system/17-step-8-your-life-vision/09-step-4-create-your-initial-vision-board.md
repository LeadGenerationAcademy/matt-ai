### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Find 6 to 18 images that represent the five pillars.** Choose images that genuinely mean something to you and make you want to work.
2. **Print them in color.** If you do not have a color printer, almost any printing store can print them for approximately $1.50 each.
3. **Place them on the wall behind your monitor.** They need to stay visible while you are actually working, not filed somewhere you look at once.
4. **Replace and add images as the vision becomes clearer.** This does not need to be the final version. You just have to get something on the wall now.

### Completion Audit

- Did you find 6 to 18 images that represent the five pillars?
- Did you print them in color?
- Did you place them on the wall behind your monitor?
- Are you replacing and adding images as the vision becomes clearer?

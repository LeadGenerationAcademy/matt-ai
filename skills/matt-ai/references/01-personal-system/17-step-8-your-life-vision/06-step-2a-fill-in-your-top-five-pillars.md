### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the five pillar notes you created earlier.** The goal template should already sitting inside each one.
2. **Choose your five pillars.** If you could only achieve five things in your life, these would be the five things. Choosing what they are is the minimum for this hour, and they can change later.
3. **Set them 20 years out.** Twenty years is practical. Going much further than that is not.
4. **Think 10 to 100 times bigger than what you think you could achieve.** The cliche is a cliche for a reason. Most people overestimate what they could do in a year and underestimate what they could do in a decade or two decades.
5. **Fill in Vision for each pillar.** This is what you want and what it looks like.
6. **Fill in What Do I Achieve for each pillar.** Everything else in your life that gets achieved as a result of this pillar.
7. **Fill in Who Do I Become for each pillar.** The person you would have to become to get there.
8. **Fill in What Do I Think for each pillar.** What you think once this is your life.
9. **Fill in What Do I Say for each pillar.** What you say once this is your life.
10. **Fill in How Does It Feel for each pillar.** What it feels like to be living it.
11. **Fill in What Does It Mean for each pillar.** What having it means to you.
12. **Fill in What Are The Causes for each pillar.** The causes you need to create to produce this effect.
13. **Give an honest answer rather than one that sounds good.** Two good signs that you are on the right track are that writing it makes you emotional and that the document feels too personal to send to someone else.

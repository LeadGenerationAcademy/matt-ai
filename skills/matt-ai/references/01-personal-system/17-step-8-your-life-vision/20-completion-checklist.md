### Your Five Pillars

- Your five pillars are chosen and set 20 years out.
- The pillars are 10 to 100 times bigger than what you currently think you could achieve.
- Every section of the Goal Template is filled in on all five.
- The answers are honest ones rather than ones that sound good.
- You built one pillar all the way through alongside the video and took it deeper than the one shown.

### Your Vision Board

- You have 6 to 18 images that represent the five pillars and genuinely make you want to work.
- They are printed in color.
- They are on the wall behind your monitor.

### Your Symbol

- You have chosen one image, object or idea that resonated with you immediately and represents the person you are becoming.
- You have a real image file of it.
- It is your phone's Home Screen and Lock Screen wallpaper.
- It is your computer's desktop background.
- A printed copy is taped to your bathroom mirror.
- A second printed copy is on or beside your monitor, visible from your work position.
- You have a physical version of it if one genuinely exists.

### Your Lists

- Every one of your eight lists has an initial entry on it.
- You started them rather than trying to complete them.
- You built one full loop alongside the video, from the cause through to the thought habit.

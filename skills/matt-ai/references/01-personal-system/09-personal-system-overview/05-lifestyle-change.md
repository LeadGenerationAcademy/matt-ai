# Lifestyle Change

Course: Personal System Overview
System: Personal System

---
Only a retard could expect to change their life without making any changes.

Whatever you currently do brought you to your current position. You cannot continue living exactly the same way and expect your life to become different.

In this course and Skool group, we are going to make actual changes to how you live. Your schedule, physical environment, information consumption, food, work, addictions if you have them, and ultimately your standards.

Everything we do here is built around that lifestyle change.

##### Making The Correct Actions Easier

You might want to complete an important project. But if you wake up without a plan, sit down at a messy desk, leave your phone beside you, and keep every distracting website available, you have made it unnecessarily difficult.

If you have very little self-mastery, you can help yourself by designing your environment to make holding good thoughts and making correct decisions easier.

We are arranging the day so the things you need to do are obvious and easier to begin.

That does not mean the work itself will be easy. You may be doing some of the most difficult work you have ever done. But you should be able to sit down, know what you are doing, and begin.

There should not be another 45 minutes of preparation required.

##### Changing How You Conduct Yourself

Some things will happen at a scheduled time. Other standards apply throughout the day.

You practice controlling yourself whenever you want to do one thing but know you should do another. You pay attention to your thoughts. You follow through on what you committed to completing.

You also notice what keeps making the correct action harder.

If something repeatedly fails, find the part causing the problem. Maybe you never decided what to work on. Maybe a break keeps turning into an hour. Maybe you keep leaving something you need in another room.

Fix the actual problem instead of giving yourself the same motivational speech every morning.

You are working on how you live, including the ordinary decisions you previously made without paying much attention.

##### Making The Change Last

We will use motivation to get started, discipline to keep going, and transformation to make the change last.

When you feel motivated, use it. When you do not, you still have a schedule, a prepared environment, a specific assignment, and a commitment to follow through.

As you repeat the behavior, you accumulate proof. You complete the work again. You get up when you said you would again. Eventually, the way you describe yourself has to account for the fact that you keep doing these things.

The goal is for this way of living to become normal. There will still be difficult days, but you should not need to have the same argument with yourself before every work hour for the rest of your life.

Will you accomplish all of your hopes and dreams in the first 30 days? Almost certainly not. But you can completely change your trajectory.

You keep improving what you do as you become more capable. You do not have a good month and then go back to doing the same shit you were doing before.

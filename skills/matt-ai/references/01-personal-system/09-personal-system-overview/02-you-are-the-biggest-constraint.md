You are not good enough. If you were, you would’ve already achieved your goals.

Most people know many of the things they should be doing. They should work more, eat better, exercise, get off their phone, sleep properly, and stop avoiding important problems.

They just cannot consistently make themselves do any of it.

##### Knowing What To Do

Knowing you should work does not mean you can already stay focused. If your focus is poor, you have to develop it.

Your beliefs affect what you consider possible, what you attempt, and what you accept from yourself. Your skills affect what you can actually do. Some of your goals require abilities you have not developed yet.

You cannot expect to achieve different goals while keeping everything about yourself the same.

Whatever you currently do brought you to your current position. We need to look at what you actually do, what you keep avoiding, and where you need to become more capable.

##### Look At What Actually Happens

You say you worked all day. When you actually time it, you worked for 90 minutes.

This is why tracking and measurement matter. Two things usually happen when you measure something: you discover you are doing worse than you thought, and seeing it changes how you behave.

Once you begin tracking, you can no longer deny what is clear as day in front of you. You can change what you are doing and see whether it improves.

The same applies when you keep failing to complete something. Look at the work. Find out why it was not completed and deal with it. Otherwise, you can spend months making plans while the same unfinished assignments keep getting moved around.

##### Becoming More Capable

We are going to work on your thoughts, attention, actions, standards, body, and time.

You will find things you need to learn and things you already know but cannot make yourself do consistently. Both need your attention.

You do not need to wait until you achieve your goals before you keep your word, take your work seriously, or behave with higher standards. You can do those things today.

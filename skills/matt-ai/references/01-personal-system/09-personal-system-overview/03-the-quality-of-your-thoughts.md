One of my absolute favorite ideas is that your position in life is determined by the quality of your thoughts.

Everything is downstream of your thoughts: your words, decisions, actions, habits, and eventually your results.

Thinking alone will not magically give you results. Actions produce results. Your thinking affects what you notice, what you tolerate, what you decide, and what you repeatedly do.

##### Your Mind Is Soil

Think of your mind as soil and your thoughts as seeds.

An apple seed will never accidentally produce a banana tree. If you plant an apple seed, an apple tree grows.

If you are not consciously tending to what is growing in your mind, you are letting random seeds grow in your garden.

What enters your mind shapes your thoughts. Your thoughts shape your self-image and standards. Your self-image affects your decisions. The way you repeatedly live each day eventually becomes your life.

Your life is not really made up of the handful of enormous moments you remember. The sale. The wedding. The breakup. The disaster. The breakthrough.

Most of your life is some version of an average Wednesday.

Your life is today, over and over again.

##### Make The Average Wednesday Extraordinary

Changing how you spend an ordinary day changes what you spend most of your life doing.

Our ordinary Wednesday should be extraordinary. Our very existence should be something we want.

Think about what that day would actually look like.

You wake up. Where are you? What do you do? Who is around you? What are you working on, and how do you feel while you are doing it?

We want to turn the life you want into something you can actually experience in your mind. Keep developing the details until you can picture yourself living that day.

Then look at an ordinary day now. Look at how you spend your time, what occupies your mind, and what you actually get done. That is where we want to see the difference.

##### What You Do Today Accumulates

You have to produce the causes of the effects you want.

Wanting a different result does not produce it. You have to do what produces it.

There is a delay between planting and harvesting. A lot of people give up on good actions before they get the results.

People also stop doing what worked and fail to notice the consequences until their crops die and they see weeds in the garden.

Your ordinary Wednesday is what you keep sowing. Your thoughts, work, decisions, and standards accumulate into the life you live.

Some of the things you want will take a long time. But you can change your trajectory before you have achieved all of them. You can change what you spend today doing, then keep doing it.

The core purpose of this course is the increase of self-mastery.

Self-mastery means making yourself do what you know you should do. It means gaining control over your thoughts, attention, actions, standards, body, and time.

Most people cannot even make themselves get up when their alarm goes off or stop eating a bag of chips. They lack basic self-mastery, let alone what is required to fulfill their potential.

##### Keeping Your Word To Yourself

Every time you say you will do something and then do not, you lose a little trust and respect for yourself. Every time you follow through, you create proof that your word means something.

This happens constantly throughout an ordinary day.

You said you would get up, but you want to stay in bed. You planned what you would eat, but now you want something else. You are supposed to be working, but you want to check your phone.

Every one of these situations is an opportunity to practice self-mastery.

Getting yourself to work is one of these opportunities, and avoiding it affects far more than the work itself. Even when you see your friends or family, your head can be somewhere else. You are thinking about the work you did not finish and how much time you wasted.

##### Give Your Actions Meaning

Most people have attached almost no meaning to how they live. Their food, sleep, exercise, wake-up time, and work mean very little to them.

You can assign meaning to these things by connecting them to your hopes, dreams, and the person you want to become.

Every time you sit down to work, what does that mean for the person you want to become and the life you want to live?

When your alarm goes off, what does waking up mean? You get to begin another day of doing the things that matter to you.

This applies to your standards, appearance, relationships, and everything else too. Self-mastery becomes harder when none of it means anything to you.

##### Practice It When The Opportunity Appears

Every time you have an opportunity to exercise self-mastery, recognize it and follow through, no matter how small the opportunity is.

If you catch yourself doing the wrong thing, correct it immediately. Do not decide that because you wasted 20 minutes, you might as well waste the rest of the day.

A lot of this will require conscious effort at first. We are going to keep practicing until it becomes how you normally live.

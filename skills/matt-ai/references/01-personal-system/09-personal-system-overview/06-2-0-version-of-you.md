You cannot expect to achieve different goals while keeping everything about yourself the same.

The 2.0 version of you is your collection of beliefs, character traits, and skills, developed into the person capable of living the life you want.

##### Beliefs, Character Traits And Skills

Your beliefs affect what you consider possible, what you attempt, and what you accept from yourself.

Your character traits affect how you behave. If your focus is poor, you have to develop it. Knowing you should work does not mean you can already stay focused.

Your skills affect what you can actually do. Some of your goals require abilities you have not developed yet.

This gives you specific things to work on. You can identify which beliefs keep getting in your way, which character traits need work, and which skills you do not have yet.

##### The Life You Want To Live

We are going to build up a picture of your entire life, then break it back down into things you can actually do.

Start by thinking about the five biggest accomplishments you actually care about achieving. If you could only achieve five things in your entire life, what would they be?

These should be much bigger than what you currently feel capable of achieving.

Think about everything that would need to happen, everything you would need to learn, and who you would need to become. You should find that a lot of your smaller goals fit inside them.

Write about what you achieve, what it means to you, how it feels, and who you become. Include the actions you need to take and the standards you need to live by.

Bring everything together so you can understand what the whole life looks like.

##### Work On Yourself Every Day

We will set aside one hour every day to work on this. I call it the 2.0 You Hour.

This is active work. You should be writing, reading, making decisions, and working through things.

At the beginning, much of the hour will go toward understanding what you actually want. As that becomes clearer, you will spend more time working on specific improvements and keeping your plans moving.

Your life vision might take decades. That is fine. But you still need to decide what you are going to accomplish in the next year and what you can actually do about it now.

Do not wait until you have finished every possible responsibility before deciding whether there is time left to work on yourself.

##### Behave Like The Person You Want To Become

You act in accordance with how you see yourself. Every time you follow through, you produce evidence that you can rely on yourself.

You do not need to wait until you achieve your goals before you keep your word, take your work seriously, or behave with the standards you wrote down.

This will take practice. You will catch yourself thinking one way, saying something completely different, and then acting differently again. Notice it and correct it.

We want the way you actually live to match the person you say you are.

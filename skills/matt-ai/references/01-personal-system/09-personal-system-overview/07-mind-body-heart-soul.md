You are one person. You are not a separate body, mind, heart, soul, bank account, personal life, and business.

We separate these areas to examine them, but they still belong to the same person. One hidden constraint can hold several of them back.

Poor sleep can make working harder. Unfinished work can leave you frustrated and distracted with your family. One problem affects several areas and other people.

##### Mind

Your mind includes your thoughts, beliefs, perceptions, and the way you interpret yourself and the world.

What enters your mind affects your decisions and actions. Your thinking affects what you notice, what you tolerate, what you decide, and what you repeatedly do.

If you spend the whole day resenting people, assuming things will fail, or telling yourself you are incapable, that deserves your attention.

You are already talking to yourself all day. We want you to pay attention to what you are saying and how it affects what you do.

##### Body

Your body includes your health, fitness, energy, and vitality.

Your body also makes your standards physically visible. It is the accumulated result of thousands of small decisions.

How do you expect yourself to be confident if you are constantly reminded of your low standards and poor decisions every time you walk past a mirror or take a photo?

Your health affects your energy, and your energy affects your work. Pay attention to the things that affect how you feel. If you keep eating huge meals and then struggling to work, change what you are doing. Take the previous night’s sleep seriously.

We want repeatable food, sleep, and exercise habits, along with actual records so you can see what is improving and what still needs to change.

##### Heart

Your heart is your emotional life.

Frustration and resentment can become huge blockers. Joy and love change how you experience your day and treat people.

There may be things you are still holding against other people or yourself. Old mistakes, wasted opportunities, and times you know you did not give your full effort can keep weighing on you.

These things need your attention too.

We will also spend time understanding what brings you joy and what you actually feel grateful for. You will still have difficult feelings and real problems to deal with, but you do not need to spend the entire day feeding them.

##### Soul

To me, this is “you” more than anything else.

You are the accumulation of your mind, body, heart, and how you choose to live. Your self-mastery is part of that.

Repeatedly acting against what you know you should do poisons how you see yourself. Every time you follow through, you create proof that your word means something.

Your thoughts, work, decisions, and standards accumulate into the person you are becoming.

We are working on the whole person. When one part is holding several others back, we need to find it, work on it, and then deal with the next constraint.

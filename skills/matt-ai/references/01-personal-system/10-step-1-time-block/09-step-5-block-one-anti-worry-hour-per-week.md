### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you prefer using.
2. **Block one hour every week for everything sitting unresolved in the back of your mind.** The appointment you need to book. The form you need to submit. The thing you need to replace. The annoying task you cannot delegate and keep avoiding.
3. **Set it to repeat every week.** Write these things down during the week instead of letting them interrupt your work. Then handle as many as possible during the scheduled hour.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Did you block one hour every week for everything sitting unresolved in the back of your mind?
- Did you set it to repeat every week?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you prefer using.
2. **Block one hour every day to work on the 2.0 version of yourself.** This is not another hour for email, client work, or whatever happens to be on fire. It is for your goals, vision, beliefs, standards, skills, and the gaps between who you are now and who you need to become.
3. **Set it to repeat at roughly the same time every day.** Roughly is enough, it does not have to land on the exact minute. Like every other block, it goes into the calendar first and everything else gets arranged around it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Did you block one hour every day to work on the 2.0 version of yourself?
- Did you set it to repeat at roughly the same time every day?

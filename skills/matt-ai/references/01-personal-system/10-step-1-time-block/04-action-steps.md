# Action Steps

Course: Step #1: Time Block
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/c15cebb8?md=a916cee34c8d4022bc8bc129f4bfca34

---
The Theory explains that people whine about not having time, and that they are probably right. They do not have time because they never time-block. They just exist and let everyone else dictate their decisions and their time for them.

This course is where you actually block the time out.

You will block the 15-Minute Daily Setup, four work hours with the breaks between them, your daily 2.0 You Hour, three gym sessions and one Anti-Worry Hour. Then you go through what is already on your calendar, move or remove whatever gets in the way, and put a real day and a real time on every block.

Do not wait until you know what all of these blocks are for. You will learn about them in rapid succession, and nothing is possible if you never made time for them.

### Table of Contents

1. **Block The 15-Minute Daily Setup:** Block 15 minutes at the start of every day to record the previous day's data, review what you need to complete, and begin working.
2. **Block The First Four Hours:** Block four separate 60-minute work periods with a 10 to 15 minute break between each one, starting as early as you can.
3. **Block The Daily 2.0 You Hour:** Block one hour every day for your goals, vision, beliefs, standards and skills.
4. **Block Three Gym Sessions Per Week:** Choose three days and times and put all three sessions on the calendar.
5. **Block One Anti-Worry Hour Per Week:** Block one hour every week to handle everything sitting unresolved in the back of your mind.
6. **Rearrange Existing Commitments:** Find every recurring commitment that interferes with your new blocks and move or remove it.
7. **Finalize The New Schedule:** Confirm all five blocks are on the calendar with actual days and times, then arrange your meetings, calls and errands around them.

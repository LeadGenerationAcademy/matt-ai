### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you prefer using.
2. **Block four separate 60-minute work periods.** These must be four actual work hours. Not four hours sitting near your computer while checking messages, taking calls, eating, and doing random shit.
3. **Block a 10 to 15 minute break between each work period.** The breaks get blocked too. Your calendar needs to hold the four hours and the breaks between them.
4. **Set the first block to start as early as you can.** Start early, protect the time, and finish all four hours before the rest of the day gets a chance to interfere.
5. **Set the blocks to repeat every day.** Leave the space empty and other people will fill it with their meetings, calls, favors, messages, and whatever else they happen to need from you.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Did you block four separate 60-minute work periods?
- Did you block a 10 to 15 minute break between each work period?
- Does the first block start as early as you can make it?
- Did you set the blocks to repeat every day?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you prefer using.
2. **Block 15 minutes at the beginning of every day.** During this time you will record the previous day's financial, health, and time data, review the three to five things you need to complete today, and immediately begin working.
3. **Set the block to repeat every day.** If something actually matters, put it in your calendar. Do not tell yourself you will remember or find time later. You will not.
4. **Confirm the block sits outside your first work hour.** This does not count as part of your first work hour. You have 15 minutes. Do not turn it into 45 minutes of rearranging tasks and pretending that planning is work.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Did you block 15 minutes at the beginning of every day?
- Did you set the block to repeat every day?
- Does the block sit outside your first work hour?

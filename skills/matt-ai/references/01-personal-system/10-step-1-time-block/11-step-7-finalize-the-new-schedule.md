### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you prefer using.
2. **Confirm your calendar contains all five blocks.** The 15-Minute Daily Setup, the First Four Hours, the daily 2.0 You Hour, three weekly gym sessions and one weekly Anti-Worry Hour.
3. **Choose the actual days and times.** Every block needs a real day and a real time on it, not a rough intention to fit it in somewhere.
4. **Arrange your meetings, calls and errands around these blocks.** The blocks go into the calendar first and everything else gets arranged around them. Once the schedule is finished, stop rebuilding it every three days because one morning felt difficult.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Does your calendar contain all five blocks?
- Did you choose the actual days and times?
- Did you arrange your meetings, calls and errands around these blocks?

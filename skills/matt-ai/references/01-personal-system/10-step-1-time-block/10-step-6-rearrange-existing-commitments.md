### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you prefer using.
2. **Find every recurring commitment that interferes with your new blocks.** A one-time event can probably stay. Something that blocks the same time every week is the problem.
3. **Rearrange or remove each one.** Moving it is enough if the commitment still matters to you. If it does not, remove it.
4. **Communicate with the people involved.** You cannot say you want a different life while protecting every part of the schedule that created your current one.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Did you find every recurring commitment that interferes with your new blocks?
- Did you rearrange or remove each one?
- Did you communicate with the people involved?

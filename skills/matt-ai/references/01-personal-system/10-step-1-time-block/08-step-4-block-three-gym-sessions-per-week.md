### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r).** Or whichever calendar you prefer using.
2. **Choose three days and times for the gym.** Monday, Wednesday and Friday works. Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday works too. Spread them out and choose times you can maintain.
3. **Block all three sessions.** Three sessions is the standard, so all three go on the calendar, not the one or two you are confident about.
4. **Set them to repeat every week.** Three every week, not three in the weeks that happen to go smoothly.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your calendar?
- Did you choose three days and times for the gym?
- Did you block all three sessions?
- Did you set them to repeat every week?

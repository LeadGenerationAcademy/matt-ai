So many people will whine that they "don't have time" to do X, Y and Z. They can whine all they want, but excuses sound best to the person making them up.

That brings us to the topic of time-blocking, which is exactly what it sounds like. We need to block off time in our calendar to do specific things.

In a sense, the whiny excuse-making people are right. They probably do not have time, because they do not time-block. They just exist in the world and let everyone else dictate their decisions and time for them. If you want to be a big success, this is not how you need to live your life.

So, in this course, we will block off the most important blocks of time. Just make sure you block these off for now. Do not wait. I know that you don't necessarily know what some of these things are yet that you are blocking time to do, but you will learn about them in rapid succession and nothing will be possible if you don't make time for them.

These essentially include :

- 15 minutes per day for our personal tracking and planning.
- 4 hours of deep work per day
- 1 hour per day to learn and work on the 2.0 version of yourself
- Three one-hour gym sessions per week
- One anti-worry hour per week

These are the things that you need to make time for as these will be foundational for this Skool group and foundational for your life.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Anti-Worry List."** It goes inside your Lists folder. This is where you will record the small unresolved problems that continue taking up space in your head.
2. **Add any obvious worries now.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Leave the rest for the weekly Anti-Worry Hour.** We will deal with them properly then. You are not solving anything here. You are only writing down what is already bothering you.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Anti-Worry List"?
- Did you add any obvious worries now?
- Did you leave the rest for the weekly Anti-Worry Hour?

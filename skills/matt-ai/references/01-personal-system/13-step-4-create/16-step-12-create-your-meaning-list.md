### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Meaning List."** It goes inside your Lists folder. This is where you will attach meaning to your actions, so they'll be more likely to stick.
2. **Add anything obvious.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Otherwise, leave it blank for now.** You will add to this as you go.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Meaning List"?
- Did you add anything obvious?
- Did you leave it blank if nothing came to mind?

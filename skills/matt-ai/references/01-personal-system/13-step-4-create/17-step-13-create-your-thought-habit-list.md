### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Thought Habit List."** It goes inside your Lists folder. This is where you'll deliberately choose what you think in certain situations.
2. **Add anything obvious.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Otherwise, leave it blank for now.** You will add to this as you go.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Thought Habit List"?
- Did you add anything obvious?
- Did you leave it blank if nothing came to mind?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Foundational Causes List."** It goes inside your Lists folder. These are ordinary moments, causes and decisions that have tremendous downstream effects and consequences.
2. **Add the core habits or things you must do on a repeated basis to achieve your life goals.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Otherwise, leave it blank for now.** We will take care of these later.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Foundational Causes List"?
- Did you add the core habits or things you must do on a repeated basis to achieve your life goals?
- Did you leave it blank if nothing came to mind?

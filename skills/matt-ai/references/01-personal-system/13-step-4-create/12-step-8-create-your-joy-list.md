Create a note called "Joy List." Add anything that reliably makes you happy, changes your state, or brings you back to yourself.

Do not turn this into an assignment and start inventing answers.

### Prerequisites

- [Step #5: Create Your Lists Folder](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/6b693ee0?md=72ff06440ee34fcb9d0a0cc7c8c11946) (Current Course)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Joy List."** It goes inside your Lists folder.
2. **Add anything that reliably makes you happy, changes your state, or brings you back to yourself.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Stop when you run out of real answers.** Do not turn this into an assignment and start inventing answers.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Joy List"?
- Did you add anything that reliably makes you happy, changes your state, or brings you back to yourself?
- Did you stop when you ran out of real answers?

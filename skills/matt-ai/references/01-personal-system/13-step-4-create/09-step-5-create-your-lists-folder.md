### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a separate folder called "Lists."** This is its own folder, not a note sitting inside your 2.0 folder. Keep it in the same notes app you used for your 2.0 folder.
2. **Keep all nine lists inside it.** Create the structure now and add only what immediately comes to mind. These lists will develop as you use the System.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a separate folder called "Lists"?
- Is it in the same notes app you used for your 2.0 folder?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Find one video that genuinely changes your state.** You listen to it in the morning to deliberately change your physical and mental state before the rest of the day begins.
2. **Do not choose something because it has dramatic music and 50 million views.** Choose the video that actually does something to you. Mine is ["I'm the One"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMAZJnevY3s&t) by Ed Mylett.
3. **Save it.** One video, not a playlist of twenty you will scroll through every morning.
4. **Download it through YouTube Premium if possible.** This is so it is always available.

### Completion Audit

- Did you find one video that genuinely changes your state?
- Did you choose the video that actually does something to you, rather than one with dramatic music and 50 million views?
- Did you save it?
- If you have YouTube Premium, did you download it?

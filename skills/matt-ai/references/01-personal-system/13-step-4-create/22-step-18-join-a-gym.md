### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Search "gyms near me" on Google Maps.** Skip this step if you are already a member of a gym.
2. **Pick the gym closest to where you live.** Walking distance is best. If that is impossible, choose the closest reasonable option.
3. **Confirm it has weights.** We need reliable access to weights three times per week. Almost any commercial gym will work.
4. **Get a membership.** Do not spend three weeks comparing equipment or convincing yourself that you need a home gym.

### Completion Audit

- Did you search "gyms near me" on Google Maps?
- Did you pick the gym closest to where you live?
- Did you confirm it has weights?

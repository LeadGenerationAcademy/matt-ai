### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Gratitude List."** It goes inside your Lists folder.
2. **Add a few things you genuinely feel grateful for right now.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Stop when you run out of real answers.** Three real answers are more useful than 50 pieces of generic bullshit you do not actually feel.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Gratitude List"?
- Did you add a few things you genuinely feel grateful for right now?
- Did you stop when you ran out of real answers?

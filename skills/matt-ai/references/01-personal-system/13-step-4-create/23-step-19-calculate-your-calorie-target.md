### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open a [maintenance-calorie calculator](https://www.calculator.net/calorie-calculator.html).** Any free one online will do. They all use the same basic inputs.
2. **Enter your age, height, weight, and activity level.** This will estimate how many calories you need to maintain your current weight.
3. **Subtract 500 calories from maintenance if you need to lose weight.** Our target is 15% body fat. My maintenance is roughly 3,000 calories because I am 6'5" and around 230 pounds, so my weight-loss target would be about 2,500.
4. **Add 500 if you need to gain weight.** Same number in the other direction.
5. **Add your final target to your calorie-tracking app.** This is the number the app holds you to every day from now on.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open a maintenance-calorie calculator?
- Did you enter your age, height, weight, and activity level?
- If you need to lose weight, did you subtract 500 calories from maintenance?

- Did you add your final target to your calorie-tracking app?

# Action Steps

Course: Step #4: Create
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/6b693ee0?md=17a8fa606d8243e4b7e87e13b3409c72

---
The Theory explains that this is one of the most important parts of the entire course, because everything after it gets set up in rapid succession on top of what you build here.

In this course you will create everything and get it ready for when you actually start putting things into it.

You will build the folders and documents your 2.0 vision, your lists and your one-year plan live in, then get the health side handled from the gym membership through to your food routine.

You can do almost all of this right now. The one part you may have to wait on is sleep tracking, which needs your wearable to arrive.

Do not skip anything in here because it looks like admin. Every single thing is a prerequisite for your health, your 2.0 version and your organized planning. Get it done and you never have to do this crap again.

### Table of Contents

1. **Create Your 2.0 Folder:** A folder in Apple Notes called "2.0" followed by your name.
2. **Create Your Five Pillar Documents:** Five notes with the Goal Template copied into each one.
3. **Create Your 2.0 Life Vision Document:** A blank note that will eventually combine all five pillars into one vision.
4. **Create 2.0 Average Wednesday Document:** A blank note that will eventually describe what that vision looks like on a normal day.
5. **Create Your Lists Folder:** A separate folder to hold all nine lists.
6. **Create Your Self Mastery List:** A note for everything you need to gain greater control over.
7. **Create Your Anti-Worry List:** A note for the small unresolved problems taking up space in your head.
8. **Create Your Joy List:** A note for anything that reliably makes you happy or brings you back to yourself.
9. **Create Your Gratitude List:** A note for a few things you genuinely feel grateful for right now.
10. **Create Your Forgiveness List:** A note for any person, event, or part of yourself you know you need to forgive.
11. **Create Your Improvement List:** A note for anything about yourself, your life, or your business that could be better.
12. **Create Your Meaning List:** A note for attaching meaning to your actions so they are more likely to stick.
13. **Create Your Thought Habit List:** A note for deliberately choosing what you think in certain situations.
14. **Create Your Foundational Causes List:** A note for the ordinary moments and decisions that carry tremendous downstream consequences.
15. **Create Your One Year Folder:** A folder for everything related to your current one-year plan.
16. **Create Four One-Year Goal Documents:** Four blank notes inside that folder, left blank until your 2.0 You Hour.
17. **Create Your 90-Day Objectives Document:** A blank note for the four objectives you will pursue over the next 90 days.
18. **Join A Gym:** The closest gym to you that has weights, with a membership.
19. **Calculate Your Calorie Target:** Your maintenance calories adjusted by 500 in either direction and entered into your app.
20. **Create Your Workout Routine:** A three-day routine built around your gym's equipment and saved as templates.
21. **Create Your Stretching Routine:** A five-minute routine built around the areas where you actually have problems, added to Bend.
22. **Set Up Sleep Tracking:** Sleep tracking turned on in your wearable's app and connected to your other health apps.
23. **Create A Simple Food Routine:** One or two simple meals that hit your targets using food you actually enjoy.
24. **Find Motivational Video:** One video that genuinely changes your state, saved and downloaded.
25. **Create Your Morning Checklist:** The morning checklist pasted into a note, with the routine itself left for later.
26. **Create Your Night Checklist:** The night checklist pasted into a note, with the routine itself left for later.

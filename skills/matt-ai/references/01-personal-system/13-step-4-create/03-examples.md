### Stretching Routines

**Stretching routine to for lower and middle back pain:**

- **0:00 to 0:30:** Cat-Cow
- **0:30 to 1:00:** Thread the Needle (Left)
- **1:00 to 1:30:** Thread the Needle (Right)
- **1:30 to 2:00:** Child's Pose
- **2:00 to 2:30:** Half-Kneeling Hip-Flexor Stretch (Left)
- **2:30 to 3:00:** Half-Kneeling Hip-Flexor Stretch (Right)
- **3:00 to 3:30:** Single Knee-to-Chest (Left)
- **3:30 to 4:00:** Single Knee-to-Chest (Right)
- **4:00 to 4:30:** Supine Knee Rolls
- **4:30 to 5:00:** Double Knee-to-Chest

**Stretching routine for neck and shoulder pain:**

- **0:00 to 0:30:** Chin Tucks
- **0:30 to 1:00:** Upper Trap Stretch (Left)
- **1:00 to 1:30:** Upper Trap Stretch (Right)
- **1:30 to 2:00:** Levator Scapulae Stretch (Left)
- **2:00 to 2:30:** Levator Scapulae Stretch (Right)
- **2:30 to 3:00:** Doorway Chest Stretch
- **3:00 to 3:30:** Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch (Left)
- **3:30 to 4:00:** Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch (Right)
- **4:00 to 4:30:** Shoulder Rolls
- **4:30 to 5:00:** Seated Thoracic Extension

**Stretching routine for tight hips and hamstrings:**

- **0:00 to 0:30:** Butterfly Stretch
- **0:30 to 1:00:** Figure-Four Stretch (Left)
- **1:00 to 1:30:** Figure-Four Stretch (Right)
- **1:30 to 2:00:** Supine Hamstring Stretch (Left)
- **2:00 to 2:30:** Supine Hamstring Stretch (Right)
- **2:30 to 3:00:** Low Lunge Hip-Flexor Stretch (Left)
- **3:00 to 3:30:** Low Lunge Hip-Flexor Stretch (Right)
- **3:30 to 4:00:** 90/90 Hip Stretch (Left)
- **4:00 to 4:30:** 90/90 Hip Stretch (Right)
- **4:30 to 5:00:** Standing Forward Fold

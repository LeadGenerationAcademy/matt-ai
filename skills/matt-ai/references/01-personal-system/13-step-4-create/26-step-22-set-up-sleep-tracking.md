### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Set up sleep tracking inside your wearable's app.** If you already own the wearable, do it now. Otherwise, do it as soon as it arrives.
2. **Connect it to your fitness app.** This is the workout-tracking app you downloaded earlier. Do this wherever possible, and skip it if the two do not offer a connection.
3. **Connect it to your connected scale.** Same rule, skip it if there is no connection available.
4. **Connect it to your calorie-tracking app.** We want your sleep, weight, workouts, and food data working together.

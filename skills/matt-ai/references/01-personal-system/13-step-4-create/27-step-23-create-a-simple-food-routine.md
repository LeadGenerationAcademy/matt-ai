### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Give ChatGPT your calorie target and macro targets.** The calorie target is the number you worked out earlier and put into your calorie-tracking app.
2. **Tell it the foods you actually enjoy eating.** If you give it food you do not like, you will not eat it, and the routine falls apart in a week.
3. **Ask it to create one or two simple meals that fit those targets.** Protein shakes and other basic options are fine. We need a routine you can follow almost every day without getting bored, complaining, or making 20 decisions whenever you become hungry.

### Completion Audit

- Did you give ChatGPT your calorie target and macro targets?
- Did you tell it the foods you actually enjoy eating?
- Did you ask it to create one or two simple meals that fit those targets?

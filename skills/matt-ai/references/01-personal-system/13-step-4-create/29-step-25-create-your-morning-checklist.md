### Template

- Set your wake-up time for 7:00 a.m. or earlier.
- Wake up.
- Drink 500 mL of water and optionally take creatine.
- Walk on the treadmill for 20 minutes while reviewing your goals and lists.
- Stretch for five minutes.
- Complete your reward.
- Begin the Daily 15-Minute Setup.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Copy the checklist above.** It is already written out for you. There is nothing to rewrite.
2. **Open Apple Notes and create a note called "Morning Checklist."** Or the same notes app you used to create the other documents.
3. **Paste the checklist into the note.** You can print it instead if you would rather have it on paper.
4. **Leave the actual routine for later.** We will explain each part later. Right now, make sure the checklist exists.

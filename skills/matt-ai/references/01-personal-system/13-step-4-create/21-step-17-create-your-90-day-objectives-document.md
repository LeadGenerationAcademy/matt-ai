### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a blank note for the four objectives you will pursue during the next 90 days.** It goes inside your One Year folder. Leave it blank for now.
2. **Start with whatever 90-day period you are currently entering.** If you begin on September 1, use September 1 through November 30. Eventually, you can settle into Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a blank note for the four objectives you will pursue during the next 90 days?
- Did you start with whatever 90-day period you are currently entering?

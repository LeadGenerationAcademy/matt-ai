### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create five separate notes inside your 2.0 folder.** Name them Pillar 1, Pillar 2, Pillar 3, Pillar 4 and Pillar 5.
2. **Copy the [Goal Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/121OlUONCeMXAQlhiTLywKPTjB8G8KqwY4w9TvYTmXpI/edit?tab=t.0) into each note.** Every one of the five gets the same template. All five notes start out looking identical. [in this skill: assets/templates/goal-template/]
3. **Add the pillar to any note where it is already obvious.** Otherwise, leave the template blank instead of inventing something just to fill the space.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create five separate notes inside your 2.0 folder?
- Did you copy the Goal Template into each note?
- Did you add the pillar to any note where it was already obvious?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Forgiveness List."** It goes inside your Lists folder.
2. **Add any person, event, or part of yourself that you already know you need to forgive.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Do not try to work through everything now.** We will do that later.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Forgiveness List"?
- Did you add any person, event, or part of yourself that you already know you need to forgive?
- Did you stop before trying to work through everything?

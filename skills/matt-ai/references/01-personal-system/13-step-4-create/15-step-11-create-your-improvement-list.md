### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Create a note called "Improvement List."** It goes inside your Lists folder. Use it to record anything you notice about yourself, your life, or your business that could be better.
2. **Add what is already obvious.** Whatever comes to mind immediately, write it down.
3. **Stop when you run out of real answers.** The list will begin filling itself once you start paying attention.

### Completion Audit

- Did you create a note called "Improvement List"?
- Did you add what is already obvious?
- Did you stop when you ran out of real answers?

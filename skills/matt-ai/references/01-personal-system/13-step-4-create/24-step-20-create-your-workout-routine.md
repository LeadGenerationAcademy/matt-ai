### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **List the dumbbells, machines, barbells, and other equipment available at your gym.** The routine is useless if it tells you to use something your gym does not have.
2. **Give the list to ChatGPT and ask it to create a simple three-day routine.** Tell it you want at least six exercises per workout, roughly 15 working sets per workout, and eight reps per set.
3. **Save all three workouts as templates inside your workout-tracking app.** Templates mean you open the app at the gym and the workout is already sitting there.
4. **Use strict form.** If you complete eight reps on the final set of an exercise, increase the weight next time.
5. **Write your own routine instead if you are an experienced bodybuilder.** Everyone else should keep it simple.

### Completion Audit

- Did you list the dumbbells, machines, barbells, and other equipment available at your gym?
- Did you give the list to ChatGPT and ask it to create a simple three-day routine?
- Did you save all three workouts as templates inside your workout-tracking app?
- Are you using strict form?
- If you are an experienced bodybuilder, did you write your own routine instead?

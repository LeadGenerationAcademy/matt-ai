### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Download the official [Brick](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/brick-ditch-distractions/id6448794069) app.** The app is where the blocks get set. The device is how you turn them on and off.
2. **Connect it to your Brick device.** Tap your phone against the Brick to pair the two.
3. **Create an account.** The app needs one before it will do anything.
4. **Confirm the physical device can activate and remove a block.** Set a test block, tap the Brick to turn it on, then tap it again to take it off. We will decide exactly what to block later.

### Completion Audit

- Did you download the official Brick app?
- Did you connect it to your Brick device?
- Did you create an account?
- Can the physical device activate and remove a block?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Install [YouTube Feed Blocker](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/block-youtube-feed-homepa/lcpclaffcdiihapebmfgcmmplphbkjmd) in Chrome.** It is a Chrome extension. It installs straight from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Turn it on.** Open the extension and switch on the blocking. YouTube is useful. Its home page, recommendations, and endless related videos are designed to pull you away from whatever you originally opened it to do.

### Completion Audit

- Did you install YouTube Feed Blocker in Chrome?
- Did you turn it on?

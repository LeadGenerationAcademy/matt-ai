### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Download the app that matches your wearable.** Use Oura for an Oura Ring or Apple Health for an Apple Watch.
2. **Create an account.** The wearable syncs into the account. It has to exist first.
3. **Connect the wearable.** Pair the device with the app. Nothing gets recorded until it is paired.
4. **Confirm it is recording data.** Do this before moving on. Open the app and check something is actually showing up. If nothing is there, it is not connected properly.

### Completion Audit

- Did you download the app that matches your wearable?
- Did you create an account?
- Did you connect the wearable?
- Did you confirm it is recording data?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the Time [Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14t3J3f-tSw_N91AczMsPPPvkLnuwWwAevCEuPEB1esk/edit?usp=sharing).** Open it and go File then Make A Copy. That gives you your own editable version to work in. [in this skill: assets/templates/tracking-spreadsheet/]
2. **Bookmark your copy in your browser.** This will track your wake-up time, completed work hours, and efficiency. You want it one click away.
3. **Name the bookmark "T."** A single letter keeps the bookmark bar short and gets you into the right sheet without reading anything.

### Completion Audit

- Did you make a copy of the Time Tracking Spreadsheet?
- Did you bookmark your copy in your browser?
- Did you name the bookmark "T."?

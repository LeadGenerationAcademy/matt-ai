### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Financial Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?usp=sharing).** Open it and go File then Make A Copy. That gives you your own editable version to work in. We will configure it later. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
2. **Bookmark your copy in your browser.** This spreadsheet gets opened constantly. You want it one click away.
3. **Name the bookmark "F."** A single letter keeps the bookmark bar short and gets you into the right sheet without reading anything.

### Completion Audit

- Did you make a copy of the Financial Tracking Spreadsheet?
- Did you bookmark your copy in your browser?
- Did you name the bookmark "F."?

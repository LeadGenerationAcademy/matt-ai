### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Install [News Feed Eradicator](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/news-feed-eradicator/fjcldmjmjhkklehbacihaiopjklihlgg) in Chrome.** It removes feeds from sites such as LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. It is a Chrome extension and installs straight from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Turn it on.** Open the extension and switch it on before you go back to any of those sites.
3. **Go through the list of sites and switch each one on.** You can pick which sites it works on. Turn on every site you have a habit of scrolling.

### Completion Audit

- Did you install News Feed Eradicator in Chrome?
- Did you turn it on?
- Did you go through the list of sites and switch each one on?

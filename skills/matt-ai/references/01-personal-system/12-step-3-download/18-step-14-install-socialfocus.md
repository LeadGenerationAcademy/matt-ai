### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Install [SocialFocus](https://socialfocus.app/).** It is a browser extension. You install it from the SocialFocus website and add it to Chrome.
2. **Turn it on.** Open the extension and switch it on before you go back to any social site.
3. **Leave your other feed blockers installed.** It overlaps with the other feed blockers. That is intentional. If one extension fails or misses something, the entire algorithm should not immediately come back.

### Completion Audit

- Did you install SocialFocus?
- Did you turn it on?
- Did you leave your other feed blockers installed?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Health Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jWjN-JJxWrH-5JzZuJ7a9r9wltk_tixpXVcAOEx8cE8/edit?usp=sharing).** Open it and go File then Make A Copy. That gives you your own editable version to work in. [in this skill: assets/templates/health-tracking-template/]
2. **Bookmark your copy in your browser.** This will eventually track your sleep, food, weight, and workouts. You want it one click away.
3. **Name the bookmark "H."** A single letter keeps the bookmark bar short and gets you into the right sheet without reading anything.

### Completion Audit

- Did you make a copy of the Health Tracking Spreadsheet?
- Did you bookmark your copy in your browser?
- Did you name the bookmark "H."?

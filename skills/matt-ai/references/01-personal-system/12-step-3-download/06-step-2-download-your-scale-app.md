### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Download the app connected to the scale you bought.** If you bought the RENPHO scale, use the official [RENPHO Health](https://renpho.com/pages/apps-download-renpho) app. A connected scale is useless without its app.
2. **Create an account.** The weigh-ins get stored against the account. It needs to exist before the scale has anywhere to send them.
3. **Connect the scale.** This is what automatically sends every weigh-in to the app. The point is to make tracking your weight require almost no effort.
4. **Weigh yourself once.** You need one real reading on there to test with.
5. **Confirm the result appears inside the app.** If it does not show up, the scale is not actually connected, and you find that out now instead of on your first morning.

### Completion Audit

- Did you download the app connected to the scale you bought?
- Did you create an account?
- Did you connect the scale?
- Did you weigh yourself once?
- Did the result appear inside the app?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Install [IGPlus](https://weblxapplications.com/igp/).** It is a browser extension. You install it from the IGPlus website and add it to Chrome.
2. **Turn it on.** Open the extension and switch the blocking on before you open Instagram again.
3. **Block almost everything on Instagram and redirect it to your inbox.** This is what I do. It lets me use messages and notifications for work without getting destroyed by the feed and Reels.

### Completion Audit

- Did you install IGPlus?
- Did you turn it on?
- Did you block almost everything on Instagram and redirect it to your inbox?

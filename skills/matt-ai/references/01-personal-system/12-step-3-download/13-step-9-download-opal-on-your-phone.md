### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Download [Opal](https://opalapp.com/) on your phone.** Same app, second device. Both need it for the blocks to cover both.
2. **Log in using the same account.** The same one from the previous step. A second account gives you two separate sets of blocks.
3. **Confirm the computer and phone appear under the same account.** Check both devices are listed in there. This is so we can eventually use the same blocks across both devices.

### Completion Audit

- Did you download Opal on your phone?
- Did you log in using the same account?
- Do the computer and phone appear under the same account?

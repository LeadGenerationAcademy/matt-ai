# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #10: Your One Year Goals
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/cddfef59?md=76d593dcc9554313a36f55c77d8ae376

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### In Your Notes

- Four one-year goals are written, one in each One-Year Goal note.
- Every goal is specific and measurable, with a clear target and a deadline inside the next 12 months.
- No goal was shrunk to make it feel achievable.
- Every goal has its vision written.
- The Goal Template is filled in underneath each goal, with why you want it, what achieving it would mean, and five answers in every section.
- The answers are honest ones rather than ones that sound good.
- Every action you could think of is written inside the relevant goal's note, as tasks you could sit down and complete.
- Your four 90-Day Objectives are written in your 90-Day Objectives note.

### In Your War Map

- All four goals are on their own tabs with the Purpose and Actions columns filled in.
- Your start date and end date are on the Next 90 Days tab.
- Your goals and objectives are worded identically in your Notes and your War Map.
- Every action you intend to complete this period has its "Next 90 Days?" box checked.
- The tasks you checked off are showing up on the Next 90 Days tab.
- No Complete checkbox is ticked on work you have not actually done.
- Every selected task has a time, a responsible person and an S through E priority against it.
- S is used sparingly.
- The tasks are sorted S, A, B, C, D, E, with E tasks left out of the schedule.
- Your first week is scheduled, three to five tasks per day starting from Day One.
- The work that unblocks other work is scheduled early enough for the dependent tasks to follow.
- Delegated assignments are on the responsible person's War Map with a completion date.

### Closing The Hour

- You stopped when the timer went off.
- You submitted a Work Form for the hour.
- You recorded what you completed and where you need to continue.

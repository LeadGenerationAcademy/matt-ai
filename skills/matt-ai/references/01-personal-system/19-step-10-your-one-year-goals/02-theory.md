Your Life Vision is so far in the future that you cannot work out the entire plan for achieving it right now.

You know what you want your life to become, but there is a lot between where you are and where you want to end up. Some of it will require knowledge and abilities you have not developed yet. You will understand more as you make progress.

So we need to bring that vision closer and figure out what you can accomplish during the next year.

##### Your One-Year Goals

Your four one-year goals describe what you want to achieve over the next 12 months to move toward your Life Vision.

This gives you something you can actually begin thinking about. You can consider where you are now, what could change within a year, and which accomplishments would make a meaningful difference.

You are choosing the part of your Life Vision you are going to work toward during this period. That gives the year a purpose and helps you decide what deserves your attention.

However, a year is still too far away to plan everything in detail.

##### Your 90-Day Objectives

For detailed planning, 90 days is about as far ahead as you can usefully think.

Beyond that, too much depends on what happens during the period in front of you. What you complete and what you learn will affect what you need to do next. Trying to work out every detail of the entire year means making decisions with information you do not have yet.

That is why we bring the plan down to four objectives for the next 90 days.

These are the things you intend to accomplish during the period you can actually work with. They should contribute to your one-year goals and give you a clear idea of what progress needs to happen now.

The connection matters. You should be able to understand how completing an objective moves you toward a one-year goal, and why achieving that goal matters to your Life Vision. That is how the work in front of you gets its meaning.

You can begin with the 90-day period you are entering now. There is no reason to wait for January or the beginning of a calendar quarter.

As you work through the period, you will have more information to use during your 2.0 You Hours. You can see what you have accomplished, where you are struggling, and what still needs to happen. The plan becomes more useful because you are developing it through actual experience.

When you plan the following 90 days, you will be doing it from a position you could not fully understand at the beginning. You will know more, have completed more, and be better able to decide what comes next.

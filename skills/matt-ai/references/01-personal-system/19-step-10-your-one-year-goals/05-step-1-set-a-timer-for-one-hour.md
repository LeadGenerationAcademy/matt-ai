### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your five pillars.** You should already have the first version of these sitting in Apple Notes, or whatever note app you set your folders up in. They are what your one-year goals come out of.
2. **Open your One Year folder.** This is where the goals themselves go, in the One-Year Goal notes you created earlier.
3. **Open your War Map.** This is the planning document all of it feeds into once the goals are broken down.
4. **Set a [timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer) for 60 minutes.** This is a hard stop. You are getting the first version of your War Map down, and you will continue working on it every day during your 2.0 Hour.
5. **Do not spend the entire hour trying to perfectly word one goal.** You still have to turn the pillars into goals, work out what you can actually do about them, and get your first week scheduled.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your five pillars?
- Did you open your One Year folder?
- Did you open your War Map?
- Did you set a timer for 60 minutes?
- Did you avoid spending the entire hour trying to perfectly word one goal?

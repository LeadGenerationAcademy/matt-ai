### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the same One-Year Goal notes from the previous step and continue where you left off.** These sections sit directly underneath the Vision section in the same template you wrote in the last step.
2. **Fill in What Does This Represent To Me for each goal.** Why you actually want it and what achieving it would mean to you. This is where the goal gets its meaning, and it is what you return to when doing the work becomes inconvenient.
3. **Fill in What Do I Achieve for each goal.** What you actually have once the goal is done.
4. **Fill in Who Do I Become for each goal.** The version of you that exists on the other side of achieving it.
5. **Fill in What Do I Think for each goal.** The thoughts you have once it is true.
6. **Fill in What Do I Say for each goal.** What you are able to say once you have achieved it.
7. **Fill in How Does It Feel for each goal.** What having it actually feels like.
8. **Fill in What Does It Mean for each goal.** What achieving it represents to you.
9. **Fill in What Are The Causes for each goal.** The causes that have to be produced to get this effect, because without causes there are no effects.
10. **Give an honest answer rather than one that sounds good.** Two good signs that you are on the right track are that writing it makes you emotional and that the document feels too personal to send to someone else.

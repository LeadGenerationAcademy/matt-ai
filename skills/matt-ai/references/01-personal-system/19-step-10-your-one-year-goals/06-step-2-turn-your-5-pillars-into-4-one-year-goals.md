### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Read your five pillars.** These are the five things you would pick if you could only achieve five things in your life, so everything you choose next has to move toward them.
2. **Choose four things you want to accomplish in the next 12 months.** They can cover your entire life. They do not all need to be business goals, and they do not need to match the five pillars one for one.
3. **Put each goal in one of the four One-Year Goal notes you created earlier.** The year starts when you start. You are not waiting for January.

### Completion Audit

- Did you read your five pillars?
- Did you choose four things you want to accomplish in the next 12 months?
- Did you put each goal in one of the four One-Year Goal notes you created earlier?

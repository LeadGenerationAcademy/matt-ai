### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the month tab in your War Map and start with Day One.** Day One is the first day you are actually doing this, not the 1st of the month. Find that date on the calendar in the month tab you are currently in.
2. **Choose three to five tasks and put them on that day.** Take them from the planner list on the side of your War Map. These are your assignments for the rest of the day, since your first four work hours still belong to your 10X Project.
3. **Look at the estimated minutes as you do it.** Three tasks that take 15 minutes each are very different from three tasks that take two hours each.
4. **Schedule the things that unblock other work early enough for the dependent tasks to happen afterward.** Those are the ones you marked S, because leaving them undone blocks multiple other things.
5. **Put delegated assignments on the responsible person's War Map too.** Give each one a clear completion date. Anything you marked D already has a name against it.
6. **Work through the remaining days of the first week.** The target for this session is to have the whole first week scheduled, so keep the plan simple enough that you actually get here.

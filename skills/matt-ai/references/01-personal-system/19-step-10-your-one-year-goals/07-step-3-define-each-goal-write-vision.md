### Framework & Criteria

1. **Specific:** What exactly are you going to accomplish?
2. **Measurable:** How will you measure the result?
3. **Achievable:** Is there a plausible way to accomplish it within the year?
4. **Relevant:** How does it contribute to the life you described in your pillars?
5. **Time-bound:** What is the deadline?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Copy the [Goal Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/121OlUONCeMXAQlhiTLywKPTjB8G8KqwY4w9TvYTmXpI/edit?tab=t.hhixovecfu5t) into each of your four One-Year Goal notes.** You created those notes blank during Setup, so the template goes in before you write anything into them. [in this skill: assets/templates/goal-template/]
2. **Fill in Specific for each goal.** What exactly you are going to accomplish, worded so you will know whether you achieved it.
3. **Fill in Measurable for each goal.** The number you check the result against at the end of the year.
4. **Fill in Achievable for each goal.** Whether there is a plausible way to reach it within the year.
5. **Fill in Relevant for each goal.** How it contributes to the life you described in your pillars.
6. **Fill in Time-bound for each goal.** The deadline. The year starts when you start, so it sits inside that window.
7. **Fill in Vision for each goal.** This is what you want and what it looks like once you have it.
8. **Do not shrink the goal to make it achievable.** It can still be a big goal. Achievable does not mean something you could comfortably do without changing anything.

### Completion Audit

- Did you fill in Time-bound for each goal?
- Did you fill in Vision for each goal?

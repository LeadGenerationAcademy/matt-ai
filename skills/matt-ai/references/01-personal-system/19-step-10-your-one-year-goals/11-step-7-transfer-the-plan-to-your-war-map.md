### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [War Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?gid=1372052660#gid=1372052660) and click the One Year Goal 1 tab.** The tabs run along the bottom of the spreadsheet. If you cannot see it, scroll right past the month tabs using the arrows at the bottom right. [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
2. **Put your first goal in the Goal field at the top of that tab.** Word it exactly the way it is worded in your One-Year Goal note.
3. **Put every action for that goal in the Actions column.** One action per row. These are the actions you wrote inside that goal's note. Leave Time, Leverage and Priority alone for now, and do not touch the Sync ID column.
4. **Fill in the Purpose column beside every action.** Every action gets its own purpose, in the same row as the action. Write what that one action does for the goal.
5. **Repeat for the One Year Goal 2, One Year Goal 3 and One Year Goal 4 tabs.** One tab per goal, in the same order as your four notes, so nothing gets mixed up later.
6. **Enter your start date and end date at the top of the Next 90 Days tab.** This is the 90-day period you chose, so if you began on September 1 the end date is November 30.
7. **Confirm your goals and objectives are identical in your Notes and your War Map.** They now exist in both places and need to be worded the same way.
8. **Update both places if you change an objective later.** Do not let your Notes describe one plan while your War Map sends you off doing something else.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your War Map and click the One Year Goal 1 tab?
- Did you put your first goal in the Goal field at the top of that tab?
- Did you put every action for that goal in the Actions column?
- Did you fill in the Purpose column beside every action?
- Did you repeat it for the One Year Goal 2, One Year Goal 3 and One Year Goal 4 tabs?
- Did you enter your start date and end date at the top of the Next 90 Days tab?
- Are your goals and objectives identical in your Notes and your War Map?
- Did you update both places when you changed an objective later?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Inside each goal's note, write down everything you could do to help achieve it.** Keeping them inside the note for the relevant goal gives you somewhere organized to add ideas as they come to you.
2. **Make sure they are actual tasks.** "Create content" does not count. "Create the thumbnail for this video" does. You need something you can schedule, sit down, complete, and check off.
3. **Break any idea that is too broad into the things you would actually need to do.** Keep breaking it down until what is left is something you could sit down and complete.
4. **Get as much down as you can without getting stuck.** This is the first version. You will think of things tomorrow that you did not think of today, and those get added as they come.

### Completion Audit

- Did you write down everything you could do to help achieve each goal inside that goal's note?
- Are they actual tasks?
- Did you break any idea that is too broad into the things you would actually need to do?
- Did you get as much down as you can without getting stuck?

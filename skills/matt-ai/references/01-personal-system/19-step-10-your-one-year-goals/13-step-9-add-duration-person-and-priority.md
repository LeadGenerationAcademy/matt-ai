### Framework & Criteria

1. **S:** Leaving this undone blocks multiple other things. Use S very sparingly.
2. **A:** Important and urgent.
3. **B:** Important, but not urgent.
4. **C:** Something you need to do eventually.
5. **D:** Delegate it to someone else. Assign an actual person.
6. **E:** Eliminate it. You put it on the list, thought about it, and realized it was dumb, unnecessary, or something you no longer want to do. You decided no.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the One Year Goal 1 tab and find the tasks you checked off for the next 90 days.** Only the ones you selected need this. The rest stay as they are until a later 90-day period.
2. **Enter how long you think each one will take in the Time column.** Minutes, not hours, and it is your estimate rather than a number you have to be exact about.
3. **Enter who is responsible for completing it in the Leverage column.** That is where the name goes, even when the name is you. A lot of this work does not need to be done by you.
4. **Assign each task a priority in the Priority column.** Use the S through E scale above.
5. **Use S very sparingly.** An S blocks multiple other things, so if half your list is marked S you have not prioritized anything.
6. **Assign an actual person to anything you mark D.** A task delegated to nobody in particular sits there until you do it yourself.
7. **Repeat on the One Year Goal 2, One Year Goal 3 and One Year Goal 4 tabs.** Every task you plan to execute this 90 days needs all three, not just the ones on the first goal.
8. **Re-sort the tasks in this order: S, A, B, C, D, E.** Your selected 90-day tasks appear in the planner on the side of your War Map, and sorted this way the work you schedule first is already sitting at the top.
9. **Leave E tasks out of the schedule.** They are things you decided not to do, not things you completed.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the One Year Goal 1 tab and read through the Actions column.** This is every action you wrote down for that goal, including the ones that will not happen for months.
2. **Think about what matters now and what needs to happen first.** Some actions will have to wait until something else is finished, and some good ideas will have to wait because something else matters more right now.
3. **Check off the "Next 90 Days?" box for every action you intend to complete during the next 90 days.** That checkbox means the task is selected for the current 90-day period, nothing more.
4. **Repeat on the One Year Goal 2, One Year Goal 3 and One Year Goal 4 tabs.** Each goal gets its own selected tasks, so go through all four before you move on.
5. **Open the Next 90 Days tab and confirm the tasks you checked off are showing up.** That tab fills itself from the boxes you checked, one column per goal, so you never type tasks into it directly.
6. **Leave the Complete checkbox alone until you have actually done the work.** That is a separate checkbox from the one you used to select the task.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the One Year Goal 1 tab and read through the Actions column?
- Did you think about what matters now and what needs to happen first?
- Did you check off the "Next 90 Days?" box for every action you intend to complete during the next 90 days?
- Did you repeat it on the One Year Goal 2, One Year Goal 3 and One Year Goal 4 tabs?
- Did you open the Next 90 Days tab and confirm the tasks you checked off are showing up?
- Did you leave the Complete checkbox alone until you had actually done the work?

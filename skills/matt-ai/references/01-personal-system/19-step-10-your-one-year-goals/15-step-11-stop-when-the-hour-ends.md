### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Stop when the timer goes off.** This is the hard stop you set at the start. Do not turn this into a four-hour setup session.
2. **Submit your Work Form.** The 2.0 Hour is real work and gets recorded the same as your four project hours.
3. **Record what you completed and where you need to continue.** Next session starts from that note instead of you trying to remember where you left off.
4. **Pick up from there during your next 2.0 Hour if you did not finish the entire week.** You are working on this every day from now on, so there is no reason to force the whole thing into one sitting.
5. **Check that previous assignments were completed.** This becomes part of the 2.0 Hour every day, not something you do once.
6. **Add useful actions as you think of them.** You will think of things tomorrow that you did not think of today, and they go in the relevant goal's note.
7. **Keep the objectives identical in Notes and the War Map.** Change one and you change the other, otherwise your Notes describe one plan while your War Map sends you off doing something else.
8. **Continue scheduling the work.** Three to five assignments per day, taken from your 90-day list.

### Completion Audit

- Did you stop when the timer went off?
- Did you submit your Work Form?
- Did you record what you completed and where you need to continue?
- If you did not finish the entire week, did you pick up from there during your next 2.0 Hour?
- Did you check that previous assignments were completed?
- Did you add useful actions as you thought of them?
- Are your objectives identical in Notes and the War Map?
- Did you continue scheduling the work?

### Example

Here are some examples of 90 day actions. Your list would run much longer than this:

- Name The Community
- Write The Community Description
- Design The Cover Image
- Set The Tier Pricing
- Write The Community Rules
- Set The Leaderboard Level Unlocks
- Write The Pinned Welcome Post
- Set Up The Win Feed
- Write The 5-Minute VSL Script
- Invite The First 50 Members

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Look at the four one-year goals.** You now have four defined goals with a list of possible actions sitting under each one, and four objectives come out of them.
2. **Decide what you intend to accomplish toward them during the next 90 days.** Start with whatever 90-day period you are currently entering. If you begin on September 1, use September 1 through November 30. Eventually you can settle into Q1, Q2, Q3 and Q4.
3. **Write those results in your 90-Day Objectives note.** The objective is the result. The tasks underneath it are the things you will do to produce that result.
4. **Be specific about what you want to have completed by the end of that period.** Even one year is a long time to plan in detail, since things change and doing the work reveals steps you could not have known about beforehand.

### Completion Audit

- Did you look at the four one-year goals?
- Did you decide what you intend to accomplish toward them during the next 90 days?
- Did you write those results in your 90-Day Objectives note?
- Were you specific about what you want to have completed by the end of that period?

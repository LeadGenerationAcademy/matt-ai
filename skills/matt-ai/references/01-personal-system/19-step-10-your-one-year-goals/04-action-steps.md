# Action Steps

Course: Step #10: Your One Year Goals
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/cddfef59?md=0c7a8fd23a0d4dacb9d5ba767e6896ff

---
The Theory explains that your Life Vision is too far away to plan in detail right now, so you bring it down to what you can accomplish this year and then down again to the next 90 days.

In this course you will turn your five pillars into four one-year goals and get your first week of work scheduled.

You will define each goal, write the purpose underneath it, and list every action you could take toward it. Then it goes into your War Map, where you pick the tasks for the next 90 days and schedule your first week.

Do not spend the hour trying to perfectly word one goal. This is a hard stop at 60 minutes, and whatever you do not finish gets picked up during your next 2.0 Hour.

### Table of Contents

1. **Set A Timer For One Hour:** Open your five pillars, your One Year folder and your War Map, then set a hard 60-minute stop.
2. **Turn Your 5 Pillars Into 4 One-Year Goals:** Four things you want to accomplish in the next 12 months, one in each of your One-Year Goal notes.
3. **Define Each Goal + Write Vision:** Run every goal through the five criteria and give it a clear target, a deadline and a vision.
4. **Define The Purpose Of Each Goal:** The Goal Template filled in underneath each goal, with five honest answers in every section.
5. **Write Down Every Action You Can Think Of:** Every actual task you could do toward a goal, written inside that goal's note.
6. **Create Your First 90-Day Objectives:** Four results you intend to accomplish during the 90-day period you are entering right now.
7. **Transfer The Plan To Your War Map:** Each goal, its purpose and its actions onto its own tab, worded identically to your notes.
8. **Select The Tasks For The Next 90 Days:** Check off every action you intend to complete during the current period.
9. **Add Duration, Person, And Priority:** A time estimate, a responsible name and an S through E priority on every selected task.
10. **Schedule 3-5 Tasks/day For Your 1st Week:** Three to five assignments per day, starting at Day One and running through the whole week.
11. **Stop When The Hour Ends:** Submit your Work Form, record where you got to, and pick it up during your next 2.0 Hour.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [War Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15a6MaSDqsrZAlJ9_xuV3gkKWII7Uq9LKj6Lp2ee9e3U/edit?usp=sharing).** This is the copy you made during Setup and should have bookmarked in your browser. [in this skill: assets/templates/war-map/]
2. **Confirm every objective has a goal written against it.** All five, in the Goal banner above each one, so you know what the objective actually is without having to remember.
3. **Confirm the Purpose column is filled in.** A purpose sitting beside every action, not just one line for the objective as a whole.
4. **Confirm the Actions column is filled in.** Every action you can think of for that objective, including the ones that will not happen for months.
5. **Confirm your Next 90 Days tasks are checked off.** The actions you actually intend to complete this period, which is what feeds the Next 90 Days tab.
6. **Confirm every one of those tasks has a time against it.** Minutes in the Time column, your estimate rather than a number you have to be exact about.
7. **Confirm every one of those tasks has a person against it.** The responsible name in the Leverage column, even when that name is you.
8. **Confirm every one of those tasks has a priority against it.** S through E in the Priority column, with S used sparingly.
9. **Confirm your first week is scheduled.** Three to five tasks per day on the month tab, starting from Day One rather than the 1st of the month.
10. **Finish anything that is still blank.** This is the last check before the War Map becomes one of the four tabs you open every single morning.

### Completion Audit

- Does every objective have a goal written against it?
- Is the Purpose column filled in?
- Is the Actions column filled in?
- Are your Next 90 Days tasks checked off?
- Does every one of those tasks have a time against it?
- Does every one of those tasks have a person against it?
- Does every one of those tasks have a priority against it?
- Is your first week scheduled?
- Did you finish anything that was still blank?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Copy the formatted transactions out of your Financial Tracking AI project.** This is the single table it returned after you wrote ALL TRANSACTIONS SENT.
2. **Paste them into the Transaction Log on the current month's tab.** Start in the Date column of the first empty row, and only paste across as far as Conversion Rate. USD Amount is a formula and works itself out.
3. **Check every row went into the correct column.** Date, Entity, Transaction Type, Category, Merchant, Original Amount, Currency, Conversion Rate. One shifted column and everything above the log is wrong.
4. **Check income and expenses are classified correctly.** Transaction Type is what decides whether something counts as income or an expense in every total on the tab.
5. **Check every transaction belongs to the correct entity.** Entity is what sends it to Personal or to one of your businesses, and a wrong tag moves money between your life and your company.
6. **Check foreign currencies were converted correctly.** Original Amount multiplied by Conversion Rate has to equal the USD Amount sitting beside it.
7. **Check internal transfers were excluded.** Money moving between two of your own accounts is neither income nor an expense, and leaving it in counts the same money twice.
8. **Check duplicate transactions were not added.** Look before you delete, because the same merchant for the same amount on the same day is often two real charges.
9. **Check pending transactions are clearly identified.** They should have Pending sitting in the Merchant field so you know to come back and confirm them.
10. **Check the formula columns were not overwritten.** If USD Amount got pasted over with a flat number, that column has stopped calculating and will keep being wrong.
11. **Repeat the whole process for the previous month.** That gives you an immediate comparison rather than one lonely month of data. Anything earlier than that is optional.

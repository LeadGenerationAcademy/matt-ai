### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Check Total Income on the current month's tab.** Total row underneath the daily table, Income column. It should match what you actually got paid this month.
2. **Check Personal Expenses.** Same Total row, Personal column. That is everything you tagged Personal and nothing else.
3. **Check Business Expenses By Entity.** The Expenses by Entity table, one line per entity. An entity sitting at zero when it should not be means those rows got tagged wrong in the log.
4. **Check Total Business Expenses.** Business Total on the Total row, which is your entities added together.
5. **Check Total Expenses.** Total Expenses on the Total row, which is business plus personal.
6. **Check Profit Or Loss.** PNL on the Total row, which is income minus total expenses. This is the number the whole sheet exists to produce.
7. **Go to the [Planned vs. Actual](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=1569452217#gid=1569452217) tab and confirm the actuals landed in the right month.** The Actual columns pull from the month tab you just filled, so they should match the totals you have been checking. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
8. **Confirm [Bank Balance](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=320221610#gid=320221610) adds every account correctly.** Total USD In Bank on the date you entered should be every account column added up, in your base currency. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]

### Completion Audit

- Did you check Total Income on the current month's tab?
- Did you check Personal Expenses?
- Did you check Business Expenses By Entity?
- Did you check Total Business Expenses?
- Did you check Total Expenses?
- Did you check Profit Or Loss?
- Did the actuals land in the right month on the Planned vs. Actual tab?
- Does Bank Balance add every account correctly?

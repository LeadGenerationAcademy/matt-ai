### Framework & Criteria

1. **Date:** Date
2. **Wake Up Time:** Time, in h:mm AM/PM format
3. **Get Up Immediately:** Yes / No
4. **Hour 1 Time:** The time you completed Hour 1
5. **Hour 2 Time:** The time you completed Hour 2
6. **Hour 3 Time:** The time you completed Hour 3
7. **Hour 4 Time:** The time you completed Hour 4
8. **Hour 5 to Hour 10 Time:** The time you completed each hour past the fourth
9. **Notes:** Anything important about the day

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the tab for the current month.** Twelve month tabs run along the bottom, all built the same way, with the dates already filled down the left.
2. **Start from today rather than trying to backfill.** You almost certainly did not write down the exact minute you finished every past work hour, so there is nothing accurate to go back and enter. One row per day from here.
3. **Enter your Wake Up Time in h:mm AM/PM format.** Type 9:00 AM, not 9 and not 900. A plain number gets read as a number and every efficiency calculation on that row goes wrong.
4. **Pick Yes or No in Get Up Immediately.** It is a dropdown. Yes means you got up right when the alarm went off.
5. **Enter the time you finished each hour in Hour 1 through Hour 4.** The time you finished, not the time you started and not how long it took. Four hours is the requirement.
6. **Use Hour 5 through Hour 10 only on days you go past four.** They stay empty the rest of the time, and empty is what tells the sheet you did four rather than ten.
7. **Write anything worth remembering about the day in Notes.** Free text. This is where the reason behind a bad number goes, so it still makes sense to you in three months.
8. **Leave a cell blank on any day you did not track it.** Blank, not a zero and not your best guess, because an invented time drags the whole row's efficiency with it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the tab for the current month?
- Did you start from today rather than trying to backfill?
- Did you enter your Wake Up Time in h:mm AM/PM format?
- Did you pick Yes or No in Get Up Immediately?
- Did you enter the time you finished each hour in Hour 1 through Hour 4?
- Did you use Hour 5 through Hour 10 only on days you went past four?
- Did you write anything worth remembering about the day in Notes?
- Did you leave a cell blank on any day you did not track it?

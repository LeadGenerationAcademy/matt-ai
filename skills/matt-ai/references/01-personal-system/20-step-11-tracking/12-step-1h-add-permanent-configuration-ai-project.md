### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Permanent Configuration Prompt Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AlF8hoV8GHyNlhxqJPH_xJiH3njDotCA5ZqwwL9-8_Q/edit?usp=sharing) and copy the whole prompt.** It has a permanent configuration section you fill in and a set of output rules you leave exactly as they are. [in this skill: assets/templates/permanent-configuration-prompt-template/]
2. **Paste it into the project instructions inside your Financial Tracking AI project.** The project instructions, not a normal chat message, so it applies to every conversation you have in there from now on.
3. **Replace the base currency with the one you chose.** Same currency as the rest of the spreadsheet, since every conversion rate it gives you has to land in that currency.
4. **Replace the entities with yours.** Personal plus every business, worded exactly the way they are worded in the Entity dropdown.
5. **Map every account you own to its entity.** Every bank account, credit card and payment processor you pull statements from. This is how it knows whether a transaction is personal or business without asking you every time.
6. **Replace the allowed expense categories with the ones from your spreadsheet.** These have to match your Category dropdown exactly, otherwise you will be fixing categories by hand every single month.
7. **Check the nine output columns match your Transaction Log.** Date, Entity, Transaction Type, Category, Merchant, Original Amount, Currency, Conversion Rate, Base-Currency Amount, in that order, so anything it gives you pastes straight in.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your [Financial Tracking Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=1818769831#gid=1818769831).** This is the copy you made during Setup and bookmarked as "F." Have a quick look around the three tabs below, you are not entering anything yet. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
2. **Open the [Planned vs. Actual](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=1569452217#gid=1569452217) tab.** This is the planned expenses part. Your personal and business budget gets typed in here, and the actual numbers pull in from the month tabs on their own. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
3. **Open the [Bank Balance](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=320221610#gid=320221610) tab.** Every account you hold money in gets its own column, and you log what is in each one by date. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
4. **Open the tab for the current month.** This is where monthly transactions go. Everything above the transaction log on that tab calculates itself from what you log.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your Financial Tracking Spreadsheet?
- Did you open the Planned vs. Actual tab?
- Did you open the Bank Balance tab?
- Did you open the tab for the current month?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Bookmark all four.** You did this in Setup as F, H and T plus the War Map. This is confirming all four actually exist and open.
2. **Pin all four tabs in your browser.** Pinned means they are already open before you sit down, so the day does not start with you hunting for links.
3. **Stand up and leave your desk.** You are running a rehearsal of tomorrow morning, and it does not work if you never actually left.
4. **Come back and sit down as if you were beginning a new day.** Same as any other morning. You sit down and the setup begins.
5. **Start a 15-minute timer.** Fifteen is the entire point of this. Without the timer you have no idea whether this takes you twelve minutes or forty.
6. **Fill in your Finance sheet.** The current balance of every account, then the new transactions since your last update.
7. **Fill in your Health sheet.** This morning's weight, last night's sleep, and yesterday's food, workout and steps.
8. **Fill in your Time sheet.** Yesterday's wake up time, the time you finished each work hour, whether you got up immediately, and any notes.
9. **Review the War Map.** Today's date on the month tab, where the tasks were already scheduled during your 2.0 Hours.
10. **Choose the three to five assignments you will complete today.** No more than five. There is only so much meaningful work that fits into one day.
11. **Confirm you finished everything in 15 minutes or less.** If you did, the setup works and you are done.
12. **Identify what slowed you down if you did not.** It is usually one thing. A login, an export you had to go dig for, a spreadsheet you could not find.
13. **Remove the friction.** Fix that one thing rather than accepting a setup that takes twice as long every morning from now on.
14. **Test it again.** This exists to get you into the work, and it cannot turn into another hour of pretending to work.

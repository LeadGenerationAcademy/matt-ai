### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Bank Balance](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=320221610#gid=320221610) tab.** Dates run down the left, one column per account across the middle, and the total at the end. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
2. **Replace the Bank 1 through Bank 6 headers with every account you currently hold money in.** Six columns ship with the template. Leave the extras blank if you have fewer, and insert new columns between the existing ones if you have more, so the total keeps counting them.
3. **Use the actual institution and account type in each name.** Chase Personal Checking, Mercury Business Checking, PayPal Business, Cash. You want to know exactly what you are looking at without thinking about it.
4. **Add the final four digits of the account number if two accounts have the same name.** Two accounts at the same bank are otherwise impossible to tell apart six months from now.
5. **Include the currency in the name if an account holds a currency other than your base currency.** HSBC Business GBP, Wise Business EUR, Emirates NBD Personal AED. You need to know which balances have to be converted before you enter them.
6. **Leave the Total USD In Bank column alone.** It adds every bank column for that day on its own, and that is where your total shows up.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Bank Balance tab?
- Did you replace the Bank 1 through Bank 6 headers with every account you currently hold money in?
- Did you use the actual institution and account type in each name?
- Did you add the final four digits of the account number if two accounts have the same name?
- Did you include the currency in the name if an account uses a different one?
- Did you leave the Total USD In Bank column alone?

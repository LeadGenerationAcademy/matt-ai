# Action Steps

Course: Step #11: Tracking
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/aaa118a0?md=3b1911cb5c9c46b599f341cbcbeb3785

---
The Theory explains that your time, your health and your finances are the three most important things in your life, and that the idea you have of how you are doing in each one is usually very different from reality.

In this course you will set up all three spreadsheets and get real numbers into them.

You will configure the Financial Tracking Spreadsheet along with an AI project that formats your transactions for it, then get your health and time numbers in. The last step is a full rehearsal of the 15-Minute Daily Setup that brings all of it into the start of your day.

Do not guess a number you did not actually track. Leave the cell blank instead, because a made-up number leaves you making decisions on something wrong, which is the exact problem tracking is supposed to solve.

### Table of Contents

1. **Configure The Financial Spreadsheet:** Open your copy and look around the Planned vs. Actual, Bank Balance and month tabs.
2. **Create A Dedicated AI Project:** A ChatGPT Project called Financial Tracking with the spreadsheet uploaded into it.
3. **Choose Your Base Currency:** The single currency every transaction and bank balance gets converted into.
4. **Configure Planned Expenses:** Your real personal and business categories and amounts replacing the examples.
5. **Configure Bank-Balance Columns:** One column per account, named so you know exactly what you are looking at.
6. **Record Today's Bank Balances:** The available balance of every account, converted into your base currency.
7. **Configure The Monthly Transaction Tab:** Your entities and your category list set up the same way on every month tab.
8. **Add Permanent Configuration - AI Project:** Your currency, entities, accounts and categories pasted into the project instructions.
9. **Export Your Transactions:** Every transaction from the 1st of the month, from every account, sent to the project.
10. **Paste The Transactions:** The formatted table pasted into the Transaction Log and checked column by column.
11. **Verify The Spreadsheet:** Your income, expenses by entity, profit or loss and bank total all checked against reality.
12. **Configure The Health Spreadsheet:** Open your copy and have a look around it.
13. **Daily Inputs:** Every field you already have, backfilled from the first day of the current month.
14. **Calculated Fields:** Tested with five fake rows and then cleared, without touching a single formula.
15. **Configure The Time Spreadsheet:** Open your copy and have a look at the two parts.
16. **Information You Enter:** Your wake-up time and the time you finished each work hour, starting from today.
17. **Information The Spreadsheet Calculates:** Tested the same way, with uneven days so you can see 4h Consistency flip.
18. **Configure The War Map:** Confirm every goal, purpose, action, time, person, priority and your first week are filled in.
19. **Test The 15-Minute Daily Setup:** A full rehearsal against a 15-minute timer, with the friction removed if you go over.

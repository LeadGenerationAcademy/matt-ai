### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Find today's date in the Date column.** The table is already built out for every day of the year, so you are filling in a row that exists rather than typing a date in yourself.
2. **Enter the current available balance of every account in its column.** Available balance, not what you expect to land later in the week.
3. **Convert foreign-currency balances into your base currency before entering them.** There are no conversion-rate columns on this tab, so whatever you type has to already be converted.
4. **Use the balance that exists after a transfer has settled.** Money moving between two of your own accounts can show up in both at once, and that counts the same money twice.

### Completion Audit

- Did you find today's date in the Date column?
- Did you enter the current available balance of every account in its column?
- Did you convert foreign-currency balances into your base currency before entering them?
- Did you use the balance that exists after a transfer has settled?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Backfill every field you already have from the first day of the current month.** First of the month through today. This is the same section you will be filling in every morning from here on.
2. **Pull each number from the app that already has it.** Weight off your connected scale, sleep off your wearable, calories and macros out of your calorie app, tonnage and PRs out of your workout app. You are moving numbers into one place, not working anything out.
3. **Pick pounds or kilograms and use the same one every time.** That covers Weight and Tonnage both. Switch units partway through and every trend built on top of them is wrong.
4. **Leave any wearable field blank if your device does not provide it.** An empty cell beats a guessed one, because the sleep calculations are built straight off those numbers.
5. **Backfill the previous month if you want an immediate comparison.** Optional, but it means you can see whether this month is better or worse right away instead of waiting 30 days to find out.

### Completion Audit

- Did you backfill every field you already have from the first day of the current month?
- Did you pull each number from the app that already has it?
- Did you pick pounds or kilograms and use the same one every time?
- Did you leave any wearable field blank where your device does not provide it?
- Did you backfill the previous month if you wanted an immediate comparison?

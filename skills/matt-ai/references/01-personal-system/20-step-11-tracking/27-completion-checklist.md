### Financial Tracking

- Your base currency is chosen and every transaction and balance uses it.
- Your Planned vs. Actual tab has your real personal and business categories with the amounts you expect to spend.
- Your Bank Balance tab has one column per account, named so you can tell them apart, with the currency in the name of any foreign account.
- Today's balances are entered and already converted into your base currency.
- Every month tab carries your actual entity names and the same Category list.
- Your Financial Tracking AI project has the spreadsheet uploaded and the Permanent Configuration prompt filled in with your currency, entities, accounts and categories.
- This month and last month are both in the Transaction Log.
- No internal transfers, duplicates or wrong entities got through.
- Pending transactions are flagged in the Merchant field.
- Every foreign-currency amount multiplies out to its base-currency amount.
- Your income, expenses by entity, total expenses and profit or loss match reality.
- The actuals landed in the right month on the Planned vs. Actual tab.
- Bank Balance adds every account correctly.
- No formula column was typed over.

### Health Tracking

- Every field you already have is backfilled from the first day of the current month.
- Every number came from the app that already had it.
- Weight and Tonnage both use the same unit throughout.
- Any field your wearable does not provide is blank rather than guessed.
- The calculated fields were tested and the fake data deleted.
- No calculated field was typed over.

### Time Tracking

- Your entries start from today rather than a backfill.
- Wake Up Time is entered in h:mm AM/PM format.
- Yesterday's hour completion times, Get Up Immediately and any notes are entered.
- The calculated columns were tested with uneven days so you saw 4h Consistency flip.
- The fake data was cleared and no calculated column was typed over.

### War Map

- Every objective has a goal, a purpose and its actions written against it.
- Your Next 90 Days tasks are checked off.
- Every one of those tasks has a time, a person and a priority against it.
- Your first week is scheduled.
- Nothing is still blank.

### The 15-Minute Daily Setup

- All four tabs are bookmarked and pinned in your browser.
- You ran the whole setup against a 15-minute timer.
- All three sheets were filled in and the War Map reviewed.
- You chose the three to five assignments for the day.
- It finished inside 15 minutes, or you found the friction, removed it and tested again.

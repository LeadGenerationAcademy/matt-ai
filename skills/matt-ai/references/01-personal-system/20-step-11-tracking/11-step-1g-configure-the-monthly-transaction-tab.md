### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the tab for the current month.** Every month has its own tab and they all work the same way.
2. **Scroll down to the Transaction Log.** This is the only part of the tab you ever type into. Everything above it is built from what goes in here.
3. **Check the nine columns are the ones every transaction needs.** Date, Entity, Transaction Type, Category, Merchant, Original Amount, Currency, Conversion Rate, USD Amount. Entity, Transaction Type, Category and Currency are dropdowns, and USD Amount is a formula, Original Amount multiplied by Conversion Rate.
4. **Rename Entity 1 and Entity 2 in D16 and E16 on the [January tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=317533972#gid=317533972).** Use the real names, something like Smith Consulting LLC and Smith Holdings Ltd. You only do this once. Every other month tab reads those two cells, so February through December, every Entity dropdown, and the Income by Entity and Expenses by Entity breakdowns all update on their own. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
5. **Leave Personal as Personal.** Anything that is not one of your businesses gets tagged Personal, and that is what keeps your personal and business spending separated the whole way up to the Planned vs. Actual tab.
6. **Set your Category list and reuse it every month.** The Category dropdown already has a default list in it. Add anything that is missing and remove anything that does not apply to you, then leave it alone, because changing categories halfway through the year kills any comparison between months.
7. **Leave everything above the Transaction Log alone.** The daily table, Income by Entity, Expenses by Entity and the Monthly Summary all calculate themselves from the log.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the tab for the current month?
- Did you scroll down to the Transaction Log?
- Did you check the nine columns are the ones every transaction needs?
- Did you rename Entity 1 and Entity 2 in D16 and E16 on the January tab?
- Did you leave Personal as Personal?
- Did you set your Category list and commit to reusing it every month?
- Did you leave everything above the Transaction Log alone?

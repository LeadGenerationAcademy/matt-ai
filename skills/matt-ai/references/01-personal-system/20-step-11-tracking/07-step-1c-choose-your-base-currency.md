### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose the single currency you want the entire spreadsheet converted into.** Usually USD, but CAD, GBP, EUR, AED or whatever makes the most sense for you works the same way.
2. **Use that one currency for every transaction and bank balance.** Everything gets converted into it, which is the only way the numbers add together and compare properly.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose the single currency you want the entire spreadsheet converted into?
- Are you using that one currency for every transaction and bank balance?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Do not paste data over the calculated fields.** All ten of them build themselves off the daily inputs you entered in Step #2B. Paste over one and it stops being a formula, it becomes a number you typed in.
2. **Copy the header row into a new ChatGPT conversation if you do not have enough real data to test the spreadsheet.** The point is to find out whether the sheet actually works before you start trusting what it tells you.
3. **Ask it for five rows of realistic fake data in the same column order and format, with every calculated field left blank.** Same order matters, otherwise the rows will not line up and the test tells you nothing. Blank calculated fields are what let you see the sheet fill them in on its own.
4. **Paste the rows into the daily input section.** The daily input section only, nowhere near the columns you are testing.
5. **Confirm every formula and summary updates correctly.** Weekly and monthly summaries included, not just the daily rows.
6. **Delete the fake data.** All five rows. Leave one behind and every trend and summary from here on is built on a number you made up.

### Completion Audit

- Did you avoid pasting data over the calculated fields?
- If you did not have enough real data, did you copy the header row into a new ChatGPT conversation?
- Did you ask it for five rows of realistic fake data in the same column order and format?
- Did you paste the rows into the daily input section?
- Did every formula and summary update correctly?
- Did you delete the fake data?

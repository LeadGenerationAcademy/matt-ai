### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Export every transaction from the first day of the current month through today.** Nothing earlier than the 1st for now, you are building this month first.
2. **Do it for every bank account, credit card, payment processor and financial account.** Miss one account and the month is wrong, and nothing in the sheet will tell you that it is.
3. **Use CSV exports wherever you can get them.** If an institution does not provide one, copied transaction tables, PDF statements or clear screenshots all work.
4. **Send everything to your Financial Tracking AI project.** The same project you configured, so it already knows your currency, entities, accounts and categories.
5. **Identify the account each export came from.** The account-to-entity mapping is useless if it cannot tell which account it is looking at.
6. **Write "ALL TRANSACTIONS SENT" once every account has been added.** It holds everything until it sees that line, then returns one clean table in the format your spreadsheet needs.

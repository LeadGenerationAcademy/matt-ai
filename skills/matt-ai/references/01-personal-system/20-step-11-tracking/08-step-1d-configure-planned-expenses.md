### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the [Planned vs. Actual](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1wRsIC7Jz7hTFuoRECKSzZCqIVMAt-aFWGWt1FZaTeD4/edit?gid=1569452217#gid=1569452217) tab.** The month-by-month comparison sits at the top of it, and the budget you are about to build sits further down. [in this skill: assets/templates/financial-tracking-template/]
2. **Scroll down to the Personal (Planned) and Business (Planned) tables.** The Planned columns in the table at the top pull straight from these, for every month of the year.
3. **Replace the example personal categories and amounts with your own.** Rent, food, transportation, insurance, subscriptions, entertainment and anything else that is actually yours. The template ships with examples in there, so type over them.
4. **Replace the example business categories and amounts with your own.** Payroll, contractors, software, advertising, legal and accounting, office, travel, taxes, and whatever else your business actually pays for.
5. **Add every meaningful recurring or expected expense with the amount you expect to spend.** Leaving things out makes the Difference columns useless later, because you will be comparing real spending against half a budget.
6. **Use the second set of Personal (Planned) and Business (Planned) tables if you run out of room.** Both sets add into the same totals, so it does not matter which one a line sits in.
7. **Do not type into the Actual, Difference, Total, Income or PNL columns.** Those contain formulas and pull their results from the monthly transactions.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open the Planned vs. Actual tab?
- Did you scroll down to the Personal (Planned) and Business (Planned) tables?
- Did you replace the example personal categories and amounts with your own?
- Did you replace the example business categories and amounts with your own?
- Did you add every meaningful recurring or expected expense with the amount you expect to spend?
- Did you use the second set of tables if you ran out of room?
- Did you avoid typing into the Actual, Difference, Total, Income or PNL columns?

# Action Steps

Course: Step #6: Environment Design
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/49a1b368?md=3aeb1d415edb4bc29be019a70e2e3803

---
The Theory explains that your environment does not force you to do anything. It changes how easy each behavior is, and that bit of friction between the impulse and the action is usually enough.

In this course you will engineer your Work Room so that doing the work becomes the most obvious thing available.

You will get the room dark and cool, turn your desk to face a blank wall, and build that wall into a record of your goals and the work you have already finished.

You still have to do the work. The point of all of this is that your environment is helping you instead of fighting you.

### Table of Contents

1. **Make The Room Dark:** Block the outside light so your monitor is the brightest thing in the room.
2. **Keep The Room Cool:** Hold the space between 21°C and 23°C, or 70°F and 73°F.
3. **Face Your Desk Toward A Wall:** Point the desk at a blank wall and leave two to six feet of it clear behind your monitor.
4. **Use One Monitor:** One screen, or one large 27-inch to 32-inch monitor if you need the space.
5. **Put The Work Timer On Your Desk:** The 60-minute timer directly underneath your monitor, left set to 60.
6. **Put The Distraction Counter Beside It:** The counter next to the timer, pressed the moment a distraction happens.
7. **Put The Vision Board Behind Your Monitor:** Directly above the screen, visible whenever your eyes move slightly up from it.
8. **Create A Sticky-Note Wall:** A large section of wall for one note after every completed hour.
9. **Hang The Physical Calendar:** Beside the sticky notes, with a large X for every day that actually met the standard.
10. **Keep A Pen And Legal Pad Beside You:** Beside your keyboard, used to write what you intend to complete before every work hour.

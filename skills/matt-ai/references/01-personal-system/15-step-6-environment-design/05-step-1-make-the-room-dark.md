### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Install [blackout curtains](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=blackout+curtains&crid=2ALGNNBDF2RWV&sprefix=blackout+curta%2Caps%2C216&ref=nb_sb_noss_2), blinds, or a temporary covering.** Blocking the outside light means there is less to distract you while you work.
2. **Confirm your monitor is the main source of light in the room.** It does not need to be pitch black. There shouldn't be a light source brighter than your monitor.
3. **Reduce the visual noise around you.** Anything lit up or moving in the corner of your eye is something your eyes go to instead of the screen.

### Completion Audit

- Did you install blackout curtains, blinds, or a temporary covering?
- Is your monitor the main source of light in the room?
- Did you reduce the visual noise around you?

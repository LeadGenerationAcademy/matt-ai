### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Use one monitor.** Two screens mean you are constantly splitting your attention between them.
2. **Get one large 27-inch to 32-inch monitor if you need more space.** That size can function like two screens without actually being two screens.

### Completion Audit

- Are you using one monitor?
- If you needed more space, did you get one large 27-inch to 32-inch monitor?

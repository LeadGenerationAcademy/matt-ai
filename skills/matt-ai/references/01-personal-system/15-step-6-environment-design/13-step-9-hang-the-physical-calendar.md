### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Hang your [calendar](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physical+wall+calendar) beside the sticky-note wall where you can always see it.** Your completed hours and your completed days then sit in the same place, so one glance tells you how the week has actually gone.
2. **Put a large X through the day after completing four work hours and submitting all four Work Forms.** The X is for a day that actually met the standard, which is what makes the chain worth looking at.

### Completion Audit

- Did you hang your calendar beside the sticky-note wall where you can always see it?
- Are you putting a large X through the day after completing four work hours and submitting all four Work Forms?

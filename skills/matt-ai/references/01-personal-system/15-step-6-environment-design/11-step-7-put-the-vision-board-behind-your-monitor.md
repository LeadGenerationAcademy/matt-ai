### Prerequisites

- [Step #3: Face Your Desk Toward A Wall](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/49a1b368?md=a9c579c34aa6465086109e2c2584d9af) (Current Course)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Place your [vision board](https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/554c839c?md=bb9a12fad3934bb2bc03dc0fa40b6d98) directly above your monitor.** This is the wall space you left clear earlier when you turned the desk to face the wall.
2. **Confirm you can see it whenever your eyes move slightly above the screen.** You should not have to open a document or turn around to look at it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you place your vision board directly above your monitor?
- Can you see it whenever your eyes move slightly above the screen?

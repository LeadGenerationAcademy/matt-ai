# Completion Checklist

Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### The Room

- Your windows are covered with blackout curtains, blinds or a temporary covering.
- Your monitor is the brightest thing in the room.
- Nothing else in the room is lit up or moving in the corner of your eye.
- Your workspace sits between 21°C and 23°C, or 70°F and 73°F.

### Your Desk

- Your desk faces a blank wall.
- There is one monitor on it.
- The 60-minute timer sits directly underneath your monitor, set to 60.
- The distraction counter sits directly beside the timer.
- A pen and small legal pad sit beside your keyboard.

### The Wall

- Your vision board is directly above your monitor and visible without moving from your chair.
- A large section of wall is reserved for completed work-hour sticky notes.
- Your physical calendar is hung beside it where you can always see it.

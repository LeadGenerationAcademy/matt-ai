### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep the [distraction counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter) directly beside the timer.** Both should sit under your monitor, within reach without moving from your chair.
2. **Record every distraction immediately.** The moment it happens, not at the end of the hour. Counting them up afterwards is how the number ends up lower than the truth.

### Completion Audit

- Did you keep the distraction counter directly beside the timer?
- Are you recording every distraction immediately?

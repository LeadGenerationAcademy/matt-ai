### Buy

- [Digital kitchen timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer)

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Place the [60-minute kitchen timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer) directly underneath your monitor.** This is the first of the two timers you bought. It sits in your eyeline, not in a drawer.
2. **Leave it set to 60 minutes.** Starting a work hour then takes almost no effort. You press one button rather than dialing in the time every single hour.

### Completion Audit

- Did you place the 60-minute kitchen timer directly underneath your monitor?
- Did you leave it set to 60 minutes?

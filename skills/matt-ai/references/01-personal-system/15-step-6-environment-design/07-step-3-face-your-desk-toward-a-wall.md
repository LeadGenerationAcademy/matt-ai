### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Face your desk toward a blank wall instead of a window or the rest of the room.** A window or an open room gives you outside light and movement to look at every time your eyes come off the screen.
2. **Leave roughly two to six feet behind your monitor for the vision board, [sticky notes](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes), and [calendar](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physical+wall+calendar).** Leave the wall empty for now. Those three go up in later steps and need to stay visible while you are working.

### Completion Audit

- Did you face your desk toward a blank wall instead of a window or the rest of the room?
- Did you leave roughly two to six feet behind your monitor for the vision board, sticky notes, and calendar?

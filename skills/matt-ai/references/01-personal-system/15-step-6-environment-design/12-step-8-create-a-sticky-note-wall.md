### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Reserve a large section of the wall for completed work-hour [sticky notes](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes).** Give it more room than you think it needs, because this fills up faster than you expect and you do not want to end up starting a second patch somewhere else.
2. **Add one note after every completed hour.** Four completed hours means four more pieces of physical proof every successful day.

### Completion Audit

- Did you reserve a large section of the wall for completed work-hour sticky notes?
- Are you adding one note after every completed hour?

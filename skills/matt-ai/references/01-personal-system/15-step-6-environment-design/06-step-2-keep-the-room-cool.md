### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Set your workspace between 21°C and 23°C, or 70°F and 73°F.** This is the range that keeps you comfortable and alert through the whole work hour.
2. **Use air conditioning, a heater, or a fan to hold that temperature.** Use whatever your room actually needs to get into that range.
3. **Control the area around your desk if you cannot control the whole room.** The temperature you are sitting in is the only one that matters.

### Completion Audit

- Is your workspace between 21°C and 23°C, or 70°F and 73°F?
- Did you use air conditioning, a heater, or a fan to hold that temperature?
- If you cannot control the whole room, did you control the area around your desk?

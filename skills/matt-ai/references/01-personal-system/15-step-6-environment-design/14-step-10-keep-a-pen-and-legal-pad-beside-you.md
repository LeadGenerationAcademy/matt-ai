### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Keep a [pen](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ballpoint+pens) and [small legal pad](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=small+yellow+legal+notepads) beside your keyboard.** Everything else on the desk is there for the work. This is there so you never have to leave the chair to write something down.
2. **Write exactly what you intend to complete before every work hour.** Do it before you start the timer, not after. Otherwise you spend the beginning of the hour deciding what to work on instead of working.

### Completion Audit

- Are you keeping a pen and small legal pad beside your keyboard?
- Are you writing exactly what you intend to complete before every work hour?

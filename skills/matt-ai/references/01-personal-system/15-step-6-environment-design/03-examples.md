### Work Room

- Blackout curtains, blinds, or a temporary covering on the window
- Room kept between 21 and 23 degrees Celsius (70 and 73°F)
- Desk facing a blank wall
- Two to six feet of wall left free behind the monitor

### Work Desk

- One monitor
- 60-minute kitchen timer directly underneath the monitor, set to 60 minutes
- Distraction counter directly beside the timer
- Pen and small legal pad beside the keyboard

![image](https://assets.skool.com/f/1d97a8218f0840a8a65c7970ccdf40ce/6dfc4f4475d945d389f1b6ce7e9d0de0b0ecf692469e484ea4211c4d18be6e4d.png)

### Work Room Wall

- Physical calendar hung where you can always see it, with an X through each completed day
- Large section beside the calendar reserved for completed work-hour sticky notes
- Vision board directly above the monitor

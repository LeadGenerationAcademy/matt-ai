### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy several [water bottles](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=water+bottles) or one large reusable bottle.** You need water at your desk and again during the morning routine. Buy for both.
2. **Have water beside your desk before work begins.** Before work begins, not after the timer has already started.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy several water bottles or one large reusable bottle?
- Is water beside your desk before work begins?

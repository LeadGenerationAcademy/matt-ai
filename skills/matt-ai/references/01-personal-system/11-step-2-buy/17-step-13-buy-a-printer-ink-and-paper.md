### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a [color printer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=color+printer), [ink](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=printer+ink) and [paper](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=printer+paper).** You will need to print several things in color. Make sure the printer handles color and the ink you buy is color, not black and white.
2. **Or find a print shop near you instead.** A print shop may be cheaper. I personally love having a printer because I can print something immediately.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a color printer, ink and paper?
- Or did you find a print shop near you instead?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy [thick red markers](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=thick+red+markers).** These are for crossing completed days off the calendar you just hung.
2. **Cross off each completed day on the calendar.** The marks should be large enough that you can see the chain clearly while sitting at your desk.

**Completion Audit**

- Did you buy thick red markers?
- Are you crossing off each completed day on the calendar?

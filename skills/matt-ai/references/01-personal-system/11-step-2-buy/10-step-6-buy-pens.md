### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a reliable supply of [pens](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ballpoint+pens).** A supply rather than one pen. They need to be in every place you work.
2. **Keep them anywhere you regularly work.** Writing something down should never require standing up and searching your entire house for a pen.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a reliable supply of pens?
- Are they kept anywhere you regularly work?

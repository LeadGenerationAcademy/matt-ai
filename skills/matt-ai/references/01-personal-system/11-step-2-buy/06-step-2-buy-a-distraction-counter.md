### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a small [handheld tally counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter).** Right now, you have an opinion about how focused you are. I want the number. The number will usually be much less flattering, which is exactly why we need it.
2. **Press it once every time you become distracted during a work hour.** One press per distraction, no rounding down at the end of the hour.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a small handheld tally counter?
- Are you pressing it once every time you become distracted during a work hour?

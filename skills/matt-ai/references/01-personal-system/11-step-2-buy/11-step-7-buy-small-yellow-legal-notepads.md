### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy [small yellow legal notepads](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=small+yellow+legal+notepads).** Approximately A5 size, small enough to sit beside your keyboard.
2. **Keep one beside your keyboard.** Beside the keyboard, not in a drawer. You need it in reach without getting up.
3. **Use it to plan each work hour and capture thoughts.** This is what stops you opening another app and getting distracted.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy small yellow legal notepads?
- Is one kept beside your keyboard?
- Are you using it to plan each work hour and capture thoughts?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a [physical wall calendar](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physical+wall+calendar).** I prefer one that shows the entire year, but some of those are enormous. A normal monthly calendar works.
2. **Hang it where you work.** The point is to make your consistency visible.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a physical wall calendar?
- Did you hang it where you work?

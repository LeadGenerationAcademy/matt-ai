### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a [fitness and sleep-tracking wearable](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=fitness+wearable+sleep+tracker).** An Apple Watch, Oura Ring, Garmin, Fitbit, or Whoop all work. This says optional, but I do not think you should skip it.
2. **Use it to track your sleep.** It can track steps, calories, and other health information, but its most important use in this course is sleep tracking.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a fitness and sleep-tracking wearable?
- Are you using it to track your sleep?

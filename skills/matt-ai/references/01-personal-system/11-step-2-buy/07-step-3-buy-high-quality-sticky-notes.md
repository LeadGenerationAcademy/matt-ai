### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a large supply of [high-quality sticky notes](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes).** Choose notes with strong adhesive. They need to remain there while the wall becomes a physical record of your accumulated work.
2. **Write one sticky note for every completed work hour and put it on the wall.** This is why you are buying a large supply instead of a single pad.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a large supply of high-quality sticky notes?
- Are you writing one sticky note for every completed work hour and putting it on the wall?

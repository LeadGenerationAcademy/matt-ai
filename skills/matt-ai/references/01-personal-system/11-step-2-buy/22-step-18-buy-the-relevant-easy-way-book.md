### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Identify the addiction doing the most damage to your life.** The Easy Way is a series about different addictions, including smoking, drinking, eating, sugar, and social media. If more than one applies, choose the one doing the most damage right now.
2. **Buy the [Easy Way version written for that addiction](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Allen+Carr+Easyway+books).** You will read it during the 30 days.

### Completion Audit

- Did you identify the addiction doing the most damage to your life?
- Did you buy the version written for that addiction?

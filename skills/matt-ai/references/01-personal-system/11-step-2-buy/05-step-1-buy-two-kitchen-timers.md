### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy [two basic kitchen timers](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer).** The cheap version does the job. All they have to do is count down and make a noise.
2. **Set one timer to 60 minutes for your work hours.** This one stays on 60 and does not get changed.
3. **Set the other timer to 15 minutes for the breaks between them.** This one stays on 15, which is why you are buying two instead of resetting one back and forth.
4. **Do not use your phone as a timer.** Picking up your phone during deep work is like inviting a crackhead into your office and trusting him to sit quietly. Your phone has beaten you before. Buy the timers.

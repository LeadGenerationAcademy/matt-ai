### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a [Brick](https://getbrick.com/products/grey-brick) or another physical phone-blocking device.** It blocks the apps and websites you choose. Removing the block requires physically touching your phone to the device.
2. **Decide where you are going to keep it.** I leave mine in my car so unblocking my phone requires walking outside to get it.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a Brick or another physical phone-blocking device?
- Did you decide where you are going to keep it?

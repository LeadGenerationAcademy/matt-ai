### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a [large garbage can](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=large+garbage+can).** Bigger is better here. If the room can take a 50 gallon can, get that.
2. **Buy matching [garbage bags](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=50+gallon+garbage+bags).** They need to fit the can you just bought. A 50 gallon can takes 50 gallon bags.
3. **Keep it beside your desk.** There is no reason for your workspace to become a mess because the garbage can is in another room.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a large garbage can?
- Did you buy matching garbage bags?
- Is it kept beside your desk?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Buy a [treadmill](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=home+treadmill) or [walking-pad treadmill](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=incline+walking+pad+treadmill).** I highly recommend this. You can control the speed and incline, use it regardless of the weather, and do other things while walking. It is one of the few forms of cardio that is realistic to do every day.
2. **Or use one at your gym or apartment building.** Do this if you lack the money or space for your own. It needs to be somewhere you can get to every day.

### Completion Audit

- Did you buy a treadmill or walking-pad treadmill?
- Or are you using one at your gym or apartment building?

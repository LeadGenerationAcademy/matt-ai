# Action Steps

Course: Step #7: Work System
System: Personal System
Skool link: https://www.skool.com/leadgenerationacademy/classroom/5c9acee6?md=1f63c5a012454ffca0478efcd5b86589

---
The Theory explains that four hours of Deep Work every day is what the whole thing runs on, and that you need a structure, a record and other people who can see whether you actually did it.

In this course you will build the system that runs those four hours and records every one of them.

You will choose the project the hours go into, build the Work Form and the spreadsheet behind it, bring in one or two people running the same system, then set your breaks and your rewards. The last step is one real hour that tests all of it at once.

Without the record, it is easy to finish an hour and decide you worked pretty well. With it you can see what you actually produced and the distractions in each hour worked.

### Table of Contents

1. **Complete Four Hours Of Deep Work:** Four separate timed hours on the same project, done as early as you can with a break between each one.
2. **Choose Your 10X Project:** One project you create yourself, worth more than 100 hours, held to a standard 10 times higher than your previous best.
3. **Create Your Work Form:** Copy the Work Form Template and rename it to your own.
4. **Connect The Work Form To A Spreadsheet:** Link it to Sheets so every submission permanently records the completed hour.
5. **Create An Accountability Group:** One or two people running the same form into the same spreadsheet, each with their own tab.
6. **Create Accountability Notifications:** A Zapier automation that emails and texts the group every time someone completes an hour.
7. **Create The Leaderboard:** A tab that totals everyone's completed hours for today, this week, this month, this quarter, this year and all time.
8. **Test: Form, Notifications & Leaderboard:** Submit a real test and confirm the spreadsheet row, both notifications and every leaderboard column.
9. **Create Percentage-Left-In-Year Messages:** A daily message showing how much of the year is gone, arriving before you wake up.
10. **Set Up The Between-Hour Timer:** The second timer set to 15 minutes, kept wherever you go during breaks.
11. **Plan The Between-Hour Routine:** Decide in advance what you do during the break and get back before the timer ends.
12. **Choose The Hourly Reward:** One small thing you genuinely enjoy that fits inside the 15-minute break.
13. **Choose The Four-Hour Reward:** A larger one you only receive after all four hours are done.
14. **Test The Entire Work System:** One real hour start to finish, from the legal pad through to returning from your break.

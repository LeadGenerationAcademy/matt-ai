### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Write the planned output on your [legal pad](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=small+yellow+legal+notepads).** This is the same thing that goes into the Planned Output field on your Work Form later. It gets written before anything else starts.
2. **Reset the [distraction counter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=handheld+tally+counter).** Back to zero. Last hour's number is not this hour's number.
3. **Start the [timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physical+wall+calendar).** The 60-minute timer sitting under your monitor, already set to 60 from the Environment Design course.
4. **Complete one real 60-minute hour on your 10X Project.** A deep work hour, not a practice run. This one counts toward your four.
5. **Count every distraction.** Press the counter the moment it happens, not from memory at the end of the hour.
6. **Record the actual output.** This is what goes in the Actual Output field. Write down what you actually produced, not what you meant to.
7. **Submit the Work Form.** Use the responder link you copied earlier, not the editor view.
8. **Confirm the spreadsheet updates.** A new row should appear in your own tab with everything you just entered.
9. **Confirm the notifications send.** Ask your accountability partners whether they got both the email and the text.
10. **Confirm the leaderboard updates.** Your Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, This Year and All Time should each have gone up by one.
11. **Add the completed-hour [sticky note](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=super+sticky+notes) to the wall.** One note per completed hour, on the section of wall you reserved for them.
12. **Take the hourly reward.** The small one you chose, and only now that the hour is actually finished.
13. **Start the 15-minute break timer.** The second timer, kept wherever you go during breaks rather than on your desk.
14. **Return to your desk before the timer ends.** Before the 15 minutes are up you should already be sitting at your desk, ready to work.

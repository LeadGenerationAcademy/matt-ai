### Complete Four Work Of Deep Work

**Wake up: 6:00 a.m.**

- 6:00 to 6:30 a.m.: Morning Routine (30 minutes, covered in the Daily Operating System course)
- 6:30 to 6:45 a.m.: 15-Minute Daily Setup (15 minutes, covered in the Daily Operating System course)
- 6:45 to 7:45 a.m.: Hour One (60 minutes)
- 7:45 to 8:00 a.m.: Break (15 minutes)
- 8:00 to 9:00 a.m.: Hour Two (60 minutes)
- 9:00 to 9:15 a.m.: Break (15 minutes)
- 9:15 to 10:15 a.m.: Hour Three (60 minutes)
- 10:15 to 10:30 a.m.: Break (15 minutes)
- 10:30 to 11:30 a.m.: Hour Four (60 minutes)

**Finished at 11:30 a.m.**, 5 hours, 30 minutes after waking up, with all four hours on the same project.

### Between-Hour Routine

**Between-Hour Routine Example #1:**

1. Stretch for a few minutes
2. Drink 250 to 500 mL of water
3. Clean up your desk or work area
4. Listen to music or something motivational

**Between-Hour Routine Example #2:**

1. Go to the bathroom
2. Walk outside with your dog
3. Fill up your water bottle
4. Review your Self-Mastery, Meaning and Thought Habits Lists (learn more in Daily Operating System Course)

**Between-Hour Routine Example #3:**

1. Drink 250 to 500 mL of water
2. Meditate for a few minutes
3. Read the Bible
4. Pray

### Hourly Reward

- Listen to your favorite song
- Watch or listen to something motivational
- Grab a drink
- Eat a snack
- Screw around for a few minutes
- Play on your phone briefly
- Watch a short video
- Check scores or whatever you actually care about
- Go outside
- Lie down for a few minutes

### Four-Hour Reward

- Go to a coffee shop, get a coffee, and chill while watching YouTube
- Go out and get lunch somewhere instead of eating at your desk
- Spend time outside with no phone
- Take time with your wife, kids, or a friend
- Sit in the sauna or take a long shower with nothing scheduled after it
- Watch an episode of a TV show
- Play video games
- Take a nap
- Get a massage
- Go for a swim

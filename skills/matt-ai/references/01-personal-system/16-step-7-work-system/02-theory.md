The Work System is how we are going to get you completing four hours of Deep Work on your most important stuff every day.

We will give those hours a consistent structure, record what you get done, and add accountability and rewards to help you keep doing it.

### How It Works

You decide what you need to work on and what you intend to complete during the next hour. Then you start a 60-minute timer and work on that assignment until the timer ends.

During the hour, you count every distraction.

When you finish, you submit a Work Form recording what you completed and how the hour went. You then take a 10- to 15-minute break before beginning the next hour.

Repeat this until you have completed four hours. We want those hours done as early and as quickly as possible, before meetings, messages, and other demands fill the day.

Each hour also gives you practice focusing. At first, staying with an assignment for the full 60 minutes might be difficult. Repeatedly doing it gives you an opportunity to improve that ability.

### The Work Form

The Work Form is a Google Form you submit immediately after each completed hour.

It records your name, date, start time, hour number, completion time, planned output, actual output, and distraction count.

This makes you compare what you intended to do with what actually happened. You can see whether you completed the assignment and how often you lost focus along the way.

Without that record, it is easy to finish an hour and decide you worked “pretty well.” If you counted 12 distractions, you have something specific to improve during the next hour.

Every submission automatically creates a new row in a connected Google Sheet. Four completed hours means four submissions.

Over time, the spreadsheet shows how consistently you worked, what you completed, and whether your focus improved. You can look back at the actual record instead of trying to remember how productive you were.

It also gives you evidence that you follow through. As you accumulate completed hours, you have a record of yourself doing difficult work consistently. That supports the self-image we are trying to develop.

### Accountability And The Leaderboard

You will use the system with one or two other people who are also completing and reporting their own hours.

Whenever someone submits a Work Form, a Zapier automation sends the group a notification showing who completed the hour and what they worked on. This can arrive through email and text, or the messaging app everyone checks.

Other people can now see whether you are doing your hours. You also see when they complete theirs.

If you are screwing around and receive a notification that someone else has finished another hour, that can be enough to get you back to work. It is harder to quietly stop following the system when other people are still using it in front of you.

The Leaderboard adds competition. It automatically totals everyone’s completed hours for today, this week, this month, this quarter, this year, and all time.

You can see who is ahead and how much work you need to do to catch them. The accountability, competition, and bragging rights give you more reasons to complete your hours.

### Breaks And Rewards

You will choose a routine for your 10- to 15-minute breaks and use a separate timer to tell you when it is time to return.

Having the routine decided means you know what happens after each hour. The break has an ending, and you know when the next work period begins.

You will also choose an hourly reward and a reward for completing all four hours. These should be things you personally look forward to.

The hourly reward gives you something enjoyable after each completed work period. The four-hour reward gives you an additional reason to finish the full daily target.

Your work may take time to produce the result you ultimately want. The rewards, completed-hour record, and Leaderboard give you something satisfying along the way.

### Urgency

We will set up a daily message showing the percentage of the year remaining, delivered around your normal wake-up time.

This is a reminder to act with some urgency and get started on the work you have decided matters.

During the Setup, we will test the form, spreadsheet, notifications, and Leaderboard, then run through the entire routine. Once everything works, each completed hour gets recorded and shared automatically. You can take your break and return for the next hour.

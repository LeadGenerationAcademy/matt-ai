### Examples

**Example Routine 1:**

1. Stretch for a few minutes
2. Drink 250 to 500 mL of water
3. Clean up your desk or work area
4. Listen to music or something motivational

**Example Routine 2:**

1. Go to the bathroom
2. Walk outside with your dog
3. Fill up your water bottle
4. Review your Self-Mastery, Meaning and Thought Habits Lists (learn more in Daily Operating System Course)

**Example Routine 3:**

1. Drink 250 to 500 mL of water
2. Meditate for a few minutes
3. Read the Bible
4. Pray

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Decide in advance what you can do during the break.** You could stretch, drink water, walk, listen to a song, watch the motivational video, or check something permitted by your Information Diet.
2. **Return to your desk before the timer ends.** Whatever you choose has to be something you can finish inside 15 minutes and be able to walk away from once the timer goes off.

### Completion Audit

- Did you decide in advance what you can do during the break?
- Are you returning to your desk before the timer ends?

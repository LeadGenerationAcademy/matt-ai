### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Make a copy of the [Accountability Work Form Spreadsheet Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ZxEcvABm7l0Bz4Jxs9l-VqMGMHZPLDdhgW-l1sf-NyA/edit?usp=sharing).** This is the spreadsheet your Work Form connects to and the one your Accountability Partners will join later. [in this skill: assets/templates/accountability-work-form-spreadsheet-template/]
2. **Rename it to Accountability Work Form.** Call it whatever you want, but you will be sharing this exact spreadsheet with your Accountability Group later.
3. **Open File then Settings and set the spreadsheet time zone to your local time.** Otherwise "today" rolls over at the wrong hour and the Leaderboard's Today column goes blank while you are still working.
4. **Open the Responses tab on your Work Form.** It is along the top of the form, beside Questions and Settings.
5. **Click the Link to Sheets icon.** It is the green Sheets icon on the right side of the Responses tab.
6. **Select "Select existing spreadsheet" and click Select.** Do not leave "Create a new spreadsheet" selected. That builds an empty spreadsheet with no Leaderboard in it.
7. **Choose the copy you just made and press Insert.** The Insert button only becomes available once the spreadsheet is actually selected.
8. **Rename the new tab to [Your Name]'s Work Form.** Right click the tab at the bottom of the spreadsheet and rename it. Mine would be Matt's Work Form.
9. **Confirm it is connected.** A Form_Responses tag sits above row 1, and row 1 has a column for every question on your form. Every submission now automatically creates a new row and permanently records the completed hour.

# Completion Checklist

Course: Step #7: Work System
System: Personal System

---
Do not mark this module complete until every box is checked.

### Your 10X Project

- You have chosen one project that could substantially transform your business.
- It is something you create rather than manage or pay somebody else to make.
- It is worth more than 100 hours of your life.
- It is held to a standard 10X higher than your previous best.

### The Work Form

- You have your own copy of the Work Form, renamed to [Your Name]'s Work Form in both the form title and the file name.
- It is connected to a spreadsheet called Accountability Work Form.
- Your tab in that spreadsheet is named [Your Name]'s Work Form.
- Every submission creates a new row in it.

### Accountability

- One or two other people are running the same Work Form System.
- Each of them has their own copy of the form and their own tab in the same spreadsheet.
- Every person's tab name matches their name in column A exactly, apostrophe included.
- A Zap sends every completed hour to the group by email and text.
- Your notification Zap sends from a specific number rather than Random.
- Your spreadsheet time zone is set to your local time.
- Your Leaderboard tab totals everyone's hours for today, this week, this month, this quarter, this year and all time.
- Nothing has been typed into Leaderboard columns B to G.

### Percentage Left In The Year

- A daily message showing the percentage of the year remaining arrives 15 to 30 minutes before you wake up.
- It arrives by email and by text.
- It fires on weekends too.
- It sends from a specific number rather than Random.

### Breaks & Rewards

- Your second kitchen timer is set to 15 minutes and kept wherever you go during breaks.
- You have decided in advance what you do during the break.
- You have an hourly reward that is small, repeatable and fits inside the 15 minutes.
- You have a larger four-hour reward that you actually want.

### Everything Tested

- You are submitting through the responder link rather than the editor link.
- A real submission created a row in your own tab.
- Every Leaderboard column went up by one.
- Your accountability partners received both the email and the text.
- The test row has been deleted.
- Every accountability partner has run the same test on their own form.
- You have completed one real hour start to finish, through the sticky note, the reward and the break timer.

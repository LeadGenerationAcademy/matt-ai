### Framework & Criteria

1. **Date:** The day the work hour happened.
2. **Start Time:** When you started the timer, not when you sat down.
3. **Hour Number:** Which of the four hours you just finished.
4. **Completion Time:** When the hour actually ended, not when you got around to submitting the form.
5. **Planned Output:** What you plan to complete during the hour. This is what forces you to plan the hour instead of sitting down and deciding as you go.
6. **Actual Output:** What you actually produced. Comparing this against the planned output is the point of having both.
7. **Distraction Count:** The number off your distraction counter. Every distraction gets counted, not the ones you remember at the end.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Click the [Work Form Template](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1i7ZgFfPYRK6CgJ7BtblZNm2lQR2_QoEnl853HOL15co/copy) link.** It takes you straight to the Copy document screen. There is nothing to build from scratch here.
2. **Click "Make a copy."** You now have your own Work Form, which is what you submit after every completed work hour.
3. **Rename it to [Your Name]'s Work Form.** Mine would be Matt's Work Form. Change both the form title and the file name in the top left corner.
4. **Delete the example answers under each question if you want.** They are there so you know what format each answer should be in. Once you have submitted a few hours you will not need them.

### Completion Audit

- Did you click the Work Form Template link?
- Did you click "Make a copy"?
- Did you rename it to [Your Name]'s Work Form in both places?
- Did you delete the example answers if you wanted to?

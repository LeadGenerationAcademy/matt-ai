### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your Work Form.** Do not assume any of it works because you finished setting it up.
2. **Click Publish in the top right if you have not published it yet.** Nothing can be submitted until the form is published, and an unpublished form tells you "No responses. Publish your form to start accepting responses."
3. **Click Copy responder link.** This sits at the bottom left of the Published options box.
4. **Press Copy.** This is the link you will actually use every day. The editor link you have been working in is not the one you submit through.
5. **Paste the link into your browser and fill in every field.** Use today's date and a real hour number. The Leaderboard only counts a submission if it has an Hour Number on it.
6. **Press Submit.** This is the moment everything downstream fires at once, the spreadsheet row, both notifications, and the Leaderboard.
7. **Open your spreadsheet and go to your own tab.** A new row should be sitting there with everything you just entered. If nothing appears, the form is writing into the wrong tab.
8. **Go to the Leaderboard tab.** Your Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, This Year and All Time should each have gone up by one. If Today stays at zero while the rest move, check the date you submitted and the spreadsheet time zone.
9. **Ask your accountability partners if they got the email.** They are the ones receiving it, so you cannot confirm this from your own inbox.
10. **Ask your accountability partners if they got the text.** This is also where you find out whether your SMS setup works in their country.
11. **Delete the test row from your Work Form tab.** The Leaderboard counts every row with an Hour Number in it, so a test submission left behind shows up as an hour you actually worked.
12. **Have each accountability partner run through steps 1 to 11 on their own form.** Yours working does not mean theirs does. Every person has their own form, their own tab and their own notifications, and all of it has to be tested separately.

### Completion Audit

- Did you open your Work Form?
- Did you click Publish if you had not published it yet?
- Did you click Copy responder link?
- Did you press Copy?
- Did you paste the link into your browser and fill in every field?
- Did you press Submit?
- Did a new row appear in your own tab?
- Did every column on the Leaderboard go up by one?
- Did your accountability partners get the email?
- Did your accountability partners get the text?
- Did you delete the test row from your Work Form tab?
- Did each accountability partner run through steps 1 to 11 on their own form?

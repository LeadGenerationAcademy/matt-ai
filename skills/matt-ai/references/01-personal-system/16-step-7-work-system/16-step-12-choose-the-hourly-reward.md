### Examples

- Listen to your favorite song
- Watch or listen to something motivational
- Grab a drink
- Eat a snack
- Screw around for a few minutes
- Play on your phone briefly
- Watch a short video
- Check scores or whatever you actually care about
- Go outside
- Lie down for a few minutes

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose one small reward for every completed work hour.** It could be a sparkling water, favorite drink, short video, or something else you genuinely enjoy.
2. **Keep it small, repeatable, and inside the 15-minute break.** You are taking this four times a day, every day. Anything expensive or slow stops working within a week.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose one small reward for every completed work hour?
- Is it small, repeatable, and inside the 15-minute break?

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Set the second [kitchen timer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=digital+kitchen+timer) to 15 minutes.** This is the other timer you bought. The 60-minute one stays under your monitor, this one does not.
2. **Keep it wherever you normally go during breaks.** A timer sitting on your desk does nothing once you have walked away from the desk.
3. **Start it immediately when each work hour ends.** Immediately, not once you have settled somewhere with a drink. The entire break must fit inside those 15 minutes.

### Completion Audit

- Did you set the second kitchen timer to 15 minutes?
- Is it kept wherever you normally go during breaks?
- Are you starting it immediately when each work hour ends?

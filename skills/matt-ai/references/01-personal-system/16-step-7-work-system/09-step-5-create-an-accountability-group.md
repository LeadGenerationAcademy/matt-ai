### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Find one or two other people who will use the same Work Form System.** Everyone must complete and report their own work hours instead of simply watching everyone else. Nobody sits in this group as a spectator.
2. **Invite them to your Accountability Work Form spreadsheet with editor access.** This is the copy you made in Step #4.
3. **Open your Work Form in the Google Forms editor.** The editor view, not the Responses tab.
4. **Copy the URL and replace "edit" at the end with "copy".** That turns your form into a copy link, the same way the template link on Step #3 worked.
5. **Send that link to your accountability partners.** They click it, press Make a copy, and get their own version of the exact same form.
6. **Have each of them rename their copy to [Their Name]'s Work Form.** Their copy arrives with your name on it.
7. **Have each of them open the Responses tab and click Link to Sheets.** Same two clicks you did on your own form.
8. **Have them select "Select existing spreadsheet" and click Select.** They are going to be joining your spreadsheet rather than making a new one.
9. **Have them click "Shared with me" in the pop up.** Your spreadsheet was shared with them rather than created by them, so it will not show up under My Drive.
10. **Have them click the spreadsheet you shared with them and press Insert.** The Insert button only becomes available once the spreadsheet is actually selected.
11. **Have each of them rename their tab to [Their Name]'s Work Form.** Right click the tab at the bottom of the spreadsheet and rename it.
12. **Confirm everyone has their own tab along the bottom of the spreadsheet.** One tab per person, named after them, each carrying the Google Forms icon beside it. Every person submitting a work form now automatically creates a row in their tab in the spreadsheet.

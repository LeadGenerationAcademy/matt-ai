### Framework & Criteria

Anything in brackets is a field you insert from your Google Form. Everything else you type out yourself as plain text.

##### Email

**Subject**

[Full Name] Completed Hour [Hour Number]

**Body**

Hour [Hour Number] is complete

[Full Name] completed a focused work hour.

Date: [Date]
Start Time: [Start Time]
Hour: [Hour Number]
Completion Time: [Completion Time]
Planned Output: [Planned Output]
Actual Output: [Actual Output]

##### SMS Message

[Full Name] Completed Hour [Hour Number]

Work Completed: [Actual Output]

Date: [Date]

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Submit one fake response on your Work Form.** Fill in every field with something realistic, a real hour number, a real date, a real sentence for planned and actual output. Zapier shows you this response when you are picking fields later, and you need to recognize your own answers to know which field is which.
2. **Create a free [Zapier](https://zapier.com) account.** The free plan is enough for this.
3. **Create a new Zap.** This is the automation that connects your Work Form to your Accountability Partners.
4. **Set the trigger app to Google Forms.** This tells the Zap to watch your Work Form for activity.
5. **Set the trigger event to "New Form Response."** This is what fires the Zap every time you submit the form.
6. **Connect your Google account.** This gives Zapier permission to see your form's responses.
7. **Select your Work Form under Form.** Choose the copy you made, not the template.
8. **Click Find new records.** This is on the Test tab. Zapier pulls your most recent responses so you have real data to build with.
9. **Select your fake response.** This is the one you submitted in step 1. Open it and check the answers match what you typed, that way you know which field name belongs to which question.
10. **Click Continue with selected record.** Every field you insert from here on pulls from this response.
11. **Add an action step and set the action app to Gmail.** This is what sends the email.
12. **Set the action event to "Send Email."** Gmail offers several events here. Send Email is the one that composes a new message rather than replying to an existing thread.
13. **Connect your Gmail account.** This gives Zapier permission to send email on your behalf.
14. **Enter your Accountability Partners' email addresses in the To field.** Every partner goes in here. Leave Cc, Bcc, From, From Name and Reply To alone, the only fields you touch on this step are To, Subject and Body.
15. **Write your Subject using the Framework above.** Type your full name and the words "Completed Hour" as plain text, then insert the Hour Number field from your Google Form at the end. Do not type a number there and do not leave it blank, it has to be the field or every email says the same hour.
16. **Write your Body using the Framework above.** Type every label out yourself, "Date:", "Start Time:", "Hour:", "Completion Time:", "Planned Output:", "Actual Output:", then insert the matching Google Form field after each one. The labels are plain text, only the values are fields.
17. **Test the action step.** Check your inbox and confirm the subject shows the right hour and every field in the body filled in with your fake response instead of showing blank.
18. **Add an action step and set the action app to SMS by Zapier.** This sends the same thing as a text. If SMS is unavailable where you are, set the action app to the group-chat app everyone actually checks instead, and get ChatGPT or Claude to walk you through setting that one up.
19. **Set the action event to "Send SMS."** This is the one that sends a plain text message to the number you connect in the next step.
20. **Connect your phone number.** Zapier sends a code to confirm it.
21. **Set the From Number to a specific number instead of "Random."** Pick any one from the list and keep it. On Random your texts arrive from a different number every time, so your partners get a new thread every hour instead of one running conversation.
22. **Write your Message using the Framework above.** Same rule as the body, labels typed out, values inserted as fields. Keep it short, SMS by Zapier caps the message at around 153 characters and anything past that gets cut, so this one only carries your name, the hour, what you completed and the date.
23. **Test the action step.** Check your phone and confirm the text arrived with the fields filled in.
24. **Publish the Zap.** Nothing sends automatically until this is turned on.

### Example

- My [6 Hour Productivity Course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l7RmB3LQd4w&t) on YouTube, that took me 130+ hours to create

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose one project that could substantially transform your business.** A course, product, lead magnet, advertising funnel, or content strategy. Once it is chosen, your first four hours are decided no matter what, and you can sit down and work. The time really flies when you do this, because you are putting your heart and soul into it.
2. **Make sure it is something you create.** The four hours go into building the thing itself, not managing it or paying somebody else to make it.
3. **Make sure it is valuable enough to deserve more than 100 hours.** At four hours a day that is more than 25 days of your life going into it. It has to be worth that.
4. **Hold it to a standard 10X higher than your previous best.** It is not some stupid little vision you are grinding out. It is literally something 10 times better than you did before.
5. **Put every piece of knowledge you have about your business into one document if you have no ideas.** This is what I would do for most business owners. You can put it out as a course on YouTube, give it away as a lead magnet, or use it to train your employees.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose one project that could substantially transform your business?
- Is it something you create?
- Is it valuable enough to deserve more than 100 hours?
- Are you holding it to a standard 10X higher than your previous best?
- If you had no ideas, did you put every piece of knowledge you have about your business into one document?

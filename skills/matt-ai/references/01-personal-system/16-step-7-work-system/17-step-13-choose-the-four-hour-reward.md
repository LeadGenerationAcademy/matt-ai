### Examples

- Go to a coffee shop, get a coffee, and chill while watching YouTube
- Go out and get lunch somewhere instead of eating at your desk
- Spend time outside with no phone
- Take time with your wife, kids, or a friend
- Sit in the sauna or take a long shower with nothing scheduled after it
- Watch an episode of a TV show
- Play video games
- Take a nap
- Get a massage
- Go for a swim

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Choose a larger reward that you receive only after completing all four work hours.** Mine is usually Starbucks, lunch, and a 30-minute break.
2. **Choose something you actually want.** If you would not go out of your way for it on a normal day, it is not going to pull you through hour four.
3. **Never give it to yourself early.** Take it after three hours and it stops being attached to finishing all four.

### Completion Audit

- Did you choose a larger reward that you receive only after completing all four work hours?
- Is it something you actually want?
- Are you giving it to yourself only after all four hours?

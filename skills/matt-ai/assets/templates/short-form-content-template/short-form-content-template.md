**New Follower Message Template**

Write the message every new follower receives automatically the moment they follow you.

This runs in ManyChat under the New Follower automation. Turn off the default opening DM first, or they get a generic message instead of this one.

Keep it light. This is an automated message and coming off salesy this early turns people off. Its only job is to separate the people who could buy from the people who are there for the content.

**Use This Template**

“Hey \[First Name\], thanks for the follow. Are you currently \[what your ICP does\], or just here for the content?”

**Example:**

“Hey John, thanks for the follow. Are you currently running an SEO agency, or just here for the content?”

**9 Question DM Script Template**

Write the conversation you run with anyone who replies to your New Follower Message.

The New Follower Message is question 1. The eight below it are questions 2 through 9.

This is a structure to follow, not a script to recite. Every conversation will differ, so the wording moves while the order stays.

**Use This Template**

Remember the New Follower Message is question 1.

1.  Hey \[First Name\], thanks for the follow. Are you currently \[what your ICP does\], or just here for the content?

2.  Good to hear. Whereabouts are you based?

3.  What got you into \[their industry\]?

4.  Mind if I ask a couple of questions to see if I can help?

5.  What's your number one struggle right now?

6.  What's your number one goal?

7.  Why is that important to you?

8.  Would you like some help?

9.  Tell you what, let's do this. Hop on a quick \[X minute\] call, we'll go through your number one struggle and your number one goal, and I'll help you put a roadmap together.

**How The Nine Move**

- **Question 1:** Separates the people who could buy from the people who are only there for the content.

- **Questions 2 and 3:** Rapport. Nothing is being sold yet.

- **Question 4:** Asks permission before you qualify, which is what stops the questions that follow feeling like an interrogation.

- **Questions 5, 6 and 7:** The qualification. Their struggle, their goal, and why that goal matters.

- **Question 8:** Closes on the idea of help, before a call is ever mentioned.

- **Question 9:** Closes on the call itself, and repeats their struggle and their goal back to them.

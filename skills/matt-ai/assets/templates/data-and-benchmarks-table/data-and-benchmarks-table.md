**Ad Spend Decision Making**

| **Unit Economics** | **Ad Spend Decision**           |
|--------------------|---------------------------------|
| Below 0.75X        | Decrease spend.                 |
| 0.75X to below 1X  | Keep spend the same.            |
| 1X to below 2X     | Increase spend by 20% per week. |
| 2X or more         | Increase spend by 50% per week. |

| **Metric**               | **Target or Definition**                                                |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Application Rate         | 6–10% of outbound clicks                                                |
| Call Booked Rate         | 3–5%+ of outbound clicks                                                |
| Show Rate                | 50–70%+ of bookings                                                     |
| Qualified Live Call Rate | 90%+ of attended calls                                                  |
| Close Rate               | Clients acquired ÷ qualified attended calls                             |
| Unit Economics           | Work toward at least 1X upfront coverage of acquisition and fulfillment |

**If This, Then That**

| **If This**                                                     | **Then That**                                                           |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Application rate is below 6%                                    | Review the above the fold, VSL, offer clarity and application friction. |
| Application rate is above 10%                                   | Review qualification and tighten the application where needed.          |
| Fewer than 90% of attended calls qualify                        | Review the ICP callout, messaging and application criteria.             |
| Show rate is below 50%                                          | Improve reminders, speed to lead and the pre-call flow.                 |
| Qualified calls are not closing                                 | Review recordings, the sales presentation, objections and follow-up.    |
| Acquisition cost is acceptable, but unit economics are below 1X | Review upfront cash collected, payment terms and fulfillment costs.     |
| A message produces clients at an acceptable cost                | Create and test more versions of it.                                    |
| Sales or fulfillment cannot handle more volume                  | Hold or reduce spend while addressing the constraint.                   |

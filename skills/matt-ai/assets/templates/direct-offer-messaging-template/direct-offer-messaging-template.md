# Direct Offer Messaging Template

**Direct Offer Messaging**

Use the most frequently mentioned problem from your research, that your Offer solves, when writing your direct Offer messaging.

**Use The Exact Language Your ICP Use To Describe:**

- Who they are

- Their current circumstances

- Their main problem

- Their desired outcome

- Why they want that outcome

- What they have already tried

- Why those previous solutions failed

- How your Offer solves the problem

**Use This Template**

"We help **\[specific level of ICP\]** solve **\[main problem\]** and achieve **\[desired outcome\]** through **\[Offer or mechanism\].**"

If you have a guarantee, add: "If **\[desired outcome\]** does not happen, **\[what they get instead\].**"

**Example:**  
"We help agency owners stop relying on referrals and build predictable lead flow through our done for you ads and funnel system. If we don't generate 20 qualified leads in 30 days, you get your money back.”

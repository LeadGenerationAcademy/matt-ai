**Ad Spend Decision Making**

Review spend weekly using the unit economics model:

Net Upfront Cash ÷ (Acquisition Cost + Fulfillment Cost)

| **Upfront Cost Coverage** | **Weekly Decision**    |
|---------------------------|------------------------|
| Below 0.75X               | Decrease spend.        |
| 0.75X to below 1X         | Keep spend the same.   |
| 1X to below 2X            | Increase spend by 20%. |
| 2X or more                | Increase spend by 50%. |

Give your sales cycle time to play out. Compare the cash and costs for the same group of customers.

These increases also require cash and capacity. If sales or fulfillment cannot handle more volume, hold or reduce spend while you fix that.

**If This, Then That**

Golden Rule: If your CPA is acceptable, the other metrics can look like shit and the campaign can still work.

Follow the same progression: Clicks → Applications → Bookings → Shows → Closes. Find where people are dropping off, then work on that part.

| **If This**                                               | **Then That**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| CPM is over \$100 and CPA is unacceptable                 | Rethink the ads. Is it instantly clear who the ad is for and what you are offering? Are you using messaging your ICP actually cares about and giving them a reason to believe you?                                                                                                               |
| Outbound CTR is below 1% and acquisition is too expensive | Work on the hook, the offer, the proof and how quickly someone can understand the ad. For a static image, look at the headline, contrast and visual hierarchy. For a video, show who it is for and what you are offering immediately, with proof within the first five seconds.                  |
| Application rate is below 6%                              | Check whether the ad appeals to the wrong people, the landing page is not good enough or the form is too strict. Figure out which one it is before changing everything.                                                                                                                          |
| Application rate is above 10%                             | Look at who is applying. Make the ICP and offer more specific in the ad and on the page, then tighten the application where needed.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Fewer than 90% of live calls qualify                      | Make the ICP and offer more specific in the ad and on the page. Then tighten the application where needed.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Show rate is below 50%                                    | Make more calendar spots available sooner and shorten the maximum booking window. Then check speed to lead, the thank you page and selfie video, the pre-call emails and sales assets, email and SMS reminders, and whether the calendar invite and emails are actually arriving.                |
| Fewer than 15% of live calls close                        | Check qualification first. If the people are qualified, listen to the recordings and find where the introduction, questions, presentation, objection handling or ask is going wrong. Check for a clear proposal, contract, payment link and next step, and that follow-up is actually happening. |
| Sales or fulfillment cannot handle more volume            | Hold or reduce spend while you fix that.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

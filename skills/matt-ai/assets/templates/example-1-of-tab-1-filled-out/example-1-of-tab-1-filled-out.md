#### **Idea \#1**

##### Big Idea: Your agency has one acquisition channel, and it is you.

**The Connection:** Your agency has one acquisition channel and it is you, so we build you one that isn't.

**Why The Old Way Does Not Work:** Every client the agency has ever won came through the owner's relationships, the owner's network, or the owner's calls. When the owner is unavailable, acquisition stops entirely.

**Why Your Approach Makes More Sense:** We build and run a channel that produces booked calls whether the owner is on a beach or in a client meeting. Nothing about it routes through him.

**Keep / Remove:** Keep

#### **Idea \#2**

##### Big Idea: An agency that stops selling when the owner gets busy is not a business yet.

**The Connection:** Your agency is not a business yet, so we build and run your acquisition channel.

**Why The Old Way Does Not Work:** The owner is the bottleneck on every function, not just acquisition.

**Why Your Approach Makes More Sense:** The honest fix here is hiring, delegation, and an operating layer. Acquisition is one piece of that and not the biggest. The connection takes three paragraphs and the prospect will not walk that far.

**Keep / Remove:** Remove

#### **Idea \#3**

##### Big Idea: Referrals are not a lead source. They are a result you cannot schedule.

**The Connection:** Referrals are a result you cannot schedule, so we build you a source you can.

**Why The Old Way Does Not Work:** Referral volume moves with somebody else's business, somebody else's memory, and somebody else's timing. None of it is yours.

**Why Your Approach Makes More Sense:** A channel you own produces the same number of calls in a quiet month as a loud one, because the input is ad spend and sequences instead of goodwill.

**Keep / Remove:** Keep

#### **Idea \#4**

##### Big Idea: Every agency is worse at marketing itself than it is at marketing its clients.

**The Connection:** You are worse at marketing yourself than at marketing your clients, so we do it for you.

**Why The Old Way Does Not Work:** Client work always wins the calendar. The agency's own marketing gets whatever is left, which is nothing.

**Why Your Approach Makes More Sense:** We run their acquisition the way they run a client account, with the same attention a paying client would get.

**Criterion \#1: Simple**

Can the Big Idea be immediately understood and repeated in one sentence?

- ⭐ 1 Star: There is no identifiable core idea

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Requires substantial explanation

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Understandable, but contains multiple messages

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Clear and repeatable with minor simplification

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Immediately understood and accurately repeated after one exposure

**Criterion \#2: Unexpected**

Does the Big Idea challenge an existing belief or introduce a surprising opportunity?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Indistinguishable from everyone else's marketing

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Generic and predictable

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Uses a familiar idea with a slightly different angle

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Presents a strong and distinct perspective

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Completely changes how the ICP understands the problem while remaining believable

**Criterion \#3: Concrete**

Can the ICP clearly picture what the Big Idea means?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Built entirely from meaningless claims or buzzwords

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Mostly abstract

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Understandable, but still contains vague language

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Mostly specific with very little abstraction

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Extremely specific and easy to visualize

**Criterion \#4: Credible**

Score this using the proof you already know exists. Do not invent proof. The complete Proof System gets built later.

- ⭐ 1 Star: Unbelievable or contradicted by the available evidence

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Has strong proof and a believable explanation

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Supported by overwhelming relevant proof and is easy to believe or verify

**Criterion \#5: Emotional**

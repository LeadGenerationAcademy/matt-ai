**Compare Against Made To Stick**

Score each of your three Big Ideas against the six qualities from Made to Stick.

Each criterion is worth five points. Maximum Total Score: 30/30.

A score of 1/5 or 2/5 in any individual criterion is fatal, regardless of the Total Score.

**Criterion \#1: Simple**

Can the Big Idea be immediately understood and repeated in one sentence?

- ⭐ 1 Star: There is no identifiable core idea

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Requires substantial explanation

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Understandable, but contains multiple messages

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Clear and repeatable with minor simplification

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Immediately understood and accurately repeated after one exposure

**Criterion \#2: Unexpected**

Does the Big Idea challenge an existing belief or introduce a surprising opportunity?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Indistinguishable from everyone else's marketing

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Generic and predictable

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Uses a familiar idea with a slightly different angle

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Presents a strong and distinct perspective

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Completely changes how the ICP understands the problem while remaining believable

**Criterion \#3: Concrete**

Can the ICP clearly picture what the Big Idea means?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Built entirely from meaningless claims or buzzwords

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Mostly abstract

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Understandable, but still contains vague language

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Mostly specific with very little abstraction

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Extremely specific and easy to visualize

**Criterion \#4: Credible**

Score this using the proof you already know exists. Do not invent proof. The complete Proof System gets built later.

Does the ICP have a reason to believe the Big Idea?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Unbelievable or contradicted by the available evidence

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Possible, but mostly unsupported

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Sounds plausible and has some support

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Has strong proof and a believable explanation

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Supported by overwhelming relevant proof and is easy to believe or verify

**Criterion \#5: Emotional**

Does the Big Idea make the ICP care?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Gives the ICP no reason to care

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Creates very little emotional response

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Valuable, but primarily logical

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Connects strongly to something the ICP cares about

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Creates an immediate and powerful emotional response

**Criterion \#6: Stories**

Score this using the stories you already know exist. Do not invent stories.

Can the Big Idea be demonstrated through real stories?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Never demonstrated through a story

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Only supported by vague anecdotes

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Supported by at least one clear story

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Supported by multiple relevant stories

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Reinforced through several compelling and specific stories

**Test Durability & Ownability**

Score every Big Idea that survived Tab 5 across three criteria.

Each criterion is worth five points. Maximum Total Score: 15/15.

A score of 1/5 or 2/5 in any individual criterion is fatal, regardless of the Total Score.

**Before You Start**

- Research what your competitors repeatedly communicate through their Offers, websites, ads, content, and funnels

**Criterion \#1: Durability**

Will this Big Idea still be relevant in five years?

- ⭐ 1 Star: The idea is short-lived and will probably become irrelevant before the business can own it

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: The idea depends heavily on a temporary trend, platform, technology, or market condition

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: The underlying problem will remain, but the idea may require meaningful changes over time

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: The belief should remain relevant for at least five years with only minor changes

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: The underlying belief will remain relevant for at least five years and can support years of marketing

**Criterion \#2: Ownability**

How much competition exists for this Big Idea?

- ⭐ 1 Star: Already strongly owned by another business or could be claimed by almost anyone

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Crowded, generic, and easily interchangeable with competitors

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Several competitors use similar ideas, but no business clearly owns the position

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Some competitors use related ideas, but yours remains distinct and realistically ownable

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: No serious competitor owns the idea, and there is a clear opportunity to become strongly associated with it

**Criterion \#3: Willingness to Commit**

This one is based entirely on you. Durability and Ownability can be researched. This cannot.

Do not give yourself a high score because the idea looks good on paper.

Are you willing to repeat this Big Idea for years?

- ⭐ 1 Star: You are unwilling to build the business around the idea or repeat it consistently

- ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: You are already reluctant and will probably abandon it before the market associates it with you

- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: You are willing to use it, but not certain you want the business associated with it long term

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: You are strongly committed to the idea with only minor reservations

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: You are completely willing to build the business around this idea and repeat it for at least five years

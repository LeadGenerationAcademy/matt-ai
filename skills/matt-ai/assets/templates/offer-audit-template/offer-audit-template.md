# Offer Audit Template

**Offer Audit**

Score every category from 1-5 stars using the criteria below.

Add all 8 scores together and compare the total against the scoring scale at the end.

**1. Built for Cold Traffic**

Does the Offer make sense to someone who does not know or trust you?

They should not need to consume 10 hours of your content before understanding what you sell and why it is relevant to them.

- ⭐⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 5 Stars:** They immediately understand what you sell and why it is relevant

- ⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 4 Stars:** They understand after a short, simple explanation

- ⭐⭐**⭐ 3 Stars:** They need additional context before they understand

- **⭐⭐ 2 Stars:** They need substantial education before the Offer makes sense

- ⭐ **1 Star:** They are still confused after you explain it

**Your Score:** \_ / 5

**2. Dream Outcome / Something People MUST Buy**

How badly does your specific ideal customer want or need the result?

The strongest Offers solve urgent, important and expensive problems connected to something the customer already wants and spends money trying to achieve.

- ⭐⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 5 Stars:** Solves an urgent problem the customer feels they must address now

- ⭐**⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars:** Solves an important problem the customer clearly wants to address

- ⭐**⭐⭐ 3 Stars:** Solves a real and valuable problem, but the customer is comfortable waiting

- **⭐⭐ 2 Stars:** Solves a minor problem or provides a useful nice-to-have

- **⭐ 1 Star:** Solves a problem the customer barely cares about

**Your Score:** \_ / 5

**3. Creates Obvious Value**

Does the Offer help the customer make more money, save time, reduce costs, reduce risk or increase speed?

How substantial is that improvement, and how clearly can the customer see its value relative to what they pay?

One extremely valuable result can earn five stars. The Offer does not need to do everything on the list.

- ⭐⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 5 Stars:** Creates a substantial, measurable improvement whose value clearly exceeds the price

- ⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 4 Stars:** Creates a substantial improvement, but its value relative to the price needs more explanation

- ⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** Creates a clear benefit, but its size or financial value is difficult to establish

- ⭐**⭐ 2 Stars:** Creates a vague or minor improvement

- **⭐ 1 Star:** Creates no obvious value for the customer

**Your Score: \_ / 5**

**4. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement**

How strongly does the customer believe your Offer will work for someone like them?

Increase their belief through relevant proof, case studies, clear deliverables, a believable explanation of how the Offer works and answers to their major objections.

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars:** Has overwhelming proof from customers in similar situations and makes success feel extremely likely

- ⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 4 Stars:** Has strong relevant proof, a clear mechanism and answers to the major objections

- ⭐⭐**⭐ 3 Stars:** Has a believable explanation and some proof

- **⭐⭐ 2 Stars:** The promise sounds possible but is mostly unsupported

- ⭐ **1 Star:** The customer has no reason to believe it will work

**Your Score:** \_ / 5

**5. Clear Result or Deliverable**

Does the customer know exactly what they are buying and receiving?

Ideally, sell a result. If you do not control the final result, sell a clear deliverable directly connected to it.

- ⭐⭐**⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars:** Clearly defines the result or deliverable, what is included and when the customer will receive it

- ⭐⭐⭐**⭐ 4 Stars:** Clearly defines the result or deliverable and what is included, but the timing is unclear

- ⭐**⭐⭐ 3 Stars:** The customer understands the general deliverable, but important details about what they receive are unclear

- ⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** Describes activities without clearly explaining what the customer receives

- ⭐ **1 Star:** The Offer is completely vague

**Your Score:** \_ / 5

**6. Time to First Value**

How quickly does the customer receive their first meaningful result or benefit?

This does not have to be the complete result. It should be something the customer can use or benefit from while you work toward it.

Measure from when the customer buys.

- ⭐⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 5 Stars:** Within 24 hours

- ⭐⭐⭐**⭐ 4 Stars:** More than 24 hours, but within seven days

- ⭐⭐**⭐ 3 Stars:** More than seven days, but within 30 days

- ⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** More than 30 days, but within 90 days

- ⭐ **1 Star:** More than 90 days

**Your Score:** \_ / 5

**7. Customer Effort and Sacrifice**

How much work must the customer complete to receive the value you promise?

Include their onboarding, meetings, decisions, approvals and required implementation.

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐**⭐ 5 Stars:** Almost everything is done for them, with minimal onboarding and almost no ongoing work

- **⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars:** The customer completes some onboarding and a small amount of ongoing work

- ⭐⭐**⭐ 3 Stars:** The customer must participate regularly, but the workload is manageable

- ⭐**⭐ 2 Stars:** The customer must complete substantial work throughout the service

- ⭐ **1 Star:** The customer must do almost everything

**Your Score:** \_ / 5

**8. Strong Risk Reversal**

How much downside does the customer face if the Offer does not work out?

Their fee is not their only risk. They can also lose time, money, opportunity and reputation if you mess something up.

A guarantee can help, but the price, scope and commitment should also limit their downside.

- ⭐⭐⭐**⭐⭐ 5 Stars:** There is almost no realistic downside for the right customer

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars:** Their financial, time and implementation risks are substantially limited

- ⭐⭐**⭐ 3 Stars:** Some risks are limited, but meaningful downside remains

- ⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** There is very little protection if the Offer does not work out

- ⭐ **1 Star:** The customer assumes all the risk

**Your Score:** \_ / 5

**Final Score**

Add up all 8 scores for a total out of 40.

**Total Score:** \_ / 40

**Offer Scoring Scale:**

- 36–40: Exceptional

- 32–35: Strong

- 24–31: Acceptable, with clear weaknesses to improve

- 16–23: Weak

- 8–15: Terrible

Fix every individual category scoring below four stars, even if your total score is high.

Start with your lowest score, improve that part of the Offer, and complete the audit again.

**Offer Amplification**

You have already determined whether your Offer is good.

Now you're going to make it more valuable using Alex Hormozi's Value Formula, broken into four scoreable parts.

**The Simple Version**

There are four ways to make an Offer more valuable:

- Give the customer a better result

- Make them believe they are more likely to achieve it

- Give them value faster

- Require less work from them

5 stars always means good. 1 star always means bad.

**The Value Formula**

Alex Hormozi's Value Formula is:

**Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort and Sacrifice)**

You do not need to calculate the actual formula.

#### **Idea \#1**

##### Big Idea: Every brand that scales is chasing \$100,000 days.

**The Connection:** Every brand that scales is chasing \$100,000 days, so we build the account and the creative that produce one.

**Why The Old Way Does Not Work:** They manage to a monthly revenue target and a blended ROAS, and both numbers can look acceptable while the brand sits exactly where it was a year ago.

**Why Your Approach Makes More Sense:** A day is a target you can aim a specific campaign and a specific batch of creative at, then hit or miss with no ambiguity about which it was.

**The Connection:** You have already had one, so we build the system that produces the rest.

**Why The Old Way Does Not Work:** The first one came from a launch or a holiday, which is a thing that happens to the brand rather than a thing the brand does.

**Why Your Approach Makes More Sense:** We work out what carried that day, then build enough creative volume and enough account structure to reproduce the conditions on an ordinary Tuesday.

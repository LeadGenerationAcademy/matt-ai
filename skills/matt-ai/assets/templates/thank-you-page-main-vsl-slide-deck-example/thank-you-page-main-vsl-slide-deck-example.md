## Slide 2

Next Steps

You're booked in. Your call is with me, John Smith, and it takes about 30 minutes.
On the call, we'll look at where your new clients come from right now, and I'll show you exactly how a cold email channel would work for your agency. Have your current monthly revenue and the type of client you want more of ready.
Before the call, scroll down and complete the three steps below this video.

## Slide 3

How much does it cost?

$4,500 a month for us to build, run, and optimize the whole channel, billed month to month. There's no setup fee.
Domains and inboxes run about $300 a month and are billed to you directly.

## Slide 4

Will this hurt my main domain?

No. We never send a single email from your main domain. Everything goes out from separate domains we buy, authenticate, and warm for you, so your deliverability holds no matter how much volume we push.

## Slide 5

How fast will I start seeing calls?

Sending goes live on day 15, and the first replies usually come back that same week. Across our agencies, the average is 17 qualified calls booked in the first 60 days.

## Slide 6

Will the calls actually be qualified?

We build the list ourselves against your exact client profile instead of buying one, so every person we contact could realistically sign with you.
You also get context on every prospect before you join the call.

## Slide 7

How much of my time does this take?

Very little. Our team buys the domains, builds the list, writes the copy, sends every day, and handles the replies.
You approve the first campaigns in week two. After that, your job is to show up to the calls.

## Slide 8

I already tried cold email. Why is this different?

Most agencies that tried it sent from their main domain, bought a list, and ran one campaign for a few weeks.
We send from separate warmed domains, build the list against your exact client profile, and test new angles every week, keeping only what gets replies.

## Slide 9

Do I need a sales team?

No. You need one person who can take the calls, and for most agencies your size that's the founder.
Once the calendar is too full for you to take every call, that's usually when agencies bring on a closer.

## Slide 10

What happens after the first 60 days?

On day 60 we review your numbers with you: sends, reply rate, calls booked, and cost per call.
Then we plan the next quarter together and put more volume behind the angles that are booking calls.

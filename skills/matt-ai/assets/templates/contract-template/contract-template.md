# Contract Template

> **Service Agreement**

This agreement is made between the Provider and the Client named below.

| **Client Name**       | \[ \] |
|-----------------------|-------|
| **Client Business**   | \[ \] |
| **Provider Name**     | \[ \] |
| **Provider Business** | \[ \] |
| **Start Date**        | \[ \] |
| **Services**          | \[ \] |
| **Fee**               | \[ \] |
| **Payment Schedule**  | \[ \] |
| **Term**              | \[ \] |
| **State or Country**  | \[ \] |

1.  **Scope of Services.** The Provider will deliver the Services described above. Anything not described above sits outside this agreement and will be quoted separately before any work on it begins.

2.  **Payment Terms.** The Client will pay the Fee on the Payment Schedule above. Invoices are due on receipt unless the Payment Schedule says otherwise. If a payment is more than 7 days late, the Provider may pause all work until the account is current.

3.  **What The Client Provides.** The Client will give the Provider the access, accounts, assets and approvals needed to do the work, and will respond to requests for feedback within 3 business days. Any delay on the Client's side extends the delivery timeline by the same amount of time.

4.  **Term and Renewal.** This agreement begins on the Start Date and runs for the Term above. It renews for the same length unless either party gives notice under the Termination section.

5.  **Termination.** Either party may end this agreement by giving 30 days written notice. The Client stays responsible for fees covering work completed through the end of that notice period.

6.  **Ownership.** Once the Client has paid in full, everything produced under this agreement belongs to the Client, including any accounts, domains and assets created on the Client's behalf. Until payment is made in full, that work remains the property of the Provider.

7.  **Confidentiality.** Both parties will keep the other's non-public information confidential and will not share it with anyone outside this agreement without written permission. This obligation continues after the agreement ends.

8.  **Independent Contractor.** The Provider is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Client. The Provider is responsible for their own taxes, insurance and equipment, and decides how the work gets done.

9.  **No Guarantee Of Results.** The Provider will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care. The Provider does not guarantee any specific result, revenue figure or outcome.

10. **Limitation Of Liability.** Neither party is liable to the other for more than the total fees paid under this agreement in the 3 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses.

11. **Governing Law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of the State or Country written above, and both parties agree to settle any dispute there.

Both parties agree to the terms above.

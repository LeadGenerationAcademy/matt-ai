# Media Partnership One-Pager Template

**Partnership One-Pager Template**

There are three one-pagers in here, each with a filled example

White label and referral go to the same type of person, so they share the same skeleton and only change how the arrangement works.

Media goes to someone with an audience instead of clients, so the fields change.

**Apply the Value Formula**

Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice). Every field is carrying one of those four parts.

Dream Outcome is the Result / Headline at the top.

Perceived Likelihood of Achievement is the Guarantee and the case studies.

Time Delay is the Timeline.

Effort and Sacrifice is the Offer Summary, which is where you show how little they have to do.

**Everything links**

Every case study links to the actual case study. The website, the YouTube channel and the Loom all link out. A one-pager with dead text where a link should be is doing half a job.

**One page**

Each of these is one page. If it runs onto a second page, cut until it does not.

**The three criteria this has to hit**

For white label and referral: what is in it for them beyond the fee, how they know you will not embarrass them, and what risk-free offer you can give their clients. For media, the middle one changes to whether you will bring your own traffic and promote it yourself.

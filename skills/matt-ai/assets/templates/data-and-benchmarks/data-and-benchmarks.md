**One-Page Learning Document Example**

# **1. Learnings On People Who Bought**

- They were already spending on ads themselves and getting nowhere, they were not starting from zero

- They came to us because referrals had slowed down, not because they had a growth plan

- Every one of them had the budget ready and did not have to ask anyone else to sign off

# **2. Learnings On People Who Didn't Buy**

- Most of them were still getting enough referrals to feel comfortable doing nothing

- Several wanted a guaranteed number of clients before they would commit to anything

- A few were the marketing manager, not the owner, and had to take it back to someone else

# **3. Questions & Objections That Keep Coming Up**

- How is this different from the last agency that ran our ads

- What happens if the ads do not work in the first month

- Do we own the ad account and the creative when this ends

# **4. What People Respond To Or Misunderstand About The Offer**

- They respond to case studies from agencies at their own revenue level

- They respond to how fast the first campaign goes live

- They misunderstand the price, several thought ad spend was included in the retainer

# **5. What You Need To Change In The Ads**

- Put the price in the ad so the ad spend question stops coming up on calls

- Test a headline built on referrals slowing down, since that is what the buyers actually said

- Swap the proof in Design 2 for an agency at the same revenue level

# **6. What You Need To Change In The Funnel**

- Answer the ad account ownership question inside the VSL

- Add the what happens if it does not work in month one answer to the pre-call emails

- Make the above the fold say referrals slowing down, so it matches the winning ad

# **7. What You Need To Change In The Sales Process**

- Add a slide on why the previous agency failed, before the slide on how our offer works

- Move the case studies earlier, they are the thing people actually respond to

- Ask about decision making authority before presenting the offer, not after

| **If This**                     | **Then That**                                                                                                                       |
|---------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| CPM \> \$100                    | Completely redo or rethink your ads.                                                                                                |
| Outbound CTR \< 1%              | Completely redo or rethink your ads.                                                                                                |
| Cost Per Outbound Click \> \$10 | Not a dealbreaker, but unlikely to work. Review the ad improvements above.                                                          |
| Application Rate \< 6%          | Rework the above the fold and VSL. Make the offer clearer, show relevant proof and make sure the page matches the ad.               |
| Application Rate \> 10%         | If you are still using the application, tighten it and add friction.                                                                |
| Close Rate \< 15%               | Check show rate first, then review pre-call framing, the sales calls and follow-up. Use the recordings to identify what needs work. |
| Close Rate \> 30%               | Increase your price.                                                                                                                |
| Unqualified Calls \> 50%        | Make the ICP and offer more specific in your ads and on the page. Tighten the application if needed.                                |
| Show Rate \<50%                 | Improve pre-call flow, reminders, speed to lead                                                                                     |

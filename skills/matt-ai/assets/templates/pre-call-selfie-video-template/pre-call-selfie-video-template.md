# Pre-Call Selfie Video Template

**Pre-Call Selfie Video**

The Selfie Video puts a face to the name before the Sales Call. It should feel like you recorded it specifically for that prospect, even though you use the same video for everyone.

# **Framework & Criteria**

- 60 seconds or less

- Film in a way that is could plausibly be custom-filmed for them

- This should be paired with the appointment setting reach out in the next step

# **Use This Template**

“Hey, it is \[Your Name\] from \[Company\].

I saw that you booked. That is great.

I am sending you a follow-up text with a few options, but other than that, I just wanted to put a face to the name.

I will talk to you soon, and we will see if what we offer is right for you.”
